Bypass Bilateral Ureters to Ileocutaneous with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this complex procedure, the ureters from both kidneys are rerouted to connect with a section of ileum, a part of the small intestine, which is brought to the skin's surface, creating an ileocutaneous urostomy. Autologous tissue substitute, typically derived from the patient's own body, is used to facilitate the bypass. The open approach involves a surgical incision to access the ureters and intestine, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and reconnect the tissues. This procedure is often performed to treat severe ureteral obstruction, injury, or cancer, and may offer improved quality of life for individuals with urinary diversion needs.
