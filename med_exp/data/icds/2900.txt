Excision of Rectum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the rectum and remove it through a natural or artificial opening, typically performed to treat rectal cancers or other conditions that require removal of the rectum. The endoscope is inserted through the anus or a small incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the rectum and surrounding tissue, and then carefully excise the rectum while minimizing damage to surrounding tissue and organs. The procedure may be performed using various endoscopic techniques, including laparoscopic or robotic-assisted surgery, and may require a temporary or permanent colostomy. The goal of the procedure is to remove the affected rectal tissue while preserving as much bowel function as possible, and to improve the patient's quality of life.
