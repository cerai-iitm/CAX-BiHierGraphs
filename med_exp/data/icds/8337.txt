High Dose Rate (HDR) Brachytherapy of Pelvic Region using Iridium 192 (Ir-192)
High Dose Rate (HDR) brachytherapy is a type of internal radiation therapy that involves placing a small amount of radioactive material, in this case, Iridium 192 (Ir-192), directly into the pelvic region to deliver a high dose of radiation to the targeted area. This procedure is often used to treat various types of cancer, including cervical, prostate, and uterine cancer, by destroying cancer cells while minimizing damage to surrounding tissues. During the procedure, a specialized applicator is inserted into the pelvic region and connected to a computer-controlled afterloader, which delivers the Ir-192 source to the targeted area for a predetermined amount of time. The entire process is carefully planned and monitored to ensure precise delivery of the radiation dose and minimize potential side effects.
