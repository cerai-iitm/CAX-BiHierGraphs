Beam Radiation of Humerus using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This non-invasive procedure utilizes photon beam radiation to target the humerus, a long bone in the upper arm. The radiation, with energies ranging from 1 to 10 million electron volts (MeV), is carefully calibrated to destroy cancer cells or alleviate symptoms caused by tumors in this region. During the treatment, the patient is positioned to ensure the photon beam is directed precisely at the affected area, minimizing damage to surrounding healthy tissue. The goal of this treatment is to relieve pain, improve function, and enhance quality of life for patients with humerus-related disorders.
