Dilation of Left External Iliac Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small balloon-tipped catheter through the skin and guiding it to the left external iliac artery, a major blood vessel in the pelvis. Once in place, the balloon is inflated to widen the artery, improving blood flow and relieving blockage or narrowing. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. The goal of the procedure is to restore adequate blood flow to the affected leg, alleviating symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness.
