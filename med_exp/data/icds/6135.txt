Replacement of Left Knee Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, the damaged or arthritic left knee joint is replaced with an uncemented synthetic substitute, allowing for a more natural joint movement and reducing pain and stiffness. The synthetic joint, typically made of metal and plastic components, is designed to mimic the natural joint's function and is implanted without the use of bone cement. The uncemented design allows for a more durable and long-lasting bond between the implant and surrounding bone tissue. The open approach involves a larger incision to access the knee joint, enabling the surgeon to directly visualize and remove damaged tissue and bone before implanting the new joint.
