Extraction of Right Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right upper leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue just beneath the skin. The surgeon then carefully extracts the targeted tissue and fascia, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding blood vessels and nerves. The procedure may be performed to remove damaged or diseased tissue, repair injuries, or treat conditions such as compartment syndrome. Following the extraction, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for post-operative complications and wound healing.
