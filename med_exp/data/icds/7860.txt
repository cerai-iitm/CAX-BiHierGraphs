Extracorporeal Membrane Oxygenation, Continuous
This life-sustaining therapy uses a complex device to oxygenate the blood outside the body, artificially supporting heart and lung function when the organs are too damaged or weak to function properly. The continuous flow of oxygen-rich blood helps to stabilize the patient's vital signs, allowing the heart and lungs to rest and recover. The extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) machine pumps the patient's blood through a membrane, which removes carbon dioxide and adds oxygen, before returning the oxygenated blood to the patient. This treatment is often used in critical care settings, such as ICUs, to support patients with severe respiratory or cardiac failure.
