Repair Duodenum, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, an incision is made in the abdomen to access the duodenum, which is the first part of the small intestine. The surgeon then repairs the duodenum by closing any holes or tears, resecting damaged tissue, or reconnecting severed ends. This complex procedure requires careful dissection and reconstruction to restore normal digestive function. The goal of the repair is to ensure the duodenum can properly absorb nutrients and fluids from food, and to prevent further complications such as infection, bleeding, or bowel obstruction.
