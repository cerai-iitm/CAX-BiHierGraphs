Inspection of Lower Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves insertion of a small scope or camera through the skin to visually examine the lower artery, allowing for real-time observation of the blood vessel. The percutaneous approach enables the healthcare provider to assess the artery's condition, including any blockages, narrowing, or damage, without the need for open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia or mild sedation, and the tiny incision is usually closed with a small bandage or dressing. The inspection of the lower artery provides valuable information to guide further treatment, such as angioplasty or stenting, to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms.
