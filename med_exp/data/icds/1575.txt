Excision of Left Saphenous Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the left saphenous vein through a small incision in the skin, utilizing a percutaneous approach to access the vein. Under imaging guidance, a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to carefully dissect and excise the targeted section of the vein. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as venous insufficiency, varicose veins, or skin ulcers, and can help alleviate symptoms like pain, swelling, and skin discoloration. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical techniques.
