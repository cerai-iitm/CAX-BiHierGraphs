Beam Radiation of Whole Body using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This procedure involves the therapeutic use of high-energy photon beams to irradiate the entire body. The radiation therapy is administered using photons with energies ranging from 1 to 10 million electron volts (MeV), which allows for deep penetration and effective treatment of systemic diseases. The whole-body irradiation is typically used to prepare patients for bone marrow or stem cell transplantation, as well as to treat certain types of blood cancer, such as leukemia. The goal of this treatment is to destroy malignant cells and suppress the immune system to prevent graft rejection.
