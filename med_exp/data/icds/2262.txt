Extirpation of Matter from Right Upper Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the inside of the airways and identify the blockage in the right upper lobe bronchus. The procedure is performed to remove foreign matter, such as mucus plugs or blood clots, that are obstructing the airway and preventing proper breathing. Through the endoscope, specialized instruments are inserted to carefully extract the blockage, restoring airflow to the affected lung. The entire process is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort.
