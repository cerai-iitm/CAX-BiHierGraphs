Reattachment of Right Lower Arm and Wrist Tendon, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the healthcare provider makes an open incision in the right lower arm to access the affected tendon. The tendon, which connects muscles to bones, is carefully reattached to its original anchor point, typically using sutures or other fixation methods. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and range of motion to the wrist and arm, alleviating symptoms such as pain, weakness, and limited mobility. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely repair the tendon and surrounding tissues.
