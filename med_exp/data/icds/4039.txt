Excision of Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the chest area, where the subcutaneous tissue and fascia are carefully excised to remove damaged or diseased tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the targeted tissue. The excision of the subcutaneous tissue and fascia helps to alleviate symptoms and improve the overall appearance of the chest area. The procedure is often performed under general anesthesia and may involve additional reconstructive steps to restore the natural contours of the chest.
