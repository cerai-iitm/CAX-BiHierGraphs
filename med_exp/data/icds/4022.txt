Drainage of Right Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a deliberate incision in the skin of the right foot to access the subcutaneous tissue and underlying fascia. The goal is to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or infectious material from the affected area, alleviating pressure and promoting healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected tissue, enabling the surgeon to remove any infected or damaged tissue and introduce antibiotic agents if necessary. Following drainage, the incision is typically closed, and the foot is immobilized to facilitate the recovery process.
