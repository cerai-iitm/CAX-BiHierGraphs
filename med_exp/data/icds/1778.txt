Excision of Spleen, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the spleen, a vital organ located in the upper left region of the abdomen, just below the diaphragm. The surgeon carefully dissects the spleen from surrounding tissues and ligates its blood vessels to minimize bleeding. The spleen is then removed from the abdominal cavity, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to restore normal anatomy. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat various conditions, including traumatic injury, cancer, or blood disorders.
