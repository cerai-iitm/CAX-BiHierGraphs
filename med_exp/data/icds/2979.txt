Insertion of Feeding Device into Stomach, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a feeding device is inserted directly into the stomach to provide long-term nutritional support. This involves making an incision in the abdomen and guiding the device through the stomach wall, securing it in place with sutures or staples. The feeding tube is then connected to a reservoir or pump, allowing for the direct delivery of nutrients and medications. This procedure is often performed in patients who have difficulty swallowing or absorbing nutrients through the digestive tract.
