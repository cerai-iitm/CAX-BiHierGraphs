Detachment at Right Index Finger, Complete, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the right index finger from the hand through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to disconnect the finger from the rest of the hand, which may be necessary due to severe trauma, infection, or tumors. The surgeon will carefully separate the finger from the surrounding soft tissue and bone, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding structures. The resulting detachment allows for the removal of the affected finger, which can help to alleviate pain, prevent further injury, or improve overall hand function.
