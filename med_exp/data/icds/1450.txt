Extirpation of Matter from Left Cephalic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description based on the provided code and long title:

This procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter from the left cephalic vein, a superficial vein located on the lateral aspect of the upper arm, through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The percutaneous approach allows for a small incision in the skin, reducing the risk of complications and scarring. During the procedure, a thin instrument is inserted through the incision to access the left cephalic vein, and the unwanted matter is then carefully extracted. The removal of the unwanted matter helps to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and discomfort in the affected arm.
