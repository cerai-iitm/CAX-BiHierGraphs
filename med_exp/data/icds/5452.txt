Reposition Right Patella with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right patella to its normal anatomical location, typically performed to treat patellar dislocation or malalignment. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area, allowing for precise repositioning of the patella. Internal fixation devices, such as screws or wires, are then used to hold the patella in place, promoting proper healing and stability. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility, and to improve overall knee function.
