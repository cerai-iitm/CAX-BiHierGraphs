Extirpation of Matter from Left Lower Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is the description:

This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of abnormal or unwanted matter from the left lower lobe bronchus, a tube that transports air to the lung, through a natural or artificial opening. The goal is to restore normal breathing function and alleviate symptoms such as coughing, wheezing, or shortness of breath. The procedure typically involves the insertion of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, through the mouth or nose to visualize the area and extract the unwanted material. By removing the obstructing matter, this procedure helps to improve lung function and overall respiratory health.
