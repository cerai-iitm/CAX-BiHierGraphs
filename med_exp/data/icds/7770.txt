Irrigation of Cranial Cavity and Brain using Irrigating Substance, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a device into the cranial cavity to irrigate and cleanse the brain tissue and surrounding spaces. The irrigating substance, typically a sterile solution, is introduced through the device to gently flush out debris, blood, or other contaminants that may be present. The goal of this procedure is to promote healing, reduce the risk of infection, and alleviate pressure on the brain. By using a percutaneous approach, the risk of complications and trauma to the patient is minimized, allowing for a faster recovery.
