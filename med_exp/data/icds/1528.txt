Destruction of Splenic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using image guidance to access the splenic vein through the skin, avoiding the need for open surgery. A small catheter is inserted through a tiny incision, and specialized instruments are used to destroy or occlude the splenic vein. This is typically done to treat conditions such as portal hypertension or splenic vein thrombosis. The procedure is usually performed in a radiology suite under local anesthesia and conscious sedation, and patients often experience minimal discomfort and a quick recovery.
