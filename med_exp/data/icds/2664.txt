Destruction of Rectum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This surgical procedure involves the destruction of the rectum through an endoscopic approach, where a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through a natural or artificial opening in the body. The endoscope allows the surgeon to visualize the rectum and perform the destruction of the tissue using specialized instruments. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and pain. The destruction of the rectum is usually necessary to treat certain medical conditions such as cancer, diverticulitis, or trauma to the rectal area.
