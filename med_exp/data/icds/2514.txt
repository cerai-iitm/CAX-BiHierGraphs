Excision of Pharynx, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the pharynx and remove tissue or lesions through the mouth or nose, allowing for a minimally invasive and diagnostic excision. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, providing a clear view of the pharynx and enabling the surgeon to identify and remove the targeted tissue. The excision is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The removed tissue is then sent to a pathology laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
