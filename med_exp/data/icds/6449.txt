Reposition Urethra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the urethra, a tube that carries urine out of the body, with the goal of repositioning it to its normal anatomical location. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the urethra directly and make any necessary adjustments to restore its proper alignment. This procedure may be necessary to correct congenital abnormalities, injuries, or other conditions that affect the urethra's function or position. By repositioning the urethra, the surgeon aims to improve urinary function, alleviate symptoms, and enhance overall quality of life.
