Removal of Totally Implantable Vascular Access Device from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the trunk area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, allowing the removal of a totally implantable vascular access device. The device is carefully extracted from its implanted site, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize patient discomfort. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the wound is dressed to promote healing.
