Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Fibula, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically removing an internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, that was previously implanted to stabilize a fracture in the right fibula. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the device, allowing the surgeon to visualize and carefully remove the implant. The goal of the procedure is to relieve discomfort, improve mobility, and restore normal anatomy, as the fixation device is no longer necessary to support the bone. Following removal, the incision is typically closed, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
