Restriction of Abdominal Aorta with Branched or Fenestrated Intraluminal Device, Three or More Arteries, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a branched or fenestrated intraluminal device to restrict blood flow in the abdominal aorta, specifically treating three or more arterial branches. The device is designed to accommodate the complex anatomy of the aorta and its branches, allowing for precise placement and optimal blood flow regulation. Through a small incision, the device is guided to the target site using imaging guidance, and once in place, it is expanded to cover the affected area. This treatment option can be particularly beneficial for patients with complex aortic anatomies or those at high risk for open surgery.
