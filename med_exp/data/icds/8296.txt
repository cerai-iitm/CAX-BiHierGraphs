Beam Radiation of Rectum using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This procedure involves the use of high-energy photon beams to deliver targeted radiation therapy to the rectum. The photon energy ranges from 1 to 10 million electron volts (MeV), allowing for precise control over the radiation dose and penetration depth. The beam is carefully directed to the rectum to minimize exposure to surrounding healthy tissues and organs. This non-invasive treatment is often used to manage rectal cancers and other conditions, and can be administered in multiple fractions or sessions over a specified period of time.
