Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Left Hip Joint, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the revision of an artificial synthetic substitute previously implanted in the left hip joint. The revision is performed through an external approach, which involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the hip joint. The synthetic substitute is removed and replaced with a new one, ensuring a proper fit and alignment to restore normal hip function. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, improve mobility, and enhance overall quality of life for the patient.
