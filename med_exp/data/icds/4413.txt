Excision of Right Lower Arm and Wrist Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right lower arm and wrist to excise, or remove, damaged or diseased muscle tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the targeted muscle tissue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional nerve block, and the incision is usually closed with sutures or staples once the muscle tissue has been removed. After the procedure, the arm and wrist are immobilized to allow for proper healing and to minimize the risk of complications.
