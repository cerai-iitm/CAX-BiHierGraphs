Excision of Radial Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the radial nerve, a major nerve in the arm responsible for controlling extension of the wrist and fingers. The nerve is then carefully excised, either partially or entirely, depending on the severity of the condition being treated. This open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the nerve and surrounding tissue, ensuring precise removal of the affected portion. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as numbness, tingling, or weakness in the hand and wrist, often caused by nerve damage or compression.
