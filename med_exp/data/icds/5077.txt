Reposition Left Humeral Head, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left humeral head using an external approach, which requires an incision to access the affected area. The goal is to restore normal anatomical alignment and relieve symptoms such as pain, limited mobility, and weakness. During the procedure, the surgeon gently manipulates the humeral head back into its correct position, often using specialized instruments and imaging guidance to ensure accurate placement. Following successful repositioning, the incision is closed, and the patient typically undergoes a period of rehabilitation to restore strength and range of motion in the affected shoulder.
