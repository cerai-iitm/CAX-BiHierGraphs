Extirpation of Matter from Right Internal Carotid Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through the skin and guiding it to the right internal carotid artery to remove undesirable matter. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the interventional radiologist navigates the catheter to the target site, suctioning or capturing the unwanted material to restore blood flow. The percutaneous approach reduces recovery time and minimizes scarring, allowing patients to return to their normal activities sooner. The goal of this procedure is to prevent stroke and other complications associated with occluded or blocked carotid arteries.
