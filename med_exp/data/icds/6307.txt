Drainage of Left Kidney Pelvis with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through a natural or artificial opening to drain excess fluid or urine from the left kidney pelvis, a funnel-shaped structure that collects urine from the kidney. The goal of the procedure is to relieve obstruction or blockage in the urinary tract, which can be caused by various factors such as kidney stones, tumors, or congenital abnormalities. During the procedure, a specially designed drainage device is carefully placed through the opening, allowing for the efficient drainage of fluids and reducing the risk of complications. This procedure can help alleviate symptoms such as severe pain, nausea, and vomiting, and improve kidney function by restoring normal urine flow.
