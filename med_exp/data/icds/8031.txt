Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Vertebral Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This diagnostic procedure employs fluoroscopic guidance to visualize the bilateral vertebral arteries, utilizing low osmolar contrast medium to enhance visibility. The contrast agent is injected through a catheter inserted into an artery, allowing for real-time imaging of the arterial structure and blood flow. The low osmolar contrast medium reduces the risk of adverse reactions and is gentler on the body compared to high osmolar contrast agents. The resulting fluoroscopic images provide valuable information to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of vascular conditions affecting the vertebral arteries.
