Extirpation of Matter from Right Femoral Artery, Bifurcation, Open Approach
During this open surgery, the healthcare provider makes an incision in the groin area to access the right femoral artery at its bifurcation point. The goal of the procedure is to remove unwanted matter, such as plaque, blood clots, or other substances, from the arterial wall or lumen. The surgeon carefully extracts the debris to restore blood flow and prevent potential complications like ischemia or limb loss. Following the extirpation, the artery is closed, and the incision site is repaired to promote healing.
