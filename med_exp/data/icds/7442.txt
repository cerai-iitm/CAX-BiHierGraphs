Extraction of Products of Conception, High, Open Approach
This obstetric surgical procedure involves the removal of a fetus or embryo from the uterus through an open incision in the abdomen, typically performed when a pregnancy is no longer viable or poses a risk to the mother's health. The high approach method involves making an incision in the upper abdomen, usually just below the ribcage, to access the uterus. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health. Following the procedure, the patient will require postoperative care to manage pain,prevent infection, and monitor for any potential complications.
