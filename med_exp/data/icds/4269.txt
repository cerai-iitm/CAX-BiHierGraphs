Replacement of Right Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the right lower arm with a graft of the patient's own tissue, harvested from another area of the body. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to remove the damaged tissue and fascia and replace it with the autologous tissue substitute. The graft is carefully secured in place to promote healing and restore normal function to the arm. Post-operative care typically involves wound management, pain management, and physical therapy to ensure a smooth recovery and optimal outcomes.
