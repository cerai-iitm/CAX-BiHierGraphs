Dilation of Right Ureter with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a thin scope and a specialized device to widen the right ureter, a narrow tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder, through a small incision in the skin. The device is inserted through the percutaneous approach, eliminating the need for open surgery, and allows for precise dilation of the ureter to restore normal urine flow. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as ureteral stricture or blockage, and can help alleviate symptoms like flank pain, nausea, and vomiting. The dilation of the ureter with an intraluminal device can also help prevent complications like kidney damage or recurrent urinary tract infections.
