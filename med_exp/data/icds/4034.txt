Excision of Right Neck Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right neck area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The damaged or diseased tissue and fascia are then carefully excised, or removed, to address issues such as infection, lesion, or tumor. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and carefully dissect the tissue to ensure complete removal of the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort.
