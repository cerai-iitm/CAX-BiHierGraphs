Drainage of Abdominal Wall, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Percutaneous abdominal wall drainage is a minimally invasive procedure used to drain accumulated fluid, pus, or blood from the abdominal wall. The procedure involves inserting a small tube or catheter through the skin and into the affected area, guided by imaging studies such as ultrasound or CT scan. The fluid is then drained out of the body, and the tube may be left in place for a few days to ensure complete drainage and promote healing. This diagnostic procedure helps to identify the cause of the accumulation, relieve symptoms, and guide further treatment.
