Packing of Face using Packing Material
This procedure involves the application of packing material to the facial area to control bleeding, promote healing, or maintain tissue stability. The packing material, which can be gauze, tampon, or other absorbent materials, is carefully placed into the facial cavity or wound to achieve the desired effect. The packing material may need to be changed regularly to ensure its effectiveness and to prevent infection. The goal of this treatment is to facilitate a faster and more successful recovery, while also reducing the risk of complications in the affected area.
