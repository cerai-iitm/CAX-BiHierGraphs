Supplement Mitral Valve with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the mitral valve with autologous tissue substitute, where a cut is made in the chest to access the heart. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's own body and is used to repair or replace the defective mitral valve. The surgeon then attaches the substitute to the valve, ensuring proper blood flow and function. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal mitral valve function, alleviating symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and heart palpitations.
