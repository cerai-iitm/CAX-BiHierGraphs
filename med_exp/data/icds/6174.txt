Reposition Left Hip Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon uses an open approach to access the left hip joint, which has become dislocated or misaligned. The goal is to reposition the joint to its normal anatomical position, and internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods are used to stabilize the joint and promote healing. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve making incisions in the skin and underlying tissues to access the joint. Once the joint is repositioned and stabilized, the incisions are closed, and the patient is monitored for recovery.
