Extirpation of Matter from Left Upper Leg Muscle, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left upper leg to access the muscle tissue. The surgeon then manually removes foreign matter, such as debris, infected tissue, or other substances, from the affected muscle. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to carefully extract the unwanted material. Once the procedure is complete, the incision is closed, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
