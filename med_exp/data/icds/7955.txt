Ultrasonography of Single Coronary Artery, Intravascular
This non-invasive diagnostic procedure utilizes high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of a single coronary artery from within the blood vessel. Intravascular ultrasound technology is advanced through a catheter to the target site, where it emits sound waves that bounce off the arterial walls, generating precise images of the vessel's inner luminal surface. The resulting images provide valuable information on artery diameter, plaque buildup, and blockages, enabling cardiologists to diagnose and monitor coronary artery disease. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces risks and promotes rapid recovery.
