Resection of Left Parotid Duct, Open Approach
Surgery is performed to remove a portion of the left parotid duct, a tube that carries saliva from the parotid gland to the mouth. An open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made in the skin and soft tissue to access the duct. The affected section of the duct is carefully dissected and removed, and the remaining duct is repaired or reattached to maintain salivary function. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as ductal obstruction, infection, or tumor, and to restore normal salivary flow and alleviate symptoms.
