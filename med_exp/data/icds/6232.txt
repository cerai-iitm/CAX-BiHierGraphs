Bypass Right Kidney Pelvis to Cutaneous with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves creating a bypass between the right kidney pelvis and the skin using a synthetic substitute, thereby diverting urinary flow. The procedure is performed percutaneously, meaning through the skin, using imaging guidance to precisely place the synthetic substitute. This bypass allows urine to drain directly from the kidney to the skin, bypassing any blockages or obstructions in the urinary tract. The goal of this procedure is to relieve urinary retention and alleviate associated symptoms such as pain, infection, and kidney damage.
