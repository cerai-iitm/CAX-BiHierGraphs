Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Lower Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical removal of a synthetic substitute, such as a vascular graft or stent, from an artery in the lower extremities through an open incision. The surgeon makes an incision in the affected area to access the artery and carefully removes the synthetic substitute, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for recovery. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or tingling in the affected leg.
