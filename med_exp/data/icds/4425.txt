Excision of Left Thorax Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left thoracic region to access the underlying muscle tissue. The affected muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, using specialized surgical instruments and techniques. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the muscle and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the target muscle. The procedure may be performed to treat a range of conditions, including muscle tumors, nerve compression, or traumatic injuries.
