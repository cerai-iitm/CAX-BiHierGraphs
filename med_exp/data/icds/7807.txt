Monitoring of Central Nervous Electrical Activity, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the use of electrodes inserted through the skin to measure and record the electrical activity of the central nervous system, providing healthcare providers with valuable insights into brain function and neural activity. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. During the procedure, the electrodes are carefully placed to capture accurate readings of electrical impulses, which are then analyzed to diagnose and monitor a range of neurological conditions. The resulting data can help physicians identify abnormalities, track disease progression, and develop targeted treatment plans.
