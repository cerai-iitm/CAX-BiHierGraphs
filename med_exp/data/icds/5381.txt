Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Metatarsal, Open Approach
During this procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left foot to access the metatarsal bone, allowing for the removal of an internal fixation device that was previously implanted to support the bone during the healing process. The device, which may include plates, screws, or rods, is carefully extracted from the bone to prevent any damage or tissue irritation. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately remove the internal fixation device and minimize the risk of complications.
