Extirpation of Matter from Left Mandible, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves accessing the left mandible, or lower jawbone, through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to expose the bone. The goal of the procedure is to remove unwanted material, such as infected tissue, foreign bodies, or tumor cells, from the affected area. The extirpation process is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of specialized instruments and imaging technology to ensure precise removal of the targeted material. Once the procedure is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for signs of complications or infection.
