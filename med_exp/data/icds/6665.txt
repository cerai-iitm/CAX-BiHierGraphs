Resection of Right Ovary, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a thin, lighted tube called an endoscope through a small incision in the skin to visualize the internal abdominal cavity. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully remove the right ovary, typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced scarring, less post-operative pain, and a faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery. The entire procedure is usually performed on an outpatient basis, enabling the patient to return home the same day.
