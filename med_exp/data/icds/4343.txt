Transfer Left Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Skin, Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of skin, subcutaneous tissue, and fascia from the left lower leg to another area of the body, using an open approach where the skin is cut open to access the underlying tissues. The subcutaneous tissue and fascia are carefully dissected and mobilized to preserve blood supply and prevent damage. The skin and underlying tissues are then transferred to the recipient site, where they are secured in place using sutures or staples. The goal of this procedure is to repair or reconstruct damaged or defective tissues, promoting healing and restoring function to the affected area.
