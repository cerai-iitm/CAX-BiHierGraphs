Destruction of Right Pulmonary Vein, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical destruction of the right pulmonary vein through an open approach, which requires a thoracotomy or median sternotomy incision to access the pulmonary vein. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as pulmonary vein stenosis or varices, which can cause symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and coughing. During the procedure, the surgeon may use various techniques such as sclerotherapy, thermal ablation, or surgical resection to destroy the affected vein. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms and improve lung function by redirecting blood flow through healthier veins.
