Dilation of Left External Iliac Artery with Two Intraluminal Devices, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This procedure involves surgically opening the left external iliac artery to perform dilation, which is the widening of the artery to improve blood flow. Two intraluminal devices are inserted into the artery during the open approach procedure to help maintain the widened diameter. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the artery, allowing the healthcare provider to directly visualize the area. The goal of this procedure is to improve blood flow and reduce symptoms related to narrowed or blocked arteries.
