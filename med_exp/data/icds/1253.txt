Occlusion of Left Uterine Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a specialized device through the skin to occlude, or block, the left uterine artery, a major blood vessel that supplies the uterus. The device is carefully guided through the artery using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure precise placement. Once in position, the device is deployed to block blood flow to the uterus, which can help to reduce or eliminate bleeding in cases of uterine fibroids or other vascular abnormalities. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and promoting faster healing.
