Repair Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the mouth or a small incision to visualize the stomach. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to repair defects or injuries in the stomach lining or wall, which may be causing bleeding, perforation, or other complications. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of clips, sutures, or other devices to close the defect. The goal of the procedure is to restore the integrity of the stomach and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
