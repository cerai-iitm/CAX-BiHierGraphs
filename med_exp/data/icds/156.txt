Release Thoracic Spinal Cord, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the thoracic spine to relieve compression or pressure on the spinal cord, often caused by injury, herniated discs, or spinal stenosis. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and carefully release the cord from surrounding tissues or bone fragments. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal spinal cord function, alleviate symptoms such as numbness, weakness, or pain, and improve mobility. By decompressing the thoracic spinal cord, this procedure can significantly improve the patient's quality of life.
