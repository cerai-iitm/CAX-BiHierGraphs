Removal of External Fixation Device from Left Ankle Joint, External Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

The removal of an external fixation device from the left ankle joint is a surgical procedure that involves the careful extraction of an external device that was previously implanted to stabilize and treat a fracture or other condition in the ankle joint. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the external fixator, which is then carefully removed to restore normal function and mobility to the ankle joint. The procedure is typically performed under local or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort, and the patient may be advised to elevate the ankle and apply ice to reduce swelling after the procedure. After removal, the wound is closed, and the patient is advised to follow a rehabilitation program to regain strength and mobility in the ankle joint.
