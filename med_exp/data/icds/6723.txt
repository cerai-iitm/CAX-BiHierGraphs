Drainage of Scrotum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves making a surgical incision in the scrotum to drain accumulated fluid or pus, allowing for the removal of abnormal substances and promoting healing. The open approach enables direct visualization of the affected area, allowing the surgeon to accurately identify and address the underlying issue. The diagnostic nature of this procedure enables physicians to collect samples for laboratory analysis, helping to determine the underlying cause of the scrotal abnormality. Following the procedure, the incision site is closed, and the scrotum is cleaned and dressed to facilitate recovery.
