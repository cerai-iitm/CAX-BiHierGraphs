Removal of Feeding Device from Stomach, External Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a feeding device, also known as a gastrostomy tube, from the stomach through an external approach. The external approach typically involves a surgical incision made in the abdomen to access the stomach and remove the device. The procedure may be performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the individual case, and is often done on an outpatient basis. The removal of the feeding device is usually done when it is no longer needed, such as when the patient can eat and digest food normally again, or when the device is causing complications.
