Fusion of 2 or more Cervical Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more cervical vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device, which is inserted through a posterior approach. The procedure is performed through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to access the posterior column of the spine. The goal of the fusion is to stabilize the cervical spine and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the neck and arms. The interbody fusion device helps to promote bone growth and fusion between the vertebrae, providing long-term relief and support.
