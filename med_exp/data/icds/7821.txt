Monitoring of Arterial Pressure, Pulmonary, Percutaneous Approach
This invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through the skin and into a pulmonary artery to continuously monitor arterial pressure. The pulmonary artery catheter allows for real-time measurement of blood pressure, cardiac output, and other hemodynamic parameters. The percutaneous approach enables minimally invasive insertion of the catheter, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The monitored data helps healthcare providers make informed decisions about patient care, particularly in critically ill patients or those undergoing complex surgical procedures.
