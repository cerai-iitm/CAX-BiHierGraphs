Dilation of Stomach, Pylorus with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the mouth or a surgical opening to reach the stomach and pylorus, allowing the physician to visualize the area. A specialized intraluminal device is then guided through the endoscope to dilate, or widen, the pylorus, which is the narrow passage that connects the stomach to the small intestine. This widening helps to relieve blockages or narrowings that can cause digestive issues, such as difficulty swallowing or vomiting. The entire process is performed under sedation to ensure the patient's comfort and safety.
