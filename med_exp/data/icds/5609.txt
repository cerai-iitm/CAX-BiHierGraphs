Drainage of Left Metacarpophalangeal Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
During this procedure, the healthcare provider uses a percutaneous approach to drain the left metacarpophalangeal joint, a connection between the hand bones. The goal is to diagnose any underlying conditions causing joint pain, swelling, or infection. A small incision is made in the skin, and a drainage tube or needle is inserted through the skin and into the joint space to remove fluid or other material. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort, and imaging guidance such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy may be used to ensure accurate placement of the drainage tube.
