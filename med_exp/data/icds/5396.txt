Reposition Lumbar Vertebra, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of imaging guidance to reposition a misplaced or misaligned lumbar vertebra through a small incision in the skin. The percutaneous approach allows for precise manipulation of the vertebrae with minimal disruption to surrounding tissues and muscles. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure on the surrounding nerves and spinal cord, alleviating symptoms such as back pain, numbness, and tingling in the legs. By restoring the natural alignment of the spine, this procedure can help improve mobility, reduce discomfort, and enhance overall quality of life.
