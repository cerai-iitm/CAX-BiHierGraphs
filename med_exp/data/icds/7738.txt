Introduction of Destructive Agent into Peripheral Nerves and Plexi, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a destructive agent, such as a neurolytic substance, into peripheral nerves or plexi, with the goal of intentionally damaging or destroying targeted nerve tissue. The agent is introduced through the skin, using imaging guidance, to accurately target the affected nerve or plexus. The procedure is typically performed to relieve chronic pain, spasticity, or other symptoms related to nerve dysfunction. The destructive agent works by interrupting or blocking pain signals, reducing muscle spasms, and promoting relaxation.
