Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the lumbar vertebral joint using autologous tissue substitute, which is taken from the patient's own body, to stabilize and strengthen the spine. The anterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the lumbar spine through an incision in the abdomen, and the anterior column is the specific area of focus. The open approach indicates that a larger incision is made to allow for direct visualization of the spine, enabling the surgeon to meticulously place the autologous tissue substitute to achieve a successful fusion. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as spinal instability, spondylolisthesis, or degenerative disc disease.
