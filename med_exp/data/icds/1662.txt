Removal of Infusion Device from Lower Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of an infusion device, such as a central venous catheter or implantable port, from a lower extremity vein, typically under image guidance. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and involves making a small incision in the skin to access the vein. The infusion device is then carefully withdrawn from the vein, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues. Following removal, the incision site is closed and the area is cleaned and dressed to minimize the risk of infection.
