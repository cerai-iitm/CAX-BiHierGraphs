Drainage of Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a needle or catheter into the lumbar vertebral joint to drain fluid or other substances. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the needle. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose the cause of back pain or other symptoms, and may involve the collection of a sample of fluid or tissue for further analysis. The diagnostic drainage procedure is usually done on an outpatient basis and can provide valuable information to guide further treatment.
