Robotic Assisted Procedure of Trunk Region, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure utilizes a robotic system to assist in the visualization and navigation of the operative site, allowing for enhanced dexterity and precision during the procedure. Through a small incision, an endoscope is inserted into the trunk region, providing a clear view of the internal structures, while the robotic system allows for precise dissection and manipulation of tissue. The percutaneous approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissue, resulting in less pain and scarring for the patient. This advanced technique enables surgeons to perform complex procedures with increased accuracy and control, leading to improved patient outcomes.
