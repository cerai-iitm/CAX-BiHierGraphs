Repair Median Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the median nerve, which runs from the forearm into the hand, to repair any damage or injury. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the nerve and surrounding tissue to identify the source of the problem. The repair may involve reconnecting severed nerve fibers, removing scar tissue or other obstacles, and repositioning the nerve to its natural alignment. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal nerve function, relieve pain and numbness, and improve motor skills in the hand and fingers.
