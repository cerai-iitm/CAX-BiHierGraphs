Extirpation of Matter from Lower Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the lower artery to remove unwanted matter, such as blood clots, plaque, or other debris, that may be obstructing blood flow or causing harm. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and remove the unwanted material. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the legs. After the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
