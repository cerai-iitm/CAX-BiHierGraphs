Resection of Tonsils, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of tonsils through an external approach, where an incision is made in the skin and underlying tissues to access the tonsillar area. The surgeon then carefully disconnects the tonsils from surrounding tissues and blood vessels before completely removing them from the throat. The external approach allows for direct visualization of the tonsils and surrounding anatomy, facilitating a more precise and controlled removal. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for a short period to ensure a smooth recovery.
