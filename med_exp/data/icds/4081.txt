Excision of Right Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right foot to access the underlying subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which is the layer of tissue that surrounds and supports the foot's muscles and other structures. The surgeon then carefully excises, or removes, the affected subcutaneous tissue and fascia to obtain a diagnostic sample, allowing for further examination and identification of any underlying conditions or abnormalities. During the procedure, the surgeon may use various instruments, such as scalpels and forceps, to carefully dissect and remove the targeted tissue. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to provide a precise diagnosis and inform subsequent treatment plans.
