Excision of Left Tibia, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This percutaneous procedure involves the removal of a portion of the left tibia through a small incision in the skin, typically performed under image guidance such as fluoroscopy or CT scan. The diagnostic excision aims to obtain a tissue sample for further examination and diagnosis of any underlying conditions or diseases affecting the left tibia. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgical procedures. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for histopathological examination to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of the underlying condition.
