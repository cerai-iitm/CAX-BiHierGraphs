Excision of Right Pleura, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the percutaneous endoscopic excision of the right pleura, a delicate membrane surrounding the lungs, to diagnose potential underlying conditions or abnormalities. The endoscope is inserted through a small incision in the chest wall, allowing visualization of the pleural cavity and precise removal of the targeted area. The percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to surrounding tissues and reduces the risk of complications. The excised tissue is then sent for further examination to aid in establishing a definitive diagnosis.
