Reposition Right Radius with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of a fractured or dislocated right radius bone in the forearm to its normal anatomical position. The procedure is performed through an open approach, where the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are then used to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper alignment and functionality to the affected arm, eliminating pain and improving range of motion.
