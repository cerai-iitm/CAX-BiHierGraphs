Removal of Drainage Device from Hepatobiliary Duct, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of a drainage device from the hepatobiliary duct, a tube-like structure that carries bile from the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine. The drainage device, typically a catheter or stent, is inserted through the skin to drain excess bile or debris that can cause jaundice, pain, or infection. Under imaging guidance, the healthcare provider accesses the drainage device through a small incision and carefully removes it from the hepatobiliary duct. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal bile flow and alleviate symptoms, which may involve follow-up care to monitor liver function and bile drainage.
