Reposition Right Metatarsal, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning the right metatarsal bone, which is located in the midfoot, to correct deformities or malalignments. The operation is performed through an external approach, where an incision is made in the skin to access the affected bone. The surgeon will then manipulate the bone into its proper position, and may use internal fixation devices such as screws or plates to hold it in place. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal alignment and function to the foot, alleviating pain and discomfort.
