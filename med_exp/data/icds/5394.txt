Reposition Lumbar Vertebra with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of one or more lumbar vertebrae to their normal anatomical position, typically to treat conditions such as spondylolisthesis, scoliosis, or spinal fractures. The open approach requires a incision in the lower back to access the affected vertebrae, and the surgeon then uses specialized instruments to realign and stabilize the vertebrae. An internal fixation device, such as rods, screws, or cages, is implanted to hold the vertebrae in place and promote fusion. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, restore spinal stability, and improve mobility and function.
