Dilation of Coronary Artery, Three Arteries, Bifurcation, with Three Drug-eluting Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a percutaneous approach to dilate three coronary arteries that have bifurcated, or split, into narrower branches. To restore blood flow, three drug-eluting intraluminal devices, also known as stents, are implanted in the affected arteries. The stents are designed to slowly release medication over time to prevent the arteries from narrowing again. The procedure is typically performed through an artery in the leg or arm, using imaging guidance and specialized instruments to navigate the coronary arteries.
