Supplement Tricuspid Valve with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the tricuspid valve with a synthetic substitute using an open approach, which requires a thoracotomy or median sternotomy incision to access the heart. The synthetic substitute is designed to augment the natural valve, enhancing blood flow and reducing symptoms associated with tricuspid valve dysfunction. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully attaches the synthetic substitute to the native valve, ensuring proper alignment and functionality. The goal of this procedure is to improve cardiovascular function and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and swelling.
