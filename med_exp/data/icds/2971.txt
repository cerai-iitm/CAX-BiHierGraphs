Extraction of Ascending Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the inside of the ascending colon through a natural or artificial opening. The procedure is performed for diagnostic purposes, allowing the physician to examine the colon lining and identify any abnormalities or diseases. During the procedure, the endoscope is inserted through the mouth or anus, or through an artificial opening in the abdominal wall, and guided to the ascending colon. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and minimize discomfort.
