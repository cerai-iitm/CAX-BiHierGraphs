Supplement Pericardium with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the pericardium, a sac that surrounds the heart, with zooplastic tissue, a type of tissue harvested from animals, through an open approach. The goal of this procedure is to reinforce the pericardium, which can be weakened by injury or disease, to prevent further complications. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the pericardium and then attaches the zooplastic tissue to the affected area. The supplemented tissue provides additional support and stability to the pericardium, promoting healthy heart function.
