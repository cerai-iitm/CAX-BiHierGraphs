Excision of Ventricular Septum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and excise a portion of the ventricular septum, the wall of tissue that separates the heart's lower chambers. The procedure is typically performed to treat defects or disorders affecting the heart's structure or function, such as ventricular septal defects. The surgeon carefully dissects the abnormal tissue, taking care to preserve surrounding healthy tissue and minimize damage to the heart muscle. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow and function to the heart, improving overall cardiac health.
