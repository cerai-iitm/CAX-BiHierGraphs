Fluoroscopy of Right External Carotid Artery using Other Contrast
Here is the detailed description:

Fluoroscopy is a real-time X-ray imaging technique used to guide interventional procedures. In this case, it is used to visualize the right external carotid artery, which is a vital blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the head and neck. A contrast agent, other than the standard iodinated or gadolinium-based agents, is injected through a catheter to enhance the visibility of the artery and its branches. The resulting high-resolution images allow the physician to assess the patency and function of the right external carotid artery, facilitating diagnosis and treatment of related disorders or conditions.
