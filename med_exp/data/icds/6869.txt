Drainage of Right Pleural Cavity with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to access the right pleural cavity, which is the space between the lungs and chest wall, to drain accumulated fluid or air. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the cavity and remove the fluid or air using a drainage device, such as a chest tube, which is inserted through the incision. The device is designed to remove the accumulated substance and promote drainage, helping to relieve symptoms such as shortness of breath and chest pain. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal lung function and alleviate respiratory distress.
