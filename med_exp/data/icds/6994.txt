Extirpation of Matter from Pelvic Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the pelvic area to access the pelvic cavity, allowing for the removal of unwanted substances or materials, such as excess tissue, foreign bodies, or infectious materials, from the pelvic cavity. The open approach requires a larger incision to provide direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully extract the unwanted matter. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and promote healing in the pelvic region. The surgeon may use specialized instruments and techniques to minimize tissue damage and promote optimal outcomes.
