Extirpation of Matter from Intracranial Subarachnoid Space, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skull to access the intracranial subarachnoid space, a region surrounding the brain and spinal cord filled with cerebrospinal fluid. The goal is to remove abnormal or foreign matter that has accumulated in this sensitive area, which can cause damage to the brain and central nervous system. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully extracts the unwanted material, taking care to minimize trauma to the delicate brain tissue and surrounding blood vessels. By removing the offending substance, the procedure aims to alleviate symptoms and prevent further complications.
