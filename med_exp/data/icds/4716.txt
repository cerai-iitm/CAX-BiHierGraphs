Reposition Right Foot Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right foot to access and reposition a tendon, correcting its alignment or placement to alleviate symptoms such as pain, weakness, or limited mobility. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling precise repositioning and any necessary repairs. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal foot function, alleviate discomfort, and improve overall mobility and balance. Following the procedure, the patient may require a period of rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and regain strength in the affected foot.
