Destruction of Uterus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the uterus through an open approach, where a large incision is made in the abdomen to access the pelvis and reproductive organs. The uterus is then carefully dissected and separated from surrounding tissues and blood vessels, and finally, it is removed from the body. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as uterine cancer, fibroids, or severe bleeding disorders that cannot be managed with other treatments. The open approach allows for direct visualization and more complex dissection, but it typically requires a longer recovery period compared to minimally invasive techniques.
