Supplement Right Middle Lobe Bronchus with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the right middle lobe bronchus with an autologous tissue substitute, which is taken from the patient's own body, to repair or replace damaged or diseased tissue. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area, allowing for direct visualization and manipulation of the tissues. The tissue substitute is then carefully placed in the right middle lobe bronchus to restore normal function and promote healing. This complex procedure requires great skill and precision to avoid complications and ensure a successful outcome.
