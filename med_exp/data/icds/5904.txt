Excision of Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the left knee joint is carefully exposed to allow for the removal of the affected joint tissue or bone. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the knee joint, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding nerves, vessels, and muscles. The excision of the left knee joint involves the complete removal of the affected joint, which may be necessary due to severe trauma, infection, or degenerative conditions such as osteoarthritis. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain, restore mobility, and improve overall function of the knee joint.
