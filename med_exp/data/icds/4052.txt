Excision of Pelvic Region Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the pelvic region to access and remove damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The subcutaneous tissue is the layer of tissue beneath the skin, and the fascia is a type of connective tissue that surrounds and supports various body structures. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the tissue and fascia to identify and excise the affected areas, taking care to preserve surrounding healthy tissue and minimize bleeding. The goal of the procedure is to remove abnormal or infected tissue, promote healing, and restore normal anatomy and function to the pelvic region.
