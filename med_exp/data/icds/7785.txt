Measurement of Cardiac Sampling and Pressure, Bilateral, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of catheters through the skin into blood vessels on both sides of the body to measure cardiac sampling and pressure. The catheters are guided through the blood vessels to reach the heart, where they collect blood samples and record pressure readings. This allows clinicians to assess cardiac function, diagnose cardiovascular diseases, and monitor treatment efficacy. The percutaneous approach ensures minimal invasiveness and reduces the risk of complications.
