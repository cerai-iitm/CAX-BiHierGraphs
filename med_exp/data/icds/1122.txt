Dilation of Left Posterior Tibial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves accessing the left posterior tibial artery through the skin to perform dilation of the vessel. The goal is to improve blood flow and relieve any blockages or stenosis that may be present. A balloon catheter is typically inserted through a small incision and guided to the affected area where it is then inflated to widen the artery. The procedure is usually performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement and minimize potential complications.
