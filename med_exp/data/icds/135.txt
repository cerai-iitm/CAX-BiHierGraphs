Insertion of Infusion Device into Spinal Canal, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the spinal canal, allowing for the direct visualization of the spinal cord and surrounding structures. During the operation, a small opening is created in the dura mater, a protective membrane that covers the spinal cord, to introduce an infusion device into the spinal canal. The infusion device is then carefully placed and secured in the desired position, allowing for the targeted delivery of medications or other therapeutic agents directly into the spinal cord. Once the device is in place, the incision is closed, and the site is monitored for any signs of complications.
