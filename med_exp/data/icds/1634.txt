Occlusion of Esophageal Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through the skin to block the flow of blood in an esophageal vein. The device is guided through a small incision and advanced to the target vein under imaging guidance. Once in place, the device expands to occlude the vein, preventing further blood flow. This minimally invasive treatment is often used to manage bleeding or other complications related to esophageal varices.
