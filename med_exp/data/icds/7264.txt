Detachment at Left Lower Leg, High, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of a body part or device from the left lower leg, specifically in the high region. The open approach is utilized, which requires a larger incision to access the area, allowing for direct visualization and manipulation of the tissues. The detachment is performed to address a specific condition or injury, such as trauma, infection, or tumor removal. The procedure may require additional interventions, such as debridement, reconstruction, or implantation of a new device, depending on the underlying indication.
