Drainage of Brain with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to remove excess cerebrospinal fluid, blood, or other liquids from the brain. The percutaneous approach allows the doctor to access the brain through a small incision in the scalp, minimizing tissue damage and scarring. The drainage device is carefully guided into place using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement and maximize the effectiveness of the procedure. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort and promote patient safety.
