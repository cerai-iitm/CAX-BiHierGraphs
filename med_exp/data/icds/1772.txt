Excision of Left Inguinal Lymphatic, Open Approach, Diagnostic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this surgical procedure, the healthcare provider makes an incision in the left inguinal area to access the lymph nodes. The affected lymph node or nodes are carefully identified and dissected free from surrounding tissue. The lymph nodes are then removed and sent to a laboratory for examination to aid in diagnosis. This open approach allows for direct visualization and precise removal of the lymph nodes, enabling accurate diagnosis and potential treatment of underlying conditions.
