Fusion of 2 to 7 Thoracic Vertebral Joints with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two to seven thoracic vertebral joints using an autologous tissue substitute, which is a biological material harvested from the patient's own body. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, accessing the spine from the back, and involves an open approach, meaning a larger incision is made to allow for greater visibility and access to the affected area. The anterior column, which is the front portion of the spine, is the specific area targeted for fusion. This complex surgery aims to stabilize and strengthen the thoracic spine, alleviating painful and debilitating conditions such as degenerative disc disease, spinal deformities, or fractures.
