Excision of Left External Iliac Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower abdomen to access the left external iliac artery, a major blood vessel located in the pelvic region. The diseased or damaged portion of the artery is then carefully excised, with the remaining healthy sections being repaired or reconstructed to maintain blood flow to the affected limb. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the artery and surrounding tissues, enabling precise removal of the affected segment. The excision of the left external iliac artery is typically performed to treat severe atherosclerosis, aneurysms, or trauma-related injuries that compromise blood flow and pose a risk to the patient's overall health.
