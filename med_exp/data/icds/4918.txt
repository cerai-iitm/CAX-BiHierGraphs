Supplement Skull with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the use of synthetic materials to replace or augment defective or missing bone tissue in the skull. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to meticulously place the synthetic substitute and secure it in position. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural contour and function of the skull, improving overall cranial structure and potentially alleviating related symptoms. The use of synthetic substitutes offers a durable and long-lasting solution, reducing the need for future revisions or corrections.
