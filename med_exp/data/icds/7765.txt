Irrigation of Peritoneal Cavity using Irrigating Substance, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a catheter through the skin to access the peritoneal cavity, a space between the abdominal wall and internal organs. A sterile irrigating substance, such as saline or antibiotics, is then instilled into the cavity to flush out infected or inflamed tissue, debris, or other foreign substances. The goal of this irrigation is to promote healing, reduce inflammation, and prevent further complications. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the catheter and effective irrigation of the affected area.
