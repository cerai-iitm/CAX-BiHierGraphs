Reposition Left Thumb Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of a displaced phalanx bone in the left thumb, restoring its normal anatomical alignment. The open approach requires a skin incision to access the affected bone, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and accurately reposition the phalanx. An internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, is then used to stabilize the bone in its correct position, promoting healing and preventing further displacement. The goal of this procedure is to restore thumb function, alleviate pain, and improve overall hand mobility.
