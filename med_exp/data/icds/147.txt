Release Brain, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the skull to access the brain, allowing the surgeon to carefully release tension or compression on brain tissue or surrounding structures. The goal of this operation is to alleviate pressure that may be causing damage or impeding blood flow, often as a result of traumatic injury, tumor, or hematoma. During the procedure, the surgeon will gently dissect and retract surrounding tissue to visualize the affected area, taking care to minimize damage to sensitive brain structures. By releasing the compression, the surgeon aims to restore normal brain function, alleviate symptoms, and improve patient outcomes.
