Repair Perineum Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the perineum, a region between the genital area and anus, to repair damaged or weakened muscles. The open approach allows for direct visualization and precise repair of the affected muscles, promoting optimal healing and function. The procedure aims to restore the natural support and strength of the perineal muscles, improving overall pelvic floor function and alleviating symptoms such as pain, incontinence, or prolapse. By repairing the perineum muscles, patients can expect to experience improved bladder and bowel control, reduced discomfort, and enhanced overall quality of life.
