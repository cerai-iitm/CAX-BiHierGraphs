Stereotactic Other Photon Radiosurgery of Brain
This non-invasive, outpatient procedure uses precise, high-dose photon beams to target and destroy specific brain tumors or lesions, often in a single session. During the treatment, the patient is fitted with a stereotactic frame or mask to immobilize the head and ensure accurate beam delivery. Computerized imaging and software are used to guide the treatment, ensuring that the high-energy photons are precisely focused on the tumor or lesion, minimizing damage to surrounding healthy brain tissue. The goal of this radiosurgery is to achieve local control of the tumor, alleviate symptoms, and improve quality of life.
