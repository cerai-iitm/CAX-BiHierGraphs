Excision of Right Abdomen Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right abdomen to access the affected muscle tissue. The damaged or diseased muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate symptoms and restore normal function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the muscle and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, improve mobility, and reduce disability in the right abdominal region.
