Inspection of Lower Intestinal Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves a visual examination of the lower intestinal tract, which includes the colon, rectum, and anus, through a natural or artificial opening. A flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, known as an endoscope, may be inserted through the anus to inspect the inside of the colon and rectum. Alternatively, the examination may be performed through an artificial opening, such as a colostomy or ileostomy, which is a surgically created opening in the abdominal wall to divert the flow of stool. The goal of the procedure is to identify any abnormalities, such as polyps, ulcers, or inflammation, and to collect tissue samples for further examination if necessary.
