Respiratory Ventilation, Less than 24 Consecutive Hours
This medical procedure involves the mechanical assistance of breathing using a ventilator for a duration of less than 24 hours, providing temporary respiratory support to patients who require oxygenation and ventilation. The ventilator takes over the work of breathing, delivering a specific volume and pressure of air to the lungs, allowing the patient to rest and recover. This intervention is often necessary for patients who have experienced respiratory failure, cardiac arrest, or undergone surgery, and require short-term ventilation to stabilize their condition. The goal of this procedure is to support the patient's respiratory function until they can breathe independently again.
