Restriction of Intracranial Artery with Intraluminal Device, Flow Diverter, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous deployment of a flow diverter device within an intracranial artery to restrict blood flow and promote healing. The device is inserted through a small incision in the skin and guided through the blood vessels to the affected area. Once in place, the flow diverter redirects blood flow away from the aneurysm or weakened artery wall, reducing the risk of rupture or further damage. This procedure is often performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate device placement.
