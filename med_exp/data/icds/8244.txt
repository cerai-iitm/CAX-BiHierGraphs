Fluoroscopy of Kidneys, Ureters and Bladder using Other Contrast
This imaging procedure utilizes a type of contrasting agent, other than the standard contrast materials, to enhance visibility of the kidneys, ureters, and bladder during fluoroscopy. The contrasting agent is administered to the patient, allowing for real-time visualization of the urinary tract structures under X-ray guidance. The procedure provides valuable diagnostic information on the morphology and function of the kidneys, ureters, and bladder, enabling clinicians to identify any abnormalities or disorders. This diagnostic imaging test plays a crucial role in guiding further treatment and management of genitourinary conditions.
