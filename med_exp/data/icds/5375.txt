Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Fibula, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screws, from the left fibula bone in the lower leg. The open approach requires a skin incision to access the device, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and disengage the fixation device from the bone. The procedure is typically performed under general or regional anesthesia and may require fluoroscopic or X-ray guidance to ensure accurate removal of the device. Post-operatively, the wound is closed, and the patient may require temporary immobilization to promote healing.
