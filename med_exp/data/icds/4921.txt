Supplement Frontal Bone with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the frontal bone, a vital part of the forehead. The procedure aims to repair or replace a damaged or defective frontal bone using a synthetic substitute, such as a prosthetic or implant. The synthetic material is carefully shaped and fitted to match the natural contours of the bone, promoting a natural appearance and function. Once the substitute is in place, the incision is closed, and the site is allowed to heal, restoring the structural integrity and aesthetic appeal of the forehead.
