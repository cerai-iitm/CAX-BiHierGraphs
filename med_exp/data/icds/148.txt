Release Cerebral Meninges, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the skull to access the cerebral meninges, which are the protective membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord. The goal of the procedure is to release any compression, adhesions, or scarring that may be affecting the meninges and causing symptoms such as headaches, seizures, or neurological deficits. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects and separates the meninges from the surrounding tissue to restore their normal function and alleviate pressure on the brain. The open approach allows for direct visualization and precise manipulation of the meninges, enabling the surgeon to achieve optimal results.
