Supplement Right Tibia with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right tibia, also known as the shinbone, to implant a synthetic substitute, typically a prosthetic or artificial material, to replace or supplement damaged or defective bone tissue. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the necessary repairs. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural structure and function of the bone, promoting stability, support, and mobility in the affected leg. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility.
