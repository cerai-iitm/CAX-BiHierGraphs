Drainage of Chest Wall with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to evacuate fluid or air that has accumulated in the chest wall, typically as a result of injury, infection, or malignancy. The percutaneous approach allows the doctor to access the affected area through a small incision, minimizing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. Once the device is in place, it enables the drainage of unwanted substances, relieving pressure and discomfort, and facilitating the healing process. Post-procedure care typically involves monitoring the patient for potential complications and adjusting the drainage device as needed to ensure optimal results.
