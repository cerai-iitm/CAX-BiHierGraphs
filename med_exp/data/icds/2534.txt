Extraction of Lower Tooth, Single, External Approach
This procedure involves the surgical removal of a single tooth located in the lower jawbone, performed through an external approach. The procedure typically involves making an incision in the gum tissue to access the tooth, followed by the use of specialized instruments to gently rock the tooth back and forth, widening the socket and eventually removing the tooth from the jawbone. The tooth is then carefully extracted, and the socket is cleaned and prepared for healing. The external approach is often used when a simple extraction is not possible due to the tooth's position, size, or shape.
