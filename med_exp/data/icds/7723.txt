Introduction of Other Antineoplastic into Spinal Canal, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a needle through the skin and into the spinal canal to deliver anti-cancer medication directly into the cerebrospinal fluid. The medication is administered through a small catheter or needle, allowing it to flow into the spinal fluid and target cancer cells in the central nervous system. This approach is useful for treating certain types of cancer that have spread to the spinal fluid or brain, such as leukemia, lymphoma, or primary central nervous system lymphoma. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgical procedures.
