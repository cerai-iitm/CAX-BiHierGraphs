Reposition Rectum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to reposition the rectum through a percutaneous approach, which means the procedure is performed through the skin. The procedure is typically performed to treat rectal prolapse, a condition where the rectum protrudes from the anus, and can also be used to repair rectal hernias. During the procedure, the endoscope is inserted through a small incision in the skin and guided to the rectal area, where specialized instruments are used to gently reposition the rectum to its normal anatomical position. The procedure offers several benefits, including minimal scarring, reduced post-operative pain, and faster recovery times compared to traditional open surgery.
