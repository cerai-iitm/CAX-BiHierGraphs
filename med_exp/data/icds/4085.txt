Excision of Left Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical excision of the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the left foot through an open approach, where an incision is made in the skin to access the affected area. The subcutaneous tissue and fascia are carefully dissected and removed to treat conditions such as infections, tumors, or traumatic injuries. The open approach allows for direct visualization and precise control during the excision, ensuring the removal of the targeted tissue and fascia. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote optimal outcomes.
