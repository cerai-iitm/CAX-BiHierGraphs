Dilation of Left Femoral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a small tube-like device (intraluminal device) into the left femoral artery through a small incision in the skin (percutaneous approach) to widen the artery and improve blood flow. The device is guided through the artery using imaging techniques such as X-ray or ultrasound to ensure precise placement. Once in place, the device expands to widen the artery, allowing for improved blood flow to the leg. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as peripheral artery disease, which can cause pain, numbness, and weakness in the legs.
