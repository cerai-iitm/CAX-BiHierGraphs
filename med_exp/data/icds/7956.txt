Ultrasonography of Multiple Coronary Arteries, Intravascular
This procedure involves the use of high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of multiple coronary arteries from inside the blood vessels. An intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) probe is inserted through a catheter to capture precise images of the artery walls and plaque buildup, providing valuable information for diagnosing and treating coronary artery disease. The real-time images help cardiologists assess the severity of blockages, identify vulnerable plaques, and guide stent placement or other minimally invasive interventions. The minimally invasive nature of IVUS reduces risks and promotes efficient treatment planning, ultimately improving patient outcomes.
