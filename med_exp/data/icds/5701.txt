Fusion of Thoracic Vertebral Joint, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the thoracic vertebral joint through a posterior approach, which means the surgeon accesses the spine from the back. The goal of the procedure is to stabilize the spine and relieve pressure on the spinal cord or nerves by joining two or more vertebrae together. The posterior column, which includes the pedicles and transverse processes, is the focus of the fusion. The open approach involves making a larger incision to directly visualize the spine and perform the fusion.
