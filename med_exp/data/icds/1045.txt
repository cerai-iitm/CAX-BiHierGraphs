Dilation of Left Internal Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a thin catheter through a small incision in the skin, typically in the groin area, to access the left internal iliac artery. A specialized balloon or stent is then advanced through the catheter to the site of blockage or narrowing, where it is expanded to widen the artery and improve blood flow. The intraluminal device is designed to remain in place after the procedure, providing long-term benefits by keeping the artery open and maintaining perfusion to the surrounding tissues. During the procedure, imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, is used to ensure precise placement of the device and monitor its deployment.
