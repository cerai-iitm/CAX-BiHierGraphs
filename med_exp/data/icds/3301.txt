Dilation of Common Hepatic Duct with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During an endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the mouth or a surgically created opening to access the common hepatic duct. A specialized device is then advanced through the tube to dilate the duct, widening the narrow passageway to improve bile flow from the liver and gallbladder. The dilation procedure helps to relieve blockages, such as those caused by gallstones or tumors, that can lead to jaundice, pain, and infection. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bile flow, alleviate symptoms, and improve the patient's overall quality of life.
