Repair Pelvic Region Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the pelvic region to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of connective tissue that surround muscles and other organs. The goal of the procedure is to repair any damage or defects in these tissue layers, which may be caused by injury, infection, or other conditions. During the procedure, the surgeon will clean and debride the area, removing any dead or damaged tissue, and then close the defect with sutures or other materials to restore normal anatomy and function. The open approach used in this procedure allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to perform a more thorough repair.
