Dilation of Anus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the gentle stretching of the anus to relax the internal and external anal sphincter muscles, allowing for a reduction in anal tone and relaxation of the anal canal. The objective is to increase the diameter of the anus, facilitating the passage of stool and reducing symptoms of anal fissures, hemorrhoids, or constipation. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may involve the use of specialized instruments, such as anal dilators or bougies, to gradually widen the anal canal. By dilating the anus, patients can experience relief from painful bowel movements and improved bowel function.
