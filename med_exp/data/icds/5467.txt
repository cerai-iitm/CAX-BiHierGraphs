Reposition Right Tibia with Ring External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous repositioning of the right tibia (shinbone) using a ring external fixation device, which is a type of stabilizing system that consists of a circular frame and wires or pins that hold the bone in place. The device is inserted through small incisions in the skin, allowing for minimal disruption to surrounding tissue. The goal of the procedure is to restore the natural alignment of the tibia, which may have been disrupted due to injury or deformity. By repositioning the bone and stabilizing it with the external fixation device, the patient can expect improved mobility and reduced pain.
