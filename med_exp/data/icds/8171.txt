Fluoroscopy of Portal and Splanchnic Veins using Other Contrast
This imaging procedure utilizes fluoroscopy to visualize the portal and splanchnic veins, which are vital blood vessels responsible for carrying blood from the digestive system to the liver. During the procedure, a special type of contrast agent, other than the standard iodinated or gadolinium-based agents, is administered to enhance the visibility of these veins. The fluoroscopic equipment emits continuous X-rays, which are captured as moving images on a monitor, allowing the radiologist to observe the flow of contrast through the portal and splanchnic veins in real-time. This helps diagnose and evaluate various conditions affecting these veins, such as portal hypertension, portal vein thrombosis, or splenic vein thrombosis.
