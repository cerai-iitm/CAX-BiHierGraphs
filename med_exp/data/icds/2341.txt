Release Right Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure is performed through the skin to release adhesions or scar tissue that may be restricting the movement of the right upper lung lobe, thereby improving lung expansion and breathing. During the procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end (endoscope) is inserted through the skin and guided to the affected area. The physician then uses specialized instruments to carefully release the adhesions or scar tissue, allowing the lung lobe to expand and function more normally. This approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical procedures.
