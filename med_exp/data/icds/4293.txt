Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the revision of a synthetic substitute, such as a mesh or implant, that has been previously placed in the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the trunk region. The operation is performed through an open approach, where an incision is made to access the affected area. The goal of the revision is to correct any complications or issues that have arisen from the initial synthetic substitute, which may include infection, erosion, or migration. The surgeon will remove or replace the synthetic material as needed, and repair or reinforce the surrounding tissue to promote optimal healing and functioning.
