Revision of Short-term External Heart Assist System in Heart, Percutaneous Approach
A revision procedure is performed to modify or replace a short-term external heart assist system, which is a temporary mechanical device that supports the heart's pumping function. During the percutaneous approach, the procedure is minimally invasive, and the device is accessed through a small incision in the skin. The revision process may involve replacing or adjusting the device's components to improve its function or resolving any complications that may have arisen from the initial insertion. This procedure is typically performed in a cardiac catheterization laboratory or operating room under fluoroscopic guidance.
