Restriction of Gastric Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through the skin to restrict the gastric artery, reducing blood flow to the stomach. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a catheter is advanced through the artery and the device is deployed to block the artery, reducing blood supply to the stomach. This percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgical procedures. The restricted blood flow can aid in weight loss and improve symptoms in patients with obesity or gastroparesis.
