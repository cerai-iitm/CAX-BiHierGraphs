Bypass Coronary Artery, One Artery from Coronary Artery, Open Approach
During this open-chest surgical procedure, a section of a healthy blood vessel is taken from another part of the body and grafted to a blocked or narrowed coronary artery, creating a detour around the blockage. The goal is to restore blood flow to the heart muscle, promoting improved heart function and reduced symptoms of coronary artery disease. The surgeon makes an incision in the chest and uses a heart-lung machine to maintain circulation during the operation. The bypass graft is carefully sewn in place, connecting the coronary artery to the newly attached vessel segment.
