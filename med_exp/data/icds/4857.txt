Replacement of Skull with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a portion of the skull with a synthetic material, typically in cases where the natural bone is damaged or diseased. The open approach requires a craniotomy, where the surgeon makes an incision in the scalp and temporarily removes a section of the skull to access the affected area. The synthetic substitute, often made of materials such as titanium or polymers, is carefully shaped and fitted to match the natural contours of the skull. The new material is then secured in place, providing structural support and restoring normal cranial function.
