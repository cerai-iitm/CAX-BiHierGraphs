Replacement of Left Radius with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left radius bone with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, allowing for direct visualization of the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the operation. The surgeon makes an incision in the forearm to expose the damaged radius bone, removes the damaged bone, and inserts the synthetic substitute, which is designed to mimic the natural bone's structure and function. The substitute is then secured in place with plates, screws, or other fixation devices to ensure stability and promote proper healing.
