Restriction of Face Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of an intraluminal device through the skin to restrict the flow of blood in a face artery, allowing for precise control over blood flow. The device is guided through the artery using imaging technology, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can be completed in under an hour, reducing recovery time and scarring. By restricting blood flow, this procedure can help treat a range of conditions, including facial tumors, AVMs, and vascular malformations.
