Detachment at Right 2nd Toe, Complete, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the right 2nd toe from the surrounding tissue and bone using an open approach. The goal of this procedure is to remove the entire toe, including the phalanges and surrounding soft tissue, to address various conditions such as severe infection, deformity, or trauma. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the anatomy and ensure a complete detachment. The procedure typically requires a hospital stay and a recovery period to allow for proper healing and rehabilitation.
