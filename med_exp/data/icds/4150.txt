Insertion of Cardiac Resynchronization Pacemaker Pulse Generator into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This cardiac procedure involves the surgical insertion of a cardiac resynchronization pacemaker pulse generator into the chest subcutaneous tissue and fascia through an open approach, allowing for the simultaneous pacing of both ventricles to improve cardiac function. The pacemaker generator is typically implanted in the chest, just below the collarbone, and connected to leads that are inserted through a vein and guided to the heart. The open approach requires a small incision in the chest to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where the generator is carefully placed to ensure proper function and minimal risk of complications. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort and promote a smooth recovery.
