Excision of Left Ankle Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the left ankle joint. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as severe arthritis, bone deformities, or joint damage caused by trauma or infection. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully excise the damaged or diseased joint, which may involve removing bone and soft tissue. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, improve mobility, and restore function to the affected ankle joint.
