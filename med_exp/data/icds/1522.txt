Bypass Portal Vein to Hepatic Vein with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous portal vein to hepatic vein bypass is a minimally invasive procedure that involves creating a new pathway for blood flow using a synthetic substitute. This is achieved by inserting a catheter through the skin and guiding it to the portal vein, then deploying the synthetic graft to connect it to the hepatic vein. This allows for improved blood flow and reduced pressure in the portal vein, which can help alleviate symptoms of portal hypertension and improve liver function. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance and can be done on an outpatient basis.
