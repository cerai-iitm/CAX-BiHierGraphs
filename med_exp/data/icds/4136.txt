Extraction of Left Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left upper leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The damaged or diseased tissue and fascia are then carefully extracted to promote healing and alleviate symptoms. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the affected tissue. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and may be done to treat conditions such as necrotizing fasciitis or compartment syndrome.
