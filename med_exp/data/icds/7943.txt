Fluoroscopy of Left Heart using Low Osmolar Contrast
This non-invasive medical imaging procedure utilizes low osmolar contrast to produce high-quality images of the left heart structure. During the procedure, a small amount of contrast agent is injected into a vein, which helps to highlight the heart's chambers and blood vessels. Fluoroscopy, a real-time X-ray imaging technique, is then used to visualize the flow of contrast through the left heart, allowing healthcare providers to assess cardiac function and diagnose potential abnormalities. The resulting images provide valuable information for guiding treatment decisions and monitoring patient progress.
