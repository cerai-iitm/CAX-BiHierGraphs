Excision of Back Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of unwanted tissue and fascia from the subcutaneous layer of the back through an open incision. The subcutaneous tissue is the layer of fatty tissue just beneath the skin, and the fascia is a type of connective tissue that surrounds muscles and other structures. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and carefully excises the targeted tissue and fascia, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding structures. The goal of the procedure is to remove excess or damaged tissue, relieving symptoms and improving the appearance of the affected area.
