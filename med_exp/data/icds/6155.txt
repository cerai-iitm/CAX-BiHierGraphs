Replacement of Left Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Ceramic Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the diseased or damaged left hip joint is replaced with a ceramic synthetic substitute, which is securely fixed in place using bone cement. The open approach involves making a single incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the replacement. The ceramic material is chosen for its biocompatibility, durability, and resistance to wear and tear, promoting a more natural range of motion and reducing the risk of future complications. The cemented fixation ensures a strong bond between the implant and the surrounding bone, providing long-term stability and support for the new joint.
