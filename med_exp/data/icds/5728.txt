Fusion of Left Wrist Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a left wrist joint fusion is performed to combine two or more bones in the wrist, effectively stabilizing and immobilizing the joint to alleviate pain and restore function. The open approach involves making a incision in the wrist to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to carefully prepare the bone surfaces and insert an autologous tissue substitute, such as a bone graft or plate, to facilitate the fusion process. This approach enables the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, ensuring precise placement of the substitute and optimal results. The goal of the procedure is to promote bone growth and fusion, ultimately leading to a stronger, more stable wrist joint.
