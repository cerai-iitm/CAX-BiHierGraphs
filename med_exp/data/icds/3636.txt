Drainage of Left Hand Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically draining an abscess, cyst, or other fluid-filled lesion on the skin of the left hand through an external incision. The goal is to relieve pressure, reduce pain, and promote healing by removing the accumulated fluid or infected material. The physician makes a small incision in the affected area, allowing the fluid to drain out, and may also remove any dead tissue or debris. After the drainage, the wound is cleaned and dressed to prevent infection and aid recovery.
