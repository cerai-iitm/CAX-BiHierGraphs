Excision of Right Carpal, Open Approach
Surgical excision of the right carpal bones is performed through an open approach, involving a skin incision to access the affected area. The procedure involves dissection of the soft tissue to expose the carpal bones, followed by removal of the targeted bone or bones. The surgical site is then closed, typically in layers, to promote healing and minimize the risk of complications. This procedure is usually done to treat conditions such as carpal bone fractures, osteoarthritis, or bone tumors that affect the wrist joint.
