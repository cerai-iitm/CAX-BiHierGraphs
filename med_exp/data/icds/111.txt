Extirpation of Matter from Intracranial Subdural Space, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the healthcare provider makes an incision in the skull to access the intracranial subdural space, a region between the brain and the dura mater, the outermost protective membrane of the brain. The goal is to remove abnormal or unwanted material, such as blood clots, abscesses, or tumors, that may be compressing brain tissue or causing neurological symptoms. The surgeon carefully removes the unwanted material, taking care not to damage surrounding brain tissue or vital structures. Once the material is removed, the incision is closed, and the wound is managed to promote healing and prevent complications.
