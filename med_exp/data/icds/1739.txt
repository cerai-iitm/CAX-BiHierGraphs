Excision of Thorax Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This diagnostic procedure involves the removal of tissue or cells from the thorax lymphatic system using a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach, guided by imaging technology such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy. A small incision is made in the skin, allowing a thin tube with a camera and specialized instruments to be inserted to visualize the lymphatic structure and collect the necessary tissue samples. The tissue samples are then sent to a laboratory for examination and diagnosis. The goal of this procedure is to determine the presence or extent of disease, such as cancer, in the thorax lymphatic system.
