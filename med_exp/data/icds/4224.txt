Removal of Cardiac Rhythm Related Device from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of a cardiac rhythm related device, such as a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator, from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the trunk. The device is accessed through a small incision in the skin, and specialized instruments are used to carefully dissect and free the device from the surrounding tissue. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be done in an electrophysiology laboratory or operating room. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
