Dilation of Left Ureter with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
The procedure involves the percutaneous introduction of an intraluminal device into the left ureter to facilitate dilation and expand its lumen. This minimally invasive approach allows for precise control and navigation through the ureteral anatomy, ensuring optimal placement of the device. The device is carefully advanced under fluoroscopic guidance to the target area, where it is expanded to achieve the desired level of dilation. By widening the ureteral passage, this procedure aims to improve urinary flow and alleviate associated symptoms.
