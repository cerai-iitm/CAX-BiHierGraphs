Resection of Left Toe Phalanx, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of the phalanx bone in the left toe through an open incision. The phalanx is the bone that forms the toe and resection is necessary to treat conditions such as osteomyelitis, fractures, or bone deformities. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected phalanx bone, and then carefully removes the damaged or infected portion. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, restore function, and improve the overall appearance of the toe.
