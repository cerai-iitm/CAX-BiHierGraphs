Drainage of Right Ankle Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a needle or drainage catheter through the skin to access the right ankle joint, allowing for the removal of fluid or other substances that may be causing pain, swelling, or infection. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing compared to open surgical methods. The diagnostic aspect of the procedure enables healthcare providers to collect samples for laboratory analysis, helping to identify the underlying cause of the joint condition. By draining the affected joint, this procedure can provide immediate relief from discomfort and improved mobility for patients.
