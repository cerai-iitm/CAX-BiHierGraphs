Resection of Sigmoid Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through the skin to visualize the sigmoid colon, allowing the physician to identify and resect (remove) the affected segment of the colon. The percutaneous endoscopic approach enables the surgeon to access the sigmoid colon through a small incision, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The resected colon segment is then removed through the endoscope or a small incision, leaving the surrounding tissue intact.
