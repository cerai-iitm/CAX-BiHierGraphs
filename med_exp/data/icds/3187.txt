Resection of Right Large Intestine, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, known as an endoscope, is inserted through the skin and guided to the right large intestine to visualize the area. Specialized instruments are then used to remove a portion of the intestine through the endoscope, allowing for the resection of damaged or diseased tissue. The percutaneous endoscopic approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. The entire procedure is performed under image guidance, ensuring precise removal of the targeted area while minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues.
