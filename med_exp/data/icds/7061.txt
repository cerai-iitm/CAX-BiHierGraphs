Removal of Drainage Device from Right Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A percutaneous endoscopic procedure is performed to remove a drainage device from the right pleural cavity, a space between the lungs and chest wall. The procedure involves inserting an endoscope, a thin flexible tube with a camera, through a small incision in the chest wall to visualize the drainage device. The device is then carefully grasped and removed under direct visualization, allowing for the pleural cavity to heal and return to its normal function. This minimally invasive procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical approaches.
