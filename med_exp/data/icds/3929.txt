Drainage of Face Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the manual drainage of excessive fluid or pus from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the face, which is a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles and other soft tissue structures. An open approach is used, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the affected area. A drainage device is inserted through the incision to facilitate the removal of the unwanted fluid or material, helping to alleviate pressure, reduce swelling, and promote healing. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and is often used to treat conditions such as facial abscesses, hematomas, or seromas.
