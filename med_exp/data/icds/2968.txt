Extraction of Ileum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the ileum, the lower part of the small intestine, through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is guided through the opening to the ileum, allowing the healthcare provider to inspect the intestinal lining and identify any abnormalities. Tissue samples may be collected for further examination, and any necessary interventions may be performed during the procedure. The diagnostic nature of this procedure enables accurate diagnosis and treatment of ileum-related conditions, such as inflammation, obstruction, or cancer.
