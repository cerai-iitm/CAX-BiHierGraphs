Drainage of Left Knee Joint, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves an open surgical approach to drain the left knee joint, which may be performed to treat conditions such as infection, inflammation, or bleeding. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the knee joint, allowing for the removal of fluid, pus, or blood that may be causing discomfort, pain, or limited mobility. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure and reduce swelling, promoting healing and restoring normal function to the knee joint. The diagnostic aspect of this procedure allows the surgeon to examine the joint and identify any underlying conditions that may require further treatment.
