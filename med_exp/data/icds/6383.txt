Fragmentation in Left Kidney Pelvis, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the left kidney pelvis and fragment kidney stones or other obstructive material into smaller pieces that can be easily removed or passed out of the body. The procedure is typically performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to access the kidney pelvis without making a large incision. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that break up the stones or obstruction, allowing for effective relief from blockages and restoring normal urinary function. This procedure is often performed under general anesthesia and may require a short hospital stay for recovery.
