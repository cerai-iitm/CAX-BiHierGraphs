Pheresis of Erythrocytes, Multiple
This medical procedure involves the removal of multiple units of red blood cells from the body through a process called pheresis, a type of automated blood component collection. During the procedure, blood is drawn from a vein and passed through a specialized machine that separates the different components of blood, including red blood cells, plasma, and platelets. The unwanted red blood cells are removed, and the remaining components are returned to the body along with a replacement fluid to maintain blood volume. This procedure is often used to treat conditions such as polycythemia vera, a blood disorder characterized by an overproduction of red blood cells.
