Excision of Aortic Valve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the aortic valve, a critical component of the heart that regulates blood flow. The damaged or diseased aortic valve is then carefully removed to prevent further complications, such as heart failure or stroke. The excision is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a cardiopulmonary bypass machine to maintain blood circulation during the operation. The goal of this open approach is to restore normal blood flow and improve overall cardiovascular health.
