Change Feeding Device in Lower Intestinal Tract, External Approach
This procedure involves replacing or exchanging a feeding device, such as a gastrostomy tube or jejunostomy tube, in the lower intestinal tract through an external approach. The external approach allows for a less invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. During the procedure, the existing feeding device is removed and a new one is inserted through the same incision site, ensuring continued nutrition and hydration for the patient. The entire process is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete.
