Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Metal on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left hip joint with a metal on polyethylene synthetic substitute, which is implanted without the use of cement, via an open approach. The uncemented prosthesis is designed to provide a stable and durable replacement for the damaged or diseased hip joint, allowing for improved mobility and reduced pain. The open approach involves a single incision, allowing the surgeon to access the hip joint and perform the replacement. The metal on polyethylene synthetic substitute is a common type of hip replacement prosthesis, known for its durability and low wear rate.
