Excision of Pancreas, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through the skin to visualize the pancreas, allowing the surgeon to precisely remove the affected tissue or tumor. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires several small incisions in the abdominal wall to facilitate the insertion of the endoscope and other instruments. The excised tissue is then sent for histopathological examination to confirm the diagnosis and guide further treatment.
