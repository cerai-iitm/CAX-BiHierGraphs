Dilation of Left Brachial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
During this percutaneous procedure, a catheter is inserted through the skin and guided to the left brachial artery to widen the narrowed or blocked artery. The artery is then dilated using a balloon or other device to restore blood flow and relieve symptoms such as arm pain or weakness. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the catheter. After the procedure, the catheter is removed and the incision site is closed, allowing the patient to recover and regain normal function of the arm.
