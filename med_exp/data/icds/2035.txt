Change Drainage Device in Pleura, External Approach
This procedure involves the replacement of an existing drainage device in the pleural space, which is the area between the lungs and chest wall, through an external approach. The pleural space may require drainage to remove excess fluid or air that can accumulate due to various conditions, such as pneumonia, cancer, or trauma. The new drainage device is inserted through a small incision in the chest wall, allowing for continuous or intermittent drainage of the pleural space. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal lung function and alleviate symptoms such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, and coughing.
