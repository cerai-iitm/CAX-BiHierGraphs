Drainage of Duodenum with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the duodenum, which is the first part of the small intestine. A drainage device is then inserted into the duodenum to drain out excess fluids, bile, or other substances that may be causing a blockage or obstruction. The device is carefully placed to ensure proper drainage and to promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting, and to restore normal digestive function.
