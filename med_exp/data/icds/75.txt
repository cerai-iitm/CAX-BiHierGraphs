Excision of Cerebral Hemisphere, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the surgical removal of a portion of the brain's cerebral hemisphere through a small incision in the skull. The percutaneous approach, also known as a minimally invasive technique, is used to minimize tissue damage and reduce recovery time. The diagnostic purpose of this excision is to examine the removed tissue for abnormalities or disease, such as tumor cells or infected tissue, to determine the underlying cause of symptoms. The surgical team uses imaging guidance, such as MRI or CT scans, to accurately locate and target the affected area of the brain.
