Supplement Mitral Valve with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a diseased or damaged mitral valve with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, requiring a thoracotomy or median sternotomy incision. The surgeon will access the valve through the opening, carefully removing the diseased valve and implanting the synthetic substitute, ensuring proper alignment and secure attachment. The new valve is designed to mimic the natural valve's function, allowing for proper blood flow between the left atrium and ventricle. Successful mitral valve replacement can significantly improve cardiac function, reducing symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and palpitations.
