Reposition Right Humeral Shaft with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the humerus bone in the upper arm is repositioned to its normal alignment after a fracture or misalignment. The operation is performed through an open approach, where the surgeon makes a skin incision to access the affected area. Internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods are then implanted to hold the bone in place while it heals. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal strength, mobility, and function to the arm and eliminate associated pain and discomfort.
