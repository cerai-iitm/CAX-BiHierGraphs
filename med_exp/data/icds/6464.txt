Resection of Urethra, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through a small incision in the skin to visualize the urethra. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to remove the desired portion of the urethra, a muscular tube that carries urine out of the body. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissue and less post-operative pain compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a short hospital stay for observation and recovery.
