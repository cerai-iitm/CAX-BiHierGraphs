Replacement of Right Breast using Latissimus Dorsi Myocutaneous Flap, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the use of a latissimus dorsi myocutaneous flap, a type of tissue flap that combines skin, fat, and muscle, to reconstruct the right breast. The open approach is employed, which means a larger incision is made to access the area, allowing for direct visualization of the surgical site. The latissimus dorsi muscle, located in the back, is mobilized and transferred to the chest area to create a natural-looking breast mound. The flap is then shaped and secured in place to achieve a natural appearance and symmetry with the opposite breast.
