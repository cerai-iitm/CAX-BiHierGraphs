Release Sigmoid Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the sigmoid colon and identify the area where it is trapped or adhered. Through a small incision, specialized instruments are inserted to gently release the colon from the surrounding tissue or adhesions. The entire procedure is performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accuracy and safety. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms caused by the trapped colon, including abdominal pain, bloating, and changes in bowel habits.
