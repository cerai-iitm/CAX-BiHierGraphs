Removal of External Fixation Device from Left Fibula, External Approach
The removal of an external fixation device from the left fibula is a surgical procedure that involves the careful disassembly and extraction of the external hardware from the outer aspect of the left fibula bone. This procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and requires a minor incision to access the fixation device. The surgeon will gently remove the pins, rods, and other hardware components, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding soft tissues or nerves. Once the device is completely removed, the incision is closed, and the affected area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
