Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Axillary Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into the right axillary vein, providing a secure and reliable means of delivering medications or fluids. The procedure involves making a small incision in the skin, followed by the percutaneous insertion of the infusion device into the vein. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the device is carefully positioned to ensure proper placement and function. The infusion device is then secured in place, allowing for effortless administration of medications or fluids as needed.
