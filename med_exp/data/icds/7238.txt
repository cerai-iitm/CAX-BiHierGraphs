Control Bleeding in Right Inguinal Region, Percutaneous Approach
The minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small instrument through the skin to control bleeding in the right inguinal region. This approach is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to accurately target the source of bleeding. The procedure is usually done under local anesthesia, and the small incision is closed with stitches or staples after the bleeding is stopped. It is often performed on an emergency basis to treat life-threatening bleeding or to prevent further complications.
