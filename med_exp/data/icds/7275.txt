Detachment at Right Foot, Partial 5th Ray, Open Approach
Surgical detachment of the partial 5th ray at the right foot is performed through an open approach, involving a skin incision to access the affected area. The procedure targets the smallest toe, which is the 5th ray, and involves the separation of the affected portion from the rest of the foot. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and detach the targeted section of the 5th ray. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as toe deformities, traumatic injuries, or infections that affect the 5th ray of the right foot.
