Reposition Right Humeral Shaft, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the humeral shaft in the right arm, which has become displaced due to injury or trauma. The procedure is performed through an external approach, where the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected area. The broken bone is then manually or instrumentally repositioned to its correct anatomical position, and stabilized with internal fixators, such as plates, screws, or rods, to facilitate healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore the natural alignment of the humerus, allowing for optimal range of motion and function of the arm.
