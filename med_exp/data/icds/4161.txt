Insertion of Tunneled Vascular Access Device into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves creating a small incision in the chest area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, allowing for the insertion of a tunneled vascular access device. The device is carefully placed under the skin and anchored in the fascia to provide a secure and stable point of access for hemodialysis or infusion therapy. The open approach requires a small surgical incision, which is typically closed with sutures or staples after the device is in place. With proper care, the tunneled vascular access device can remain in place for an extended period, allowing for repeated access to the bloodstream without the need for repeated punctures.
