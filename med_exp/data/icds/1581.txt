Excision of Left Foot Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left foot vein, followed by the removal of the targeted vein segment. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the vein and surrounding tissue, enabling precise excision of the affected area. The excised vein segment is then removed, and the incision site is closed, typically with sutures or staples. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as varicose veins, venous insufficiency, or venous ulcers.
