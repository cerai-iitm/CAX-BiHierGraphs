Repair Sigmoid Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the sigmoid colon, which is the lower part of the large intestine. The goal of the operation is to repair any damage or defects in the sigmoid colon, such as a perforation or a blockage, to restore normal bowel function. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly and perform the necessary repairs using surgical instruments. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for post-operative care.
