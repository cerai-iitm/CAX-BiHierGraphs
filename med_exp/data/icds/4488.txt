Release Right Upper Leg Muscle, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the right upper leg to access and release tense or contracted muscles, relieving compression or restriction on surrounding tissues and nerves. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected muscle, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and dissect the adhesions or scar tissue contributing to the restriction. The release of the muscle allows for improved range of motion, reduced pain and stiffness, and enhanced overall function of the affected leg. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient may require a period of rehabilitation to restore strength and mobility to the treated area.
