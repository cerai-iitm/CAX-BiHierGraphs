Restriction of Thoracic Aorta, Descending with Intraluminal Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves restricting the thoracic aorta, specifically the descending portion, using an intraluminal device, which is inserted through an open approach, allowing surgeons to directly access the affected area. The device is designed to narrow or obliterate the aortic lumen, reducing blood flow and pressure, and thereby treating conditions such as aortic aneurysms or dissections. The open approach typically involves a thoracotomy, where the chest is opened to access the thoracic aorta, allowing the surgeon to visualize and directly manipulate the aorta and surrounding structures. This complex procedure is typically performed in a qualified hospital setting under general anesthesia, with careful postoperative care and monitoring to minimize complications.
