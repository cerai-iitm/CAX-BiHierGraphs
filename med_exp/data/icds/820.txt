Occlusion of Intracranial Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an intraluminal device to occlude an intracranial artery, effectively blocking blood flow to the targeted area. The device is inserted through a small puncture in the skin, requiring only a small incision, and guided through the blood vessels to reach the intracranial artery. Once in place, the device is expanded to occlude the artery, preventing blood from flowing through it. This procedure is often used to prevent bleeding or reduce the risk of rupture in weakened or damaged arteries.
