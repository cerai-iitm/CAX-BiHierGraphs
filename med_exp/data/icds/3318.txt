Dilation of Pancreatic Duct with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive endoscopic procedure involves widening the pancreatic duct using an intraluminal device, which is inserted through a natural or artificial opening. The device is carefully guided through the duct to expand its narrow passage, allowing for improved drainage and relief from blockages. This procedure is typically performed under sedation and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure precise placement of the device. The goal is to restore normal pancreatic function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
