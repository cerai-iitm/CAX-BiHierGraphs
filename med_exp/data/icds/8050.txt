Fluoroscopy of Intracranial Arteries
This medical imaging procedure utilizes continuous X-ray beams to visualize the internal structures of the brain, focusing specifically on the intricate network of arteries within the cranium. During the examination, a contrast agent is often injected into a blood vessel to enhance the visibility of the arteries and improve diagnostic accuracy. The real-time imaging capabilities of fluoroscopy allow for precise guidance during interventional procedures, such as angioplasty or stent placement, to treat conditions like cerebral vasospasm or atherosclerosis. By providing high-resolution images of the intracranial arteries, this procedure facilitates accurate diagnosis and effective treatment of vascular disorders affecting the brain.
