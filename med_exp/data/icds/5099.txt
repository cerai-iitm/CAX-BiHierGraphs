Reposition Right Ulna, External Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the skin and soft tissue to access the right ulna bone in the forearm. The surgeon then carefully manipulates the bone to restore its normal anatomical position, ensuring proper alignment and rotation. Internal fixation devices, such as plates or screws, may be used to hold the bone in place while it heals. The goal of this operation is to relieve symptoms and improve function in the arm and hand by correcting deformities or misalignments of the right ulna.
