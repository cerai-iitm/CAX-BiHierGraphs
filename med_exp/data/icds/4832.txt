Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Skull, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skull to insert an internal fixation device, which provides stability and support to the cranial bones. The device is carefully positioned and secured to restore the natural alignment and functionality of the skull. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the fixation device and address any underlying bone fragments or deformities. Post-operatively, the device remains in place to promote healing and facilitate recovery.
