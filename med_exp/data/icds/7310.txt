Detachment at Left 4th Toe, High, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the left 4th toe at a high level, which means the separation occurs proximally, closer to the foot. The open approach suggests that the surgery is performed through a skin incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the affected area directly. The operation aims to resolve issues such as hammertoe deformity, toe fracture, or chronic pain and stiffness in the toe joint. The procedure requires careful dissection and isolation of the affected toe bones and surrounding soft tissues to ensure proper detachment and minimal trauma to the surrounding area.
