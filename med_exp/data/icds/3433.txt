Excision of Pancreas, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During an endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the pancreas. The healthcare provider then uses the endoscope to locate and remove a portion of the pancreas, using specialized instruments to carefully dissect and extract the tissue. This procedure is typically performed under sedation or general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
