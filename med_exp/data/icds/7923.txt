Plain Radiography of Right Heart using Other Contrast
This non-invasive medical imaging procedure involves the use of a special dye or contrast agent, other than the typical iodine-based agents, to highlight the structures of the right heart. The contrast is administered through a vein, and then X-rays are taken to produce images of the heart. The resulting radiographs provide valuable information to aid in the diagnosis of conditions affecting the right heart, such as valve problems, cardiac tumors, or cardiomyopathy. The use of other contrast agents may be necessary for patients with certain medical conditions or allergies, allowing for a safer and more effective imaging process.
