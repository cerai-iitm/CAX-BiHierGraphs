Destruction of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the destruction of the right upper lung lobe, which is a minimally invasive approach that reduces post-operative complications and promotes faster recovery. During the procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the lung tissue. The damaged or diseased portion of the right upper lung lobe is then destroyed using specialized instruments, allowing healthy tissue to heal and function normally. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, emphysema, or bronchiectasis.
