Excision of Right Kidney, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the right kidney through a small incision in the skin, with the guidance of imaging technology. The percutaneous approach allows for a smaller incision and reduced tissue damage compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to diagnose and treat various kidney-related conditions, including cancer, tumors, or disease. The goal of the procedure is to excise the affected kidney, which can help alleviate symptoms, improve quality of life, and potentially save the patient's life.
