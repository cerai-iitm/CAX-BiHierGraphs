Occlusion of Left Atrial Appendage with Extraluminal Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an extraluminal device through a small incision in the groin area, guided by live X-ray images and echocardiography, to reach the left atrial appendage. The device is then deployed to occlude the left atrial appendage, a pouch-like region in the upper chamber of the heart, thereby reducing the risk of stroke and blood clots. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for precise navigation and visualization of the heart structures, ensuring accurate placement of the device. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and takes about an hour to complete.
