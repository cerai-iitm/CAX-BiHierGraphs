Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Kidneys using Other Contrast
"This radiological procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the bilateral kidneys, which means both kidneys are examined simultaneously. During the procedure, a type of contrast agent, other than the standard or typical ones, is administered to enhance the visibility of the kidneys and their structures. The fluoroscope, a type of X-ray machine, produces continuous X-ray beams that allow the healthcare provider to monitor the movement of the contrast agent in real-time. This provides valuable information about the kidney function, anatomy, and any potential abnormalities or blockages."
