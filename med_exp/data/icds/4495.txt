Repair Facial Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the facial area to access and repair damaged or weakened muscles that control facial expressions, such as those used for smiling, frowning, or blinking. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected muscle tissue, enabling precise repair and reconstruction to restore normal muscle function and facial mobility. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as facial paralysis, Bell's palsy, or cosmetic deformities resulting from trauma or congenital defects. By repairing the damaged muscle, this procedure aims to improve facial aesthetics and restore patients' ability to express emotions naturally.
