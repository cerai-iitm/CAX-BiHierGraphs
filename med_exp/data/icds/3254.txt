Revision of Feeding Device in Upper Intestinal Tract, External Approach
This procedure involves the revision of a feeding device, such as a gastric tube or jejunal tube, placed in the upper intestinal tract to facilitate nutrition. The revision is performed through an external approach, which means the surgery is done from outside the body. The goal of the procedure is to adjust or replace the feeding device to ensure proper function and prevent complications. The revision may be necessary due to various reasons, including device malfunction, tube displacement, or blockage.
