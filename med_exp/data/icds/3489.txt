Release Liver, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Percutaneous endoscopic release of the liver is a minimally invasive procedure where a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, is inserted through the skin to visualize the liver. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort. Through the endoscope, specialized instruments are guided to release adhesions or scar tissue that may be compressing the liver, restoring normal liver function and relieving symptoms. This approach allows for faster recovery and fewer complications compared to open surgical techniques.
