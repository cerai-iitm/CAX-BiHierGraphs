Excision of Common Bile Duct, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdomen to access the common bile duct, a tube that carries bile from the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose any abnormalities or blockages in the duct, such as tumors, stones, or narrowing. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully excises, or removes, a portion of the common bile duct to examine it for any signs of disease or damage. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve additional steps, such as repairing or reconstructing the duct, depending on the underlying condition.
