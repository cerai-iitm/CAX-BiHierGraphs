Resection of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a thin tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the left upper lung lobe and guide the removal of the affected lobe through small incisions in the chest wall. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal invasion and reduced trauma to surrounding tissues. The resection is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the insertion of a chest tube to drain fluids and air from the pleural space. The procedure aims to treat conditions such as lung cancer, tuberculosis, or benign tumors, and can help alleviate symptoms like coughing, chest pain, and shortness of breath.
