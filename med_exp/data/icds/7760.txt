Irrigation of Lower GI using Irrigating Substance, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to clean and flush out the lower gastrointestinal (GI) tract using a fluid irrigating substance introduced through a natural opening or an artificial opening using an endoscope. The scope is carefully guided through the lower GI tract to deliver the irrigating substance, allowing for thorough cleansing of the area. This procedure helps to remove debris, mucus, or other obstructions, and can be used to prepare the area for further diagnostic or therapeutic procedures. The endoscopic approach enables real-time visualization of the lower GI tract, ensuring accurate and targeted irrigation.
