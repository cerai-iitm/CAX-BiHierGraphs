Replacement of Right Hand Skin with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Full Thickness, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the replacement of the skin on the right hand with a non-autologous tissue substitute, which is a biological or synthetic material sourced from outside the patient's body. The full thickness of the skin is replaced, meaning that all layers of the skin are restored, including the epidermis, dermis, and hypodermis. The external approach is used, where the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the area requiring replacement. The goal of this procedure is to restore function and appearance to the right hand, often following trauma, injury, or disease that has caused significant skin loss or damage.
