Excision of Left Lower Leg Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower leg to access and remove a damaged or diseased tendon. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The surgeon carefully dissects the tissue to expose the affected tendon, then uses specialized instruments to excise the tendon, taking care to preserve surrounding tissues and structures. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the leg is immobilized to allow for proper healing.
