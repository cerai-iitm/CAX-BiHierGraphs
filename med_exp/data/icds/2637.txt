Bypass Descending Colon to Cutaneous, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to create a bypass in the descending colon, rerouting it to the skin through a new opening, allowing waste to exit the body. This is achieved through a percutaneous endoscopic approach, which involves using an endoscope and specialized instruments inserted through the skin to perform the bypass. The procedure is typically done to divert the flow of stool away from a diseased or damaged portion of the colon, allowing the affected area to heal or be surgically repaired. The resulting stoma, or artificial opening, on the skin allows for the elimination of waste, often as a temporary solution until further treatment or surgery.
