Reposition Right Fibula, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right fibula, a bone in the lower leg, to its correct anatomical position through an external approach. The external approach allows the surgeon to access the fibula through a skin incision, enabling them to manually reposition the bone to its normal alignment. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as fracture, malunion, or deformity of the fibula. The goal of the surgery is to restore the normal functionality and movement of the ankle and leg.
