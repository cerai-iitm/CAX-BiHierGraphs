Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the peritoneal cavity and drain accumulated fluid or debris under image guidance. The procedure is performed through a small incision in the abdominal wall, allowing for precise placement of the drainage tube. The endoscope guides the insertion of the drainage tube, enabling the removal of fluid or debris without causing damage to surrounding tissues or organs. This diagnostic procedure is often used to diagnose and manage conditions such as peritonitis, ascites, or intra-abdominal abscesses.
