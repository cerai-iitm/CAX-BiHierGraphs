Dilation of Left Internal Carotid Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a catheter through the skin and into the left internal carotid artery to widen the vessel using a balloon or other device. The goal is to improve blood flow to the brain by expanding the artery and reducing blockages. The percutaneous approach is a minimally invasive method that avoids open surgery, reducing recovery time and risk of complications. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the catheter is carefully navigated to the affected area, where the dilation device is then deployed to widen the artery.
