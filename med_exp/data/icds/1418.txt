Dilation of Upper Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small catheter through the skin to reach the upper vein, allowing for the dilation of the narrowed or blocked section. A balloon or other specialized device is then inflated to widen the vein, restoring blood flow and relieving symptoms such as pain, swelling, and cramping. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the healthcare professional carefully navigates the catheter to precisely target the affected area, ensuring an effective and safe procedure.
