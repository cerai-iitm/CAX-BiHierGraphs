Drainage of Face Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous drainage of infected or inflamed subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the face, a minimally invasive approach that reduces tissue damage and promotes healing. The procedure typically begins with the administration of local anesthesia, followed by the insertion of a small drainage tube or catheter through a small skin incision to access the affected area. The purulent or inflammatory fluid is then aspirated or drained, and the area is flushed with sterile solution to remove any remaining debris. The procedure is usually performed in an outpatient setting and may require antibiotics and wound care to facilitate recovery.
