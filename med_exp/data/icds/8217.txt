Fluoroscopy of Biliary and Pancreatic Ducts using Low Osmolar Contrast
This imaging procedure involves the use of low osmolar contrast medium to visualize the biliary and pancreatic ducts, allowing for clear identification of any blockages, stones, or other abnormalities. Fluoroscopy is used to guide the procedure, providing real-time images of the ducts to aid in accurate diagnosis. The low osmolar contrast medium is injected into the ducts through a catheter, and its movement is monitored using continuous X-ray beams. The resulting images help clinicians assess the functionality and anatomy of the biliary and pancreatic systems, facilitating diagnosis and treatment planning.
