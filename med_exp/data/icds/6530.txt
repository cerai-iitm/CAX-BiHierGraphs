Excision of Left Ovary, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdominal wall to gain access to the pelvic cavity, where the left ovary is located. The surgeon then carefully identifies and isolates the ovary, ligating and dividing the blood vessels and other attachments to facilitate its removal. The ovary is then carefully dissected and excised, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding structures. The incision is subsequently closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
