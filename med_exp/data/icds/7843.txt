Assistance with Cardiac Output using Balloon Pump, Intermittent
This procedure involves the use of an intra-aortic balloon pump to assist with cardiac output on an intermittent basis. The balloon pump is inserted through the femoral artery in the leg and guided to the aorta, where it inflates and deflates in sync with the heart's natural rhythm. This helps to increase myocardial oxygen perfusion and reduce workload on the heart, which can improve cardiac function and reduce symptoms in patients with heart failure or cardiogenic shock. The device is typically used in critical care settings, such as an intensive care unit, and is often employed as a bridge to further treatment or as a stabilizing measure until the patient's condition improves.
