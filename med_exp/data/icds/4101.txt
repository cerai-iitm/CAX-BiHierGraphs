Extirpation of Matter from Right Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this procedure, a surgical incision is made in the right hand to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The purpose of the procedure is to remove unwanted material, such as infected or damaged tissue, from the affected area. The surgeon will carefully dissect the tissue to locate and extirpate the targeted matter, ensuring complete removal to promote healing and prevent further complications. The wound is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, to facilitate recovery and minimize scarring.
