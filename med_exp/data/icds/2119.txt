Drainage of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Percutaneous drainage of the right lower lung lobe is a minimally invasive procedure used to remove fluid, pus, or air from the lung tissue. During the procedure, a thin needle or tube is inserted through the skin and guided by imaging technology to reach the affected area. The fluid or air is then suctioned out, and the needle or tube is removed, allowing the lung to re-expand and promoting healing. This diagnostic procedure helps to identify the underlying cause of the lung condition and guides further treatment.
