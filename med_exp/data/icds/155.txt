Release Cervical Spinal Cord, Open Approach
The procedure involves a surgical incision to access the cervical spinal cord, followed by the careful dissection and release of any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstructions that may be compressing or constricting the spinal cord. This open approach allows for direct visualization and precise manipulation of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and resolve the underlying issue. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure on the spinal cord, alleviate symptoms, and promote optimal neural function and recovery. By releasing the compression, the procedure can help to restore normal spinal cord function and improve overall quality of life.
