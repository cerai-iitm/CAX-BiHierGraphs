Drainage of Pancreas with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting an endoscope through the mouth and guiding it through the stomach to reach the pancreas, allowing for the visualization of the pancreatic duct. A small drainage device is then placed through the scope to drain excess fluid or pancreatic secretions, which can help alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. The percutaneous endoscopic approach enables the doctor to access the pancreas without open surgery, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The drainage device remains in place until the pancreatic duct has healed and the fluid has drained, at which point it can be removed.
