Extirpation of Matter from Left Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter or substances from the left pleural cavity through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach. During the procedure, a thin, lighted tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through a small incision in the chest wall to visualize and extract the unwanted material. The pleural cavity is the space between the lungs and chest wall, and the removal of matter from this area can help to alleviate symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, and coughing. The percutaneous endoscopic approach minimizes tissue damage and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgical methods.
