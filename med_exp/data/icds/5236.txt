Excision of Left Tarsal, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left foot to access the tarsal bones, which form the hindfoot and midfoot. The affected tarsal bone is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate symptoms such as pain, inflammation, or infection. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissue, enabling precise dissection and removal of the affected area. Following excision, the incision is typically closed, and the foot is immobilized to promote healing and minimize complications.
