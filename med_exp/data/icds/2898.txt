Excision of Rectum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the rectum through a natural or artificial opening, often performed to treat conditions such as rectal cancer, ulcerative colitis, or familial adenomatous polyposis. The surgery typically involves a laparoscopic or open approach, where the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen or anus to access the rectum. The rectum is then carefully dissected and separated from surrounding tissues, and finally, the affected portion is removed. The remaining rectal tissue is then closed, and the patient may require a temporary or permanent colostomy to divert the flow of stool.
