Excision of Cervical Spinal Cord, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical incision to access the cervical spine, followed by removal of a portion of the spinal cord in the cervical region. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the targeted section of the spinal cord. Depending on the underlying condition, this procedure may be performed to relieve compression, remove a tumor or lesion, or treat a cervical spinal cord injury. The goal of the surgery is to alleviate symptoms, restore function, and improve the patient's quality of life.
