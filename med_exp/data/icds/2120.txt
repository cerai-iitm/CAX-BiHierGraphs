Drainage of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin or an existing opening into the right lower lung lobe to remove excess fluid or air that has accumulated in the lung. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and treat conditions such as pleural effusion, empyema, or pneumothorax, which can cause respiratory distress and discomfort. The drainage tube is carefully guided into the lung lobe using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement and minimize potential complications. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort and anxiety.
