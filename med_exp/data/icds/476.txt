Insertion of Pacemaker Lead into Left Ventricle, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a pacemaker lead through the skin and into a blood vessel, typically in the groin area, and guiding it through the vascular system to the left ventricle. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the lead is positioned and secured in place to regulate the heart's rhythm. The pacemaker generator is then connected to the lead, allowing it to transmit electrical impulses to the left ventricle, ensuring a normal heartbeat. After the procedure, the pacemaker is programmed to optimize its function and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
