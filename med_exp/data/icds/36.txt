Drainage of Cerebral Hemisphere, Open Approach
This procedure involves creating an opening in the cranium to access the cerebral hemisphere, a vital part of the brain responsible for motor, sensory, and cognitive functions. The goal of the surgery is to drain accumulated fluids, blood, or other substances that may be exerting pressure on brain tissue, causing damage or impairment. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and remove the unwanted substances, relieving pressure and promoting healing. The procedure is often performed as an emergency measure to treat conditions such as hematomas, abscesses, or cerebral edema.
