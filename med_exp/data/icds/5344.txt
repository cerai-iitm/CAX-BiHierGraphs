Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Upper Femur, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, from the left upper femur, also known as the proximal femur, through an open approach. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the internal fixation device, which allows the surgeon to visualize and carefully remove the device to prevent damage to surrounding soft tissues or nerves. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and pain. The removal of the internal fixation device is often necessary once the bone has fully healed and the device is no longer required to provide stability and support to the femur.
