Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Left Hip Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a synthetic substitute, such as a prosthetic device or artificial joint, from the left hip joint through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the joint. The goal of this procedure is to remove the faulty or damaged synthetic substitute that is causing pain, discomfort, or limited mobility in the hip joint. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissue to ensure a thorough removal of the synthetic substitute. Post-operative care and rehabilitation are crucial to ensure proper healing and restoration of hip function.
