Drainage of Pelvic Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the pelvic region to drain accumulated fluids, air, or pus from the pelvic cavity. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately locate and drain the problematic site. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as pelvic abscesses, hematomas, or seromas that develop as a complication of surgery, trauma, or infection. The goal of the procedure is to promote healing, relieve symptoms, and prevent further complications.
