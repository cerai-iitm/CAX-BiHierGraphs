Insertion of Infusion Device into Inferior Vena Cava, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the skin to access the inferior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the lower body to the heart. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a thin catheter is inserted through the incision and guided into the inferior vena cava, allowing for the placement of an infusion device. The device is designed to administer medication, fluids, or nutrients directly into the bloodstream over an extended period. Once the device is in place, the catheter is removed, and the incision site is closed, typically with a small bandage or stitches.
