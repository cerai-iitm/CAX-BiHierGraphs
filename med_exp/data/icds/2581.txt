Resection of Right Submaxillary Gland, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the right submaxillary gland, a salivary gland located under the jawbone. The gland is then carefully dissected and removed, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves and blood vessels. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as salivary gland tumors, stones, or chronic infections. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the gland and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the affected gland.
