Excision of Right Radius, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right radius bone through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the bone. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as bone tumors, osteomyelitis, or severe fractures that cannot be corrected with other treatments. The surgeon will make an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to expose the affected bone, and then carefully remove the damaged or diseased bone tissue. The wound is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is taken to the recovery room to begin the healing process.
