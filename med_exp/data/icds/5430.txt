Reposition Right Femoral Shaft with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This is a minimally invasive surgical procedure where the surgeon realigns the right femoral shaft, which has become misaligned due to fracture or deformity, and stabilizes it with an internal fixation device inserted through a small incision. The intramedullary device is placed within the marrow canal of the femur to provide stability and support, promoting proper healing and alignment. The percutaneous approach involves making a small opening in the skin, allowing the surgeon to access the affected area with minimal tissue disruption. The procedure aims to restore the natural anatomy of the femoral shaft, reducing pain and restoring mobility and function to the affected leg.
