Drainage of Stomach, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the stomach, allowing the surgeon to drain accumulated fluids or gases that have built up due to obstruction, injury, or disease. The open approach provides direct visualization of the stomach and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to identify and address any underlying conditions contributing to the accumulation. The drainage of the stomach helps to alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting, and can also help to prevent complications like infection and organ damage. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be accompanied by additional interventions, such as repair of any underlying defects or removal of foreign objects.
