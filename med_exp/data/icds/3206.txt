Resection of Rectum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a portion of the rectum through a small incision in the skin, utilizing an endoscope to visually guide the surgeon. The endoscope is inserted through the anus, allowing for direct visualization of the rectal tissue, and specialized instruments are used to dissect and remove the affected area. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for a large open incision, reducing trauma to surrounding tissues and promoting faster healing. The resection of the rectum is typically performed to treat conditions such as rectal cancer, polyps, or other abnormalities.
