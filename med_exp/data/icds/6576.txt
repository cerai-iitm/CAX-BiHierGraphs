Extirpation of Matter from Uterus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the removal of abnormal or unwanted material from the uterus through a natural or artificial opening. The goal is to restore the uterus to a healthy state by eliminating any unwanted substances or tissues that may be causing symptoms or compromising fertility. This may be performed to treat conditions such as retained placenta, uterine polyps, or endometrial lesions, and can be done through a minimally invasive approach using specialized instruments or through an open surgical incision. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety.
