Supplement Female Perineum with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During a surgical procedure, a synthetic material is implanted through an open approach to reinforce the female perineum, a region between the vagina and anus. This procedure is often performed to repair defects or weaknesses in the perineal body, which can occur due to childbirth, trauma, or other conditions. The synthetic substitute is carefully placed to provide additional support and stability to the perineal muscles and tissues. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal anatomy and function to the perineal area, alleviating symptoms such as pain, incontinence, or difficulty with bowel movements.
