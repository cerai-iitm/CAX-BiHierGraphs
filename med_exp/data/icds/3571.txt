Excision of Right Thyroid Gland Lobe, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right thyroid gland lobe through an open approach, which means a single incision is made in the neck to access the thyroid gland. The diagnostic excision is typically performed to obtain a tissue sample for further examination, usually to diagnose or rule out thyroid cancer or other thyroid-related disorders. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the right thyroid gland lobe and removes it, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves and blood vessels. The removed tissue is then sent to a laboratory for histopathological examination to determine the underlying condition.
