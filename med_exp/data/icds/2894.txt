Excision of Rectum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the entire rectum through an open incision in the abdomen, typically performed to treat conditions such as rectal cancer, ulcerative colitis, or familial adenomatous polyposis. During the operation, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the rectum, carefully dissecting and mobilizing the rectum from surrounding tissues before removing it entirely. The procedure often involves the creation of a colostomy, where the healthy portion of the colon is diverted to an opening on the abdomen to allow for the elimination of waste. In some cases, the procedure may be combined with other surgeries, such as a proctectomy or abdominoperineal resection, to completely remove the rectum and anus.
