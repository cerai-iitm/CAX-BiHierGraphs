Revision of Tracheostomy Device in Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the trachea and facilitate the revision of a tracheostomy device, which is a tube inserted into the windpipe to assist breathing. The revision process is performed through a natural or artificial opening in the trachea, allowing the healthcare provider to correct any issues with the existing device or replace it with a new one. The endoscopic approach enables a more precise and gentle procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. Through this procedure, patients can experience improved breathing and overall quality of life.
