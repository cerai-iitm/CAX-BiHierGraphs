Introduction of Vasopressor into Peripheral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Administration of a vasopressor medication is performed through a peripheral vein, which is a vein located in the arm or leg, using a percutaneous approach. This involves inserting a small catheter or needle through the skin and into the vein, allowing for the delivery of the vasopressor directly into the bloodstream. The percutaneous approach is a minimally invasive method that reduces the risk of complications compared to other approaches. The introduction of a vasopressor into a peripheral vein helps to increase blood pressure in patients who are experiencing hypotension or shock.
