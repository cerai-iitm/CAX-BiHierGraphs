Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Tarsal, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device from the left tarsal bones through an open approach, requiring a skin incision to access the implant. The tarsal bones are located in the midfoot and include the calcaneus, talus, cuboid, navicular, and cuneiform bones. The internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, was previously implanted to stabilize and heal a fracture or other bone defect in the left foot. The removal procedure is typically performed under general or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort, and the surgeon takes care to avoid damaging surrounding soft tissues and bone.
