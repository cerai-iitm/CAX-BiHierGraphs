Introduction of Destructive Agent into Peripheral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to introduce a destructive agent into a peripheral vein through the skin, utilizing a percutaneous approach. This involves inserting a small needle or catheter into the vein, typically in the arm or leg, to deliver the agent directly to the affected area. The-purpose of this procedure is to treat various vascular conditions, such as varicose veins or other benign lesions, by sclerosing or destroying the targeted tissue. The treatment is usually performed under local anesthesia and is often used as an alternative to surgical excision.
