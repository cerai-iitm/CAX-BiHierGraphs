Excision of Left Foot Muscle, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a surgeon makes an open incision in the left foot to access the underlying muscle tissue. The affected muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate pain, weakness, or limited mobility in the foot. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the damaged or diseased muscle tissue. Following the excision, the incision is closed, and the patient begins the recovery process, which may involve physical therapy to restore strength and function to the left foot.
