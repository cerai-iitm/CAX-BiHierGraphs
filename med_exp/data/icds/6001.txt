Insertion of Spacer into Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this open approach procedure, a surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the right knee joint. A spacer is then carefully inserted into the joint, usually to replace a damaged or infected joint surface, or to stabilize the joint until a more permanent replacement can be performed. The spacer is designed to restore joint function and relieve pain, allowing for improved mobility and reduced discomfort. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the operation.
