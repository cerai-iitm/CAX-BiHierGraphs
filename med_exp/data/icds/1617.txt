Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Right Femoral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert a device into the right femoral vein, which is a major blood vessel in the thigh. This insertion is done percutaneously, meaning through the skin, without the need for a surgical incision. The device is designed to remain within the vein, allowing for the treatment or management of various vascular conditions. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the device.
