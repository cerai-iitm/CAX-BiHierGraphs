Replacement of Right Foot Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Partial Thickness, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing damaged skin on the right foot with a partial thickness autologous tissue substitute, which is a biological material derived from the patient's own tissues. The external approach is used, where the surgeon makes an incision on the skin to access the affected area. The goal is to restore the skin's natural function, promote healing, and improve mobility and comfort. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully shaped and secured to cover the affected area, providing a durable and natural-feeling replacement for the damaged skin.
