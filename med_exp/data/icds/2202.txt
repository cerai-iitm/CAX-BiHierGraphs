Excision of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the right upper lung lobe through a small incision using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The surgeon uses the endoscope to visualize the lung lobe and carefully excise it, reducing bleeding and trauma to surrounding tissues. The percutaneous approach allows for faster recovery, less pain, and reduced risk of complications compared to open-chest surgery. The excised lung tissue is then removed through the small incision, which is closed and dressed to promote healing.
