Drainage of Bilateral Lungs, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This therapeutic procedure involves the use of an endoscope to drain fluid and/or air from both lungs through natural or artificial openings. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or a pre-existing incision to visualize the lung tissue and identify the area of fluid accumulation. The fluid is then suctioned out using specialized equipment attached to the endoscope, restoring lung function and alleviating symptoms such as shortness of breath and chest pain. This minimally invasive procedure is often performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety.
