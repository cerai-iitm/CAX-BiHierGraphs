Insertion of Pacemaker Lead into Pericardium, Open Approach
This is a surgical procedure where a pacemaker lead is inserted into the pericardium, a sac that surrounds the heart, through an open approach, which involves making a larger incision in the chest to access the pericardium. The lead is carefully guided into the correct position within the pericardium, where it will transmit electrical signals to regulate the heartbeat. During the procedure, the surgeon will visually inspect the pericardium and surrounding tissues to ensure proper placement of the lead. Once the lead is in place, the incision is closed, and the pacemaker is programmed to regulate the heartbeat.
