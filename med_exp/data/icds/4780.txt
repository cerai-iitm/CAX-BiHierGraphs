Repair Left Elbow Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left elbow to access and repair damaged or inflamed bursa, a fluid-filled sac that reduces friction between moving parts, as well as torn or stretched ligaments, which connect bones and provide joint stability. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary repairs, which may involve removing inflamed or damaged tissue, reattaching or reanchoring ligaments, and closing the incision site to facilitate healing. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, improve range of motion, and restore function to the left elbow. Depending on the extent of the damage, the surgeon may also perform additional repairs or reconstructions to ensure optimal outcomes.
