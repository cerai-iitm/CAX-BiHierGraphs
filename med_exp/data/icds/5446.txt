Reposition Left Lower Femur with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left lower femur bone to its normal anatomical position, treating conditions such as fractures, deformities, or malunions. An open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. An intramedullary internal fixation device, such as a rod or nail, is inserted into the marrow canal of the femur to stabilize and align the bone. The device is secured in place with screws or bolts to allow the bone to heal in its correct position.
