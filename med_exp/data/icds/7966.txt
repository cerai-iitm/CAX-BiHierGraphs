Ultrasonography of Right and Left Heart, Intravascular
This non-invasive diagnostic test uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the heart's chambers, valves, and blood vessels, allowing for the assessment of cardiac structure and function. Intravascular ultrasound involves the insertion of a small probe through a blood vessel in the leg or arm, which is then guided to the heart to gather detailed information on heart condition. The procedure provides valuable information for diagnosing and monitoring various heart conditions, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, and valvular heart disease. Real-time images obtained during the test enable healthcare providers to evaluate blood flow, detect blockages, and measure vessel diameter with high accuracy.
