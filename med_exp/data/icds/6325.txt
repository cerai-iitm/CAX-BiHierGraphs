Excision of Right Kidney, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the right flank area to access the kidney, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the organ and surrounding tissues. The procedure requires careful dissection and ligation of blood vessels and ureter to minimize bleeding and prevent injury to surrounding structures. Once the kidney is freed from its attachments, it is carefully removed from the body, often along with surrounding fatty tissue and fascia. The incision is then closed, usually in layers, to promote healing and reduce the risk of complications.
