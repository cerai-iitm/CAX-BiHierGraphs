Dilation of Left Main Bronchus with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an intraluminal device to dilate the left main bronchus, a vital airway passage, to improve breathing and relieve obstruction. The procedure is performed endoscopically, where a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth or a tracheostomy, to access the bronchus. The intraluminal device is then guided through the endoscope to the narrowed or blocked section of the bronchus, where it is used to gently dilate the airway and restore normal airflow. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure patient safety.
