Reposition Right Metacarpophalangeal Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to reposition the right metacarpophalangeal joint, which is the joint that connects the metacarpal bone of the hand to the phalanges of the finger. This joint is realigned to its normal anatomical position to restore function and alleviate symptoms. An open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made to access the joint, and an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screw, is implanted to stabilize the joint and promote healing. The goal of the procedure is to improve range of motion, reduce pain, and enhance overall hand function.
