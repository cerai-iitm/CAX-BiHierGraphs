Occlusion of Right Internal Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the right internal carotid artery through a small incision in the groin area, followed by the insertion of a specialized intraluminal device to occlude the artery. The device is guided through the artery using fluoroscopic imaging to ensure accurate placement. Once in position, the device is deployed to block the flow of blood through the artery, which can help to treat conditions such as aneurysms or arteriovenous malformations. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and reduces recovery time, allowing patients to return to their normal activities shortly after the procedure.
