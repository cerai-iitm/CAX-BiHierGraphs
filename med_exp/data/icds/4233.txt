Repair Face Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an open incision to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the face, which are layers of soft tissue that lie beneath the skin. The goal is to repair damaged or torn tissue, fascia, or underlying structures, often as a result of trauma, injury, or surgical complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect and directly repair the affected area, restoring normal anatomy and functionality. The repair may involve suturing, stapling, or using other closure techniques to close the incision and promote healing.
