Reposition Left Finger Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a small incision is made in the skin to access the affected phalanx in the left finger. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a specialized device is inserted through the skin to reposition the phalanx into its normal anatomical alignment. The internal fixation device is then deployed to stabilize the bone, promoting proper healing and recovery. The percutaneous approach reduces tissue damage and scarring, allowing for a faster and less painful recovery.
