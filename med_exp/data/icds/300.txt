Bypass Coronary Artery, Two Arteries from Left Internal Mammary with Autologous Arterial Tissue, Open Approach
This coronary artery bypass grafting procedure involves harvesting the left internal mammary artery and using it to bypass two blocked coronary arteries, restoring blood flow to the heart. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the breastbone is divided to access the heart, and the autologous arterial tissue is grafted onto the blocked arteries. The internal mammary artery, which is typically used due to its durability and resistance to blockage, is typically anastomosed to the coronary arteries beyond the blockages. The goal of the procedure is to improve blood flow, reduce symptoms, and improve overall cardiac function.
