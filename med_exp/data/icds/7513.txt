Immobilization of Left Lower Arm using Splint
This procedure involves the application of a splint to immobilize the left lower arm, typically following a fracture or other injury. The splint is carefully positioned and secured to hold the arm in place, allowing for proper healing and reducing pain and discomfort. The immobilization process helps to maintain alignment and stability of the affected area, facilitating the recovery process. The splint is designed to provide support and protection to the left lower arm, promoting optimal healing and minimizing the risk of further injury.
