Replacement of Left Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This orthopedic procedure involves surgically replacing the femoral surface of the left hip joint with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, which requires a larger incision to access the joint. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate pain caused by damaged or diseased joint surfaces. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement and anatomy of the hip joint, providing a durable and long-lasting solution for patients. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully remove the damaged or diseased surfaces and implant the synthetic substitute, ensuring proper alignment and fit to promote optimal recovery and mobility.
