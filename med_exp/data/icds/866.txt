Replacement of Right Radial Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right radial artery with a biological substitute derived from the patient's own tissues, using an open approach. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's body and carefully grafted onto the affected area, restoring blood flow and functional integrity to the radial artery. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the surgical site, enabling precise dissection and implantation of the tissue substitute. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with radial artery damage or disease.
