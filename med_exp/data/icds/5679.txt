Fusion of 2 or more Cervical Vertebral Joints with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more cervical vertebral joints in the neck using a non-autologous tissue substitute, such as bone grafts or synthetic materials. The open approach allows the surgeon to access the affected area directly, providing a clear view of the spine. Through an anterior approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the front of the neck to access the cervical spine, reducing the risk of nerve damage. The non-autologous tissue substitute is then inserted to promote bone growth and stabilize the spine, alleviating pain and improving mobility.
