Excision of Neck, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the neck to access the underlying tissue, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove a portion of the neck tissue or lesion. The open approach enables direct visualization of the area, facilitating precise excision of the targeted tissue or growth. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, depending on the complexity of the case. Post-operative care involves close monitoring to minimize the risk of complications, such as infection or bleeding, and to promote optimal healing.
