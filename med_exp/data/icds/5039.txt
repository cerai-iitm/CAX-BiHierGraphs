Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Left Humeral Head, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the left shoulder area to access the humeral head, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove a synthetic substitute, such as a failed implant or prosthetic device. The open approach provides a direct view of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and extract the artificial material. The removal process is typically performed to alleviate pain, restore range of motion, and prevent further complications. Following the removal, the incision is closed, and the shoulder is stabilized to promote healing and facilitate rehabilitation.
