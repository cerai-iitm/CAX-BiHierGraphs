Excision of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the left lower lung lobe through a minimally invasive approach. A flexible endoscope is inserted through the skin to visualize the lung tissue, allowing the surgeon to precisely excise the affected lobe. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces tissue trauma and promotes faster healing compared to open surgery. The diagnostic excision is performed to diagnose and treat various lung conditions, such as lung cancer, pleurisy, or pulmonary embolism.
