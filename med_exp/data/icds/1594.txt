Extirpation of Matter from Portal Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of unwanted material or substances from the portal vein, a vital blood vessel responsible for transporting blood from the digestive organs to the liver. The percutaneous approach enables the doctor to access the portal vein through a small skin incision, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. Using imaging guidance, the physician inserts a specialized instrument or catheter through the incision to identify and extract the unwanted material, restoring normal blood flow and function to the portal vein. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, ensuring the patient's comfort throughout the process.
