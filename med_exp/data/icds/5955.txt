Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves joining or fusing the lumbosacral joint through a posterior approach, which means the incision is made in the lower back. The posterior column, the back part of the spine, is accessed to perform the fusion, and an open approach is used, requiring a larger incision to expose the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the spine and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or tingling in the lower back and legs. The fusion helps to stop the painful motion between the vertebrae, allowing for improved mobility and reduced discomfort.
