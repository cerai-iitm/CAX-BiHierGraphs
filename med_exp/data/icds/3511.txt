Removal of Drainage Device from Pancreas, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an external drainage device from the pancreas, which was previously inserted to drain fluid or abscesses. The external approach indicates that the procedure is performed through a small incision in the skin, allowing the surgeon to access the pancreas and remove the drainage device. The removal of the device is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize patient discomfort. After the procedure, the incision site is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
