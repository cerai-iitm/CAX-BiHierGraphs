Repair Neck, Stoma, External Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the neck to access the stoma, a surgically created opening in the neck that connects the trachea to the outside of the body, allowing for breathing. The goal of the repair is to restore the normal anatomy and function of the stoma, which may have been damaged or disrupted due to trauma, disease, or previous surgery. The surgical repair involves careful dissection and reconstruction of the stoma and surrounding tissues to ensure proper healing and function. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize and access the stoma, enabling precise repair and minimizing complications.
