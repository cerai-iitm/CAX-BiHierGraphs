Insertion of Biventricular Short-term External Heart Assist System into Heart, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a short-term external heart assist system into the heart through a percutaneous approach, where a thin catheter is inserted through the skin and guided to the heart. The device is designed to provide temporary mechanical support to both ventricles, helping to restore cardiac function and improve blood flow. The procedure is typically performed in emergency situations, such as cardiogenic shock or cardiac arrest, to help stabilize the patient until further treatment can be administered. The system is usually removed once the patient's heart function has improved or a more permanent solution, such as a ventricular assist device, can be implanted.
