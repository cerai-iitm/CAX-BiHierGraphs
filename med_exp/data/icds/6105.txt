Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Articulating Spacer, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically replacing the right hip joint with an articulating spacer through an open approach, which typically involves a larger incision to access the affected joint. The spacer is designed to restore joint function and provide temporary relief from pain and inflammation, often as a preliminary step before a permanent prosthetic replacement. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, facilitating the removal of damaged or infected tissue and ensuring a more accurate placement of the spacer. Following the procedure, patients typically require a period of recovery and rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility in the affected hip.
