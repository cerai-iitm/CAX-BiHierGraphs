Insertion of Pacemaker, Single Chamber into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This cardiac procedure involves surgically creating an incision in the chest to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where a single-chamber pacemaker is carefully inserted and secured in place. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the area and ensure proper placement of the device. The pacemaker is designed to regulate the heartbeat by sending electrical impulses to the heart, helping to restore a normal heart rhythm. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may take several hours to complete, depending on the individual's overall health and complexity of the case.
