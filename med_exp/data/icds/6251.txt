Change Drainage Device in Kidney, External Approach
During a surgical procedure, a healthcare provider accesses the kidney through an external incision to replace a faulty or infected drainage device, restoring proper kidney function and alleviating symptoms such as flank pain or urinary tract infections. The old device is carefully removed, and a new one is inserted to facilitate the drainage of waste and excess fluids from the kidney. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure the patient's safety. Post-operative care involves monitoring for potential complications and adjusting the new device to promote optimal kidney function.
