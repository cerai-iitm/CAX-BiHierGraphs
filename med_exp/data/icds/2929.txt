Excision of Peritoneum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove a portion of the peritoneum, a thin layer of tissue that lines the abdominal cavity, for diagnostic purposes. The objective is to examine the peritoneum and surrounding tissues to identify any abnormalities or diseases. The open approach requires a larger incision in the abdominal wall, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the peritoneum and perform the excision. The excised tissue is then sent for further examination, such as biopsy or histopathology, to aid in diagnosis and guide further treatment.
