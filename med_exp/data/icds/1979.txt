Extirpation of Matter from Nasal Mucosa and Soft Tissue, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the nasal mucosa and surrounding soft tissue through a natural or artificial opening. Using an endoscope, the surgeon visualizes the area to locate the matter that needs to be extirpated. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may involve the use of suction, irrigation, or specialized instruments to remove the matter. Once the material is removed, the area is cleaned and inspected to ensure complete removal, promoting healing and alleviating symptoms.
