Excision of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize and remove the right lower lung lobe through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, is inserted through the opening, allowing the surgeon to locate and excise the targeted lung tissue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to diagnose and treat conditions such as lung cancer, pulmonary sequestration, or severe lung disease. The minimally invasive approach reduces post-operative pain and scarring, promoting faster recovery and fewer complications.
