Dilation of Left Popliteal Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through the skin and guiding it to the left popliteal artery to expand the vessel using a balloon. A drug-eluting intraluminal device, such as a stent, is then placed inside the artery to maintain its dilation and prevent restenosis. The drug-eluting properties of the device allow for controlled release of medications to prevent tissue growth and maintain the artery's patency. This percutaneous approach enables treatment of peripheral artery disease and alleviates symptoms such as leg pain, numbness, and weakness.
