Supplement Vagina with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to guide the insertion of a synthetic vaginal substitute through the skin. The substitute material is carefully selected to mimic the natural vaginal tissue, promoting a natural feel and function. Once in place, the substitute is secured to promote optimal healing and tissue integration. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for minimal tissue disruption, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a speedy recovery.
