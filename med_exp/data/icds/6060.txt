Removal of Liner from Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
During an open surgery, a liner from the left knee joint is removed to alleviate pain and restore function. This procedure is typically performed to address issues such as wear and tear, infection, or loosening of the liner. The surgical approach involves making an incision in the knee area to access the joint and carefully removing the existing liner. The goal of this procedure is to prepare the joint for replacement with a new liner or to revise the joint to its natural state.
