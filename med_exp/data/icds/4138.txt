Extraction of Right Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue just beneath the skin. The goal of the procedure is to remove or excise the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which may be necessary to treat conditions such as infection, injury, or tumor. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and carefully remove the tissue and fascia. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed in a hospital setting by an orthopedic or general surgeon.
