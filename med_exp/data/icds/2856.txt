Excision of Right Large Intestine, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the right large intestine through an endoscopic approach, which uses a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end to visualize the inside of the intestine. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening in the body, such as the anus or a colostomy, and is typically done under general anesthesia or sedation. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and treat conditions such as cancer, polyps, or inflammatory bowel disease. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
