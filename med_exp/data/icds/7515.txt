Immobilization of Right Hand using Splint
This procedure involves the application of a splint to immobilize the right hand, providing stabilization and support to the affected area. The goal is to restrict movement and promote healing by maintaining a fixed position, often used to treat injuries or conditions such as fractures, sprains, or strains. The splint is carefully fitted and secured to the hand, allowing for proper alignment and minimizing the risk of further injury. By immobilizing the right hand, patients can recover more quickly and effectively, reducing pain and discomfort during the healing process.
