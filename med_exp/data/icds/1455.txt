Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Innominate Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to place an infusion device into the left innominate vein, which is a major blood vessel located in the upper chest. Under image guidance, a small incision is made in the skin and a thin tube called a cannula is inserted through the incision into the vein. The infusion device is then advanced through the cannula and positioned within the vein, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or nutrients. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and carries a low risk of complications, making it a safe and effective way to deliver necessary treatments.
