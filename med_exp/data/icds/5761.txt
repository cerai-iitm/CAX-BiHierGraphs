Release Left Shoulder Joint, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through a small incision in the skin to visualize the left shoulder joint, allowing the physician to release any scar tissue or adhesions that may be restricting movement or causing pain. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and promotes faster healing. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or sedation to ensure patient comfort. By releasing the joint, patients can experience improved range of motion, reduced pain, and enhanced overall shoulder function.
