Excision of Lower Esophagus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the lower esophagus through a minimally invasive, percutaneous endoscopic approach. During the procedure, an endoscope is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided to the lower esophagus, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the area and remove the affected tissue. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for smaller incisions and reduced tissue damage compared to open surgical procedures. The excised tissue is then removed through the endoscope or a small incision, minimizing trauma to the surrounding area.
