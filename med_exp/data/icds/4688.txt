Repair Right Lower Arm and Wrist Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right lower arm and wrist to access and repair damaged or torn tendons. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected tendons and perform the necessary repairs. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and movement to the wrist and forearm by reattaching or reinserting the tendons to their corresponding bones. The surgery typically requires careful dissection and manipulation of surrounding soft tissues to avoid further injury or complication.
