Excision of Pelvis Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the pelvic area to access the lymphatic system, allowing the surgeon to excise or remove affected lymph nodes or tissue. During the open approach, the surgical team gains direct visualization of the pelvic lymphatic system, enabling precise dissection and removal of the targeted tissue. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as lymphoma, melanoma, or other types of cancer that have spread to the pelvic lymph nodes. Following the excision, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for post-operative recovery.
