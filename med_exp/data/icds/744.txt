Excision of Left Temporal Artery, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This invasive diagnostic procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the left temporal artery, a blood vessel located on the side of the head. The artery is then carefully dissected and excised, allowing for the removal of a sample for further examination. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the artery and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the necessary tissue. The excised tissue is then sent for diagnostic testing to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of various conditions, such as vascular disorders or trauma.
