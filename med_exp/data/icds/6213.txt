Supplement Left Knee Joint with Liner, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves opening the left knee joint to supplement it with a liner, which is a medical device designed to replace or support damaged or diseased joint tissue. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the area and perform the supplementation. The liner is carefully placed within the joint to restore its natural function and alleviate pain or discomfort caused by arthritis, injury, or other conditions. By supplementing the left knee joint with a liner, the procedure aims to improve mobility, reduce stiffness, and enhance the overall quality of life for the patient.
