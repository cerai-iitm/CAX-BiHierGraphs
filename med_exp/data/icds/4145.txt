Extraction of Left Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves removing subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the left foot through small skin incisions, allowing for a minimally invasive approach to address issues such as chronic pain, swelling, or infection. The procedure typically begins with the administration of local anesthesia to numb the affected area, followed by the insertion of specialized instruments through the incisions to gently dissect and remove the targeted tissue. The removal of excess or damaged tissue can help to alleviate pressure and tension on surrounding structures, promoting healing and reducing discomfort. The small incisions are then closed, and the area is dressed to facilitate a smooth recovery.
