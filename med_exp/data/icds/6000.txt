Insertion of Spacer into Left Hip Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left hip joint to insert a spacer, typically used to maintain joint space and align the bone ends in preparation for a future total hip replacement surgery. The spacer is usually an antibiotic-impregnated cement spacer that helps to resolve infection and promote healthy bone growth. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the soft tissue and expose the joint, then insert the spacer into the left hip joint, taking care to ensure proper alignment and placement. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, restore function, and prepare the joint for a successful total hip replacement surgery in the future.
