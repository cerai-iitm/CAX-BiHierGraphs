Excision of Perineum Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the perineal region to access the affected muscle tissue. The perineal muscle is then carefully dissected and excised, with the goal of removing any diseased or damaged tissue. The surrounding tissue is meticulously closed, and the wound is sutured or stapled to promote healing. Post-operatively, pain management and wound care are crucial to minimize complications and ensure a speedy recovery.
