Bypass Coronary Artery, One Artery from Left Internal Mammary, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically bypassing a blockage in one coronary artery by grafting the left internal mammary artery to redirect blood flow, thereby restoring oxygenation to the heart muscle. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the heart and perform the bypass. The left internal mammary artery is harvested and connected to the blocked coronary artery, allowing blood to flow around the blockage and reducing the risk of heart attack. This invasive yet effective procedure is typically performed in patients with severe coronary artery disease to improve heart function and reduce symptoms.
