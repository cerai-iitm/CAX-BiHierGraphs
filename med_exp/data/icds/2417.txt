Resection of Left Lung, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of the left lung through an open incision, requiring general anesthesia and hospitalization. The operation typically begins with a thoracotomy, a surgical incision between the ribs, allowing the surgeon to access the left lung. The diseased or damaged lung tissue is then carefully resected, and the remaining lung tissue is repaired and closed. The incision is subsequently closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is closely monitored in the post-anesthesia care unit to ensure a smooth recovery.
