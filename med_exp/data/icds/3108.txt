Repair Stomach, Pylorus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the stomach and pylorus, a narrowing at the bottom of the stomach that connects to the small intestine. The goal of the operation is to repair any damage, defects, or abnormalities in the stomach and pylorus, which can be caused by various conditions such as ulcers, cancer, or trauma. The repair may involve removing damaged tissue, closing perforations, or reconstructing the stomach and pylorus to restore normal digestive function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary repairs under direct vision.
