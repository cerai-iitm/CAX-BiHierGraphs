Assistance with Respiratory Ventilation, 24-96 Consecutive Hours, Continuous Positive Airway Pressure
This medical procedure involves the continuous support of a patient's breathing for 24-96 consecutive hours, utilizing a technique that maintains a constant pressure in the airways to help keep them open. The patient receives supplemental oxygen through a mask or nasal interface, which helps to increase oxygen levels in the blood. During this period, healthcare professionals closely monitor the patient's breathing, oxygen saturation, and overall condition to ensure the treatment is effective and safe. The goal of this therapy is to improve oxygenation, reduce respiratory distress, and promote a stable and healthy breathing pattern.
