Insertion of Spacer into Lumbosacral Joint, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure based on the code and long title:

This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower back to access the lumbosacral joint, which connects the last vertebra of the spine to the sacrum. A spacer is then carefully implanted into the joint to help maintain proper alignment, relieve pressure, and promote healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the spacer and address any underlying issues. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically advised to rest and follow a rehabilitation program to facilitate recovery.
