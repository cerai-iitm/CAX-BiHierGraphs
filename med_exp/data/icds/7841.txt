Measurement of Cardiac Defibrillator, External Approach
This procedure involves the measurement of a cardiac defibrillator device that is external to the body, which is typically used in emergency situations to restore a normal heartbeat. The measurement process assesses the device's functionality and settings to ensure it is working correctly and providing the necessary therapeutic treatment. The external approach allows for non-invasive measurement, eliminating the need for surgical intervention. The goal of this measurement is to optimize the defibrillator's performance and provide effective treatment for cardiac arrhythmias.
