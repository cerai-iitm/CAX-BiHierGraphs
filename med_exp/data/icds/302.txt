Bypass Coronary Artery, Two Arteries from Coronary Artery, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves bypassing two blocked coronary arteries to restore blood flow to the heart. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart. The surgeon then harvests a healthy blood vessel, often from the leg or arm, and attaches it to the blocked coronary arteries, creating a detour for blood to flow around the blockages. The goal is to reduce symptoms of coronary artery disease, such as chest pain and shortness of breath, and improve overall heart health.
