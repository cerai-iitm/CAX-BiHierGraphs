Reposition Right Fibula with External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right fibula bone to its correct anatomical position using an external fixation device, which is a stabilizing system consisting of pins or screws connected to a external frame. The percutaneous approach means that the procedure is performed through small skin incisions, minimizing tissue damage and reducing recovery time. The external fixation device is attached to the bone through these small incisions, allowing for precise control and gradual repositioning of the fibula. Once the bone is in its correct position, the external fixation device is left in place for several weeks to ensure proper healing and stability.
