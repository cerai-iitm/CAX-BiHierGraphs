Reposition Left Tibia, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves realigning the left tibia bone to its correct anatomical position through an external approach. During the operation, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the tibia, and then manually or using specialized instruments repositions the bone to restore proper alignment and relieve any associated pain or discomfort. The goal of the procedure is to improve mobility, strength, and overall function of the affected leg. Post-operatively, the patient may require immobilization and rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and prevent complications.
