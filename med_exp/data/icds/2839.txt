Excision of Jejunum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This laparoscopic procedure involves making small incisions in the abdomen to access the jejunum, a section of the small intestine. Through an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, the affected portion of the jejunum is visually examined and the diseased or damaged segment is carefully excised. The excision is typically performed using specialized instruments and electrocautery, and the remaining healthy sections of the jejunum are then reattached. The minimally invasive approach reduces recovery time and post-operative complications, allowing patients to return to normal activities sooner.
