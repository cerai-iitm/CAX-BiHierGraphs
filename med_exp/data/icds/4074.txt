Excision of Left Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left upper leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The subcutaneous tissue, which is the layer of tissue just beneath the skin, and the fascia, a layer of connective tissue, are then carefully excised, or cut out, from the affected area. This excision is typically performed to remove damaged or diseased tissue, such as in cases of skin cancer or trauma. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and remove the targeted tissue with precision.
