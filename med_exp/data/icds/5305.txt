Insertion of Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device into Right Tibia, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive surgical procedure, a slender, rod-like internal fixation device is carefully inserted through a small incision in the skin, into the marrow cavity of the right tibia, or shinbone, to stabilize and align the bone. This percutaneous approach enables the surgeon to access the affected area with minimal tissue damage and trauma. The internal fixation device is designed to provide support and stability to the tibia, facilitating the healing process and promoting optimal bone growth. Once in place, the device helps to restore normal function and mobility to the affected leg.
