Reposition Right Finger Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning a misaligned phalanx bone in the right finger, typically performed to treat conditions such as bone fractures or dislocations. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the bone into its correct alignment. Once aligned, an internal fixation device is implanted to hold the bone in place while it heals, promoting stability and ensuring proper fusion. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal finger function, alleviate pain, and improve overall hand mobility.
