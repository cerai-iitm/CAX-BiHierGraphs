Resection of Right Adrenal Gland, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the right adrenal gland, a small triangular-shaped gland located on top of the right kidney. The adrenal gland is carefully dissected and removed from its attachments to surrounding structures, and then the gland is excised. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as adrenal cancer, Cushing's syndrome, or pheochromocytoma. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the gland and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the gland while minimizing damage to nearby tissues.
