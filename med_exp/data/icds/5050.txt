Repair Sternum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the sternum, followed by manual repair of any fractures, defects, or deformities using various techniques and materials such as wires, plates, or screws. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the sternum, enabling the surgeon to precisely restore its anatomical structure and function. The repair may involve realigning and stabilizing the sternum, as well as reattaching surrounding tissues and muscles to promote proper healing and minimize complications. The goal of this procedure is to restore chest stability, alleviate symptoms such as pain and breathing difficulties, and improve overall quality of life.
