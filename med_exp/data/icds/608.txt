Supplement Pericardium with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this open approach procedure, the pericardium is supplemented with a biological tissue substitute derived from the patient's own body. The autologous tissue substitute is used to repair or rebuild the pericardial sac that surrounds the heart, which may have been damaged due to infection, injury, or disease. The surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the pericardium and carefully shapes and secures the autologous tissue substitute in place. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal pericardial function, alleviate symptoms, and improve cardiac function.
