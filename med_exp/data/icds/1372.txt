Restriction of Left Internal Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to restrict blood flow through the left internal iliac artery using an intraluminal device inserted through a small incision in the skin. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a catheter is navigated through the femoral artery to reach the target site, and the device is deployed to occlude the artery. This procedure is often used to treat conditions such as pelvic congestion syndrome or arteriovenous malformations. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery.
