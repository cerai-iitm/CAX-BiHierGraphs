Pheresis of Leukocytes, Single
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This therapeutic procedure involves the withdrawal of blood from a donor or patient, followed by the separation of leukocytes, also known as white blood cells, from the rest of the blood components. The isolated leukocytes are then removed, and the remaining blood components are returned to the donor or patient. This process is often performed to treat leukemias, autoimmune disorders, and other conditions where the removal of excess white blood cells is necessary. The goal of this procedure is to reduce the number of leukocytes in the bloodstream, which can help alleviate symptoms and improve overall health.
