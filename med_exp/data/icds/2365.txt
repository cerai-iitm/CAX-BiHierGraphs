Removal of Drainage Device from Pleura, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the external removal of a drainage device from the pleura, a delicate membrane surrounding the lungs. The goal of this intervention is to eliminate the device that was previously inserted to drain fluid, air, or blood from the pleural space. During the procedure, the physician makes an incision in the chest wall to access the drainage device, carefully disconnects it, and removes it from the pleural cavity. The removal of the drainage device allows the pleural space to heal and return to its normal function, promoting the patient's recovery and alleviating symptoms such as chest pain and difficulty breathing.
