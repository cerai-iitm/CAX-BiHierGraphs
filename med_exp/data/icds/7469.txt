Change Pressure Dressing on Abdominal Wall
This procedure involves the removal of the existing pressure dressing from the abdominal wall, followed by a thorough inspection of the wound site for signs of infection, necrosis, or other complications. The wound is then cleaned and prepared for the application of a new pressure dressing, which is securely fastened in place to promote healing and prevent further trauma. The new dressing is carefully selected to accommodate the wound's specific needs, taking into account factors such as its size, depth, and drainage requirements. Throughout the process, the healthcare provider takes precautions to minimize discomfort and prevent cross-contamination.
