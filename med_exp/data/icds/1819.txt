Resection of Pelvis Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the pelvic lymphatic system through a small incision in the skin. Under image guidance, a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to remove a portion of the pelvis lymphatic tissue, allowing for diagnostic sampling and potentially treating conditions such as lymphedema or cancer. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery. The resected tissue is then sent to a laboratory for examination, providing valuable information for further treatment and management.
