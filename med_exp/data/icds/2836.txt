Excision of Jejunum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the jejunum, a section of the small intestine. The surgeon then carefully removes a portion of the jejunum to examine it for abnormalities or disease. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area and enables the surgical team to collect tissue samples for further testing. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to identify the underlying cause of symptoms such as abdominal pain, weight loss, or intestinal obstruction.
