Fusion of Thoracolumbar Vertebral Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of thoracolumbar vertebral joints using a synthetic substitute, which is performed through a posterior approach. The posterior column of the spine is accessed through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the affected area. The synthetic substitute, such as a cage or implant, is then inserted into the joint space to promote bone growth and stabilize the spine. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as degenerative disc disease, spinal stenosis, or spondylolisthesis, and is often combined with other procedures to achieve optimal results.
