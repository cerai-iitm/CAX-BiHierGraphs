Immobilization of Right Lower Leg using Cast
During this procedure, a healthcare provider applies a cast to the right lower leg to immobilize it, stabilizing the affected area and preventing further injury. The cast is typically made of plaster or fiberglass and is molded to fit the shape of the leg, providing support and protection. The immobilization process helps to reduce pain and inflammation, promote healing, and improve mobility in the affected leg. The cast is usually left in place for several weeks, depending on the severity of the injury or condition, and is regularly monitored by a healthcare provider for proper fit and effectiveness.
