Replacement of Right Knee Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the damaged or diseased right knee joint is replaced with an artificial joint made of synthetic materials, using a cemented fixation method to secure the implant in place. The open approach involves a single, larger incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the replacement. The synthetic substitute is carefully positioned and secured to the surrounding bone, restoring the natural alignment and function of the knee joint. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor recovery and manage potential complications.
