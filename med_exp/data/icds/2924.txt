Excision of Mesentery, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the mesentery, which is a fold of tissue that attaches the small intestine to the back of the abdominal wall. The surgeon then carefully excises, or removes, a portion of the mesentery for diagnostic purposes, such as to examine it for signs of disease or injury. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area and allows the surgeon to assess the mesentery and surrounding tissues more accurately. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
