Occlusion of Rectum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the rectum, with the goal of intentionally blocking or closing off the rectum to prevent the passage of stool or other substances. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the rectal area and directly perform the occlusion. The procedure may be necessary to treat conditions such as rectal trauma, rectal prolapse, or cancer, and is often performed in emergency situations. A successful occlusion can help alleviate symptoms and prevent further complications associated with rectal dysfunction.
