Reposition Left Femoral Shaft with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of a fractured left femoral shaft, which is the long bone in the upper leg, to its normal anatomical position. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to realign the bone fragments. An intramedullary internal fixation device, such as a rod or nail, is then inserted into the medullary canal of the femur to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The fixation device is designed to provide strength and support to the bone as it heals, allowing the patient to regain mobility and function in the affected leg.
