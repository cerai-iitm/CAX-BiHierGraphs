Replacement of Right Shoulder Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right shoulder joint with a synthetic substitute using an open approach, where the surgeon makes a larger incision to access the joint. The damaged or diseased joint is removed and replaced with an artificial joint made of materials such as metal, ceramic, or plastic. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural joint's function, allowing for a range of motion and reducing pain and discomfort. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the joint and surrounding tissue, ensuring a precise and accurate replacement.
