Excision of Left Lower Arm and Wrist Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower arm and wrist to access the underlying muscle tissue. The damaged or diseased muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate symptoms and improve functional ability. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and pain. Following excision, the incision is closed, and the arm is immobilized to promote healing and facilitate a smooth recovery.
