Excision of Bilateral Fallopian Tubes, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both fallopian tubes through an open incision in the abdomen. The goal of this procedure is to prevent pregnancy or treat conditions such as ectopic pregnancy or cancer. During the operation, the surgeon makes a large incision in the lower abdomen to access the fallopian tubes, which are then carefully identified and severed from their connections to the uterus and ovaries. The tubes are then removed, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples.
