Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Internal Carotid Arteries using High Osmolar Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of high osmolar contrast material to visualize the bilateral internal carotid arteries. Fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, is used to guide the injection of contrast material into the arteries, allowing for real-time imaging of blood flow and vessel anatomy. The high osmolar contrast material enhances the visibility of the arteries and their branches, enabling healthcare providers to assess blood flow and identify any blockages or abnormalities. The procedure is commonly used to diagnose and monitor conditions such as cerebral vasospasm, carotid stenosis, and arterial occlusion.
