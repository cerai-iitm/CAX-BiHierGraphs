Excision of Left Carpal Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to excise the left carpal joint, which is a complex joint in the wrist that connects the hand to the forearm. During the procedure, an incision is made in the wrist to access the joint, and the damaged or diseased joint tissue is carefully removed. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain and restore function to the wrist and hand by removing the affected joint tissue. The surgery typically requires a period of postoperative recovery and rehabilitation to ensure optimal outcomes.
