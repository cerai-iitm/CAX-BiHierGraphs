Excision of Left Internal Mammary Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to access the left internal mammary lymphatic vessels. The affected lymphatic tissue is then carefully dissected and removed to prevent the spread of disease or to treat a condition such as lymphedema. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the excision with precision. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal lymphatic function and alleviate symptoms such as swelling, pain, and infection.
