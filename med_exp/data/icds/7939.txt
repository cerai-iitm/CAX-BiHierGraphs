Fluoroscopy of Multiple Coronary Artery Bypass Grafts using Other Contrast
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this procedure, a low-dose X-ray technology called fluoroscopy is used to visualize multiple coronary artery bypass grafts, which are surgically created pathways that redirect blood flow around blocked or narrowed coronary arteries. Other contrast agents, such as carbon dioxide or ultrasound, are inserted into the bloodstream to enhance visibility of the grafts and surrounding vessels. The fluoroscopic images guide the healthcare team in evaluating the patency and integrity of the grafts, identifying any potential blockages or stenosis. This diagnostic procedure helps inform treatment decisions and monitor the effectiveness of previous surgical interventions.
