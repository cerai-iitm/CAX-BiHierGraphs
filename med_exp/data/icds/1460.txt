Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Brachial Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A percutaneous procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into the right brachial vein, allowing for the administration of medications or fluids directly into the bloodstream. The brachial vein is accessed through a small incision in the skin, and the infusion device is guided into place using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy. The infusion device is designed to provide long-term vascular access, reducing the need for frequent needle sticks and improving patient comfort. Once in place, the infusion device can be used to deliver medications, nutrients, or fluids over an extended period.
