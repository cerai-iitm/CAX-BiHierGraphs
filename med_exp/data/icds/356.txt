Dilation of Coronary Artery, One Artery, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to widen a narrowed or blocked coronary artery, restoring blood flow to the heart. A catheter is inserted through a small incision in the skin and guided through the artery to the blockage, where a balloon is inflated to compress the plaque and widen the artery. Once the artery is dilated, the balloon is deflated and removed, allowing for improved blood flow. This procedure is often performed in conjunction with other interventions, such as stenting, to ensure optimal outcomes.
