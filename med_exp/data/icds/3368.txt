Drainage of Pancreatic Duct with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device into the pancreatic duct to alleviate obstruction or blockage. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for direct access to the duct. The drainage device is designed to restore normal pancreatic juice flow, reducing symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. Under endoscopic guidance, the drainage device is precisely placed to provide effective drainage and promote healing.
