Introduction of Destructive Agent into Upper GI, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a needle through the skin and into the upper gastrointestinal tract to deliver a destructive agent, such as ethanol or acetic acid, to treat lesions or tumors. Under imaging guidance, the healthcare provider carefully targets the affected area to ensure accurate delivery of the agent. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and is often used to treat conditions such as bleeding ulcers or cancer. By introducing the destructive agent directly into the affected area, the procedure aims to reduce bleeding, shrink tumors, or alleviate symptoms.
