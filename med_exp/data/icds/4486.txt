Release Left Abdomen Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left abdomen to access the affected muscle, followed by manual release of the muscle to alleviate tension or adhesions. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the muscle and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the underlying issue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and relaxation. Post-operative care involves monitoring for potential complications and promoting wound healing to facilitate a successful recovery.
