Drainage of Buttock Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the buttock to drain accumulated fluid, pus, or blood that has collected underneath the skin. The external approach allows the healthcare provider to access the affected area directly, enabling them to effectively drain the fluid and alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and redness. The goal of the procedure is to promote healing, prevent further infection, and restore normal skin function. Depending on the severity of the condition, the procedure may be performed under local or general anesthesia, and antibiotics may be administered to prevent infection.
