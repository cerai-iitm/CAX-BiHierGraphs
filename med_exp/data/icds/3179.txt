Resection of Duodenum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the duodenum, a section of the small intestine that receives bile and pancreatic juices to aid in digestion. The affected portion of the duodenum is then carefully removed, and the remaining sections are reconnected to restore normal digestive function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the duodenum and surrounding tissues, enabling precise dissection and removal of the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as tumors, injuries, or blockages that are causing symptoms including abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
