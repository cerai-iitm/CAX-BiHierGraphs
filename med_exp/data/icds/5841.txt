Drainage of Right Hip Joint with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the right hip joint, followed by the insertion of a drainage device to remove excess fluid, blood, or infected material that may have accumulated in the joint. The goal of this open approach is to relieve pressure, reduce pain, and promote healing in the affected joint. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully navigate the complex anatomy of the hip joint to ensure the drainage device is correctly positioned and functioning properly. After the device is in place, the incision is typically closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any signs of complications or infection.
