Release Sciatic Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the sciatic nerve, which is a major nerve that runs from the lower back down to the legs, and releasing any compression or entrapment that may be causing pain, numbness, or weakness in the lower extremities. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure on the nerve and restore normal nerve function. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully identifies the compressed or entrapped portion of the nerve and releases it from surrounding tissues, bone, or other structures that may be causing compression. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may be recommended for patients with sciatica or other conditions that cause sciatic nerve compression.
