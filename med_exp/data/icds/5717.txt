Fusion of Thoracolumbar Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the joints of the thoracolumbar spine using a patient's own tissue substitute, accessed through a posterior approach, to stabilize the posterior column of the spine. The open approach requires a surgical incision to expose the affected area, allowing the surgeon to implant the autologous tissue substitute to facilitate fusion. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and instability caused by spinal disorders, such as degenerative disc disease or spinal fractures, by promoting bone growth and fusion between adjacent vertebrae. By stabilizing the spine, this procedure can help restore mobility and improve overall quality of life for patients suffering from thoracolumbar spinal conditions.
