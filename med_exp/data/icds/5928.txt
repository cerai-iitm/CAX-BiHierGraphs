Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing a lumbar vertebral joint together using an interbody fusion device, accessed through a posterior approach. The posterior column of the spine is stabilized through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area. The interbody fusion device, typically a cage or spacer, is implanted into the disc space to restore the natural height and alignment of the lumbar spine, promoting bone growth and fusion. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as degenerative disc disease, spondylolisthesis, or spinal stenosis, and can help to alleviate back pain, numbness, or weakness in the legs.
