Inspection of Retroperitoneum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to visually examine the retroperitoneum, a space in the back of the abdominal cavity that contains organs such as the kidneys, adrenal glands, and pancreas. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly inspect the area for any abnormalities, injuries, or diseases, such as cancer or inflammation. The procedure may be performed to diagnose or treat conditions affecting the retroperitoneum, including tumors, abscesses, or bleeding. During the procedure, the surgeon may also collect tissue samples for further examination or perform any necessary repairs or interventions.
