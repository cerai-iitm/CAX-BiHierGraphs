Extraction of Products of Conception, Low, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of products of conception from the uterus through an open incision in the lower uterus. The procedure is typically performed to complete a miscarriage, remove remaining tissue after a miscarriage, or to remove fetal remains after a failed pregnancy. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the uterus and surrounding tissues, enabling precise removal of the products of conception. The goal of the procedure is to prevent infection, bleeding, and other complications that can occur when fetal tissue remains in the uterus.
