Inspection of Pancreatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During an endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or a small incision to visualize the pancreatic duct. The pancreatic duct is a tubular structure that carries digestive enzymes from the pancreas to the small intestine, and inspection of this duct is crucial for diagnosing and treating conditions such as pancreatitis or pancreatic cancer. The endoscope allows the healthcare provider to directly visualize the duct and identify any blockages, narrowing, or abnormalities. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and can help guide further treatment or intervention.
