Repair Abdomen Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of connective tissue that surround and support various organs. The goal of the repair is to restore the integrity of these tissue layers, which may have been damaged due to injury, infection, or previous surgery. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the affected area, remove any damaged or infected tissue, and repair or reconstruct the subcutaneous tissue and fascia using sutures or other materials. The repair is typically done in an open approach, where the incision is made to directly visualize the affected area and facilitate the repair process.
