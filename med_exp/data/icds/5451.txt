Reposition Left Lower Femur, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left lower femur, also known as the thigh bone, to its normal anatomical position using an external approach. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the femur, and then manually repositions it to improve alignment and mobility. Post-operatively, the patient may require rehabilitation to restore strength and range of motion in the affected leg.
