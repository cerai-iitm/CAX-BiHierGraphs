Extirpation of Matter from Right External Carotid Artery, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the neck to access the right external carotid artery, allowing the surgeon to physically remove or extirpate unwanted material, such as a blood clot or plaque, that is obstructing blood flow. The open approach enables direct visualization of the artery, facilitating the precise removal of the harmful substance. The procedure requires careful dissection and retraction of surrounding tissues to expose the affected artery segment. Once the obstruction is removed, the incision is closed, and the blood flow is restored to normal.
