Drainage of Right Middle Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the mouth or nose to access the right middle lung lobe, allowing for visualization and drainage of accumulated fluid or pus. The endoscope is guided through natural or artificial openings, such as the trachea or a surgical incision, to reach the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and treat conditions such as pneumonia, abscesses, or effusions that may be causing respiratory distress or other symptoms. By draining the accumulated fluid, the procedure aims to alleviate symptoms, promote healing, and improve lung function.
