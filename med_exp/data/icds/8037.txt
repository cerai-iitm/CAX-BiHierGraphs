Fluoroscopy of Left Upper Extremity Arteries using Other Contrast
During this procedure, a specialized X-ray machine uses continuous beams of X-rays to guide the insertion of a catheter into the left upper extremity arteries. A contrast agent, other than iodine or barium, is injected through the catheter to highlight the arteries and improve visibility. The fluoroscope displays real-time images of the arteries, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the flow of blood and diagnose any blockages or abnormalities. The procedure helps diagnose conditions such as peripheral artery disease, aneurysms, or arteriovenous malformations in the left upper extremity.
