Detachment at Right 2nd Toe, High, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically detaching the right 2nd toe at a high level, near the base of the toe, using an open approach. This means that the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the toe and perform the detachment. The goal of this procedure is often to treat conditions such as severe toe deformities, infections, or injuries that cannot be managed with more conservative treatments. The detachment allows for the removal of the affected toe, which can improve overall foot function and alleviate pain.
