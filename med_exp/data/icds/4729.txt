Transfer Right Foot Tendon, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the damaged or diseased tendon in the right foot is replaced with a healthy tendon from another part of the body or from a donor. The surgeon makes an incision in the right foot to access the affected tendon, carefully removes the damaged tissue, and then transfers the healthy tendon to restore normal function and mobility. The transferred tendon is then secured in place using sutures or other fixation devices to promote healing and stability. Post-operatively, the patient may require physical therapy and rehabilitation to regain strength and range of motion in the affected foot.
