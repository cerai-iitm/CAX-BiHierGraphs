Restriction of Splenic Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small device through the skin to restrict blood flow in the splenic artery, which supplies the spleen. A thin catheter is guided through an artery in the leg or arm to the splenic artery, where the device is deployed to narrow the artery. This reduction in blood flow can help to shrink the spleen, reducing the risk of bleeding or rupture, particularly in individuals with cirrhosis or other conditions that cause splenomegaly. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate device placement and minimize complications.
