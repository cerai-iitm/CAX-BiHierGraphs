Release Left Vitreous, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous release of the left vitreous is a minimally invasive surgical procedure that involves creating a small incision in the sclera, the white part of the eye, to access the vitreous humor. This procedure is usually performed to treat conditions such as vitreomacular adhesion or macular hole, where the vitreous gel is pulling on the retina. Through a small instrument, the surgeon gently dissolves or removes the vitreous gel to release the traction on the retina, allowing it to reattach and heal. This percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to the eye and promotes rapid recovery.
