Excision of Left Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left upper leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The subcutaneous tissue, which is the layer of tissue just beneath the skin, and the fascia, a layer of connective tissue, are then carefully excised, or removed, for diagnostic purposes. This may be necessary to examine the tissue for signs of disease, injury, or other abnormal conditions. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the tissue and fascia, enabling a more comprehensive examination and diagnosis.
