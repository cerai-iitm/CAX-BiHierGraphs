Fluoroscopy of Right Vertebral Artery using Other Contrast
This fluoroscopically-guided procedure involves the injection of a contrast medium, other than standard iodine-based contrast, into the right vertebral artery to opacity its lumen and highlight any abnormalities. Under continuous X-ray visualization, the flow of the contrast agent is tracked as it moves through the artery, allowing for real-time assessment of blood vessel patency and potential areas of stenosis or occlusion. The procedure is typically performed to diagnose and monitor vascular diseases, such as atherosclerosis, aneurysms, or arteriovenous malformations, affecting the right vertebral artery. By using fluoroscopy, the interventional radiologist can precisely guide the contrast injection and visualize the artery's anatomy in detail.
