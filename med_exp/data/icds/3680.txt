Excision of Left Upper Arm Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the left upper arm to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to excise damaged or diseased skin tissue. The external approach enables direct visualization of the skin and underlying structures, facilitating precise removal of the targeted tissue. The procedure may be performed to treat various skin conditions, such as skin cancer, or to repair skin defects resulting from injury or trauma. Following excision, the skin is closed, and the wound is managed to promote optimal healing and minimize scarring.
