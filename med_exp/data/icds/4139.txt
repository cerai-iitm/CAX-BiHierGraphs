Extraction of Right Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the removal of diseased or damaged subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the right lower leg. A small incision is made in the skin, allowing for the insertion of specialized instruments to extract the affected tissue and fascia. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as cellulitis, necrotizing fasciitis, or chronic wounds. The goal of the procedure is to promote healing, reduce the risk of infection, and improve overall lower leg function.
