Removal of Other Device from Left Lower Extremity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left lower extremity to access and remove a device that is no longer needed or is causing complications. The device may have been implanted previously for various reasons, such as fracture fixation, joint replacement, or soft tissue repair. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the tissue to expose the device, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves and blood vessels. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
