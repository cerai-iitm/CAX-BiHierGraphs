Drainage of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device or catheter into the left upper lung lobe through a natural or artificial opening to remove excess fluid or air from the lung tissue. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to identify the cause of respiratory symptoms or disease, such as pulmonary edema, pneumothorax, or pleural effusion. A chest tube may be inserted through the skin and into the pleural space to facilitate drainage, and imaging guidance such as fluoroscopy or CT scan may be used to ensure accurate placement. Following the procedure, the patient may require ongoing monitoring and further treatment based on the underlying diagnosis.
