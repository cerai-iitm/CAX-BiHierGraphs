Replacement of Left Knee Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left knee joint with a synthetic substitute, utilizing a cemented open approach. The procedure begins with an open incision, allowing the surgeon to access the affected joint. The damaged or diseased joint is then removed and replaced with a synthetic implant, which is secured in place using bone cement. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal knee function and alleviate pain and discomfort associated with joint damage or disease.
