Supplement Intracranial Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves accessing the intracranial artery through an open approach, where a craniotomy is performed to expose the targeted blood vessel. The goal is to supplement the artery with an autologous tissue substitute, which is derived from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection or immunological response. The substitute is carefully implanted to reinforce the artery, promoting healthy blood flow and mitigating the risk of rupture or occlusion. The procedure requires precise technical skill and meticulous attention to detail to ensure successful outcomes and minimize complications.
