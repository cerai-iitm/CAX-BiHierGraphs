Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Basilic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This medical procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device into the left basilic vein, a superficial vein located on the medial aspect of the upper arm. The percutaneous approach is used, which means that the device is inserted through the skin using a needle and guiding catheter. The basilic vein is chosen as it provides a safe and accessible route for the infusion device, allowing for efficient delivery of medications or fluids. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and is commonly used for patients requiring long-term intravenous therapy.
