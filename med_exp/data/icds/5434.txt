Reposition Left Femoral Shaft with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
During this procedure, the surgeon manipulates the left femoral shaft back into its normal anatomical position through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, using image guidance to ensure precise alignment. Next, an internal fixation device, such as a rod or plate, is inserted through the skin and soft tissue to stabilize the femur, providing structural support and promoting healing. The internal fixation device is carefully positioned to hold the bone in place, allowing for optimal recovery and minimizing the risk of further injury or complications. The entire process is typically performed under general anesthesia or sedation to ensure the patient's comfort and safety.
