Reposition Right Ankle Joint, Open Approach
During an open repositioning procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the right ankle joint. The joint is then manipulated to restore its normal alignment, which may involve realigning the bones or repairing surrounding soft tissue structures. Once the joint is repositioned, the incision is closed, and the ankle is immobilized to allow for healing. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as ankle fractures or dislocations that require surgical intervention to restore proper joint function and alleviate pain.
