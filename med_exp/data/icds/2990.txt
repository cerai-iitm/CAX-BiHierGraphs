Insertion of Feeding Device into Duodenum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of a feeding device, such as a duodenal tube, through a natural or artificial opening into the duodenum, which is the first part of the small intestine. The feeding device is carefully guided through the opening and into the duodenum, allowing for nutrition to be delivered directly into the small intestine. This procedure is often performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the device. The inserted feeding device can remain in place for an extended period, providing long-term nutrition to individuals who have difficulty consuming adequate nutrients orally.
