Dilation of Common Bile Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the common bile duct and identify any blockages or narrowing. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth and guided through the digestive tract to the ampulla of Vater, where the common bile duct opens. Once the blockage is identified, specialized instruments are used to dilate the duct, allowing for normal bile flow and relief from symptoms such as jaundice and abdominal pain. The dilation procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete.
