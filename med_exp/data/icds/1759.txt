Excision of Aortic Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a lymphatic structure or tissue in the aortic region through a small incision in the skin, guided by endoscopic visualization. The endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, is inserted through the incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the excision. This approach reduces tissue trauma, promotes faster healing, and minimizes scarring compared to traditional open surgical methods. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort during the operation.
