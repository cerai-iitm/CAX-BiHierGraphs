Excision of Right Shoulder Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the right shoulder muscle. The affected muscle tissue is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate symptoms such as pain, weakness, or limited mobility. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the muscle and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the affected tissue. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as muscle tears, tumors, or infections, and can help restore normal function and mobility to the shoulder joint.
