Supplement Spinal Meninges with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a non-autologous tissue substitute, typically a biomaterial or biologic graft, is implanted to supplement the spinal meninges, which are the protective membranes surrounding the spinal cord. The open approach involves making a small incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and repair the spinal meninges. The tissue substitute is carefully placed to support and reinforce the natural tissue, promoting healing and restoring function. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as meningitis, trauma, or congenital abnormalities that have damaged the spinal meninges.
