Excision of Cervicothoracic Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the cervical and thoracic regions of the spine, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove a herniated or damaged vertebral disc that is compressing the spinal cord or nerve roots. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure on the affected spinal structures, alleviate pain and discomfort, and restore normal spinal function. The surgeon will use specialized instruments to carefully remove the problematic disc tissue, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues or structures. Post-operatively, the patient may require rehabilitation and physical therapy to regain strength and mobility in the affected area.
