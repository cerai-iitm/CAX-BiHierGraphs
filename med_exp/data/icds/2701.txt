Dilation of Large Intestine, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to dilate the large intestine through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for the visualization and treatment of obstructions or narrowed areas. The endoscope is inserted through the anus or a surgical opening, and specialized instruments are used to dilate the intestine, relieving blockages and facilitating the passage of stool. This minimally invasive procedure is often performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may be used to treat conditions such as colon cancer, Crohn's disease, or diverticulitis. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bowel function and improve the patient's quality of life.
