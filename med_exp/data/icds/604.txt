Supplement Left Ventricle with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a piece of zooplastic tissue, derived from animal sources, is implanted to supplement the left ventricle of the heart. This tissue is used to enhance the ventricle's functionality and improve its ability to pump blood efficiently. The open approach involves a traditional incision in the chest, allowing the surgeon to directly access and visualize the heart and surrounding tissues. The zooplastic tissue is carefully sutured into place, ensuring a secure and stable attachment to the left ventricle.
