Repair Nasal Mucosa and Soft Tissue, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repairing damaged or defective nasal mucosa and soft tissue through an external approach, often performed to address issues such as nasal trauma, congenital defects, or tumor removal. The surgical site is accessed through an incision made on the external surface of the nose, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal nasal function, alleviate symptoms such as nasal obstruction or bleeding, and improve cosmetic appearance. The repair may involve suturing, grafting, or other techniques to restore the integrity of the nasal mucosa and soft tissue.
