Repair Esophagogastric Junction, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through the mouth to visualize the esophagogastric junction, allowing the physician to repair any defects or damage in the area where the esophagus meets the stomach. The procedure is performed percutaneously, meaning through the skin, under image guidance to ensure precise repair. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal functioning of the esophagogastric junction, alleviating symptoms such as heartburn, regurgitation, and difficulty swallowing. The percutaneous endoscopic approach offers a less invasive alternative to traditional open surgery, resulting in reduced recovery time and fewer complications.
