Extirpation of Matter from Right Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of abnormal or diseased tissue or material from the right lower lung lobe through an endoscope, which is inserted via a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that allow the surgeon to visualize and extract the unwanted material, promoting healing and improving lung function. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as lung abscesses, tumors, or pneumonia, and is typically done under general anesthesia. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal lung function and alleviate symptoms such as coughing, chest pain, and shortness of breath.
