Reposition Left Tibia with External Fixation Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning the left tibia, a bone in the lower leg, to its proper alignment. An external fixation device is attached to the bone through an open incision, providing stability and support during the healing process. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissue, enabling precise repositioning and fixation. The external fixation device remains in place for several weeks or months, allowing the bone to heal in its new position.
