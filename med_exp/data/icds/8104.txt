Fluoroscopy of Other Intra-Abdominal Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This imaging procedure involves the use of low osmolar contrast material to visualize other intra-abdominal arteries beyond the hepatic, splenic, and renal arteries. Fluoroscopy guidance enables real-time monitoring during the procedure, which is essential for precise placement of the contrast agent. The low osmolar contrast material is injected through a catheter inserted into an artery, allowing for clear visualization of the vessel anatomy and any potential abnormalities. The resulting images help healthcare providers diagnose and treat conditions affecting the intra-abdominal arteries, such as stenosis, occlusion, or aneurysms.
