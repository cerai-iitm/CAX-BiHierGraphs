Repair Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for visualization of the esophagus using an endoscope. The esophagus is repaired using specialized instruments and techniques to restore its integrity and function. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. Post-procedure, patients may experience some discomfort and may be advised to follow a soft food diet to facilitate healing.
