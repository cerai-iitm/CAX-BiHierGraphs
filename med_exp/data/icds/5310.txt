Insertion of Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device into Left Tibia, Open Approach
In an open surgical approach, a surgeon makes an incision in the left tibia to access the medullary canal, allowing for the insertion of an intramedullary internal fixation device. This device is designed to provide stability and support to the tibia, facilitating the healing process and restoring function to the affected leg. The procedure typically involves reaming the canal to prepare it for the device, followed by its insertion and securing it in place with screws or bolts. Post-operatively, the patient will require immobilization and rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and prevent complications.
