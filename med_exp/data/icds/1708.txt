Drainage of Thorax Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, through the skin to visualize the thoracic lymphatic system. The endoscope guides the surgeon to the exact location of the lymphatic blockage or fluid accumulation, allowing for precise drainage. The percutaneous approach means the procedure is done through a small skin incision, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The diagnostic purpose of the procedure enables healthcare professionals to identify underlying conditions, such as lymphedema or cancer, and develop an effective treatment plan.
