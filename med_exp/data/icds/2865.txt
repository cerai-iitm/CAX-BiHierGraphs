Excision of Appendix, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the appendix, a small, finger-like pouch attached to the large intestine. The surgeon then carefully locates and isolates the inflamed or diseased appendix and removes it from the surrounding tissue. The excised appendix is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to determine the underlying cause of the symptoms. The incision is subsequently closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for post-operative complications.
