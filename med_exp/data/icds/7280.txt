Detachment at Left Foot, Partial 3rd Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the 3rd ray, a partial portion of the left foot, through an open approach. The 3rd ray consists of the intermediate phalanx, metatarsal, and cuboid bones, and the surrounding soft tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully detach the 3rd ray, preserving as much healthy tissue as possible. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal foot function and alleviate conditions such as pain, deformity, or infection.
