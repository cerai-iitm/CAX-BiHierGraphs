Introduction of Recombinant Bone Morphogenetic Protein into Joints, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a recombinant bone morphogenetic protein is introduced into the joints to stimulate bone growth and repair. This bioactive molecule is designed to promote osteogenesis, enhancing the body's natural healing process. The protein is carefully inserted into the affected joint, where it facilitates the regeneration of bone tissue, ultimately leading to improved joint function and reduced pain. By harnessing the power of this protein, surgeons can enhance the body's natural ability to repair damaged joints, resulting in more effective and sustainable outcomes.
