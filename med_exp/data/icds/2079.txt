Dilation of Left Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called a bronchoscope, is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided through the airways to the left main bronchus. Once in place, a specialized instrument is used to dilate or widen the bronchus, which can become narrowed or blocked due to conditions such as bronchial stenosis or tumors. This dilation helps to restore airflow and improve breathing by widening the airway passage. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort and allow the patient to relax during the procedure.
