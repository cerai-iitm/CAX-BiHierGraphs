Extirpation of Matter from Left Femoral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of unwanted material or substances from the left femoral vein through a small incision in the skin. A thin catheter is inserted through the incision and guided to the affected area of the vein, where the matter is then suctioned out or removed using a specialized instrument. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate removal of the matter.
