Inspection of Abdominal Wall, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the visualization and examination of the abdominal wall through a small incision or puncture site, allowing for a thorough assessment of the underlying tissues and structures. During the procedure, a thin instrument or scope is inserted through the skin, enabling the physician to inspect the abdominal wall and identify any potential abnormalities or defects. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing and recovery. The detailed inspection of the abdominal wall is crucial in diagnosing and treating various conditions, including hernias, injuries, or infections.
