Division of Radial Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the radial nerve, a major nerve that runs down the arm and controls extension of the wrist and fingers. The nerve is carefully divided to relieve compression or tension that may be causing pain, numbness, or tingling in the arm or hand. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the nerve and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any problems. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms and improve functional ability in the affected arm or hand.
