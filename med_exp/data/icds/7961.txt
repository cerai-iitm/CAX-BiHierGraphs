Ultrasonography of Left Heart using Other Contrast
This non-invasive diagnostic imaging test uses high-frequency sound waves to produce images of the left heart, which includes the left atrium, left ventricle, mitral valve, and adjacent blood vessels. The use of an "other" contrast agent, different from the standard echo-contrast, enhances the visibility of the heart's structures and improves image quality. The test is performed by a trained sonographer or cardiologist who applies a transducer to the chest, which sends and receives sound waves, transmitting the resulting images to a computer monitor. The procedure is essential in diagnosing and monitoring various heart conditions, such as cardiac valve problems, cardiomyopathy, and coronary artery disease.
