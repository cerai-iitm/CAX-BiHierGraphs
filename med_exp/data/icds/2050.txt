Destruction of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the destruction of the left upper lung lobe through a minimally invasive approach. The procedure is typically performed using image-guided techniques such as CT or fluoroscopy to accurately target the lobe. During the procedure, a small instrument or device is inserted through the skin and into the left upper lung lobe to ablate or destroy the targeted tissue. The destruction of the lung lobe is usually performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, emphysema, or other diseased or damaged lung tissue.
