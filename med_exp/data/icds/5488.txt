Reposition Left Fibula with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left fibula, one of the two bones in the lower leg, to its anatomically correct position. An open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. Once the fibula is repositioned, an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screws, is implanted to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the affected leg, alleviating symptoms and promoting a full recovery.
