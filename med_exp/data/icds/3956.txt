Drainage of Abdomen Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal skin to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where excess fluid or infected material is drained from the abdomen through an open approach, allowing for a more direct and precise access to the affected area. The surgeon carefully dissects the tissue layers to identify the source of the fluid accumulation or infection, and then uses suction or irrigation to remove the unwanted material. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as abscesses, seromas, or hematomas that require immediate attention. The open approach allows for a more thorough exploration of the abdominal cavity and facilitates the removal of any infected or damaged tissue.
