Release Vagina, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to release the vagina from adhesions, scar tissue, or other constraints that impede its normal function. The operation is performed to treat conditions such as vaginal stenosis, vaginismus, or labial adhesions, which can cause pain, discomfort, and difficulty with sexual intercourse or gynecological exams. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision to access the vagina and carefully releases the entrapped tissue, taking care to preserve surrounding structures and promote optimal vaginal health. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal vaginal function, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall quality of life.
