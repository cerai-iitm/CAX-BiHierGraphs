Excision of Right Femoral Region, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right femoral region to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the targeted tissue or lesion. The excision process is performed under direct visualization, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and dissect the affected tissue. The goal of this operation is to remove the diseased or damaged tissue, while preserving as much healthy surrounding tissue as possible. Following the excision, the incision is closed, and the area is repaired to promote optimal healing.
