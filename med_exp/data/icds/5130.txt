Resection of 3 or More Ribs, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of three or more ribs through an open approach, typically performed to treat conditions such as thoracic deformities, tumors, or injuries. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to carefully resect the affected ribs and surrounding tissue. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest wall, exposes the ribs, and carefully removes the damaged or diseased sections. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal thoracic function, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall quality of life.
