Reposition Right Mandible with External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves the realignment of the right mandible (lower jawbone) using an external fixation device, which is attached to the bone through small incisions in the skin. The device is inserted percutaneously, meaning through the skin, and is used to stabilize and reposition the mandible to its correct anatomical position. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as fractures, deformities, or malunions of the mandible, and is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation. The external fixation device is usually left in place for several weeks or months to allow the bone to heal properly before being removed.
