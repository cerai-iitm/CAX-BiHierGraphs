Drainage of Right Axilla, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right axilla, or armpit, to drain accumulated fluid or other substances. The open approach requires a larger incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and address the underlying issue. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to identify and treat the underlying cause of the accumulation, which may be related to infection, inflammation, or other conditions. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local sedation to minimize patient discomfort.
