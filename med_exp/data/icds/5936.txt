Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more lumbar vertebral joints using a patient's own tissue substitute, accessed through an anterior approach, which means the surgeon operates from the front of the spine. The anterior column is stabilized through an open approach, which requires a larger incision to expose the spine. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate back pain and instability caused by conditions such as degenerative disc disease, spondylolisthesis, or spinal stenosis. By fusing the vertebrae, the spine is stabilized, and motion is restricted, allowing the patient to recover and return to normal activities.
