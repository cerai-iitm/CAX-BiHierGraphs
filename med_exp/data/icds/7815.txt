Monitoring of Cardiac Output, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a specialized catheter through the skin into a peripheral artery or vein to continuously monitor cardiac output, which is the amount of blood pumped by the heart per minute. The catheter is equipped with sensors that measure the cardiac output and transmit the data to a monitoring system, allowing healthcare providers to track changes in real-time. This percutaneous approach enables minimally invasive monitoring of cardiac function, reducing the risk of complications and allowing for prompt detection of any abnormalities. By providing accurate and continuous cardiac output measurements, this procedure plays a crucial role in guiding treatment decisions and improving patient outcomes.
