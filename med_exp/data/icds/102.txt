Excision of Thoracic Spinal Cord, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves making an incision in the thoracic region of the spine to access the spinal cord, allowing for the removal of a portion of the cord for diagnostic examination. The open approach involves a larger incision, which provides direct visualization of the spinal cord and surrounding tissues. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further analysis, enabling healthcare professionals to gain insight into the underlying condition or disorder. The diagnostic findings from this procedure can inform treatment decisions and guide future care for the patient.
