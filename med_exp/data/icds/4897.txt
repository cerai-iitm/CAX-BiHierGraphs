Reposition Left Orbit with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of the left orbit, which is the bony structure surrounding the eye, to its normal anatomical position. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize the orbit and surrounding tissues. Internal fixation devices, such as plates or screws, are then used to stabilize the repositioned orbit and promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural alignment and function of the orbit, alleviating symptoms such as double vision, eye socket deformity, or difficulty moving the eye.
