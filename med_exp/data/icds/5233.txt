Excision of Right Tarsal, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove a portion of the right tarsal bone, which is a cluster of seven bones in the midfoot region. The open approach involves making a surgical incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the affected bone, whereas the excision process involves carefully cutting out the bone tissue using specialized instruments. The goal of the procedure is to treat various conditions affecting the right tarsal bone, such as osteomyelitis, bone cancer, or severe fractures, and to restore normal foot function and mobility. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed in an operating room setting.
