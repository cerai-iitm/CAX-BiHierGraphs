Excision of Perineum Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of skin tissue from the perineum, a region located between the genitals and anus, through an external approach. The purpose of this excision is to treat conditions such as skin cancer, wounds, or chronic infections that affect the perineal skin. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the perineum to access the affected skin, carefully removes the damaged tissue, and closes the wound with sutures or staples. After the procedure, the patient may require pain management and wound care to ensure proper healing and minimize the risk of complications.
