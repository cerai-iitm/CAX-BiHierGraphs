Detachment at Left Foot, Partial 2nd Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of a portion of the left foot, specifically the partial 2nd ray, which includes the second toe and corresponding metatarsal bone. The open approach implies a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and separate the tissues to detach the specified ray. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as chronic deformities, infections, or traumatic injuries that affect the normal functioning of the foot. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the left foot, alleviating pain and improving mobility.
