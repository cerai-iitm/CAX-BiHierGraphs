Destruction of Sigmoid Colon, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an open incision is made in the abdomen to access the sigmoid colon, a portion of the large intestine located in the lower left side of the belly. The affected sigmoid colon is then carefully separated from surrounding tissues and blood vessels, and the damaged or diseased section is removed. The remaining healthy parts of the colon are then closed or reattached using sutures or staples, and the incision is closed. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as diverticulitis, cancer, or traumatic injury to the sigmoid colon.
