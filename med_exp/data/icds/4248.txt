Repair Left Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower arm to access and repair damaged subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The subcutaneous tissue, which is the layer of tissue just beneath the skin, and the fascia, a band of connective tissue that surrounds muscles and other structures, are carefully examined and repaired using sutures or other materials. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal anatomy and function to the affected area, promoting healing and reducing the risk of complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the tissues and make precise repairs.
