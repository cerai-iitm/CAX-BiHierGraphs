Destruction of Bladder Neck, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the bladder neck, allowing the surgeon to precisely target and destroy the affected area. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, providing access to the bladder neck without the need for a large incision. Once in place, the surgeon uses specialized instruments to destroy the targeted tissue, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as bladder neck obstruction or cancer, and can be done under local or general anesthesia depending on the individual case.
