Repair Back Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an open incision to access the damaged area, where the surgeon manually repairs and reattaches the subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the back region. The goal is to restore normal anatomy and function, alleviating symptoms such as pain, swelling, or limited mobility. The incision is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the wound is dressed to promote healing. Post-operatively, patients may require a period of rest and rehabilitation to ensure a successful outcome.
