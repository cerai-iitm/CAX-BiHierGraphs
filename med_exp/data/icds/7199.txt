Excision of Right Hand, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the entire right hand through an open incision, typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize pain and discomfort. The surgery requires careful dissection and separation of the hand from the surrounding tissues, followed by ligation of blood vessels and nerves to prevent bleeding and promote healing. The resulting wound is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and dressed to protect the area from infection. Post-operative care typically involves pain management, rehabilitation, and wound care to ensure a smooth recovery.
