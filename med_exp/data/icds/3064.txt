Release Sigmoid Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the sigmoid colon, a part of the large intestine, with the goal of releasing any adhesions or obstructions that may be causing blockages or complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the affected area and manually release any adhesions or scar tissue that may be binding the colon. This procedure may be necessary to treat conditions such as sigmoid volvulus, intussusception, or bowel obstruction. By releasing the sigmoid colon, the surgeon can restore normal bowel function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
