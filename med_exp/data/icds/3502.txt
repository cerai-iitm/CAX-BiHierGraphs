Removal of Intraluminal Device from Hepatobiliary Duct, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of an intraluminal device, such as a stent or catheter, from the hepatobiliary duct, which connects the liver, gallbladder, and small intestine. The doctor uses image-guided techniques, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to locate the device and guide the removal instrument through the skin and into the duct. The device is then carefully grasped and withdrawn from the body, alleviating any obstruction or damage to the duct. Following the procedure, the patient is monitored for any complications, such as bleeding or infection.
