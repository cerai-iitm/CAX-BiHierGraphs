Release Left Lower Arm and Wrist Muscle, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically releasing the contracted muscles in the left lower arm and wrist through an open approach, which requires a skin incision to access the affected muscles. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal range of motion and alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility in the wrist and hand. The surgeon will make a incision in the left arm and wrist, carefully identifying and releasing the contracted muscles to promote relaxation and flexibility. Following the procedure, the patient will require rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility in the affected arm and wrist.
