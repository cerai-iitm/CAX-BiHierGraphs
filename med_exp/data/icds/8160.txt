Fluoroscopy of Right Upper Extremity Veins using Low Osmolar Contrast, Guidance
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This non-invasive medical imaging procedure uses fluoroscopy to visualize the veins in the right upper extremity, which includes the arm, forearm, and hand. A low osmolar contrast agent is injected into the veins to highlight the blood vessels and enhance image quality. Under continuous X-ray guidance, the movement of the contrast agent is tracked in real-time, providing healthcare professionals with precise information on blood flow and vessel structure. This diagnostic test helps identify blockages, narrowing, or other abnormalities in the veins that may be causing symptoms such as pain, swelling, or numbness in the arm.
