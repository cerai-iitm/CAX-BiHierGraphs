Extirpation of Matter from Right Common Iliac Artery, Bifurcation, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous removal of unwanted material from the right common iliac artery at its bifurcation point. Under image guidance, a small incision is made in the skin, and a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to access the affected artery. The obstructing material is then carefully extracted from the artery, restoring blood flow and reducing the risk of complications. The percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to the surrounding tissue, promoting faster recovery and reduced scarring.
