Drainage of Pancreatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the doctor to visualize the pancreatic duct and drain any blockages or obstructions. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and potentially treat conditions such as pancreatitis or pancreatic pseudocysts. Under guided imaging, the doctor navigates the endoscope through the duct, removing any debris or stones that may be causing the blockage, and installing a stent or drainage tube if necessary. This minimally invasive procedure offers a less traumatic alternative to open surgery, promoting faster recovery and reduced risk of complications.
