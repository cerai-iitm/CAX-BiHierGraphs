Drainage of Left Shoulder Joint, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to remove fluid or pus that has accumulated in the left shoulder joint. The percutaneous approach allows for a small incision, reducing trauma to surrounding tissues and promoting faster healing. The goal of the drainage is to relieve pressure, reduce inflammation, and promote recovery from conditions such as septic arthritis or joint infections. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device.
