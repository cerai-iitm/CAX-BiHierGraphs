Excision of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This percutaneous endoscopic procedure involves the removal of the right lower lung lobe using an endoscope inserted through the skin, allowing for a minimally invasive approach with reduced risk of complications. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. Imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scans, is often used to visualize the lung lobe and guide the endoscope to the target area. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to collect tissue samples for further examination and diagnosis of lung diseases or abnormalities.
