Excision of Right Kidney, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the right kidney through a small incision in the skin, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the kidney and surrounding tissues. The surgeon inserts specialized instruments through the incision to carefully dissect and free the kidney from its attachments, after which it is removed through the same small opening. The percutaneous approach allows for reduced tissue trauma and minimizes scarring, promoting faster patient recovery and less post-operative pain. The entire procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety.
