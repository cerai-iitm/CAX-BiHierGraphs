Transplantation of Liver, Allogeneic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transplantation of a liver from a donor into the patient's body through an open incision. The allogeneic liver transplant is performed to replace the patient's diseased or damaged liver with a healthy one from a compatible donor. The open approach requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the liver, allowing the surgeon to remove the diseased liver and implant the new one. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may take several hours to complete, depending on the complexity of the case.
