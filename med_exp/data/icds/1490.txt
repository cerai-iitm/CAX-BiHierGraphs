Removal of Infusion Device from Upper Vein, External Approach
The procedure involves accessing the upper vein through an external approach to remove an infusion device, which is a medical apparatus used to administer medications, fluids, or nutrients directly into the bloodstream. This device is typically inserted through the skin and into the vein, and its removal requires a deliberate and careful approach to minimize bleeding and prevent infection. Following removal, the site is typically cleaned and dressed to promote healing and prevent complications. The removal of the infusion device from the upper vein is usually performed by a trained healthcare professional in a clinical setting.
