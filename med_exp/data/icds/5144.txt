Supplement Left Humeral Shaft with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this orthopedic surgical procedure, the humeral shaft in the left arm is accessed through an open incision to implant an autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft derived from the patient's own tissue, to supplement and support the weakened or damaged bone structure. The goal of this intervention is to restore the normal function and anatomy of the humerus, alleviating pain and improving mobility in the affected arm. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully inserted and secured into place, promoting bone growth and fusion. The surgeon then closes the incision, and the patient is monitored for a successful outcome.
