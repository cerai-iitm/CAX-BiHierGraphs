Resection of Right Parotid Gland, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right parotid gland, a salivary gland located in the neck, through an open incision. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a thorough understanding of the surrounding anatomy to avoid damaging vital structures, such as the facial nerve. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin, carefully dissects the gland from surrounding tissues, and removes the diseased or damaged gland. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as parotid gland tumors, abscesses, or chronic infections.
