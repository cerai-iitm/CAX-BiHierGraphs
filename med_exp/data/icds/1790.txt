Extraction of Aortic Lymphatic, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to extract aortic lymphatic tissue through a natural or artificial opening for diagnostic purposes. The endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, is inserted through the opening to visualize the lymphatic tissue, allowing the surgeon to extract a sample for further examination. The extracted tissue is then sent to a laboratory for analysis, helping doctors to diagnose and treat lymphatic disorders or diseases affecting the aorta. This minimally invasive procedure offers a safer and more effective alternative to traditional open surgical methods, reducing recovery time and scarring.
