Excision of Thoracic Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the thoracic region to access and remove a specific nerve or portion of a nerve that is causing pain, numbness, or other symptoms. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the nerve and surrounding tissue, enabling precise excision of the affected nerve segment. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms and improve quality of life by eliminating the source of pain or discomfort. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed in a hospital setting by a trained surgeon or a specialist in thoracic surgery.
