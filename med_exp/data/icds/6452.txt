Resection of Right Kidney, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the right kidney through a small incision in the skin, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the kidney and surrounding tissues. A thin, lighted tube with a camera and surgical instruments is inserted through the incision, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the kidney. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and promotes faster recovery, reducing the risk of complications and scarring. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a short hospital stay for post-operative care and monitoring.
