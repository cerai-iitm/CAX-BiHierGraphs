Repair Atrial Septum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the atrial septum and perform the necessary repairs. The atrial septum is a thin wall of tissue that separates the upper chambers of the heart, and defects or holes in this septum can cause oxygen-rich blood to mix with oxygen-poor blood, leading to serious health complications. During the open approach, the surgeon will use surgical instruments to close the hole or defect, and may use patches or other materials to reinforce the septum and ensure proper blood flow. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal heart function and improve the patient's overall cardiovascular health.
