Release Abdomen Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves freeing the abdominal skin from adhesions or scar tissue that may be holding it in place. The external approach is used, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the abdominal skin to access the underlying tissues. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal mobility and flexibility to the abdominal skin, which can become restricted due to injury, surgery, or other conditions. By releasing the skin from its attachments, the surgeon can promote healing, reduce tension, and improve overall abdominal function.
