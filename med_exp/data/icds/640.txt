Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Aortic Valve, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the revision of a synthetic substitute in the aortic valve through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, using imaging guidance to access the valve. The synthetic substitute, typically a bioprosthetic valve, is revised to restore its normal function, which may have been compromised due to wear and tear, calcification, or other factors. The percutaneous approach allows for a smaller incision and less tissue damage compared to open-heart surgery, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The revised valve is designed to improve blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and swelling.
