Excision of Bilateral Vas Deferens, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of both vas deferens, the tubes responsible for carrying sperm, through small incisions using an endoscope. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for a quicker recovery and less scarring compared to traditional open surgeries. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and involves the insertion of an endoscope through a small incision in the scrotum to visualize the vas deferens. The vas deferens are then cut and sealed to prevent sperm from being released during ejaculation, resulting in sterility.
