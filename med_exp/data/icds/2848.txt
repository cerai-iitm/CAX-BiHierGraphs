Excision of Ileocecal Valve, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
Here is the description:

This procedure involves the removal of the ileocecal valve, a vital structure that separates the small and large intestines, through an endoscopic approach. The operation is performed via a natural or artificial opening, allowing the surgeon to access the valve without the need for a large incision. Using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, the surgeon is able to visualize the valve and remove it with precision. The diagnostic component of this procedure allows for the examination of the removed tissue to determine the presence of any abnormalities or diseases.
