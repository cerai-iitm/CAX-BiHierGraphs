Detachment at Right Foot, Partial 3rd Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves detaching a portion of the third toe on the right foot, using an open approach where the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to remove a damaged or deformed section of the toe, which can help alleviate pain, correct deformities, or improve mobility. The partial detachment of the third ray, which includes the metatarsal bone, toe bones, and surrounding soft tissues, is performed under sterile conditions to minimize the risk of infection. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the foot is immobilized to facilitate healing and recovery.
