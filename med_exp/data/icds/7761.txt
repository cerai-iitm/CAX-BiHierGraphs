Irrigation of Genitourinary Tract using Irrigating Substance, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the introduction of an irrigating substance into the genitourinary tract to cleanse and flush out debris, bacteria, or other unwanted substances. The irrigating substance is administered through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for effective drainage and removal of unwanted materials. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal genitourinary function, alleviate symptoms, and prevent potential complications. By flushing out the genitourinary tract, this procedure helps to promote healing, reduce inflammation, and maintain overall urological health.
