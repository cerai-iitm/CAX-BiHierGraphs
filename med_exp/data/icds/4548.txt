Supplement Right Lower Leg Muscle with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this open approach procedure, a surgeon accesses the right lower leg muscle to supplement it with an autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The goal of the procedure is to restore muscle function and strength, often due to injury, disease, or trauma. The autologous tissue substitute is typically harvested from a donor site, such as the abdominal wall, and then transferred to the affected area in the lower leg. This procedure may involve realigning or reattaching tendons or ligaments to ensure proper muscle function and stability.
