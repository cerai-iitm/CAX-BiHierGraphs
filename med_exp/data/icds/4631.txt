Excision of Left Upper Arm Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a tendon in the left upper arm through an open approach, where the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area. The tendon is carefully excised, and the incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, restore function, and improve range of motion in the arm, often following injuries or conditions that cause tendon inflammation or tears. Post-operative care typically includes physical therapy to promote healing and strengthen the surrounding muscles.
