Release Cerebellum, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the cranium is opened to access the cerebellum, a region in the posterior fossa of the skull that controls movement, balance, and coordination. The surgical team releases tension or compression on the cerebellum by relieving pressure from surrounding tissues or structures. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the source of compression. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal cerebellar function and alleviate associated symptoms, such as dizziness, unsteady gait, or loss of coordination.
