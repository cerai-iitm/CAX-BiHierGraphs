Drainage of Left Lung, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to drain fluid or air from the left lung, often performed to treat conditions such as pleural effusion or pneumothorax. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, allows the healthcare provider to visualize the lung and surrounding tissue, enabling precise drainage of the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, and may involve the insertion of a drainage tube to facilitate the removal of fluid or air. The goal of this procedure is to relieve respiratory symptoms, improve lung function, and promote healing.
