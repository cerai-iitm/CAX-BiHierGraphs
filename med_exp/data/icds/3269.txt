Bypass Common Hepatic Duct to Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves connecting the common hepatic duct to the small intestine, redirecting bile flow from the liver and gallbladder to the intestine, allowing for digestion and absorption of fats and fat-soluble vitamins. The operation is performed through an open approach, where the abdomen is opened to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the duct. The bypass reroutes the flow of bile around an obstruction, such as a tumor or narrowing, restoring the normal flow of bile into the intestine. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms like jaundice, abdominal pain, and itching, and to improve digestion and overall quality of life.
