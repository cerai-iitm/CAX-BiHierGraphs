Excision of Right Mandible, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the right mandible, also known as the lower jawbone. The bone is then carefully dissected and removed, either partially or completely, depending on the extent of the disease or damage. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a team of specialists, including oral and maxillofacial surgeons, otolaryngologists, and anesthesiologists. Post-operatively, the patient may require reconstruction and rehabilitation to restore normal jaw function and aesthetics.
