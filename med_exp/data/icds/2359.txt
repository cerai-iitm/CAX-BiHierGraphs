Removal of Intraluminal Device from Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of an intraluminal device from the trachea through a natural or artificial opening, using a flexible or rigid scope to visualize the device and guide its extraction. The procedure is typically performed under visualization with the aid of a camera and specialized instruments passed through the scope. The device is carefully grasped and withdrawn through the scope, taking care to avoid any trauma to the tracheal tissue or surrounding structures. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort and safety.
