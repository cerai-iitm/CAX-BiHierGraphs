Supplement Duodenum with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a portion of the duodenum with autologous tissue, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The open approach requires a large incision to access the duodenum, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the supplementation. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully sutured into place to restore the integrity of the duodenal wall. The goal of this procedure is to repair or reconstruct the duodenum, ensuring proper digestive function and alleviating symptoms associated with duodenal damage or disease.
