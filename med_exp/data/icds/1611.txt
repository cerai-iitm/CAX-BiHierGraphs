Insertion of Infusion Device into Superior Mesenteric Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device into the superior mesenteric vein, a major blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the small intestine. Through a percutaneous approach, a small incision is made in the skin, and a catheter is guided through the vein to reach the superior mesenteric vein. Once in place, the infusion device can deliver medication, nutrition, or other fluids directly into the bloodstream, allowing for targeted treatment of conditions affecting the digestive system. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the device.
