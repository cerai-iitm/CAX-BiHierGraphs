Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Humeral Shaft, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screws, from the right humeral shaft through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the internal fixation device. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and minimize pain. The surgeon carefully exposes the internal fixation device and removes it from the humeral shaft, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissue or bone. After the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
