Resection of Bilateral Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both breasts through an open approach, which means the surgeon makes a large incision in the skin to access the breasts. The procedure is typically performed to treat breast cancer or as a preventive measure for individuals at high risk of developing the disease. The surgery can be extensive, requiring the removal of breast tissue, skin, and sometimes underlying muscle, depending on the extent of the cancer or the patient's overall health. The goal of the procedure is to remove all cancerous tissue and prevent the spread of the disease to other parts of the body.
