Drainage of Right Middle Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to drain the right middle lobe bronchus, a part of the airway that connects the trachea to the lungs, through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, is inserted through the mouth or nose to visualize the bronchial tubes and identify the area of blockage. Once the blockage is located, a drainage device or suction tool is inserted through the endoscope to remove mucus, blood, or other debris that may be obstructing airflow. This diagnostic procedure helps to improve breathing and allows for the collection of samples for further testing.
