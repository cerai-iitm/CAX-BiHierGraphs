Reposition Left Humeral Head with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left humeral head to its normal anatomic position, utilizing an open approach to access the affected area. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are implanted to stabilize the joint and promote healing. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as shoulder dislocations, fractures, or bone deformities that prevent normal shoulder function. By repositioning and stabilizing the humeral head, the procedure aims to restore shoulder mobility, reduce pain, and improve overall quality of life.
