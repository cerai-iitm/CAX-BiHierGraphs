Restriction of Thoracic Aorta, Descending with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of an intraluminal device to restrict blood flow through the descending thoracic aorta. The procedure is performed percutaneously, through a small incision in the groin or arm, using imaging guidance such as X-ray or ultrasound. A catheter is inserted through the incision and guided to the targeted area of the aorta, where the device is then deployed to constrict the aorta and reduce blood flow. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as aortic aneurysms or aortic dissections by reducing pressure on the aortic wall and preventing rupture or further damage.
