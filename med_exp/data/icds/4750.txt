Excision of Lower Spine Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a bursa, a fluid-filled sac, and a ligament, a fibrous connective tissue, from the lower spine area through an open approach, requiring a skin incision. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain, inflammation, and discomfort caused by the affected bursa and ligament. The surgery typically involves the use of general anesthesia and takes around 1-2 hours to complete, depending on the complexity of the case. Post-operative care includes rest, pain management, and physical therapy to facilitate a smooth recovery.
