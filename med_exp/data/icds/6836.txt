Control Bleeding in Gastrointestinal Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to locate and control bleeding in the gastrointestinal tract, which can be accessed through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the inside of the gut and identify the source of bleeding. Once the bleeding site is located, the healthcare provider can use specialized instruments to stop the bleeding, such as applying electrocautery, injecting clotting medications, or using endoscopic clips or bands. The goal of the procedure is to restore hemostasis and prevent further bleeding complications.
