Fluoroscopy of Single Coronary Artery using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize a single coronary artery, which is accomplished by injecting a contrast agent through a catheter inserted into an artery in the leg or arm. The contrast agent highlights the coronary artery, allowing for real-time X-ray images to be taken as the dye flows through the artery. The fluoroscopy machine produces continuous X-ray beams that are transmitted to a monitor, enabling the physician to guide the catheter and visualize the artery in real-time. The resulting images help diagnose blockages, narrowing, or other abnormalities in the coronary artery.
