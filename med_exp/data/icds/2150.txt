Drainage of Right Pleura with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin and into the right pleural space to remove excess fluid or air that has accumulated between the lungs and chest wall. Using image-guided technology, the doctor inserts a small tube or catheter into the pleural space, allowing for the drainage of fluid or air to relieve pressure and promote healing. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, allowing the patient to remain awake but comfortable during the procedure.
