Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Shoulder Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an open approach is utilized to access the right shoulder joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the internal fixation device directly. The device, typically composed of metal plates, screws, or rods, is carefully removed from the joint to promote healing and alleviate potential discomfort or limited mobility. The open approach requires a incision to be made in the skin and underlying tissue to expose the joint, enabling the surgeon to safely and effectively remove the internal fixation device. Following removal, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
