Replacement of Left Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the femoral surface of the left hip joint with a synthetic substitute using an uncemented method through an open approach. The goal is to restore the natural function of the hip joint, alleviating pain and improving mobility. The uncemented implant is designed to integrate with the surrounding bone, promoting long-term stability and durability. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and accurately position the implant for optimal results.
