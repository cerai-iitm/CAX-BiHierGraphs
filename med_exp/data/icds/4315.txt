Transfer Buttock Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Skin, Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open transfer of buttock subcutaneous tissue and fascia, along with the skin, to a new location on the body. The procedure typically involves making an incision in the buttock area to access the underlying tissue and fascia, which are then carefully dissected and mobilized for transfer. The transferred tissue and fascia are then reattached at the new site using sutures or staples, and the skin is closed over the transfer site. This complex procedure requires a high degree of precision and care to ensure optimal aesthetic and functional results.
