Dilation of Right External Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device to widen the narrowed or blocked right external iliac artery, improving blood flow and reducing the risk of further complications. The minimally invasive approach allows the interventional radiologist to access the affected area through a small puncture in the skin, minimizing tissue damage and promoting faster recovery. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the device is carefully navigated to the site of the blockage, where it is expanded to dilate the artery and restore normal blood flow. After the procedure, the device is removed, and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
