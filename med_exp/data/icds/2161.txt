Drainage of Left Pleura with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain excess fluid from the left pleural space, a region between the lungs and chest wall, using an endoscope and a drainage device inserted through the skin. This percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for visualization of the pleural cavity and precise placement of the drainage device, typically under local anesthesia and conscious sedation. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as hemothorax, pneumothorax, or pleural effusion, and can help alleviate symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, and coughing. The drainage device is designed to remove excess fluid and promote healing, and may be left in place for several days to ensure complete drainage.
