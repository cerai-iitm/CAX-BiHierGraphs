Dilation of Esophagogastric Junction, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the gentle widening of the esophagogastric junction, a crucial passage between the esophagus and stomach, using specialized instruments inserted through the mouth or an existing opening. The goal is to alleviate symptoms such as difficulty swallowing, regurgitation, and chest pain associated with narrowing or obstruction at this critical juncture. Under guided visualization, the physician carefully dilates the junction, allowing for improved passage of food and reduced discomfort. This minimally invasive approach enables precise control and immediate assessment of the treatment's effectiveness.
