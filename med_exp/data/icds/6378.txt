Extirpation of Matter from Bladder, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the bladder and identify the presence of abnormal matter. Through the natural or artificial opening, the endoscope is guided into the bladder, allowing the surgeon to locate and remove the unwanted material. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or sedation to ensure patient comfort. Upon completion, the bladder is flushed to remove any remaining debris, and the patient is closely monitored to prevent any potential complications.
