Fusion of 2 to 7 Thoracic Vertebral Joints with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This procedure involves fusing two to seven thoracic vertebral joints using autologous tissue substitute, which is taken from the patient's own body. The posterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the affected area from the back, and the posterior column is targeted to stabilize the spine. An open approach is employed, which means a larger incision is made to allow for direct visualization and access to the spinal column. The goal of this procedure is to restore spinal stability and alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or weakness.
