Drainage of Bladder with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through a natural or artificial opening to remove urine or other fluids from the bladder. The goal of this intervention is to relieve urinary retention, treat urinary incontinence, or manage bladder infections. The drainage device may be a catheter, stent, or other specialized instrument designed for this purpose. The procedure may be performed under local anesthesia and is typically done in a hospital or clinical setting by a trained healthcare professional.
