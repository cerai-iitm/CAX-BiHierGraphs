Excision of Small Intestine, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the small intestine through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, typically using laparoscopic or endoscopic guidance. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to examine the intestine for abnormalities, such as tumors, blockages, or inflammatory conditions, and to obtain tissue samples for further examination. The percutaneous approach allows for smaller incisions and less tissue disruption, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. During the procedure, the surgeon uses specialized instruments and imaging technology to visualize the intestine and precisely excise the targeted section.
