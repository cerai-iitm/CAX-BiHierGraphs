Inspection of Neck, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a incision in the neck to visually examine the tissues and structures within, allowing for a thorough evaluation of the anatomy and identification of any potential abnormalities or lesions. The open approach enables the surgeon to directly inspect the affected area, taking into account the patient's specific condition and medical history. The procedure may be performed to diagnose conditions such as thyroid nodules, lymph node enlargement, or soft tissue injuries, and may be used in conjunction with other diagnostic tests or imaging studies. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully explore the neck region, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves, blood vessels, and other vital structures.
