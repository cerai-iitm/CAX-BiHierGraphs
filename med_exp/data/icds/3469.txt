Inspection of Liver, Open Approach
During an open approach, the surgical team visually examines the liver to identify any abnormalities, such as tumors, cysts, or signs of liver disease. This invasive procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the liver, allowing the surgeon to directly inspect the organ. The inspection may involve palpation, or feeling the liver, to detect any unusual textures or masses. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and potentially treat liver-related health issues, providing valuable information for guiding further medical interventions.
