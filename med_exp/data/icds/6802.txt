Control Bleeding in Head, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the site of bleeding in the head, allowing the surgeon to visually identify and control the source of hemorrhage. The open approach provides direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely locate and treat the bleeding vessel or site. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed in an operating room setting to ensure optimal patient safety and sterility. The goal of the procedure is to stop active bleeding, restore hemostasis, and prevent further complications or damage to surrounding brain tissue.
