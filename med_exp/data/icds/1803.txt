Repair Left Axillary Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to repair the left axillary lymphatic vessels, which are responsible for draining lymph fluid from the arm and surrounding tissues. During the operation, the surgeon makes an incision in the armpit area to access the affected lymphatic vessels. The damaged or blocked lymphatic vessels are then carefully repaired using sutures or other appropriate materials to restore normal lymphatic flow and alleviate symptoms such as swelling, pain, and infection. The goal of this procedure is to improve lymphatic circulation, reduce morbidity, and enhance overall quality of life.
