Excision of Left Shoulder Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and tissues of the left shoulder to access the joint, followed by the removal of the damaged or diseased joint. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain and restore function to the shoulder, which may have been affected by conditions such as osteoarthritis or traumatic injury. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the joint and surrounding tissues, ensuring a thorough excision of the damaged areas. Once the joint has been removed, the incision is closed, and the wound is dressed to promote healing.
