Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Left Tibia, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive surgical procedure, a small incision is made in the skin of the left leg to access the tibia. A guided imaging technique, such as fluoroscopy or computed tomography, is used to help the surgeon accurately position the internal fixation device. The device is then inserted through the incision and into the tibia to stabilize and support the bone, promoting healing and alignment. This percutaneous approach reduces tissue damage and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery.
