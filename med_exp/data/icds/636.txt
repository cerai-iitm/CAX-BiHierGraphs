Revision of Cardiac Lead in Heart, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves revising a cardiac lead in the heart through a percutaneous approach, which means the procedure is performed through the skin using a minimally invasive technique. The cardiac lead is a thin wire that connects a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD) to the heart, and revision may be necessary if the lead becomes damaged, dislodged, or infected. During the procedure, the physician inserts a small catheter through a vein in the groin or arm, guided by X-ray or ultrasound, to access the cardiac lead and make any necessary repairs or replacements. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper functioning of the pacemaker or ICD and ensure the heart is beating regularly and efficiently.
