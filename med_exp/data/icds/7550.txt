Traction of Right Lower Leg using Traction Apparatus
This procedure involves the application of a controlled force to the right lower leg using a specialized traction apparatus, with the goal of relieving pressure, reducing muscle spasms, and promoting healing. The apparatus is carefully adjusted to provide the necessary pulling force, which helps to stretch and align the soft tissues and bones in the affected area. The traction may be continuous or intermittent, depending on the specific needs of the patient, and is often used in conjunction with other therapies to enhance its effectiveness. By slowly and gently stretching the tissues, this procedure helps to improve mobility, reduce pain, and promote a faster recovery.
