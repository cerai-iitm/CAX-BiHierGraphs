Excision of Right Submaxillary Gland, Open Approach
The procedure involves a surgical incision in the neck to access the right submaxillary gland, a salivary gland located beneath the jawbone. The gland is then carefully dissected and removed, which may involve ligating blood vessels and nerves to prevent bleeding and damage. The surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the wound is cleaned and dressed. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as tumors, infection, or obstruction of the salivary gland.
