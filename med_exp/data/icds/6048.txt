Removal of Spacer from Left Hip Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove a spacer device from the left hip joint. The spacer, typically made of antibiotic-impregnated cement or other materials, is carefully extracted from the joint space to facilitate proper healing and prepare the area for future implantation of a prosthetic device. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, restore mobility, and promote infection-free healing in patients who have had previous hip surgeries or traumatic injuries. Through a direct visualization method, the surgeon carefully dissects the tissue and removes the spacer, taking care to preserve surrounding soft tissue and bone structures.
