Restriction of Abdominal Aorta with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a catheter through a small puncture in the femoral artery, which is then guided to the abdominal aorta using imaging technology. A specially designed intraluminal device is then deployed through the catheter to restrict blood flow in the abdominal aorta, effectively treating conditions such as abdominal aortic aneurysms or aortoiliac occlusive disease. The procedure is performed percutaneously, eliminating the need for open surgery and promoting faster recovery times. The device is designed to provide long-term stability and protection against further complications.
