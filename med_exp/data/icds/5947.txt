Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This procedure involves fusing the lumbosacral joint using a patient's own tissue to stabilize the spine and alleviate pain or discomfort. The surgical approach is posterior, meaning the incision is made from the back, and focuses on the anterior column, which is the front part of the spine. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and accurately place the autologous tissue substitute. The goal of the procedure is to restore spinal stability and reduce symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or tingling in the legs.
