Repair Right Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the right foot to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, allowing the surgeon to repair any damage or defects in these layers. The open approach enables direct visualization of the affected area, facilitating more precise repair and reconstruction of the tissue and fascia. During the procedure, the surgeon may remove any damaged or infected tissue, and then use sutures or staples to close the incision. The goal of this procedure is to restore the integrity and function of the right foot's subcutaneous tissue and fascia.
