Drainage of Jejunum, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, to drain its contents. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the jejunum and identify the area that requires drainage. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. The goal of this procedure is to relieve obstruction, remove blockages, or alleviate symptoms caused by an underlying condition, such as bowel obstruction or inflammation.
