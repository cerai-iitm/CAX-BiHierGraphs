Transfusion of Nonautologous 4-Factor Prothrombin Complex Concentrate into Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This medical procedure involves the percutaneous administration of a four-factor prothrombin complex concentrate (4F-PCC) into a vein. The 4F-PCC is a nonautologous, ready-to-use combination of four vitamin K-dependent clotting factors (II, VII, IX, and X) that helps to rapidly correct coagulopathy and bleeding complications. The percutaneous approach allows for direct insertion of the concentrate into a vein, minimizing the risk of bleeding and hematoma formation. The goal of this transfusion is to restore hemostasis and promote clotting in patients experiencing severe bleeding or at high risk of bleeding complications.
