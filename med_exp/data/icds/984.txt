Bypass Right Popliteal Artery to Foot Artery with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass in the right popliteal artery, which is located behind the knee, to restore blood flow to the foot. The bypass is constructed using a graft made from the patient's own venous tissue, which is harvested from another part of the body. The open approach involves making an incision to access the affected artery, and the graft is carefully sutured in place to reconnect the popliteal artery to the foot artery. The goal of the procedure is to improve blood flow and oxygen delivery to the tissues of the foot.
