Replacement of Aortic Valve with Zooplastic Tissue, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves replacing the aortic valve with a zooplastic tissue valve through a small incision in the leg or arm, using a catheter to guide the valve to the heart. The zooplastic tissue valve is made from animal-derived tissue, providing a durable and biocompatible replacement for the native aortic valve. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open-heart surgery, reducing recovery time and risk of complications. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires careful imaging guidance to ensure proper placement of the new valve.
