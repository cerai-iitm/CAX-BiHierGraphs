Repair Right Foot Skin, External Approach
During this surgical procedure, an external approach is used to repair damaged skin on the right foot. The surgeon makes an incision in the affected area, carefully assessing the extent of the damage before cleaning and debriding the wound. Any dead or damaged tissue is removed, and the surrounding skin is prepared for closure. The wound is then closed using sutures or staples, and the area is dressed to promote healing and prevent infection.
