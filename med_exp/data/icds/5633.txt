Excision of Left Elbow Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left elbow to access the joint, followed by the removal of the entire joint or damaged tissue. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling precise excision and repair. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, restore function, and improve mobility in the affected elbow joint. Post-operative care typically involves rehabilitation to regain strength and range of motion in the elbow.
