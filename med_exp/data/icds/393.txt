Excision of Atrial Septum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the atrial septum, a thin wall of tissue that separates the upper chambers of the heart. The surgeon then carefully excises the septum to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath and fatigue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the heart and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the septum and address any additional defects. Following the procedure, the incision is closed and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
