Resection of Uterus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a laparotomy, a type of open abdominal surgery, where an incision is made in the abdomen to access the uterus. The uterus is then carefully dissected and removed, a process that requires great precision and care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and organs. The surgery typically takes several hours to complete and may involve removal of other reproductive organs such as the ovaries and fallopian tubes, depending on the underlying condition being treated. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications or adverse reactions.
