Excision of Stomach, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Vertical
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through the mouth to visualize the stomach, allowing the surgeon to remove a portion of the stomach through small incisions in the abdominal wall. The vertical excision of the stomach is performed under image guidance, ensuring precise removal of the affected tissue. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort during the procedure.
