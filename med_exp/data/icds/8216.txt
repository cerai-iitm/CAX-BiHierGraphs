Fluoroscopy of Bile Ducts using Other Contrast
This non-invasive procedure uses a specialized X-ray imaging technology to visualize the bile ducts, allowing doctors to diagnose and treat conditions such as gallstones, bile duct blockages, and tumors. A contrast agent, other than the standard iodinated agents, is introduced into the bile ducts to enhance visualization during fluoroscopy. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation to minimize discomfort, and real-time imaging enables precise guidance during interventions. The resulting high-quality images aid in accurate diagnosis and effective treatment planning.
