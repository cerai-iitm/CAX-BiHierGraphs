Drainage of Pericardial Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a needle or catheter through the chest wall to drain excess fluid or blood from the pericardial cavity, the sac surrounding the heart. Guided by imaging techniques such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, the healthcare provider carefully inserts the drainage device to avoid damaging nearby structures. The procedure helps alleviate symptoms of pericardial effusion, including chest pain, shortness of breath, and cardiac tamponade, by relieving pressure on the heart. Successful drainage can significantly improve cardiac function and overall patient outcomes.
