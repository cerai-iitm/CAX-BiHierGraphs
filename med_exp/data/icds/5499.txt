Reposition Right Tarsal with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning the bones in the right ankle, known as the tarsal bones, into their correct alignment. To achieve this, an open approach is used, where an incision is made in the skin and soft tissue to access the affected area. An internal fixation device, such as screws, plates, or rods, is then applied to hold the bones in place and facilitate healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal ankle function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and limited mobility.
