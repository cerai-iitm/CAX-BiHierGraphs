Supplement Left Brachial Vein with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this procedure, a surgical incision is made to access the left brachial vein, a major blood vessel located in the upper arm. A synthetic substitute, such as a vascular graft, is then used to supplement the vein, restoring blood flow and promoting healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manual manipulation of the vein, enabling the surgeon to precisely place the substitute and ensure a secure attachment. The goal of this procedure is to improve blood flow and circulation in the affected limb.
