Excision of Liver, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Percutaneous excision of liver tissue is a minimally invasive diagnostic procedure performed through the skin to obtain a sample of liver tissue for examination. This procedure involves inserting a needle through the skin and into the liver to collect a tissue sample, which is then sent to a laboratory for analysis. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery time and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery. The resulting tissue sample is examined to diagnose liver diseases or conditions, such as cancer, cirrhosis, or hepatitis.
