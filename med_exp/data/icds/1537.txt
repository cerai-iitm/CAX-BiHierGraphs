Dilation of Splenic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a needle or catheter through the skin to access the splenic vein, followed by the inflation of a balloon or other device to widen the vessel. The goal of this percutaneous procedure is to improve blood flow and relieve obstruction or blockage in the splenic vein. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the dilation device. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and allows for faster recovery times compared to open surgical approaches.
