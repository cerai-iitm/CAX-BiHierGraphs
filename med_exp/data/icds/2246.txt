Excision of Right Pleura, Open Approach
The surgical removal of the right pleura, a thin layer of tissue that surrounds the lungs, is performed through an open approach, which involves a large incision in the chest to access the affected area. This procedure is often necessary to treat conditions such as pleural effusion, empyema, or pleural tumors, and is usually performed under general anesthesia. The surgeon carefully dissects and excises the diseased or damaged pleural tissue, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding structures and promote optimal healing. The incision is then closed, and the patient is monitored for complications and recovery.
