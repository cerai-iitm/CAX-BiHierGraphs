Bypass Cerebral Ventricle to Peritoneal Cavity with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves creating a passage to divert cerebrospinal fluid from the cerebral ventricles to the peritoneal cavity using a synthetic substitute. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive technique, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The synthetic material is carefully inserted through a small incision in the skin, allowing for the creation of a new pathway for CSF drainage. By bypassing the ventricles and redirecting the fluid to the peritoneal cavity, this procedure aims to alleviate conditions such as hydrocephalus and relieve associated symptoms.
