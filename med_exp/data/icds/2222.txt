Excision of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove a portion of the left upper lung lobe using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, which is inserted through a small incision in the chest. The procedure is guided by real-time imaging to accurately identify the area of the lung requiring excision. During the procedure, the physician uses specialized instruments to remove the diseased or damaged portion of the lung tissue, while preserving as much healthy tissue as possible. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to obtain a tissue sample for further examination and to diagnose lung conditions such as cancer, infections, or inflammatory diseases.
