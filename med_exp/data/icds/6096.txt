Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Metal Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the diseased or damaged right hip joint with an artificial joint made of metal and synthetic materials. The open approach is used, which involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected joint. The damaged joint is then removed and replaced with the prosthetic one, which is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the hip joint. The goal of this procedure is to restore mobility, reduce pain, and improve the overall quality of life for the patient.
