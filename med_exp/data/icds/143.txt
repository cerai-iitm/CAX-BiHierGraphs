Inspection of Spinal Canal, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small instrument through the skin to visualize the spinal canal and its contents. The procedure allows for a direct examination of the spinal cord and surrounding structures, enabling the detection of any abnormalities or pathologies. Through the percutaneous approach, the risk of complications and tissue damage is significantly reduced compared to open surgical methods. The inspection is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the instrument and visualization of the target area.
