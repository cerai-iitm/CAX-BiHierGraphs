Reposition Left Shoulder Joint, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the left shoulder joint from the outside. The joint is then manually manipulated to restore its normal anatomical position, correcting any deformity or malalignment. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, improve range of motion, and enhance overall shoulder function. The approach is typically done under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort during the procedure.
