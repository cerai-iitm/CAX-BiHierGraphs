Drainage of Mesenteric Lymphatic, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
Here is the detailed description:

This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to access the mesenteric lymphatic system. The goal of the procedure is to drain accumulated fluids or substances from the lymphatic vessels, which play a crucial role in immune function and the removal of waste products. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that allow the healthcare provider to visualize the lymphatic vessels and perform the drainage procedure under visualization. The diagnostic nature of this procedure allows for the identification of any underlying conditions or abnormalities that may be contributing to the accumulation of fluids or substances in the mesenteric lymphatic system.
