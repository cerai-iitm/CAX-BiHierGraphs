Replacement of Left Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the femoral surface of the left hip joint with a synthetic substitute, which is cemented into place to provide stability and support. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, ensuring precise placement of the prosthetic component. The cemented synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the hip joint, providing relief from pain and restoring mobility to the patient. The goal of this procedure is to improve the patient's quality of life by alleviating discomfort and enabling them to resume normal activities.
