Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Inferior Vena Cava, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a device into the inferior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the lower body to the heart. The device is inserted through a small incision in the skin, using a percutaneous approach, which reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery. During the procedure, the device is guided into place using imaging technologies such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. The inserted device helps to filter blood clots or prevent pulmonary embolism, a life-threatening condition that occurs when a blood clot travels to the lungs.
