Removal of Cardiac Rhythm Related Device from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This procedure involves surgically removing a cardiac rhythm related device, such as a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator, from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the trunk. A open approach is used, which means a surgical incision is made to access the device. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize discomfort. The removal of the device is often necessary due to infection, device malfunction, or other complications.
