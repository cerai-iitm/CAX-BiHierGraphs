Inspection of Peritoneum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During a minimally invasive procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, is inserted through a small incision in the abdominal wall to visually examine the peritoneum, the thin layer of tissue that lines the abdominal cavity. This allows the healthcare provider to inspect the peritoneum for any signs of disease, injury, or inflammation. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes about 30-60 minutes to complete. It is often used to diagnose and manage conditions such as peritonitis, malignancy, or abdominal trauma.
