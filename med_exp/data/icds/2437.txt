Supplement Right Diaphragm with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to supplement the right diaphragm with a synthetic material, restoring its normal function and alleviating respiratory distress. Through a small incision, an endoscope is inserted to visualize the diaphragm, allowing for precise placement of the synthetic substitute. The substitute is gently guided into position, ensuring proper alignment and secure attachment to the surrounding tissue. This percutaneous approach minimizes tissue disruption, promoting faster recovery and reduced post-operative complications.
