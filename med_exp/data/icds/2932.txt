Excision of Peritoneum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure is a minimally invasive surgery that utilizes an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the peritoneum, a membrane lining the abdominal cavity. Through a small incision, the endoscope is inserted to inspect the peritoneum, and any abnormal tissue or lesions are removed for diagnostic purposes. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal scarring and reduced recovery time. The excised tissue is then sent for further examination to determine the presence of any diseases or conditions, facilitating diagnosis and treatment planning.
