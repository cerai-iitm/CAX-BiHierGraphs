Removal of Other Device from Lower Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected lower artery, carefully removing the unwanted device or fragment, and closing the incision to allow for proper healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms caused by the presence of the device. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the artery and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to precisely locate and extract the device. Following the procedure, the patient will require close monitoring to ensure proper healing and prevent potential complications.
