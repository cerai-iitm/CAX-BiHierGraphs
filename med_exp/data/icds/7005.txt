Insertion of Infusion Device into Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to place an infusion device within the peritoneal cavity, a region of the abdominal space that contains the intestines, stomach, and other organs. Through a small incision in the abdomen, an endoscope is guided through the peritoneum, a thin layer of tissue lining the cavity, to visualize the treatment area. The infusion device, which is typically a small catheter or port, is then carefully inserted into the peritoneal cavity under direct visualization, allowing for the administration of medications or fluids directly into the cavity. This approach enables precise placement and minimizes tissue disruption, promoting a faster and less painful recovery.
