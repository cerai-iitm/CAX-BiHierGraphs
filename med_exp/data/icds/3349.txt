Drainage of Right Hepatic Duct with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous drainage of the right hepatic duct is a minimally invasive procedure where a small incision is made in the skin to access the duct. Under imaging guidance, a drainage device is introduced through the incision to drain excess bile and relieve obstruction in the right hepatic duct. This approach avoids the need for open surgery and minimizes tissue damage. The drainage device is carefully positioned to ensure effective drainage and alleviate symptoms such as jaundice, itching, and abdominal pain.
