Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Femoral Artery, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into the left femoral artery, which is accessed through a small incision in the skin. Guided by imaging technology, a thin catheter is advanced through the incision and into the artery, allowing the infusion device to be placed in the desired position. The device is then secured in place, and the incision site is closed. This procedure enables the administration of medications, fluids, or other therapeutic agents directly into the bloodstream.
