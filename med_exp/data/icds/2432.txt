Supplement Left Main Bronchus with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made to access the left main bronchus, which is a vital airway that supplies oxygenated air to the lungs. The goal of the surgery is to supplement the left main bronchus with a synthetic substitute, which can be a prosthetic device or artificial material designed to replicate the function of the natural bronchus. The synthetic substitute is carefully implanted to restore the structural integrity and functionality of the airway, ensuring proper breathing and oxygenation. This open approach requires careful precision and expertise to minimize potential risks and complications.
