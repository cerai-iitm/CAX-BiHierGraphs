Resection of Left Ovary, Via Natural or Artificial Opening With Percutaneous Endoscopic Assistance
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the left ovary through a natural or artificial opening, assisted by a percutaneous endoscope. The endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, is inserted through a small incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the ovary and surrounding tissue. The surgeon then performs the resection, or removal, of the left ovary using specialized instruments, minimizing damage to surrounding tissue and reducing recovery time. This procedure is often performed to treat various conditions, such as ovarian cancer, ovarian cysts, or endometriosis.
