Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Ceramic Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right hip joint with a ceramic synthetic substitute, which is designed to mimic the natural joint and provide a durable and long-lasting solution. The uncemented approach is used, where the synthetic joint is inserted into the hip socket without the use of cement, allowing for a more natural fit and increased mobility. The open approach involves making a single incision in the skin and tissues to access the hip joint, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and perform the replacement. The ceramic synthetic substitute is carefully positioned and secured to the surrounding bone, ensuring a stable and functional joint.
