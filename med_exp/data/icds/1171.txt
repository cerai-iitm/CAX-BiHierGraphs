Extirpation of Matter from Left Internal Iliac Artery, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a catheter is inserted through the skin and guided through the blood vessels to the left internal iliac artery to remove unwanted material. The catheter is equipped with specialized tools, such as suction or grasping instruments, to extract the unwanted matter from the artery. This percutaneous approach allows for a smaller incision, reduced bleeding, and faster recovery compared to open surgery. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms related to the blockage or occlusion of the left internal iliac artery.
