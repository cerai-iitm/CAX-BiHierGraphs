Extraction of Endometrium, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the removal of the endometrium, the inner lining of the uterus, through a natural or artificial opening. The process is typically performed to treat various gynecological conditions such as endometrial cancer, abnormal uterine bleeding, or to prevent cancer growth. The extraction may be done through a minimally invasive procedure, such as laparoscopy or hysteroscopy, or through an open surgical incision. The goal of the procedure is to remove the diseased or abnormal tissue, relieving symptoms and improving reproductive health.
