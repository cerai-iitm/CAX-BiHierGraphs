Excision of Lumbar Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove a herniated or damaged lumbar vertebral disc, which is a common treatment for conditions such as herniated discs, degenerative disc disease, or spinal stenosis. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon makes an incision in the lower back to access the affected disc. The disc is then removed, relieving pressure on the surrounding nerves and potentially alleviating symptoms such as numbness, tingling, or pain in the back or legs. The procedure often requires a hospital stay and a period of rehabilitation to ensure a safe and successful recovery.
