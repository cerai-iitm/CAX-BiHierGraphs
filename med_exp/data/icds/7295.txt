Detachment at Left 2nd Toe, High, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the separation of the second toe on the left foot from the surrounding soft tissue, nerves, and blood vessels at a high level, typically above the proximal phalanx. The open approach requires a skin incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and manipulate the tissues directly. The detachment is performed to address conditions such as trauma, infection, or congenital deformities that affect the toe's alignment or functionality. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal toe alignment and function, alleviate pain, and improve overall foot mobility and appearance.
