Excision of Brain, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of brain tissue through a craniotomy, a surgical opening of the skull, to access the affected area. The goal of the excision is to remove lesions, tumors, or damaged tissue that are causing neurological symptoms or impairing brain function. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a team of specialized surgeons, anesthesiologists, and nursing staff. The brain tissue is carefully dissected and excised using specialized instruments, and the surgical site is closed once the procedure is complete.
