Resection of Lesser Omentum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the lesser omentum, a double layer of peritoneum that attaches the stomach to the liver. The surgeon then carefully dissects and removes the affected portion of the lesser omentum, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and organs. The removal of the lesser omentum is often performed to treat conditions such as gastric cancer, liver cancer, or traumatic injury. Following the resection, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any post-operative complications.
