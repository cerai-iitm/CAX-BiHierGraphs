Drainage of Pelvic Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin to remove accumulated fluid, blood, or infected material from the pelvic cavity. Guided by imaging techniques such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, the healthcare provider carefully accesses the pelvic cavity to introduce the drainage tube. The tube is positioned to allow for effective drainage, and the incision site is closed and dressed to promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms, prevent complications, and promote healing by removing harmful substances from the pelvic cavity.
