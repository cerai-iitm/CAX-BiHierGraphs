Insertion of Defibrillator Lead into Pericardium, Open Approach
During this open procedure, the surgeon accesses the pericardium, a sac that surrounds the heart, to implant a defibrillator lead. The lead is carefully inserted into the pericardium to connect to the defibrillator device, which is typically implanted under the skin near the collarbone. Once in place, the lead monitors the heart's rhythm and can deliver electrical shocks to restore a normal heartbeat if needed. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the pericardium and precise placement of the lead.
