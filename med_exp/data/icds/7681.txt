Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Respiratory Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This therapeutic procedure involves the administration of a medicinal substance directly into the respiratory tract through a natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth, nose, or tracheostomy tube, to provide localized treatment for respiratory conditions. The substance may be in the form of an aerosol, mist, or liquid, and is often used to manage symptoms such as bronchospasm, inflammation, or infection. The medication is carefully delivered into the respiratory tract using a device or instrument, ensuring accurate placement and minimizing potential side effects. This targeted approach allows for direct treatment of the affected area, promoting efficient relief and recovery.
