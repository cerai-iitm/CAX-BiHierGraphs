Extirpation of Matter from Sigmoid Colon, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the sigmoid colon is accessed through an incision in the abdominal wall, allowing for direct visualization of the affected area. The surgeon then carefully removes any foreign matter or debris that has accumulated in the sigmoid colon, taking care to avoid damaging the surrounding tissue or organs. This process helps to clear the colon of any blockages or obstructions, restoring normal bowel function and alleviating symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and constipation. The procedure typically requires a hospital stay and post-operative care to ensure a smooth recovery.
