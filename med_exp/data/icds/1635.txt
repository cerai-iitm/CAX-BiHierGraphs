Occlusion of Esophageal Vein with Extraluminal Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure is used to block the flow of blood in the esophageal vein to prevent bleeding, typically in patients with esophageal varices. During the procedure, a small incision is made in the skin and a catheter is guided through the vein to the esophageal vein under fluoroscopic guidance. An extraluminal device, such as a coil or plug, is then deployed through the catheter to occlude the esophageal vein. This percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery.
