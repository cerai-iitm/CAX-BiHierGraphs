Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Cervical Vertebra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as plates, screws, or rods, that were previously implanted to stabilize and treat a fracture or deformity in the cervical vertebrae. The open approach requires a skin incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and carefully extract the internal fixation device. The goal of the procedure is to relieve discomfort, improve range of motion, and reduce the risk of potential complications associated with the retained hardware. The removal process typically takes place under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for post-operative recovery and monitoring.
