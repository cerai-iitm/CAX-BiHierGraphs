Extracorporeal Introduction of Endothelial Damage Inhibitor to Vein Graft, New Technology Group 3
This minimally invasive procedure involves the external administration of an endothelial damage inhibitor to a vein graft, utilizing advanced technology to promote graft patency and prevent long-term complications. The inhibitor helps to protect the endothelial cells lining the graft, reducing the risk of damage and promoting a healthy vascular environment. This novel approach has been shown to improve graft survival rates and reduce the need for repeat interventions. By introducing the inhibitor externally, clinicians can target the affected area precisely, minimizing systemic side effects and promoting optimal outcomes.
