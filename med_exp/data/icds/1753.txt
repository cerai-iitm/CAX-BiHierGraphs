Excision of Pelvis Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This percutaneous endoscopic procedure involves the removal of pelvic lymph nodes or lymphatic tissue through a small incision in the skin, guided by endoscopic visualization. The goal of the procedure is diagnostic, aiming to collect tissue samples for further examination and testing. A flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through the incision, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the pelvic lymph nodes and remove the necessary tissue. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may provide valuable information for diagnosing and staging certain conditions, such as cancer.
