Resection of Uterus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the uterus through a natural or artificial opening, either vaginally or laparoscopically, under general or regional anesthesia. The surgery may be performed to treat various conditions, such as uterine cancer, fibroids, or endometriosis, and may involve the removal of ovaries and fallopian tubes as well. The procedure typically requires a hospital stay of several days, and a recovery period of 4-6 weeks, during which time the patient may experience vaginal bleeding, discharge, and pelvic pain. Postoperative care includes pain management, wound care, and follow-up appointments to monitor for any complications.
