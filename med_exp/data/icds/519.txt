Removal of Intraluminal Device from Heart, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves removing an intraluminal device, such as a catheter or stent, from the heart through a small incision in the skin. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a healthcare provider inserts a specialized device through the incision to carefully extract the intraluminal device from the heart. The procedure is typically performed in a cardiac catheterization laboratory or an electrophysiology laboratory, and local anesthesia is usually sufficient to ensure patient comfort. The removal of the intraluminal device is often necessary due to device malfunction, obstruction, or infection, and the procedure aims to restore normal heart function and alleviate related symptoms.
