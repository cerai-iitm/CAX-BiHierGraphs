Extirpation of Matter from Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left knee joint to remove unwanted material, such as blood, infection, or foreign bodies, that may be causing pain, inflammation, or impaired mobility. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and extract the material, which may be done using specialized instruments or suction devices. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms, restore joint function, and promote healing. By removing the offending material, the procedure helps to reduce inflammation, improve range of motion, and enhance overall knee function.
