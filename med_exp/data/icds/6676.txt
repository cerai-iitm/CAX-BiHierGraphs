Resection of Bilateral Ovaries, Via Natural or Artificial Opening With Percutaneous Endoscopic Assistance
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of both ovaries through a small incision, utilizing a combination of natural or artificial openings and percutaneous endoscopic assistance to visualize the internal anatomy. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be recommended for patients with ovarian cancer, ovarian cysts, or other conditions requiring bilateral ovariectomy. The percutaneous endoscopic assistance enables the surgeon to accurately identify and dissect the ovaries, minimizing damage to surrounding tissues and promoting a faster recovery. The natural or artificial openings allow for a laparoscopic approach, reducing the risk of complications and scarring associated with traditional open surgery.
