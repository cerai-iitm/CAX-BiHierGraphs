Detachment at Right Upper Leg, Mid, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves detaching a body part or device from the middle section of the right upper leg through an open incision. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the detachment with precision. The procedure may be necessary to treat a variety of conditions, including traumatic injuries, infections, or failed surgical implants. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms in the affected leg.
