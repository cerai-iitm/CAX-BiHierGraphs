Excision of Thoracolumbar Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a thoracolumbar vertebral disc through an open approach, which means a larger incision is made to access the affected area. The surgeon will carefully excise the damaged or herniated disc, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissue and spinal nerves. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure on the spinal cord or nerves and alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or weakness. By removing the problematic disc, the surgeon aims to restore spinal stability and improve the patient's overall quality of life.
