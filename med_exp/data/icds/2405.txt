Resection of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right upper lung lobe through an open approach, providing direct visualization and access to the affected area. The surgery is typically performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, emphysema, or pulmonary fibrosis, which are confined to the right upper lobe. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a thoracotomy incision, usually in the fifth or sixth intercostal space, to access the chest cavity. The right upper lobe is then carefully dissected and removed, and the remaining lung tissue is closed and secured to ensure proper healing.
