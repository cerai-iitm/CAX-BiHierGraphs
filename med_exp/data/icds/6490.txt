Drainage of Right Ovary, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a small incision in the abdominal wall to access the right ovary, allowing the surgeon to drain any abnormal fluid or blood accumulation. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the ovary and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the source of the issue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure optimal outcomes. Following drainage, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any post-operative complications.
