Revision of External Fixation Device in Left Tibia, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves modifying an existing external fixation device in the left tibia bone to improve its stability and alignment. The external approach is used, where the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the fixator and make necessary adjustments. The revision may include replacing or repositioning pins, rods, or other components to ensure proper alignment and stabilization of the tibia. The goal of the procedure is to promote optimal healing and restore normal function to the affected leg.
