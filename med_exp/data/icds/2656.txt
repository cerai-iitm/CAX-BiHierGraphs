Destruction of Ascending Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the destruction of the ascending colon through a natural or artificial opening, utilizing specialized instruments and visualization technology to access and remove damaged or diseased tissue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or sedation to ensure patient comfort and minimize risk. During the procedure, the endoscope is inserted through the natural opening, such as the mouth or anus, or through an artificial opening, such as a stoma, to reach the ascending colon. The goal of the procedure is to remove the affected tissue, relieve symptoms, and improve overall gastrointestinal health.
