Fluoroscopy of Intracranial Arteries using Other Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the intracranial arteries, which are the blood vessels that supply the brain. A contrast agent, other than the standard iodine-based contrast, is administered to enhance the visibility of the arteries during the procedure. The fluoroscopy machine emits continuous X-rays, which produce real-time images of the arteries, allowing the physician to assess their structure and function. This diagnostic imaging test is used to diagnose and monitor various vascular conditions affecting the brain, such as arteriovenous malformations, aneurysms, or vasospasm.
