Excision of Pituitary Gland, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a craniotomy, a hole in the skull, to access the pituitary gland, which is located at the base of the brain. The gland is then carefully removed, often to treat conditions such as pituitary tumors, hyperpituitarism, or Cushing's disease. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the gland and surrounding tissues, ensuring complete removal of the affected gland. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for post-operative care and monitoring.
