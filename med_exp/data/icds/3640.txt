Drainage of Right Lower Leg Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right lower leg to drain accumulated fluid, abscess, or hematoma. The goal is to relieve pressure, prevent infection, and promote healing. The external approach allows the surgeon to access the affected area directly, minimizing risk to surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may require sutures or dressings to close the wound.
