Supplement Left Hip Joint, Acetabular Surface with Liner, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a liner is inserted onto the acetabular surface of the left hip joint to supplement and reinforce the joint, enhancing its stability and functionality. This involves making an incision in the affected area to access the joint, followed by the careful placement of the liner onto the acetabular surface. The liner serves as a replacement for damaged or worn-out joint tissue, promoting smoother movement and reducing discomfort. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain and stiffness, restoring the patient's mobility and overall quality of life.
