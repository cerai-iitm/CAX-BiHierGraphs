Excision of Left Neck Lymphatic, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a sample of tissue or cells from the left neck lymphatic system through a small incision in the skin. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery and less scarring compared to open surgery. The diagnostic excision is typically performed under local anesthesia, and the extracted tissue is sent to a laboratory for examination to diagnose potential diseases or conditions. The procedure is usually done in an outpatient setting and may take around 30 minutes to an hour to complete.
