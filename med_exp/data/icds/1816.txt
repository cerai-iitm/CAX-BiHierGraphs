Resection of Thorax Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a small incision in the skin to visualize the thorax lymphatic system. Under endoscopic guidance, a specialized instrument is used to remove a portion of the affected lymphatic tissue or lesion. This percutaneous approach allows for reduced tissue trauma and scarring compared to open surgical techniques. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia or conscious sedation, allowing for faster recovery and less postoperative pain.
