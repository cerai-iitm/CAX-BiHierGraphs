Reposition Left Mandible, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left mandible, or lower jawbone, through an external approach, typically performed under general anesthesia. The surgery is usually necessary to correct conditions such as temporomandibular joint disorders, facial asymmetry, or improper alignment of the jaw. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the mandible, repositions it to the correct alignment, and secures it in place using plates, screws, or wires. Post-operative care typically includes a liquid diet, pain management, and physical therapy to restore normal jaw function and alleviate discomfort.
