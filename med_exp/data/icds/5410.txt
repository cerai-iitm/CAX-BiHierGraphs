Reposition Right Acetabulum, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right acetabulum, the cup-like socket of the hip joint, to its natural anatomic position. An external approach is used, where the surgeon makes an incision on the outer aspect of the right hip to access the affected area. The acetabulum is then manually repositioned to restore proper alignment and function of the hip joint. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as hip dislocation or acetabular fractures.
