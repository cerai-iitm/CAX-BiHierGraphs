Release Stomach, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the stomach, followed by the manual release of adhesions or scar tissue that may be binding the stomach, allowing for restoration of normal anatomical alignment and function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the stomach and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any anatomical abnormalities. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as gastric volvulus, stomach torsion, or internal herniation, which can cause symptoms including abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms, restore normal stomach function, and prevent complications.
