Excision of Lung Lingula, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the lingula, a small lobe-like structure in the upper lobe of the left lung. Through a small incision, the endoscope is inserted into the chest cavity, allowing the healthcare professional to locate and remove the lingula for diagnostic purposes. The entire procedure is performed under image guidance, ensuring precise removal of the targeted tissue while minimizing trauma to surrounding areas. The lingula tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and analysis to aid in diagnosis.
