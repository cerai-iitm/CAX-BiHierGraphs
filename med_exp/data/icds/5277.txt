Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Right Upper Femur, Percutaneous Approach
During this procedure, a surgical incision is made through the skin and underlying tissues to access the right upper femur. A device is then inserted through the incision to stabilize and support the femur, using a percutaneous approach, which involves making small incisions to minimize tissue damage. The internal fixation device is carefully positioned within the bone to promote healing and restore function. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the device.
