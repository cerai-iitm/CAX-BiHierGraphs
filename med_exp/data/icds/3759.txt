Release Back Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the back to release tension or constriction, allowing for improved mobility and reduced discomfort. The external approach is used to access the affected area, and the procedure may be performed to treat a range of conditions, including contractures, scars, or skin lesions. The release of the skin allows for a greater range of motion and can alleviate symptoms such as pain or numbness. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and may require post-operative care to ensure proper healing and minimize the risk of complications.
