Drainage of Face Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the face, allowing for the drainage of accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances. The open approach enables direct visualization of the affected area, allowing the surgeon to identify and address any underlying issues. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health. The goal of the procedure is to resolve any underlying conditions, such as abscesses or hematomas, and promote healing and tissue repair in the affected area.
