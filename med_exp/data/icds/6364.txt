Extirpation of Matter from Right Kidney, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During a minimally invasive procedure, a flexible endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to access the right kidney, allowing for visualization of the affected area. The scope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the removal of unwanted matter, such as stones or tissue, from the kidney. This endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort.
