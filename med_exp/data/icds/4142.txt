Extraction of Right Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right foot to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles and other soft tissues. The damaged or diseased tissue is then carefully extracted, and the area is cleaned and prepared for closure. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as infections, abscesses, or tumors that affect the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the right foot. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the damaged tissue.
