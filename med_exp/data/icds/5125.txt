Reposition Left Finger Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning a displaced phalanx bone in the left finger to its normal anatomical position. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to realign the bone and secure it with an internal fixation device. The internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, is implanted to provide Stability and support to the repositioned bone, promoting proper healing and alignment. The goal of this procedure is to restore optimal finger function and alleviate associated symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility.
