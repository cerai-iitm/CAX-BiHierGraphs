Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Radius, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically removing an internal fixation device, such as a plate, screws, or rod, from the left radius bone in the forearm through an open incision. The fixation device is typically used to stabilize the bone after a fracture or osteotomy, and is removed once the bone has healed sufficiently. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the fixation device, carefully dissects the surrounding soft tissue, and removes the device in its entirety. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is typically able to return to normal activities within a few weeks.
