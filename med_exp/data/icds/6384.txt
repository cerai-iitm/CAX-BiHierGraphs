Fragmentation in Right Ureter, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the right ureter and locate the fragment in need of removal. Through a natural or artificial opening, the endoscope equipped with specialized instruments is inserted to carefully break down the fragment into smaller pieces, allowing for their safe and effective removal. This technique helps to alleviate blockages and restore normal urinary function. The endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional surgical methods.
