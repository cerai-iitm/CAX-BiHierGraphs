Excision of Right Middle Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove a section of the right middle lung lobe through a small incision in the skin. This percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery and less scarring compared to open surgery. Using imaging guidance, a small instrument is inserted through the incision to excise the targeted lung tissue, which is then sent for diagnostic examination. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort during the operation.
