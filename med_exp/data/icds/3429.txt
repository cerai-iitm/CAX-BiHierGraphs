Excision of Pancreas, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the pancreas, allowing the surgeon to visualize the organ and surrounding tissue. The pancreatic tissue is then carefully dissected and removed, taking care to avoid damaging nearby blood vessels and structures. Depending on the location and extent of the disease or lesion, the surgeon may remove a portion of the pancreas or the entire organ. The incision is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, and the patient is closely monitored for any complications.
