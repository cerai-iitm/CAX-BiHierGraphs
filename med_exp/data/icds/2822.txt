Excision of Stomach, Pylorus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the stomach and pylorus, which is the lower part of the stomach that connects to the small intestine. Through a natural or artificial opening, the endoscope is guided to the stomach, allowing the physician to inspect the area and identify any abnormalities. Once the area is visualized, the physician proceeds to excise, or remove, the affected portion of the stomach and pylorus using specialized instruments inserted through the endoscope. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety.
