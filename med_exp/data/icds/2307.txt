Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Tracheobronchial Tree, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
During this procedure, a flexible tube or scope is guided through the mouth or nose, or through a tracheostomy if present, to access the tracheobronchial tree. A small device, such as a stent, is then inserted through the scope to restore airflow or relieve obstruction in the airways. The device is carefully positioned and deployed within the tracheobronchial tree to ensure proper placement and optimal results. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize discomfort and ensure patient comfort.
