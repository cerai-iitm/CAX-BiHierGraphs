Extirpation of Matter from Left Cephalic Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left cephalic vein, a superficial vein located in the arm, to remove harmful or unwanted substances, such as clots, infections, or foreign bodies, from the vessel. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the vein and manually remove the matter, restoring blood flow and promoting healing. The procedure may be performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the individual case, and is often necessary to treat conditions such as deep vein thrombosis, abscesses, or injuries affecting the vein. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
