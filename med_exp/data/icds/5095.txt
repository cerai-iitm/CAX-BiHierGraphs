Reposition Right Ulna with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During this open approach procedure, the surgeon accesses the right ulna through an incision to reposition the bone into its normal anatomical alignment. The fractured or displaced segments of the ulna are manipulated and restored to their correct position, ensuring proper alignment and stability. The repositioned bone is then secured with an internal fixation device, which may include plates, screws, or rods, to maintain the corrected position and facilitate healing. The internal fixation device provides structural support to the repositioned bone, promoting optimal bone growth and fusion.
