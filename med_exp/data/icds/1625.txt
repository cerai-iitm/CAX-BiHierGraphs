Inspection of Lower Vein, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visually examine the veins in the lower extremities. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, allows the healthcare provider to directly inspect the internal structures of the veins, enabling the detection of any abnormalities, such as blood clots or venous insufficiency. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for a large incision, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, and the entire process usually takes around 30 minutes to an hour to complete.
