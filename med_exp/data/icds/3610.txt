Drainage of Face Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves the external drainage of skin on the face to remove excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may have accumulated due to injury, infection, or other conditions. The external approach allows the surgeon to access the affected area directly through the skin, making it a less invasive procedure compared to other methods. During the procedure, the skin is cleaned and prepared, and the drainage site is identified and accessed. The fluid or substance is then carefully drained, and the site is closed, with the goal of promoting healing and resolving the underlying issue.
