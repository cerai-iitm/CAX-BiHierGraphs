Excision of Liver, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to guide the removal of a sample of liver tissue through the skin. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the instrument. A small incision is made in the skin, and a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to collect the liver tissue sample. The sample is then sent to a laboratory for examination to help diagnose certain liver conditions or diseases.
