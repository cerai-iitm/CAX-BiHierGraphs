Extirpation of Matter from Right Lower Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the right lower lobe bronchus, allowing the surgeon to locate and remove accumulated matter such as mucous plugs, blood clots, or foreign objects that may be obstructing airflow. Through a natural or artificial opening, the endoscope is guided into the bronchus, providing a clear view of the affected area. The extirpation of matter is performed using specialized instruments inserted through the endoscope, ensuring a precise and gentle removal of the obstructing material. This procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure a successful outcome.
