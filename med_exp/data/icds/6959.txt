Excision of Mediastinum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Percutaneous endoscopic excision of the mediastinum is a minimally invasive procedure that utilizes an endoscope to visualize the mediastinal tissue. This approach allows for precise dissection and removal of abnormal or diseased tissue through small incisions, reducing trauma to surrounding structures and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of specialized instruments, such as graspers or cutting instruments, to facilitate tissue removal. The excised tissue is then sent for pathological examination to determine the underlying cause of the condition.
