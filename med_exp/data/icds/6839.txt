Control Bleeding in Respiratory Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visually examine the respiratory tract and identify the source of bleeding. Through the endoscope, specialized instruments are inserted via natural or artificial openings to control bleeding in the lungs, trachea, or bronchi. The procedure allows for real-time visualization of the bleeding site, enabling the healthcare provider to precisely target the bleeding vessel or area. By using this minimally invasive approach, patients can benefit from reduced bleeding, improved oxygenation, and a faster recovery.
