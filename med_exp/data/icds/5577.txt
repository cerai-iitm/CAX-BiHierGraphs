Drainage of Right Shoulder Joint, Open Approach
The procedure involves making an incision in the skin over the right shoulder joint to expose the affected area, allowing the physician to drain excess fluid or debris that has accumulated in the joint space. This open approach enables direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, facilitating the removal of any inflammatory substances or infectious agents that may be contributing to pain, swelling, or limited mobility. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure and restore joint function by removing the accumulated fluid, which can also help to reduce pain and inflammation. Following the drainage, the incision is typically closed, and the wound is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
