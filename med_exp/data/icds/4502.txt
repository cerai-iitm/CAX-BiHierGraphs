Repair Right Lower Arm and Wrist Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the muscles of the right lower arm and wrist, followed by the repair of damaged or injured muscle tissue using sutures or other fixation devices. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and address the specific muscle damage. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal muscle function, strength, and mobility to the right arm and wrist. By repairing the damaged muscles, this procedure can help alleviate pain, improve range of motion, and enhance overall upper limb function.
