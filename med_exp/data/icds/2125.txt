Drainage of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to drain accumulated fluid or air from the left upper lung lobe via a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided to the affected area, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the lung tissue and identify the source of the accumulation. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The goal of the procedure is to relieve respiratory distress and promote healing by removing the obstruction and allowing the lung to expand normally.
