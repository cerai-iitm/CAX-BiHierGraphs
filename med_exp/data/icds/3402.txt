Excision of Right Hepatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
Here is the description:

During an endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or a small incision to access the right hepatic duct. The duct is then excised, or surgically removed, to treat conditions such as bile duct obstruction or injury. This minimally invasive approach allows for a more precise and controlled removal of the affected duct, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety throughout the operation.
