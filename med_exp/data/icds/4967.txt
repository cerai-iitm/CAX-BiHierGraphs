Excision of Left Clavicle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the left clavicle, a long bone located between the shoulder blade and the sternum. The objective of the operation is to remove all or part of the left clavicle, which may be necessary due to trauma, bone disease, or other conditions. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the excision with precision. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve a hospital stay, followed by a period of rehabilitation to restore strength and mobility.
