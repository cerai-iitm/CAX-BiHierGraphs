Excision of Duodenum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the surgical removal of the duodenum, the first part of the small intestine, through a natural or artificial opening in the body. The duodenum is a critical structure responsible for digestion and nutrient absorption, and its excision is typically performed to treat severe diseases or injuries affecting the organ. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and involves a team of healthcare professionals, including surgeons, anesthesiologists, and nurses. The diagnostic nature of this procedure suggests that it may be performed to obtain tissue samples for further examination, to determine the extent of disease or injury, or to guide future treatment decisions.
