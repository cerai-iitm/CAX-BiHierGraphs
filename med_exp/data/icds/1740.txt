Excision of Thorax Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of lymphatic tissue in the thorax area through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach, which requires only small incisions. A scope with a camera and specialized instruments are inserted through the incisions to visualize and excise the affected lymphatic tissue. This technique allows for a less invasive and potentially less painful alternative to open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
