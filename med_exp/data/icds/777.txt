Extirpation of Matter from Right Internal Carotid Artery, Bifurcation, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the right internal carotid artery through the skin to remove matter or debris that may be obstructing blood flow or causing damage. Using imaging guidance, a specialist inserts a catheter through a small incision to reach the bifurcation of the artery, where the carotid artery divides into internal and external branches. The matter is then carefully extracted through the catheter, and the incision is closed. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as atherosclerosis, thrombosis, or embolism, which can lead to stroke or other serious complications.
