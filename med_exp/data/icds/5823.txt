Reposition Left Finger Phalangeal Joint, External Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to gain access to the affected phalangeal joint in the left finger. The joint is then manually repositioned to its normal anatomical alignment to restore proper finger function and alleviate any associated pain or discomfort. The joint is stabilized in its new position using fixation devices, such as pins or screws, to allow for proper healing. The incision is then closed, and the finger is immobilized in a splint or cast to facilitate the recovery process.
