Drainage of Pelvic Cavity, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin and soft tissues of the pelvis to evacuate infected fluid or abscesses from the pelvic cavity. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery time and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgical drainage. Under imaging guidance, the drainage tube is precisely placed to target the affected area, and any collected fluid or debris is sent for further analysis to guide further treatment. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, and patients can usually resume their normal activities within a few days.
