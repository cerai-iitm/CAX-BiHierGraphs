Excision of Transverse Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the transverse colon, which is the middle section of the large intestine, through small incisions using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope is inserted through the abdominal wall, allowing the surgeon to visualize the colon and perform the excision without the need for a large open incision. This percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery. The excised tissue is then removed through the same small incision, leaving minimal scarring.
