Ultrasonography of Right Subclavian Vein, Guidance
This non-invasive medical imaging procedure utilizes high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the right subclavian vein, a major blood vessel located beneath the collarbone. During the process, a handheld transducer is gently moved over the skin, emitting ultrasonic waves that bounce off the vein and return to the transducer, converting them into real-time images on a monitor. This guidance technique allows healthcare professionals to accurately visualize the vein and surrounding tissue, facilitating the placement of medical devices such as catheters and guidewires. The resulting images provide valuable information for diagnosing and treating conditions affecting the subclavian vein, such as blood clots or narrowing of the vessel.
