Extirpation of Matter from Right Posterior Tibial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove unwanted material from the right posterior tibial artery, a vital blood vessel in the lower leg. Through a small skin incision, a catheter or other specialized device is inserted to access the affected artery. The blockage or debris is then carefully extracted or broken up to restore proper blood flow to the lower leg. This percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster recovery.
