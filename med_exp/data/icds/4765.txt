Extraction of Right Elbow Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to the right elbow, where the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area. The goal is to extract or remove the inflamed or damaged bursa, a fluid-filled sac that reduces friction between moving parts, as well as any affected ligaments, which connect bones to each other. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to carefully remove the affected tissues and repair any damaged structures. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote optimal recovery.
