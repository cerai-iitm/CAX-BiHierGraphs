Manual Extraction of Products of Conception, Retained, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the manual removal of retained products of conception from the uterus through the cervix, which can be done through a natural or artificial opening. The goal of this procedure is to complete a miscarriage, remove fetal tissue, or manage a missed abortion. The procedure typically involves a gentle scraping of the uterine lining with a curette, usually under anesthesia or conscious sedation. The extraction is typically performed in a clinical setting by a trained healthcare provider to minimize the risk of complications and promote a safe and effective outcome.
