Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Renal Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure uses low osmolar contrast material to visualize the bilateral renal arteries, which supply blood to the kidneys. Fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, is employed to guide the injection of contrast material through a catheter, allowing for real-time imaging of the arterial structures. The resulting images provide valuable information on blood flow, vessel morphology, and any potential blockages or stenoses in the renal arteries. The low osmolar contrast agent helps minimize the risk of adverse reactions and is gentler on the kidneys compared to traditional contrast media.
