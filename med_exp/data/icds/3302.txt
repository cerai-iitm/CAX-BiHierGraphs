Dilation of Common Hepatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting an endoscope through the mouth or a previously created opening to access the common hepatic duct. The duct is then dilated using specialized instruments and balloons to widen its narrow passages, allowing for improved bile flow and relief from blockages or obstructions. During the procedure, the endoscope provides visualization of the duct, enabling the physician to accurately identify the area requiring dilation. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bile drainage and alleviate symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and fever.
