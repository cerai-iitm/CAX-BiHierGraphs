Fluoroscopy of Single Coronary Artery Bypass Graft using Low Osmolar Contrast
During this radiological procedure, a single coronary artery bypass graft is visualized using low osmolar contrast medium under fluoroscopic guidance, allowing for real-time imaging of the graft's anatomy and functionality. The low osmolar contrast agent is injected through a catheter into the graft, enhancing its visibility on the fluoroscope screen. This procedure helps diagnose graft stenosis, occlusion, or other complications, and is typically performed in conjunction with coronary angiography to provide a comprehensive assessment of the coronary circulation. The resulting images facilitate accurate diagnosis and treatment planning for patients with coronary artery disease.
