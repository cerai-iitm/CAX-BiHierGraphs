Extraction of Endometrium, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a sample of tissue from the lining of the uterus, known as the endometrium, for diagnostic purposes. The tissue is extracted through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for a minimally invasive approach. The extracted tissue is then sent to a laboratory for examination to help diagnose conditions affecting the uterus, such as abnormal bleeding, infertility, or cancer. The diagnostic information obtained from this procedure aids in guiding further treatment and management of related disorders.
