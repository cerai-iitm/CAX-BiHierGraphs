Insertion of Spacer into Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an orthopedic surgeon makes an incision in the left knee to access the joint space. A spacer, typically made of antibiotic-infused cement or metal, is then inserted into the joint to maintain the natural space between the knee bones. This is usually done to treat severe knee infections, such as osteomyelitis, or to prepare the joint for future implantation of a prosthetic device. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissue to ensure proper placement of the spacer and debridement of infected tissue.
