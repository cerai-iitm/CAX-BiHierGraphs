Drainage of Left Fallopian Tube, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the left fallopian tube, allowing a surgeon to directly visualize and drain any abnormal fluid or debris that may be causing obstruction or infection. The open approach provides a clear view of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely locate and remove the blockage or infected material. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal functioning of the fallopian tube and alleviate symptoms such as pelvic pain, abnormal bleeding, or infertility. Following drainage, the incision is closed and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
