Inspection of Gallbladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visually examine the gallbladder, allowing for direct visualization of the organ and its contents. The endoscope is equipped with a light and camera, which transmit images to a video monitor, enabling the healthcare provider to assess the gallbladder's anatomy and identify any abnormalities. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. During the procedure, the healthcare provider may also collect tissue samples or remove small stones or debris from the gallbladder for further examination.
