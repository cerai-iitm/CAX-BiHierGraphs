Drainage of Spinal Canal, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a needle or small instrument through the skin to drain excess cerebrospinal fluid or blood from the spinal canal. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery time and less tissue damage compared to open surgical methods. Under fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance, the healthcare provider carefully accesses the spinal canal to remove the unwanted fluid, which can help alleviate symptoms such as headache, nausea, and vomiting. Following the procedure, the patient is typically monitored for a short period to ensure there are no complications or adverse reactions.
