Supplement Spinal Meninges with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the spinal meninges with a tissue substitute derived from the patient's own body, which is harvested and prepared before being implanted to repair or augment the protective membranes surrounding the spinal cord. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the meninges and perform the supplementation. The autologous tissue substitute is used to enhance the structural integrity and function of the spinal meninges, promoting healthy tissue growth and repair. By using the patient's own tissue, the risk of rejection and immunological reaction is minimized, ensuring a more successful and safer outcome.
