Immobilization of Left Upper Extremity using Cast
This procedure involves the application of a cast to immobilize the left upper extremity, typically to treat or stabilize a fracture, injury, or surgical site. The cast is carefully molded to fit the patient's arm, providing support and restricting movement to promote healing and reduce pain. The immobilization process may involve the use of plaster, fiberglass, or other materials, and may require adjustments or replacements as the patient's condition progresses. By immobilizing the left upper extremity, the cast helps to maintain proper alignment, reduce swelling, and facilitate a safe and effective recovery.
