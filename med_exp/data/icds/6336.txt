Excision of Right Ureter, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the right ureter, a muscular tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder. The ureter is then carefully dissected and removed, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and structures. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as ureteral obstruction, cancer, or damage, and to restore normal urinary function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to perform the excision with greater precision and control.
