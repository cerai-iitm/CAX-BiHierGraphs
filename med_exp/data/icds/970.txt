Bypass Left Femoral Artery to Posterior Tibial Artery with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass from the left femoral artery to the posterior tibial artery using autologous venous tissue, which is taken from the patient's own body. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and create a detour around any blockages in the arteries, restoring blood flow to the lower leg. The autologous venous tissue is used to create a graft that connects the two arteries, promoting efficient blood flow and reducing the risk of complications. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as leg pain, cramping, and numbness, and to prevent more severe complications like gangrene or amputation.
