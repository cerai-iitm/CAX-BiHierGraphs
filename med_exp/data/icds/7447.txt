Extraction of Products of Conception, Vacuum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This is a surgical procedure involving the removal of the products of conception from the uterus using a vacuum method through the natural or artificial opening of the cervix. The procedure is typically performed to terminate a pregnancy or to treat a miscarriage. During the procedure, a cannula is inserted through the cervix and connected to a vacuum device, which gently suctions out the pregnancy tissue from the uterus. The procedure is usually performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to minimize discomfort.
