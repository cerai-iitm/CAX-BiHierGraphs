Control Bleeding in Gastrointestinal Tract, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the gastrointestinal tract and control bleeding from ulcers, ruptures, or other sources. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and identify the source of bleeding. Once the bleeding site is located, the surgeon uses various techniques, such as suturing, cauterization, or clipping, to control the bleeding and prevent further blood loss. The procedure often requires careful dissection and mobilization of surrounding tissues to ensure complete control of the bleeding.
