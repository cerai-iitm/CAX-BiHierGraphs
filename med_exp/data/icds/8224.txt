Fluoroscopy of Gallbladder, Bile Ducts and Pancreatic Ducts using Low Osmolar Contrast
This imaging procedure utilizes low osmolar contrast to visualize the gallbladder, bile ducts, and pancreatic ducts in real-time using continuous X-ray beams. The low osmolar contrast agent is injected into the bloodstream, and the fluoroscope tracks its movement as it passes through the targeted areas, producing images that aid in the diagnosis of conditions such as gallstones, bile duct obstruction, and pancreatitis. The continuous X-ray beam allows for precise monitoring of the contrast flow, enabling clinicians to pinpoint potential blockages or abnormalities. The resulting images provide valuable information for guiding treatment decisions and intervention planning.
