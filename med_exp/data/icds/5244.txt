Excision of Right Toe Phalanx, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skin of the right foot to access the affected phalanx, which is a toe bone. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize discomfort and pain. The surgeon carefully excises the damaged or diseased phalanx, taking care to preserve surrounding tissues and minimize bleeding. The wound is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the foot is immobilized to promote healing and reduce the risk of complications.
