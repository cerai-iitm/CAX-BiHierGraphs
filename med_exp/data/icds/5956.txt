Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the lumbosacral joint, which is the joint that connects the last vertebra of the lower back to the sacrum, through a posterior approach. The posterior approach means that the incision is made in the back, allowing the surgeon to access the affected joint. The fusion is performed on the anterior column, which refers to the front portion of the spine, and is done through an open approach, meaning the incision is large enough for the surgeon to directly visualize the joint. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the joint and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, and weakness in the lower back and legs.
