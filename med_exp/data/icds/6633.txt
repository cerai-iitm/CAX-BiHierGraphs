Removal of Contraceptive Device from Uterus and Cervix, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the removal of an intrauterine device (IUD) from the uterus and cervix through the natural or artificial opening, typically performed in a healthcare setting by a trained medical professional. The procedure is usually done under local anesthesia and may involve the use of specialized instruments to gently grasp and remove the IUD. The removal process typically takes only a few minutes and is often performed in conjunction with a pelvic exam or other reproductive health services. After removal, the site is inspected for any signs of complications or bleeding, and the patient may be advised on post-removal care and follow-up appointments.
