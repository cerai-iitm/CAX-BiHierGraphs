Excision of Descending Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the descending colon, which is then surgically removed. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the abdominal cavity and surrounding tissues to ensure complete removal of the affected colon segment. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as colon cancer, diverticulitis, or traumatic injury to the descending colon. The removal of the descending colon may be performed in conjunction with other procedures, such as the resection of surrounding lymph nodes or repair of nearby tissues.
