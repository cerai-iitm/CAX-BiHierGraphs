Resection of Pelvis Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the pelvic region to access the lymphatic system, allowing the surgeon to remove a portion of the pelvic lymph nodes or lymphatic vessels. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as cancer, injury, or infection that affect the pelvic lymphatic system. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the affected lymphatic tissue. The procedure requires careful dissection and ligation of surrounding tissues to prevent bleeding and promote healing.
