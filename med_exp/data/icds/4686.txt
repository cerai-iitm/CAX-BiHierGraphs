Repair Right Shoulder Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right shoulder area to access the damaged tendon, allowing the surgeon to visualize the affected area and perform the necessary repairs. The tendon is then repaired using sutures or other surgical materials, and the incision is closed. The open approach provides a clear view of the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately assess the damage and make precise repairs. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal functioning and alleviate symptoms such as pain, weakness, or limited range of motion in the right shoulder.
