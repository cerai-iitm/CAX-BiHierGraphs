Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Common Carotid Arteries using Other Contrast
Here is the detailed description:

This radiologic procedure utilizes fluoroscopy to examine the bilateral common carotid arteries, which are vital vessels supplying oxygenated blood to the brain. During the procedure, a specialized contrast agent, other than the typical iodinated or barium-based agents, is administered to enhance visualization of the arteries. Real-time X-ray imaging (fluoroscopy) is then employed to guide the examination, allowing the practitioner to assess the arterial anatomy, identify any abnormalities, and diagnose conditions such as stenosis, occlusion, or arteritis. The resulting images provide valuable information for devising an effective treatment plan for patients with suspected carotid artery disease.
