Extirpation of Matter from Right External Iliac Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right lower abdomen to access the external iliac artery, a major blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the lower extremities. The surgeon then carefully removes any unwanted material, such as blood clots, plaque, or other debris, from the artery to restore blood flow and prevent further complications. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the artery and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the obstructing material. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any adverse reactions or signs of infection.
