Fluoroscopy of Right Kidney using Low Osmolar Contrast
This radiological procedure involves the use of low osmolar contrast media to visualize the right kidney, providing high-quality images to aid in diagnosis and treatment. The fluoroscopy machine produces continuous X-ray beams to capture real-time images of the kidney's internal structures. The low osmolar contrast agent is administered through a vein to highlight the kidney's anatomy, allowing healthcare professionals to identify any abnormalities or diseases. The resulting images help guide medical decisions, facilitating timely and effective interventions to address kidney-related issues.
