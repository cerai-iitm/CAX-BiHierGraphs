Drainage of Right Middle Ear with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the right middle ear to drain accumulated fluid or infected material, with the assistance of a drainage device to facilitate the removal of unwanted substances. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely locate and drain the affected region. The drainage device is carefully inserted into the ear to collect and remove the unwanted material, promoting healing and alleviating symptoms such as hearing loss, ear pain, and discharge. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal ear function, alleviate discomfort, and prevent further complications.
