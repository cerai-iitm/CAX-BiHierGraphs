Extirpation of Matter from Right Common Carotid Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the right common carotid artery, allowing for the direct removal of unwanted matter, such as plaque, blood clots, or other debris, that may be obstructing blood flow or causing damage to the artery. The open approach enables the surgeon to visually inspect the artery and manually extract the offending material, ensuring a thorough and precise removal. The ultimate goal is to restore healthy blood flow to the brain and surrounding tissues, potentially preventing strokes, aneurysms, or other serious complications. By extirpating the unwanted material, the procedure aims to improve overall cardiovascular health and reduce the risk of future vascular events.
