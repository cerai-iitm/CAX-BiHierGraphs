Extirpation of Matter from Left Hepatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of abnormal material or foreign substances from the left hepatic duct, a vital conduit for bile drainage from the liver. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the endoscope to access the duct and extract the unwanted matter. The use of an endoscope enables direct visualization of the duct and minimizes tissue trauma, promoting a faster recovery. By extirpating the obstructing material, this procedure can help alleviate symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and fever, and improve overall liver function.
