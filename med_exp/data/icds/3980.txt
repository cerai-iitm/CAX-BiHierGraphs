Drainage of Left Upper Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left upper arm to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, allowing for the drainage of excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may have accumulated in the area. The open approach enables the surgeon to visualize the affected tissue directly, facilitating the removal of unwanted material and promoting healing. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as infections, abscesses, or hematomas, and is often done under local or general anesthesia. Following the procedure, the incision is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the arm may be wrapped in a bandage to promote healing.
