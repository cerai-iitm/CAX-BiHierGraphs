Reposition Nasal Bone, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves manipulating the nasal bone to restore its normal position, with the surgeon making an external incision to access the bone. The goal is to correct deformities, irregularities, or misalignments of the nasal bone, which can be caused by injury, congenital conditions, or other factors. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully reposition the nasal bone to achieve a more normal alignment, which can improve respiratory function, facial aesthetics, and overall quality of life. The external approach allows for more direct access to the nasal bone, enabling the surgeon to make precise adjustments and corrections.
