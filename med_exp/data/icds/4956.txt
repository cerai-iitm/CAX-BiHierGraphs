Excision of Cervical Vertebra, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of a cervical vertebra through a small incision in the skin. Guided by imaging technology, the surgeon inserts specialized instruments through the percutaneous approach to carefully excise the targeted vertebra. The diagnostic purpose of this procedure is to examine the removed tissue for abnormalities or diseases affecting the cervical spine. By performing this procedure, the healthcare provider aims to identify the underlying cause of symptoms such as neck pain, numbness, or tingling sensations in the arms or legs.
