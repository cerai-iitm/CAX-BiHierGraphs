Extraction of Rectum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to access the rectum through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for visualization and diagnostic exploration of the rectal tissue. The endoscope is inserted through the anus or an existing stoma, and guided to the rectum, where it enables the physician to examine the mucosal lining and identify any abnormalities or lesions. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia, and may be used to investigate symptoms such as rectal bleeding, pain, or changes in bowel habits. The diagnostic information obtained during the procedure helps inform treatment decisions and guide further management of rectal conditions.
