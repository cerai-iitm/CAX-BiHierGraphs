Reattachment of Left Upper Extremity Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves reattaching a bursa, a fluid-filled sac that reduces friction between moving parts, and ligaments, which connect bones to bones, in the left upper extremity. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the damaged tissues. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate pain or discomfort caused by the detachment. Successful reattachment can improve range of motion, reduce inflammation, and enhance overall mobility in the affected arm.
