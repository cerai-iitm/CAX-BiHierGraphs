Occlusion of Right Vertebral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small intraluminal device through a percutaneous approach to occlude the right vertebral artery, typically performed to treat aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or fistulas. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the device is navigated through an incision in the skin to reach the target area in the vertebral artery. The device is then deployed to block blood flow, preventing further complications. Post-procedure monitoring is crucial to ensure the device is functioning correctly and to watch for potential complications such as stroke or bleeding.
