Extraction of Right Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
The surgical removal of subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the right hand involves an open approach, where the skin is incised to access the affected area. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such asymphedema, lipedema, or fibrosis, which cause abnormal growth or thickening of the subcutaneous tissue. The surgeon makes a precise incision to expose the underlying tissue, carefully excising the affected tissue and fascia to alleviate symptoms and improve hand function. Postoperatively, the wound is closed, and the hand is immobilized to promote healing and minimize complications.
