Extirpation of Matter from Right Radial Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the right radial artery, a vital blood vessel located in the forearm that supplies oxygenated blood to the hand. The purpose of the procedure is to remove foreign matter, such as a thrombus or embolus, that is obstructing blood flow through the artery. The surgeon carefully isolates the affected segment of the artery and extracts the offending material, taking care to minimize damage to the surrounding tissue. Once the blockage has been removed, the incision is closed and the artery is repaired, restoring normal blood flow to the hand.
