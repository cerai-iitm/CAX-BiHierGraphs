Reposition Right Upper Arm Tendon, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the right upper arm to access and reposition a tendon that has become displaced or malaligned, restoring its normal anatomical position. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately reposition and secure the tendon. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, improve range of motion, and restore normal function to the affected arm. By repositioning the tendon, the procedure can also help to prevent further injury or degeneration of the surrounding tissues.
