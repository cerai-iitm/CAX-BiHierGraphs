Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into the right pleural cavity through the skin, allowing for the administration of medications or fluids directly into the chest cavity. This percutaneous approach involves making a small incision in the chest wall, through which a catheter is guided into the pleural space. The infusion device is then secured in place, providing a safe and effective means of delivering therapeutic agents. This technique is often used to treat conditions such as pleural effusions, pneumothorax, or cancer.
