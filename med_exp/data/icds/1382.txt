Restriction of Lower Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous placement of an intraluminal device to restrict blood flow in the lower artery, typically performed to treat peripheral artery disease or critical limb ischemia. Under image guidance, a catheter is inserted through a small incision in the skin and navigated to the targeted area of the lower artery. The device, such as a stent or angioplasty balloon, is then deployed to compress or occlude the artery, reducing blood flow to the affected area. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and may require several hours of observation following the procedure.
