Release Peritoneum, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the peritoneum, a thin layer of tissue lining the abdominal cavity, is released from its adhesions or attachments to surrounding structures. This open approach involves making a incision in the abdomen to access the peritoneum, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the release. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal anatomy and alleviate symptoms such as bowel obstruction or abdominal pain caused by the adhesions. By releasing the peritoneum, the surgeon can help restore normal bowel function and reduce the risk of future complications.
