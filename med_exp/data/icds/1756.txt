Excision of Aortic Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of the aortic lymphatic system through an open incision. The aortic lymphatic system, located in the chest cavity, plays a crucial role in the immune system by helping to filter out toxins and diseases. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest wall to access the lymphatic tissue, and then carefully removes the affected or diseased portion. The goal of this surgery is to treat conditions such as lymphoma, cancer, or infection that have spread to the aortic lymphatic system.
