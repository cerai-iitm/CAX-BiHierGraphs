Release Ileum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the ileum, a portion of the small intestine. The goal of the operation is to release any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstructions that may be impeding the normal functioning of the ileum. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the affected area directly and take necessary steps to restore patency and mobility to the intestine. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to ensure adequate recovery and monitoring.
