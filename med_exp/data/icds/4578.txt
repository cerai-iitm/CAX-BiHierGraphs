Transfer Left Thorax Muscle with Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of muscle tissue, along with skin and subcutaneous tissue, from the left thorax region to a different bodily location. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the targeted muscle and surrounding tissue, which is then carefully dissected and mobilized for transfer. The procedure is typically performed to restore function or aesthetics in areas where tissue is damaged or deficient. The goal of the surgery is to achieve optimal reconstruction and functional outcomes, often in cases of traumatic injury, congenital defects, or tumor resection.
