Restriction of Thoracic Aorta, Ascending/Arch with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a specialized device through a small incision in the skin, guided by X-ray or ultrasound, to reach the thoracic aorta in the ascending or arch portion. The device is then deployed to restrict blood flow and reduce pressure on the aortic wall, helping to prevent further damage or rupture. The percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgical repair. The procedure is often performed to treat aortic aneurysms, dissections, or other vascular conditions that require a minimally invasive solution.
