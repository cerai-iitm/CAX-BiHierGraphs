Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Wrist Joint, Open Approach
The removal of internal fixation devices from the right wrist joint is a surgical procedure that involves an open approach, where an incision is made to access the affected area. This procedure is typically performed to remove metal implants, such as plates, screws, or rods, that were previously inserted to stabilize and treat fractures or other bone-related injuries. The surgical removal of these devices is usually done when they are no longer needed, as they can cause discomfort, irritation, or restrict movement. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal wrist function and alleviate any related symptoms.
