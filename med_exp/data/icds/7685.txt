Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Respiratory Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the administration of therapeutic substances directly into the respiratory tract through natural or artificial openings using endoscopic guidance. The substances can include medications, vaccines, or other agents designed to treat respiratory conditions such as chronic bronchitis, pneumonia, or lung cancer. The endoscope allows for visualization of the respiratory tract, enabling the precise delivery of the therapeutic substance to the targeted area. This minimally invasive approach can help reduce symptoms, improve lung function, and enhance overall respiratory health.
