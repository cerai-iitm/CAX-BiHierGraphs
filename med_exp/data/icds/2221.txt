Excision of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the left upper lung lobe through a small incision in the skin, utilizing a minimally invasive approach to reduce trauma to the surrounding tissues. The diagnostic nature of this excision aims to examine the lung tissue for abnormal cells or lesions, allowing for further evaluation and treatment planning. The percutaneous approach enables the physician to precisely target the affected area while minimizing scarring and promoting a faster recovery. The excised lung tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further analysis to determine the underlying cause of the condition.
