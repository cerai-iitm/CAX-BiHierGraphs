Extirpation of Matter from Right Femoral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this minimally invasive procedure, a healthcare provider uses a percutaneous approach to access the right femoral vein, a major blood vessel in the leg. Through a small incision in the skin, specialized instruments are inserted to remove unwanted matter, such as blood clots, from within the vein. The removal of this matter helps to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with vein blockages. This treatment is performed under image guidance, ensuring precise localization and removal of the unwanted material.
