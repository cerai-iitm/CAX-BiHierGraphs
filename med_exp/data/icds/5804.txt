Reposition Right Shoulder Joint, External Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the skin and underlying tissue to access the right shoulder joint from the outside. The joint is then manipulated to restore its normal anatomic position, alleviating any deformity or misalignment. The surrounding soft tissues, such as muscles and ligaments, may also be repaired or released to facilitate proper joint function. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, improve range of motion, and enhance overall joint stability and mobility.
