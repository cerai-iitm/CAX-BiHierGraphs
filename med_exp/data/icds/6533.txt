Excision of Bilateral Ovaries, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both ovaries through an open incision, allowing for direct visualization of the abdominal cavity. The open approach provides optimal access to the ovaries, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and excise the ovaries, while minimizing potential complications. The diagnostic nature of this procedure implies that it is performed to obtain tissue samples or to confirm a diagnosis, rather than solely for therapeutic purposes. The resulting absence of ovarian function may impact hormone production and reproductive capabilities, necessitating comprehensive patient counseling and follow-up care.
