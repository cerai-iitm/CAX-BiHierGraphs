Insertion of Monitoring Device into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert a monitoring device into the chest subcutaneous tissue and fascia using a percutaneous approach. This involves making a small incision in the skin, typically under local anesthesia, to access the targeted area. The device, often a sensor or electrode, is then carefully placed beneath the skin and secured in position to continuously monitor vital signs, cardiac activity, or other physiological parameters. The procedure is usually performed in an outpatient setting, and the device can be left in place for an extended period to facilitate ongoing monitoring and treatment.
