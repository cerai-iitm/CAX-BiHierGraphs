Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Heart, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves the open approach removal of a synthetic substitute, such as a prosthetic heart valve or a heart patch, that has been implanted in the heart. This procedure requires a sternotomy or thoracotomy to access the heart, and the synthetic material is carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding tissue. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal heart function and alleviate symptoms caused by the presence of the synthetic substitute. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require cardiopulmonary bypass to ensure adequate blood flow and oxygenation during the operation.
