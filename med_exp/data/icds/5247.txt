Excision of Left Toe Phalanx, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a phalanx bone from the left toe through an open approach, which requires a skin incision to access the affected area. The phalanx is one of the small bones that make up the toe, and its excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as osteoarthritis, bone deformity, or trauma. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and accurately remove the damaged bone. Following the procedure, the incision site is closed, and the toe is immobilized to promote healing and recovery.
