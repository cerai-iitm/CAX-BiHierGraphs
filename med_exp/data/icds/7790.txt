Measurement of Arterial Pressure, Peripheral, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a small sensor or cannula through the skin and into a peripheral artery, typically in the arm or leg, to measure arterial blood pressure. The sensor or cannula is connected to a monitoring device that displays the pressure reading in real-time, allowing healthcare providers to continuously track blood pressure changes. This percutaneous approach is often used in critical care settings, such as intensive care units, to closely monitor patients with hypertension, hypotension, or those at risk for blood pressure instability. The measured pressure readings can help guide treatment decisions, such as adjusting medication or fluid administration, to ensure optimal patient care.
