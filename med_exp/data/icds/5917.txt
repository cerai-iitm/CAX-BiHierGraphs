Extirpation of Matter from Left Hip Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an open approach is used to access the left hip joint, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area. The purpose of the procedure is to remove unwanted material, such as infectious debris, inflamed tissue, or foreign bodies, from the joint space. The open approach enables the surgeon to effectively extirpate the matter, thereby promoting healing and preventing further complications. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure optimal comfort and relaxation for the patient.
