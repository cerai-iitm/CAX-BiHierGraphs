Control Bleeding in Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a flexible endoscope is inserted through the abdominal wall to visualize the peritoneal cavity, allowing the healthcare provider to identify and control bleeding sources. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that can be used to stop the bleeding, such as electrocautery or injection of hemostatic agents. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort and minimize discomfort. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces recovery time and minimizes scarring compared to open surgical methods.
