Supplement Left Femoral Artery with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left femoral artery to access the damaged or blocked area. A synthetic substitute, such as a polyester or ePTFE graft, is then used to supplement the artery, restoring blood flow to the lower extremities. The synthetic material is carefully sutured into place, and the incision is closed, typically in a layered manner. The goal of this procedure is to improve blood circulation, relieve symptoms such as leg pain or cramping, and prevent further complications.
