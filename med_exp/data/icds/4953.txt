Excision of 3 or More Ribs, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of three or more ribs from the thoracic cavity through an open incision, providing surgeons with direct visualization and access to the affected area. The operation is typically performed to treat conditions such as thoracic trauma, tumors, or infections that affect multiple ribs, and may involve reconstruction or stabilization of the chest wall to maintain respiratory function. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a large incision in the chest wall to expose the ribs, carefully dissecting and excising the affected ribs while preserving surrounding tissues and nerves. The resulting defect may be closed using sutures, staples, or prosthetic materials to restore the structural integrity of the thorax.
