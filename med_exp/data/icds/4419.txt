Excision of Right Trunk Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the trunk muscle located on the right side of the body through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The operation is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. The surgeon carefully dissects the soft tissue to expose the trunk muscle, and then carefully excises the muscle, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves and blood vessels. The surgical site is then closed in layers, and the wound is dressed to promote healing and minimize the risk of complications.
