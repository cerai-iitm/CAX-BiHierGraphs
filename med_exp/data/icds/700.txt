Dilation of Left Common Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure is performed to widen the left common carotid artery, which supplies blood to the brain, to improve blood flow and relieve blockages. A small incision is made in the groin area, and a thin tube called a catheter is guided through an artery to the blocked area of the carotid artery. A small balloon or other intraluminal device is then used to enlarge the artery, allowing for improved blood flow to the brain. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes about 30-60 minutes to complete.
