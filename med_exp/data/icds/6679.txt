Resection of Right Fallopian Tube, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During a minimally invasive surgical procedure, a thin tube with a camera and light is inserted through a small incision in the abdomen to visually examine the reproductive organs. The surgeon uses specialized instruments to carefully remove the right fallopian tube, a slender muscular structure that connects the ovary to the uterus, through the same small incision. This percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced tissue damage, less post-operative pain, and a shorter recovery time compared to traditional open surgery. The resected fallopian tube is then removed from the body through the small incision.
