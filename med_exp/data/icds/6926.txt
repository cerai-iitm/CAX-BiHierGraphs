Drainage of Pelvic Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a drainage device into the pelvic cavity through the skin to remove excess fluid or infected material. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and scarring. The drainage device is carefully guided into the pelvic cavity using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement. The device is then secured in place to facilitate drainage and promote healing.
