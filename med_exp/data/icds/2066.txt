Dilation of Trachea with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an intraluminal device to dilate the trachea, or windpipe, through a natural or artificial opening in the airway. The device is inserted through the mouth or a tracheostomy tube, allowing the doctor to visualize the trachea using an endoscope. The device is then used to gently stretch and widen the trachea, helping to relieve narrowing or blockage of the airway. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or sedation to ensure the patient's comfort and safety.
