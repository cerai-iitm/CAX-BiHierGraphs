Extraction of Left Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the left hand through an open incision. The surgeon makes a deliberate cut in the skin to access the affected area, allowing for direct visualization and manual removal of the targeted tissues. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. Post-operatively, the wound is closed, and the hand is immobilized to promote healing and reduce the risk of complications.
