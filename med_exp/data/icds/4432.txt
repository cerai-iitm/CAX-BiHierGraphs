Excision of Right Hip Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the right hip muscle, where a portion of the muscle is then removed or excised. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the damaged or diseased muscle tissue. The excision may be performed to treat a range of conditions, including muscle trauma, infection, or tumors. Following the excision, the incision is closed, and the patient may require post-operative care to facilitate healing and recovery.
