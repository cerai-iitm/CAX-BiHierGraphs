Excision of Aortic Lymphatic, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This percutaneous procedure involves the removal of a sample from the lymphatic tissue surrounding the aorta, a major artery that arises from the left ventricle of the heart, to aid in diagnosis. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate needle placement. The diagnostic excision is usually done through a small incision in the skin, resulting in minimal tissue disruption and scarring. The extracted tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis of any potential disorders or diseases.
