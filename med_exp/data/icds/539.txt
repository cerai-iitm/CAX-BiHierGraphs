Repair Right Atrium, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the chest cavity to access the right atrium, a chamber of the heart that receives oxygen-depleted blood from the body. The surgeon repairs any defects or damage to the right atrium, which may include closing holes or repairing tears in the atrial wall. The procedure requires careful dissection and suturing to ensure proper blood flow and prevent complications. The goal of the repair is to restore normal cardiac function and improve overall heart health.
