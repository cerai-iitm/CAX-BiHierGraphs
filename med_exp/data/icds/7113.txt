Supplement Abdominal Wall with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open repair of an abdominal wall defect or hernia by supplementing the weakened area with a synthetic substitute, such as a mesh. The surgery is performed through a single incision in the abdominal wall, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the area and accurately place the synthetic material. The synthetic substitute is designed to provide long-term support and stability to the abdominal wall, reducing the risk of further herniation or complications. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for a period of time to ensure a smooth recovery.
