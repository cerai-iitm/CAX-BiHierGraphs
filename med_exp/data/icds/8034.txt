Fluoroscopy of Right Upper Extremity Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This non-invasive medical imaging procedure uses X-rays and a low osmolar contrast medium to visualize the arteries in the right upper extremity. During the procedure, a small amount of contrast agent is injected into a vein in the arm, which then flows through the arterial system, allowing the arteries to be visualized on a real-time X-ray screen. The procedure is performed under fluoroscopic guidance, which enables the healthcare provider to monitor the flow of contrast agent and capture images of the arteries from different angles. The resulting images help diagnose and treat conditions such as arterial stenosis, aneurysms, and arteriovenous malformations affecting the right upper extremity.
