Supplement Abdominal Wall with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is the description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope to guide the insertion of a synthetic material through a small incision in the skin to reinforce the abdominal wall. The endoscope allows the surgeon to visualize the area and accurately place the substitute material, reducing the risk of injury to surrounding tissues. The synthetic material provides additional support and stability to the abdominal wall, helping to repair or reinforce weakened areas. By using a percutaneous endoscopic approach, the procedure typically results in less tissue damage, scarring, and post-operative pain compared to open surgical methods.
