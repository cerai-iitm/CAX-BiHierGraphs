Excision of Right Lower Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a section of the right lower lobe bronchus, which is a passageway that allows air to flow into the lungs, through an endoscope inserted via a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and specialized instruments, allows the surgeon to visualize the bronchus and excise the affected area with precision. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as lung cancer, tumors, or other abnormalities that may be affecting the bronchus. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery.
