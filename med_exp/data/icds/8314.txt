Beam Radiation of Pelvic Bones using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This therapeutic procedure involves the use of high-energy photons, with an energy range of 1-10 million electron volts (MeV), to deliver beam radiation directly to the pelvic bones. The goal of this treatment is to destroy cancer cells or relieve pain and symptoms in patients with certain types of cancer or bone metastases affecting the pelvis. During the procedure, a linear accelerator or other radiation therapy equipment is used to produce the high-energy photons, which are then focused into a precise beam and directed at the targeted area of the pelvis. The treatment is typically performed in a series of sessions, with careful planning and monitoring to minimize radiation exposure to surrounding healthy tissues.
