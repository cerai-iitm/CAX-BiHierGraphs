Replacement of Ulnar Nerve with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open replacement of the ulnar nerve with a non-autologous tissue substitute, a synthetic or biological material sourced from a donor or created in a laboratory. The goal of this operation is to restore functioning to the affected nerve, which controls sensations and movements in the forearm and hand. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision to access the damaged ulnar nerve and carefully removes the affected segment, replacing it with the non-autologous tissue substitute. The substitute is then secured in place to facilitate healing and promote optimal nerve function recovery.
