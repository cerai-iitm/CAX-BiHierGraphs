Replacement of Right Inner Ear with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves accessing the right inner ear through an open approach, where the native inner ear structures are carefully removed and replaced with a synthetic substitute. The synthetic material is designed to mimic the function of the natural inner ear, restoring balance and hearing functions. The replacement procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a skilled surgeon with expertise in otolaryngology. Post-operative care includes close monitoring and rehabilitation to ensure optimal outcomes and minimal complications.
