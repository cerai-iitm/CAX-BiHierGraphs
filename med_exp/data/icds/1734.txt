Excision of Left Axillary Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left axillary lymph node, which is located in the armpit region, through an open incision. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the area and directly access the lymph node, ensuring its complete excision. The goal of the procedure is to remove cancerous or diseased tissue, and the excised node is typically sent for further examination to determine the presence and extent of disease. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort and promote optimal outcomes.
