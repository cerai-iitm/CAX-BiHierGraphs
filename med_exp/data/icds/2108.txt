Drainage of Left Lower Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the mouth or nose and into the airways to drain the left lower lobe bronchus. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, allows the physician to visually examine the bronchus and identify the area of blockage or infection. Using specialized instruments, the physician then removes the blockage or infected material, restoring airflow to the lung. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort during the procedure.
