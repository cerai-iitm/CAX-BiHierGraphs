Excision of Left Radial Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the left radial artery, a major blood vessel in the forearm. The artery is then carefully dissected and excised, or removed, from the surrounding tissue. The radial artery is a vital structure that supplies oxygenated blood to the hand, but its removal may be necessary in certain cases, such as to repair an aneurysm or to reconstruct a damaged portion of the blood vessel. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the artery and surrounding tissues, enabling precise excision and minimizing potential complications.
