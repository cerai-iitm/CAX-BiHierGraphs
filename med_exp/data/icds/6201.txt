Resection of Lumbosacral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower back to access the lumbosacral region, where the problematic disc is located. The goal of the operation is to remove the herniated or diseased disc that is compressing nearby nerves and causing pain, numbness, or weakness in the lower back and legs. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully remove the damaged disc tissue, relieving pressure on the surrounding nerves and allowing them to heal. Following the removal, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically required to undergo a period of recovery and rehabilitation to ensure a successful outcome.
