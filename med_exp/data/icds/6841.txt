Control Bleeding in Genitourinary Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
During open or endoscopic surgery, this procedure is performed to stop bleeding in the genitourinary tract, which includes organs such as the kidneys, ureters, bladder, and urethra. The surgeon uses various techniques to identify and ligate or cauterize the bleeding vessel, depending on the location and severity of the bleeding. The procedure may be performed through a natural opening, such as the urethra, or an artificial opening created by the surgeon. The goal is to restore hemostasis and prevent further blood loss, which can be life-threatening if left untreated.
