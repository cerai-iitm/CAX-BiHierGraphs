Resection of Bilateral Fallopian Tubes, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the pelvic cavity, where the fallopian tubes are located. The surgeon then carefully identifies and isolates the fallopian tubes, one on each side, and removes them entirely. The tubes are then sent to a laboratory for further examination. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the pelvic organs and facilitates the removal of the fallopian tubes, which can be an effective way to prevent pregnancy or treat certain gynecological conditions.
