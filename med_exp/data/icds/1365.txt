Restriction of Right Common Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through a small puncture in the skin to restrict blood flow in the right common iliac artery, which is a major artery that supplies oxygenated blood to the pelvis and legs. The device is guided to the targeted area using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure precise placement. Once in position, the device is expanded or deployed to narrow the artery, reducing blood flow and promoting treatment of conditions such as aneurysms or atherosclerosis. This percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and scarring, allowing for a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery.
