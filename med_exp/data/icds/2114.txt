Drainage of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to drain accumulated fluid or air from the right upper lung lobe through a natural or artificial opening. During the procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided to the affected area, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the lung lobe and perform the drainage. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that can remove fluid or air, helping to relieve respiratory distress and promote healing. The goal of this minimally invasive procedure is to restore lung function and improve oxygenation.
