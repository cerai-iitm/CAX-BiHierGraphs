Fusion of Right Ankle Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the right ankle joint to alleviate pain and instability, typically caused by arthritis, trauma, or previous injuries. During the open approach, the surgeon makes an incision to access the ankle joint, removes any damaged or diseased tissue, and prepares the joint surfaces for fusion. An internal fixation device, such as plates or screws, is then implanted to stabilize the joint and promote healing. Following the procedure, the ankle joint is immobilized to allow the fusion to take place, and the patient may require a period of non-weightbearing to ensure proper recovery.
