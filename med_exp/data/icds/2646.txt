Destruction of Esophagogastric Junction, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the esophagogastric junction, where the esophagus meets the stomach. Through the natural opening of the mouth or an artificial opening, the endoscope is guided to the target area, allowing the healthcare provider to accurately locate and destroy abnormal tissue or lesions at the esophagogastric junction. The destruction of tissue is typically achieved through various Energy-based modalities such as heat, cold, or electrical energy. This procedure is often performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety.
