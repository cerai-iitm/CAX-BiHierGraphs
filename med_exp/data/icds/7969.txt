Ultrasonography of Heart with Aorta, Intravascular
This non-invasive diagnostic procedure uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the heart and aorta from inside the blood vessels. It involves the insertion of a specialized ultrasound probe through the skin and into a blood vessel, typically in the leg or arm, which then emits sound waves that bounce off the heart and aorta, creating detailed images of their structure and movement. These images can help diagnose conditions such as aortic aneurysms, aortic dissections, and blood clots, as well as detect any blockages or narrowing of the blood vessels. The procedure is usually performed in a hospital or outpatient setting by a trained healthcare professional.
