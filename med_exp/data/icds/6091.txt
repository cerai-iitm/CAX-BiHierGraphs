Repair Right Ankle Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the right ankle joint, allowing for direct visualization of the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to repair any damaged or degenerated tissue, such as cartilage, ligaments, or bone, in order to restore normal function and alleviate pain and discomfort in the ankle joint. The open approach requires careful dissection and retraction of surrounding soft tissue to access the joint, and may involve the use of surgical instruments, such as osteotomes or rasps, to remove damaged tissue or reshape bone. The procedure may also involve the use of prosthetic devices, such as joint implants, or biological grafts to promote healing and restore joint stability.
