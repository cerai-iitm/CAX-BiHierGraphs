Resection of Cervical Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the cervical spine, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the damaged or herniated disc, and relieve pressure on the adjacent nerves or spinal cord. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain, numbness, tingling, or weakness in the neck, arms, and hands caused by the compressed or irritated spinal nerves. During the procedure, the surgeon takes great care to avoid damaging nearby structures, such as the spinal cord, nerves, and major blood vessels. After the disc removal, the surgical site is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for a successful recovery.
