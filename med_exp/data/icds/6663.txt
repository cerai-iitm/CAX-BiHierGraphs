Reposition Vagina, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves manually repositioning the vagina to its normal anatomical position through an external approach, often to correct vaginal prolapse or other anatomical abnormalities. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or sedation to minimize discomfort. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the perineum, the area between the vagina and anus, to access the vagina and reposition it. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal vaginal function, alleviate symptoms such as pain or discomfort, and improve overall quality of life.
