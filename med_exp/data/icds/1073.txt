Dilation of Right Femoral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
A percutaneous procedure is performed to dilate the right femoral artery, which involves making a small incision in the skin to access the artery. A specialized intraluminal device, such as a balloon angioplasty catheter, is inserted through the incision and guided to the narrowed or blocked portion of the artery. Once in place, the device is expanded to widen the artery, improving blood flow and relieving any blockages. The device is then removed, and the incision is closed, allowing the patient to recover and regain normal blood flow to the affected area.
