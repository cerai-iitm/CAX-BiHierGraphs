Reposition Right Fibula with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves repositioning the right fibula bone to its normal anatomical position using a percutaneous approach, which means that the surgery is performed through small incisions in the skin. A specialized device, called an internal fixation device, is used to hold the bone in place and promote healing. The device is inserted through the small incisions, and the bone is manipulated into its correct position under imaging guidance. The internal fixation device provides stable fixation, allowing the bone to heal properly and restoring normal function to the affected limb.
