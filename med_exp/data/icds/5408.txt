Reposition Right Acetabulum with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This orthopedic procedure involves repositioning the right acetabulum, the socket-shaped structure in the pelvis, to its correct anatomical position. The surgeon uses a minimally invasive, percutaneous approach, making a small incision in the skin to access the affected area. Under fluoroscopic or CT guidance, the surgeon inserts specialized instruments and an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screw, to stabilize the repositioned acetabulum and promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal alignment and function to the hip joint, alleviating pain and discomfort, and improving mobility and range of motion.
