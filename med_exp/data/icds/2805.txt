Excision of Esophagogastric Junction, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of a portion of the esophagogastric junction, where the esophagus meets the stomach, through a natural or artificial opening. The procedure is performed under visualization using an endoscope to guide the removal of the affected tissue. This diagnostic excision is typically performed to obtain tissue samples for further examination and diagnosis of potential abnormalities or diseases in this region. The endoscopic approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure with reduced risk of complications and faster recovery time.
