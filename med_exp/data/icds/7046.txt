Inspection of Genitourinary Tract, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This medical procedure involves using an endoscope to visually examine the genitourinary tract through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, allowing doctors to diagnose and treat conditions such as urinary tract infections, kidney stones, and bladder cancer. The endoscope is inserted through the skin, eliminating the need for open surgery, and provides a clear view of the tract's internal structures. During the procedure, the doctor may collect tissue samples or remove small stones or other obstructions, and can also perform therapeutic interventions as needed. The entire process is typically performed under local anesthesia, minimizing discomfort and promoting a quick recovery.
