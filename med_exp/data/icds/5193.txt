Excision of Left Pelvic Bone, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Percutaneous excision of the left pelvic bone involves a minimally invasive surgical approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the affected bone. Under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to remove the targeted portion of the bone. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed to obtain a tissue sample for further examination, helping to confirm a diagnosis or monitor the progression of a disease. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and enables faster recovery compared to open surgical methods.
