Reposition Left Femoral Shaft with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During an open surgical approach, the femoral shaft is manually repositioned to its normal anatomical alignment. This is typically performed to treat a fracture or deformity of the left femur, restoring the bone's natural shape and function. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, rods, or screws, are then implanted to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms, improve mobility, and enable the patient to resume normal activities.
