Cerebral Embolic Filtration, Dual Filter in Innominate Artery and Left Common Carotid Artery, Percutaneous Approach, New Technology Group 2
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting two filters, one in the innominate artery and one in the left common carotid artery, to capture blood clots and debris that may break loose during vascular interventions or surgical procedures, reducing the risk of stroke and cerebral embolism. Through a small incision in the groin, a catheter is guided through the artery to the target location, and the filters are deployed to filter out any embolic material. The filters are designed to capture embolic particles as small as 40 microns, providing comprehensive protection for the brain. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance, allowing for precise placement and real-time monitoring.
