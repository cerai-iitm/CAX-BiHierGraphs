Removal of Tracheostomy Device from Trachea, External Approach
Surgical removal of a tracheostomy tube from the trachea is a delicate procedure performed under sterile conditions to minimize the risk of infection. During the procedure, the healthcare provider carefully examines the tracheostomy site to ensure proper healing and removes the device through an external approach. The trachea is inspected for any signs of trauma or complications before the site is closed and dressed. After removal, the patient is monitored for any adverse reactions or breathing difficulties.
