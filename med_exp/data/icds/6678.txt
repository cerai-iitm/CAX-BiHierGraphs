Resection of Right Fallopian Tube, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower abdomen to access the pelvic cavity, where the right fallopian tube is located. The tube is then carefully dissected and separated from surrounding tissues and blood vessels. The damaged or diseased portion of the tube is removed, and the remaining healthy sections are repaired or reconnected as necessary. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, to complete the procedure.
