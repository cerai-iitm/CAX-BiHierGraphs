Insertion of Tissue Expander into Right Breast, Open Approach
During a surgical procedure, a tissue expander is inserted into the right breast through an open approach, which involves making a surgical incision to access the breast tissue. This procedure is often performed as a first step in breast reconstruction after a mastectomy or to correct a congenital breast defect. The tissue expander is a temporary device that is gradually filled with saline solution over time to stretch the breast skin and tissue, preparing it for a permanent breast implant. The goal of this procedure is to create a natural-looking breast shape and size, restoring the patient's natural appearance and confidence.
