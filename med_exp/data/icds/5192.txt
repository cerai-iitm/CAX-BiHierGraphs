Excision of Left Pelvic Bone, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissues of the left pelvic region to gain access to the underlying bone. The surgeon then carefully removes the affected portion of the left pelvic bone, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues and structures. The excised bone tissue is then removed from the body, and the incision is closed, typically with sutures or staples, to promote healing. In some cases, additional procedures, such as repair of adjacent soft tissues or implantation of prosthetic devices, may be performed concurrently to restore normal anatomy and function.
