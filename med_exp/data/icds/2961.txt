Extraction of Esophagogastric Junction, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of tissue or a lesion from the esophagogastric junction, where the esophagus meets the stomach, through a natural or artificial opening. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort. A flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through the mouth or an artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the area and extract the targeted tissue or lesion. The extracted tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in diagnosis and guide treatment.
