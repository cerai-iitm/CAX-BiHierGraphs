Repair Head Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the affected muscle, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect and repair damaged or injured muscle tissue in the head region. The open approach enables the surgeon to directly visualize the muscle and surrounding tissues, ensuring a more accurate and thorough repair. The procedure may be necessary to treat conditions such as muscle tears, lacerations, or other injuries affecting the head muscles, which can cause pain, weakness, or limited mobility. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal muscle function, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall quality of life.
