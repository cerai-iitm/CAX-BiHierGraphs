Resection of Left Ovary, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the left ovary through a small incision, using an endoscope to visualize the internal structures. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and minimal scarring. A laparoscope is inserted through the incision, providing a clear view of the ovary, and surgical instruments are used to carefully dissect and remove the organ. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as ovarian cysts, cancer, or endometriosis.
