Resection of Right Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right breast through an open approach, which entails making a single incision in the skin to access the breast tissue. The surgeon will carefully dissect the breast tissue to identify and remove the affected area, which may include the entire breast or a portion of it, depending on the underlying condition. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure the patient's comfort during the operation. The goal of the resection is to remove the diseased or cancerous tissue while preserving as much healthy tissue as possible, and may be followed by reconstructive surgery or other therapies as necessary.
