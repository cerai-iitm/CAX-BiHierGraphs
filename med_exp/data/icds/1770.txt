Excision of Right Inguinal Lymphatic, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
A minimally invasive diagnostic procedure involves the removal of a sample of lymph tissue from the right inguinal area using a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the lymphatic system. This procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate targeting of the lymph node. The sample is then sent to a laboratory for examination to aid in the diagnosis of various conditions, including cancer, infection, or inflammation. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal tissue disruption and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical techniques.
