Immobilization of Right Upper Arm using Splint
This procedure involves the application of a splint to immobilize and stabilize the right upper arm, typically to treat injuries or conditions such as fractures, sprains, or strains. The splint is carefully fitted and secured to maintain proper alignment and prevent further injury or discomfort. The immobilization process helps to reduce pain, swelling, and inflammation, allowing the affected area to heal properly. By restricting movement, the splint enables the arm to rest and recover, promoting optimal outcomes and facilitating a faster return to normal activities.
