Fluoroscopy of Right Common Carotid Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This radiologic procedure involves the use of low osmolar contrast to visualize the right common carotid artery through fluoroscopy, allowing for real-time guidance during the examination. The procedure provides high-quality images of the artery and its surrounding structures, enabling accurate diagnosis and treatment of vascular conditions. The use of low osmolar contrast reduces the risk of adverse reactions and improves patient safety. The fluoroscopic images are captured and recorded for further analysis and consultation with specialists.
