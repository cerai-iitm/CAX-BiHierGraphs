Release Duodenum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the duodenum, which is the first part of the small intestine. The duodenum is then carefully released from any adhesions or scar tissue that may be constricting it, allowing for normal digestive function to resume. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the duodenum and surrounding tissues, enabling precise dissection and release of the affected area. Once the release is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for post-operative recovery.
