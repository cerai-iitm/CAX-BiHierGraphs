Excision of Right Large Intestine, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical operation to remove the right large intestine through an open incision, typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort. The surgery requires a careful dissection of the affected area to identify and isolate the diseased or damaged portion of the intestine. The surgeon then carefully excises the targeted segment of the intestine, taking care to minimize trauma to surrounding tissues and preserve blood supply to the remaining healthy sections. The remaining healthy intestine is then reconnected, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing and restore normal bowel function.
