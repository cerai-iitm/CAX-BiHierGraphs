Removal of Contraceptive Device from Uterus and Cervix, External Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a contraceptive device, such as an intrauterine device (IUD), from the uterus and cervix using an external approach. The healthcare provider will typically perform a pelvic exam to locate the device and gently remove it from the uterus and cervix using specialized instruments. Depending on the type of device, local anesthesia or pain medication may be used to minimize discomfort during the procedure. After removal, the patient may experience some light bleeding or cramping, but this is usually temporary and resolves on its own within a few days.
