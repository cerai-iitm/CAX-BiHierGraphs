Dilation of Cervix, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves widening the cervix, which is the lower, narrow part of the uterus that opens into the vagina, to facilitate childbirth or other gynecological interventions. The dilation can be performed through natural or artificial openings, and may be done manually or with the aid of instruments such as dilators or tents. The purpose of cervical dilation is to allow for a safe and uncomplicated passage of the baby during childbirth, or to enable the insertion of instruments for diagnostic or therapeutic procedures. The extent of dilation required may vary depending on the individual case and the specific goals of the procedure.
