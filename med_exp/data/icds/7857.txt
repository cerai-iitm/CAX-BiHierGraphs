Performance of Cardiac Pacing, Intermittent
This procedure involves the temporary activation of a cardiac pacing system to regulate the heart's rhythm, ensuring a consistent heartbeat during periods of abnormal cardiac function. The cardiac pacing system is designed to stimulate the heart to beat at a normal rate, preventing bradycardia or other arrhythmias that can cause dizziness, fainting, or even cardiac arrest. During the procedure, the pacing system is set to intermittently stimulate the heart, allowing for normal cardiac function to resume when possible. This temporary intervention helps to stabilize the heart's rhythm and ensures adequate blood flow to the body.
