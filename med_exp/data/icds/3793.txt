Repair Finger Nail, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically repairing a damaged or injured fingernail using an external approach, where the healthcare provider makes an incision in the skin and nail plate to access the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to restore the nail's natural shape and function, and may involve removing debris or infected tissue, correcting nail bed lacerations, or reattaching a partially or completely detached nail. The procedure typically requires local anesthesia and may involve the use of surgical instruments such as scissors, forceps, and sutures. After the procedure, the nail is usually protected with a dressing or bandage to promote healing and prevent infection.
