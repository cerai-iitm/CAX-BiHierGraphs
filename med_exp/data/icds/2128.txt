Drainage of Lung Lingula, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to drain the lung lingula, a region in the upper lobe of the left lung, through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or a small incision, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the lung tissue and identify the area requiring drainage. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. Once the area is accessed, the healthcare provider uses specialized instruments to remove excess fluid or debris, restoring normal lung function and alleviating symptoms such as shortness of breath or chest pain.
