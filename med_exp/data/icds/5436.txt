Reposition Left Femoral Shaft with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves repositioning the left femoral shaft to its normal anatomic alignment through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The Orthopedic surgeon makes a small incision in the skin and inserts an intramedullary internal fixation device into the femoral shaft to stabilize and hold the bone in place. The device is designed to provide internal support and promote healing of the bone, allowing for early mobilization and rehabilitation. The percutaneous approach reduces tissue disruption and minimizes soft tissue trauma, resulting in less post-operative pain and a faster recovery time.
