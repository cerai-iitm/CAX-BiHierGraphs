Introduction of Hormone into Female Reproductive, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of hormones into the female reproductive system through a natural or artificial opening, typically to treat hormonal imbalances or fertility issues. The hormones are administered directly into the reproductive tract, allowing for targeted delivery and absorption. This approach can help regulate menstrual cycles, improve fertility, or alleviate symptoms associated with hormonal deficiencies. The procedure is usually performed under the guidance of a healthcare professional and may involve the use of specialized instruments or catheters to facilitate the delivery of the hormones.
