Excision of Uterus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the surgical removal of the uterus through small incisions using an endoscope, a thin, lighted tube with a camera, to visualize the uterus and surrounding tissues. The percutaneous approach allows for faster recovery and less scarring compared to traditional open surgery. During the procedure, the uterus is detached from surrounding tissues and blood vessels, and then removed through one of the small incisions. The remaining tissue is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, to complete the procedure.
