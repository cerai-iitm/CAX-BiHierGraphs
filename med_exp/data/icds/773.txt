Extirpation of Matter from Left Common Carotid Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the left common carotid artery, a major blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the brain. The goal of the procedure is to remove unwanted matter, such as a blood clot, plaque, or other debris, that may be obstructing blood flow and posing a risk to the patient's health. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the artery and gently remove the obstructing material, restoring normal blood flow to the brain and surrounding tissues. Following the procedure, the incision site is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any complications or adverse reactions.
