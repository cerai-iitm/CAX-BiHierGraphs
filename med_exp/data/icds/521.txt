Removal of Cardiac Lead from Heart, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the minimally invasive removal of a cardiac lead, a thin wire connected to a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator, from the heart through the skin. The percutaneous approach utilizes a small incision to access the cardiac lead, allowing the physician to gently pull out the lead from the heart tissue. Fluoroscopy or echocardiography may be used to guide the removal process, ensuring the lead is taken out safely and efficiently. After removal, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications.
