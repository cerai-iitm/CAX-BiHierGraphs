Extirpation of Matter from Peritoneal Cavity, Open Approach
Surgical removal of abnormal or infected material from the peritoneal cavity is performed through an open approach, which involves making a large incision in the abdominal wall to access the affected area. This procedure is typically done under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as peritonitis, abscesses, or foreign objects in the peritoneal cavity. The surgeon carefully dissects the abnormal material, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and organs. Once the material is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications.
