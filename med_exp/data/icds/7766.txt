Irrigation of Peritoneal Cavity using Dialysate, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of a catheter through the skin and into the peritoneal cavity to administer dialysate, a specialized fluid, which is then instilled to flush and clean the cavity. The goal is to remove waste products and excess fluids that can accumulate in the peritoneum, a membrane lining the abdominal cavity. During the procedure, the dialysate is allowed to dwell in the cavity for a period of time before being drained out, taking with it the waste products and excess fluids. This process is repeated multiple times to achieve optimal cleansing and relief from symptoms associated with peritoneal disease or dysfunction.
