Supplement Right Main Bronchus with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the chest cavity to access the right main bronchus, which is the airway that branches off from the trachea and leads to the right lung. A synthetic material is then used to supplement or reinforce the bronchus, providing additional support to the airway. This open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bronchus and ensure proper placement of the synthetic substitute. The goal of this procedure is to improve lung function and facilitate breathing by stabilizing the airway.
