Control Bleeding in Neck, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to visualize and control bleeding from damaged blood vessels or surrounding tissues. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the source of bleeding. The procedure may be performed in response to traumatic injuries, surgical complications, or other conditions that cause bleeding in the neck. By controlling the bleeding, this procedure can help restore blood flow, promote healing, and reduce the risk of further complications.
