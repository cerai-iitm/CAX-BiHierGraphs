Inspection of Upper Intestinal Tract, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visualize the upper intestinal tract, allowing for direct examination of the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum. The endoscope is equipped with a light and camera, which transmit images to a video monitor for the healthcare provider to view. This minimally invasive approach enables the diagnosis of various conditions, such as ulcers, tumors, and inflammation, without the need for surgical incisions. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation, ensuring patient comfort throughout the examination.
