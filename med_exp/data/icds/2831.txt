Excision of Duodenum, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the abdomen to access the duodenum, which is the first part of the small intestine that receives bile and pancreatic juices from the liver and pancreas. The affected portion of the duodenum is carefully isolated and removed, and the remaining healthy portions are reconnected to maintain normal digestive function. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and can take several hours to complete, depending on the complexity of the case and the individual patient's overall health. Postoperatively, patients often require close monitoring in the intensive care unit and a prolonged hospital stay to ensure a smooth recovery.
