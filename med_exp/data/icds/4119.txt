Extraction of Abdomen Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal skin to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue that lie just beneath the skin. The damaged or diseased tissue and fascia are then carefully removed, taking care to minimize disruption to surrounding tissues and structures. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as necrotizing fasciitis, a severe bacterial infection that can cause rapid tissue death. After the extraction, the incision is closed, and the wound is managed to promote healing and prevent complications.
