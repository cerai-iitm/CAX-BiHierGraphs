Measurement of Cardiac Electrical Activity, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of electrodes or sensors through the skin to measure the electrical activity of the heart. The purpose of this measurement is to diagnose and monitor various cardiac conditions, such as arrhythmias, palpitations, and atrial fibrillation. During the procedure, the electrodes or sensors are strategically placed to capture the heart's electrical signals, which are then recorded and analyzed to identify any abnormalities. The results of this measurement aid healthcare providers in developing an effective treatment plan to restore a normal heart rhythm and alleviate symptoms.
