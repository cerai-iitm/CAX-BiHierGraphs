Removal of Spacer from Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
The surgical removal of a spacer from the right knee joint involves an open approach, where an incision is made to access the joint. During the procedure, the spacer is carefully extracted from the knee joint, and the area is inspected for any remaining debris or infection. The spacer, often used as a temporary fix to maintain joint space and stability, is removed to facilitate further treatment or to allow for permanent joint replacement. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and spacer, enabling a more precise and effective removal process.
