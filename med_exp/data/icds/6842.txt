Control Bleeding in Genitourinary Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the genitourinary tract and identify the source of bleeding. Once the bleeding site is located, specialized instruments are used to control the bleeding through various techniques such as cauterization, ligation, or application of hemostatic agents. The procedure is typically performed through natural or artificial openings, such as the urethra, bladder, or via a surgical incision. The goal of the procedure is to restore hemostasis and prevent further bleeding in the genitourinary tract.
