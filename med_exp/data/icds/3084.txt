Removal of Feeding Device from Stomach, Percutaneous Approach
A percutaneous procedure is performed to remove a feeding device from the stomach, which is typically a gastrostomy tube or PEG tube. This procedure is usually done under local anesthesia and involves making a small incision in the abdominal wall to access the stomach. The feeding device is then carefully extracted from the stomach through the incision, and the site is closed with sutures or staples. The entire process typically takes about 30 minutes to an hour to complete.
