Extraction of Back Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the back subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of connective tissue that surround and support the muscles. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure the patient's comfort. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects and removes the targeted subcutaneous tissue and fascia, taking care to preserve surrounding tissues and structures. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
