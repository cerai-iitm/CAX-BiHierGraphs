Detachment at Right 3rd Toe, High, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to detach the right third toe at a high level, which means the separation occurs proximal to the phalanges. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to expose the toe. The detachment is achieved by cutting through the soft tissues and bone, allowing for the removal of the affected digit. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as severe toe deformities, infections, or trauma that necessitate amputation.
