Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Facial Bone, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of metal plates, screws, or rods used to stabilize facial bones, typically performed under general anesthesia. The operation involves a surgical incision to access the site where the internal fixation device was previously inserted, followed by careful dissection to expose the device. The device is then carefully removed, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues or nerves. The incision is subsequently closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
