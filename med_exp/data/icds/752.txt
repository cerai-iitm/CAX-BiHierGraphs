Extirpation of Matter from Right Brachial Artery, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the right arm to access the brachial artery, a major blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the arm. The purpose of the procedure is to remove unwanted matter, such as a thrombus or embolus, that is obstructing blood flow and potentially causing harm to the patient. The surgical team carefully extracts the obstructing material from the artery, taking care to minimize damage to the surrounding tissues. After the extirpation is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored to ensure proper healing and restoration of normal blood flow.
