Replacement of Bilateral Breast with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both breasts and replacement with synthetic implants through an open incision. The goal of the procedure is to restore the natural shape and appearance of the breasts, often performed for patients who have undergone mastectomy or have congenital deformities. The surgeon makes an incision in the breast area, removes the existing tissue, and inserts the synthetic substitute, carefully positioning and securing it to ensure a natural look and feel. The procedure requires general anesthesia and is typically performed in a hospital setting, followed by a recovery period to allow the body to heal and adjust to the new implants.
