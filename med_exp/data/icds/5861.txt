Drainage of Left Knee Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the percutaneous drainage of the left knee joint, a minimally invasive technique that uses a small incision to access the joint. A thin needle or tube is inserted through the skin and guided into the joint space to remove excess fluid or inflammatory material. The procedure is usually performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to relieve pressure, reduce pain and inflammation, and collect a sample of joint fluid for further analysis.
