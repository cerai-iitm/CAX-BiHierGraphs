Supplement Dura Mater with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During a complex neurosurgical procedure, a surgeon uses an open approach to access the dura mater, the protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord. The goal of the operation is to supplement the natural dura mater with a non-autologous tissue substitute, which is a synthetic or biological material derived from a donor or artificial source. This supplement is carefully implanted to reinforce the natural dura mater, providing additional support and protection to the delicate neural tissues. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the surgical site, ensuring precise placement of the tissue substitute for optimal outcomes.
