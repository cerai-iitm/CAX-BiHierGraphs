Introduction of Blinatumomab Antineoplastic Immunotherapy into Central Vein, Percutaneous Approach, New Technology Group 1
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

Blinatumomab, a bispecific T cell engager (BiTE) antibody, is administered into a central vein through a percutaneous approach. This immunotherapy targets CD19 and CD3 proteins to treat B-cell precursor acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) and relapsed or refractoryPhiladelphia chromosome-negative (Ph-) ALL. The percutaneous introduction allows for direct delivery of the medication into the central venous circulation, enabling effective distribution throughout the body. This novel approach enables the treatment of complex hematological malignancies with a high degree of precision and efficacy.
