Supplement Thoracic Aorta, Descending with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a catheter to introduce a synthetic substitute into the descending thoracic aorta to supplement its structure and function. Through a small incision in the leg, the substitute is guided through the blood vessels to the thoracic aorta, where it is deployed to reinforce the weakened area. This percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery. The synthetic substitute, typically a stent graft, expands to conform to the aorta's natural shape, providing a secure and durable repair.
