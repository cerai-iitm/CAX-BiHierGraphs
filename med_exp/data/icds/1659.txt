Removal of Intraluminal Device from Lower Vein, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the lower vein, where an intraluminal device, such as a stent or filter, is located. The surgeon then carefully removes the device from the vein, taking care to minimize damage to the surrounding tissue. The incision is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for any complications. The removal of the intraluminal device is usually performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort during the procedure.
