Excision of Left Lobe Liver, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the liver, followed by surgical removal of the left lobe. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the liver and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to carefully excise the affected lobe. The diagnostic purpose of the procedure is to obtain tissue samples for further examination, helping to determine the nature and extent of any liver disease or condition. The excision of the left lobe liver is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety during the procedure.
