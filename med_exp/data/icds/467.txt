Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Right Ventricle, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an intraluminal device into the right ventricle of the heart through a small incision in the skin. The device is guided through a catheter to the right ventricle, where it is deployed to improve blood flow and cardiac function. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance and local anesthesia, and is often used to treat conditions such as heart failure, cardiomyopathy, and cardiac arrhythmias. The insertion of the device can help to reduce symptoms and improve overall quality of life for patients.
