Dilation of Right Hepatic Duct with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a thin tube through the skin and into the right hepatic duct to widen the passageway using an intraluminal device. The aim is to facilitate the flow of bile and relieve any obstruction or narrowing in the duct. Under imaging guidance, the provider manipulates the device to gently dilate the duct, allowing for improved drainage and alleviating symptoms such as jaundice and abdominal pain. The percutaneous approach enables a quicker recovery and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgery.
