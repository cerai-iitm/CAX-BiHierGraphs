Replacement of Abdominal Aorta with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a diseased or damaged abdominal aorta with a synthetic substitute using an open approach, where the surgeon makes a large incision in the abdomen to access the aorta. The synthetic graft is made of durable materials, such as Dacron or polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), which are designed to mimic the natural aorta and withstand the high blood pressure in the abdominal region. Once the damaged section of the aorta is removed, the synthetic substitute is connected to the healthy ends of the aorta, allowing for the restoration of normal blood flow to the abdomen and lower limbs. The synthetic substitute is carefully secured in place to prevent leakage or rupture, ensuring a safe and effective repair of the abdominal aorta.
