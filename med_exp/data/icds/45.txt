Drainage of Spinal Canal with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the spine to access the spinal canal, allowing a drainage device to be inserted to remove excess cerebrospinal fluid or other substances that may be causing pressure or infection. The open approach requires a larger incision to directly visualize the spinal canal and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the drainage device. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure, reduce symptoms, and promote healing by removing unwanted substances and allowing the spinal canal to drain properly. Post-procedure, the patient is typically monitored for any complications and the drainage device is removed once the issue has been resolved.
