Extirpation of Matter from Right Popliteal Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the right popliteal artery through a small skin incision.Using specialized instruments and fluoroscopic guidance, the physician accesses the artery through a percutaneous approach, allowing for precise control and minimal trauma to surrounding tissue. The extirpation of matter from the artery aims to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the affected leg. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes a faster recovery compared to open surgical procedures.
