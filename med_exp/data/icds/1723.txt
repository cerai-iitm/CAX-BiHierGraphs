Excision of Right Neck Lymphatic, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
A percutaneous excision of the right neck lymphatic is a minimally invasive procedure that involves removing a sample of lymph tissue from the right side of the neck through a small incision. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to numb the area, and a small instrument is inserted through the incision to extract the lymph tissue. The extracted tissue is then sent to a laboratory for examination to aid in the diagnosis of diseases or infections. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical procedures.
