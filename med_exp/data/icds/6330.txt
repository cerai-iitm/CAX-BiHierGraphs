Excision of Left Kidney, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the skin to access the left kidney through the percutaneous approach. Using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, the surgeon inserts specialized instruments to remove the kidney tissue or lesion for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The excision is often used to diagnose and treat conditions such as kidney tumors, cysts, or other abnormalities, providing an accurate diagnosis and potential treatment plan.
