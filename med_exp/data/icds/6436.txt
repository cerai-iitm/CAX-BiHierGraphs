Repair Bladder, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the bladder, allowing the surgeon to visually examine and directly repair any damage or defects to the bladder wall or surrounding tissues. The open approach allows for precise dissection and repair of complex bladder injuries or conditions, such as bladder rupture or fistula. The surgeon may use sutures, grafts, or other materials to reinforce the bladder wall and ensure a watertight seal. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bladder function and prevent further complications, such as incontinence or infection.
