Fluoroscopy of Multiple Coronary Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize multiple coronary arteries, which are the blood vessels that supply oxygenated blood to the heart muscle. Low osmolar contrast is injected into the coronary arteries through a catheter, allowing the arteries to be visible under X-ray. The fluoroscope produces real-time images of the coronary arteries, enabling the physician to assess blood flow and diagnose any blockages or narrowing. The use of low osmolar contrast reduces the risk of an allergic reaction or kidney damage compared to traditional contrast agents.
