Introduction of Nutritional Substance into Peripheral Artery, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a healthcare provider access a peripheral artery through the skin to introduce a nutritional substance. The substance is delivered directly into the artery using a catheter, allowing for targeted and efficient nutrition supplementation. This approach enables the administration of essential nutrients, vitamins, and minerals to areas of the body that may have limited access to them. The goal of this procedure is to improve overall health and well-being by ensuring adequate nutrition is delivered to specific regions of the body.
