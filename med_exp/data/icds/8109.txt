Fluoroscopy of Aorta and Bilateral Lower Extremity Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
During this procedure, a low osmolar contrast agent is injected into the aorta and bilateral lower extremity arteries to enhance visualization. Fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, is used to guide the injection and capture real-time images of the vessels. The resulting images allow physicians to assess blood flow, diagnose vascular diseases, and identify potential blockages or stenosis in the aorta and lower extremity arteries. The use of low osmolar contrast agent minimizes the risk of adverse reactions and reduces the overall contrast load.
