Excision of Aortic Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the aortic lymphatic system through a small incision in the skin. The procedure is performed under image guidance, allowing the healthcare provider to precisely identify the area of interest. A specialized instrument is then inserted through the endoscope to excise or remove a sample of the aortic lymphatic tissue for diagnostic purposes. The percutaneous approach enables a quicker recovery time and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgical methods.
