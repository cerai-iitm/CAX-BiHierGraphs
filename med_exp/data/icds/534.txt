Repair Coronary Artery, One Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically repairing a blocked or damaged coronary artery to restore blood flow to the heart. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart, and the coronary artery is repaired using a graft or bypass. The goal is to improve blood flow, reduce symptoms of coronary artery disease, and lower the risk of heart attack. The surgery is typically performed by a cardiothoracic surgeon and may be done in conjunction with other procedures, such as a valve repair or heart transplant.
