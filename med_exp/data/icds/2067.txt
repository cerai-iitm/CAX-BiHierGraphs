Dilation of Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the use of a flexible tube with a light and camera on the end to visualize the trachea, allowing the doctor to directly see the area of narrowing or blockage. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or nose, or through a tracheostomy tube, and guided to the affected area. Once the area is visualized, a specialized instrument is used to gently dilate or widen the trachea, restoring airflow and relieving symptoms such as difficulty breathing or shortness of breath. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety.
