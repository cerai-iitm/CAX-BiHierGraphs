Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two or more lumbar vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device, accessed through a posterior approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the lower back. The procedure is performed as an open approach, where the surgical site is exposed through a larger incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the spine. During the procedure, the interbody fusion device is inserted between the affected vertebrae to promote bone growth and stabilize the spine. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate back pain, restore spinal alignment, and improve mobility by fusing the affected vertebral joints together.
