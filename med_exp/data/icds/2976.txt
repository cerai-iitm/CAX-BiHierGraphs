Extraction of Anus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the anus through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for the extraction of tissue or foreign bodies from the anal canal. The endoscope is inserted through the anus or an artificial opening, such as a colostomy, and navigates to the affected area. Once in position, specialized instruments are used to extract the targeted tissue or foreign body, which is then removed through the endoscope or a separate incision. This minimally invasive procedure is often performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort.
