Excision of Right Ovary, Open Approach, Diagnostic
The surgical removal of the right ovary is performed through an open incision, allowing direct visualization of the pelvic cavity. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed to examine the ovary for abnormalities, such as tumors or cysts, and to obtain a tissue sample for further evaluation. The surgeon makes an incision in the lower abdomen to access the ovary, carefully dissecting the surrounding tissues to isolate the organ before its removal. Following excision, the ovary is sent to the laboratory for histopathological analysis to determine a definitive diagnosis.
