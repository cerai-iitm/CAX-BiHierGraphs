Removal of Drainage Device from Gallbladder, External Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

The removal of a drainage device from the gallbladder is a minor surgical procedure performed under local anesthesia. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a small incision in the skin to access the drainage device, which is then carefully removed from the gallbladder. The incision site is closed with stitches or staples, and the wound is dressed with sterile gauze to promote healing. Post-procedure care typically involves monitoring for signs of infection, such as redness, swelling, or increased pain, and following instructions for wound care and follow-up appointments.
