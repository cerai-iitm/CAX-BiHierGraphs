Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Metal on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right hip joint with a metal on polyethylene synthetic substitute, which is an uncemented type of implant. The open approach is used, which means a larger incision is made to access the joint, allowing for better visualization and removal of damaged or diseased tissue. The uncemented implant is designed to bond directly with the surrounding bone, promoting a secure and stable fit. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate pain in the right hip joint, allowing for improved mobility and quality of life.
