Occlusion of Left Femoral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using a catheter to deliver an intraluminal device, such as a stent or coil, to the left femoral artery through a small incision in the skin. The device is designed to occlude, or block, the blood flow through the artery, which can be used to treat a variety of conditions, including aneurysms or bleeding. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the catheter is navigated to the targeted area, and the device is deployed to achieve the desired occlusion. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and is often used in conjunction with other interventions to ensure optimal treatment outcomes.
