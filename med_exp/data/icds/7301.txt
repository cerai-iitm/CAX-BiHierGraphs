Detachment at Right 3rd Toe, Low, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the right 3rd toe at a low level, utilizing an open approach. The goal of the detachment is to remove the affected toe, which may be necessary due to severe injury, deformity, or infection. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the affected toe, carefully detaching it from the surrounding tissue and bone. The resulting wound is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, to promote healing and facilitate recovery.
