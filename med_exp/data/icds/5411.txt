Reposition Left Acetabulum with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left acetabulum, the socket of the hip joint, to its correct anatomical alignment through an open approach. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are used to stabilize the acetabulum and promote healing. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary repairs. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal hip function, alleviate pain, and improve mobility.
