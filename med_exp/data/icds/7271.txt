Detachment at Right Foot, Partial 1st Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the first metatarsal bone and the medial cuneiform bone in the right foot, also known as a partial 1st ray amputation, performed through an open incision. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, improve mobility, and enhance quality of life for patients suffering from severe foot deformities, trauma, or chronic conditions. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an open incision to access the affected area, carefully detaches the specified bones, and performs any necessary repairs or reconstructions. The detachment is typically performed to address conditions that have failed to respond to conservative treatments, such as osteoarthritis, diabetic foot ulcers, or severe infections.
