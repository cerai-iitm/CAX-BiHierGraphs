Computerized Tomography (CT Scan) of Multiple Coronary Arteries using Intravascular Optical Coherence
This non-invasive diagnostic procedure uses intravascular optical coherence to produce high-resolution images of multiple coronary arteries. The CT scan technology creates detailed cross-sectional images of the coronary arteries, allowing for precise visualization of the vessel walls and any potential blockages or narrowing. The procedure is typically performed to diagnose coronary artery disease, monitor its progression, or guide treatment decisions. During the procedure, a contrast agent may be injected into a vein to highlight the coronary arteries and improve image quality.
