Excision of Right Shoulder Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to excise the right shoulder joint, which may be necessary to treat conditions such as severe arthritis, trauma, or infection that cannot be managed with other treatments. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon makes an incision in the shoulder to access the joint. The affected joint is then carefully removed, and any damaged or diseased tissue is debrided to promote healing. The surgical site is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for a period of time to ensure proper healing and to manage any post-operative pain.
