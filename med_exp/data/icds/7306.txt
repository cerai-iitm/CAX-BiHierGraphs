Detachment at Right 4th Toe, Complete, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a complete detachment of the right 4th toe from the surrounding tissue and bone, performed through an open approach. The procedure requires a surgical incision to access the affected toe, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and separate the toe from the surrounding soft tissue and bone. Once detached, the toe is completely removed, and the wound is closed, typically with sutures or staples. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as severe trauma, infection, or deformity affecting the right 4th toe, restoring normal function and alleviating pain.
