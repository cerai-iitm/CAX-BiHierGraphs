Extraction of Right Acetabulum, Open Approach
During an open surgery, the damaged or diseased portion of the right acetabulum, the cup-shaped cavity at the base of the pelvis that forms the hip joint, is surgically removed to alleviate pain, restore function, or prevent further damage. The procedure involves an incision in the skin and tissue to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to carefully extract the damaged acetabulum. This surgical intervention is often performed to treat conditions such as osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, or hip fractures. The goal of the procedure is to restore the natural anatomy of the hip joint and improve the patient's mobility and quality of life.
