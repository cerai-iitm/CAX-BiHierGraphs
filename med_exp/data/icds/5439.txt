Reposition Right Lower Femur with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to reposition the right lower femur, which involves relocating the bone to its normal anatomical position. This is achieved through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then inserted into the medullary canal of the femur to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The fixation device is designed to provide support and maintain the corrected position of the femur, allowing for optimal recovery and function.
