Resection of Perineum Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the perineal region to access the muscle tissue. The affected muscle is then carefully resected, or removed, to alleviate symptoms or treat a condition such as cancer, injury, or congenital defect. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the damaged muscle. Following the resection, the incision is closed, and the area is allowed to heal, promoting recovery and restoration of normal function.
