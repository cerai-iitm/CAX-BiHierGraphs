Excision of Right Upper Femur, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the right upper femur, with the goal of removing a portion of the bone for diagnostic purposes. The operation is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully excises the affected section of the femur, taking care to preserve surrounding tissues and minimize trauma to the surrounding anatomy. Following excision, the removed bone tissue is sent to a laboratory for further examination and analysis to aid in diagnosis and guide future treatment decisions.
