Repair Descending Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the descending colon, which is the portion of the large intestine that extends from the left colic flexure to the sigmoid colon. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect and manipulate the colon to repair any damage, defects, or lesions. The repair may involve removing damaged tissue, resecting and reanastomosing the colon, or using sutures or staples to close any perforations or tears. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal colon function and prevent complications such as bowel obstruction, sepsis, or peritonitis.
