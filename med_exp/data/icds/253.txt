Replacement of Radial Nerve with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open replacement of a damaged or diseased radial nerve with a synthetic substitute, aiming to restore normal nerve function and alleviate related symptoms. The radial nerve is a major nerve in the arm that controls extension of the wrist and fingers, and damage to it can lead to significant motor and sensory deficits. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected nerve, removes the damaged segment, and implants the synthetic substitute to facilitate nerve regeneration and rebalancing of nerve function. The goal of this complex procedure is to improve muscle strength, sensation, and overall motor control in the affected arm.
