Supplement Left Tibia with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the skin and tissue to access the left tibia, also known as the shinbone, to supplement it with a synthetic substitute. The goal of this operation is to restore the structural integrity and function of the damaged or diseased tibia, often due to trauma, infection, or cancer. The synthetic material used is designed to mimic the natural bone tissue, providing support and stability to the affected leg. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully shapes and secures the substitute in place, ensuring a proper fit and promoting optimal healing and recovery.
