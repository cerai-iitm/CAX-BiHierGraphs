Excision of Female Perineum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the female perineal area to remove damaged or diseased tissue, such as scar tissue, abscesses, or masses. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the affected tissue and repair any defects. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal anatomy and function to the perineal area, alleviating symptoms such as pain, discomfort, or difficulty with bowel movements. The procedure is typically performed under general or regional anesthesia and may require a period of recovery and wound care to ensure proper healing.
