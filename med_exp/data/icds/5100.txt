Reposition Left Ulna with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the left ulna bone. The bone is then repositioned to its normal anatomical alignment to restore proper function and relieve symptoms. An internal fixation device, such as a plate and screws, is implanted to stabilize the bone and facilitate healing. The device is typically secured to the bone using screws or other fasteners to hold it in place while the bone heals.
