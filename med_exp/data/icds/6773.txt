Resection of Prostate, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the prostate gland through a small incision in the skin, allowing the surgeon to remove the affected tissue or tumor through the same incision. A camera and specialized instruments are inserted through the skin to perform the resection, minimizing trauma to surrounding tissue and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the patient is usually able to return home the same day or after a short hospital stay. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and allows for more precise removal of the affected prostate tissue.
