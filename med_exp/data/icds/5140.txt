Supplement Left Humeral Head with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves an open approach to supplement the left humeral head with autologous tissue substitute, which is harvested from the patient's own body. The goal of this surgical intervention is to restore damaged or deteriorated tissues in the humeral head, promoting joint stability and mobility. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully selected and prepared to match the natural tissue structure and function, ensuring a safe and effective supplement. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, enabling precise placement and shaping of the tissue substitute to achieve optimal results.
