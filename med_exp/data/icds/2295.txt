Extraction of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the right lower lung lobe, allowing for the extraction of tissue or material from the lung lobe for diagnostic purposes. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the extraction of lung tissue or foreign bodies, and the procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation. The procedure is often used to diagnose conditions such as lung cancer, pneumonia, or lung abscesses, and can also be used to retrieve foreign objects that have entered the lung. The use of an endoscope provides a less invasive alternative to open surgery, resulting in less trauma to the patient and reduced recovery time.
