Bypass Left Femoral Artery to Right Femoral Artery with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this open surgery, a synthetic graft is used to create a bypass between the left and right femoral arteries, rerouting blood flow to improve circulation. The procedure involves making an incision in the groin area to access the femoral arteries, then sewing the synthetic substitute in place to redirect blood flow. The synthetic graft is carefully selected to match the diameter and length of the patient's natural artery, ensuring a secure and durable connection. This bypass surgery can help alleviate symptoms such as leg pain, numbness, or cramping, and improve overall blood flow to the lower extremities.
