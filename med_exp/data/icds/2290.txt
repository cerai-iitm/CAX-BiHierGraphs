Extraction of Left Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During this procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided through the airways to the left main bronchus. The healthcare provider uses the endoscope to visualize the bronchus and identify any abnormalities or blockages. The procedure is usually performed under conscious sedation or topical anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The endoscope is equipped with a specialized instrument to remove tissue or foreign objects from the left main bronchus, allowing for diagnostic sampling or removal of obstruction.
