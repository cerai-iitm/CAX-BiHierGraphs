Fluoroscopy of Gallbladder using Other Contrast
Here is the detailed description:

Fluoroscopy of the gallbladder utilizes X-rays and a contrast agent to enhance visibility of the organ and its internal structures. During the procedure, the contrast material is administered through an injection or orally, allowing the gallbladder to be visualized in real-time on a fluoroscopic screen. The fluoroscope produces continuous X-ray images, enabling the healthcare provider to guide the examination and capture images of the gallbladder from multiple angles. This diagnostic test helps identify gallstones, inflammation, or other abnormalities in the gallbladder.
