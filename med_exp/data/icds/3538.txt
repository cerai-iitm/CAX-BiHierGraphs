Resection of Pancreas, Open Approach
This invasive surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the pancreas, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove the affected portion of the pancreas. The open approach provides direct visualization of the pancreas and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the targeted area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve removal of surrounding lymph nodes, blood vessels, and other tissues. Following resection, the remaining pancreas tissue is carefully closed, and the incision is sutured or stapled to promote healing.
