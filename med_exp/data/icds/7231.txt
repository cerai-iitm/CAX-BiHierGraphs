Repair Left Ring Finger, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skin of the left ring finger to access the affected area. The goal of the operation is to restore the finger's normal function and anatomy by repairing damaged or injured tissues, such as muscles, tendons, or ligaments. The surgeon carefully dissects the tissue layers to identify the source of the problem and performs the necessary repairs, which may include suturing, reattaching, or replacing damaged structures. Once the repair is complete, the incision is closed, and the finger is stabilized to promote healing and prevent further injury.
