Insertion of Spacer into Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lower back to access the lumbar vertebral joint, where a spacer is inserted to alleviate pressure on the surrounding nerves and vertebrae. The spacer helps to maintain the natural spacing between the vertebrae, reducing pressure and pain in the lower back. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding anatomy, ensuring precise placement of the spacer. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully position the spacer to achieve optimal alignment and support for the affected joint.
