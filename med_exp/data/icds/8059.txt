Computerized Tomography (CT Scan) of Intracranial Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure utilizes computerized tomography technology to produce high-resolution images of the intracranial arteries, enabling visualization of the blood vessels within the brain. During the examination, a low osmolar contrast agent is injected into a vein to enhance image quality and facilitate accurate diagnosis. The resulting images are then analyzed to identify potential abnormalities, such as aneurysms, stenosis, or occlusions, which can inform treatment decisions and guide further interventions. This non-invasive test is typically performed on an outpatient basis and is commonly used to evaluate patients presenting with symptoms such as headaches, numbness, or vision changes.
