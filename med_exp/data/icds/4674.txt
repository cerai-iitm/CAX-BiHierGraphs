Release Left Hand Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left hand to access and release the tendon, which has become stuck or entrapped, causing pain, stiffness, and limited mobility. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the affected area and carefully free the tendon from surrounding tissue, scar tissue, or other structures that may be constricting its movement. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal tendon function, alleviate pain, and improve range of motion in the hand. Post-operative rehabilitation may include physical therapy to strengthen the hand and promote optimal healing.
