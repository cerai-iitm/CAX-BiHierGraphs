Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Finger Phalangeal Joint, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screws, from the right finger phalangeal joint through an external approach. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the internal fixation device. The device is then carefully removed, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and structures. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the finger is immobilized to promote healing and prevent complications.
