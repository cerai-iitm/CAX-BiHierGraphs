Repair Nasal Mucosa and Soft Tissue, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the use of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end to visualize the nasal passages and repair damaged or injured nasal mucosa and soft tissue. Through a natural or artificial opening, the surgeon uses specialized instruments to restore normal function and anatomy to the affected area. The minimally invasive approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster healing. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as nasal congestion, bleeding, or difficulty breathing, and to improve overall nasal function and appearance.
