Drainage of Right Lobe Liver, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a thin needle or catheter through the skin and into the right lobe of the liver to drain accumulated fluid, abscess, or blood. Guided by imaging techniques such as ultrasound or CT scans, the healthcare provider carefully navigates the instrument to the targeted area to alleviate pressure and promote healing. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgical methods and allows for faster recovery times. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may require post-procedure monitoring to ensure effective drainage and prevent further complications.
