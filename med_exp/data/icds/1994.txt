Repair Left External Ear, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision on the surface of the left external ear to access the affected area. The goal of the operation is to repair any damage or defects to the ear's external structure, which may be causing discomfort, pain, or affecting hearing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the ear's anatomy, enabling the surgeon to carefully repair any tears, lacerations, or other damage. The procedure is typically performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the complexity of the repair and the patient's overall health.
