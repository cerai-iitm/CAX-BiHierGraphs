Revision of Tunneled Vascular Access Device in Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves revision of a tunneled vascular access device located in the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the trunk. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and dissecting through the subcutaneous tissue and fascia to access the device. The revision process may involve replacement of the device, repositioning of the device, or removal of the device and implantation of a new one. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper function of the vascular access device, ensuring uninterrupted delivery of medications, nutrients, or other therapies.
