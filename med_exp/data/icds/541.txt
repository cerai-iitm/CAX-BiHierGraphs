Repair Chordae Tendineae, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repair of chordae tendineae, which arefibrous cords that connect the papillary muscles to the tricuspid valve andmitral valve in the heart, ensuring proper valve function. The open approach requires a thoracotomy, where the chest cavity is opened to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the damaged chordae tendineae. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal valve function, improving blood flow and reducing the risk of regurgitation. By repairing the chordae tendineae, the surgeon can help to alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and swelling in the legs and feet.
