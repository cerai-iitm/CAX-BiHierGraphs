Repair Right Main Bronchus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, which is inserted through the mouth or nose to repair the right main bronchus. The bronchus is a vital airway that branches off the trachea and leads to the lungs, and damage to it can cause severe breathing difficulties. Under visualization with the endoscope, the surgeon inserts specialized instruments through the scope to repair the damaged area, which may involve suturing or patching the injured site. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and facilitates a faster recovery compared to open surgery.
