Supplement Right Tibia with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the right tibia to supplement it with a non-autologous tissue substitute, which is a material taken from a donor or synthetic source. The goal of this operation is to restore the structural integrity and function of the tibia, which may have been compromised due to injury, disease, or congenital defect. The tissue substitute is carefully shaped and fitted to match the natural contours of the tibia, and secured in place using appropriate fixation methods. Once the procedure is complete, the surgical site is closed, and the patient is typically required to undergo a period of rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and recovery.
