Fluoroscopy of Left Internal Mammary Bypass Graft using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left internal mammary bypass graft, which is a surgical conduit used to redirect blood flow around a blockage in the coronary arteries. During the procedure, a low-osmolar contrast agent is injected into the graft to enhance X-ray visibility. Real-time fluoroscopic images are then captured to assess the patency and functionality of the graft, providing valuable information for diagnosis and treatment planning. The procedure is typically performed in a catheterization laboratory or fluoroscopy suite by a trained healthcare professional.
