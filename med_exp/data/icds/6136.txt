Replacement of Left Knee Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the damaged or diseased left knee joint with a synthetic substitute, typically a prosthetic implant, through an open incision. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and tissue to access the knee joint, removes the damaged or diseased joint, and implants the synthetic substitute, which is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the knee. The prosthetic implant is usually composed of metal, ceramic, or plastic materials and is secured to the surrounding bone with cement or a porous coating. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, restore mobility, and improve the overall quality of life for patients with severe knee joint damage or degeneration.
