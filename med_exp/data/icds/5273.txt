Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Right Acetabulum, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive surgical procedure is performed to stabilize the right acetabulum, which is the socket of the hip joint. A small incision is made in the skin and underlying tissue to access the acetabulum. Through this incision, an internal fixation device, such as a screw or plate, is inserted to provide support and stability to the acetabulum. The percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery.
