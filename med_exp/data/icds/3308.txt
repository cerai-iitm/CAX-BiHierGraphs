Dilation of Common Bile Duct, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a small instrument through the skin to widen the common bile duct, a tube that carries bile from the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine. The goal is to relieve blockage or narrowing of the duct, which can cause symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and fever. Using imaging guidance, a specialist advances a small tube through the skin and into the bile duct, then inflates a balloon or inserts a specialized instrument to widen the duct. This minimally invasive approach allows for faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery.
