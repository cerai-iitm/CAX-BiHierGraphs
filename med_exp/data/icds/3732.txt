Extraction of Left Ear Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the left ear to access and remove a lesion, cyst, or other abnormal growth. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and carefully extract the targeted tissue or lesion, minimizing damage to surrounding skin and structures. The procedure may be performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health. Post-operative care typically involves keeping the affected area clean and dry to promote healing and reduce the risk of infection.
