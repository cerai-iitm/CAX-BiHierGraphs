Restriction of Stomach with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through a natural or artificial opening in the stomach using an endoscope. The device is designed to restrict the stomach's capacity, promoting weight loss by limiting food intake. Under sedation and guided by real-time imaging, the endoscope is navigated through the mouth and esophagus into the stomach, where the device is deployed and secured in place. The procedure is typically performed on an outpatient basis, with patients often experiencing rapid weight loss and improvement in obesity-related health conditions.
