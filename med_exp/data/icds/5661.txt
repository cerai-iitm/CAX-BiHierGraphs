Fusion of Cervical Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the joint of the cervical spine using an interbody fusion device, accessed through an anterior approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the front of the neck. The anterior column of the spine is stabilized through an open approach, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding anatomy. The interbody fusion device is implanted to replace the damaged or degenerated disc, promoting bone growth and fusion between the adjacent vertebrae. The procedure aims to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the neck, arm, or hand, and to restore normal spinal alignment and function.
