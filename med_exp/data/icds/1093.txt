Dilation of Right Popliteal Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves dilating the narrowed or blocked right popliteal artery to restore blood flow and relieve symptoms such as leg pain or cramping. A catheter is inserted through the skin and guided to the affected artery, where a drug-eluting stent is deployed to widen the artery and prevent further blockage. The stent releases medication to prevent the growth of new blockages and promotes healthy blood vessel healing. The percutaneous approach allows for a quick recovery and minimized scarring, with most patients returning to their normal activities within a few days.
