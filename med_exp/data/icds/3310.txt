Dilation of Common Bile Duct with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device to dilate the common bile duct through a natural or artificial opening. The device is guided through the duct using an endoscope, allowing the physician to visualize the area and perform the dilation. This may be performed to treat conditions such as bile duct stones or strictures that are blocking the flow of bile. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation, and the patient may experience some discomfort during the procedure.
