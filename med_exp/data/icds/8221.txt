Fluoroscopy of Gallbladder and Bile Ducts using Low Osmolar Contrast
This imaging procedure utilizes low osmolar contrast to visualize the gallbladder and bile ducts, allowing for accurate diagnosis of various conditions affecting the biliary system. During the procedure, a contrast agent is injected into the body, and then fluoroscopy, a type of X-ray, is used to capture real-time images of the gallbladder and bile ducts. The low osmolar contrast agent helps to highlight the structures of the biliary system, enabling the radiologist to identify any abnormalities, such as gallstones, inflammation, or blockages. The resulting images provide valuable information for healthcare providers to develop an effective treatment plan for the patient.
