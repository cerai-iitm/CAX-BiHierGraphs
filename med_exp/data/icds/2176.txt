Excision of Right Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the airways and locate the right main bronchus, which is a vital part of the bronchial tree. Through a natural or artificial opening, the healthcare provider inserts specialized instruments alongside the endoscope to carefully excise, or surgically remove, the right main bronchus. The excision may be necessary to treat conditions such as lung cancer, bronchiectasis, or benign tumors that are obstructing the airway. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure allows for reduced recovery time and less trauma to surrounding tissues compared to traditional open surgical methods.
