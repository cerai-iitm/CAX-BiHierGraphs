Replacement of Left Femoral Artery with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the replacement of the left femoral artery with a synthetic graft through an open approach, requiring a surgical incision to access the affected area. The synthetic substitute is carefully selected and sized to match the patient's anatomy, ensuring a proper fit and minimizing the risk of complications. During the procedure, the diseased or damaged section of the femoral artery is removed, and the synthetic graft is sewn in place using sutures or staples, restoring blood flow to the lower extremities. The entire process is performed under general anesthesia, and post-operative care involves close monitoring to prevent bleeding, infection, or other potential complications.
