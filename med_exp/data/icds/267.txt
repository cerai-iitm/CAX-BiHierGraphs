Supplement Radial Nerve with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves supplementing the radial nerve with an autologous tissue substitute, using an open approach, to restore or enhance nerve function. Autologous tissue substitutes are derived from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection or allergic reactions. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the nerve and surrounding tissues, enabling precise placement of the substitute. This intervention can help alleviate symptoms such as numbness, weakness, or paralysis in the arm or hand, and promote improved motor and sensory function.
