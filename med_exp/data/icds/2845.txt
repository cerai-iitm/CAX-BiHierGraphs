Excision of Ileum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the small intestine and identify the diseased or damaged segment of ileum. Through a small incision, a specialized instrument is inserted to carefully excise the affected portion of the ileum, allowing for precise removal of the targeted area while minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat a range of conditions, including obstruction, injury, or cancer. The percutaneous endoscopic approach offers several benefits, including reduced scarring, less postoperative pain, and a shorter hospital stay.
