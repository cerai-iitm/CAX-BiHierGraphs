Ultrasonography of Gastrointestinal Tract
This non-invasive diagnostic imaging procedure uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the gastrointestinal tract, which includes the stomach, small intestine, and large intestine. The ultrasound probe is moved over the abdomen to capture images of the digestive organs, allowing healthcare professionals to assess their structure and function. This test is often used to diagnose conditions such as gallstones, liver disease, and inflammatory bowel disease, as well as to guide biopsies and other procedures. The procedure is typically painless and does not require any special preparation, making it a valuable tool for evaluating gastrointestinal health.
