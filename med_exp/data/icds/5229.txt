Excision of Right Fibula, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower right leg to access the fibula, a smaller bone in the lower leg. The fibula is then carefully excised, or removed, by a trained orthopedic surgeon in a controlled operating room environment. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the damaged or diseased portion of the fibula. Following the excision, the wound is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for post-operative complications.
