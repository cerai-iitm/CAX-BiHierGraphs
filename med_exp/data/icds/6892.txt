Drainage of Pericardial Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to gain access to the pericardial cavity, a fluid-filled sac surrounding the heart. The procedure is performed to drain excess fluid or blood that has accumulated in the cavity, which can compress the heart and impede its function. The surgeon uses specialized instruments to carefully drain the fluid, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues or organs. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored to ensure proper healing and resolution of symptoms.
