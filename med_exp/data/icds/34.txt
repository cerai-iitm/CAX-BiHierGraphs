Drainage of Cerebral Ventricle with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device into the cerebral ventricle through a small incision in the skull to remove excess cerebrospinal fluid. Using an endoscope to visualize the ventricle, the surgeon guides the drainage device to the affected area to restore normal fluid flow and alleviate pressure on the brain. The percutaneous approach enables a faster recovery and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgical techniques. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as hydrocephalus, intracranial hypertension, or cerebral edema.
