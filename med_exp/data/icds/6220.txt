Supplement Right Knee Joint, Femoral Surface with Liner, Open Approach
During this orthopedic procedure, the femoral surface of the right knee joint is supplemented with a liner to restore or enhance its natural function. This involves an open approach, where an incision is made in the skin and soft tissue to access the joint. The liner is carefully inserted and secured to the femoral surface to facilitate smooth movement and cushioning within the knee joint. The goal of this supplementation is to alleviate pain, improve mobility, and enhance overall joint stability.
