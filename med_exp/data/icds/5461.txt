Reposition Right Tibia with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves realigning the right tibia bone to its normal anatomical position using an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then inserted into the medullary canal of the tibia to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The device is typically a metal rod or nail that runs down the center of the bone, holding it in place until it fully recovers. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the bone and surrounding tissues, ensuring accurate placement of the fixation device and reducing the risk of complications.
