Excision of Nasal Mucosa and Soft Tissue, External Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the surgical removal of mucous membrane and soft tissue from the nasal cavity through an external approach, typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation. The external approach allows the surgeon to access the nasal tissue through an incision made on the outside of the nose, rather than through the nostrils. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in the diagnosis of underlying conditions such as nasal polyps, chronic sinusitis, or nasal tumors. The diagnostic excision helps identify the presence of abnormal cells or infections, guiding further treatment and management of the nasal condition.
