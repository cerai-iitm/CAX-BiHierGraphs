Excision of Ampulla of Vater, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
Here is the detailed description:

This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of the ampulla of Vater, a small muscular valve that regulates the flow of bile and pancreatic juice into the small intestine, through a natural or artificial opening. The procedure is typically performed under endoscopic guidance, where a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or nose to visualize the area. The ampulla is then carefully excised using specialized instruments, allowing for diagnostic tissues to be collected for further examination. The procedure is often used to diagnose and treat conditions related to the pancreaticobiliary system, such as cancer, stones, or inflammation.
