Reposition Left Metacarpal with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves manually repositioning the left metacarpal bone to its correct anatomical location through an open approach, where the skin and underlying tissue are incised to access the affected area. Internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods are then used to stabilize the bone and hold it in place while it heals. The fixation devices are implanted through the same incision, providing a stable environment for bone union. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the hand, alleviating pain and improving mobility and dexterity.
