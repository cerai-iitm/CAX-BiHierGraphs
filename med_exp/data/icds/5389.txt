Replacement of Left Patella with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left patella with an artificial substitute through an open approach, where the surgeon makes a larger incision to access the damaged joint. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain and improve mobility in patients with severe patellar damage or degeneration. During the operation, the surgeon removes the diseased or damaged patella and replaces it with a synthetic substitute, which is designed to mimic the natural structure and function of the patella. Post-operatively, patients typically undergo rehabilitation to restore strength and range of motion in the affected knee.
