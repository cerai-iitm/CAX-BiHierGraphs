Restriction of Right Internal Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of an intraluminal device in the right internal carotid artery to restrict blood flow through the vessel. The procedure is performed percutaneously, through a small incision in the skin, using imaging guidance to ensure accurate device placement. The intraluminal device is designed to reduce blood flow to the targeted area, helping to prevent damage from conditions such as aneurysms, stenosis, or arteriovenous malformations. Following the procedure, patients are typically monitored for signs of complications, such as bleeding or stroke, before being discharged from the hospital.
