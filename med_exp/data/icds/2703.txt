Dilation of Transverse Colon with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During an endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the mouth or anus to visualize the transverse colon. A specialized intraluminal device is then advanced through the tube to reach the narrow or blocked area of the transverse colon. Once in place, the device is gently inflated to dilate the colon, widening the passage to allow for easier passage of stool or other contents. This minimally invasive procedure is usually performed under sedation to ensure patient comfort.
