Fusion of Thoracic Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing adjacent thoracic vertebrae together using an interbody fusion device, which is implanted through an anterior (frontal) approach to stabilize the spine and alleviate painful motion. The anterior column of the spine is accessed through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the vertebral joint and accurately place the fusion device. The interbody fusion device is typically made of metal, ceramic, or plastic and is designed to promote bone growth and fusion between the adjacent vertebrae. Over time, the device will be incorporated into the surrounding bone, forming a solid fusion that restores spinal stability and reduces pain.
