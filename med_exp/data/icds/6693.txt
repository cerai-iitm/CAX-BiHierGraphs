Resection of Uterus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting an endoscope through a small incision in the abdomen to visualize the uterus, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove the uterus using specialized instruments. The percutaneous endoscopic approach enables a less traumatic and less invasive surgical experience, reducing post-operative pain, scarring, and recovery time. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is often performed to treat conditions such as uterine cancer, fibroids, or endometriosis. After removal, the uterus is typically extracted through the same small incision, leaving minimal scarring.
