Fluoroscopy of Right Heart using Other Contrast
This imaging procedure utilizes fluoroscopy to visualize the right heart structures, employing an alternative contrast agent to enhance visibility. During the procedure, a specialized X-ray machine produces continuous, real-time images of the heart, allowing the physician to assess cardiac function and anatomy. The use of other contrast, rather than traditional iodinated contrast, may be necessary for patients with specific sensitivities or allergies. The resulting images provide valuable diagnostic information for conditions such as valvular disease, cardiac shunts, or chamber enlargement.
