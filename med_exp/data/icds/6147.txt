Replacement of Right Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Ceramic Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the femoral surface of the right hip joint with a ceramic synthetic substitute using an uncemented approach through an open incision. The goal is to restore normal hip function and alleviate pain caused by damaged or arthritic joint surfaces. The ceramic material is chosen for its biocompatibility, durability, and low friction properties, allowing for a more natural range of motion. The uncemented approach involves implanting the substitute without using bone cement, relying on the natural growth of bone tissue to secure the implant.
