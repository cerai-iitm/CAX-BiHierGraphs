Repair Ascending Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the ascending colon, which is the first part of the large intestine, located on the right side of the abdominal cavity. The surgeon then repairs the damaged or diseased section of the ascending colon, which may be affected by conditions such as a tumor, ulcer, or injury. The repair may involve removing the affected segment and reattaching the healthy ends of the colon, or repairing any holes or perforations in the colon wall. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bowel function and prevent further complications.
