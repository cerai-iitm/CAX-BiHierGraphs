Repair Rectum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the rectum, allowing the surgeon to visually examine and repair any damaged or diseased tissue. The open approach enables direct visualization of the rectal area, facilitating the repair of tears, perforations, or other defects in the rectal wall. The surgery may be performed to treat rectal injuries, rectal prolapse, or other conditions affecting the rectum, and may involve the use of surgical mesh or other materials to reinforce the repair. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal rectal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, bleeding, or incontinence.
