Dilation of Larynx, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end through the mouth or a tracheostomy tube to access the larynx, allowing for visualization of the vocal cords and surrounding tissue. The scope is carefully maneuvered to reach the narrowed or blocked area, and a specialized instrument is used to dilate the larynx, widening the airway passage to improve breathing. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and reduce bleeding risk. By dilating the larynx, this procedure can help treat conditions such as laryngeal stenosis, vocal cord paralysis, and airway obstruction.
