Reposition Sacrum with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the manipulation and restoration of the sacrum to its normal anatomical position using an open approach, where the skin and soft tissues are incised to access the affected area. The procedure necessitates the placement of an internal fixation device, such as screws, rods, or plates, to provide stability and support to the sacrum, enabling it to heal in the correct position. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the sacrum and surrounding tissues, enabling accurate repositioning and fixation. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the lower back, legs, or pelvis, resulting from sacral misalignment or instability.
