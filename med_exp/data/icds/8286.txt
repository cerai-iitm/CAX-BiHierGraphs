Beam Radiation of Lung using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This procedure involves the use of high-energy photons with an energy range of 1-10 MeV to deliver beam radiation directly to the lung tissue. The treatment is typically performed using a linear accelerator or other external beam radiation therapy equipment. The precise delivery of photon beams to the lung helps to destroy cancer cells while minimizing damage to surrounding healthy tissue. This therapy is often used to treat lung cancer, either as a standalone treatment or in combination with other therapies such as chemotherapy or surgery.
