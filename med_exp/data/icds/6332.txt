Excision of Left Kidney, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the left kidney through a small incision in the skin, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the internal structures. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon uses specialized instruments to carefully dissect and remove the kidney. The percutaneous approach allows for less tissue damage and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery. After the kidney is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any postoperative complications.
