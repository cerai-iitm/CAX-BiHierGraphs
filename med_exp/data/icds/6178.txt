Reposition Right Knee Joint, External Approach
The procedure involves a surgical approach to reposition the right knee joint to its normal anatomical position, typically performed to correct deformities, misalignments, or malunions. The external approach allows direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately reposition the knee joint and restore proper alignment. This procedure may be necessary to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility, and can be performed under general or regional anesthesia. Post-operatively, the patient will likely require a period of rehabilitation to restore strength and range of motion in the affected knee.
