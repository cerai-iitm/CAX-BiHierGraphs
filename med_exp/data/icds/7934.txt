Fluoroscopy of Multiple Coronary Arteries using Other Contrast
This imaging procedure utilizes fluoroscopy to visualize multiple coronary arteries, which are the blood vessels that supply oxygenated blood to the heart muscle. During the procedure, a contrast agent is administered to enhance the visibility of the coronary arteries, allowing for precise imaging and diagnosis. The fluoroscopy machine produces continuous X-ray beams that are transmitted through the body, capturing real-time images of the coronary arteries and their blood flow. This diagnostic test is essential for identifying coronary artery disease, blockages, or other abnormalities that may be contributing to symptoms such as chest pain or shortness of breath.
