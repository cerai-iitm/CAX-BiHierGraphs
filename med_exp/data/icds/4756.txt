Excision of Right Ankle Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right ankle to access the affected area, followed by the excision of the inflamed bursa, a fluid-filled sac that reduces friction between moving parts. The surrounding ligaments are also removed as necessary to alleviate pain and restore mobility. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and the incision is closed with sutures or staples after the excision is complete. Post-operatively, the patient is usually advised to elevate the ankle and apply ice to reduce swelling, and to undergo physical therapy to regain strength and range of motion.
