Change Drainage Device in Cranial Cavity, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a drainage device located in the cranial cavity, which is the space within the skull that contains the brain. The external approach indicates that the procedure is performed through an incision in the skin and underlying tissue, rather than through an endoscopic or percutaneous approach. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully remove the existing drainage device and replace it with a new one, taking care to minimize trauma to the surrounding brain tissue and ensure proper placement and function of the new device. The goal of the procedure is to restore or maintain proper drainage of cerebrospinal fluid, which is essential for maintaining healthy pressure and function within the central nervous system.
