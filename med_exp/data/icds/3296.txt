Dilation of Left Hepatic Duct, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a thin, flexible tube called a catheter through the skin and into the liver to access the left hepatic duct. Under imaging guidance, a specialized instrument is passed through the catheter to gently dilate or widen the duct, allowing for improved drainage and relief of blockages. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and minimizing scarring. By dilating the left hepatic duct, this procedure can effectively treat conditions such as cholestasis and jaundice, improving bile flow and overall liver function.
