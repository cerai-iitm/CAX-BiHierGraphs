Supplement Stomach with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
The procedure involves using a patient's own tissue to reinforce the stomach, which may be weakened or damaged due to various conditions such as trauma or surgical complications. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the stomach, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the supplementation. The autologous tissue substitute, typically harvested from another part of the body, is then used to reinforce the stomach, promoting healing and strengthening the organ. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as gastroparesis, gastric ulcers, or stomach hernias, and can help alleviate symptoms and improve digestive function.
