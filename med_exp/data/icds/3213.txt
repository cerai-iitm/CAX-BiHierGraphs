Resection of Omentum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the omentum, a fold of fatty tissue that surrounds the intestines. The affected portion of the omentum is then carefully dissected and removed, either partially or entirely, depending on the underlying condition. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and excise the diseased or damaged tissue. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as omental tumors, adhesions, or inflammatory diseases that affect the omentum.
