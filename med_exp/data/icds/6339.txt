Excision of Right Ureter, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This surgical procedure involves the percutaneous endoscopic removal of the right ureter, a tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder. During the procedure, a small incision is made in the skin, and an endoscope is inserted through the incision to visualize the ureter. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully excise the right ureter. This minimally invasive approach reduces post-operative pain and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgical methods.
