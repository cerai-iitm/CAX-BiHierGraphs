Reattachment of Left Thumb, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves reattaching the left thumb using an open approach, which requires a skin incision to access the affected area. The surgeon carefully reattaches the severed thumb, ensuring proper alignment and connection of bone, tendons, and ligaments to restore normal function and mobility. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the thumb's internal structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately repair damaged tissues and reestablish blood flow. Post-operative care typically involves immobilization of the hand to promote healing and prevent further injury.
