Dilation of Left Common Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a percutaneous approach to access the left common iliac artery, where a balloon or other intraluminal device is inserted to dilate the artery and improve blood flow. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure precise placement of the device. The dilation of the left common iliac artery helps to relieve blockages or stenosis, reducing the risk of complications such as leg pain, claudication, and gangrene. By restoring blood flow to the affected area, this procedure can significantly improve overall circulation and quality of life.
