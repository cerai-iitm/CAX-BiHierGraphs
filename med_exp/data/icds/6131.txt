Replacement of Right Knee Joint with Patellofemoral Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
This orthopedic procedure involves replacing the right knee joint with a patellofemoral synthetic substitute, which is a type of artificial joint that mimics the natural patellofemoral joint. The procedure is performed through an open approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected joint. The synthetic substitute is bonded to the surrounding bone using bone cement to ensure a secure and stable fit. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and restore function to the right knee joint, allowing the patient to regain normal mobility and activities.
