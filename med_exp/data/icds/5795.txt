Replacement of Right Elbow Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right elbow joint with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, where the surgeon makes a larger incision to access the joint. The damaged or diseased joint is removed and replaced with an artificial joint, made of materials such as metal, ceramic, or plastic, designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the elbow. The synthetic substitute is securely attached to the surrounding bone and tissue, allowing for a range of motion and reducing pain and discomfort. The open approach provides the surgeon with a clear view of the joint and surrounding tissue, enabling a more precise and accurate replacement procedure.
