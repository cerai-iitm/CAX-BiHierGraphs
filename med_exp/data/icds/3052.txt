Release Right Large Intestine, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the large intestine and identify areas of adhesion or constriction. Through a small incision in the skin, the endoscope is guided to the affected area of the right large intestine, where specialized instruments are used to carefully release any adhesions or scar tissue. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing recovery time and risk of complications. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal intestinal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, bloating, and bowel obstruction.
