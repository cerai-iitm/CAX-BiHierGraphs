Excision of Lesser Omentum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the lesser omentum, a fold of peritoneum that attaches the stomach to the liver. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia and may be performed as a diagnostic measure to examine the lesser omentum for signs of disease or injury. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully excises the lesser omentum, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues or blood vessels. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
