Drainage of Right Knee Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain fluid from the right knee joint using a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the joint. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure and reduce swelling in the knee joint, which can be caused by various conditions such as injury, infection, or arthritis. Under imaging guidance, a thin needle or drainage tube is inserted through the skin and into the joint space to remove the excess fluid. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can be done on an outpatient basis, with the patient able to return home shortly after the procedure is completed.
