Fluoroscopy of Right Brachiocephalic-Subclavian Artery using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to produce real-time X-ray images of the right brachiocephalic-subclavian artery. During the procedure, a contrast agent, other than the standard iodinated or gadolinium-based agents, is injected into the artery to enhance visualization. The fluoroscope is adjusted to capture high-quality images from various angles, allowing the physician to examine the artery's structure and blood flow. The resulting images help diagnose and treat conditions affecting the artery, such as stenosis, aneurysms, or blockages.
