Introduction of Other Diagnostic Substance into Products of Conception, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the administration of a diagnostic substance into products of conception, which can include embryonic or fetal tissue, via a natural or artificial opening. The purpose of this introduction is to aid in the diagnosis of a condition or disease affecting the products of conception. The diagnostic substance may be a dye, contrast agent, or other type of material that helps healthcare providers visualize or analyze the tissue. The substance is carefully inserted through a natural or artificial opening, such as the cervix or a surgically created incision, to obtain the necessary diagnostic information.
