Excision of Abdominal Wall, Open Approach
Surgical removal of damaged or diseased tissue from the abdominal wall is accomplished through an open incision, allowing for direct visualization and manual dissection of the affected area. This procedure involves making a deliberate cut in the skin and underlying tissue to access the abdominal wall, followed by excision of the targeted tissue. The open approach enables surgeons to directly observe the anatomy and precisely remove the tissue, reducing the risk of damage to surrounding structures. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and minimize movement during the surgery.
