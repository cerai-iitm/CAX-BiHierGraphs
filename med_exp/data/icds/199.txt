Supplement Spinal Meninges with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the spinal meninges, the protective membranes surrounding the spinal cord, with a synthetic substitute material through an open approach. The goal is to enhance or restore functionality to the damaged or diseased meninges, promoting healing and improving spinal health. During the procedure, an incision is made to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to implant the synthetic substitute material. By supplementing the natural meninges, this procedure aims to reduce inflammation, alleviate pain, and improve overall spinal function.
