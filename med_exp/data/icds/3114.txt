Repair Jejunum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the jejunum, a portion of the small intestine, to repair any damage or defects. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary repairs, which may include closing perforations, resecting damaged tissue, or reconnecting severed ends. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal intestinal function and prevent complications such as infection, bleeding, or intestinal blockage. The surgeon will carefully close the incision site after completing the repair to promote healing and minimize the risk of complications.
