Extirpation of Matter from Thoracic Aorta, Ascending/Arch, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter from the thoracic aorta, specifically the ascending and arch sections, utilizing an open approach. During the operation, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the affected area, allowing for direct visualization and manual removal of the unwanted material. This may include thrombi, emboli, or other debris that can obstruct blood flow and pose a risk to the patient's health. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow and prevent serious complications, such as stroke or cardiac failure.
