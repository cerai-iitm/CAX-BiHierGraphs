Excision of Left Metatarsal, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the foot to access the left metatarsal bone, which is one of the five long bones in the midfoot. The metatarsal bone is then carefully excised, or removed, to treat conditions such as osteomyelitis, bone tumors, or malunions that are resistant to conservative treatments. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the bone and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately remove the affected bone and any associated debris. Following excision, the wound is closed, and the patient is typically immobilized to facilitate healing and promote optimal outcomes.
