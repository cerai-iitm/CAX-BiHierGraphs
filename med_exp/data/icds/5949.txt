Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the lumbosacral joint, which connects the last vertebra of the lower back to the sacrum, using an interbody fusion device. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, which means the surgeon accesses the joint from the back, and involves an open incision. The goal of the procedure is to stabilize the joint and alleviate symptoms such as pain and instability. The interbody fusion device is implanted between the vertebrae to promote bone growth and fuse the joint, providing long-term support and relief.
