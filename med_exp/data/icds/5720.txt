Fusion of Thoracolumbar Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the thoracolumbar vertebral joint using an interbody fusion device through a posterior approach, where the incision is made in the back. The anterior column of the spine is accessed through this posterior approach, allowing the surgeon to place the interbody fusion device in the correct position. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the spine and the surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the device and ensure proper fusion. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the spine and alleviate pain, numbness, or weakness caused by conditions such as degenerative disc disease, spondylolisthesis, or spinal fractures.
