Extirpation of Matter from Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the chest area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are the layers of tissue beneath the skin. The goal of this procedure is to remove any abnormal or infected matter, such as pus or dead tissue, that may have accumulated in these layers. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local sedation to minimize discomfort and pain. The procedure may be necessary to treat a variety of conditions, including soft tissue infections, necrotizing fasciitis, or foreign body reactions, and can help to promote healing, reduce the risk of further complications, and improve overall health outcomes.
