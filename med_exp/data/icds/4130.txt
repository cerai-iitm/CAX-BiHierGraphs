Extraction of Left Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
Surgical removal of subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the left lower arm is performed through an open approach, involving a skin incision to access the affected area. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully removes the damaged or diseased tissue and fascia, taking care to preserve surrounding healthy tissue and minimize scarring. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and extract the targeted tissue. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as infection, trauma, or tumors affecting the left lower arm, and to promote healing and restore normal function.
