Drainage of Cerebral Ventricle with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access the cerebral ventricle, a fluid-filled cavity in the brain. The goal is to drain excess cerebrospinal fluid that may be causing increased pressure and potential harm to the brain. A drainage device is carefully inserted into the ventricle to remove the fluid, which is then directed out of the body or into another location for absorption. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the ventricle and surrounding brain tissue during the procedure, ensuring precise placement of the drainage device.
