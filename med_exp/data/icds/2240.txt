Excision of Right Lung, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the right lung through a natural or artificial opening. The purpose of the procedure is diagnostic, allowing the healthcare provider to collect tissue samples or remove abnormal tissue from the lung. The endoscope is inserted through a small incision or a natural opening, such as the mouth or nose, and guided to the lung using imaging technology. The excision of tissue or lesions is then performed using specialized instruments, providing a precise and accurate diagnosis for further treatment.
