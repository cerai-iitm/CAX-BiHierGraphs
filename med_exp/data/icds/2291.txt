Extraction of Left Upper Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the left upper lobe bronchus and extract tissue or foreign material through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided through the airways to the left upper lobe bronchus, where the extraction is performed using specialized instruments. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. The extracted tissue or material is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
