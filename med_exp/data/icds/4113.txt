Extraction of Right Neck Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right neck to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles, blood vessels, and nerves. The surgeon then carefully extracts the affected tissue and fascia to remove any damaged, infected, or abnormal tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the targeted tissue. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and the incision is typically closed with sutures or staples to promote healing.
