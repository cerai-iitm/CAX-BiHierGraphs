Replacement of Thoracic Aorta, Descending with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the descending thoracic aorta with a synthetic substitute using an open approach, which requires a large incision in the chest or abdomen to access the affected area. The surgeon carefully removes the diseased or damaged section of the aorta and replaces it with a synthetic graft, which is typically made of durable, woven polyester or other materials. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the function of the natural aorta, allowing blood to flow freely to the rest of the body. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the replacement with precision and accuracy.
