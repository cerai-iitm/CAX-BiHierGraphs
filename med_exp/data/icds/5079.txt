Reposition Right Humeral Shaft with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right humeral shaft to its normal anatomical position, which has been disrupted due to a fracture or other trauma. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the bone. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then implanted within the medullary cavity of the humerus to stabilize and hold the bone in place while it heals. The goal of this procedure is to restore alignment, stability, and function to the affected arm.
