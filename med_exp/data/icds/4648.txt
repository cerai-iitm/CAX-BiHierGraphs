Extraction of Left Hand Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the affected tendon in the left hand, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the tendon and surrounding tissue. The damaged or diseased portion of the tendon is then carefully extracted, and the area is cleaned and prepared for closure. The open approach provides optimal visualization and control, enabling the surgeon to meticulously remove the affected tendon segment while minimizing damage to surrounding tissue. Once the extraction is complete, the incision is closed, and the hand is stabilized to promote healing and facilitate optimal recovery.
