Excision of Right Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the right upper leg, where the damaged or diseased tissue is carefully identified and excised. The surgeon uses a precise dissection technique to preserve surrounding healthy tissue and minimize scarring. Following excision, the wound is closed in a layered fashion to promote optimal healing and reduce the risk of complications. The goal of this procedure is to remove abnormal or infected tissue, alleviate symptoms, and restore normal function to the affected limb.
