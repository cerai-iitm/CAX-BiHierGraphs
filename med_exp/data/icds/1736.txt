Excision of Thorax Lymphatic, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the thorax, or chest cavity, to access the lymphatic system. The purpose of the operation is diagnostic, aiming to collect tissue samples or examine the lymphatic vessels and nodes in the thorax to identify any abnormalities or diseases. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the lymphatic system, enabling the surgeon to assess the extent of any disease or damage. The excised tissue or node samples are then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
