Bypass Ileum to Ileum, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a section of the small intestine, specifically the ileum, is bypassed to connect two healthy segments, restoring normal bowel function and alleviating symptoms. The open approach involves a large incision in the abdomen to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the intestinal section. The bypassed segment of the ileum is then closed and diverted, and the healthy ends are joined together to restore normal intestinal flow. This procedure is often performed to treat complications such as intestinal obstruction, perforation, or injury.
