Excision of Right Lobe Liver, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdominal wall to access the liver, a vital organ responsible for detoxification and metabolism. The surgeon then carefully dissects and removes the right lobe of the liver, taking care to ligate and divide the blood vessels and bile ducts supplying it. The procedure requires precise surgical technique and careful attention to detail to minimize bleeding and ensure the remaining liver tissue is healthy and functional. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the liver and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to tackle complex cases and achieve optimal outcomes.
