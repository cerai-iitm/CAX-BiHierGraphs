Drainage of Sigmoid Colon with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device into the sigmoid colon through the skin to remove fluids, gases, or infectious material that may be causing symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The drainage device is carefully guided into the sigmoid colon using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement and optimal drainage. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote a smooth recovery.
