Extirpation of Matter from Upper Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the upper vein through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, which allows for small incisions and reduced tissue damage. Using imaging guidance, a thin catheter or needle is inserted through the skin and advanced to the affected vein, enabling the safe extraction of the target material. The percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster recovery. The goal of the procedure is to restore healthy blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with the presence of foreign matter in the upper vein.
