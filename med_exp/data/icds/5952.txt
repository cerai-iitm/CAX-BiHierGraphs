Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical fusion of the lumbosacral joint, which connects the lower back and pelvis, using a non-autologous tissue substitute, such as a bone graft or bone morphogenetic protein. The surgery is performed through an anterior approach, meaning the incision is made in the front of the abdomen, to access the anterior column of the spine. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the anatomical structures and perform the fusion. The goal of the procedure is to stabilize the joint, relieve pain, and improve mobility by connecting the vertebrae and promoting bone growth.
