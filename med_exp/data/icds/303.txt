Bypass Coronary Artery, Two Arteries from Right Internal Mammary, Open Approach
A coronary artery bypass grafting procedure involves the use of the right internal mammary artery to bypass two blocked or narrowed coronary arteries, improving blood flow to the heart. This open approach surgery requires a sternotomy, or division of the breastbone, to access the heart and graft the internal mammary artery onto the blocked coronary arteries. The internal mammary artery is a reliable and durable conduit that provides a long-term solution for restoring blood flow to the heart. By using the internal mammary artery, surgeons can achieve a higher success rate and reduce the need for future revascularization procedures.
