Release Left Lung, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visualize the left lung and relieve any compression or constriction. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. Using specialized instruments, the healthcare provider carefully releases the left lung from any adhesions or restrictive tissue, restoring its natural functionality and alleviating symptoms such as shortness of breath or chest pain. The entire procedure is performed through a small incision, resulting in reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and faster recovery times.
