Excision of Lung Lingula, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to access the lung lingula, a narrow, tongue-shaped lobe located in the upper lobe of the left lung. The surgeon then carefully dissects and removes the lingula, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and blood vessels. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the affected tissue. Once the lingula is excised, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for post-operative recovery.
