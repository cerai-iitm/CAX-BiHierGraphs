Excision of Right Shoulder Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves making an incision in the right shoulder to access the affected bursa and ligament. The surgeon then carefully excises the inflamed or damaged bursa and ligament tissue to relieve pain and improve range of motion. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the problematic tissue. Following excision, the incision is closed, and the shoulder is immobilized to promote healing and reduce the risk of complications.
