Inspection of Pancreas, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to visually examine the pancreas. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, allows the healthcare provider to directly visualize the pancreatic tissue and identify any abnormalities or lesions. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation, and the insertion site may be numbed with a local anesthetic to minimize discomfort. The entire procedure is usually completed within 30-60 minutes, and the patient is typically monitored for a short period of time after the procedure to ensure there are no complications.
