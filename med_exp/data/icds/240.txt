Release Sacral Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical intervention involves making an incision to access the sacral nerve, which is a complex network of nerves located at the base of the spine. The procedure aims to relieve compression or entrapment of the nerve, which can cause chronic pain, numbness, or weakness in the lower back, pelvis, or legs. During the open approach, the surgeon carefully dissects the surrounding tissue to identify and gently release the entrapped nerve, restoring its normal function. By releasing the sacral nerve, this procedure can help alleviate painful symptoms and improve mobility and quality of life for patients.
