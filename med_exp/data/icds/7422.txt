Supplement Left Inguinal Region with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the left inguinal region to supplement the area with a synthetic substitute, such as a mesh or implant. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and accurately place the substitute material. The goal of this procedure is to provide additional support or reinforcement to the left inguinal region, which may be necessary due to a hernia, injury, or other condition. By supplementing the area with a synthetic substitute, the surgeon can help restore normal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain or discomfort.
