Supplement Left Internal Carotid Artery with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this surgical procedure, the left internal carotid artery is supplemented with a nonautologous tissue substitute, which is a biocompatible material sourced from outside the patient's body. The open approach involves making an incision in the neck to access the artery, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the area. The substitute material is then implanted to repair or replace damaged or diseased sections of the artery, restoring blood flow to the brain. This complex procedure requires precise surgical skill and careful planning to ensure a successful outcome.
