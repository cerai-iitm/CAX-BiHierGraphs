Excision of Right Wrist Joint, Open Approach
Surgical removal of the right wrist joint is a complex procedure that involves making an open incision to access the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to excise the damaged or diseased joint to alleviate pain, restore mobility, and improve overall function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, ensuring a precise and thorough excision. Post-operative care typically involves immobilization and rehabilitation to promote healing and optimal outcomes.
