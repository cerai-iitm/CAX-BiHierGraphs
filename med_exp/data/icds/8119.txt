Fluoroscopy of Other Lower Arteries using Other Contrast
This imaging procedure uses X-ray technology and a contrast agent to visualize the lower arteries, excluding the renal and iliac arteries, allowing clinicians to diagnose and monitor vascular conditions such as peripheral artery disease, stenosis, or aneurysms. During the procedure, a catheter is inserted through a small incision in the skin and guided through the arteries, releasing a contrast medium that highlights the blood vessels. The fluoroscope then captures real-time images of the arteries, enabling the radiologist to assess blood flow, vessel anatomy, and any potential abnormalities. The resulting images aid in the development of personalized treatment plans for patients with suspected or confirmed vascular diseases.
