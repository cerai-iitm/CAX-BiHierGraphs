Ultrasonography of Stomach
This non-invasive imaging test uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the stomach and its surrounding tissues. During the procedure, a probe called a transducer is placed on the abdomen, which emits sound waves that bounce off the stomach and are converted into images on a computer screen. The test is commonly used to evaluate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting, and can help diagnose conditions like stomach cancer, ulcers, and inflammatory diseases. The procedure is typically painless and does not require any preparation or sedation, making it a safe and convenient diagnostic tool.
