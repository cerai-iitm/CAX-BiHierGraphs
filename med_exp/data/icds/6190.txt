Reposition Left Ankle Joint, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left ankle joint to restore its natural alignment and function. The external approach allows the surgeon to access the affected joint through a incision made in the skin and soft tissue. The goal of the procedure is to correct any deformities or misalignments that may be causing pain, stiffness, or limited mobility in the ankle. By repositioning the joint, the surgeon can alleviate symptoms and improve the overall range of motion and stability of the ankle.
