Excision of Common Bile Duct, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal area to access the common bile duct, a tube that carries bile from the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine. The damaged or diseased portion of the duct is carefully excised, and the remaining healthy sections may be repaired or reconnected to restore normal bile flow. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bile duct and surrounding tissues, enabling precise dissection and removal of the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as bile duct obstruction, injury, or cancer, and to alleviate symptoms like jaundice, pain, and digestive issues.
