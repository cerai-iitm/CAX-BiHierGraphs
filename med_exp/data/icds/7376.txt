Excision of Left Foot, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left foot through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the foot. The goal of this procedure is to treat severe conditions affecting the left foot, such as gangrene, osteomyelitis, or severe trauma, that cannot be managed with other treatment options. The procedure requires careful dissection and ligation of blood vessels and nerves to ensure a clean excision of the foot. Following the procedure, the patient will require extensive rehabilitation and may require the use of prosthetic devices to regain mobility and functionality.
