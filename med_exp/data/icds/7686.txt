Introduction of Nutritional Substance into Upper GI, Percutaneous Approach
Through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, a nutritional substance is introduced directly into the upper gastrointestinal tract, providing essential nutrients to patients who have difficulty consuming food orally or require supplementation. This procedure involves creating a small incision in the abdomen, through which a feeding tube is inserted and guided into the stomach or small intestine. The nutritional substance, such as a specialized formula, is then administered through the tube, bypassing the mouth and esophagus. This approach allows for effective and efficient delivery of vital nutrients, supporting the patient's overall health and recovery.
