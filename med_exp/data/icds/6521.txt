Drainage of Vulva with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device to remove excess fluid, blood, or pus from the vulva, which is the external female genital area. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the healthcare provider to accurately place the drainage device. The goal of the procedure is to relieve discomfort, prevent infection, and promote healing by eliminating any accumulated fluids or debris. The drainage device is left in place for a period of time to allow for continuous drainage and monitoring of the affected area.
