Supplement Mitral Valve with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the mitral valve through a small incision in the groin area, where a catheter is guided through the blood vessels to the heart. A synthetic substitute is then deployed through the catheter to supplement the damaged mitral valve, allowing for improved blood flow and reduced symptoms. The entire procedure is performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or echocardiography, to ensure accurate placement of the synthetic substitute. The percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to the body and reduces recovery time compared to traditional open-heart surgery.
