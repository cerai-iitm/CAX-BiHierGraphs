Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Metal Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
In a surgical procedure, a diseased or damaged left hip joint is replaced with a metal synthetic substitute to restore normal hip function and alleviate pain. The uncemented technique is used, which means the implant is not fixed into the bone with cement, allowing for bone growth onto the implant surface. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes a single incision to access the affected joint, removing the damaged or arthritic joint and implanting the metal synthetic substitute. The goal of this procedure is to improve mobility, reduce pain, and enhance the overall quality of life for the patient.
