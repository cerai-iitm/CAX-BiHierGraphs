Transfer Left Trunk Muscle, Latissimus Dorsi Myocutaneous Flap, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves the transfer of the latissimus dorsi muscle and its overlying skin, known as a myocutaneous flap, from the trunk region to a new site to repair or reconstruct defects. The open approach means the procedure is performed through a surgical incision, allowing direct visualization of the anatomy. The latissimus dorsi muscle is a large, fan-shaped muscle located in the back, which is mobilized and transposed to the desired location. The goal of this procedure is to restore form and function to the affected area, often used in breast reconstruction, wound coverage, or other reconstructive surgeries.
