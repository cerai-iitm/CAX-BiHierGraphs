Excision of Back Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the surgical removal of skin tissue from the back through an external incision, performed for diagnostic purposes. The goal is to extract a sample of skin tissue for further examination, helping to identify underlying conditions or diseases. The external approach allows for direct access to the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely target the skin tissue to be excised. The tissue sample is then sent to a laboratory for analysis, supporting a definitive diagnosis and informing subsequent treatment decisions.
