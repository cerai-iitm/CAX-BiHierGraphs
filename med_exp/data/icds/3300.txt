Dilation of Common Hepatic Duct, Percutaneous Approach
This medical procedure involves using a percutaneous approach to dilate the common hepatic duct, which is a critical pathway for bile drainage from the liver and gallbladder. Through a small incision in the skin, a specialized instrument is inserted to gently widen the duct, restoring normal bile flow and alleviating blockages. The percutaneous method allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing recovery time and scarring. By relieving obstruction, this treatment helps to mitigate symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and fever, ultimately improving overall liver function and patient health.
