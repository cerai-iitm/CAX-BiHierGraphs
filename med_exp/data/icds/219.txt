Excision of Median Nerve, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical incision to access the median nerve, which runs from the forearm into the hand, to excise or remove a portion of the nerve. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the nerve and surrounding tissues to accurately identify and remove the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to relieve compression, inflammation, or trauma to the median nerve, which can cause symptoms such as numbness, tingling, or weakness in the hand and fingers. By removing the damaged portion of the nerve, the procedure aims to restore normal nerve function and alleviate symptoms.
