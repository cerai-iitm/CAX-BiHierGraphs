Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two or more lumbar vertebral joints using an autologous tissue substitute, a biological material harvested from the patient's own body. The operation is performed through a posterior approach, where the surgeon accesses the spine from the back, and involves an open incision to expose the vertebral joints. The autologous tissue substitute is used to facilitate the fusion process, promoting bone growth and stability in the affected area of the lower back. The anterior column, which includes the vertebral bodies and intervertebral discs, is also addressed during the procedure to ensure proper alignment and support.
