Excision of Bladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin and into the bladder to remove the bladder tissue, allowing for precise visualization and targeted removal of the affected area. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for a large incision, reducing recovery time and scarring. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and specialized instruments, enables the surgeon to navigate and excise the bladder tissue with precision and control. This technique provides an effective treatment option for patients with bladder cancer, tumors, or other abnormalities, offering a faster and less painful recovery compared to traditional open surgery.
