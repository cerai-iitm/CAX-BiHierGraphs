Fusion of Left Metatarsal-Phalangeal Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the left metatarsal-phalangeal joint, a process that aims to immobilize the joint and facilitate bone growth. During the open approach, the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected joint, removes any damaged or diseased tissue, and secures the joint with an internal fixation device to promote stability and alignment. The internal fixation device, typically consisting of plates, screws, or rods, is carefully positioned to ensure proper healing and weight-bearing capacity. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and restore function to the affected foot, allowing patients to engage in normal activities without discomfort or limitation.
