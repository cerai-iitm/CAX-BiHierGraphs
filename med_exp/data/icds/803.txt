Insertion of Infusion Device into Upper Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device into an upper artery through a small incision in the skin. The device is guided into place using imaging technology, such as X-ray or ultrasound, to ensure precise placement. Once in position, the device can be used to deliver medications, fluids, or other substances directly into the artery. Following the procedure, the incision site is typically closed with a small bandage or dressing, and the patient can expect to experience minimal discomfort and a quick recovery.
