Bypass Left Saphenous Vein to Lower Vein, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the vascular system, allowing the physician to bypass a diseased or blocked section of a lower vein by creating a detour through a healthy left saphenous vein. The procedure is performed through small incisions, reducing tissue trauma and promoting faster healing. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a catheter is inserted through the skin and navigated to the affected vein, where a graft or stent is placed to reroute blood flow. The percutaneous endoscopic approach offers fewer complications and less post-operative pain compared to traditional open surgical techniques.
