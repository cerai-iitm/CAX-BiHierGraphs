Inspection of Genitourinary Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Approach
This procedure involves the visual examination of the genitourinary tract, which includes the kidneys, bladder, ureters, and urethra. The examination is performed through a natural or artificial opening, such as the urethra or a surgical incision. The purpose of the inspection is to identify any abnormalities, diseases, or injuries in the genitourinary tract, such as tumors, inflammation, or blockages. The procedure may be performed using a scope or other specialized instruments, and is often done as a diagnostic tool to guide further treatment or intervention.
