Supplement Right Lower Lobe Bronchus with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically supplementing the right lower lobe bronchus with a tissue substitute derived from the patient's own body, a process that promotes healthy tissue growth and stability. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and repair the bronchus. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully implanted to reinforce the bronchial structure, ensuring proper airflow and preventing complications. This procedure is often performed to address congenital defects, injuries, or diseases that have damaged the bronchial tissue, promoting improved respiratory function and overall health.
