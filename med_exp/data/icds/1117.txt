Dilation of Left Anterior Tibial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the left anterior tibial artery through a small incision in the skin, typically in the groin area, using a percutaneous approach. A catheter with a balloon tip is then guided through the artery to the point of narrowing or blockage, where it is inflated to widen the artery and improve blood flow. The goal of the procedure is to treat peripheral artery disease, a condition characterized by narrowing or blockage of the arteries in the legs, which can cause pain, numbness, and weakness. Following the procedure, the incision site is usually closed with a small bandage and the patient is monitored for a short period to ensure there are no complications.
