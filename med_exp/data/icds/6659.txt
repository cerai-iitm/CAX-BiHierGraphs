Reposition Vagina, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves manually repositioning the vagina to its normal anatomical location through an open incision. The goal of this intervention is to correct vaginal prolapse, a condition where the vagina drops or slips out of place, often causing symptoms such as pelvic pressure, painful intercourse, and urinary incontinence. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen or vagina to access the affected area, gently repositions the vagina, and secures it in place using sutures or other fixation devices. The repositioning of the vagina helps to alleviate symptoms, restore pelvic anatomy, and improve overall quality of life.
