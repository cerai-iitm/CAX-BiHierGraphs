Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Upper GI, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the insertion of a therapeutic substance through a natural or artificial opening in the upper gastrointestinal tract using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The substance is guided through the upper GI tract under direct visualization, allowing for targeted delivery and minimizing potential side effects. The procedure is typically performed by a gastroenterologist or endoscopist in a hospital or outpatient setting, and may be used to treat various conditions affecting the esophagus, stomach, or duodenum. The use of an endoscope enables real-time visualization of the upper GI tract, ensuring accurate placement of the therapeutic substance.
