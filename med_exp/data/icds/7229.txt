Repair Left Index Finger, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the damaged tissues of the left index finger, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the affected area directly. The open approach enables the surgeon to inspect the finger's internal structures, identifying and addressing any damage to bones, tendons, or ligaments. The repair process may involve reattaching or reconnecting severed tissues, realigning or stabilizing bones, or removing any debris or fragments that may be impeding healing. Once the repairs are complete, the incision is closed, and the finger is immobilized to allow for proper healing and recovery.
