Extirpation of Matter from Left External Carotid Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the left external carotid artery, a major blood vessel supplying the head and neck. The surgeon then physically removes or extirpates unwanted matter, such as a blood clot, plaque, or other obstructive material, from the artery to restore blood flow and prevent further complications. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the artery and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately target the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to prevent stroke, improve cerebral perfusion, and alleviate symptoms related to carotid artery disease.
