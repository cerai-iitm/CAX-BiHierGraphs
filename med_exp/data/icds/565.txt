Replacement of Mitral Valve with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the replacement of the mitral valve with a synthetic substitute using an open approach, requiring a sternotomy or thoracotomy incision to access the heart. The diseased or damaged mitral valve is removed and replaced with a mechanical or bioprosthetic valve, restoring normal blood flow between the left atrium and left ventricle. The synthetic valve is carefully selected based on factors such as the patient's age, overall health, and lifestyle to ensure optimal outcomes. Post-operatively, patients typically require several days of hospitalization and several weeks of recovery to regain strength and full function.
