Drainage of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
The procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to access the right upper lung lobe, allowing for visualization and drainage of the area. This minimally invasive technique is used to diagnose and manage conditions such as pneumonia, lung abscesses, or pleural effusions. During the procedure, the endoscope is guided through the airways or a surgically created opening to reach the affected lobe, and a drainage tube or suction device is used to remove excess fluid or debris. The goal of the procedure is to restore lung function and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, chest pain, or coughing.
