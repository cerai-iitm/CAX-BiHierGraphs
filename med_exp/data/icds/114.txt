Extirpation of Matter from Intracranial Subarachnoid Space, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through a small incision in the skin to access the intracranial subarachnoid space. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, allows the surgeon to visualize the area and locate the abnormal material. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully remove the unwanted matter, such as blood clots, inflammation, or infection, from the space surrounding the brain and spinal cord. This approach reduces tissue disruption and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgical techniques.
