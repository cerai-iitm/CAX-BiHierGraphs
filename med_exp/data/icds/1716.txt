Drainage of Spleen with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves piercing the skin to access the spleen, allowing a drainage device to be inserted to relieve excess fluid or abscesses. Under imaging guidance, a thin catheter or tube is advanced through the skin and into the spleen to facilitate drainage. The procedure is typically performed in a radiology suite or operating room under local anesthesia and conscious sedation. The goal of the procedure is to drain infected or excess fluid, reducing the risk of splenic rupture and promoting healing.
