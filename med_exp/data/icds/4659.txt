Reattachment of Right Hand Tendon, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an open approach is utilized to reestablish the connection of a damaged or severed tendon in the right hand. The surgeon makes an incision in the affected area to access the injured tendon, and then carefully reattaches it to the surrounding tissue or bone. This delicate process requires precision and expertise to ensure proper healing and restore normal function to the hand. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain, improve range of motion, and enable the individual to regain strength and dexterity in the affected hand.
