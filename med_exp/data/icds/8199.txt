Ultrasonography of Mediastinum
This non-invasive diagnostic imaging test uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the mediastinum, the region in the chest cavity that contains the heart, trachea, esophagus, and major blood vessels. During the procedure, a handheld probe called a transducer is placed on the chest and abdomen to transmit and receive the sound waves, which are then converted into images on a video screen. These images help healthcare providers assess the mediastinum for any abnormalities, such as tumors, cysts, or fluid accumulation, and guide further diagnostic or therapeutic interventions. The test is typically performed in a radiology department or cardiology clinic and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete.
