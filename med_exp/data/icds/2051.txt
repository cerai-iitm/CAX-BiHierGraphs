Destruction of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the destruction of diseased or damaged tissue in the left upper lung lobe through an endoscope, which is inserted via a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope guides the surgeon to the targeted area, allowing for precise removal of the affected tissue, with minimal trauma to surrounding healthy tissue. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, emphysema, or benign tumors. The destruction of the damaged tissue can help to improve lung function, relieve symptoms, and enhance overall quality of life.
