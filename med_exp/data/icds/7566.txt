Transfusion of Nonautologous Platelets into Peripheral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Platelet transfusion is a medical procedure in which platelets from a donor are infused into a patient's peripheral vein through a percutaneous approach. This is typically performed to treat thrombocytopenia, a condition characterized by a low platelet count, or to prevent bleeding in patients undergoing surgery or chemotherapy. The platelets are obtained from a donor and packaged in a unit, which is then administered to the patient through a needle inserted into a vein, usually in the arm. The goal of the procedure is to increase the patient's platelet count and reduce the risk of bleeding complications.
