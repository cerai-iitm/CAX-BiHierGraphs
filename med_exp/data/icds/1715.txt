Drainage of Spleen, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the spleen, a vital organ located in the upper left region of the abdominal cavity. The surgeon then carefully drains the spleen to remove excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may be causing discomfort, pain, or infection. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the spleen and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to identify and address any anatomical abnormalities or pathologies. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms, prevent further complications, and promote healing.
