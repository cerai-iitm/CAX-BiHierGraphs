Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
Revision of synthetic substitute in the right knee joint involves a surgical procedure where an existing artificial component is removed and replaced with a new one to restore joint function and alleviate pain. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the revision. This complex procedure requires meticulous attention to detail to ensure proper alignment and fixation of the new synthetic substitute. The goal is to improve mobility, reduce discomfort, and enhance overall quality of life for the patient.
