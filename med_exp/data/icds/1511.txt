Revision of Infusion Device in Upper Vein, Open Approach
During an open procedure, the existing infusion device in the upper vein is revised to improve its functionality or replace a malfunctioning component. This may involve surgically exposing the device, inspecting its components, and replacing or repairing any faulty parts to ensure effective infusion therapy. The revision process is typically performed under general anesthesia or local sedation to minimize patient discomfort. The goal of the procedure is to restore the device's function, allowing for the safe and efficient delivery of medications or fluids to the patient.
