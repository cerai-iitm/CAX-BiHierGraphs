Removal of Contraceptive Device from Uterus and Cervix, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower abdomen to access the uterus and cervix, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and remove a contraceptive device, such as an intrauterine device (IUD), that has been previously inserted for birth control purposes. The open approach requires a larger incision, which enables the surgeon to manually retrieve the device, usually under general anesthesia. The procedure is typically performed in an operating room setting, and the surgeon may use specialized instruments to gently grasp and extract the device from the uterus and cervix. Following the removal, the incision is closed, and the patient is usually monitored for a short period to ensure no complications arise.
