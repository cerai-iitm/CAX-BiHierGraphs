Bypass Coronary Artery, One Artery from Right Internal Mammary with Autologous Arterial Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves bypassing a blocked coronary artery by grafting a piece of the right internal mammary artery to improve blood flow to the heart. The internal mammary artery is used as an autologous arterial tissue, meaning it is taken from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest is surgically opened to access the heart and perform the bypass. The goal of this procedure is to restore blood flow and oxygen delivery to the heart muscle, relieving symptoms such as chest pain and shortness of breath.
