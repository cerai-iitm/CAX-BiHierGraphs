Repair Left Shoulder Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left shoulder to access the tendon, which is then repaired using sutures or other materials to restore its natural function and strength. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the damaged tendon and surrounding tissue, enabling a more accurate and effective repair. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia, and the incision is usually closed with sutures or staples after the repair is complete. Post-operatively, the patient may require physical therapy to regain strength and range of motion in the shoulder.
