Extraction of Lumbar Vertebra, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This surgical procedure involves removing a lumbar vertebra through an open incision in the lower back. The operation requires a careful dissection of the surrounding soft tissues to expose the affected vertebra, which is then carefully extracted from the spine. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pressure on the spinal cord and nerve roots, often caused by conditions such as spinal stenosis, herniated discs, or spinal tumors. By removing the affected vertebra, the procedure aims to relieve back pain, numbness, or weakness in the legs, and improve mobility and overall quality of life.
