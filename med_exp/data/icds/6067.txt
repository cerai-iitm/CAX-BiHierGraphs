Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Right Ankle Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right ankle joint to access and remove a synthetic substitute, such as an artificial implant or prosthetic device, that has been previously inserted into the joint. The open approach requires a larger incision to allow direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues. Once the substitute is removed, the joint is thoroughly cleansed and irrigated to remove any remaining debris or bacteria. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the site is dressed to promote healing and prevent infection.
