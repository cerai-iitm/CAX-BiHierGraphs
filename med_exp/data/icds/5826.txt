Resection of Thoracic Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the thoracic region to access the spine, allowing the surgeon to remove a herniated or degenerated disc that is compressing the spinal cord or nerve roots. The objective is to relieve pressure, alleviate pain and numbness, and restore mobility to the affected area. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the surrounding tissue, remove the damaged disc, and may also perform additional procedures such as spinal fusion or instrumentation to stabilize the spine. Post-operative care typically involves a period of rest and rehabilitation to facilitate a smooth recovery.
