Supplement Thoracic Aorta, Descending with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical supplementation of the descending thoracic aorta using zooplastic tissue, which is a biocompatible material derived from animal sources. During the open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the affected area and carefully supplements the aorta with the zooplastic tissue to restore its function. This may be necessary to treat conditions such as aneurysms, aortic dissections, or atherosclerosis, which can compromise blood flow and put patients at risk of rupture or other life-threatening complications. The goal of the procedure is to reinforce and stabilize the aorta, promoting normal blood circulation and reducing the risk of future complications.
