Extraction of Right Middle Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the right middle lung lobe. The endoscope provides direct visualization of the lung tissue, allowing the surgeon to identify and remove the affected lung tissue or lesion. The extraction is performed through the endoscope, minimizing the need for a large incision and promoting less tissue damage and scarring. This diagnostic procedure is used to collect tissue samples for further examination and diagnosis of respiratory disorders or diseases.
