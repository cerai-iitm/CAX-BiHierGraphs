Excision of Lumbar Spinal Cord, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lower back to access the lumbar spinal cord, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove a section of the spinal cord tissue. The goal of this procedure is often to alleviate symptoms caused by spinal cord injuries, tumors, or other abnormalities that compress or damage the spinal cord. During the procedure, the surgeon takes great care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves and tissues. The excision of the targeted spinal cord segment is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require several hours to complete.
