Extraction of Sigmoid Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During a sigmoid colon extraction procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, known as an endoscope, is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the inside of the sigmoid colon. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort. Using specialized instruments, the surgeon removes the sigmoid colon through the natural or artificial opening, taking care to minimize trauma to surrounding tissues. The procedure is usually performed to treat conditions such as diverticulitis, colon cancer, or bowel obstruction, and may be performed laparoscopically or open, depending on the individual case.
