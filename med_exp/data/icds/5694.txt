Fusion of Thoracic Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this surgical procedure, the anterior approach is employed to access the thoracic vertebral joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the joint directly. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to expose the joint, and an autologous tissue substitute is used to facilitate fusion. The substitute is typically harvested from the patient's own body, such as from the iliac crest, and is used to promote bone growth and stabilize the joint. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and instability in the thoracic spine by fusing the affected joint, restoring normal spinal alignment and function.
