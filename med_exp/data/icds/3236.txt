Supplement Transverse Colon with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the transverse colon with autologous tissue substitute, which is a biological material derived from the patient's own body, to repair or replace damaged or diseased tissue. The open approach requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the colon and perform the supplementation. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully implanted to restore the colon's natural function and structure, promoting healing and reducing the risk of complications. This complex procedure requires a skilled surgeon and a sterile operating environment to minimize the risk of infection and ensure a successful outcome.
