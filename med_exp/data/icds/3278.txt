Change Drainage Device in Gallbladder, External Approach
A minimally invasive surgical procedure is performed to replace a drainage device in the gallbladder, which is a hollow organ located under the liver that stores bile. The procedure is done through an external approach, involving a small incision in the skin and underlying tissue. The new drainage device is inserted to facilitate the flow of bile and relieve any blockage or obstruction. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal gallbladder function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, nausea, and vomiting associated with gallbladder disease.
