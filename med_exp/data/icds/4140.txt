Extraction of Left Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
The procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles and other soft tissue structures. The surgeon then carefully extracts the affected subcutaneous tissue and fascia, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues and structures. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the targeted tissue and fascia. The extracted tissue and fascia are then sent for further examination or analysis, helping to guide further treatment and management of the underlying condition.
