Replacement of Head Muscle with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open replacement of a head muscle with an autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft taken from the patient's own body. The goal of the procedure is to restore muscle function and alleviate symptoms such as weakness, pain, or limited range of motion. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the affected area to access the damaged muscle, removes the damaged tissue, and replaces it with the autologous tissue substitute. The graft is then secured in place and the incision is closed, allowing the patient to recover and eventually regain normal muscle function.
