Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Left Upper Femur, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and tissue of the left upper thigh to access the femur bone. An internal fixation device, designed to provide stability and support, is then carefully inserted into the bone through the open incision. The device helps to hold the bone in place while it heals, promoting proper alignment and reducing the risk of further injury. Once the device is securely in place, the incision is closed, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
