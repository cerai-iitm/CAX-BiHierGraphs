Release Pelvic Region Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to release the pelvic region's subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue that surround and support muscles, organs, and other structures. The goal of this procedure is to relieve tension, compression, or restriction in the pelvic area, which can be caused by a variety of conditions such as scarring, trauma, or inflammation. The surgeon will carefully dissect and release the affected tissue and fascia to restore normal mobility, flexibility, and function to the pelvic region. This procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as pelvic pain, limited mobility, or nerve entrapment.
