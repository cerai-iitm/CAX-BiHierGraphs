Occlusion of Left Inguinal Lymphatic, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the left groin area to access the inguinal lymphatic vessels. The objective is to occlude, or block, the left inguinal lymphatic vessel to prevent the flow of lymphatic fluid. This is typically performed to treat conditions such as lymphedema or lymphatic leaks. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the affected area and directly occlude the vessel, ensuring accurate and effective treatment.
