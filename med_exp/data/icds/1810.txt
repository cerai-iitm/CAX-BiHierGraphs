Resection of Right Neck Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of lymphatic tissue in the right neck area through an open approach, which may be necessary to diagnose or treat conditions such as lymphoma, cancer, or infections. The operation typically begins with an incision in the neck, followed by careful dissection and identification of the affected lymphatic structures. The targeted lymphatic tissue is then carefully resected, and the incision is closed in layers to promote optimal healing. The goal of this procedure is to remove the diseased or damaged lymphatic tissue, reduce symptoms, and improve overall health and well-being.
