Release Gallbladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, inserted through the skin to visualize the gallbladder. The endoscope is guided through the skin and into the gallbladder to release any blockages or obstructions, allowing for the normal flow of bile. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and guided by imaging technologies such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery time and less scarring compared to traditional open surgery.
