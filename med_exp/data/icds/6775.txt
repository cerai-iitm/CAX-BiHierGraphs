Resection of Bilateral Seminal Vesicles, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the surgical removal of both seminal vesicles, which are glands located behind the prostate gland, through small incisions in the skin. A flexible scope with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through the incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the glands. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as seminal vesicle stones or abscesses that are causing symptoms.
