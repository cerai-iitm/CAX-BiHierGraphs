Excision of Left Saphenous Vein, Open Approach
This vascular procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left saphenous vein, which is then surgically removed through an open approach. The goal of this procedure is often to harvest the vein for use in bypass grafting or to treat venous insufficiency or varicosities. The surgery typically takes place under general anesthesia and requires careful dissection to avoid damaging surrounding tissues. The excised vein segment can be used to create a graft for coronary artery bypass or peripheral artery bypass surgeries.
