Excision of Right Saphenous Vein, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, the doctor makes a small incision in the skin to access the right saphenous vein. Using specialized instruments and imaging guidance, the affected section of the vein is carefully excised through a percutaneous approach, minimizing trauma to surrounding tissue. This procedure is often performed to treat venous insufficiency, varicose veins, or other vein-related disorders. The excision of the saphenous vein can help alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and skin discoloration, improving overall circulatory health.
