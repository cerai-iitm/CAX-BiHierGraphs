Excision of Gallbladder, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the gallbladder, which is then carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding tissues and organs. The surgeon may also inspect the bile ducts and remove any stones or debris present in the ducts or gallbladder. The entire gallbladder is carefully excised, ensuring that no fragments or remnants are left behind, and the incision is then closed, typically with sutures or staples. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the gallbladder and reduce the risk of complications.
