Release Left Pleura, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the left pleura, a thin membrane surrounding the lungs, through a small incision in the chest wall. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that allow the physician to carefully release any adhesions or scar tissue that may be causing the pleura to stick together. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing. Once the pleura is released, the endoscope is removed, and the incision is closed, allowing the patient to recover from the procedure.
