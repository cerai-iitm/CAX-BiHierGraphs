Drainage of Chest Wall, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Here is a detailed description based on the ICD 10 Procedure Code 0W983ZX and its long title:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin and into the chest wall to remove fluid, blood, or air that has accumulated in the pleural space. The percutaneous approach allows for a small incision and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgical drainage. Imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan, is often used to ensure accurate placement of the drainage tube. The goal of this procedure is to promote healing, relieve symptoms, and improve lung function by removing the offending fluid or material.
