Resection of Ascending Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the ascending colon through a small incision using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the area. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced tissue damage and scarring compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may involve the use of specialized instruments to dissect and remove the affected colon segment. Post-operative care usually involves close monitoring and management of potential complications, such as infection or bleeding, to promote a smooth recovery.
