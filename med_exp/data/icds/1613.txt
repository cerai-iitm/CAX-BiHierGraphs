Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Common Iliac Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device into the right common iliac vein, a major blood vessel located in the lower abdomen. Access to the vein is gained through a small incision in the skin, typically in the groin area, allowing the device to be inserted percutaneously, or through the skin. The infusion device is then guided through the vein using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement. Once in place, the device allows for the administration of medications, fluids, or other treatments directly into the bloodstream.
