Drainage of Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the right knee joint, allowing for direct visualization and manipulation of the joint space. The goal of the procedure is to drain excess fluid, blood, or other material that may be causing discomfort, pain, or inhibiting movement in the knee joint. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly inspect the joint and remove any debris or inflammatory materials that may be contributing to the patient's symptoms. After the drainage is complete, the incision is typically closed, and the joint is allowed to heal, restoring normal function and alleviating pain and discomfort.
