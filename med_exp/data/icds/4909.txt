Reposition Right Mandible, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an external incision in the right mandible to gain access to the affected area. The goal is to reposition the right mandible, which may have been displaced due to injury or deformity, into its correct anatomical position. The surgeon will carefully manipulate the bone and surrounding soft tissue to achieve proper alignment, ensuring that the jaw functions correctly and the patient's facial aesthetics are restored. Post-operative care typically involves a recovery period, pain management, and a soft food diet to minimize discomfort and facilitate healing.
