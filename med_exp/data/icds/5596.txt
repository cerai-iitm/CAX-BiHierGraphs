Drainage of Right Wrist Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the skin to access the right wrist joint, allowing the surgeon to drain excess fluid, blood, or other debris that may be causing pain, swelling, or limited mobility. The open approach enables the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, facilitating the removal of any abnormal substances or matter. The procedure aims to restore normal joint function, alleviate symptoms, and promote healing. Following drainage, the incision is closed, and the wrist is typically immobilized to facilitate recovery.
