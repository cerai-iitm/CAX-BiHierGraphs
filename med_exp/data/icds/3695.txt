Excision of Left Lower Leg Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of skin tissue from the left lower leg through an external approach, typically performed to diagnose and treat skin lesions, ulcers, or wounds. The external approach allows the surgeon to access the affected skin area directly, enabling a more precise excision of the damaged tissue. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin, carefully removes the affected tissue, and closes the wound using sutures or staples. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to determine the underlying cause of the condition.
