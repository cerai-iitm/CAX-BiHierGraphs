Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Left Shoulder Joint, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left shoulder joint to access the artificial substitute that requires removal. The implant, which was previously inserted to replace or augment a damaged or diseased joint, is carefully extracted from the joint cavity to prevent any further complications. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve repairing or reconstructing surrounding tissue and bone to restore joint stability and function. Post-operatively, the patient may require physical therapy to regain strength and mobility in the affected shoulder.
