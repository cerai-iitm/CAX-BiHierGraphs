Dilation of Bilateral Ureters with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to access the bilateral ureters through a natural or artificial opening. A small intraluminal device is then inserted through the endoscope to dilate the ureters, allowing for the widening of the narrow tubes that carry urine from the kidneys to the bladder. This procedure is typically performed to treat ureteral strictures or narrowing, which can cause obstruction of urine flow and lead to complications. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal urine flow and alleviate associated symptoms such as flank pain and urinary tract infections.
