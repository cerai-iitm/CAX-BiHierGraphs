Resection of Right Adrenal Gland, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the right adrenal gland through a small incision, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the area. The percutaneous approach allows for a less invasive and more precise resection, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. Under guidance from radiological imaging, the surgeon inserts specialized instruments through the small incision to carefully dissect and remove the gland. Post-procedure, the small incision is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for a short period before discharge.
