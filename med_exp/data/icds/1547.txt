Dilation of Left Common Iliac Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of a thin catheter through the skin and guided to the left common iliac vein, allowing for the precise placement of an intraluminal device to dilate the narrowed or blocked vein. The device is designed to widen the vein, restoring blood flow and alleviating symptoms caused by venous obstruction. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the device is expanded to the desired diameter, and its position is confirmed to ensure optimal results. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces recovery time and allows patients to quickly return to their normal activities.
