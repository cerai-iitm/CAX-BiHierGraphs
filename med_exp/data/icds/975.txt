Bypass Left Femoral Artery to Popliteal Artery with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical intervention involves creating a bypass graft to redirect blood flow from the left femoral artery to the popliteal artery, effectively bypassing a blocked or damaged section of the artery. The procedure is performed through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. A synthetic substitute, such as a synthetic graft or stent, is used to create the bypass, which is then implanted to restore blood flow to the lower leg. This complex vascular surgery is typically performed to alleviate severe symptoms of peripheral artery disease, such as leg pain, numbness, and weakness.
