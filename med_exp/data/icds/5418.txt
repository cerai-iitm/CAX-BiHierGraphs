Reposition Right Upper Femur with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
During this percutaneous surgical procedure, the surgeon corrects the alignment of the right upper femur, also known as the proximal femur, using an intramedullary internal fixation device. The procedure is performed through small incisions, minimizing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. The internal fixation device is inserted through the medullary canal of the femur, providing stability and support to the bone. Once the bone is repositioned, the device is secured in place, allowing the bone to heal in its corrected position.
