Replacement of Right Neck Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this surgical procedure, the damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the right neck area are removed and replaced with autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and repair the damaged tissue. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully grafted into place to restore the normal anatomy and function of the neck. The goal of this procedure is to improve the aesthetic appearance and functional mobility of the neck, while minimizing the risk of complications and promoting optimal healing.
