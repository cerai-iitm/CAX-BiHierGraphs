Excision of Ileum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the ileum, a portion of the small intestine, through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that allow the surgeon to excise a portion of the ileum for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires minimal incisions, resulting in less tissue damage and scarring. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in the diagnosis of any underlying conditions or diseases.
