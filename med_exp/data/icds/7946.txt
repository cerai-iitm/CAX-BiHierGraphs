Fluoroscopy of Right and Left Heart using Other Contrast
This radiological procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the right and left heart chambers in real-time, using a contrast agent other than iodine or barium. The contrast agent is injected into the patient's bloodstream, allowing the cardiologist to observe the flow of blood through the heart and its chambers. The fluoroscopic images provide detailed information about the heart's structure and function, helping to diagnose conditions such as heart valve problems, cardiac shunts, and congenital heart defects. The procedure is typically performed in a catheterization laboratory or radiology suite, and is often used in conjunction with other diagnostic tests to guide treatment decisions.
