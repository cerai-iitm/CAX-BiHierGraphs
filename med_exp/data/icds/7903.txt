Robotic Assisted Procedure of Trunk Region, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the use of a robotic system to assist in an open approach operation on the trunk region of the body. The robotic system provides enhanced visualization, precision, and dexterity, allowing the surgeon to perform complex dissections and reconstructions with minimal trauma to surrounding tissues. The open approach involves a larger incision to access the area, which enables the surgeon to directly view the anatomy and manipulate tissues. The combination of robotic assistance and open approach allows for a high degree of accuracy and control during the procedure.
