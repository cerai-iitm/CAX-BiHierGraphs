Drainage of Right Pleura, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the percutaneous drainage of the right pleura, a region between the lungs and chest cavity, to remove excess fluid or air that may be causing discomfort, difficulty breathing, or other symptoms. To perform the procedure, a healthcare professional will insert a small needle or catheter through the skin and into the pleural space using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan. The fluid or air is then drained from the pleural space, which can help to relieve symptoms and allow for proper lung function. The diagnostic aspect of this procedure allows for the collection of a sample for further analysis to determine the underlying cause of the fluid accumulation.
