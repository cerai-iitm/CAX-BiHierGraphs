Excision of Right Femoral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the removal of a section of the right femoral vein through a minimally invasive approach, utilizing small incisions and specialized instruments to access the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to treat venous diseases or injuries, such as deep vein thrombosis or vascular malformations, by excising the damaged or diseased portion of the vein. Under guided imaging, the interventional radiologist or vascular surgeon carefully excises the targeted segment of the right femoral vein, allowing for improved blood flow and alleviation of symptoms. The percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to surrounding tissues, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery.
