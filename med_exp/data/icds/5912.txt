Excision of Right Toe Phalangeal Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skin and soft tissues of the right foot to access the phalangeal joint of the right toe. The joint is then carefully excised, removing the affected or damaged joint tissue. The surrounding soft tissues are then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the incision is repaired to promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain and improve mobility in the right toe by eliminating the damaged joint.
