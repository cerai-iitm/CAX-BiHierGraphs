Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Subclavian Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an infusion device into the left subclavian vein, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or blood products directly into the bloodstream. A small incision is made in the skin, and a guidewire is inserted through the incision to access the vein. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the infusion device is then inserted over the guidewire and positioned within the left subclavian vein. The device is secured in place, and the incision site is closed, providing a secure and efficient way to deliver necessary therapies to the patient.
