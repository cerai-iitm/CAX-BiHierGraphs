Release Ulnar Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the ulnar nerve, which runs from the neck to the hand. The goal of the procedure is to relieve compression or pressure on the nerve, often caused by conditions such as cubital tunnel syndrome. During the operation, the surgeon carefully identifies and releases the compressed or scarred tissue surrounding the nerve, allowing it to move freely and function properly. By relieving the compression, the procedure aims to alleviate symptoms such as numbness, tingling, and weakness in the hand and fingers.
