Reposition Maxilla with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of the maxilla, which is the upper jawbone, to its correct alignment using an internal fixation device. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the bone and securely fasten it in place with an internal fixation device, such as plates or screws. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the upper jaw, often correcting issues related to facial asymmetry, bite problems, or traumatic injuries. The internal fixation device provides stable support to the bone as it heals, promoting optimal recovery and minimizing the risk of complications.
