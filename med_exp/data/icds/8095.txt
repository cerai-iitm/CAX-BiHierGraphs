Fluoroscopy of Right Renal Artery using Other Contrast
During this radiological procedure, a contrast agent is administered through an intravenous line or arterial injection to enhance visibility of the right renal artery. The procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam that produces real-time images of the artery and its surroundings. The contrast agent helps to highlight the artery and its branches, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize any abnormalities or blockages. This diagnostic test aids in the detection of conditions such as renal artery stenosis, aneurysms, or atherosclerosis that may affect blood flow to the kidneys.
