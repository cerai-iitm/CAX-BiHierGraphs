Replacement of Right Breast using Deep Inferior Epigastric Artery Perforator Flap, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical replacement of the right breast using a deep inferior epigastric artery perforator (DIEP) flap, which is a type of autologous tissue reconstruction. The DIEP flap is harvested from the lower abdomen and transferred to the chest to create a new breast mound. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and reconnect the blood vessels and nerves. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural appearance and function of the breast following a mastectomy or other breast deformity.
