Extirpation of Matter from Left Popliteal Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the left popliteal area to access the popliteal artery, a major blood vessel located behind the knee. The surgeon then removes unwanted substances, such as plaque, blood clots, or other debris, from the artery to restore proper blood flow to the lower leg. The open approach requires a surgical incision, which allows the surgeon to directly visualize the artery and perform the extirpation procedure under direct vision. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
