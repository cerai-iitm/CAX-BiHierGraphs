Replacement of Right Tibia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right tibia with a biological tissue substitute, using an open approach that allows direct visualization of the surgical site. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection and promoting a more natural healing process. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the affected area, carefully removing the damaged or diseased portion of the tibia and replacing it with the substitute tissue. The goal of this procedure is to restore function, stability, and alignment to the affected limb, alleviating pain and improving mobility.
