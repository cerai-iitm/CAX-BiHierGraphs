Excision of Left Pleura, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visualize the left pleura, a thin membrane lining the left lung. The procedure is used to diagnose various conditions that affect the pleura, such as pleural effusion, pleurisy, or mesothelioma. Under guided visualization, a small instrument is used to excise a sample of the pleural tissue for further examination. The percutaneous endoscopic approach is a low-risk procedure that allows for a quicker recovery and reduces the risk of complications compared to traditional open surgical methods.
