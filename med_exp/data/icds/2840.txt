Excision of Jejunum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to remove a portion of the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, through a natural or artificial opening in the body. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. During the procedure, the endoscope is inserted through the natural or artificial opening, allowing the surgeon to visualize the jejunum and excise the targeted section. The removed tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to help diagnose and treat underlying conditions.
