Extraction of Lower Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During this diagnostic endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the patient's mouth or a small incision to visualize the lower esophagus. The healthcare provider uses specialized instruments to extract tissue or cells from the lower esophagus for further examination. This procedure is usually performed under sedation or local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The extracted tissue samples are then sent to a laboratory for analysis to help diagnose conditions affecting the lower esophagus, such as cancer, inflammation, or gastroesophageal reflux disease.
