Bypass Coronary Artery, Two Arteries from Aorta with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This cardiac surgical procedure involves creating a detour around blocked or damaged coronary arteries to improve blood flow to the heart. The surgeon uses a section of healthy vein taken from the patient's own body to create a bypass graft, connecting it to the aorta and then to two coronary arteries. The open approach requires a thoracotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart and coronary arteries. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow, reduce symptoms, and improve overall heart function.
