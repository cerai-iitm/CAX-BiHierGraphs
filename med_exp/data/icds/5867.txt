Drainage of Right Ankle Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin over the right ankle joint to access the affected area. The healthcare provider then uses specialized instruments to drain excess fluid, blood, or other debris from the joint space. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any underlying issues. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure, reduce pain and inflammation, and promote healing in the affected joint.
