Destruction of Ampulla of Vater, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to access the Ampulla of Vater, a small muscular sac located at the junction of the bile and pancreatic ducts. Through the endoscope, a specialized instrument is used to destroy the ampulla, which is typically done to treat conditions such as gallstones or cancer that are blocking the bile ducts. The procedure is usually performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The destruction of the ampulla allows for the relief of bile duct obstruction, reducing symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and fever.
