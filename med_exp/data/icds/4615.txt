Division of Right Foot Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right foot to access and divide a specific tendon, typically to relieve tension or address inflammation. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tendon, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and separate the affected tendon fibers. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the tendon, taking care to preserve surrounding tissues and minimize scarring. Following division, the wound is closed, and the foot is immobilized to facilitate healing and promote optimal recovery.
