Introduction of Anesthetic Agent into Peripheral Nerves and Plexi, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the administration of an anesthetic agent into peripheral nerves and plexi to numb a specific area of the body, providing pain relief and facilitating medical interventions. The anesthetic is carefully inserted through the skin using a needle, allowing for precise targeting of the affected nerve or plexus. This approach is often used in outpatient settings, enabling patients to return to their normal activities shortly after the procedure. The procedure requires a high degree of precision and expertise to ensure accurate placement of the anesthetic and minimal risks of complications.
