Supplement Left Atrium with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This procedure involves an open surgical approach to supplement the left atrium with zooplastic tissue, a biocompatible material derived from animal sources. The goal is to repair or replace damaged or diseased tissue in the left atrium, which can improve cardiac function and overall health. The zooplastic tissue is carefully implanted into the left atrium through an incision, allowing for natural tissue integration and restored function. By using zooplastic tissue, this procedure offers a viable alternative to synthetic materials, promoting a more natural and durable repair.
