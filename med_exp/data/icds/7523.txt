Immobilization of Right Lower Leg using Splint
This procedure involves the application of a splint to the right lower leg to immobilize and stabilize the area, typically following a fracture, sprain, or other injury. The splint is carefully positioned and secured to restrict movement and promote healing, while also reducing pain and discomfort. The immobilization process helps to maintain proper alignment and prevent further injury to the affected area. By limiting mobility, the splint allows the body to focus on the healing process, promoting a faster and more effective recovery.
