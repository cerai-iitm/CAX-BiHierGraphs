Destruction of Diaphragm, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen or chest to access the diaphragm, a dome-shaped muscle that separates the chest cavity from the abdominal cavity. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to destroy or remove the damaged or diseased portion of the diaphragm. This may be necessary to treat conditions such as diaphragmatic paralysis or hernia, where the diaphragm is severely weakened or torn, causing breathing difficulties or other complications. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal diaphragmatic function and alleviate symptoms.
