Irrigation of Respiratory Tract using Irrigating Substance, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this endoscopic procedure, a specialized instrument is inserted through the mouth or nose to reach the respiratory tract, allowing for direct visualization of the affected area. A sterile irrigating substance is then flushed through the instrument to remove debris, mucus, or other obstructive materials from the airways. This gentle yet effective technique helps to restore patency and promote healthy breathing. Under the guidance of a trained healthcare professional, this minimally invasive procedure is performed to alleviate respiratory distress and improve overall lung function.
