Reposition Left Tarsal with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the abnormal alignment of the left tarsal bones is corrected through an open approach, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area. The procedure involves the use of an internal fixation device to stabilize and realign the bones, promoting proper healing and fusion. The internal fixation device may include plates, screws, or rods, which are carefully positioned to ensure optimal bone alignment and minimal risk of complications. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal anatomy and function to the left foot, alleviating pain and discomfort related to the misaligned tarsal bones.
