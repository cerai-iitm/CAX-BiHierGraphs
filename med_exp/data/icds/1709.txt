Drainage of Thorax Lymphatic, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
During this minimally invasive procedure, an endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the thoracic lymphatic system. The scope is guided to the affected area, allowing the physician to identify the source of the lymphatic issue. The physician then proceeds to drain the thoracic lymphatic system, removing excess fluid or other substances that may be causing symptoms. This diagnostic procedure helps to identify and treat conditions such as chylothorax, lymphatic leaks, or other lymphatic disorders.
