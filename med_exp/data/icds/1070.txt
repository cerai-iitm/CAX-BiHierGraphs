Dilation of Right Femoral Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through a small incision in the groin area to access the right femoral artery. A drug-eluting intraluminal device is then deployed through the catheter to dilate the narrowed or blocked section of the artery. The device releases medication to help prevent the artery from narrowing again and reduce the risk of restenosis. The percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery.
