Monitoring of Products of Conception, Nervous Electrical Activity, External Approach
This procedure involves the non-invasive external monitoring of the electrical activity of the nervous system in products of conception, such as a fetus, to assess its neurological development and detect any potential abnormalities. The monitoring is typically performed during pregnancy to identify any potential risks or complications, and to inform clinical decision-making. The external approach used in this procedure allows for real-time monitoring without the need for invasive procedures, making it a safer and less risky option for the mother and the fetus. The data collected through this monitoring can provide valuable insights into the neurological development of the fetus and help guide prenatal care.
