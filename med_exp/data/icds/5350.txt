Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Femoral Shaft, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device from the left femoral shaft through an open approach, where the surgeon makes an incision to access the device. The internal fixation device, which was previously inserted to stabilize and align the bone, is carefully removed to allow for full mobility and strength to return to the affected leg. This procedure is often performed once the bone has fully healed and the device is no longer necessary, reducing the risk of future complications. Under the guidance of a skilled surgeon, the removal process is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure a smooth recovery.
