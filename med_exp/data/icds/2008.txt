Repair Left Sphenoid Sinus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a light and camera, to visualize the left sphenoid sinus through a small incision. The endoscope is inserted through the nasal passage or a small opening in the face, allowing the surgeon to access the affected sinus. Under direct visualization, the surgeon can then repair any damage or abnormalities, such as removing polyps or other obstructions, and restore normal sinus function. The percutaneous approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissues, promoting faster healing and minimizing scarring.
