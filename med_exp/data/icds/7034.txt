Inspection of Pelvic Cavity, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the pelvic cavity is visually examined to identify any abnormalities or pathologies. This involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the pelvic region, allowing the surgeon to directly inspect the organs and tissues within. The procedure may be performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as endometriosis, ovarian cysts, or pelvic inflammatory disease. The open approach provides a clear view of the pelvic cavity, enabling the surgeon to assess the extent of the condition and develop an appropriate treatment plan.
