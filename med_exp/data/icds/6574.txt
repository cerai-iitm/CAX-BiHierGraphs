Excision of Vulva, External Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of affected tissue or lesions from the external vulva through an open approach, allowing for diagnostic examination of the excised tissue to determine the presence and type of disease or abnormality. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and may involve a incision in the vulva to access the affected area. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for pathological examination to provide a definitive diagnosis. The goal of this procedure is to diagnose and treat conditions such as vulvar cancer, precancerous lesions, or other abnormalities affecting the external vulva.
