Excision of Omentum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdominal wall to access the omentum, a fatty tissue that lines the abdominal cavity. The omentum is then carefully excised, or removed, to aid in diagnostic purposes, such as to examine the tissue for signs of disease or infection. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the omentum and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the affected tissue. Following the excision, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
