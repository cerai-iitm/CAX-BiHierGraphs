Resection of Products of Conception, Ectopic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves removing an ectopic pregnancy, where the fertilized egg implants outside the uterus, often in the fallopian tube, through an open incision in the abdomen. The goal is to prevent rupture and internal bleeding, which can be life-threatening if left untreated. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the affected area and carefully removes the ectopic pregnancy, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissue and organs. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for post-operative care and recovery.
