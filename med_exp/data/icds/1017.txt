Dilation of Left Renal Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small instrument through the skin to access the left renal artery, which is then dilated to improve blood flow using an intraluminal device, such as an angioplasty balloon or stent. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the interventional radiologist navigates the device to the targeted site, carefully inflating or deploying it to widen the artery and restore normal blood flow. As a result, this procedure can effectively treat conditions such as renal artery stenosis, which can lead to high blood pressure, kidney damage, and other complications.
