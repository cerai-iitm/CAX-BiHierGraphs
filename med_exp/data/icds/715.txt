Dilation of Right External Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through the skin and into the right external carotid artery to widen the narrowed or blocked artery. The device is guided through the artery using X-ray or ultrasound imaging to ensure precise placement. Once in position, the device expands to dilate the artery, restoring blood flow to the brain and other tissues. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and takes about an hour to complete.
