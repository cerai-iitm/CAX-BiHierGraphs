Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to drain excess fluid from the peritoneal cavity, a space in the abdominal region that contains the intestines, stomach, and liver. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The drainage device is carefully guided into the peritoneal cavity using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement and effective drainage. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as peritonitis, ascites, or intra-abdominal abscess, and can provide relief from symptoms such as abdominal pain, bloating, and fever.
