Excision of Rectum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the rectum through small incisions in the skin and abdominal wall, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the area. The surgeon inserts specialized instruments through the incisions to carefully dissect and separate the rectum from surrounding tissues and blood vessels. Once isolated, the rectum is then excised and removed through one of the small incisions, minimizing trauma to the surrounding area. The entire procedure is performed under image guidance, ensuring precise dissection and removal of the rectum while reducing the risk of complications.
