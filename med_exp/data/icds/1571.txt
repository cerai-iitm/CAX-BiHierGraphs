Excision of Right Saphenous Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the right saphenous vein, which is then carefully dissected and removed. The saphenous vein is a superficial vein located in the leg that plays a significant role in the return of blood to the heart. During the excision, the surgeon takes care to identify and ligate any smaller branching veins to prevent bleeding and promote healing. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as varicose veins or to harvest a segment of the saphenous vein for use in coronary artery bypass grafting.
