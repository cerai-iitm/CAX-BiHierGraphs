Excision of Left Lobe Liver, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a portion of the left lobe of the liver through a small incision in the skin, utilizing an endoscope to guide the surgical instruments. The diagnostic excision is performed under image guidance, allowing the surgeon to precisely target the area of the liver requiring examination. The percutaneous approach reduces recovery time and scarring, making it a desirable option for patients requiring liver tissue sampling. The extracted tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis of any underlying liver conditions.
