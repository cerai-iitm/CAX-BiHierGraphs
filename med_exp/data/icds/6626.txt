Release Uterus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the uterus and surrounding tissue through a small incision in the skin. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully release any adhesions or scar tissue that may be restricting the uterus or causing pelvic pain. The percutaneous approach allows for reduced recovery time and less post-operative pain compared to open surgery. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal uterine mobility and alleviate symptoms such as pelvic pain, heavy bleeding, and infertility.
