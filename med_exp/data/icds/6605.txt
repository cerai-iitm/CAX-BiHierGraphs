Inspection of Vagina and Cul-de-sac, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the vagina and cul-de-sac, which is the pouch-like space between the uterus and rectum. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to allow for direct visualization of the areas. The procedure is typically performed to diagnose and manage various gynecological conditions, such as abnormal bleeding, pelvic pain, or suspicious lesions. During the procedure, the healthcare provider can also collect tissue samples or perform other interventions as needed.
