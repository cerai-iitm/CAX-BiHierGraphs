Dilation of Right Ureter with Intraluminal Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the right ureter, which is then dilated using an intraluminal device to widen the pipe-like structure. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the ureter and surrounding tissue, enabling precise placement of the device to relieve blockages or strictures. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as kidney stones, tumors, or congenital abnormalities that obstruct urine flow. By dilating the ureter, the surgeon can help restore normal urinary function and alleviate associated symptoms such as pain, infection, or kidney damage.
