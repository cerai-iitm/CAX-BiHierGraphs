Excision of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of the right lower lung lobe through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach, which requires only small incisions. A thin, flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through the skin and guided to the lung lobe, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the affected tissue. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces tissue trauma and promotes faster healing compared to traditional open surgery. The excision of the right lower lung lobe is typically performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, abscesses, or severe lung damage.
