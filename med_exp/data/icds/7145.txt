Detachment at Right Thumb, Low, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the right thumb at a low level, meaning the separation occurs closer to the thumb's base rather than near the tip. The open approach indicates that the surgery requires a skin incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the thumb's anatomy. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or tingling in the thumb and surrounding areas. The surgery typically requires careful dissection and repair of surrounding tissues to ensure optimal healing and minimize complications.
