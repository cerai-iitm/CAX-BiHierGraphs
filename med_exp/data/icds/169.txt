Removal of Neurostimulator Lead from Cranial Nerve, Open Approach
During a surgical procedure, a neurostimulator lead is carefully removed from a cranial nerve, which involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the implant. The lead is gently dislodged from its attachment site and freed from surrounding tissue, taking care to avoid damaging nearby nerves or blood vessels. Once removed, the incision is closed, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing. This procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed in a hospital setting under the guidance of a qualified neurosurgeon or pain management specialist.
