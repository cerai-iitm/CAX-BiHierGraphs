Introduction of Adhesion Barrier into Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is the description:

During this minimally invasive procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, is inserted through a small incision in the abdominal wall to visualize the peritoneal cavity. A specialized adhesion barrier is then introduced through the endoscope to prevent or reduce the formation of adhesions, which are bands of fibrous tissue that can cause organs to stick together. The adhesion barrier helps to reduce the risk of complications during future surgeries and promotes healthy tissue healing. The entire procedure is performed under guided visualization using the endoscope, allowing for precise placement of the adhesion barrier and minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues.
