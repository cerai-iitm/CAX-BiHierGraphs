Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Subclavian Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to place an infusion device into the right subclavian vein, which is a major blood vessel located under the collarbone. Under imaging guidance, a small incision is made in the skin and a thin catheter is inserted through the incision into the vein. The infusion device is then advanced through the catheter and positioned in the vein, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or nutrients. Once in place, the infusion device is secured and the incision site is closed, enabling patients to receive necessary treatments with minimal discomfort and scarring.
