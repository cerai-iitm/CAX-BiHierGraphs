Repair Pelvic Region Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making small incisions in the skin to access and repair damaged or damaged subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the pelvic region. Through a percutaneous approach, the surgeon uses specialized instruments to close any defects or tears in the tissue, restoring normal anatomy and promoting healing. The procedure is typically performed under local or regional anesthesia, allowing for a quicker recovery and reduced risk of complications. By repairing the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, this procedure can help alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and instability in the pelvic region.
