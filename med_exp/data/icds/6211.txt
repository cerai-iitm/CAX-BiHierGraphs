Supplement Right Knee Joint with Liner, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the right knee joint with a liner, which is inserted through an open incision. The goal of the procedure is to restore the natural function and movement of the knee joint, alleviating pain and discomfort caused by damaged or worn-out joint surfaces. The surgeon makes an open incision in the affected area, exposing the knee joint, and then inserts a liner to cushion and support the joint. The liner helps to redistribute pressure, reducing wear and tear on the joint, and promoting improved mobility and function.
