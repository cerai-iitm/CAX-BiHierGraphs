Excision of Right Lobe Liver, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves using a percutaneous approach to remove tissue or a lesion from the right lobe of the liver for diagnostic purposes. The process typically begins with imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan, to locate the target area. A small incision is then made in the skin, and a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to reach the liver lobe. The affected tissue or lesion is then excised and removed for further examination and diagnosis.
