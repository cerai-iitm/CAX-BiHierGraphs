Insertion of Endotracheal Airway into Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of a tube into the trachea through the mouth or nose, or through a tracheostomy opening, to facilitate ventilation and oxygenation. The healthcare provider uses direct visualization or other guidance methods to ensure proper placement of the endotracheal tube, which is then secured in place to maintain a patent airway. The tube is inserted through the vocal cords and into the trachea, allowing for mechanical ventilation or oxygen therapy to support the patient's respiratory function. Proper insertion and positioning of the endotracheal tube are crucial to prevent complications and ensure effective respiratory management.
