Fluoroscopy of Right Heart using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure uses low osmolar contrast agents to visualize the right heart structures under fluoroscopic guidance, allowing for real-time assessment of cardiac anatomy and function. The procedure involves injecting a low osmolar contrast medium into the right heart, which is then visualized using continuous X-ray beams to produce fluoroscopic images. These images enable healthcare providers to evaluate the right heart's structure, function, and blood flow patterns, facilitating diagnosis and treatment of various cardiovascular conditions. The use of low osmolar contrast agents helps minimize potential allergic reactions and nephrotoxicity, making the procedure a safer option for patients.
