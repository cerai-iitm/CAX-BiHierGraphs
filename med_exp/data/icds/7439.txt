Abortion of Products of Conception, Laminaria, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the termination of a pregnancy using laminaria, a type of cervical dilator, through a natural or artificial opening in the cervix. The laminaria is inserted into the cervical canal to gradually dilate it, allowing for the removal of the products of conception. This method is often used in second-trimester abortions and may be performed in a hospital or clinical setting. The goal of the procedure is to safely and effectively end the pregnancy while minimizing potential complications.
