Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Renal Arteries
This imaging procedure utilizes continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time images of the bilateral renal arteries, allowing healthcare providers to visualize the blood vessels that supply the kidneys. The fluoroscopy machine produces instant images on a monitor, enabling physicians to assess the arteries' structure, function, and blood flow. The procedure is commonly used to diagnose and monitor conditions such as renal artery stenosis, aneurysms, and arteriovenous malformations. During the procedure, a contrast agent may be injected into the arteries to enhance image quality and provide more detailed information about the vascular anatomy.
