Extirpation of Matter from Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right knee joint to access and remove unwanted material, such as infected tissue, debris, or foreign bodies. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint, enabling the surgeon to carefully extract the matter and clean the affected area. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions like septic arthritis, osteomyelitis, or joint infections, and may be necessary to prevent further damage or complications. Following the extirpation, the incision is closed, and the knee joint is stabilized to promote healing and restore normal function.
