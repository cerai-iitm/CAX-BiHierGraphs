Excision of Left Lobe Liver, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left lobe of the liver through an open approach, which requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the liver. The surgeon carefully dissects the left lobe from the surrounding tissues and blood vessels, and then removes it from the body. The procedure is typically performed to treat liver cancer, liver tumors, or liver injuries, and is often done in conjunction with other surgical procedures. After the excision, the incision is closed and the patient is monitored for any complications.
