Inspection of Ureter, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small scope through a small incision in the skin to visually examine the ureter, allowing for direct visualization of the internal ureteral anatomy. The percutaneous approach enables a more detailed inspection of the ureter's internal structures, facilitating the diagnosis and treatment of various ureteral disorders. During the procedure, the healthcare provider uses fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance to navigate the scope through the skin and into the ureter. The inspection allows for the identification of any blockages, stones, or other abnormalities that may be causing symptoms or impacting urinary function.
