Excision of Esophagus, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to remove the esophagus through an open approach, which involves making a large incision in the chest or abdomen to access the esophagus. This procedure is typically performed to treat esophageal cancer or other diseases that affect the esophagus, such as Barrett's esophagus or achalasia. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects and isolates the esophagus from surrounding tissues and blood vessels before removing it. The removal of the esophagus can be a complex and delicate procedure that requires great skill and attention to detail to ensure optimal outcomes.
