Fusion of Right Sacroiliac Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open fusion of the right sacroiliac joint using an autologous tissue substitute, which is derived from the patient's own body. The goal of the fusion is to stabilize the joint and alleviate symptoms such as pain and instability. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an open incision to access the joint and prepares the bone surfaces for fusion. The autologous tissue substitute is then applied to the joint, which promotes bone growth and fusion over time.
