Removal of Intraluminal Device from Kidney, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous removal of an intraluminal device from the kidney involves a minimally invasive procedure where a small incision is made in the skin to access the device. Under imaging guidance, a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to carefully retrieve the device from within the kidney. This procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and is often used to remove stents, catheters, or other devices that have been implanted in the kidney to treat conditions such as kidney stones or blockages. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal kidney function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, infection, or decreased urine output.
