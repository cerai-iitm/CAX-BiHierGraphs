Supplement Left Upper Lobe Bronchus with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a section of the left upper lobe bronchus is reinforced with an autologous tissue substitute, which is harvested from the patient's own body, to provide additional structural support and promote healing. This supplementation is necessary to repair or replace damaged or defectively formed portions of the bronchial tissue, ensuring proper airflow and ventilation. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully selected and prepared to match the patient's natural tissue, minimizing the risk of rejection or adverse reaction. The surgical team takes great care to restore the natural anatomy and function of the left upper lobe bronchus, ensuring optimal respiratory health and well-being.
