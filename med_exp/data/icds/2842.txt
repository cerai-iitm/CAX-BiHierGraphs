Excision of Ileum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the ileum, a section of the small intestine. The surgeon then carefully dissects and removes the affected portion of the ileum, taking care to minimize damage to nearby tissues and organs. The remaining healthy sections of the intestine are then reattached, and the incision is closed. The purpose of this procedure is to treat conditions such as Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, or intestinal blockages that cannot be resolved through other means.
