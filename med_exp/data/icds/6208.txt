Supplement Right Hip Joint, Acetabular Surface with Liner, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this surgical procedure, the acetabular surface of the right hip joint is supplemented with a liner through an open approach, which involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural function of the hip joint by providing a smooth, articulating surface for the femoral head to move against. The liner is carefully positioned and secured to ensure proper fit and alignment, promoting optimal joint mobility and reducing the risk of future complications. By supplementing the acetabular surface, this procedure aims to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility in the right hip joint.
