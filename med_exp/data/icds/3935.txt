Drainage of Right Neck Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right neck area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of connective tissue beneath the skin. The goal of the procedure is to drain excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may have accumulated in these tissues. During the open approach, the surgeon visually examines the affected area and manually removes the accumulated material, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues or structures. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as infection, abscess, or trauma to the neck region.
