Inspection of Hepatobiliary Duct, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical exploration of the hepatobiliary ducts, which connect the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine, allowing bile to flow through. During the open approach, a incision is made in the abdomen to access the ducts, allowing the healthcare provider to visually examine the ducts and identify any abnormalities or blockages. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and potentially treat conditions affecting the hepatobiliary system, such as gallstones, strictures, or cancer. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed in an operating room setting.
