Insertion of Contraceptive Device into Uterus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the placement of a contraceptive device into the uterus through either the natural opening of the cervix or an artificial opening created by a healthcare professional. The device is typically inserted using a specialized instrument, and the process is usually performed in a healthcare provider's office or clinic. The contraceptive device is designed to prevent pregnancy by blocking the fertilization of an egg or preventing a fertilized egg from implanting in the uterus. The procedure is a common form of long-acting reversible contraception, offering an effective and convenient method for women to manage their reproductive health.
