Drainage of Left Hip Joint, Open Approach, Diagnostic
During an open surgical procedure, a healthcare provider makes an incision to access the left hip joint, allowing for direct visualization of the joint cavity. The goal of this procedure is to drain accumulated fluid, such as blood or pus, that may be causing symptoms like pain, swelling, or limited mobility. Using sterile equipment, the surgeon carefully evacuates the collected fluid, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding soft tissues or joint structures. After drainage, the incision is typically closed, and the patient is monitored for signs of improvement.
