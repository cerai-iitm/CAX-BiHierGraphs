Insertion of External Fixation Device into Left Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower leg to access the tibia, followed by the insertion of an external fixation device to stabilize and align the bone. The external fixator is attached to the bone through pins or screws, which are connected to an external rod or frame that holds the bone in place. This type of procedure is often performed to treat complex fractures, deformities, or infections of the tibia that require stabilization and alignment to promote healing. The goal of external fixation is to restore the normal anatomy and function of the leg, allowing patients to regain mobility and reduce pain.
