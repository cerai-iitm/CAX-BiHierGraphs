Transfer Perineum Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Skin, Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of perineum subcutaneous tissue and fascia with skin from a donor site to a recipient site using an open approach. The open approach involves making a large incision to access the tissues, allowing for direct visualization and dissection of the fascia and subcutaneous tissue. The transferred tissue is then secured in place using sutures or staples, ensuring a secure and stable repair. The goal of this procedure is to restore form and function to the perineum, often following trauma, injury, or congenital defects.
