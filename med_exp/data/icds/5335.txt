Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Lumbar Vertebra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a previously implanted internal fixation device from the lumbar vertebra through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. The device, typically composed of rods, screws, and plates, is carefully disassembled and extracted from the vertebral bodies, taking care to avoid damage to surrounding tissues and spinal structures. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the operative site, enabling the surgeon to meticulously remove the hardware and restore normal anatomy. Following the procedure, the wound is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for potential complications and undergoes a period of rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility.
