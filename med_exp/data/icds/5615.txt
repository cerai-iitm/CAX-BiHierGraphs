Excision of Cervical Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
The surgical removal of a damaged or herniated cervical vertebral disc is performed through an open approach, where an incision is made in the neck to access the spine. The procedure involves dissecting the soft tissue and retracting the surrounding muscles to expose the affected disc, which is then carefully excised to alleviate pressure on the adjacent nerves and spinal cord. The goal of the surgery is to relieve symptoms such as pain, numbness, and tingling in the neck, arms, or legs, and to restore normal spinal function. The procedure requires meticulous technique and precise dissection to avoid injury to surrounding neural structures.
