Drainage of Right Hand Tendon, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right hand to access the affected tendon. The goal of the operation is to drain excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may be causing swelling, pain, or impaired movement in the hand. During the open approach, the surgeon will carefully dissect the soft tissue to localize the affected tendon and remove any abnormal accumulations. Following the drainage, the incision is closed, and the patient's hand is supported to promote healing and restore normal function.
