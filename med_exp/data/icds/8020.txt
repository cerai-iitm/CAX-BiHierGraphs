Fluoroscopy of Left External Carotid Artery
This procedure involves the use of continuous X-ray beams, known as fluoroscopy, to guide and visualize the left external carotid artery in real-time. The doctor injects a contrast agent into the artery to enhance visibility, allowing for precise imaging of the blood vessel. The resulting images enable the identification of any blockages, stenosis, or other abnormalities that may be affecting blood flow to the brain. The fluoroscopic guidance allows for accurate diagnosis and potential treatment of conditions such as carotid artery stenosis or occlusion.
