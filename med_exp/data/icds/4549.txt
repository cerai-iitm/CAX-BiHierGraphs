Supplement Left Lower Leg Muscle with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the muscles in the left lower leg with a substitute made from the patient's own tissue, obtained from a different part of the body. The open approach involves a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to carefully implant the autologous tissue substitute into the muscle. This procedure is used to repair or augment defective or inadequate muscles, often due to injury or disease. The goal is to restore normal muscle function, improve mobility, and enhance overall quality of life.
