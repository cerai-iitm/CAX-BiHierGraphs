Transplantation of Right Kidney, Allogeneic, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to transplant a kidney from a donor into the recipient's body, replacing the diseased or damaged right kidney. The allogeneic transplant involves taking a kidney from a genetically non-identical donor, requiring immunosuppressive therapy to prevent rejection. The open approach involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the area, allowing the surgeon to manually transplant the new kidney. This complex procedure is performed under general anesthesia, typically taking several hours to complete.
