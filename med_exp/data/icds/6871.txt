Drainage of Right Pleural Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin and into the right pleural cavity to remove excess fluid or air that has accumulated in the space surrounding the lung. A percutaneous approach is used, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the pleural cavity. The drainage device is guided into place using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement. The goal of this procedure is to relieve respiratory distress and improve lung function by removing the excess fluid or air that is compressing the lung.
