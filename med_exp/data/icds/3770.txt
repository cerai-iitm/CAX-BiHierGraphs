Repair Scalp Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the restoration of the scalp skin through an external approach, which may be necessary due to trauma, injury, or congenital defects. The procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the damaged skin, followed by the removal of any dead or damaged tissue. The skin is then repaired using sutures, staples, or skin grafts, and the wound is closed with careful attention to ensure proper blood supply and tissue alignment. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural appearance and function of the scalp, promoting healthy healing and minimizing scarring.
