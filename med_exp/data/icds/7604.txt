Introduction of Other Antineoplastic into Peripheral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the introduction of antineoplastic agents into a peripheral vein to treat cancer. A peripheral vein is selected and accessed using a needle, and the antineoplastic medication is administered through the inserted needle. The procedure is typically performed in a healthcare setting, such as a hospital or outpatient clinic, and may be performed by a healthcare professional, such as a nurse or physician. The goal of the procedure is to deliver targeted therapy to cancer cells, reducing the risk of tumor growth and improving treatment outcomes.
