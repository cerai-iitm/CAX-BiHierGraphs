Measurement of Cardiac Sampling and Pressure, Right Heart, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a catheter through the skin and into a vein, typically in the groin or arm, to access the right heart chambers. Once in place, the catheter is used to measure pressures and collect blood samples from the right heart, providing valuable diagnostic information. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the catheter's movement and ensure accurate placement. The collected data is then used to diagnose and manage various heart conditions, such as pulmonary hypertension, cardiomyopathy, and heart failure.
