Drainage of Right Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Here is the detailed description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage catheter through the skin and into the right pleural cavity to remove excess fluid or air that has accumulated between the lung and chest wall. Under imaging guidance, a needle is inserted into the pleural space to locate the fluid collection, and a drainage catheter is then passed over the needle to allow for the removal of the fluid. The procedure is typically performed to diagnose the cause of pleural effusion, a condition in which fluid builds up in the space between the lungs and chest wall. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms such as shortness of breath and chest pain, and to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of underlying conditions.
