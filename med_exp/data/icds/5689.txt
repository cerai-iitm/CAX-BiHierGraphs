Fusion of Cervicothoracic Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the cervicothoracic vertebral joint, which connects the neck and thoracic spine, using an interbody fusion device. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, where the surgeon accesses the spine from the back, and an open approach, meaning a larger incision is made to allow for direct visualization of the surgical site. The anterior column, which includes the vertebral bodies and intervertebral discs, is stabilized using the interbody fusion device to promote bone growth and fusion. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, restore spinal stability, and improve mobility by fusing the affected vertebral joint.
