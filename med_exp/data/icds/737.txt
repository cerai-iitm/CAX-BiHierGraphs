Excision of Intracranial Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access and remove a section of an intracranial artery, which is a blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the brain. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the artery and surrounding brain tissue to ensure precise removal of the targeted segment. The excision of the intracranial artery is typically performed to treat conditions such as aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or blockages that can cause hemorrhaging or stroke. The goal of the procedure is to restore healthy blood flow to the brain and prevent future complications.
