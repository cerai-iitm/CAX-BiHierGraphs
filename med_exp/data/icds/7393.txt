Inspection of Right Foot, Open Approach
During this procedure, a surgical incision is made in the right foot to allow for direct visualization of the affected area. The surgeon then carefully examines the foot to identify any abnormalities, such as tumors, cysts, or other lesions, and assesses the extent of any damage or disease. The open approach allows for a more comprehensive inspection of the foot's internal structures, including bones, tendons, and soft tissues. The goal of this procedure is to gather diagnostic information to guide further treatment or develop a plan for surgical intervention.
