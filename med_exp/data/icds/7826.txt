Monitoring of Gastrointestinal Pressure, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This non-invasive procedure involves the use of specialized equipment to measure and track pressure levels in the gastrointestinal tract, which includes the stomach, small intestine, and large intestine. The measurement is typically taken through a natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth, nose, or a surgically created stoma. The pressure monitoring helps healthcare professionals diagnose and manage gastrointestinal motility disorders, such as gastroparesis, intestinal obstruction, or constipation, and can also guide treatment decisions, including medication adjustments or surgical interventions. By providing real-time pressure data, this procedure enables prompt identification of abnormal patterns and early intervention to mitigate potential complications.
