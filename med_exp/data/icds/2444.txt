Revision of Tracheostomy Device in Trachea, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to replace or adjust a tracheostomy tube that has been previously inserted into the trachea. During the open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the neck to access the trachea and carefully removes the existing device. The tracheostomy tube is then replaced with a new one, ensuring proper fit and function to facilitate breathing. After the new device is secured, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for post-operative recovery.
