Excision of Omentum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdominal wall to access the omentum, a layer of fatty tissue that surrounds the abdominal organs. The omentum is then carefully dissected and removed, taking care to avoid damaging nearby blood vessels and organs. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be accompanied by other surgical interventions, such as tumor removal or repair of damaged tissue. The goal of the procedure is to remove diseased or damaged tissue, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall health outcomes.
