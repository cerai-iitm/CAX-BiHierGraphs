Reposition Left Pelvic Bone with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left pelvic bone to its correct anatomical position, which has been displaced due to injury or trauma. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the bone and stabilize it with an internal fixation device. The internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, is then attached to the bone to hold it in place and promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore the normal function and alignment of the pelvic bone, alleviating pain and discomfort, and enabling the patient to regain mobility and strength.
