Drainage of Right Kidney with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device into the right kidney through the skin, using imaging guidance, to drain excess fluid or urine that has accumulated due to a blockage or obstruction. The interventional radiologist or urologist makes a small incision in the skin and uses a guide wire and dilators to create a pathway to the kidney, allowing the drainage device to be placed accurately. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be accompanied by conscious sedation, and post-procedure care includes monitoring for bleeding, infection, and managing any discomfort or pain. The goal of this procedure is to relieve symptoms, restore kidney function, and prevent further complications.
