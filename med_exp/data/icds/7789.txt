Measurement of Arterial Flow, Coronary, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves using a minimally invasive percutaneous approach to measure the blood flow in the coronary arteries, which supply blood to the heart muscle. A small catheter is inserted through the skin and guided through an artery in the leg or arm to the coronary arteries. Sensors on the catheter measure the blood flow and pressure in the coronary arteries, providing valuable information about the presence and severity of any blockages. The results of this test help cardiologists diagnose and treat conditions such as coronary artery disease, which can lead to heart attacks and other cardiac problems.
