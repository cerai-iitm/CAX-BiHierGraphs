Excision of Abdomen Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision through the skin and subcutaneous tissue of the abdomen to access the underlying soft tissue. The skin of the abdomen is then dissected free from the underlying fascia, and the affected skin is removed. The wound is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, to promote healing and minimize scarring. The goal of the procedure is to remove damaged or diseased skin, improve the appearance of the abdomen, and prevent potential complications.
