Resection of Right Thorax Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to the right thorax, where the muscle is resected, or surgically removed, to treat various conditions such as tumors, injuries, or congenital defects. The procedure typically begins with an incision in the chest wall, followed by the dissection of the surrounding tissues to expose the affected muscle. The surgeon then carefully removes the damaged or diseased muscle tissue, taking care to preserve surrounding structures and minimize bleeding. After the resection, the incision is closed in layers, and the patient is monitored for any postoperative complications.
