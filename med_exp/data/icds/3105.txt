Repair Stomach, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the stomach, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect and manually repair any defects, injuries, or pathologies affecting the stomach. The open approach provides direct visualization and tactile feedback, enabling the surgeon to carefully repair tears, remove damaged tissue, or reconnect severed blood vessels. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as gastric perforations, ulcers, or bleeding lesions, and may involve resecting and reanastomosing portions of the stomach to restore its normal anatomical and physiological function. The goal of the procedure is to restore the stomach's integrity, alleviate symptoms, and promote healing and recovery.
