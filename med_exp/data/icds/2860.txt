Excision of Cecum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the cecum, a pouch-like structure at the beginning of the large intestine, and carefully removing it. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly and perform the excision with precision. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as cancer, diverticulitis, or intestinal obstruction that affect the cecum. The surgery typically requires a hospital stay and a period of recovery to allow the body to heal and the digestive system to adjust.
