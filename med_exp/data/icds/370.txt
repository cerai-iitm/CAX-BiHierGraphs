Dilation of Coronary Artery, Three Arteries, Bifurcation, with Two Drug-eluting Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves widening of three coronary arteries, including a bifurcation site, using a percutaneous approach. Two drug-eluting intraluminal devices are implanted to restore blood flow and reduce the risk of re-narrowing. The procedure is performed through a small incision in the arm or leg, and a catheter is guided through the blood vessels to the blocked coronary arteries. The drug-eluting stents are then expanded to reopen the arteries, and the catheter is removed, allowing the patient to recover.
