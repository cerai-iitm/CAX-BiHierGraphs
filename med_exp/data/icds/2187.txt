Excision of Left Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the left main bronchus, a major airway that connects the trachea to the left lung, through a natural or artificial opening. Under endoscopic guidance, a surgical instrument is then used to excise, or surgically remove, the left main bronchus. This complex procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, bronchial stenosis, or other abnormalities that affect the left main bronchus. The use of endoscopy allows for minimally invasive access and visualization of the airway, enabling precise dissection and excision of the affected bronchus.
