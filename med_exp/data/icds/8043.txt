Fluoroscopy of Other Upper Arteries using Other Contrast
This fluoroscopically guided procedure employs specialized contrast agents to visualize the anatomy of upper arteries beyond the aorta, examining branch vessels and their tributaries. The contrast material is injected through a catheter, allowing for real-time imaging and precise identification of potential blockages, stenoses, or aneurysms. The imaging technique enables clinicians to diagnose and treat vascular disorders, such as arteriosclerosis, in these often-hard-to-reach areas. By providing high-resolution, dynamic images, fluoroscopy facilitates accurate diagnosis and guides interventional procedures, ensuring optimal patient care.
