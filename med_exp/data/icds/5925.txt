Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of a lumbar vertebral joint using an autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft taken from the patient's own body. The posterior approach is used, where the incision is made in the back, and the procedure is performed through an open approach, meaning a larger incision is made to access the spine. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the spine and relieve symptoms such as pain, numbness, and tingling in the lower back and legs. The autologous tissue substitute is used to promote bone growth and heal the affected joint, restoring proper spinal alignment and function.
