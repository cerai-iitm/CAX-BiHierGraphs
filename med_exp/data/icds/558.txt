Replacement of Aortic Valve with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the aortic valve with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, which requires a sternotomy or thoracotomy incision to access the heart. The diseased or damaged aortic valve is removed, and the synthetic substitute is sewn into place, ensuring proper blood flow from the heart to the aorta. The synthetic valve is designed to mimic the function of a natural valve, allowing for efficient blood flow and reducing the risk of complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the valve and surrounding tissues, enabling precise placement and securing of the synthetic valve.
