Supplement Right Neck Muscle with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the right neck muscle with autologous tissue substitute using an open approach. During the procedure, an incision is made in the right neck area to access the target muscle. The autologous tissue substitute, typically harvested from the patient's own body, is then implanted to reinforce or augment the muscle. The goal of this procedure is to restore function, alleviate pain, or improve aesthetics in the affected neck region.
