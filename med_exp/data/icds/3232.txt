Supplement Jejunum with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the jejunum, a section of the small intestine, with an autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the jejunum, allowing the surgeon to effectively supplement the area with the tissue substitute. This procedure is typically performed to repair or reconstruct damaged or diseased sections of the jejunum, restoring normal digestive function and alleviating associated symptoms. The autologous tissue substitute is used to provide additional support and structure to the jejunum, promoting a successful outcome and reducing the risk of complications.
