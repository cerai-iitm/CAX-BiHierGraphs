Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the lumbosacral joint, located at the lower region of the spine, using an autologous tissue substitute, which is harvested from the patient's own body. The procedure is performed through an anterior approach, meaning the surgeon accesses the joint from the front of the body, and involves an open approach, requiring a surgical incision. The anterior column of the spine is stabilized during the procedure to ensure proper alignment and support. The goal of the surgery is to alleviate pain and instability in the lower back caused by conditions such as degenerative disc disease or spondylolisthesis.
