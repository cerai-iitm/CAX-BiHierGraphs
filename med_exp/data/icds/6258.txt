Dilation of Right Kidney Pelvis with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the skin to access the right kidney through a percutaneous approach. A specialized instrument is then inserted through the incision to dilate the renal pelvis, which is the funnel-shaped structure that collects urine from the kidney and channels it into the ureter. The dilation is achieved using an intraluminal device, which helps to relieve any obstruction or narrowing in the renal pelvis. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as kidney stones, hydronephrosis, or other blockages that can cause pain, infection, or impaired kidney function.
