Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Axillary Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device, typically a central venous catheter, into the left axillary vein through a percutaneous approach. The axillary vein is a blood vessel located in the armpit region, and the percutaneous approach means the insertion is done through the skin and underlying tissues without open surgery. The infusion device is advanced through the vein under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement. The device is then secured in place, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or other treatments directly into the vein.
