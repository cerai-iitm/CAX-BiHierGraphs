Excision of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of the left lower lung lobe through a minimally invasive approach, utilizing an endoscope inserted through the skin to guide the surgical instruments. The percutaneous endoscopic method allows for less tissue trauma and reduced recovery time compared to traditional open surgery. During the procedure, the surgeon makes small incisions in the chest wall and inserts the endoscope, which provides a clear view of the affected area, allowing for precise dissection and removal of the diseased lung tissue. The removal of the left lower lung lobe is typically performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, emphysema, or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
