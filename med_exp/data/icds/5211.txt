Excision of Left Femoral Shaft, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the left femoral shaft, which is the long bone in the upper leg. The damaged or diseased portion of the femoral shaft is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate pain and restore function to the affected limb. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort and ensure optimal results. Following the excision, the incision is closed, and the leg is immobilized to facilitate healing and prevent further injury.
