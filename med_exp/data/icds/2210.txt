Excision of Right Middle Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the right middle lung lobe. Under image guidance, the affected lobe is carefully excised to remove diseased or damaged tissue. The endoscopic approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and potentially less post-operative pain and scarring. The diagnostic excision also enables the collection of tissue samples for further examination and diagnosis.
