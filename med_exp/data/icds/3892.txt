Transfer Perineum Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves relocating skin from the perineum, the area between the anus and genitals, to another part of the body. The perineum skin is carefully excised and transferred to a recipient site, where it is grafted into place to replace damaged or missing skin. The external approach allows the surgeon to access the perineum skin through a small incision, minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues. The goal of this procedure is to restore skin integrity and promote healing in the affected area.
