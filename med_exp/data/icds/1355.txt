Restriction of Hepatic Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an intraluminal device into the hepatic artery to restrict blood flow, which can be used to treat various liver conditions, including cancer and liver lesions. The device is introduced through a small incision in the skin and guided to the hepatic artery using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. Once in place, the device reduces blood flow to the targeted area, which can help to shrink tumors or slow disease progression. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can be completed in under an hour.
