Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Upper Extremity Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This imaging procedure involves the use of a low osmolar contrast agent to visualize the bilateral upper extremity arteries, providing a detailed evaluation of blood flow and vessel anatomy. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the contrast material is injected into the arteries, allowing for real-time visualization of the arterial system. The resulting images help diagnose and monitor conditions such as arterial stenosis, occlusion, or aneurysms, and guide interventions or surgical planning. The use of low osmolar contrast minimizes the risk of adverse reactions and provides optimal image quality.
