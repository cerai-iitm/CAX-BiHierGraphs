Excision of Lower Vein, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a lower vein through a small incision in the skin, using an endoscope to visualize the vein and guide the excision. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, allowing the patient to remain comfortable throughout. The endoscope is inserted through the incision, and specialized instruments are used to carefully dissect and remove the targeted section of the vein. The procedure is often used to treat varicose veins, spider veins, and other venous disorders, and can help alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and skin discoloration.
