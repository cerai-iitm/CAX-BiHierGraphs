Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Products of Conception, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the administration of a therapeutic substance into the products of conception, which can include a fetus or embryo, through a natural or artificial opening. The substance is introduced to achieve a specific therapeutic goal, such as treating a congenital anomaly or providing essential nutrients. The introduction of the substance can be performed through various methods, including injection or infusion, and may be performed under ultrasound guidance to ensure accurate placement. The goal of this procedure is to promote the healthy development of the fetus or embryo and alleviate any potential complications.
