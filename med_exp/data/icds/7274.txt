Detachment at Right Foot, Partial 4th Ray, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the partial 4th ray of the right foot is detached through an open approach, which involves a single incision made in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected area. The detached portion may include the 4th toe, or a portion of the 4th metatarsal bone, depending on the extent of the detachment. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures involved, enabling the surgeon to precisely detach the affected ray while minimizing damage to surrounding tissues. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate pain or discomfort in the right foot.
