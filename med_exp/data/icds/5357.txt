Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Patella, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to remove an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screws, from the right patella, also known as the kneecap. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the device. The surgeon carefully dissects the surrounding tissue to expose the fixation device and gently removes it to avoid damaging the surrounding bone or soft tissue. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
