Insertion of Neurostimulator Generator into Skull, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the skull, where a neurostimulator generator is implanted to deliver electrical signals to specific areas of the brain or nervous system. The generator is typically connected to one or more electrodes that have been previously implanted, and is programmed to deliver specific patterns and intensities of stimulation. The implantation procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia, and may involve image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or computed tomography, to ensure accurate placement of the generator. After the generator is secured in place, the incision is closed, and the device is programmed to begin delivering therapy.
