Excision of Left Pleura, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a sample of the left pleura, the thin layer of tissue that surrounds the lungs, through a small incision in the skin. Using a percutaneous approach, a needle or similar instrument is inserted through the skin and into the pleural space to collect a tissue sample. The sample is then sent to a laboratory for examination to aid in the diagnosis of conditions such as pleurisy or mesothelioma. This minimally invasive diagnostic procedure helps to identify the cause of symptoms such as chest pain, coughing, or difficulty breathing.
