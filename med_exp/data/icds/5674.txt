Fusion of 2 or more Cervical Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two or more cervical vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device, which is inserted through an anterior approach, meaning the incision is made in the front of the neck. The device is placed in the anterior column, providing stability and support to the affected area. The open approach indicates a larger incision is made, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the spine and accurately place the fusion device. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure on nerves and spinal cord, alleviate pain and stiffness, and promote long-term spinal stability.
