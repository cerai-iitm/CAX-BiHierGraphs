Fluoroscopy of Right Kidney, Ureter and Bladder using Other Contrast
This procedure utilizes fluoroscopic guidance to produce real-time X-ray images of the right kidney, ureter, and bladder, providing detailed anatomical visualization. A contrasting agent, introduced through an injection or other means, enhances the visibility of these urinary tract structures, facilitating diagnostic assessment. The fluoroscope's continuous X-ray beam helps track the contrast medium as it flows through the urinary system, enabling the detection of potential blockages, tumors, or other abnormalities. This imaging technique is particularly useful for evaluating conditions such as kidney stones, ureteropelvic junction obstruction, or bladder tumors.
