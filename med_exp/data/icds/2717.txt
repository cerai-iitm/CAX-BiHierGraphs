Division of Esophagogastric Junction, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous endoscopic division of the esophagogastric junction, a region where the esophagus and stomach meet. During the procedure, an endoscope is inserted through the mouth and guided to the esophagogastric junction, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the area. A specialized instrument is then used to divide the tissue at the junction, which can help to relieve symptoms associated with conditions such as achalasia or diffuse esophageal spasm. The procedure is typically performed under sedation and can result in faster recovery times and fewer complications compared to traditional open surgical approaches.
