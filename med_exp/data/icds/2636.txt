Bypass Descending Colon to Cutaneous, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass between the descending colon and the skin, rerouting the flow of intestinal contents to an artificial opening on the abdomen. The open approach necessitates a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and connect the colon to the skin. The bypass allows waste to exit the body through the artificial opening, diverting it away from the diseased or damaged section of the colon. This intervention is often performed to treat complex conditions such as colon cancer, diverticulitis, or traumatic injury, and requires a hospital stay with careful post-operative care.
