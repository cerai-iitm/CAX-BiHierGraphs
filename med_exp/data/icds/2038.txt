Destruction of Right Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the right main bronchus, and then applying energy or destroying tissue to eradicate a portion of the bronchus. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for precise destruction of the targeted area while minimizing damage to surrounding tissue. The endoscope provides real-time visualization, enabling the physician to accurately identify and destroy the affected area of the right main bronchus. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as lung tumors, bronchial stenosis, or excessive bleeding.
