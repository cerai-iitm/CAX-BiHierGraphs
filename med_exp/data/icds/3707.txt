Excision of Right Breast, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right breast through an external approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision on the skin to access the breast tissue. The goal of the operation is to excise the entire breast, which may be necessary to treat cancer, precancerous conditions, or other breast-related disorders. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the breast tissue and surrounding structures, enabling a more precise excision of the affected area. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may be performed in conjunction with other surgical interventions, such as lymph node dissection or reconstruction.
