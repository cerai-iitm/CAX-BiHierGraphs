Dilation of Right Common Carotid Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This medical procedure involves the use of a percutaneous approach to dilate the right common carotid artery, which is a major blood vessel in the neck that supplies oxygenated blood to the brain. The goal of this procedure is to widen the artery and improve blood flow, which can help to alleviate symptoms such as dizziness, blurred vision, and weakness in the face or extremities. During the procedure, a small incision is made in the skin, and a catheter is inserted through the incision and guided to the narrowed or blocked area of the artery. The dilation is then performed using a balloon or other specialized device to widen the artery and restore normal blood flow.
