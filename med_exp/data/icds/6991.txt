Extirpation of Matter from Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove abnormal or infected material from the peritoneal cavity, a fluid-filled space in the abdominal area. Using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, the physician accesses the peritoneal cavity through a small incision in the skin. Under guided visualization, the problematic material is carefully extracted using specialized instruments, minimizing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. This percutaneous endoscopic approach offers a less traumatic alternative to traditional open surgery, reducing post-operative pain and scarring.
