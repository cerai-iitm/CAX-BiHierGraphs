Insertion of Feeding Device into Jejunum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open insertion of a feeding device, such as a jejunal tube, directly into the jejunum, which is the middle section of the small intestine. The jejunum is accessed through an incision in the abdomen, allowing the physician to visualize the area and accurately place the feeding device. Once in place, the device enables nutrition to be delivered directly into the jejunum, bypassing the stomach and upper small intestine. This type of feeding is often necessary for patients who have difficulty ingesting or digesting food orally due to various medical conditions.
