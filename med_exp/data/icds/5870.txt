Drainage of Left Ankle Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the left ankle joint to allow for the drainage of accumulated fluid, blood, or other debris that may be causing pain, swelling, or infection. The open approach involves a direct visualization of the joint through the incision, allowing the surgeon to directly access and remove the unwanted substances. This procedure is often necessary to alleviate symptoms and promote healing in the affected joint. The surgical site is typically closed with sutures or staples after the drainage is complete.
