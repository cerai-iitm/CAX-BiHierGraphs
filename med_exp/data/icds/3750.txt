Extraction of Toe Nail, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically removing a toenail through an external approach, typically performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The surgeon makes a small incision in the skin around the nail bed to access the nail plate, and then carefully extracts the entire nail or a portion of it. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain and discomfort caused by an ingrown toenail, fungal infection, or other conditions affecting the nail. Afterwards, the wound is cleaned and dressed to promote healing and prevent infection.
