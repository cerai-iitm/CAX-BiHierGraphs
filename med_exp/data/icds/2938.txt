Extirpation of Matter from Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter or foreign objects from the esophagus through a natural or artificial opening. Using a flexible tube with a camera and light, the healthcare provider visually examines the esophagus to locate the obstructing material. Specialized instruments are then inserted through the endoscope to carefully extract the foreign object or debris, restoring normal swallowing function. The entire process is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and minimize discomfort.
