Drainage of Right Metacarpophalangeal Joint, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue over the right metacarpophalangeal joint to access the joint space. The joint is then drained of any accumulated fluid, blood, or debris that may be causing pain, swelling, or limited mobility. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the operation. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain and restore normal function to the affected joint.
