Transplantation of Pancreas, Allogeneic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves transplanting a donor pancreas into a patient through an open approach, where a large incision is made in the abdomen to access the transplant site. The allogeneic pancreas, obtained from a genetically non-identical donor, is carefully connected to the patient's blood vessels and digestive tract to restore normal pancreatic function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the internal organs and ensure proper placement and connection of the transplanted pancreas. The goal of this complex procedure is to improve blood sugar control and alleviate symptoms associated with pancreatic insufficiency or diabetes.
