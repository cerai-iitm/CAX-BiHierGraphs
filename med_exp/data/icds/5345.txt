Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Femoral Shaft, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate, rod, or screws, that was previously implanted in the right femoral shaft to stabilize a fracture or other bone abnormality. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the device, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and carefully disassemble the hardware. The removal process typically involves loosening or removing the screws or plates that hold the device in place, and then extracting the device from the bone. The procedure is usually performed under general or regional anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and relaxation.
