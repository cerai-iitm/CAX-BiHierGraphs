Replacement of Occipital Bone with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made to access the occipital bone, located at the base of the skull, which is then removed and replaced with a synthetic substitute. The synthetic material is carefully shaped and fitted to match the natural contours of the bone, ensuring a seamless integration with the surrounding tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the substitute and restore the structural integrity of the skull. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the neck and head, and to improve overall cranial stability.
