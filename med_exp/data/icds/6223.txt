Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Right Hip Joint, External Approach
The procedure involves revising a previously implanted synthetic substitute in the right hip joint through an external approach, which allows the surgeon to access the joint from outside the body. This revision surgery is typically performed to correct problems with the original implant, such as wear and tear, loosening, or infection. The synthetic substitute is removed and replaced with a new one, which is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the hip joint. The external approach allows the surgeon to visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, ensuring a precise and effective revision procedure.
