Traction of Left Lower Leg using Traction Apparatus
This surgical procedure involves the application of a controlled force to the left lower leg using a traction apparatus, with the goal of restoring normal anatomical alignment or relieving pressure on the affected area. The traction apparatus is carefully set up and calibrated to provide a precise amount of tension, which is then gradually increased or decreased as needed to achieve the desired therapeutic effect. During the procedure, the patient's left lower leg is carefully positioned and secured to ensure proper alignment and minimal discomfort. The controlled traction force is maintained for a specific period of time, allowing for effective treatment of musculoskeletal or orthopedic conditions affecting the left lower leg.
