Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Left Hip Joint, Femoral Surface, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically removing a synthetic substitute, such as a artificial joint or implant, from the femoral surface of the left hip joint through an open approach. The open approach requires a larger incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and remove the synthetic substitute. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate pain or discomfort caused by the presence of the synthetic substitute. The removal of the synthetic substitute can help to improve mobility and reduce the risk of further complications or infection in the hip joint.
