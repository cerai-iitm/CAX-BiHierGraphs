Drainage of Right External Ear, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin to remove accumulated fluid, pus, or debris from the right external ear canal. The percutaneous approach allows for a quick and relatively painless intervention, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster healing. The drainage tube is carefully placed to target the affected area, and the obstructing material is gently suctioned or drained out, restoring the ear's natural function and alleviating discomfort or pain. Following the procedure, the ear is cleaned and dressed to prevent infection and promote a smooth recovery.
