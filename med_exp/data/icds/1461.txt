Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Brachial Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into the left brachial vein, allowing for the administration of medications or fluids directly into the bloodstream. The procedure involves a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the vein. Under imaging guidance, a thin catheter is advanced through the incision and guided into the left brachial vein, where the infusion device is then inserted. The device is secured in place, and the incision site is closed, enabling safe and effective delivery of treatments.
