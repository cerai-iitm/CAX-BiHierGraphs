Monitoring of Peripheral Nervous Electrical Activity, Intraoperative, External Approach
During surgery, a specialized monitor is used to track the electrical activity of peripheral nerves in real-time, helping surgeons avoid damaging these critical structures. This technology allows for precise monitoring of nerve function, enabling surgeons to adjust their technique accordingly and minimize potential complications. The external approach refers to the non-invasive placement of sensors on the skin, typically on the arms or legs, to capture the electrical signals transmitted by the nerves. By providing real-time feedback, this monitoring ensures the highest level of care and safety for patients undergoing surgical procedures.
