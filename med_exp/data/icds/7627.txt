Introduction of Other Thrombolytic into Central Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a thrombolytic agent through the skin and into a central vein to dissolve blood clots. The percutaneous approach allows for a quick and efficient introduction of the medication, which works to break down the clot and restore blood flow. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the thrombolytic agent. Once administered, the medication works to rapidly dissolve the clot, helping to alleviate symptoms and restore normal circulation.
