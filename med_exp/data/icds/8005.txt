Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Common Carotid Arteries
Here is the description:

Fluoroscopy is used to guide real-time imaging of the bilateral common carotid arteries, which are the two main arteries that supply oxygenated blood to the head and neck. This diagnostic procedure involves the use of continuous X-ray beams to produce images of the arteries, allowing healthcare providers to visualize the flow of blood and identify any potential blockages or abnormalities. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete. The resulting images provide valuable information for diagnosing and treating conditions such as carotid artery stenosis, atherosclerosis, and other vascular disorders.
