Bypass Portal Vein to Lower Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves creating a bypass between the portal vein and a lower vein using an intraluminal device to redirect blood flow. The minimally invasive approach uses imaging guidance to access the targeted veins through the skin, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The intraluminal device is advanced through the access site and positioned to establish a stable connection between the portal vein and the lower vein, allowing blood to flow freely. By restoring venous circulation, this procedure aims to alleviate symptoms associated with portal hypertension and improve overall liver function.
