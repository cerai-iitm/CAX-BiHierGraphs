Extraction of Sternum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the sternum, a long, flat bone located in the center of the chest. The sternum is then carefully removed, either partially or completely, depending on the individual's specific medical needs. The open approach allows surgeons to directly visualize the affected area, ensuring a more precise and effective extraction. During the procedure, the surgical team takes great care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues and ensure proper closure of the incision to promote optimal healing.
