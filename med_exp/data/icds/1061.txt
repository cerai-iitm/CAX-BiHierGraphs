Dilation of Left External Iliac Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through the skin to reach the left external iliac artery, where a balloon is inflated to widen the artery and improve blood flow. A drug-eluting intraluminal device is then placed inside the artery to prevent the growth of new blockages and maintain vessel patency. The device releases medication over time to reduce the risk of restenosis, or the recurrence of blockages. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery and less trauma to the surrounding tissue compared to open surgery.
