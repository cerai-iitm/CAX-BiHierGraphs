Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Radial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small infusion device into the radial artery on the left side of the wrist through a percutaneous approach, allowing for efficient and targeted delivery of medications or fluids directly into the arterial bloodstream. The procedure typically begins with local anesthesia and ultrasound guidance to ensure accurate placement of the device. Once in place, the infusion device enables healthcare providers to administer medications, contrast agents, or other therapeutics as needed, often for prolonged periods or in high-volume situations. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgical methods, promoting a faster recovery and minimizing scarring.
