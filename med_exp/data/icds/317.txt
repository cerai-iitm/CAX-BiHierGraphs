Bypass Coronary Artery, Four or More Arteries from Aorta with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This cardiac surgical procedure involves creating a detour around blocked coronary arteries by grafting four or more autologous venous tissue conduits from the aorta. The open approach requires a median sternotomy, allowing the surgeon to access the heart and aorta directly. The venous tissue, typically harvested from the patient's leg, is grafted to the coronary arteries, restoring blood flow to the heart muscle and alleviating symptoms of coronary artery disease. The goal of this complex and invasive procedure is to improve cardiac function, reduce symptoms, and enhance overall quality of life.
