Fluoroscopy of Left External Carotid Artery using Other Contrast
During this imaging procedure, a specialized machine emits continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time, moving images of the left external carotid artery. A contrast agent, other than standard iodine or barium, is injected into the patient's bloodstream to enhance visibility of the artery and its blood vessels. The fluoroscope's high-intensity X-rays capture the contrasting agent's trajectory, allowing the medical team to assess blood flow, vessel patency, and identify potential blockages or abnormalities. This diagnostic procedure provides valuable insights into the carotid artery's function and helps guide further treatment decisions.
