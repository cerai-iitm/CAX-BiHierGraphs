Excision of Left Ureter, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the left ureter through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the urinary tract. The endoscope is inserted through the urethra or a small incision, allowing the surgeon to access the ureter and perform the excision. The diagnostic aspect of the procedure enables the surgeon to examine the ureter and surrounding tissues to identify any abnormalities or diseases. The entire procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure the patient's comfort and safety.
