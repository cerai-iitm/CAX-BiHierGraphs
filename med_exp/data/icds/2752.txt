Drainage of Appendix with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous drainage of the appendix, which means a small tube is inserted through the skin to drain infected or inflamed material from the appendix. Guided by imaging studies, the healthcare provider uses a drainage device to remove the unwanted fluid or material, helping to relieve symptoms and prevent further complications. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of scarring and promoting faster recovery. The goal of this treatment is to alleviate the severity of appendicitis and potentially avoid the need for surgical intervention.
