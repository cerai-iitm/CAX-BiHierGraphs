Ultrasonography of Abdomen
This non-invasive medical imaging exam uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the abdominal organs and structures, including the liver, gallbladder, pancreas, kidneys, and spleen. The procedure involves a handheld probe called a transducer being placed on the abdomen, which transmits the sound waves and records the echoes as they bounce off the internal structures. The resulting images are then used to diagnose a range of abdominal conditions, including gallstones, liver disease, and kidney stones. The exam is typically painless and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete, depending on the complexity of the case.
