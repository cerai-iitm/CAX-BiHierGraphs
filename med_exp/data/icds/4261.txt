Repair Left Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
Here is the description:

Surgical repair of the left foot's subcutaneous tissue and fascia is performed through an open approach, which involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected area. The procedure is typically done to treat conditions such as traumatic injuries, infections, or chronic wounds that have damaged the soft tissue. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the tissue, removes any damaged or infected tissue, and repairs or replaces it with healthy tissue. The goal of the procedure is to restore the normal anatomy and function of the left foot, alleviate pain and discomfort, and promote healing.
