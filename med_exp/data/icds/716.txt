Dilation of Left External Carotid Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the left external carotid artery, which is then dilated to improve blood flow and relieve any blockages or stenosis. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the artery, enabling the surgeon to accurately target the area of narrowing and perform the necessary dilation. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow to the brain and surrounding tissues, reducing the risk of stroke and other complications. Throughout the procedure, the surgeon takes great care to avoid damaging surrounding structures and ensure a safe and effective outcome.
