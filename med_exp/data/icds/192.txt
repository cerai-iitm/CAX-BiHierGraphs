Supplement Dura Mater with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves supplementing the dura mater, a protective membrane covering the brain and spinal cord, with a substitute derived from the patient's own tissue. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the substitute material to be precisely placed to repair or reinforce the damaged dura mater. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection or adverse reaction. The goal of this procedure is to restore the integrity of the dura mater, alleviating symptoms and promoting optimal neural function.
