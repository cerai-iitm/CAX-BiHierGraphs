Excision of Left Axillary Lymphatic, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left axillary region to access the lymphatic tissue, which is then carefully excised to obtain a tissue sample for diagnostic examination. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the lymphatic tissue, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the affected tissue. The excised tissue is sent to a laboratory for further analysis to determine the presence of any abnormalities or diseases. The diagnostic excision of the left axillary lymphatic tissue helps clinicians establish a definitive diagnosis and develop an effective treatment plan.
