Drainage of Left Hip Muscle, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a needle or drainage catheter through the skin and into the affected left hip muscle to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances. The percutaneous approach allows for a less invasive and potentially less painful treatment option compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms, reduce swelling, and promote healing in the affected hip muscle.
