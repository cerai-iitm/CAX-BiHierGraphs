Removal of Other Device from Heart, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the chest to access the heart and carefully removing an implanted device, such as a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator, that is no longer needed or is malfunctioning. The surgeon makes an incision in the chest wall and separates the breastbone to expose the heart, allowing for direct visualization and removal of the device. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the breastbone is reinstated to its original position. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety.
