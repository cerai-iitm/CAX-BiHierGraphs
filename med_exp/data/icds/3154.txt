Reposition Ileum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the small intestine, specifically the ileum, which is the lower part of the small intestine. The ileum is then repositioned to its normal anatomical position, correcting any displacement or malrotation. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the abdominal cavity and precisely reposition the ileum, ensuring proper blood flow and function. Following repositioning, the incision is closed, and the patient typically requires postoperative care to ensure a smooth recovery.
