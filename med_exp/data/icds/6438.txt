Repair Bladder, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves restoring the bladder to its normal function by repairing any defects or injuries through a natural or artificial opening. The surgeon makes an incision through the urethra, vagina, or abdomen to access the bladder and performs the necessary repairs. The goal of the procedure is to restore bladder function, relieve symptoms such as incontinence or urinary retention, and improve overall quality of life. The surgeon may use stitches, grafts, or other materials to close any holes or tears in the bladder wall, and may also repair or reconstruct surrounding tissues as needed.
