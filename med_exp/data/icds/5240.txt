Excision of Right Metatarsal, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical incision to access the right metatarsal bone, a long bone in the foot that connects the toes to the rest of the foot. The surgeon makes an open incision in the skin and soft tissue to expose the affected metatarsal bone, allowing for its removal. The excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as bone fractures, infections, or tumors that affect the metatarsal bone. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, restore function, and improve mobility in the affected foot.
