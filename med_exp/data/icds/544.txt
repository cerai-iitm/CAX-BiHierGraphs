Repair Mitral Valve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the chest to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the mitral valve directly. The valve is typically approached through the left atrium, and the surgeon may repair or replace the valve leaflets, chords, or annulus to restore proper blood flow. The goal of the procedure is to normalize mitral valve function, alleviate symptoms, and improve cardiac hemodynamics. The open approach allows for precise visualization and manipulation of the valve, enabling the surgeon to address complex valve pathologies.
