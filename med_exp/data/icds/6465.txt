Supplement Right Kidney Pelvis with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the use of autologous tissue substitute to supplement the right kidney pelvis through an open approach. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the kidney pelvis, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the autologous tissue substitute. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection and promoting a faster healing process. The goal of this procedure is to improve kidney function and alleviate symptoms associated with kidney pelvis abnormalities.
