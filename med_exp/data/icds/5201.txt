Excision of Right Upper Femur, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the right upper femur, also known as the proximal femur, which is the upper end of the thigh bone. The goal of the operation is to remove a portion of the femur, which may be necessary due to a tumor, infection, or severe fracture that cannot be treated with other methods. The surgical team will carefully dissect the tissue to expose the affected area of the femur, and then use specialized instruments to excise the damaged or diseased segment of bone. The procedure typically requires a hospital stay and a period of rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and regain function of the affected leg.
