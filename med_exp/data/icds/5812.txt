Reposition Left Wrist Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves manually repositioning the left wrist joint to its normal anatomical position, using an open approach that requires a surgical incision. The repositioning is achieved by realigning the bones and soft tissues of the wrist joint, ensuring proper alignment and stability. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are then used to hold the joint in place, providing additional stability and support while the joint heals. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and mobility to the left wrist joint, alleviating pain and discomfort associated with the initial misalignment.
