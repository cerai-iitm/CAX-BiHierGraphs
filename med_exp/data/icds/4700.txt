Repair Left Knee Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left knee to access the damaged tendon, which is then repaired using sutures or other fixation devices to restore its normal function and anatomy. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the extent of the damage. The repair is typically performed under general or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote optimal outcomes. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility, and to enable the patient to return to normal activities and regain full knee function.
