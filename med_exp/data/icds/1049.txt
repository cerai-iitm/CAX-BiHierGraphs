Dilation of Right External Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Open Approach
A surgical intervention is performed to dilate the right external iliac artery, a crucial blood vessel responsible for supplying oxygenated blood to the lower extremities. Through an open approach, a surgical incision is made to access the affected artery, enabling the insertion of an intraluminal device to widen the constricted vessel. This procedure aims to improve blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with peripheral artery disease, such as claudication, pain, and weakness. By restoring normal blood flow, this intervention can significantly enhance the patient's quality of life and reduce the risk of severe complications.
