Replacement of Right Hand Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Partial Thickness, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a portion of the skin on the right hand with an autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft derived from the patient's own tissue. The substitution is partial thickness, meaning it only affects the outermost layers of skin. The external approach indicates that the surgical incision is made on the outer surface of the hand, allowing the surgeon to access the affected area directly. The goal of this procedure is to restore skin integrity and functionality to the affected area, promoting wound healing and improving overall hand function.
