Repair Abdominal Wall, Stoma, External Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the affected area, where the abdominal wall and stoma are carefully repaired to restore anatomical integrity and functionality. The external approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the tissues, enabling the surgeon to meticulously correct any defects or damage. The repair process typically involves the use of sutures, staples, or mesh materials to reinforce the abdominal wall and stoma, ensuring a secure and durable reconstruction. Post-operative care involves close monitoring and management to prevent complications and promote optimal healing.
