Excision of Uterus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves removing the uterus through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, allowing for visualization of the internal organs. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the endoscope is inserted through a small incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the uterus and surrounding tissues. The uterus is then carefully dissected and removed through the natural or artificial opening, minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues and reducing post-operative pain and scarring. The entire procedure is typically performed on an outpatient basis, allowing patients to recover quickly and return to normal activities soon after.
