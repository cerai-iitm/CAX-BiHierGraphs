Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Femoral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a small incision is made in the skin near the left femoral vein, and a thin catheter is inserted through the incision into the vein under fluoroscopic guidance. The infusion device, such as a port or pump, is then attached to the catheter, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or nutrients directly into the bloodstream. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing. Once in place, the device enables repeated access to the vein, eliminating the need for multiple needle sticks.
