Release Jejunum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the jejunum, a segment of the small intestine. The jejunum is then released from any adhesions, scar tissue, or other restrictive structures that may be impeding its normal functioning. This open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the intestine and surrounding tissues, ensuring a more precise and effective release. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for any post-operative complications.
