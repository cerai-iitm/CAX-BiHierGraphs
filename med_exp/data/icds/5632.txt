Excision of Right Elbow Joint, Open Approach
A surgical intervention is performed to remove the right elbow joint through an open approach, involving a skin incision to access the affected area. This procedure is typically indicated in cases of severe arthritis, bone fractures, or joint damage, where preservation of the joint is no longer possible. The surgeon will carefully dissect the surrounding tissue to expose the right elbow joint, and then excise the damaged or diseased joint, taking care to minimize trauma to surrounding structures. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, restore mobility, and improve overall function of the affected arm.
