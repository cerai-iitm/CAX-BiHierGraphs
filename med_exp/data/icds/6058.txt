Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left knee joint to access the internal fixation device, such as plates, screws, or rods, that were previously implanted to stabilize a fracture or injury. The surgeon will carefully dissect the soft tissue and expose the internal fixation device, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves and blood vessels. Once the device is exposed, the surgeon will remove the screws, plates, or rods, and carefully close the incision to promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to remove the internal fixation device once the underlying fracture or injury has fully healed, restoring normal knee function and relieving any discomfort or pain.
