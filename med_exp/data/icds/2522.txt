Excision of Larynx, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the larynx, also known as voice box, through an endoscopic approach. The surgeon uses an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through the mouth or a tracheostomy to visualize the larynx. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon uses specialized instruments to dissect and remove the larynx, which may be cancerous or severely damaged. The goal of this procedure is to treat laryngeal cancer or other conditions that affect the larynx, and may involve reconstruction of the throat and vocal cords.
