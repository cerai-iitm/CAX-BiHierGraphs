Excision of Stomach, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the stomach, and then carefully removing all or part of the stomach, depending on the severity of the disease or injury. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the abdominal cavity and closely inspect the stomach and surrounding tissues to ensure complete removal of the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of surgical instruments, such as scalpels and forceps, to dissect and remove the stomach tissue. After the excision, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is closely monitored for any post-operative complications.
