Excision of Right Ventricle, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Percutaneous excision of the right ventricle is a minimally invasive procedure that involves the removal of a sample tissue or lesion from the right ventricle through the skin. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance such as fluoroscopy or echocardiography to visualize the heart structures. The procedure involves making a small incision in the skin and inserting a catheter or other instrument through a blood vessel to access the right ventricle. The excised tissue is then sent for further examination to aid in the diagnosis of cardiac conditions, such as cardiac tumors, arrhythmias, or other abnormalities.
