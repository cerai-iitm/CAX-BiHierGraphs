Drainage of Right Kidney Pelvis, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the right kidney pelvis, allowing the physician to identify and drain any blockages or obstruction. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, providing access to the kidney pelvis without the need for open surgery. Once the blockage is located, a specialized instrument is used to drain the affected area, helping to relieve pressure and alleviate symptoms. The diagnostic aspect of the procedure enables the physician to assess the kidney pelvis and identify any underlying conditions that may be contributing to the blockage.
