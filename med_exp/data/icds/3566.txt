Excision of Right Adrenal Gland, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the right adrenal gland through a small incision using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope and specialized instruments are inserted through the skin, allowing the surgeon to visualize the adrenal gland and carefully dissect and remove it. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery. The excised adrenal gland is then sent to a laboratory for examination to confirm the diagnosis and determine the presence of any adrenal disorders.
