Excision of Mediastinum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the chest cavity to access the mediastinum, a region in the thoracic cavity that contains vital organs and structures such as the heart, trachea, and esophagus. The purpose of the excision is to collect tissue samples for diagnostic purposes, allowing healthcare professionals to examine the tissue for signs of disease or abnormality. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the mediastinal tissue to identify and remove the affected area, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding structures. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
