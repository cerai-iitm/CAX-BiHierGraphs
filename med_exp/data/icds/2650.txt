Destruction of Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to destroy or remove a diseased or damaged portion of the small intestine, typically performed to treat conditions such asintestinal obstruction, cancer, or infection. The procedure requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the small intestine, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the affected section. The remaining healthy portions of the intestine are then reconnected to restore normal digestive function. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the intestine, enabling the surgeon to successfully remove the damaged section and promote healing.
