Fluoroscopy of Right Lower Extremity Arteries using Other Contrast
This non-invasive imaging procedure uses X-ray technology and a contrast agent other than iodine to visualize the arteries in the right lower extremity. During the procedure, a small amount of contrast material is injected into the arteries, allowing the radiologist to capture real-time images of blood flow and vessel structure using fluoroscopy. The resulting images help diagnose and monitor conditions such as peripheral artery disease, blood clots, and aneurysms in the legs. By guiding the radiologist to identify areas of narrowing or blockage, this procedure informs treatment decisions and helps prevent complications.
