Excision of Right Hand Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissues of the right hand to access the affected tendon. The damaged or diseased tendon is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility. The tendon excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as tendonitis, tendinosis, or tendon rupture, which can occur due to injury, overuse, or degenerative disease. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the affected tendon and facilitate proper healing.
