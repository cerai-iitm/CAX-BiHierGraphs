Insertion of Monitoring Device into Pulmonary Trunk, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the description:

A minimally invasive procedure is performed to place a monitoring device directly into the pulmonary trunk, the main artery that carries deoxygenated blood from the heart to the lungs, via a percutaneous approach through the skin. This allows for continuous monitoring of blood flow, pressure, and oxygen saturation. The device is inserted through a small incision, typically in the groin area, and guided through blood vessels to the pulmonary trunk using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. The monitoring device provides valuable real-time data to healthcare providers, enabling them to make informed decisions about patient care and treatment.
