Ultrasonography of Right and Left Heart, Transesophageal
This non-invasive medical imaging test utilizes high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the heart's internal structures and blood vessels, providing real-time visualization of cardiac function and blood flow. Performed under mild sedation, a flexible tube with a probe at its tip is guided through the mouth and into the esophagus, allowing for unobstructed views of the heart's chambers, valves, and vessels. The test is particularly useful in diagnosing heart valve disease, cardiac tumors, and blood clots, and can also help guide minimally invasive heart procedures. By examining both the right and left sides of the heart, this comprehensive test provides a thorough understanding of cardiac anatomy and function.
