Insertion of Pacemaker, Single Chamber Rate Responsive into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where a single chamber rate-responsive pacemaker is carefully inserted to regulate the heartbeat. The pacemaker is designed to respond to the body's physical activity and adjust the heart rate accordingly. Once inserted, the pacemaker is secured in place and the incision is closed. This open approach allows for precise placement and secure fixation of the pacemaker, ensuring optimal performance and minimizing potential complications.
