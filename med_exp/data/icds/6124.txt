Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, the diseased or damaged left hip joint is replaced with a synthetic substitute that is not secured with cement. The uncemented device is designed to allow for bone growth onto the surface, providing a secure and stable fixation. The surgeon makes an incision in the left hip area to access the joint, carefully removes the damaged or arthritic joint, and implants the artificial joint, ensuring proper alignment and fit. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, restore function, and improve mobility in the left hip joint.
