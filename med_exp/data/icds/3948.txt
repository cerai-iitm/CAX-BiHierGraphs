Drainage of Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the removal of fluid or other substances from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the chest, typically performed using a needle or catheter inserted through the skin. The goal is to relieve pressure, reduce swelling, and promote healing by draining accumulated fluid, pus, or blood that may be causing discomfort, pain, or other complications. The treatment is often used to manage conditions such as abscesses, hematomas, or seromas in the chest area. Under local anesthesia and image guidance, the healthcare provider carefully inserts the drainage device into the affected area to remove the unwanted substance.
