Excision of Right Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the right main bronchus and remove a portion of it through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided to the bronchus, allowing the surgeon to examine the area and identify any abnormalities. The excision is typically performed using specialized instruments passed through the endoscope, minimizing trauma to the surrounding tissue. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may be followed by a diagnostic evaluation to determine the underlying cause of the bronchial condition.
