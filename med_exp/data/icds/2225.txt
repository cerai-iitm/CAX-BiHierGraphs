Excision of Left Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of the left upper lung lobe through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope allows the surgeon to visualize the lung tissue and accurately identify the area to be removed. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a small incision in the chest wall and inserts the endoscope, which guides the surgical instruments to the target area. The affected lung tissue is then carefully excised, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples.
