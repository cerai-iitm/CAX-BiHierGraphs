Drainage of Bladder with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain the bladder using a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the bladder. A drainage device is inserted through the incision to remove excess fluid and relieve pressure on the bladder. This procedure is often necessary to treat conditions such as urinary retention, bladder obstruction, or bladder trauma. The drainage device is typically left in place for a period of time to allow the bladder to heal and function properly.
