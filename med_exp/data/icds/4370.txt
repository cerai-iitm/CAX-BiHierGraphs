Drainage of Right Trunk Muscle, Percutaneous Approach
The percutaneous drainage procedure involves inserting a small tube or catheter through the skin to access the right trunk muscle. This minimally invasive approach allows for the removal of excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may have accumulated in the muscle, helping to alleviate pain and swelling. During the procedure, imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, is used to precisely locate the affected area and guide the drainage process. The entire process is typically performed under local anesthesia, ensuring minimal discomfort for the patient.
