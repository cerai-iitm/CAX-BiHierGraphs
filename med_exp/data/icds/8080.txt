Fluoroscopy of Abdominal Aorta using Other Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the abdominal aorta, which is the main blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the abdominal organs. A contrast agent, other than the standard iodinated contrast, is injected into the bloodstream to enhance the imaging of the aorta during the procedure. The fluoroscopy machine produces real-time X-ray images that guide the healthcare provider in examining the abdominal aorta for any abnormalities or blockages. The use of alternative contrast agents may be necessary for patients who have a sensitivity to iodine or require a specialized imaging protocol.
