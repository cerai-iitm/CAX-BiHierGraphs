Dilation of Right Vertebral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device into the right vertebral artery to dilate it, thereby improving blood flow to the brain and relieving symptoms of vertebral artery stenosis or occlusion. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the device. The intraluminal device is designed to expand the artery from within, reducing the blockage and restoring normal blood flow. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical procedures.
