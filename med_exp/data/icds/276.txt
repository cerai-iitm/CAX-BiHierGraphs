Transfer Median Nerve to Median Nerve, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical transfer of a portion of the median nerve to another segment of the same nerve, typically to repair damaged or injured nerve tissue. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually access the affected area, carefully dissecting and mobilizing the nerve to facilitate the transfer. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal nerve function, alleviating symptoms such as numbness, tingling, or weakness in the hand or arm. By reconnecting and reinnervating the affected area, this procedure can improve motor function and sensory perception.
