Drainage of Sigmoid Colon, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small tube through the skin and into the sigmoid colon to drain contents, helping to alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, bloating, and constipation. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. During the procedure, imaging guidance such as fluoroscopy or CT scan is used to precisely locate the drainage site and ensure safe insertion of the tube. The drained material is then sent to a laboratory for analysis to help diagnose underlying conditions affecting the sigmoid colon.
