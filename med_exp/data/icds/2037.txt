Destruction of Carina, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the endoscopic destruction of the carina, a landmark structure within the tracheobronchial tree, through a natural or artificial opening. The carina is a crucial area where the trachea divides into the right and left main bronchi, and its destruction is usually performed to manage conditions such as airway obstruction or bleeding. The endoscopic approach allows for minimally invasive access to the carina, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. During the procedure, a flexible or rigid endoscope is inserted through the natural or artificial opening, and specialized instruments are used to destroy the carina using various energy sources or mechanical debridement.
