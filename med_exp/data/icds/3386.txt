Excision of Liver, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting an endoscope, a thin tube with a camera and light, through the skin and into the liver to visualize the affected area. Under guided imagery, a specialized instrument is utilized to remove the targeted lesion or tissue from the liver. The entire process is performed through a small incision, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be used to diagnose and treat various liver conditions, including cancer.
