Plain Radiography of Bile Ducts using Other Contrast
This non-invasive diagnostic imaging test uses a type of contrast agent other than iodine or barium to visualize the bile ducts, which are tubes that carry bile from the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine. During the procedure, the contrast agent is administered into the bile ducts through an endoscope or a thin needle, allowing the radiologist to capture high-quality X-ray images of the ducts. The resulting images provide valuable information to diagnose conditions affecting the bile ducts, such as blockages, stones, or strictures. The test is often performed to investigate symptoms like abdominal pain, jaundice, or fever, and can help guide further treatment or surgical interventions.
