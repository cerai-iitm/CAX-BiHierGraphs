Repair Diaphragm, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure utilizes an endoscope to visualize the diaphragm, allowing the surgeon to repair defects or injuries through small incisions in the skin. A flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the incision, providing a clear view of the diaphragm. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to repair the diaphragm, typically using sutures or mesh to reinforce the area. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissues and minimizes post-operative pain and recovery time.
