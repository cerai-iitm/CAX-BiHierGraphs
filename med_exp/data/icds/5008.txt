Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Right Humeral Shaft, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a internal fixation device is implanted into the right humeral shaft to stabilize and align the bone. This involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the humerus, followed by the insertion of the device to promote healing and restore function. The fixation device may include plates, rods, or screws that are carefully positioned to hold the bone in place. The goal of the procedure is to treat fractures, deformities, or other conditions affecting the humerus, and to facilitate recovery and rehabilitation.
