Removal of Monitoring Device from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the removal of a monitoring device previously inserted into the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the trunk. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance to ensure precise localization of the device. The skin is cleansed and a small incision is made to access the device, which is then gently extracted through the same incision. Once the device is removed, the incision site is closed and dressed to promote healing.
