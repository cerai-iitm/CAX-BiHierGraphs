Extraction of Pancreas, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to extract tissue or cells from the pancreas for diagnostic purposes. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the pancreas and collect the necessary samples. The extracted tissue or cells are then sent to a laboratory for further examination, which helps in diagnosing conditions such as pancreatitis, pancreatic cancer, or other pancreatic disorders. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and allows for a faster recovery compared to traditional surgical methods.
