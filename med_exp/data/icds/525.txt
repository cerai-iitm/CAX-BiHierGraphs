Removal of Infusion Device from Heart, External Approach
This procedure involves the external removal of an infusion device from the heart, a medical device that is used to deliver medications or fluids directly into the heart. The device is typically implanted under the skin, and the removal process requires a small incision to access the device. During the procedure, the healthcare provider will carefully disconnect the device from the heart and remove it from the body. The removal of the infusion device is usually performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote a quick recovery.
