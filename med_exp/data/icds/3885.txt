Transfer Scalp Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves relocating skin from the scalp area to another part of the body to replace damaged or missing skin. The scalp skin is carefully excised and transferred to the desired location, where it is secured in place to promote healing and tissue integration. The goal of this procedure is to restore skin integrity and promote aesthetic appeal. The external approach allows surgeons to access the scalp skin directly, ensuring a precise and controlled transfer process.
