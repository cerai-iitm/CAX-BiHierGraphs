Removal of Infusion Device from Lower Vein, External Approach
This procedure involves the removal of an infusion device, such as an implantable port or a peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC), from a lower extremity vein, typically in the femoral or popliteal region. The removal process is performed through an external approach, which means the device is accessed through the skin, rather than through a surgical incision. The medical professional will carefully manipulate the device to free it from the surrounding tissue and vessel, taking care to avoid any damage to the surrounding blood vessels or nerves. Once the device is freed, it is carefully withdrawn from the body, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
