Inspection of Upper Intestinal Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visually examine the upper intestinal tract, which includes the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or a small incision, allowing the healthcare provider to directly view the internal structures and identify any abnormalities, such as ulcers, tumors, or bleeding. The procedure is typically performed under sedation and may involve the collection of tissue samples or removal of foreign objects. It is a minimally invasive and relatively low-risk diagnostic tool used to diagnose and manage various gastrointestinal conditions.
