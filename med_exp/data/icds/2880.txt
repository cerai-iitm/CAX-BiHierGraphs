Excision of Transverse Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of the transverse colon, a section of the large intestine, through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope. The endoscope is a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, which allows the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the excision. The transverse colon is located in the middle of the abdomen and plays a crucial role in absorbing water and electrolytes from digested food. The removal of the transverse colon is typically performed to treat conditions such as colon cancer, diverticulitis, or inflammatory bowel disease.
