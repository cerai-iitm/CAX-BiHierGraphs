Excision of Pancreas, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the pancreas through the skin, using imaging guidance, to remove a sample of tissue or cells for diagnostic purposes, often to confirm or rule out pancreatic cancer or other conditions. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and minimizing scarring. A small incision is made in the skin, and a thin needle or instrument is inserted through the incision to reach the pancreas, allowing for the targeted removal of tissue or cells. The collected sample is then sent to a laboratory for further analysis and diagnosis.
