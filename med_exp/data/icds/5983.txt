Fusion of Left Tarsometatarsal Joint with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the left tarsometatarsal joint, which connects the long bones of the foot to the arch, using a non-autologous tissue substitute. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to prepare the bone surfaces and insert the graft material. The non-autologous tissue substitute, such as a bone graft or synthetic material, is used to promote bone growth and fusion between the joint surfaces. The goal of the procedure is to stabilize the joint, relieve pain and inflammation, and restore normal function to the foot.
