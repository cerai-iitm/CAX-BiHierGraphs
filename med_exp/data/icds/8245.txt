Fluoroscopy of Kidneys, Ureters and Bladder
This fluoroscopic examination uses continuous low-dose X-ray beams to produce real-time images of the kidneys, ureters, and bladder, allowing for the dynamic visualization of the urinary tract. The procedure helps diagnose and treat conditions such as kidney stones, ureteral obstruction, and bladder dysfunction, as well as guide interventional procedures like ureteral stenting and nephrostomy tube placement. The fluoroscopy machine allows for precise adjustment of the X-ray beam and the examination table to ensure optimal imaging of the urinary tract structures. The resulting images provide valuable information for healthcare providers to develop an effective treatment plan for patients with urinary tract disorders.
