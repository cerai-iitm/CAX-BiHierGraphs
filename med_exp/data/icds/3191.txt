Resection of Cecum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
The cecum, a pouch-like structure at the beginning of the large intestine, is surgically removed through a minimally invasive procedure. Utilizing an endoscope, inserted through the skin, the surgeon visualizes the area and performs the resection without requiring a traditional open incision. This approach reduces post-operative discomfort, scarring, and promotes faster healing. The procedure is typically recommended for patients with conditions such as colon cancer, diverticulitis, or bleeding lesions in the cecum.
