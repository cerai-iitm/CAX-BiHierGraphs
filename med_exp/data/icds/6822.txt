Control Bleeding in Abdominal Wall, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to directly visualize and control bleeding from injured blood vessels or organs. The open approach allows the surgeon to manually explore the abdominal cavity, identify the source of bleeding, and apply appropriate techniques to stop the bleeding, such as ligating or suturing damaged vessels or repairing injured tissues. The procedure is often performed in emergency situations, such as traumatic injuries or ruptured aneurysms, where prompt control of bleeding is crucial to preventing further complications or death. The surgeon's ability to directly visualize the bleeding site enables precise and effective control of hemorrhage, reducing the risk of ongoing blood loss and promoting patient stabilization.
