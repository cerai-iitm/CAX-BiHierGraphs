Excision of Prostate, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the prostate gland through small incisions in the perineum, using an endoscope to visualize the prostate and surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or spinal anesthesia, and a small camera and specialized instruments are inserted through the incisions to facilitate the excision. The surgeon uses the endoscope to guide the instruments and carefully dissect the prostate gland from surrounding structures, before removing it through the incision site. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) or prostate cancer.
