Division of Left Fibula, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the left fibula, a bone in the lower leg. The fibula is then deliberately divided or cut, typically to facilitate healing or to remove a damaged or diseased section of the bone. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the bone and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to carefully control the division process. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as a non-healing fibula fracture, bone tumor, or congenital deformity.
