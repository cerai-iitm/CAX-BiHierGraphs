Insertion of Monitoring Device into Ureter, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a monitoring device through the ureter, using an endoscope to visualize the area, to monitor urinary tract function and detect potential issues. The device is inserted via a natural or artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to access the ureter without the need for open surgery. The endoscope provides a clear view of the ureter, enabling the healthcare provider to accurately place the monitoring device in the desired location. Once in place, the device can provide valuable insights into ureter function, helping to diagnose and manage conditions such as kidney stones or ureteral obstruction.
