Fluoroscopy of Left External Carotid Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This non-invasive medical imaging procedure utilizes low osmolar contrast to visualize the left external carotid artery, allowing doctors to diagnose and monitor blood flow and vessel damage. During the procedure, a special dye is injected into the artery, and continuous X-rays are taken to capture real-time images of the artery and its blood flow. The low osmolar contrast material is less toxic and causes fewer allergic reactions compared to traditional contrast agents. The resulting images help clinicians identify blockages, aneurysms, or other abnormalities in the left external carotid artery.
