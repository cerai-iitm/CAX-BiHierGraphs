Excision of Pancreatic Duct, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This open surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of the pancreatic duct to aid in diagnostic evaluation. The goal is to collect tissue or fluid samples for examination, helping to identify underlying conditions affecting the pancreas, such as tumors, cysts, or benign lesions. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the pancreas and carefully excises a section of the pancreatic duct. The removed tissue or fluid is then sent to a laboratory for histopathological examination and further analysis.
