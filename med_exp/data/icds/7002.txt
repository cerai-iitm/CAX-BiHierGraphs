Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device into the left pleural cavity through a percutaneous approach, typically using a needle or trocar inserted through the skin and chest wall. The infusion device, such as a catheter or port, is placed into the pleural space to allow for the administration of medications, fluids, or other treatments directly into the lung cavity. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is often performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the infusion device.
