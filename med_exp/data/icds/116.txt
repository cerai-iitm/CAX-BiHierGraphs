Extirpation of Matter from Cerebral Hemisphere, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves removing abnormal or foreign matter from the cerebral hemisphere, a region of the brain responsible for higher-level cognitive functions, through an open approach, which requires a craniotomy to access the affected area. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia and may involve the removal of blood clots, tumors, or other Lesions causing harm to surrounding brain tissue. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms, restore function, and prevent further damage to the brain. After the procedure, the affected area is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any post-operative complications.
