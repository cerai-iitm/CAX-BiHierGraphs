Reposition Right Ankle Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the right ankle joint, which has become dislocated or misaligned. The goal is to restore the joint to its normal anatomic position, and to achieve this, an internal fixation device such as a plate, screw, or rod is used to hold the bones in place. The fixation device is inserted through the open incision, and once in place, it provides stability to the joint, allowing it to heal properly. As the joint heals, the internal fixation device remains in place, providing long-term support and promoting optimal recovery.
