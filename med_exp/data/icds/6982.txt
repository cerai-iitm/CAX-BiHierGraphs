Extirpation of Matter from Right Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove foreign matter or unwanted substances from the right pleural cavity, a space surrounding the lungs. Through a small incision, an endoscope is inserted into the chest, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and extract the unwanted material using specialized instruments. This percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical methods. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or sedation to ensure patient comfort during the operation.
