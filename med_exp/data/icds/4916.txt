Resection of Left Mandible, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left side of the face to access the mandible, also known as the lower jawbone. The surgeon then removes a portion of the left mandible, which may be necessary to treat conditions such as bone cancer, infections, or severe fractures. The goal of the resection is to remove the affected area and help restore the natural function and appearance of the jaw. Depending on the patient's individual needs, reconstructive surgery may be performed during the same procedure to repair the affected area.
