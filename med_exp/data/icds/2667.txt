Destruction of Peritoneum, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical destruction of the peritoneum, a thin layer of tissue that lines the abdominal cavity, through an open approach. The surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the peritoneum, and then uses specialized instruments to destroy the targeted area of the peritoneum. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as peritoneal cancer, peritoneal carcinomatosis, or peritoneal metastases. The goal of the procedure is to remove or destroy cancerous cells or tissues in the peritoneum, and may be performed in conjunction with other surgical procedures or as part of a larger treatment plan.
