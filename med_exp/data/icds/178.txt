Repair Dura Mater, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a craniotomy to access the dura mater, the protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord, to repair any tears, holes, or defects. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully suture or patch the dura mater to restore its integrity. The goal of the procedure is to prevent cerebrospinal fluid leaks, reduce the risk of infection or meningitis, and alleviate symptoms such as headaches, seizures, or neurological deficits. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve collaboration between neurosurgeons and other specialized healthcare professionals.
