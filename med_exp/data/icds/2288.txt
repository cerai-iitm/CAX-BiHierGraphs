Extraction of Right Middle Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the right middle lobe bronchus, a branch of the airway that supplies oxygen to the middle lobe of the right lung. Through a natural or artificial opening, the endoscope is guided to the target site, allowing the healthcare provider to identify any abnormalities or lesions. The extraction process is then performed under direct visualization, enabling the removal of tissue or foreign material from the bronchus for diagnostic purposes. The endoscopic approach enables a more precise and less traumatic procedure compared to traditional open surgical methods.
