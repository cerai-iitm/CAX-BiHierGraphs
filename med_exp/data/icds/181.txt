Repair Spinal Meninges, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves accessing the spinal meninges through an open incision to repair any tears, holes, or weaknesses in the delicate membranes that surround the spinal cord. The goal of the repair is to prevent spinal fluid leaks, reduce the risk of infection, and alleviate symptoms such as headaches, nausea, and fatigue. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully exposes the affected area, identifies the defect, and uses sutures or patches to seal the meninges. The repair is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor for potential complications.
