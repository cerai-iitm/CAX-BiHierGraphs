Excision of Cerebral Meninges, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access the cerebral meninges, a protective membrane surrounding the brain, and carefully removing the affected or damaged portion. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the meninges, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the targeted area. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as meningiomas, infections, or hematomas that affect the meninges and potentially compress or damage the brain. The excision is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety during the operation.
