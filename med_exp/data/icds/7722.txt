Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Cranial Cavity and Brain, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of a therapeutic substance directly into the cranial cavity and brain through the skin, providing targeted treatment for specific neurological conditions. The substance is injected through a small incision, allowing for precise delivery and minimizing damage to surrounding tissue. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical procedures. The introduced substance can help alleviate symptoms, slow disease progression, or even promote healing in the brain.
