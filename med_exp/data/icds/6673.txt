Resection of Bilateral Ovaries, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of both ovaries through small incisions, using an endoscope to visually guide the surgeon. The process is typically performed under general anesthesia and takes around 1-2 hours to complete. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a few tiny openings in the abdomen, inserts the endoscope and specialized instruments, and carefully separates the ovaries from surrounding tissues before removing them. The use of an endoscopic approach reduces post-operative pain, scarring, and recovery time compared to traditional open surgery.
