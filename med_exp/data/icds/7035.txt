Inspection of Pelvic Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a device or instrument through the skin to visually examine the pelvic cavity, allowing for a detailed evaluation of the pelvic organs and structures. The percutaneous approach enables a minimally invasive exploration of the pelvic area, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. During the procedure, the healthcare provider may use specialized instruments, such as endoscopes or laparoscopes, to illuminate and magnify the pelvic cavity, providing a clear view of any abnormalities or conditions. The inspection may help diagnose or treat various conditions affecting the pelvic organs, including cysts, tumors, or adhesions.
