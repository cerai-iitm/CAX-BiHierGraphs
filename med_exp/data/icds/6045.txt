Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Right Hip Joint, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical removal of a synthetic substitute, such as a hip implant or prosthetic, from the right hip joint through an open approach. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia and involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the hip joint. The synthetic substitute is then carefully removed, and any damaged or infected tissue may be debrided or repaired during the procedure. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain and restore function to the hip joint by removing the faulty or malfunctioning synthetic substitute.
