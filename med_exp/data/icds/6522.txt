Drainage of Vulva, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the vulva to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or pus from an abscess or hematoma. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, enabling precise identification and removal of the problematic fluid or tissue. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort and may involve antibiotics to prevent infection. Post-operative care often includes pain management, wound cleaning, and monitoring for signs of complication or infection.
