Dilation of Left Ventricle, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical approach to directly access the left ventricle, the lower chamber of the heart responsible for pumping oxygenated blood to the body. The goal is to dilate or widen the ventricle to improve its function and increase blood flow. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the heart and uses specialized instruments to carefully dilate the left ventricle. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as cardiomyopathy or ventricular hypertrophy, which can lead to heart failure if left untreated.
