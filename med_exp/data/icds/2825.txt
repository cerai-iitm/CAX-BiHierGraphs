Excision of Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the small intestine, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove a portion of the small intestine. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a hospital stay. The excision of the small intestine is often necessary to treat conditions such as bowel obstruction, cancer, or traumatic injury. The surgeon will carefully disconnect and remove the affected segment of intestine, and then reattach the remaining healthy portions to restore normal digestive function.
