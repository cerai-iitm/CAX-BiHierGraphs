Destruction of Mesentery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdominal wall to access the mesentery, a fold of tissue that attaches the small intestine to the back of the abdominal wall. The goal of the procedure is to destroy or remove damaged or diseased portions of the mesentery, which can be necessary to treat conditions such as cancer, inflammation, or obstruction. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully identifies and isolates the affected area of the mesentery and uses specialized instruments to destroy or remove the damaged tissue. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve a hospital stay to monitor for any post-operative complications.
