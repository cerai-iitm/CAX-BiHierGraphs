Release Large Intestine, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the large intestine, also known as the colon, to release any adhesions or attachments that may be causing obstruction or constriction. The surgeon carefully identifies the affected area and gently frees the intestine from any surrounding tissue or structures. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bowel function and relieve symptoms such as abdominal pain, bloating, and constipation. Once the release is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient can begin the recovery process.
