Repair Left Ankle Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the left ankle joint and repairing any damaged or degraded tissue, bone, or cartilage. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any issues. The goal of the repair is to restore normal anatomy and function to the ankle joint, alleviating pain, stiffness, and limited mobility. The procedure may involve realigning or stabilizing bones, repairing or replacing damaged ligaments or tendons, or removing inflamed or damaged tissue to promote healing and return to normal activity.
