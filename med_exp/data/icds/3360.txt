Drainage of Common Bile Duct with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the detailed description:

Percutaneous drainage of the common bile duct is a minimally invasive procedure that involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin and into the duct to remove bile and relieve obstruction. Under imaging guidance, a small incision is made in the skin, and a drainage catheter is inserted through the incision and advanced into the common bile duct. The catheter is then connected to a drainage bag or other collection device, allowing bile to drain externally. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as bile duct obstruction, gallstones, or bile leaks, and can help alleviate symptoms like jaundice, abdominal pain, and fever.
