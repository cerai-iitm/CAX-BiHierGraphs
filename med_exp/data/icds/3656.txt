Excision of Scalp Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the skin, allowing for the removal of a section of skin tissue for diagnostic purposes. The external approach is used, where the incision is made directly on the scalp, providing direct access to the affected skin area. The excised skin tissue is then sent to a laboratory for examination to help diagnose underlying conditions or diseases. The goal of the procedure is to obtain a skin sample for analysis, which can aid in identifying skin disorders, infections, or other conditions affecting the scalp.
