Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the peritoneal cavity, a fluid-filled space surrounding the abdominal organs. The goal is to drain excess fluid or infected material that has accumulated in the cavity, which can cause symptoms such as abdominal pain, swelling, or difficulty breathing. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the cavity and remove the unwanted material, promoting healing and resolution of the underlying condition. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor for potential complications.
