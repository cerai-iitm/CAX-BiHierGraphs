Inspection of Gastrointestinal Tract, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a thorough examination of the gastrointestinal tract through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the internal organs and tissues. The open approach provides direct access to the digestive system, enabling the identification of any abnormalities, lesions, or diseases that may not be visible through non-invasive diagnostic tests. The surgeon may use various instruments and techniques to explore the tract, including palpation, observation, and sampling of tissue for further examination. The goal of this procedure is to diagnose and potentially treat conditions such as gastrointestinal tumors, bleeding, or obstruction.
