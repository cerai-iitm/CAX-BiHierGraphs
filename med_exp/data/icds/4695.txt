Repair Right Upper Leg Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right upper leg to access and repair a damaged or torn tendon. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected tendon and perform the necessary repairs, which may involve reattaching the tendon to the bone or repairing a partial tear. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve additional steps, such as removing damaged tissue or repairing surrounding soft tissues. After the repair is complete, the incision is closed and the leg is immobilized to allow the tendon to heal properly.
