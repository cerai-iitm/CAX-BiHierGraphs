Inspection of Peritoneum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdominal wall to visually inspect the peritoneum, a thin layer of tissue lining the abdominal cavity. The surgeon gently retracts the abdominal organs to access the peritoneum, allowing for a thorough examination of the tissue. This open approach enables the direct visualization of any abnormalities, such as inflammation, adhesions, or tumors, which can inform further treatment decisions. The procedure is often performed to diagnose and manage conditions affecting the peritoneum, such as peritonitis or abdominal cancer.
