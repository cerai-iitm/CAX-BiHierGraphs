Excision of Left Neck Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left side of the neck to access the lymphatic system. The surgeon then carefully excises the affected lymphatic tissue or node, which may be enlarged, infected, or cancerous. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the targeted tissue while minimizing damage to surrounding structures. Following excision, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
