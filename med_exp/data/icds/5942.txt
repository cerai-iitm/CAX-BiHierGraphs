Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Synthetic Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, two or more lumbar vertebral joints are fused together using a synthetic substitute material, promoting spinal stability and alleviating pain. The posterior approach is used, accessing the spine from the back, and the open approach allows for a more traditional incision. The synthetic material is carefully positioned in the posterior column, replacing the damaged or degenerated discs, and the vertebral joints are secured in place to promote fusion. This complex procedure requires great precision and skill to achieve optimal results and improve the patient's overall quality of life.
