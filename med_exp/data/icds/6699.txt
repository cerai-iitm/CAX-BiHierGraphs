Resection of Cervix, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visually examine the cervix and guide the removal of affected tissue or a small tumor. The endoscope is inserted through the vagina, allowing the healthcare provider to access the cervix without the need for an open incision. The affected tissue or tumor is then carefully resected, or cut away, using specialized instruments inserted through the endoscope. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure typically results in less pain, scarring, and recovery time compared to traditional open surgical methods.
