Release Transverse Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the transverse colon through a small incision in the skin. The endoscope is guided through the abdominal wall to reach the transverse colon, allowing the healthcare provider to release any adhesions or scar tissue that may be causing obstruction or discomfort. This percutaneous approach enables the procedure to be performed without the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and minimizing scarring. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bowel function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and bowel obstruction.
