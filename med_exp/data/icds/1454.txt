Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Innominate Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small infusion device through the skin and into the right innominate vein, which is a major blood vessel located in the upper chest. The device is guided into place using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement. Once in position, the infusion device allows for the administration of medications, fluids, or other substances directly into the bloodstream. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for a surgical incision, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery.
