Dilation of Right Femoral Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the skin to access the right femoral artery, allowing a catheter to be inserted through the incision and guided to the targeted area. Once in place, a specialized balloon is inflated to gently dilate or widen the artery, improving blood flow and relieving any blockages. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the physician carefully monitors the procedure to ensure accurate placement and effective dilation. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as leg pain or weakness.
