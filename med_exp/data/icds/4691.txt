Repair Right Hand Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected tendon in the right hand, allowing the surgeon to visually examine and repair the damaged tendon tissue. The open approach enables the surgeon to directly visualize the tendon and surrounding soft tissue, facilitating a more precise and effective repair. The damaged tendon fibers are carefully trimmed and reattached, and any associated tissue such as ligaments or muscle may also be repaired or reconstructed as necessary. Once the repair is complete, the incision is closed, and the hand is immobilized to promote healing and reduce the risk of further injury.
