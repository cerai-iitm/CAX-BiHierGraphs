Monitoring of Arterial Pressure, Peripheral, Percutaneous Approach
This invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through the skin and into a peripheral artery to continuously monitor arterial blood pressure. The catheter is connected to a transducer, which converts the pressure waves into electrical signals that are displayed on a monitor. This close surveillance of arterial pressure allows healthcare providers to quickly identify any changes or abnormalities, enabling prompt interventions to prevent complications. By providing real-time feedback, this monitoring enables precise titration of medications and other therapies to ensure optimal management of cardiovascular conditions.
