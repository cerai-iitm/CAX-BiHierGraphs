Fluoroscopy of Splenic Arteries using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure utilizes fluoroscopy to visualize the splenic arteries, which supply blood to the spleen, an organ involved in filtering the blood and storing red blood cells. During the procedure, a contrast agent, other than the standard type, is administered to enhance the visibility of the arteries and surrounding structures. The fluoroscope, a continuous X-ray beam, is used to guide the procedure and produce real-time images, allowing the healthcare provider to accurately assess the splenic arteries. The resulting images can help diagnose and treat conditions affecting the splenic arteries, such as aneurysms, stenosis, or occlusions.
