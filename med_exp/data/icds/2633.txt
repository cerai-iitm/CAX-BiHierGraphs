Bypass Transverse Colon to Cutaneous, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass from the transverse colon to the skin, allowing fecal matter to divert through an artificial opening, known as a colostomy, rather than passing through the anus. The open approach involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the affected area. The bypass is created by attaching the transverse colon to the skin, creating a new pathway for waste elimination. The goal of this procedure is to reroute the flow of feces and relieve bowel obstruction or other complications associated with diseased or damaged sections of the colon.
