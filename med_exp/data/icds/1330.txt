Supplement Right Femoral Artery with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using a catheter to guide a synthetic graft through an artery in the leg, repairing the right femoral artery by supplementing it with a durable synthetic substitute, allowing for improved blood flow and reducing the risk of further damage or blockage. The percutaneous approach ensures minimal tissue disruption and scarring, promoting faster healing and recovery. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural artery, providing a strong and flexible structure that can withstand blood pressure and flow. The entire process is typically performed under local anesthesia and imaging guidance, ensuring a high degree of accuracy and precision.
