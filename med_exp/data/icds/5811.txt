Reposition Right Wrist Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
Surgical correction of a misaligned right wrist joint involves an open approach, where a surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area, and uses an internal fixation device to reposition and stabilize the joint. The internal fixation device, such as a plate and screws, is implanted to hold the bones in place while they heal. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the operation. Post-operative care involves immobilization of the wrist in a cast or splint to facilitate proper healing and minimize complications.
