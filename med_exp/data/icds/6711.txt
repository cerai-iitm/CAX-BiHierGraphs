Supplement Vagina with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the use of a non-autologous tissue substitute, such as a synthetic or biological material, to supplement or augment the vagina. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the vaginal area, allowing the surgeon to implant the tissue substitute and reshape the surrounding tissue as needed. The goal of this procedure is to restore functional and aesthetic integrity to the vagina, often in cases where tissue damage or loss has occurred due to injury, disease, or congenital conditions. By supplementing the vagina with a non-autologous tissue substitute, individuals can experience improved sexual function, reduced discomfort, and enhanced overall quality of life.
