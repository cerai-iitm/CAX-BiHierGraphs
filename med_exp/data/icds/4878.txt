Reposition Frontal Bone with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the frontal bone to its normal anatomical position to restore the natural contours of the forehead and facial structure. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the bone, enabling precise repositioning and stabilization. An internal fixation device, such as a plate or screw, is then used to hold the bone in place while it heals. The goal of this procedure is to restore functional and aesthetic balance to the face, addressing issues such as deformity, asymmetry, or facial trauma.
