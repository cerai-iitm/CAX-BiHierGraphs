Excision of Left Atrium, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the left atrium, a chamber of the heart that receives oxygenated blood from the lungs. The cardiothoracic surgeon then carefully excises the left atrium, a delicate process that requires significant expertise to avoid damaging surrounding tissue and structures. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the heart and its structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the affected area. Following excision, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any potential complications.
