Fusion of Occipital-cervical Joint, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to fuse the occipital-cervical joint, which connects the base of the skull and the cervical spine, to stabilize and treat conditions such as instability, deformity, or trauma. The posterior approach involves an incision in the back of the neck, allowing the surgeon to access the affected joint. Through an open approach, the surgeon removes any damaged or diseased tissue, realigns the joint, and uses grafting material or internal fixation devices to promote fusion. This procedure aims to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, and weakness, and improve mobility and quality of life.
