Excision of Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of tissue from the lumbar vertebral joint through a small incision using a percutaneous approach, which allows for a faster recovery time and less tissue damage. The diagnostic excision is performed to collect a sample of tissue for examination and diagnosis of underlying conditions or diseases affecting the joint. During the procedure, a small instrument is inserted through the skin and guided to the joint using imaging technology, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure precise removal of tissue. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and analysis.
