Extraction of Stomach, Pylorus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of the stomach, specifically the pylorus, through a natural or artificial opening. The pylorus is the lower part of the stomach that connects to the small intestine. During the procedure, an endoscope is inserted through the opening, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the area and perform the extraction. The diagnostic nature of this procedure suggests that it may be performed to obtain tissue samples for biopsy or to diagnose conditions affecting the stomach, such as ulcers or cancer.
