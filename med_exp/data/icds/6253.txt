Change Drainage Device in Bladder, External Approach
This medical procedure involves the replacement of an existing drainage device in the bladder through an external approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the bladder. The purpose of this procedure is to correct any issues with the current drainage device, such as blockages or infections, and to ensure proper urine drainage from the bladder. The new drainage device is carefully placed in the bladder to restore normal urinary function and alleviate any discomfort or pain associated with the malfunctioning device. The procedure requires close monitoring and careful handling to minimize the risk of complications and ensure a successful outcome.
