Other Electroconvulsive Therapy
This procedure involves the administration of a carefully controlled electrical current to the brain, intentionally triggering a seizure to treat severe mental health conditions, such as depression, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia. The treatment is performed under general anesthesia, and muscle relaxants are used to minimize muscle contractions. The goal of this therapy is to stimulate changes in brain chemistry that can help alleviate symptoms and improve mood regulation. This treatment option is typically reserved for patients who have not responded to other forms of therapy or medication.
