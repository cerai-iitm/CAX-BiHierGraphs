Reposition Left Patella, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left patella, also known as the kneecap, to its normal anatomical position. The procedure is performed through an external approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the patella. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, improve mobility, and restore proper tracking of the patella in the trochlear groove of the femur. The surgeon may also perform additional procedures, such as tightening or loosening surrounding soft tissues, to ensure proper patellar alignment and stability.
