Removal of Autologous Tissue Substitute from Upper Vein, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically removing an autologous tissue substitute, which is a tissue substitute taken from the patient's own body, from a vein in the upper extremity. The procedure is performed through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected vein. During the procedure, the autologous tissue substitute is carefully removed from the vein, and the incision is then closed. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal blood flow and alleviate any symptoms caused by the presence of the tissue substitute.
