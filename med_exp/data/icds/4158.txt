Insertion of Cardiac Rhythm Related Device into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, a cardiac rhythm related device is implanted into the chest subcutaneous tissue and fascia to regulate the heart's rhythm. The device is carefully positioned to ensure accurate sensing and transmission of electrical signals. The procedure involves making an incision in the chest area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, followed by the gentle insertion of the device into the targeted location. The device is then secured in place, and the incision is closed, allowing the patient to recover and ultimately benefit from the restored normal heart rhythm.
