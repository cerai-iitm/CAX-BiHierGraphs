Excision of Right Metacarpal, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the hand to access the right metacarpal bone, which is one of the five long bones in the hand that connects the wrist to the fingers. The procedure requires a careful dissection of the surrounding soft tissues to expose the bone, followed by the removal of the damaged or diseased portion of the metacarpal bone. The goal of this operation is to relieve pain, improve mobility, and restore normal hand function by removing the affected bone. The surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the hand is immobilized in a cast or splint to allow for proper healing.
