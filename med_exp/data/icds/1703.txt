Revision of Intraluminal Device in Lower Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the lower vein through the skin, typically in the groin or behind the knee, to revise an existing intraluminal device. The device is typically a stent or filter that was previously placed to treat a blockage or clot in the vein. Using imaging guidance, the physician navigates through the vein to reach the device and performs the necessary revisions to ensure its proper function and positioning. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and may require several hours of bed rest after the procedure.
