Reposition Right Finger Phalangeal Joint, External Approach
This procedure involves manually realigning the right finger phalangeal joint to its normal anatomical position using an external approach, which may be necessary to treat conditions such as dislocation, subluxation, or deformity. The physician will typically use imaging studies and physical examination to assess the joint's alignment and mobility before performing the repositioning. During the procedure, the joint is gently manipulated to restore its normal alignment, and the affected finger is then immobilized to allow for healing. Following repositioning, the patient may require pain management and physical therapy to regain full range of motion and function in the affected finger.
