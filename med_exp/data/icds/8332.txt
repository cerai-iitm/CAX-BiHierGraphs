Beam Radiation of Abdomen using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This medical procedure involves the use of high-energy photons with an energy range of 1-10 million electron volts (MeV) to deliver radiation therapy to the abdominal region. The photons, produced by a linear accelerator, are focused into a precise beam that targets the affected area, allowing for precise and effective treatment of abdominal cancers or tumors. The radiation beam is carefully calibrated and monitored to ensure that the desired dose is delivered to the target area while minimizing exposure to surrounding healthy tissues. The procedure is typically performed on an outpatient basis and may be administered in conjunction with other cancer treatments, such as chemotherapy or surgery.
