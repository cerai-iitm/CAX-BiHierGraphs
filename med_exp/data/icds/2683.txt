Dilation of Stomach with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and light through the mouth or a surgically created opening to reach the stomach. Once in place, an intraluminal device, such as a balloon dilator, is used to gently stretch and widen the stomach to its normal diameter, relieving any blockages or narrowing. The procedure is typically performed under sedation to ensure patient comfort and may be done as an outpatient procedure. The goal of the dilation is to restore normal stomach function and alleviate symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain.
