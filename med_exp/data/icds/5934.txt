Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the lumbar vertebral joint through a posterior approach, focusing on the posterior column, to stabilize the spine and alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or tingling. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize the joint and perform the necessary repairs. During the procedure, the surgeon will remove any damaged or degenerated discs or bone, and then implant a bone graft or other stabilizing device to facilitate fusion. The goal of this procedure is to restore spinal stability and alleviate symptoms, improving the patient's overall quality of life.
