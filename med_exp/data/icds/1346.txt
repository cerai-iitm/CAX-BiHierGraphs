Restriction of Abdominal Aorta with Branched or Fenestrated Intraluminal Device, One or Two Arteries, Open Approach
This procedure involves the placement of a branched or fenestrated intraluminal device in the abdominal aorta through an open approach to restrict blood flow. The device is designed to treat complex aortic aneurysms or dissections that involve one or two arteries. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the aorta and accurately position the device to ensure proper blood flow to vital organs. The procedure requires a thorough understanding of the patient's anatomy and may involve additional corrective measures to ensure optimal outcomes.
