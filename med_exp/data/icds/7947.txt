Fluoroscopy of Right Internal Mammary Bypass Graft using Low Osmolar Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, to visualize the right internal mammary bypass graft. Low osmolar contrast medium is injected into the graft to enhance visualization and aid in diagnosis. The internal mammary artery is used as a bypass graft in coronary artery bypass surgery, and fluoroscopy is used to assess graft patency and detect any potential blockages or stenosis. The low osmolar contrast helps to reduce the risk of allergic reactions and kidney damage, making it a safer option for patients.
