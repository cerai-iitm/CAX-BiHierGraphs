Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right hip joint with a synthetic substitute, utilizing an open approach. The operation requires a large incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to remove the damaged or deteriorated joint and implant the artificial replacement. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of a healthy hip joint, providing greater mobility and reducing pain for the patient. The open approach allows for a more direct visualization of the joint, enabling the surgeon to achieve a more precise and effective replacement.
