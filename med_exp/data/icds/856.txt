Repair Left Hand Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the left hand artery, allowing the surgeon to visually examine and repair any damage or defects. The open approach enables direct visualization of the artery, facilitating the repair of any lesions, aneurysms, or other vascular abnormalities. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and minimize movement during the operation. Upon completion, the incision is closed, and the hand is immobilized to promote healing and prevent further injury.
