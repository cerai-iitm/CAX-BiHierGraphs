Excision of Left Trunk Muscle, Open Approach, Diagnostic
Here is the detailed description:

During this surgical procedure, the left trunk muscle is excised through an open approach, which involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected area. This diagnostic procedure is performed to examine the muscle tissue and identify any abnormalities or diseases. The surgeon will carefully dissect the muscle tissue to remove the affected portion, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues. The excised tissue will then be sent to a laboratory for further examination to determine the underlying cause of the condition.
