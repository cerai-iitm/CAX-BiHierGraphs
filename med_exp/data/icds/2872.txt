Excision of Ascending Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope to visualize the ascending colon through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for the excision of the affected portion of the colon. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the physician to remove the diseased segment while minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. By using an endoscopic approach, patients can benefit from reduced post-operative pain, faster recovery, and minimal scarring.
