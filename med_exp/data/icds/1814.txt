Resection of Left Axillary Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left axillary region to access the lymphatic tissue, which is then removed through an open approach. The goal of the procedure is to excise the affected lymph nodes, which may be cancerous or damaged, to prevent further spread of disease or alleviate symptoms. The surgeon carefully dissects the tissue to identify and isolate the lymphatic structures, taking care to preserve surrounding nerves, blood vessels, and other vital structures. The excised tissue is then sent for pathological examination to confirm the diagnosis and guide further treatment.
