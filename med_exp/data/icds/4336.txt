Transfer Right Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of subcutaneous tissue and fascia from one area to another in the right upper leg through an open approach, requiring a skin incision to access the underlying tissue. The procedure aims to repair or reconstruct damaged tissue and fascia, restoring function and appearance to the affected area. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully mobilizes and transfers the subcutaneous tissue and fascia to the desired location, ensuring proper alignment and securing it in place. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the operative site, enabling the surgeon to achieve precise dissection and transfer of tissue.
