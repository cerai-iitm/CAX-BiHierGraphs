Drainage of Right Hip Joint, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the right hip joint, allowing the surgeon to drain accumulated fluid, pus, or blood from the joint cavity. This open approach enables direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, facilitating the removal of any infected or inflammatory material. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and limited mobility, and to promote healing and restore normal joint function. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve debridement, irrigation, or other adjunctive techniques to ensure thorough cleansing and drainage of the affected joint.
