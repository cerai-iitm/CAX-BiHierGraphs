Drainage of Left Hand Tendon, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a skin incision is made on the left hand to access the affected tendon. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to drain excess fluid, blood, or other debris that may be causing inflammation, pain, or restriction of movement in the tendon. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tendon, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the source of the problem. Once the drainage is complete, the incision is closed, and the hand is immobilized to promote healing and prevent further injury.
