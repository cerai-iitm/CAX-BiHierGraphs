Excision of Cervical Vertebra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the cervical spine, allowing the surgeon to remove a problematic or damaged cervical vertebra. The open approach provides direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully excise the vertebra and any associated tissue or bone fragments. Depending on the individual case, the procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as spinal tumors, vertebral fractures, or herniated discs that are compressing the spinal cord or nerve roots. Following the excision, the surgical site is typically closed and the patient is monitored for a period of time to ensure a smooth recovery.
