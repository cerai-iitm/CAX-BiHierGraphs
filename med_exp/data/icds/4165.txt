Insertion of Cardiac Resynchronization Pacemaker Pulse Generator into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a cardiac resynchronization pacemaker pulse generator into the chest subcutaneous tissue and fascia through a small incision. The pacemaker is implanted percutaneously, meaning through the skin, to help regulate abnormal heart rhythms and improve cardiac function. The pulse generator is positioned under the skin and connected to electrodes that stimulate the heart, enabling coordinated contractions of the ventricles. The goal of this procedure is to improve heart function, reduce symptoms of heart failure, and enhance overall quality of life.
