Release Radial Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the radial nerve, which runs from the spinal cord to the hand, to release compression or entrapment that can cause numbness, tingling, or weakness. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the nerve and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and free the nerve from any constraints. This can help to relieve pressure on the nerve and restore normal sensation and motor function to the affected arm and hand. By releasing the radial nerve, this procedure can effectively treat conditions such as radial tunnel syndrome or radial nerve entrapment.
