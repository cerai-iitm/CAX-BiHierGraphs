Fusion of Cervicothoracic Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the cervicothoracic vertebral joint to stabilize the spine and alleviate symptoms such as pain and numbness. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, where an incision is made in the back to access the vertebral joint. An autologous tissue substitute, typically harvested from the patient's own body, is used to facilitate the fusion process. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the spine and accurately place the graft to promote optimal healing and spinal stability.
