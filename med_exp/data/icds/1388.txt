Revision of Intraluminal Device in Lower Artery, Percutaneous Approach
Revision of an intraluminal device in the lower artery involves a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, where the surgeon accesses the device through a small incision in the skin. The procedure aims to repair or replace the existing device, which may be malfunctioning or causing complications. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the surgeon navigates to the site of the device and performs the necessary revisions to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms. The percutaneous approach reduces recovery time and minimizes scarring, offering a less invasive alternative to traditional open surgery.
