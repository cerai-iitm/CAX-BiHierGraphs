Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Internal Carotid Arteries
This non-invasive medical imaging procedure involves the use of continuous X-ray beams to visualize the internal carotid arteries on both sides of the neck in real-time. The fluoroscope allows the medical practitioner to observe the flow of contrast dye as it moves through the arteries, helping to identify any blockages, narrowing, or other abnormalities. The procedure is typically performed to diagnose and monitor conditions such as carotid artery stenosis, vasculitis, or arteriovenous malformations. The use of fluoroscopy provides a dynamic and detailed assessment of blood flow and vessel anatomy, enabling accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.
