Drainage of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
This procedure involves inserting a drainage tube or instrument through a natural or artificial opening in the chest wall to drain fluid, pus, or blood from the left lower lung lobe. The purpose of this diagnostic procedure is to identify the underlying cause of symptoms such as fever, cough, or chest pain, or to investigate abnormal imaging test results. The procedure may be performed using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or computed tomography, to ensure proper placement of the drainage tube. Successful drainage can help alleviate symptoms, improve lung function, and guide further treatment decisions.
