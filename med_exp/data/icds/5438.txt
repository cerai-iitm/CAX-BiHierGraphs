Reposition Right Lower Femur with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of the right lower femur bone into its normal anatomical alignment using an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. The procedure is performed to correct deformities, fractures, or other abnormalities in the lower femur. Internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods are used to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The overall goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and mobility to the affected leg.
