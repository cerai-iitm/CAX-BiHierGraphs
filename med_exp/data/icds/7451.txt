Extraction of Products of Conception, Retained, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the removal of retained products of conception, which are leftover tissues from a pregnancy, through a natural or artificial opening in the body. The goal of this procedure is to prevent infection, heavy bleeding, and other complications that can arise when these tissues are not expelled naturally. The procedure may be performed in a clinical or hospital setting, and anesthesia options vary depending on the individual case. In some cases, medication may be used to help the uterus contract and expel the retained tissues, while in other cases, surgical intervention may be necessary.
