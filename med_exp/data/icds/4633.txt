Excision of Left Lower Arm and Wrist Tendon, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the affected tendon in the left lower arm and wrist is excised through an open approach, which involves making an incision in the skin to access the tendon. The surgeon carefully identifies and removes the damaged or diseased tendon tissue, taking care to minimize disruption to surrounding tissues. The excision is performed under direct visualization, allowing the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the affected tendon. The procedure aims to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility in the wrist and arm, promoting healing and functional recovery.
