Reposition Skull with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves repositioning the skull bones to their normal anatomical position in order to correct deformities or defects. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and manipulate the bones directly. Internal fixation devices, such as plates and screws, are then used to stabilize the skull bones in their new position, promoting healing and restoring normal function. The goal of the procedure is to improve cranial symmetry, alleviate symptoms, and enhance overall quality of life.
