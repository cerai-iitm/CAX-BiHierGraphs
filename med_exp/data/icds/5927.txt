Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two or more adjacent lumbar vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device, which is introduced through an anterior approach, providing direct access to the anterior column of the spine. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully position the device and promote optimal fusion. The anterior column fusion device serves as a scaffold for bone growth, stabilizing the spine and promoting a solid fusion of the adjacent vertebrae. By fuseing the lumbar vertebral joints, this procedure aims to alleviate chronic back pain, correct spinal deformities, and improve overall spinal stability.
