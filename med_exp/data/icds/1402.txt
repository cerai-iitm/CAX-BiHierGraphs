Dilation of Left Subclavian Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small catheter through the skin to access the left subclavian vein, allowing for the controlled widening of the vein using specialized balloons or dilators. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and promoting less scarring. The goal of this procedure is to improve blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with narrowed or blocked subclavian veins, such as arm swelling, pain, or weakness. The interventional radiologist or cardiologist performs the procedure under imaging guidance, ensuring precise navigation and optimal outcomes.
