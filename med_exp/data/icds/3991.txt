Drainage of Right Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right hand to access the underlying subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of connective tissue that surround muscles and other structures. The purpose of the procedure is to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances that may be causing infection, swelling, or other complications. A drainage device is inserted through the incision to facilitate the removal of the unwanted substances and promote healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the underlying cause of the condition.
