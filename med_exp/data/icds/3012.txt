Inspection of Lower Intestinal Tract, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This non-invasive diagnostic procedure involves inserting a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end through the skin to visualize the inside of the lower intestinal tract. The procedure is performed under sterile conditions to minimize the risk of infection. The camera transmits live images of the intestinal lining to a video monitor, allowing the healthcare provider to examine the tract for any abnormalities, such as ulcers, polyps, or bleeding. The entire process is typically performed under conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort.
