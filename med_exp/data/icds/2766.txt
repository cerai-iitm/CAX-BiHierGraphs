Drainage of Rectum with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a drainage device is inserted through the skin to drain fluids or abscesses from the rectum, providing relief from symptoms such as pain, swelling, and infection. The percutaneous approach allows for a small incision in the skin, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The drainage device is carefully guided into place using imaging guidance, ensuring accurate placement and optimal drainage. Once in place, the device allows for the removal of infected or obstructed material, promoting healing and resolution of the underlying condition.
