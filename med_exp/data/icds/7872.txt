Performance of Urinary Filtration, Continuous, Greater than 18 hours Per Day
This continuous urinary filtration procedure involves the removal of waste products from the blood through a filtration device, operating for more than 18 hours a day. The prolonged filtration duration allows for more efficient elimination of toxins and excess fluids, providing relief to patients with impaired kidney function. The continuous nature of the procedure enables consistent and gentle renal support, reducing the risk of complications and promoting stable vital signs. By extending the filtration period beyond 18 hours, this treatment option offers an enhanced level of care for patients requiring prolonged renal replacement therapy.
