Replacement of Left Brachial Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical replacement of the left brachial artery with a graft made from the patient's own tissue, typically harvested from a healthy blood vessel. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to remove the damaged or diseased portion of the artery and replace it with the autologous tissue substitute. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow to the arm and improve overall circulation. The autologous tissue substitute is preferred over synthetic grafts as it reduces the risk of rejection and promotes better integration with the surrounding tissue.
