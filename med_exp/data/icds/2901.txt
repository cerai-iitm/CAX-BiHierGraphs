Excision of Anus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the anus, followed by the careful dissection and removal of the anus, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissue and preserve continence. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a significant amount of post-operative care to ensure proper healing and minimize complications. The anus is a complex structure responsible for regulating bowel movements, and its removal can have significant implications for the patient's quality of life. In some cases, a colostomy may be necessary to divert the flow of feces, requiring ongoing management and care.
