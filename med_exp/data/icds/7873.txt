Restoration of Cardiac Rhythm, Single
This procedure involves the use of a device to restore a normal heart rhythm in a patient who has an abnormal heart rhythm, also known as an arrhythmia. The device, such as a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator, is used to provide an electrical signal to the heart to restore a normal rhythm. This may be done in cases where the heart is beating too slowly, too quickly, or irregularly, and the patient is experiencing symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, or dizziness. The goal of the procedure is to restore a normal heart rhythm and alleviate symptoms, improving the patient's overall quality of life.
