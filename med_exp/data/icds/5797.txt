Replacement of Right Wrist Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right wrist joint with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, requiring a surgical incision to access the joint. The damaged or arthritic wrist joint is removed and replaced with a prosthetic joint, which is designed to mimic the natural movement of the wrist. The synthetic substitute is typically made of metal, ceramic, or plastic materials and is attached to the surrounding bone using bone cement or other fixation methods. The goal of the procedure is to restore wrist function, alleviate pain, and improve overall quality of life.
