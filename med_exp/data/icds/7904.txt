Robotic Assisted Procedure of Trunk Region, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure utilizes a robotic system to assist in a minimally invasive surgical intervention on the trunk region, which includes the chest and abdominal areas. Through a small incision, the robotic instruments are inserted percutaneously, allowing for precise dissection and manipulation of tissues with enhanced dexterity and control. The robotic system provides high-definition, three-dimensional visualization, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and target specific areas of interest. The combination of minimally invasive access and robotic assistance promotes reduced tissue trauma,-less post-operative pain, and faster recovery times.
