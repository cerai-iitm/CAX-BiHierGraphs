Release Mesentery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the mesentery, a fold of membranous tissue that attaches the small intestine to the back of the abdominal wall. The goal of the procedure is to release or free the mesentery from any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstructions that may be impeding normal intestinal function. The open approach requires a larger incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary manipulations to release the mesentery. The procedure may be necessary to treat conditions such as intestinal obstruction, bowel strangulation, or internal herniation.
