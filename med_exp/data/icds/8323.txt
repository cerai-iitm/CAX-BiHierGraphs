Contact Radiation of Other Bone
Radiation therapy is used to treat malignant or benign tumors in bones other than the spine, ribs, or sternum. This method involves the use of a small radiation device placed close to the affected area, allowing for precise targeting of the tumor while minimizing damage to surrounding healthy tissue. The contact radiation procedure requires careful planning and execution to ensure optimal results, often involving a multidisciplinary team of healthcare professionals. By delivering a high dose of radiation directly to the tumor site, this treatment option can help alleviate symptoms, prevent further tumor growth, and improve quality of life for patients.
