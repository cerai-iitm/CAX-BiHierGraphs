Drainage of Mediastinum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to access the mediastinum, a region in the chest cavity that contains vital organs such as the heart, trachea, and esophagus. The endoscope is equipped with a specialized instrument that allows for the drainage of fluid or air from the mediastinum, which can become accumulated due to various medical conditions such as infection, inflammation, or cancer. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The entire process is guided by real-time imaging and visualization, ensuring precise and effective drainage of the mediastinum.
