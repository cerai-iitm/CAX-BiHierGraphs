Beam Radiation of Other Bone using Heavy Particles (Protons,Ions)
This non-invasive procedure utilizes high-energy heavy particles, such as protons or ions, to deliver targeted radiation therapy to specific bones in the body. The precise beam of particles is carefully calibrated to conform to the exact shape and size of the affected bone, minimizing damage to surrounding tissues and organs. The heavy particles have a higher mass than traditional X-rays, allowing them to penetrate deeper into the body and deposit their energy directly into the tumor site, resulting in more effective treatment. The treatment is typically performed on an outpatient basis, with patients usually undergoing multiple sessions over several weeks.
