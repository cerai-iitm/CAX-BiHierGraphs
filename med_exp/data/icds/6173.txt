Reposition Right Hip Joint, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves manually repositioning the right hip joint to its normal anatomic position through an external approach, often used to treat hip dislocations, deformities or fractures. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected joint, utilizes visualization techniques and manual manipulation to reposition the joint, and may use internal fixation devices or other stabilization methods to maintain the corrected position. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal hip function, alleviate pain and discomfort, and improve mobility and range of motion. Post-operative care typically includes rehabilitation and physical therapy to strengthen surrounding muscles and promote optimal healing.
