Removal of Intraluminal Device from Ureter, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of an intraluminal device from the ureter, a potentially life-saving intervention to restore normal urinary function and alleviate symptoms. Through a small skin incision, a specialist uses fluoroscopic guidance to access the ureter and carefully extract the device, taking care to minimize trauma to the surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be accompanied by antibiotic therapy to prevent infection. Successful removal of the device can resolve symptoms such as flank pain, hematuria, and urinary tract infections.
