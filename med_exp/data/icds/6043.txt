Removal of Liner from Right Hip Joint, Open Approach
During a surgical procedure, the artificial liner from the right hip joint is carefully removed through an open incision, allowing for direct visualization of the joint. This removal is typically performed to address issues such as wear and tear, infection, or implant failure. The open approach provides the surgeon with better access to the affected area, enabling a more precise removal of the liner. Once the liner is removed, any damaged or infected tissue is cleaned and prepared for the possible implantation of a new artificial joint component.
