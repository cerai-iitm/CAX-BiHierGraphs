Excision of Right Upper Leg Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right upper leg to access and remove a tendon, which is a cord of fibrous tissue connecting muscles to bones. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the affected area directly and accurately excise the damaged or diseased tendon. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, restore function, and improve mobility in the affected leg. Post-operative care typically includes immobilization, physical therapy, and rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and prevent complications.
