Excision of Left Wrist Region, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue of the left wrist region to remove a portion of tissue, lesion, or growth. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the targeted tissue while minimizing damage to surrounding structures. The procedure may be performed to treat various conditions, such as tumors, cysts, or infected tissue, and can help alleviate symptoms like pain and limited mobility. Following excision, the wound is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the patient may require a period of rehabilitation to regain full range of motion in the wrist.
