Excision of Cerebral Ventricle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scalp and cranium to access the cerebral ventricle, a cavity in the brain that produces and circulates cerebrospinal fluid. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia and requires a craniotomy, a surgical opening of the skull, to expose the ventricle. The surgeon then carefully excises the affected portion of the ventricle, taking care to preserve surrounding brain tissue and critical neurological structures. The incision is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, and the patient is closely monitored for postoperative complications.
