Extirpation of Matter from Left Common Iliac Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the left common iliac vein, a major blood vessel located in the lower abdomen, through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. A small incision is made in the skin, and a thin catheter or needle is inserted through the incision to access the affected vein. The unwanted material, which may be a blood clot, plaque, or other obstruction, is then carefully removed through the catheter or needle, restoring blood flow to the affected area. The percutaneous approach allows for faster recovery and fewer complications compared to open surgery, making it a preferred method for treating vein-related conditions.
