Insertion of Feeding Device into Jejunum, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert a feeding device directly into the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, through the skin. This percutaneous approach involves creating a small incision in the abdomen, through which a guidewire is inserted to guide the feeding device into place. Once in position, the device is secured and a tube is brought out through the skin, allowing for nutrition to be delivered directly into the jejunum. This procedure is often used for patients who have difficulty eating or absorbing nutrients orally, or those who require long-term enteral nutrition.
