Removal of Drainage Device from Right Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of a drainage device from the right pleural cavity, a minimally invasive approach that reduces the risk of complications. The drainage device, previously inserted to drain excess fluid or air from the pleural cavity, is carefully withdrawn through the skin under image guidance. This outpatient procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may require a chest X-ray or ultrasound to ensure proper placement and removal of the device. Once the device is removed, the wound is closed and the patient is monitored for any signs of complications.
