Removal of Feeding Device from Lower Intestinal Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to remove a feeding device from the lower intestinal tract through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is inserted through the opening and guided to the location of the feeding device, allowing the healthcare provider to visually examine the area and carefully remove the device. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. The removal of the feeding device can help to restore normal bowel function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
