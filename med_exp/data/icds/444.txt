Extirpation of Matter from Superior Vena Cava, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of unwanted material or substances from the superior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the upper body to the heart, through a small incision in the skin. Using imaging guidance, a thin tube or catheter is inserted through the incision and advanced to the superior vena cava to extract the unwanted material. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance to ensure precise placement of the catheter and minimize complications. By removing the unwanted material, this procedure can help restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms related to blockages or obstructions in the superior vena cava.
