Excision of Lumbar Vertebra, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a lumbar vertebra through a small incision in the skin, using a specialized instrument inserted through the skin to access the spine. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT, to ensure accurate placement of the instrument and minimize damage to surrounding tissues. The diagnostic nature of this procedure allows for the examination of the removed vertebra to identify any underlying conditions or abnormalities, providing valuable information for treatment planning. Overall, this percutaneous approach offers a less invasive alternative to open surgery, resulting in reduced tissue trauma, less post-operative pain, and a faster recovery time.
