Drainage of Abdomen Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin to remove excess fluid or infected material from the abdomen's subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The percutaneous approach allows for a small incision, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster healing. During the procedure, imaging guidance such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy may be used to ensure accurate placement of the drainage tube. The drained fluid or material is then collected in a bag or container, helping to alleviate symptoms and promote recovery.
