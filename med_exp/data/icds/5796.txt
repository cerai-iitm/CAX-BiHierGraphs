Replacement of Left Elbow Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, the damaged or diseased left elbow joint is replaced with a synthetic substitute, also known as an artificial joint, to restore normal elbow function and alleviate pain. The synthetic joint is designed to mimic the natural anatomy of the elbow joint, allowing for a smooth and stable range of motion. The surgical approach involves making an incision in the left elbow to access the joint, and the damaged or diseased joint is carefully removed and replaced with the synthetic substitute. The synthetic joint is then secured in place with bone cement or other fixation devices to ensure a stable and durable repair.
