Fusion of Cervicothoracic Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical fusion of the cervicothoracic vertebral joint, which connects the neck and upper back regions, using a posterior approach, meaning the incision is made from the back. The anterior column of the spine is accessed through an open approach, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area directly. Autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body, is used to facilitate the fusion process, promoting a secure and stable bond between the vertebrae. The goal of this complex operation is to alleviate pain, restore spinal stability, and improve mobility in patients with degenerative or traumatic spinal conditions.
