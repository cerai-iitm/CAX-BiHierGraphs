Insertion of Infusion Device into Lower Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small medical device, known as an infusion device, into a vein in the lower body through the skin. The device is typically used to administer medication, fluids, or nutrients over an extended period. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery time and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgery. The infusion device is carefully guided into the vein using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement and minimize discomfort.
