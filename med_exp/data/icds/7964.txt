Ultrasonography of Left Heart
This non-invasive medical imaging test uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the left heart, including the left atrium and ventricle, allowing healthcare professionals to evaluate cardiac structure and function. During the procedure, a handheld probe called a transducer is placed on the chest, which emit sound waves that bounce off the heart and return to the transducer, converting them into electrical signals that are used to create real-time images. These images can help diagnose a range of cardiac conditions, including heart failure, cardiomyopathy, and valvular heart disease. The test is typically performed in a hospital or clinic setting and is commonly used to guide further diagnostic tests or procedures.
