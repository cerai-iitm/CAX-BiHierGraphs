Excision of Large Intestine, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the large intestine, followed by the removal of a portion of the intestine for diagnostic purposes. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the intestine and remove any affected tissue or lesions. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to diagnose conditions such as colon cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, or diverticulitis. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and testing to determine the underlying cause of the patient's symptoms.
