Introduction of Regional Anesthetic into Spinal Canal, Percutaneous Approach
During this procedure, a healthcare professional inserts a small needle into the spinal canal through the skin, using a percutaneous approach. This allows for the administration of regional anesthetic medication directly into the spinal canal. The goal of this procedure is to numb a specific region of the body, typically the lower back, legs, or abdomen, to manage pain or discomfort during surgery or other medical procedures. The introduction of the anesthetic medication into the spinal canal provides effective pain relief, allowing patients to remain comfortable and relaxed during the procedure.
