Dilation of Abdominal Aorta, Bifurcation, with Three Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the widening of the abdominal aorta and bifurcation using a percutaneous approach, where access is gained through the skin and soft tissues. Three intraluminal devices are inserted through a small incision in the groin or arm to facilitate the dilation, restoring blood flow and reducing the risk of further narrowing or blockage. The devices are carefully positioned and expanded using a balloon angioplasty, widening the aorta and its bifurcation to improve blood flow to the legs and other organs. This procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance to minimize risks and ensure accurate device placement.
