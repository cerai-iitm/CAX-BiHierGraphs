Control Bleeding in Cranial Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access the cranial cavity, where the surgeon identifies and controls bleeding from damaged blood vessels or other sources. The goal is to stop active hemorrhaging, reduce intracranial pressure, and prevent further brain damage. The surgeon may use various techniques, such as applying pressure, closing bleeding vessels, or repairing damaged structures to achieve hemostasis. After bleeding is controlled, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for post-operative complications.
