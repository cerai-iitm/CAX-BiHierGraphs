Excision of Abdomen Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdominal region to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The damaged or diseased tissue is then carefully excised, and the area is cleaned and closed. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the targeted tissue. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal anatomical structure and function to the abdominal region, alleviating symptoms and promoting optimal healing.
