Removal of Liner from Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the liner of a right knee joint replacement is carefully removed to alleviate issues related to wear, loosening, or other complications. This complex process involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the joint, followed by the gentle dissection of the surrounding tissue to expose the liner. The liner is then grasped and carefully withdrawn from the joint, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding bone or soft tissue. The removal of the liner is often a necessary step prior to revision or replacement with a new joint component.
