Assistance with Cardiac Output using Other Pump, Continuous
This procedure involves the use of a mechanical device to support and maintain cardiac output, ensuring a continuous flow of oxygenated blood to vital organs. The device works in tandem with the patient's native heart, augmenting its pumping ability to ensure adequate blood pressure and circulation. This assistance can be crucial in situations where the heart is weakened or compromised, such as during cardiogenic shock or cardiac arrest. The continuous support provided by the device helps to stabilize the patient's hemodynamic status, allowing for further treatment or intervention to address the underlying condition.
