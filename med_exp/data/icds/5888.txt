Excision of Lumbar Vertebral Disc, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the skin of the lower back to access the lumbar spine. Through this tiny opening, specialized instruments are inserted to remove a portion of the vertebral disc that is causing compression on nearby nerves or the spinal cord. The procedure is guided by fluoroscopic or CT imaging to ensure accurate placement of the instruments and minimize tissue damage. The excised disc material is then removed through the same small incision, allowing for a faster recovery and less post-operative pain compared to traditional open surgery.
