Replacement of Right Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Ceramic Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This surgical procedure involves a total hip replacement surgery where the femoral surface of the right hip joint is replaced with a ceramic synthetic substitute. The open approach is used, which involves making a single, longer incision in the skin to access the hip joint, allowing for direct visualization of the affected area. The ceramic synthetic substitute is implanted to restore the natural function of the hip joint, providing a smooth surface for the joint to move freely. This procedure aims to alleviate pain and improve mobility in patients with severe hip joint damage or osteoarthritis.
