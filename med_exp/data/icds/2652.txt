Destruction of Jejunum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves using an endoscope to access the jejunum, a section of the small intestine, through a natural or artificial opening in the body. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that allow the healthcare provider to carefully destroy or remove the affected portion of the jejunum. This may be necessary to treat conditions such as cancer, ulcers, or bleeding in the small intestine. The endoscopic approach offers a minimally invasive alternative to open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring.
