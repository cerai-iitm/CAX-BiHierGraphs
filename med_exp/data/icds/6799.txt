Change Drainage Device in Retroperitoneum, External Approach
During this medical procedure, a surgeon accesses the retroperitoneum, the region behind the peritoneum, which is the lining of the abdominal cavity, through an external incision. The goal is to replace or exchange a drainage device, such as a catheter or drain, that is currently in place to remove fluid or pus from the area. The new device is inserted through the same incision, and the old device is carefully removed to prevent any further complications. This procedure is often performed under general anesthesia or sedation to minimize patient discomfort and ensure a smooth recovery.
