Reposition Left Mandible with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left mandible, or lower jawbone, to its correct position using an internal fixation device. The open approach requires an incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the bone and secure it with a device such as a plate and screws. The internal fixation device is designed to hold the bone in place while it heals, promoting proper alignment and stability. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal jaw function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, difficulty chewing, or limited jaw mobility.
