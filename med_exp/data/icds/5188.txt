Excision of Right Pelvic Bone, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the right pelvic bone, followed by the removal of the affected bone tissue or structure. The goal is to treat conditions such as bone cancer, tumors, or severe fractures that cannot be repaired through other means. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the excision with precision. Afterwards, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for proper healing and recovery.
