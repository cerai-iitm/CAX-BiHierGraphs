Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Ankle Joint, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the orthopedic surgeon exposes the right ankle joint to access the existing internal fixation device, which may include plates, screws, or rods. The device is then carefully disassembled and removed from the ankle joint, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding soft tissue or bone. The removal process may involve dismantling the device piece by piece, or removing it in its entirety, depending on the type and complexity of the fixation device. Once the device is completely removed, the incision is typically closed and the ankle joint is stabilized to promote optimal healing.
