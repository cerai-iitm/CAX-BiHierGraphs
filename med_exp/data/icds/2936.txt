Extirpation of Matter from Lower Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter from the lower esophagus, which is the muscular tube that carries food and liquids from the throat to the stomach. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to access the lower esophagus without making an incision. Using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, the provider visually examines the area and removes the unwanted material. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal functioning of the esophagus and alleviate symptoms such as difficulty swallowing or chest pain.
