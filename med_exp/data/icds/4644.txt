Excision of Left Foot Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissues of the left foot to access the affected tendon. The tendon is then carefully excised, or surgically removed, to correct any damage or disease that may be causing pain, stiffness, or limited mobility in the foot or ankle. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any anatomical issues. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient will typically require a period of rehabilitation to restore strength and function to the affected foot.
