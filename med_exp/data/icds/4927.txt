Supplement Occipital Bone with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the occipital bone, located at the base of the skull, is accessed through an open incision. A synthetic substitute material is then inserted to supplement and support the bone, restoring its natural function and structure. This may be necessary due to defects, injuries, or diseases that have affected the bone's integrity. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms, such as pain or limited mobility, and improve overall quality of life.
