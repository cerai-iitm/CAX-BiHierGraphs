Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Ceramic on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the damaged or diseased right hip joint with a ceramic on polyethylene synthetic substitute, utilizing an open approach. The ceramic component is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the hip joint, while the polyethylene synthetic material provides a durable and wear-resistant surface. The open approach allows the surgeon to access the hip joint directly, enabling precise placement and alignment of the prosthetic components. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, restore mobility, and improve overall quality of life for patients with hip joint damage or degenerative conditions.
