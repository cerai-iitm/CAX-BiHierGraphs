Bypass Pancreas to Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a detour around the pancreas to connect it directly to the small intestine, allowing digestive enzymes to bypass the pancreas and flow directly into the small intestine. The open approach involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the pancreas and small intestine. The bypass is typically performed to treat conditions such as chronic pancreatitis or pancreatic cancer, where the pancreas is damaged or blocked. By rerouting the digestive enzymes, this procedure helps to improve digestion and reduce symptoms such as abdominal pain and malabsorption.
