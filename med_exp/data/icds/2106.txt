Drainage of Lingula Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end through the mouth or nose to visually examine the lingula bronchus, a smaller airway branch in the lung. The purpose of the procedure is to drain accumulated fluid, mucus, or debris from the lingula bronchus, which can be blocking airflow and causing respiratory distress. Using specialized instruments, the blockage is carefully removed through the natural or artificial opening, allowing for improved breathing and relief of symptoms. The diagnostic aspect of the procedure also enables the visualization of any underlying conditions or abnormalities in the lingula bronchus.
