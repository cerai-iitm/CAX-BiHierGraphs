Destruction of Left Atrial Appendage, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a direct, open approach to access the left atrial appendage, a small ear-shaped sac in the upper chamber of the heart. The left atrial appendage is a common site for blood clots to form, which can lead to stroke or other complications. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest and directly visualizes the left atrial appendage to remove or obliterate it, thereby eliminating the risk of clot formation and reducing the risk of stroke. The procedure is often performed in conjunction with other cardiac surgeries, such as heart valve repair or coronary bypass, to provide additional protection against stroke.
