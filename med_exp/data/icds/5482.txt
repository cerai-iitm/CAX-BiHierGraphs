Reposition Right Fibula with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right fibula bone to its normal anatomical position, typically following a fracture or deformity. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the fibula bone. Internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods are then used to stabilize the bone in its new position, promoting proper healing and alignment. The internal fixation device is left in place to provide ongoing support and stability to the bone as it heals.
