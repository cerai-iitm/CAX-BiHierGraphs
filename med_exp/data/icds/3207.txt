Resection of Anus, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical removal of the anus through an open approach, typically performed under general anesthesia. The surgery requires a incision in the perianal region to access the anus, followed by the dissection and removal of the anorectal junction. The procedure may be performed to treat certain conditions such as anal cancer, severe anal trauma, or complex fistulas. The surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored post-operatively for any complications.
