Plain Radiography of Abdominal Aorta using Other Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of a special type of contrast medium to visualize the abdominal aorta, which is the main blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the abdominal organs and lower limbs. The procedure uses plain radiography, a non-invasive imaging modality that produces high-quality images of internal structures, to capture detailed images of the abdominal aorta. The contrast medium is administered through an intravenous injection, which helps to highlight the aorta and its surrounding blood vessels, enabling radiologists to diagnose conditions such as aneurysms, stenosis, or occlusion. The resulting images are then examined by a radiologist to identify any abnormalities or blockages in the abdominal aorta.
