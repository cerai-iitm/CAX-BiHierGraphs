Fluoroscopy of Left Common Carotid Artery
This medical imaging procedure utilizes continuous X-ray beams to generate real-time images of the left common carotid artery, allowing clinicians to visualize blood flow and vessel movement. During the procedure, a contrast agent may be injected to enhance image quality and highlight any blockages or abnormalities. The fluoroscope's video monitor displays the artery's anatomy and dynamics, facilitating diagnoses and guiding interventional procedures such as angioplasty or stenting. The non-invasive nature of fluoroscopy minimizes risks and promotes patient comfort during the examination.
