Restriction of Left Common Iliac Artery with Branched or Fenestrated Intraluminal Device, One or Two Arteries, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the use of a branched or fenestrated intraluminal device to restrict blood flow in the left common iliac artery, a major artery located in the pelvis. The device is inserted through a small incision in the skin and guided to the target site using minimally invasive techniques. The branched or fenestrated design of the device allows for blood flow to be diverted or restricted, reducing the risk of rupture or bleeding in the treated artery. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance to ensure precise placement of the device and minimize complications.
