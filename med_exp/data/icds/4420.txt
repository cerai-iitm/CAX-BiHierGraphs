Excision of Right Trunk Muscle, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This is a minimally invasive procedure performed to remove a portion of the trunk muscle on the right side of the body. The procedure involves making a small incision through the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected muscle. Using specialized instruments, the surgeon carefully removes the targeted section of muscle, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves and blood vessels. The diagnostic nature of this procedure suggests that it may be performed to confirm a suspected diagnosis or to gather more information about the extent of a musculoskeletal condition.
