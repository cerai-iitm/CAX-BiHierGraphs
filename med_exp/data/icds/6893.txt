Drainage of Pericardial Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to remove excess fluid accumulated in the pericardial cavity, a sac that surrounds the heart. A thin tube is guided through a small incision to the pericardial space, allowing for the drainage of fluid and relief of pressure on the heart. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical methods. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device.
