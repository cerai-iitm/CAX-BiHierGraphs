Drainage of Right Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the right main bronchus and remove any accumulated fluids, mucus, or debris through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is guided through the nostrils or mouth, allowing the physician to directly visualize the bronchus and perform the drainage procedure. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and treat respiratory issues, such as bronchiectasis, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), or pneumonia, by relieving airway obstruction and promoting proper drainage. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, and the risks and complications are generally minimal.
