Drainage of Gallbladder with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a drainage device through the skin and into the gallbladder to drain bile and other fluids that have accumulated due to a blockage or obstruction. Under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, a thin tube or catheter is percutaneously inserted into the gallbladder to facilitate drainage. The drainage device is designed to allow for the removal of infected or obstructed bile and promote healing. The percutaneous approach is a minimally invasive method that reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgical intervention.
