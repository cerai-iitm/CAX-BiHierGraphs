Transfer Left Upper Arm Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves transferring a tendon from one location to another in the left upper arm through an open incision. The goal is to restore normal function and relieve symptoms such as pain, weakness, or stiffness in the affected arm. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling precise transfer and reattachment of the tendon. Following the procedure, the patient typically requires a period of rehabilitation to regain strength and range of motion in the affected arm.
