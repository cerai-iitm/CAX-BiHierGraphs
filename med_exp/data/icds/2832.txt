Excision of Duodenum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the minimally invasive removal of a portion of the duodenum, the first part of the small intestine, through a percutaneous endoscopic approach. Using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and surgical instruments, the healthcare provider accesses the duodenum through a small incision in the skin. The damaged or diseased portion of the duodenum is then carefully excised and removed through the endoscope, minimizing the need for open surgery and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and is often used to treat conditions such as duodenal tumors, ulcers, or injuries.
