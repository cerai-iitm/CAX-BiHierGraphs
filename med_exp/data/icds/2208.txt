Excision of Right Middle Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This medical procedure involves the removal of the middle lobe of the right lung through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach. The procedure is performed under endoscopic visualization, allowing the surgeon to access the lung lobe through a small incision. The diagnostic excision is typically done to examine the lung tissue for abnormalities, such as tumors, and to obtain a biopsy sample for further examination. The percutaneous approach enables a faster recovery and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgery.
