Occlusion of Inferior Mesenteric Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an intraluminal device to occlude the inferior mesenteric vein, a blood vessel that supplies the large intestine. The minimally invasive approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster recovery. Under imaging guidance, a catheter is inserted through the skin and navigated to the target site, where the device is deployed to block the flow of blood through the vein. This intervention is typically performed to treat conditions such as portal hypertension or variceal bleeding.
