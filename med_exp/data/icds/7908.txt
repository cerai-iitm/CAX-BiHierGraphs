Computer Assisted Procedure of Trunk Region, With Computerized Tomography
This minimally invasive procedure utilizes advanced computer technology to facilitate precise navigation during a surgical or therapeutic intervention on the trunk region. The assistance of computerized tomography (CT) imaging enables real-time visualization of the internal anatomy, allowing the practitioner to accurately target the treatment area. By combining computer assistance with CT guidance, this procedure enhances the accuracy and safety of the intervention, reducing the risk of complications and promoting optimal outcomes. The fusion of technological advancements and medical expertise enables a more effective and efficient treatment experience for patients undergoing procedures on the trunk region.
