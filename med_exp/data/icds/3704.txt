Excision of Right Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right breast through an open incision, typically performed to treat breast cancer, tumors, or other breast-related abnormalities. The surgery requires a general anesthetic and involves making an incision in the skin to access the breast tissue, which is then carefully dissected and removed. The entire breast, including the nipple and areola, is excised, and the incision is closed with sutures or staples. The goal of the procedure is to completely remove the affected breast tissue and prevent further cancer growth or recurrence.
