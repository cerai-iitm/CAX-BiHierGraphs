Excision of Lesser Omentum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the lesser omentum, a fold of peritoneum that attaches the stomach to the liver. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon carefully dissects and removes the affected portion of the lesser omentum, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and organs. The excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as cancer, inflammation, or injury to the lesser omentum, and to relieve symptoms such as abdominal pain or vomiting. Once the excision is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient is taken to the recovery room for post-operative care.
