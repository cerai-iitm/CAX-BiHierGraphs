Excision of Right Lower Arm and Wrist Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a tendon in the right lower arm and wrist through an open approach, where the skin is incised to access the affected area. The tendon is carefully excised to alleviate conditions such as tendinitis or damage caused by injury or disease. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely locate and remove the affected tendon. Following excision, the incision is closed, and the arm and wrist are immobilized to facilitate healing and promote optimal recovery.
