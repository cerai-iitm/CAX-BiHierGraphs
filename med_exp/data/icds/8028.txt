Fluoroscopy of Left Vertebral Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of low osmolar contrast agent to visualize the left vertebral artery under fluoroscopic guidance. The vertebral artery is a vital vessel that supplies blood to the brain, and this test is often performed to diagnose conditions such as vertebral artery stenosis or occlusion. During the procedure, a small amount of contrast agent is injected into the artery, which allows the artery to be visualized in real-time using X-ray technology. The resulting images provide valuable information to healthcare providers about the morphology and patency of the left vertebral artery.
