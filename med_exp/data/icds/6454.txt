Resection of Left Kidney, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the left kidney through a small incision in the skin using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the kidney and guide the resection. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon uses specialized instruments to dissect and remove the kidney through the small incision. The percutaneous approach reduces tissue damage and promotes faster healing compared to traditional open surgery. The resection of the left kidney is usually performed to treat conditions such as cancer, injury, or disease that affect the kidney's function.
