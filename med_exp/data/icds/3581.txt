Resection of Pituitary Gland, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access the pituitary gland, a small endocrine gland located at the base of the brain. The goal of the surgery is to remove part or all of the pituitary gland, which can be damaged due to tumors, cysts, or other conditions that affect its function. The open approach requires a craniotomy, where a section of the skull is temporarily removed to expose the gland, allowing the surgeon to visualize and carefully dissect the gland and its surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve collaboration between neurosurgeons, endocrinologists, and other medical specialists.
