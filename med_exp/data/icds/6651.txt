Repair Vagina, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the perineum or vulva to gain access to the vagina, allowing the surgeon to repair damaged or defective tissues, muscles, or structures. The external approach enables the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, facilitating the repair of vaginal defects, such as holes, tears, or fistulas. The procedure may also involve the reconstruction of vaginal tissue, correction of vaginal prolapse, or restoration of normal vaginal anatomy. The goal of the surgery is to restore normal vaginal function, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall quality of life for the patient.
