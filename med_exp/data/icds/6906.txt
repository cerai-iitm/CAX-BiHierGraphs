Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the peritoneal cavity, a space between the abdomen and pelvis that contains the digestive organs. A drainage device is then inserted to remove excess fluid or infected material that has accumulated in the cavity, helping to alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain and swelling. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and precisely place the drainage device, ensuring effective removal of the unwanted fluid or material. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any signs of complications or infection.
