Resection of Ileum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the ileum, the lower part of the small intestine, through a minimally invasive approach using an endoscope. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows surgeons to access the ileum through a small incision in the skin, reducing trauma to surrounding tissues and promoting faster recovery. During the procedure, the affected segment of the ileum is identified and resected using specialized instruments, and the remaining portions are reconnected to restore normal intestinal function. The entire process is performed under visual guidance using an endoscope, which provides precise visualization of the internal anatomy.
