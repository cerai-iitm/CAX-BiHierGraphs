Replacement of Left Lower Arm Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Partial Thickness, External Approach
This procedure involves replacing damaged or diseased skin on the left lower arm with a partial thickness autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft made from the patient's own tissue, harvested from another area of the body. The replacement is performed through an external approach, allowing the surgeon to access the affected area directly. The aim is to restore the natural barrier function and aesthetic appearance of the skin, while minimizing scarring and promoting optimal healing. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully selected and prepared to ensure a seamless integration with the surrounding tissue, providing a natural-looking and feeling outcome.
