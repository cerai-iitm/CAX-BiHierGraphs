Excision of Cerebral Hemisphere, Open Approach
This invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of an entire cerebral hemisphere, a drastic measure typically reserved for severe cases of epilepsy, brain tumors, or traumatic brain injuries. The open approach requires a craniotomy, where a section of the skull is temporarily removed to access the brain, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and excise the affected hemisphere. The procedure aims to relieve debilitating symptoms, improve quality of life, and potentially eliminate life-threatening complications. Due to the complexity and high risks involved, this surgery is usually performed by a skilled neurosurgeon in a specialized medical center.
