Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Left Upper Femur, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a small incision is made in the skin above the left upper femur to allow insertion of an internal fixation device. The device is carefully guided into place through the incision using fluoroscopic or radiographic imaging to ensure accurate placement. The internal fixation device is designed to stabilize and support the fractured or weakened bone, promoting healing and restoring function. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and reduces the risk of complications, allowing for a faster recovery and less scarring.
