Resection of Transverse Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdomen to access the transverse colon, which is a section of the large intestine that extends across the upper abdomen. The compromised or diseased portion of the transverse colon is then carefully resected, or removed, to treat conditions such as cancer, diverticulitis, or inflammatory bowel disease. The remaining healthy sections of the colon are then reattached to restore normal bowel function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the colon and surrounding tissue to ensure complete removal of the affected segment.
