Detachment at Left Ring Finger, Complete, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical detachment of the entire left ring finger through an open approach, requiring a skin incision to access the affected area. The goal of the surgery is to completely remove the finger, often performed due to severe injury, infection, or disease that cannot be treated with other methods. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and ligate blood vessels and nerves. The detachment is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the procedure.
