Extirpation of Matter from Coronary Artery, One Artery, Bifurcation, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of obstructive material from a single coronary artery at the point where it diverges into two branches, utilizing a percutaneous approach through the skin. The goal is to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with blocked or narrowed arteries. A specialized catheter is inserted through a small incision and guided to the affected area, where the blockage is then removed or dissolved. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure precise visualization of the artery.
