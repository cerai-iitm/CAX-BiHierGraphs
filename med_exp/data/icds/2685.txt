Dilation of Stomach, Pylorus, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous dilation of the stomach and pylorus is a minimally invasive procedure that helps to widen the narrow passageway between the stomach and small intestine. Through a small incision in the skin, a balloon dilator is guided into the pylorus under fluoroscopic or endoscopic guidance to dilate the narrowed area. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as pyloric stenosis or gastric outlet obstruction, which can cause symptoms like vomiting, nausea, and abdominal pain. By widening the passage, the procedure helps to restore normal digestive function and alleviate symptoms.
