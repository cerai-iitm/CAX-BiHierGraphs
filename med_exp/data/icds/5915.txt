Extirpation of Matter from Lumbosacral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lower back to access the lumbosacral joint, which connects the spine to the pelvis. The purpose of the operation is to remove unwanted material, such as bone fragments or damaged tissue, from within the joint. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the soft tissue to expose the joint, then uses specialized instruments to extract the problematic material. Once the material has been removed, the incision is closed, and the area is allowed to heal.
