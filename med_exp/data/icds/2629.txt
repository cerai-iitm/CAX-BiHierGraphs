Bypass Ileum to Anus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves creating a bypass of the ileum, a part of the small intestine, to the anus using an endoscope, which is a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, inserted through the skin to visualize the internal organs. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural passage of food and waste by reconnecting the small intestine to the anus, allowing for normal bowel function. In this percutaneous endoscopic approach, the surgeon uses specialized instruments and techniques to create a new pathway for waste elimination, minimizing the need for open surgery and promoting faster recovery. This procedure is often performed to treat complex gastrointestinal disorders, such as intestinal obstruction or cancer, and can significantly improve the quality of life for patients with severe digestive issues.
