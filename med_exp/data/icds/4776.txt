Release Right Knee Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to release the right knee bursa and ligament, which are inflamed or damaged tissues that can cause pain, stiffness, and limited mobility in the knee joint. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary decompression to relieve pressure on the surrounding tissues. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and limited range of motion in the right knee. By releasing the affected bursa and ligament, the surgeon can help the patient regain normal knee function and mobility.
