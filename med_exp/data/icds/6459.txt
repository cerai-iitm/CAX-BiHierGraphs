Resection of Left Ureter, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Percutaneous endoscopic resection of the left ureter is a minimally invasive surgical procedure that involves using an endoscope inserted through a small incision in the skin to remove a portion of the ureter. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and involves creating a small hole in the skin and underlying tissues to access the ureter. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and specialized instruments, is then guided through the hole to the affected area, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove the diseased or damaged section of the ureter. Once the resection is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient is taken to the recovery room for postoperative care.
