Replacement of Right Shoulder Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Humeral Surface, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right shoulder joint with a synthetic substitute, specifically focusing on the humeral surface, through an open approach. The humeral surface is a critical component of the shoulder joint, and replacing it with a synthetic substitute can significantly improve joint mobility and reduce pain. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the synthetic substitute and ensure proper alignment. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay, post-operative rehabilitation, and ongoing physical therapy to achieve optimal outcomes.
