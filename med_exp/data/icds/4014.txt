Drainage of Right Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which is the layer of connective tissue surrounding muscles. The goal of the procedure is to drain excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may have accumulated in the tissue and fascia due to injury or infection. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the affected area and remove any debris or infected material, promoting healing and reducing the risk of further complications. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and may require post-operative care to manage pain and promote wound healing.
