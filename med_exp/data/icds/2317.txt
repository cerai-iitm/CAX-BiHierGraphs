Insertion of Endobronchial Valve into Right Middle Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small, one-way valve into the right middle lobe bronchus through a flexible tube called a bronchoscope, inserted through the mouth or nose. The valve is designed to prevent air from entering the lung tissue, allowing the lung to deflate and reduce symptoms of emphysema. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation, and the bronchoscope is equipped with a camera and light to guide the insertion of the valve. The goal of the procedure is to improve lung function, reduce breathlessness, and enhance quality of life for patients with severe emphysema.
