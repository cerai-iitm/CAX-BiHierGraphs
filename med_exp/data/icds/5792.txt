Replacement of Left Shoulder Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Humeral Surface, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left shoulder joint with a synthetic substitute, focusing on the humeral surface, through an open approach. The surgeon makes an incision in the shoulder area to access the damaged joint, removing any diseased or damaged tissue and bone. The synthetic substitute is then carefully inserted and secured to the humerus, restoring the joint's natural function and mobility. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, improve range of motion, and enhance overall quality of life for the patient.
