Supplement Right Inguinal Region with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a synthetic material is used to supplement the right inguinal region, which is the area located in the groin near the thigh crease. This involves making an incision in the skin to access the area and placing the synthetic substitute to provide additional support or reinforcement. The synthetic material is carefully positioned to ensure proper fit and function, and the incision is then closed to allow for healing. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as hernias or tissue defects in the inguinal region.
