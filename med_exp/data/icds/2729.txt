Drainage of Stomach with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through a natural or artificial opening to drain the stomach, providing relief from obstruction or blockage. The device is guided through the opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the area. The drainage device is then placed to drain fluid, gas, or other substances from the stomach, restoring normal digestive function. The procedure is typically performed under sedation to minimize discomfort and is often used to treat conditions such as gastric volvulus, pyloric stenosis, or gastric cancer.
