Excision of Left Femoral Shaft, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left thigh to access the femoral shaft, which is the long bone in the upper leg. The goal of the excision is to remove a portion of the femoral shaft for diagnostic purposes, allowing for further examination and evaluation. This open approach provides direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify any abnormalities or lesions. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further testing and analysis, helping to guide treatment decisions and facilitate a more definitive diagnosis.
