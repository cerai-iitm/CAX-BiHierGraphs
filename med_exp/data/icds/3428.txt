Excision of Pancreas, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the pancreas, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect and remove a portion of the pancreas for diagnostic purposes. The open approach provides direct visualization of the pancreas, enabling the surgeon to identify any abnormalities, such as tumors, cysts, or inflammation. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the pancreas from surrounding tissues and blood vessels to ensure minimal damage to surrounding structures. Following excision of the pancreatic tissue, the incision is closed, and the tissue is sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in diagnosis.
