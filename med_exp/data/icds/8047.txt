Fluoroscopy of Cervico-Cerebral Arch using Other Contrast
This radiologic procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the cervico-cerebral arch, a critical region where the cervical spine meets the base of the brain. During the procedure, a contrast agent other than the standard options is utilized to enhance image quality, allowing for a more detailed examination of the blood vessels and surrounding structures. The fluoroscope emits continuous X-rays, which are then converted into real-time images, enabling the physician to assess the anatomy and identify any potential abnormalities or blockages. This diagnostic examination plays a vital role in guiding further treatment or surgical interventions related to the cervico-cerebral region.
