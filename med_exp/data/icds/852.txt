Repair Left Ulnar Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left arm to access the ulnar artery, which supplies blood to the forearm and hand. The damaged or injured section of the artery is then repaired using sutures or grafts to restore normal blood flow. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve temporarily stopping blood flow to the affected area to facilitate repair. After the repair is complete, the incision is closed and the patient is monitored for any complications or bleeding.
