Excision of Bladder, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the bladder, which is then carefully dissected and removed. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the bladder and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and excise the affected area. The diagnostic nature of this procedure means that it is typically performed to obtain a tissue sample for further examination, often to confirm a cancer diagnosis or rule out other potential causes of symptoms. The bladder is then carefully closed, and the incision site is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing.
