Bypass Coronary Artery, One Artery from Right Internal Mammary with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
During this open-chest surgery, a section of the right internal mammary artery is used to create a bypass around a blocked coronary artery, restoring blood flow to the heart. The autologous venous tissue, typically taken from the patient's leg, is used as a graft to extend the mammary artery, allowing it to bypass the blocked section. The procedure involves making an incision in the chest, exposing the heart, and carefully constructing the bypass using the graft material. The goal of the procedure is to improve blood flow to the heart, relieving symptoms such as chest pain and shortness of breath.
