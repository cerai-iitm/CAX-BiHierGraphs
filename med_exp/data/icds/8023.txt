Fluoroscopy of Bilateral External Carotid Arteries using Other Contrast
This radiological procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the bilateral external carotid arteries, which are responsible for supplying oxygenated blood to the head and neck region. To enhance image quality, a contrast agent other than the standard iodine-based dye is administered to the patient. During the procedure, continuous X-ray beams are transmitted through the body, and the resulting images are captured in real-time, allowing the healthcare provider to assess the blood vessels and diagnose any potential blockages or abnormalities. The procedure is typically performed in a hospital or outpatient imaging center under the guidance of a trained radiologist or interventional radiologist.
