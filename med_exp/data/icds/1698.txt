Restriction of Gastric Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a specialized device through the skin and into the gastric vein to restrict blood flow, thereby reducing the risk of bleeding or other complications. The intraluminal device is carefully guided into place using imaging technology to ensure precise placement. Once in position, the device constricts the vein, reducing blood flow and pressure on the surrounding tissues. This percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to traditional open surgical methods.
