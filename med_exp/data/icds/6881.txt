Drainage of Left Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin and into the left pleural cavity to remove excess fluid, blood, or air that has accumulated between the lungs and chest wall. Under imaging guidance, the healthcare provider makes a small incision in the chest wall and advances the tube into the pleural space to facilitate drainage. The tube is then connected to a drainage system, allowing the collected fluid to be monitored and removed as needed. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, chest pain, and coughing associated with pleural effusion or pneumothorax.
