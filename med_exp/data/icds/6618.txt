Release Uterine Supporting Structure, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visualize the uterine supporting structures, allowing the surgeon to identify and release any adhesions or constrictions that may be contributing to pelvic pain or other symptoms. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the surgeon to carefully dissect and free the affected structures, restoring normal anatomy and function. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms and improve quality of life for women with uterine support-related disorders.
