Bypass Ileum to Transverse Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass between the ileum, the lower part of the small intestine, and the transverse colon, the middle part of the large intestine, using an open approach. The surgery is typically performed to redirect the flow of food and waste around a diseased or obstructed section of intestine, allowing the patient to regain normal bowel function. The open approach involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the issue. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting, and to improve the patient's overall quality of life.
