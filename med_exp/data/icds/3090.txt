Removal of Feeding Device from Lower Intestinal Tract, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove a feeding device, such as a gastrostomy tube, from the lower intestinal tract. The procedure is typically performed in an operating room under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. The surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the feeding device and carefully removes it from the intestinal tract, taking care to minimize tissue damage and bleeding. After the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
