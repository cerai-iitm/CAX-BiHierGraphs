Reposition Right Metacarpal, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue of the hand to access the right metacarpal bone. The goal of the operation is to reposition the metacarpal bone, which is one of the five long bones in the hand, to its normal anatomical position. This may be necessary to treat conditions such as fractures, dislocations, or bone deformities that affect the hand's function and mobility. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissues, ensuring a precise repositioning of the metacarpal bone to restore proper hand function.
