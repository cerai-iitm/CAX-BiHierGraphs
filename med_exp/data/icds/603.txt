Supplement Left Ventricle with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During open-heart surgery, a biological tissue substitute is implanted to supplement the left ventricle, enhancing its structure and function. This procedure aims to improve cardiac function and alleviate symptoms associated with left ventricular dysfunction. The autologous tissue substitute, derived from the patient's own tissue, is carefully placed to reinforce the ventricular wall, promoting more efficient blood pumping. This complex surgical intervention requires a skilled cardiothoracic surgeon and a multidisciplinary team to ensure optimal outcomes.
