Detachment at Left 1st Toe, Complete, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the left 1st toe from the surrounding tissue and bone, performed through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. The detachment process involves separating the toe from the surrounding soft tissue, ligaments, and bones, requiring careful handling to avoid causing further damage. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and dissect the anatomical structures involved. Following detachment, the wound is typically closed, and the patient may require post-operative care to facilitate healing and prevent complications.
