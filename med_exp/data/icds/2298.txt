Extraction of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the left lower lung lobe and remove tissue or lesions through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and specialized instruments, is inserted through the mouth or a small incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the lung tissue and perform the extraction. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be accompanied by diagnostic tests, such as biopsies, to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of lung conditions. The goal of the procedure is to remove abnormal tissue or lesions while preserving as much healthy lung tissue as possible.
