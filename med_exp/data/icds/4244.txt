Repair Right Upper Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the right upper arm, allowing the surgeon to repair damaged or injured tissue and restore normal anatomy and function. The open approach enables direct visualization of the affected area, facilitating precise repair and minimizing further trauma. The surgical repair may involve techniques such as debridement, suturing, or grafting to restore the integrity of the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms, improve range of motion, and promote proper healing in the affected upper arm.
