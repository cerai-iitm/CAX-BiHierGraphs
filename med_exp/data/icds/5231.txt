Excision of Left Fibula, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lower leg to access the left fibula, a smaller bone in the lower leg that runs parallel to the tibia. The fibula is then carefully excised, or removed, from the surrounding tissue and bone. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and safely remove the fibula, which may be necessary due to trauma, tumor, or other conditions that affect the bone. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve a hospital stay for recovery and rehabilitation.
