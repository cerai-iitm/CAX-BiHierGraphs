Revision of Cardiac Lead in Heart, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the heart and manipulate or replace a malfunctioning or damaged cardiac lead, which is a thin wire that connects a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator to the heart. The surgeon will carefully expose the lead and remove any scar tissue that may be holding it in place, then carefully extract the lead from the heart tissue, and if necessary, replace it with a new one. The new lead is then securely fastened to the heart tissue and the incision is closed. The goal of this revision is to restore proper functioning of the cardiac device and ensure the patient's heart rhythm is regulated correctly.
