Resection of Right Large Intestine, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure based on the code and long title:

This invasive surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdominal wall to access the right large intestine, also known as the ascending colon. The damaged or diseased portion of the intestine is carefully identified and removed, and the healthy ends are reattached to restore normal bowel function. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually exam the intestine and surrounding tissue to ensure complete removal of the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a temporary colostomy to divert intestinal contents during the healing process.
