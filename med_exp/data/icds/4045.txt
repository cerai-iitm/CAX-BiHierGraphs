Excision of Abdomen Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdominal area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue just beneath the skin. The objective is to excise, or remove, a portion of these tissues for diagnostic purposes, such as to examine for abnormal cell growth or infection. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the targeted tissue. By doing so, the procedure aids in determining the underlying cause of a patient's abdominal symptoms or abnormality.
