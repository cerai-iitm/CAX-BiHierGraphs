Repair Abdominal Wall, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access and repair the damaged or weakened area. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, enabling a more comprehensive repair. The goal of the procedure is to restore the integrity of the abdominal wall, alleviating symptoms such as pain, bulging, or herniation. The surgeon will use sutures, mesh, or other materials to reinforce the abdominal wall, promoting strength and stability.
