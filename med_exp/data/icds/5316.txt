Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Right Fibula, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a internal fixation device into the right fibula bone through a small incision in the skin, using a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The internal fixation device is designed to provide stability and support to the fractured or weakened bone, promoting proper healing and alignment. The percutaneous approach allows for reduced tissue trauma and less post-operative pain compared to traditional open surgical methods. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the fixation device.
