Excision of Left Lower Femur, Open Approach, Diagnostic
The surgical removal of a portion of the left lower femur is performed through an open approach, allowing the surgeon to visualize the affected area directly. This diagnostic excision is typically indicated for conditions such as tumors, infections, or deformities that require a tissue sample for further examination. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the left lower femur, carefully excising the affected portion while minimizing damage to surrounding tissues. Once the excised tissue is removed, it is sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in diagnosis and treatment.
