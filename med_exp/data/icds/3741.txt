Extraction of Left Lower Arm Skin, External Approach
Here is a detailed description tailored to the code:

This surgical procedure involves making an external incision in the skin of the left lower arm to access the underlying tissue. The surgery is performed to excise or remove a portion of the skin, which may be damaged, diseased, or affected by a lesion. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and carefully dissect the skin to remove the targeted tissue. The procedure is typically performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health.
