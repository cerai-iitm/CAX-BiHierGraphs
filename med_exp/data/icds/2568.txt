Replacement of Tongue with Autologous Tissue Substitute, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically replacing a portion of the tongue with a substitute made from the patient's own tissue. The external approach is used to access the tongue, allowing the surgeon to remove the damaged or diseased section and replace it with the autologous tissue substitute. This complex procedure requires careful planning and execution to ensure the substitute tissue is properly anchored and functional. Post-operative care and rehabilitation are critical to achieving optimal speech and swallowing outcomes.
