Supplement Right Abdomen Muscle with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right abdomen to access the affected muscle tissue. A synthetic substitute material is then implanted to supplement and reinforce the weakened or damaged muscle, restoring its natural function and structure. The open approach allows for direct visualization and precise placement of the synthetic material, ensuring optimal results. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the muscle, alleviate pain, and improve overall abdominal function and mobility.
