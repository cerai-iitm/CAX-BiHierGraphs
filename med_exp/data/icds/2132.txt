Drainage of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the mouth or nose to reach the left lower lung lobe, allowing the doctor to visualize the area and identify the source of the problem. A drainage tube or device is then inserted through the natural or artificial opening to drain excess fluid, pus, or blood that may be accumulated in the lung lobe. The endoscope guides the drainage procedure, enabling the doctor to accurately target the affected area and avoid damaging surrounding tissue. The goal of the procedure is to relieve respiratory distress, promote healing, and prevent further complications.
