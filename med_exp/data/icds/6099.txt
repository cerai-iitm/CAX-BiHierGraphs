Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Ceramic Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the diseased or damaged right hip joint is removed and replaced with a ceramic synthetic substitute, which is secured in place using cement. The open approach indicates that the surgery involves a larger incision to access the hip joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area directly. The ceramic material used in the substitute is biocompatible and designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the original hip joint. The cemented fixation ensures a strong and stable bond between the implant and the surrounding bone, providing optimal support and stability for the patient.
