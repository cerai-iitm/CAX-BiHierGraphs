Supplement Left Lower Lobe Bronchus with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the chest cavity to access the left lower lobe bronchus, a vital airway that supplies oxygen to the lungs. The surgeon then supplements the bronchus with autologous tissue, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body, to repair or replace damaged or diseased areas. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the bronchus and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to meticulously restore normal function and promote optimal breathing. The goal of this procedure is to improve lung function, alleviate respiratory symptoms, and enhance the patient's overall quality of life.
