Dilation of Left Popliteal Artery with Two Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of two intraluminal devices into the left popliteal artery to dilate the vessel and improve blood flow. The devices are introduced through a small incision in the skin, using a percutaneous approach, and guided to the affected area using fluoroscopic imaging. The dilation process helps to widen the artery, relieving any blockages or narrowing, and restoring blood flow to the lower leg. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may take around 30-60 minutes to complete.
