Supplement Cervical Vertebral Joint with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the cervical spine to access the affected vertebral joint, which is then supplemented with a non-autologous tissue substitute, such as a bone graft or artificial disc, to restore stability and alleviate pain. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding anatomy, ensuring precise placement of the tissue substitute. The goal of this procedure is to promote healing, improve spinal alignment, and enhance range of motion in the cervical spine. By using a non-autologous tissue substitute, the risk of rejection or donor-site morbidity is minimized, offering a viable solution for patients requiring vertebral joint supplementation.
