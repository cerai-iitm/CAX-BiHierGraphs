Fluoroscopy of Right Internal Carotid Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
During this non-invasive medical imaging procedure, a low-osmolar contrast medium is injected into the right internal carotid artery to visualize its internal structures. The contrast medium helps to highlight the artery and its branches, allowing for clear visualization of any blockages, stenosis, or other abnormalities. Fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, is used to guide the injection of the contrast medium and capture real-time images of the artery. The resulting images help healthcare providers diagnose and treat conditions such as cerebral vasculature disorders, carotid artery stenosis, and cerebral ischemia.
