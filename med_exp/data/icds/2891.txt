Excision of Sigmoid Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the sigmoid colon through an endoscopic approach, which utilizes a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end to visualize the colon through a natural or artificial opening. The sigmoid colon is a portion of the large intestine that is responsible for absorbing water and storing feces until they are eliminated from the body. During the excision, the surgeon will make an incision in the abdominal wall, allowing for the insertion of the endoscope, and will then carefully dissect and remove the affected portion of the sigmoid colon. The diagnostic nature of this procedure allows the surgeon to examine the colon and surrounding tissues for any abnormalities or disease.
