Destruction of Left Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the destruction of the left main bronchus, a major airway that supplies oxygen to the lungs, using an endoscope inserted through a natural or artificial opening. The destruction is achieved through various methods, such as electrocautery, cryotherapy, or laser ablation, under direct visualization through the endoscope. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, where the tumor is located in the left main bronchus and is causing symptoms like coughing, shortness of breath, or hemoptysis. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms and improve quality of life in patients with advanced lung disease.
