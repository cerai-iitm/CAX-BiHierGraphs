Detachment at Left 1st Toe, High, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical detachment of the left first toe at a high level, typically performed through an open approach, where the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area. The high-level detachment implies that the toe is being separated from the foot at a point closer to the metatarsal bone, rather than at the more distal phalanges. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures and enables the surgeon to carefully dissect and ligate the blood vessels and nerves. The goal of this procedure is to remove the toe due to injury, disease, or other conditions that render it non-viable or severely compromised.
