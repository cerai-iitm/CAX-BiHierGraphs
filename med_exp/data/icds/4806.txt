Excision of Skull, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves using a small incision or puncture in the skin to access the skull, allowing for the removal of a small sample of skull tissue or bone for diagnostic examination. The percutaneous approach is typically done under local anesthesia and imaging guidance, such as CT or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate targeting of the affected area. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further analysis to help diagnose conditions such as bone infections, tumors, or metabolic disorders. The procedure is usually performed on an outpatient basis and can provide valuable information to guide further treatment.
