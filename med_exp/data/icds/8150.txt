Fluoroscopy of Inferior Vena Cava
This medical imaging procedure utilizes continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time images of the inferior vena cava, the largest vein in the body that carries deoxygenated blood from the lower body to the heart. During the procedure, the patient is positioned on an examination table and a contrast agent is injected into a vein to highlight the inferior vena cava and its surrounding structures. The fluoroscope, a special X-ray machine, is then used to take continuous images of the vein as it moves, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize its anatomy and function. This non-invasive test is used to guide minimally invasive procedures, diagnose vascular conditions, and monitor the effectiveness of treatments.
