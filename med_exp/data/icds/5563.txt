Supplement Left Metatarsal with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this procedure, a surgeon makes an open incision in the left foot to access the metatarsal bone, which is one of the five long bones in the midfoot. The goal is to supplement the bone with an autologous tissue substitute, which is a biocompatible material derived from the patient's own tissues. This may involve harvesting tissue from another part of the body, such as bone marrow or platelet-rich plasma, and processing it to create a suitable graft. The substitute is then implanted into the metatarsal bone to promote healing, stability, and strength.
