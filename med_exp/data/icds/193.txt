Supplement Dura Mater with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the supplementation of the dura mater, a protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord, with a synthetic substitute material. The open approach requires a craniotomy or incision to access the dura mater, allowing the surgeon to repair or replace damaged areas with the synthetic material. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural barrier function of the dura mater, which can be compromised due to injury, infection, or congenital defects. By using a synthetic substitute, the procedure provides a safe and effective way to promote healing and protect the delicate tissues of the central nervous system.
