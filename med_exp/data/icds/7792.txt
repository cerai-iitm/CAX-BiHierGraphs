Measurement of Arterial Pressure, Coronary, Percutaneous Approach
This invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through the skin and into a coronary artery to directly measure arterial pressure. The catheter is guided through the artery using fluoroscopy or X-ray imaging. Once in place, the catheter transmits pressure readings to a monitor, allowing healthcare providers to accurately assess coronary artery function and diagnose conditions such as coronary artery disease or stenosis. The results of the procedure can inform treatment decisions, including the need for interventions such as angioplasty or stenting.
