Respiratory Ventilation, 24-96 Consecutive Hours
This procedure involves the use of mechanical ventilation to support breathing for a prolonged period of time, typically between 24 to 96 hours. During this time, the patient's lungs are assisted by a ventilator, which delivers a constant flow of oxygen-rich air to ensure adequate oxygenation of the blood. The ventilator may be set to deliver a specific tidal volume, rate, and oxygen concentration to meet the patient's individual needs. The patient is closely monitored by healthcare professionals to ensure the ventilator is functioning properly and making adjustments as needed to maintain optimal respiratory function.
