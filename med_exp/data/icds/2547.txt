Repair Upper Lip, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the upper lip to access the underlying tissue and repair any defects or damage. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully repair any lacerations, tears, or other injuries. The procedure may involve reapproximating tissue layers, repairing or replacing damaged skin, and closing the incision to restore the natural appearance and function of the upper lip. The goal of this procedure is to restore form and function to the upper lip, improving overall facial aesthetics and oral function.
