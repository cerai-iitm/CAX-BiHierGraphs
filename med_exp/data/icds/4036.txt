Excision of Left Neck Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the left side of the neck through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The procedure requires careful dissection to separate the subcutaneous tissue and fascia from surrounding structures, ensuring minimal damage to adjacent tissues. The excised tissue is then removed, and the incision site is closed, typically with sutures or staples, to promote healing. This procedure may be performed to treat various conditions, including tumors, infections, or trauma, and is often performed under general anesthesia.
