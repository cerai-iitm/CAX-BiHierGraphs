Excision of Right Lower Leg Muscle, Open Approach
Surgical excision of the right lower leg muscle involves an open approach, where a skin incision is made to access the affected muscle tissue. The damaged or diseased muscle is then carefully isolated and removed, taking care to preserve surrounding nerves, blood vessels, and other vital structures. This procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as muscle tumors, injuries, or infections, and is often necessary to prevent further complications or improve mobility and function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the affected muscle tissue.
