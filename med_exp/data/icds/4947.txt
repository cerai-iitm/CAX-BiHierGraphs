Excision of Sternum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the sternum, a long, flat bone located in the center of the chest. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon uses specialized instruments to carefully excise the affected portion of the sternum. The surgery is often necessary to treat conditions such as tumors, infections, or deformities of the sternum. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms, with post-operative care involving close monitoring and management of pain and wound healing.
