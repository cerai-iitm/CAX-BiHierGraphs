Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Female Reproductive, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This medical procedure involves the introduction of therapeutic substances into the female reproductive system through natural or artificial openings, aiming to treat various conditions such as infections, inflammation, or hormonal imbalances. The procedure is performed under sterile conditions to minimize the risk of complications. The therapeutic substance, which may be in the form of a medication, hormone, or other agent, is administered directly into the reproductive tract, allowing for targeted and localized treatment. Through this procedure, healthcare providers can effectively manage a range of female reproductive health issues, promoting optimal wellness and fertility.
