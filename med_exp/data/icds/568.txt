Replacement of Mitral Valve with Zooplastic Tissue, Percutaneous Approach
In this minimally invasive procedure, a zooplastic tissue valve is implanted in the mitral position through a percutaneous approach, eliminating the need for open-heart surgery. The procedure involves accessing the mitral valve through a small incision in the leg or arm, and guiding the valve replacement device through a catheter to the mitral valve location. Once in place, the zooplastic tissue valve is expanded and secured, allowing for proper blood flow between the heart's left atrium and ventricle. The percutaneous approach reduces recovery time and minimizes the risk of complications compared to traditional open-heart surgery.
