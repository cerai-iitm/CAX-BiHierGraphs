Restriction of Abdominal Aorta, Bifurcation, with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a small tube, or catheter, inserted through the skin and guided to the abdominal aorta, a major blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the abdominal organs and lower limbs. A small intraluminal device, such as a stent or stent-graft, is then advanced through the catheter and positioned at the bifurcation point of the abdominal aorta, where it divides into two smaller arteries. The device is deployed and expanded to restrict blood flow and treat conditions such as aneurysms or arterial stenosis. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the device.
