Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a synthetic substitute, such as an artificial joint or implant, from the left knee joint through an open incision. The goal is to restore normal knee function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, or limited mobility caused by the defective or damaged implant. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left knee to access the affected area, carefully removes the synthetic substitute, and may also repair or replace surrounding tissues as needed. The removal of the synthetic substitute can significantly improve the patient's quality of life and overall knee health.
