Insertion of External Fixation Device into Left Patella, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to expose the left patella, also known as the kneecap. An external fixation device is then inserted into the patella to provide stability and support to the affected area. The device is typically attached to the bone using pins or screws and remains in place until the bone has fully healed. This open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly and ensuring proper placement of the device for optimal results.
