Supplement Trachea with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves the supplementation of the trachea with a synthetic substitute material through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the trachea. The synthetic material is used to replace or augment the natural tracheal tissue, restoring the function and structure of the airway. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as tracheal stenosis, tracheomalacia, or tracheal tumors, which can cause breathing difficulties or obstruction. The open approach allows for direct visualization and access to the trachea, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the synthetic substitute and ensure a secure and effective repair.
