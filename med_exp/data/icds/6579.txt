Extirpation of Matter from Cervix, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the removal of abnormal or unwanted material from the cervix, which is the lower part of the uterus that opens into the vagina. The extirpation is performed through the natural opening of the cervix or through an artificial opening created for the purpose of the procedure. The goal of this procedure is to treat cervical abnormalities, such as cervical dysplasia or cervical cancer, by removing the affected tissue. The extirpation of matter from the cervix is typically performed under anesthesia, and the removed tissue is sent for further examination to determine the cause of the abnormality.
