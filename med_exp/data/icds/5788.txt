Replacement of Right Shoulder Joint with Reverse Ball and Socket Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the damaged or arthritic right shoulder joint is replaced with a reverse ball and socket synthetic substitute, a type of implant designed to reverse the normal anatomy of the shoulder joint, where the ball is attached to the shoulder blade and the socket is attached to the upper arm bone. The open approach involves a larger incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the replacement. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement of the shoulder joint, providing increased stability and mobility. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, restore function, and improve overall quality of life for patients with severe shoulder joint damage or arthritis.
