Extraction of Right Upper Leg Muscle, Open Approach
Surgical excision of the right upper leg muscle is performed through an open approach, involving a skin incision to access the affected muscle tissue. This procedure is often necessary to treat conditions such as muscular tumors, trauma, or infections that require removal of damaged or diseased muscle tissue. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects and excises the affected muscle, taking care to preserve surrounding structures and minimize tissue damage. The incision is then closed, and the leg is stabilized to promote proper healing and restoration of muscle function.
