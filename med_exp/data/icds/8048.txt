Fluoroscopy of Intracranial Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure utilizes low osmolar contrast to visualize the intracranial arteries, providing detailed images of blood vessels within the brain. During the procedure, a low-osmolar contrast agent is injected into a vein, and a fluoroscope is used to guide the flow of the contrast medium through the arteries, allowing for real-time imaging. This helps radiologists and clinicians to diagnose and monitor various cerebrovascular conditions, such as aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, and stroke.
