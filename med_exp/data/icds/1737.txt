Excision of Thorax Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of abnormal or diseased lymphatic tissue in the thorax region through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The thorax lymphatic system plays a crucial role in the body's immune function, and excision of diseased tissue helps to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the affected tissue. The goal of this procedure is to improve lymphatic flow and reduce the risk of complications, such as infection or cancer.
