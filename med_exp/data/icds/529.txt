Removal of Infusion Device from Great Vessel, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of an infusion device, such as a catheter or port, from a great vessel, which refers to a major blood vessel that arises from the aortic arch. The percutaneous approach means that the removal is done through the skin, eliminating the need for open surgery. The procedure typically involves making a small incision in the skin, locating the infusion device, and carefully removing it from the vessel. The removal process may require fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance to ensure safe and successful extraction.
