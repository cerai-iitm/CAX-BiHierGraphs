Replacement of Left Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the left hand are replaced with a substitute derived from the patient's own body. The open approach involves a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to remove the damaged tissue and fascia before implanting the autologous tissue substitute. This procedure aims to restore normal hand function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or limited mobility. The use of autologous tissue minimizes the risk of rejection and promotes optimal healing and tissue integration.
