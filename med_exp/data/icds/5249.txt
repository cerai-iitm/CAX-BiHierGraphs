Excision of Coccyx, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the coccyx, also known as the tailbone, through small incisions using an endoscope. The surgeon uses a thin, lighted tube with a camera to visualize the area and carefully dissect the surrounding tissue to access the coccyx. The coccyx is then excised, and the incisions are closed, typically with absorbable sutures. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for less tissue damage, reduced scarring, and a faster recovery compared to open surgical techniques.
