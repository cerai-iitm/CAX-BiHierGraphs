Reposition Left Lower Femur with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the patient is given general anesthesia to ensure comfort and relaxation. The surgeon makes an incision in the left lower femur to access the affected area, and then manually repositions the bone to its normal alignment. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are implanted to stabilize the bone and promote healing. Once the bone is secured, the incision is closed, and the patient is taken to the recovery room for post-operative care.
