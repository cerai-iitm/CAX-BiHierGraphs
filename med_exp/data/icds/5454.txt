Reposition Left Patella with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left patella to its normal anatomical position through an open approach, utilizing an internal fixation device to stabilize the bone. The patella, or kneecap, is relocated to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility caused by patellar malalignment. The internal fixation device, which may consist of screws, plates, or wires, is carefully inserted to hold the patella in place, allowing for proper healing and restoration of normal knee function. Post-operatively, physical therapy and rehabilitation are typically necessary to regain strength and mobility in the affected knee.
