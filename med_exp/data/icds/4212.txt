Removal of Monitoring Device from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the trunk area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where a monitoring device is implanted. The device is carefully disconnected from the surrounding tissue and fascia, taking care not to cause any damage. The device is then gently removed from the body, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote a smooth recovery.
