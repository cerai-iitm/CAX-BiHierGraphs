Reposition Right Tarsal, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue of the right foot to access the tarsal bones. The tarsal bones are then manually repositioned to restore their normal anatomical alignment, which can help to relieve pain and discomfort caused by misalignment. The bones are stabilized in their new position using internal fixation devices, such as plates or screws, to promote healing and prevent further displacement. After the procedure, the incision is closed, and the foot is immobilized to allow the bones to heal in their new position.
