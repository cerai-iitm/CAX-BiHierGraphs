Extirpation of Matter from Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves removing unwanted substance or debris from the trachea, or windpipe, through either a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope allows the healthcare provider to visualize the trachea and removal of the unwanted substance is done under direct visualization. This may be performed to clear the airway of foreign objects, blood, or other debris that is obstructing breathing or causing respiratory distress. The goal of this procedure is to restore a clear airway and improve breathing.
