Release Cecum, Open Approach
This surgical intervention involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the cecum, a pouch-like structure at the beginning of the large intestine. The procedure is performed to relieve any obstruction, adhesions, or compression that may be affecting the normal functioning of the cecum. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly, enabling precise dissection and release of the cecum from any constricting tissues. Once released, the cecum is restored to its normal position, and the abdominal incision is closed, promoting healing and recovery.
