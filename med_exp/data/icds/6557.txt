Excision of Cul-de-sac, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the cul-de-sac, a potential space between the pelvic viscera and the peritoneum. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the area and remove any abnormal tissue, masses, or lesions that may be present. The diagnostic excision aims to identify any underlying conditions or diseases, such as endometriosis, adhesions, or malignancies, that may be affecting the patient's health. The removed tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
