Extirpation of Matter from Coronary Artery, One Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of unwanted substances from a single coronary artery, using an open approach. The goal is to restore blood flow and prevent further blockages or damage to the artery. The procedure typically involves making an incision in the chest and using specialized instruments to locate and extract the obstructing material. By clearing the artery, the risk of heart attack or other serious cardiac complications is significantly reduced.
