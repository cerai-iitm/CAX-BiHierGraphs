Occlusion of Left Atrial Appendage with Extraluminal Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical closure of the left atrial appendage using an extraluminal device, which is implanted through an open surgical approach. The device is designed to occlude the left atrial appendage, a small pouch-like structure in the upper chamber of the heart, to reduce the risk of stroke and bleeding complications. The procedure is typically performed in patients with atrial fibrillation who are at high risk of stroke or bleeding. The device is implanted directly onto the left atrial appendage, and once in place, it helps to prevent blood clots from forming and reduces the risk of stroke and bleeding.
