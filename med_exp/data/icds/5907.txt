Excision of Right Ankle Joint, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves an open approach to the right ankle joint, where the surgeon makes an incision to access the joint. The goal of the procedure is to remove damaged or diseased tissue, bone, or other structures within the ankle joint, with the aim of restoring function and alleviating pain. The excision process typically involves careful dissection and removal of the affected areas, followed by thorough cleaning and closure of the incision site. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as osteoarthritis, joint instability, or infection.
