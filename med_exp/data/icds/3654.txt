Drainage of Left Breast, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin and into the left breast to remove excess fluid or infected material. The procedure is usually performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage tube. The tube is left in place until the infection clears or the fluid collection resolves, and it may be connected to a drainage bag or a suction device. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms, promote healing, and prevent further complications.
