Resection of Appendix, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the appendix and guide the surgical instruments to remove it through a small incision in the skin. The surgeon makes a few tiny incisions in the abdomen to insert the endoscope and specialized instruments, allowing for precise dissection and removal of the inflamed or infected appendix. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the entire process usually takes around 1-2 hours to complete. After the procedure, the tiny incisions are closed, and the patient can expect a shorter hospital stay and faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery.
