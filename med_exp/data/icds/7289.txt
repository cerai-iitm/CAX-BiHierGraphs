Detachment at Left 1st Toe, Low, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the left first toe at a low level, typically performed to treat severe injuries, deformities, or diseases affecting the toe. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the toe and surrounding tissues. The detachment is done at a low level, which means the separation occurs closer to the base of the toe rather than at a more distal point. The goal of this procedure is to remove the affected toe and promote healing, alleviate pain, and improve mobility in the affected foot.
