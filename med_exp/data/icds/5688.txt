Fusion of Cervicothoracic Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically fusing the cervicothoracic vertebral joint, which connects the neck and upper back, using an interbody fusion device. The anterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the spine from the front, typically through an incision in the chest or neck. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the spine and implant the device, which is designed to stabilize the joint and promote bone growth. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness caused by spinal instability or degenerative conditions.
