Supplement Right Internal Carotid Artery with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the right internal carotid artery to supplement it with a non-autologous tissue substitute, typically used to repair or replace damaged sections of the artery. The substitute material, which is not derived from the patient's own body, is carefully inserted into the artery to restore blood flow and promote healing. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the artery and perform the necessary repairs. The goal of this procedure is to improve blood flow to the brain and reduce the risk of stroke or other complications.
