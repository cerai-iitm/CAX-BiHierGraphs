Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of multiple lumbar vertebral joints to stabilize the spine and alleviate chronic back pain or deformity. The procedure is performed through an open posterior approach, which allows the surgeon to access the spine from the back. An interbody fusion device is inserted to promote bone growth and fusion between the affected vertebrae, and the anterior column is stabilized to ensure proper alignment and support. The goal of this procedure is to restore spinal stability, reduce pain, and improve mobility and quality of life for the patient.
