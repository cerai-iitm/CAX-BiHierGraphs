Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Humeral Head, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical removal of an internal fixation device from the right humeral head through an open approach, which allows direct visualization of the affected area. The goal is to safely extract the device, which may have been implanted to stabilize a fracture or other bone-related issue, once the region has fully healed. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the humeral head, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the device. Following the procedure, the surgical site is closed, and the patient can begin the recovery process.
