Reposition Left Fibula with External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the repositioning of the left fibula, a bone in the lower leg, to its normal anatomical position using an external fixation device. The percutaneous approach means that the procedure is performed through small incisions in the skin, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. The external fixation device is attached to the bone through pins or screws and remains outside the skin, providing structural support and stability to facilitate proper healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore the normal function and alignment of the fibula, alleviating pain and discomfort while promoting optimal recovery.
