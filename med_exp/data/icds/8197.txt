Fluoroscopy of Diaphragm
This non-invasive imaging procedure utilizes continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time images of the diaphragm, a dome-shaped muscle that separates the chest cavity from the abdominal cavity, to assess its movement and function. The fluoroscopy machine is positioned to capture images of the diaphragm from various angles, allowing healthcare professionals to evaluate its contractions and relaxations. The test is often used to diagnose conditions such as diaphragmatic paralysis or weakness, which can cause breathing difficulties. The procedure is usually performed under the guidance of a trained radiologist or healthcare professional, ensuring accurate and safe imaging of the diaphragm.
