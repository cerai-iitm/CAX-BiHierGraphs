Excision of Bilateral Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both breasts through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the breasts. The goal of this procedure is often to treat or prevent breast cancer, or to alleviate symptoms associated with breast hypertrophy or macromastia. During the procedure, the surgeon will make an incision in each breast, carefully dissect the breast tissue, and remove the entire breast, including the nipple-areola complex. The procedure may require a hospital stay and is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the operation.
