Extirpation of Matter from Right Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually guide the removal of unwanted matter from the right lower lung lobe through a small incision in the skin. The endoscope is inserted percutaneously, or through the skin, and advanced to the affected area of the lung lobe. Once in place, specialized instruments are used to carefully extirpate, or remove, the targeted matter, which can include foreign objects, abscesses, or other abnormal tissue. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the endoscope and removal of the targeted matter.
