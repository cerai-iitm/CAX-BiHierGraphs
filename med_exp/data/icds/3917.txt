Division of Left Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower arm to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are the layers of tissue that surround muscles and other structures. The goal of the procedure is to divide or separate these layers of tissue and fascia, which may be necessary to treat a variety of conditions such as trauma, infection, or tumors. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary repairs or interventions. The procedure requires careful dissection and manipulation of the tissue and fascia to avoid damaging surrounding nerves, blood vessels, and other structures.
