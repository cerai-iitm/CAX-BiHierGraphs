Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Hepatobiliary Duct, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an intraluminal device into the hepatobiliary duct, utilizing imaging guidance to facilitate precise placement. The device is introduced through the skin and advanced through the liver and bile ducts to reach the target area. This approach allows for effective treatment of various hepatobiliary conditions, such as biliary obstruction or leaks, while minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may require post-procedure monitoring to ensure optimal outcomes.
