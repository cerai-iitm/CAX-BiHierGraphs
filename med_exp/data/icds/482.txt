Insertion of Monitoring Device into Right Pulmonary Artery, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a monitoring device through the skin and into the right pulmonary artery to monitor blood flow and pressure. The device is typically inserted through a small incision in the groin or arm, and guided through the blood vessels to the target location using fluoroscopy or ultrasound. Once in place, the device provides real-time data on pulmonary artery pressure and cardiac output, helping clinicians diagnose and manage conditions such as heart failure, pulmonary hypertension, and cardiogenic shock. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete.
