Release Trigeminal Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the trigeminal nerve, which is responsible for transmitting sensory information from the face to the brain. The goal of the procedure is to release the nerve from any compressive forces or adhesions that may be causing pain, numbness, or tingling in the face. The surgeon carefully dissects the surrounding tissue to free the nerve, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding structures. The procedure can be performed to treat conditions such as trigeminal neuralgia, a chronic pain disorder characterized by brief, electric shock-like pains in the face.
