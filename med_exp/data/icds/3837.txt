Replacement of Right Foot Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Full Thickness, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing damaged or defective skin on the right foot with a full-thickness autologous tissue substitute, which is a bioengineered material derived from the patient's own tissues. The external approach is used, where the surgeon makes an incision on the surface of the skin to access the affected area. The damaged skin is then removed, and the autologous tissue substitute is carefully implanted to promote healing and restore normal skin function. The goal of this procedure is to improve wound healing, reduce the risk of infection, and restore normal mobility and function to the right foot.
