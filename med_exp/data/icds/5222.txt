Excision of Right Tibia, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lower leg to access the right tibia, also known as the shinbone. The purpose of the operation is diagnostic, aiming to examine the bone and surrounding tissue to identify any abnormalities or diseases. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully excise, or remove, a section of the tibia to facilitate further examination or testing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely locate and remove the targeted bone segment.
