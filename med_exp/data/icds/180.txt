Repair Facial Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the facial nerve, which is then repaired through direct visualization and manipulation of the nerve fibers. The open approach allows for precise identification and treatment of the damaged area, enabling the surgeon to restore normal nerve function and alleviate symptoms such as facial weakness or paralysis. The procedure typically requires careful dissection and mobilization of surrounding tissues to expose the affected nerve segment, followed by meticulous repair using microsurgical techniques. Post-operatively, patients may require rehabilitation therapy to regain full facial motor function and expression.
