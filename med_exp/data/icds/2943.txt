Extirpation of Matter from Duodenum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the mouth or a surgical opening to reach the duodenum, the first part of the small intestine. The scope allows the healthcare provider to visually examine the duodenum and identify the matter that needs to be removed. Specialized instruments are then used to extirpate the unwanted material, which can include foreign objects, blood clots, or other substances that are causing a blockage or obstruction. The entire process is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety.
