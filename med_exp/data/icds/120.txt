Extirpation of Matter from Spinal Canal, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the spine to access the spinal canal, allowing the surgeon to manually remove abnormal matter or foreign substances that may be compressing or irritating the spinal cord or surrounding nerves. The open approach provides direct visualization of the spinal canal, enabling the surgeon to meticulously extirpate the offending material, which may include blood clots, bone fragments, or infectious debris. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as numbness, weakness, or paralysis caused by compression or irritation of the spinal cord or nerves. By removing the abnormal matter, the surgeon aims to restore spinal cord function, promote healing, and improve patient outcomes.
