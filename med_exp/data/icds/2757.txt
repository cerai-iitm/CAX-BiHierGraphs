Drainage of Sigmoid Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the sigmoid colon, a part of the large intestine, to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances. The open approach requires a larger incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the affected area and directly access the sigmoid colon to perform the drainage. The goal of this procedure is to relieve symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting caused by blockage or obstruction in the sigmoid colon. The surgeon may also repair any underlying damage or abnormalities in the sigmoid colon during the procedure to prevent future complications.
