Extraction of Descending Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the descending colon through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visually examine the colon and guide the extraction. The endoscope is inserted through the natural opening of the anus or through an artificial opening created surgically, allowing the healthcare provider to access the descending colon. The procedure is performed under anesthesia to minimize discomfort and is often used to diagnose and treat conditions such as colon cancer, polyps, or diverticulitis. The extraction of the descending colon is a complex procedure that requires precise dissection and ligation of blood vessels and other surrounding tissues to ensure a safe and successful outcome.
