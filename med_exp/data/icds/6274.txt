Dilation of Left Ureter with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
The dilation of the left ureter is a minimally invasive procedure that involves the use of an intraluminal device to widen the narrow ureteral passage. This procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, allowing the surgeon to visually examine the ureter and guide the device to the affected area. The device is then used to gently dilate the ureter, allowing for improved drainage and relief from blockage or obstruction. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can be done on an outpatient basis, with patients often returning to normal activities within a few days.
