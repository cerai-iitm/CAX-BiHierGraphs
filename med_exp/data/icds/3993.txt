Drainage of Right Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skin to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the right hand, allowing for the drainage of excess fluid, blood, or other materials that may have accumulated due to injury or infection. The open approach enables the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and manually remove the unwanted material, promoting healing and reducing the risk of further complications. The procedure is typically performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the hand is immobilized to facilitate the healing process.
