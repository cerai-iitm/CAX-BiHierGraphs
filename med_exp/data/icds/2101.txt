Drainage of Left Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During a diagnostic endoscopy, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the mouth or nose to visualize the left main bronchus. The bronchoscope allows the healthcare provider to directly visualize the airway and identify any abnormalities or blockages. A drainage procedure is then performed to remove excess mucus, blood, or other debris from the left main bronchus through the natural or artificial opening. This minimally invasive procedure helps to diagnose and treat conditions such as bronchiectasis, lung cancer, or respiratory infections, and can also provide relief from respiratory symptoms like coughing or shortness of breath.
