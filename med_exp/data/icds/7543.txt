Traction of Right Lower Extremity using Traction Apparatus
This procedure involves the application of a controlled force to the right lower extremity using a specialized traction apparatus, which helps to stretch and realign the affected joints and soft tissues. The goal of this treatment is to reduce muscle spasms, relieve pressure on the joints, and improve range of motion. The traction apparatus is carefully calibrated to provide a specific amount of force, which is then applied to the affected limb through a system of pulleys, weights, or other mechanical devices. By gradually increasing the force over time, the procedure helps to promote relaxation, reduce pain, and improve overall mobility in the right lower extremity.
