Inspection of Larynx, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves visual examination of the larynx, also known as the voice box, through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for a direct view of the vocal cords and surrounding tissue. A flexible or rigid scope is inserted through the mouth or nose, or through a tracheotomy opening, to inspect for any abnormalities, injuries, or diseases affecting the larynx. The procedure is often performed under local anesthesia or sedation to minimize discomfort and is typically an outpatient procedure. The detailed visual inspection enables healthcare providers to diagnose and monitor conditions such as vocal cord polyps, cancer, or nerve damage, and plan appropriate treatment.
