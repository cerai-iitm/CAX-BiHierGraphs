Insertion of Monitoring Device into Upper Artery, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to implant a monitoring device into an upper artery, typically the radial or brachial artery, through a small incision in the skin. The device is inserted percutaneously, meaning through the skin, and guided into position using imaging technology such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. Once in place, the device can continuously monitor blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and other vital signs, providing real-time data to healthcare professionals. This procedure allows for close monitoring of patients with cardiovascular disease, shock, or other critical conditions.
