Repair Chest Wall, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical operation to repair defects or deformities of the chest wall, which may be caused by trauma, tumors, or congenital conditions, using an open approach, where the surgeon makes a incision in the chest to access the affected area, and then repairs or reconstructs the chest wall using various techniques and materials, such as bone grafts, prosthetics, or tissue flaps. The goal of the procedure is to restore the normal anatomy and function of the chest wall, improve respiratory function, and enhance the patient's overall quality of life. In some cases, the procedure may also involve the repair of associated injuries, such as rib fractures or lung damage. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the necessary repairs with precision and accuracy.
