Bypass Coronary Artery, Four or More Arteries from Left Internal Mammary with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This cardiovascular surgical procedure involves creating a bypass in the coronary artery by using the left internal mammary artery as the donor vessel and autologous venous tissue as the graft material. The open approach necessitates a sternotomy, which allows the surgeon to access the heart and its surrounding structures. The bypass is constructed by connecting the graft material to the aorta and the diseased coronary artery, restoring blood flow to the heart muscle. This complex procedure typically involves bypassing four or more arteries to treat advanced coronary artery disease and improve cardiac function.
