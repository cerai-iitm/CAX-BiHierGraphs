Reattachment of Left Ring Finger, Open Approach
The surgical reattachment of a severed left ring finger involves an open approach, where the skin and underlying tissue are incised to access the affected area. The procedure requires careful handling of the amputated digit to preserve as much tissue as possible, followed by meticulous reconnection of blood vessels, nerves, and tendons under microscopic magnification. The reattached finger is then stabilized with internal fixation devices, such as K-wires or plates, to ensure proper alignment and promote healing. Post-operative care involves close monitoring to prevent complications and promote functional recovery of the reattached finger.
