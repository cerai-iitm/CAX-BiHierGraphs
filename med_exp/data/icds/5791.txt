Replacement of Left Shoulder Joint with Reverse Ball and Socket Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves replacing the left shoulder joint with a reverse ball and socket synthetic substitute through an open approach, requiring a surgical incision to access the affected joint. The surgeon removes the damaged or diseased joint and replaces it with a synthetic substitute that allows for improved mobility and reduced pain. The reverse ball and socket design enables the shoulder to rotate more naturally, increasing range of motion and reducing wear on the joint. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal shoulder function, alleviate pain, and enhance overall quality of life for the patient.
