Supplement Left Temporal Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, a segment of autologous tissue substitute is used to supplement the left temporal artery, restoring its structure and function. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection and promoting a more natural repair. The temporal artery is a vital blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the brain, and this procedure is often performed to improve blood flow and resolve related symptoms. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the artery and surrounding tissues, enabling a precise and detailed repair.
