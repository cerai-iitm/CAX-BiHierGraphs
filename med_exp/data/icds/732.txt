Excision of Right Radial Artery, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope to visualize the right radial artery through a small incision in the skin. The radial artery is then carefully excised, or removed, using specialized instruments inserted through the incision. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for precise dissection and minimal tissue damage, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster healing. The excised artery is then removed from the body, and the incision site is closed, typically with sutures or staples.
