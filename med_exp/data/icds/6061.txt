Removal of Articulating Spacer from Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an articulating spacer from the left knee joint using an open approach. The articulating spacer is typically used as a temporary measure to maintain joint space and alignment after an infection or failed joint replacement. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left knee to access the affected joint, carefully removes the spacer, and may perform any necessary debridement or repair to the surrounding tissue. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal knee function and prepare the joint for future treatment, such as revision joint replacement surgery.
