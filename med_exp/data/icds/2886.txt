Excision of Descending Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of the descending colon, a section of the large intestine, through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope guides the surgical instruments to the site, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the excision. This minimally invasive approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissue and results in less post-operative pain and scarring. The excised colon segment is then removed through the natural or artificial opening, promoting a faster recovery and return to normal digestive function.
