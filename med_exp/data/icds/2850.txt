Excision of Large Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the large intestine, also known as the colon, and removing a portion of it. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the affected area and precisely excise the diseased or damaged section of the intestine. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as colorectal cancer, diverticulitis, or intestinal obstruction. Following excision, the surgeon may reconnect the healthy sections of the intestine or perform a colostomy, depending on the individual case.
