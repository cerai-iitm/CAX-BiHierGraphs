Excision of Lumbar Vertebra, Open Approach, Diagnostic
During this surgical procedure, the patient is placed under general anesthesia and an incision is made in the lower back to access the lumbar spine. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully remove a portion of a lumbar vertebra, typically to diagnose conditions such as vertebral fractures, tumors, or infections. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis. The procedure is typically performed in a hospital setting and may involve a postoperative recovery period to manage pain and discomfort.
