Insertion of Spacer into Left Ankle Joint, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left ankle joint, followed by the insertion of a spacer device into the joint cavity. The spacer is designed to fill any gaps or voids in the joint, providing support and stability to the surrounding bones and soft tissues. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding anatomy, enabling the surgeon to precisely place the spacer and address any underlying issues. The ultimate goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, improve mobility, and restore normal function to the left ankle joint.
