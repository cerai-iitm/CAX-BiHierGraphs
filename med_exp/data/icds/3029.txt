Occlusion of Stomach with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an intraluminal device to occlude the stomach, which is performed through a natural or artificial opening in the body. The procedure is performed endoscopically, where a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or a surgically created opening to visualize the stomach. The device is then placed through the endoscope to block the stomach, treating conditions such as stomach bleeding, perforation, or obstruction. This procedure offers a less invasive alternative to traditional open surgery, resulting in less trauma, pain, and scarring for the patient.
