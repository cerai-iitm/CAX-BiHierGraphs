Fluoroscopy of Biliary and Pancreatic Ducts using Other Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a real-time X-ray imaging technology, to visualize the biliary and pancreatic ducts. A contrast agent, other than the standard options, is injected into the ducts to enhance visibility. The fluoroscope produces continuous X-ray images, allowing the physician to guide the procedure and observe the passage of the contrast agent through the ducts. The resulting images help diagnose issues such as blockages, narrowing, or abnormalities in the biliary and pancreatic ducts.
