Excision of Right Ovary, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the right ovary through small incisions using an endoscope, which is a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope is inserted through the abdominal wall, allowing the surgeon to visualize the ovary and surrounding tissues. Under guidance from the endoscope, specialized instruments are used to carefully dissect and excise the right ovary, which is then removed through the small incision site. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and is often used to treat conditions such as ovarian cysts or tumors, or as part of a hysterectomy.
