Extracorporeal Oxygenation, Membrane, Peripheral Veno-arterial
This surgical procedure involves connecting a patient's peripheral vein and artery to a membrane oxygenator, which is a device located outside the body, to provide both oxygenation and carbon dioxide removal from the blood. The procedure is used to support patients who require cardiopulmonary bypass, such as those undergoing cardiothoracic surgery or those with cardiogenic shock. During the procedure, the patient's blood is pumped out of the body and into the oxygenator, where it receives oxygen and has carbon dioxide removed before being pumped back into the body. This temporary support allows the patient's own heart and lungs to rest and recover during a period of crisis.
