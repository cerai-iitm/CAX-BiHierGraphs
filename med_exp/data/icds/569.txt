Replacement of Tricuspid Valve with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the tricuspid valve with a zooplastic tissue valve, which is a bioprosthesis derived from animal sources, using an open approach. During the procedure, the patient's chest is opened, and the heart is stopped, allowing the surgeon to access the tricuspid valve. The diseased or damaged valve is then removed, and the zooplastic tissue valve is implanted in its place, ensuring proper blood flow from the right ventricle to the right atrium. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the valve and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to perform the replacement with precision and accuracy.
