Excision of Left Upper Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to excise, or remove, a portion of the left upper lobe bronchus through a natural or artificial opening. The bronchus is a passageway that leads to the lungs, and this procedure is typically performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as lung cancer, bronchiectasis, or other obstructions. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided to the affected area, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the bronchus and perform the excision. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
