Insertion of Defibrillator Generator into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
During an open chest procedure, a defibrillator generator is surgically inserted into the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles. This device is designed to regulate abnormal heart rhythms by sending electrical impulses to the heart. The generator is typically placed in the chest wall, just beneath the skin, and is connected to one or more leads that are inserted into a vein and guided to the heart. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may take several hours to complete.
