Drainage of Left Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left foot to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are the layers of tissue just beneath the skin. The goal of the procedure is to drain any accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances that may be causing pain, swelling, or infection in the foot. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected tissue and fascia, enabling them to accurately identify and remove any abnormal substances. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort and may require post-operative care to promote healing and prevent complications.
