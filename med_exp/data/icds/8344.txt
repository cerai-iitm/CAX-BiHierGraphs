Electroconvulsive Therapy, Bilateral-Multiple Seizure
This therapeutic treatment involves the application of an electric current to the brain to induce a seizure, with the intention of relieving severe mental health symptoms. The bilateral approach involves placement of electrodes on both sides of the head, typically above the eyebrows, to stimulate both hemispheres of the brain simultaneously. The multiple seizure protocol may be used for patients who require a more intensive treatment course, involving a series of seizures induced during a single anesthesia session. This treatment is usually reserved for patients with treatment-resistant depression, catatonia, or other severe mental illnesses that have not responded to other therapies.
