Drainage of Intracranial Subdural Space, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves using a needle or catheter to drain fluid or blood from the subdural space, a region between the surface of the brain and the thin membrane covering it, through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as CT or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure on the brain and alleviate symptoms such as headache, confusion, and seizures caused by the accumulated fluid or blood. The procedure is often performed in an emergency setting to treat life-threatening conditions, such as subdural hematomas.
