Insertion of Totally Implantable Vascular Access Device into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
A totally implantable vascular access device is inserted into the chest subcutaneous tissue and fascia through a percutaneous approach, providing a safe and efficient way to administer chemotherapy, antibiotics, or other medications. This minimally invasive procedure involves creating a small incision in the chest wall and guiding the device into place using imaging guidance. The device is then anchored to the underlying tissue to secure its position, allowing for easy access to the bloodstream for future treatments. The entire procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes about 30-60 minutes to complete.
