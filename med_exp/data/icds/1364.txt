Restriction of Inferior Mesenteric Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous introduction of an intraluminal device to restrict blood flow through the inferior mesenteric artery, typically performed under imaging guidance. The device is advanced through a small incision in the skin and navigated through the artery using a catheter, allowing for precise placement and deployment. The restriction of blood flow through the inferior mesenteric artery can help to manage conditions such as gastrointestinal bleeding or arteriovenous malformations. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical methods.
