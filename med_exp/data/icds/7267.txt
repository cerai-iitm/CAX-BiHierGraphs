Detachment at Right Foot, Complete, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the right foot from the leg, performed through an open approach, which means a larger incision is made to access the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient is comfortable and pain-free during the operation. The detachment of the right foot is often necessary due to severe trauma, infection, or tumors that have compromised the foot's functionality or pose a significant health risk. Following the procedure, the wound is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any potential complications during the recovery period.
