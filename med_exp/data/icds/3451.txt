Extirpation of Matter from Pancreatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter or debris from the pancreatic duct through a natural or artificial opening. The pancreatic duct is a tube that carries digestive enzymes from the pancreas to the small intestine, and blockages can cause digestive problems. The endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, is inserted through the mouth or a surgically created opening to visualize the pancreatic duct and identify the blockage. The obstructing material is then carefully extracted using specialized instruments, allowing for the restoration of normal pancreatic function and relief of symptoms.
