Excision of Buttock Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision on the outer skin of the buttock to access and remove damaged or diseased tissue, such as skin cancer or scar tissue. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and carefully excise the targeted skin, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissue. The goal of the procedure is to remove the affected skin and promote healthy healing, restoring the natural appearance and function of the buttock. Post-operative care typically involves managing pain, promoting wound healing, and monitoring for signs of infection or other complications.
