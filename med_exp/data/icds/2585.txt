Resection of Larynx, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the larynx, also known as the voice box, through an open approach, where an incision is made in the neck to access the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as laryngeal cancer, severe trauma, or chronic infection that cannot be managed with other treatments. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects and removes the affected larynx, and may also remove related tissues and lymph nodes to ensure complete removal of diseased tissue. The resulting defect may be closed with sutures or reconstructed with tissue from another part of the body, depending on the individual case.
