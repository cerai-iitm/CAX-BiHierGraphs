Replacement of Upper Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the diseased or damaged upper artery is replaced with a natural tissue substitute harvested from the patient's own body. The open approach involves a large incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and perform the replacement. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully grafted into place, restoring blood flow and promoting healthy circulation. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, and weakness, and to prevent further complications associated with upper artery disease.
