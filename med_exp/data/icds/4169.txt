Insertion of Tunneled Vascular Access Device into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
A tunneled vascular access device is inserted into the chest subcutaneous tissue and fascia through a percutaneous approach, creating a safe and reliable pathway for frequent blood draws and medication administration. This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the chest wall and creating a tunnel under the skin to the access device, which is then secured in place with sutures or staples. The device's catheter is guided through the tunnel and into a large vein, allowing for easy access to the bloodstream. Once in place, the device can remain for extended periods, reducing the need for repeated needle sticks and improving patient comfort and care.
