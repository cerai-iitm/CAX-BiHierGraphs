Division of Vagus Nerve, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, an endoscope is inserted through the skin to visualize the vagus nerve, which runs from the base of the brain to the abdomen. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to precisely divide the vagus nerve, disrupting the abnormal nerve signals that are causing symptoms. This percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and minimizes scarring. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as seizures, tremors, or other conditions related to vagus nerve dysfunction.
