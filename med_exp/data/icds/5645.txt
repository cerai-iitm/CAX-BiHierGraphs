Excision of Left Finger Phalangeal Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the skin and soft tissue of the left finger to access the phalangeal joint. The affected joint is then carefully dissected and removed, typically to treat conditions such as osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, or trauma-induced damage. The excision involves the removal of the damaged or diseased joint tissue and bone, with the goal of alleviating pain, improving mobility, and enhancing overall finger function. The surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the finger is immobilized to allow for proper healing.
