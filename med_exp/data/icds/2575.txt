Reposition Upper Tooth, Multiple, External Approach
This procedure involves the manual manipulation of multiple upper teeth to reposition them into a more aligned or aesthetically pleasing arrangement. The external approach method is used, which means the repositioning is performed from outside the mouth, rather than through a surgical incision. The goal of this procedure is to improve the overall appearance and function of the teeth, which can enhance the patient's smile and confidence. The repositioning process typically requires a thorough examination of the teeth and surrounding tissues to determine the best approach for achieving optimal results.
