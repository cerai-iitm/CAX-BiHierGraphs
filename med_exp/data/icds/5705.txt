Fusion of 2 to 7 Thoracic Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of 2 to 7 thoracic vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device, which is inserted through an anterior approach to the anterior column of the spine. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully place the device and ensure proper alignment of the vertebrae. The interbody fusion device is designed to promote bone growth and stabilize the spine, alleviating symptoms such as chronic back pain and numbness or tingling in the extremities. By fusing the affected vertebrae, the procedure helps to restore spinal stability and improve overall mobility.
