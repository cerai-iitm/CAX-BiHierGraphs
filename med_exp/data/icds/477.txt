Insertion of Defibrillator Lead into Left Ventricle, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive surgical procedure is performed to insert a defibrillator lead into the left ventricle of the heart through a percutaneous approach, which involves making a small incision in the skin. This procedure allows for the placement of an implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD) that can detect and correct abnormal heart rhythms. The defibrillator lead is carefully guided through a vein in the chest and into the left ventricle, where it is securely positioned to allow for effective heart rhythm regulation. The goal of this procedure is to prevent sudden cardiac death and improve overall cardiac function.
