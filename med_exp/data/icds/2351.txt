Release Right Lung, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the right lung and release any adhesions or scar tissue that may be restricting its expansion or causing breathing difficulties. A small incision is made in the chest wall, allowing the endoscope and specialized instruments to be inserted through the skin and into the lung cavity. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure the patient's comfort during the procedure. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal lung function and improve breathing by releasing any restrictions or blockages that may be impeding lung expansion.
