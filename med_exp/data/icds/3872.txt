Resection of Left Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left breast through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the area and facilitate the resection. The open approach provides direct access to the breast tissue, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and minimize discomfort. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as breast cancer, and it is often followed by reconstructive surgery to restore the shape and appearance of the breast.
