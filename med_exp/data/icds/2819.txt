Excision of Stomach, Pylorus, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the stomach and pylorus, which is the lower part of the stomach that connects to the small intestine. The surgeon then carefully removes the stomach and pylorus, examining the tissue to identify any abnormalities or disease. The goal of this diagnostic excision is to identify the presence of cancer, ulcers, or other conditions that may be affecting the stomach and pylorus. The open approach allows for direct visualization and thorough examination of the tissue, enabling a accurate diagnosis and informing further treatment decisions.
