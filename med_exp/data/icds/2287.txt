Extraction of Right Upper Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the right upper lobe bronchus and perform a diagnostic extraction through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is guided through the mouth or nose and into the airways, allowing the physician to directly visualize the bronchus and collect a sample for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and to allow the physician to complete the procedure quickly and efficiently. The sample collected during the procedure is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and analysis.
