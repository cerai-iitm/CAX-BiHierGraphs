Destruction of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the destruction of the right lower lung lobe through a percutaneous approach, which means that the procedure is performed through the skin, typically using imaging guidance such as CT or fluoroscopy. A small incision is made in the chest wall and a probe or device is inserted through the incision to reach the affected lung lobe. The probe or device is then used to destroy the targeted lung tissue, which may be cancerous or damaged, using techniques such as radiofrequency ablation, microwave ablation, or cryoablation. The goal of the procedure is to remove or destroy the diseased lung tissue, alleviating symptoms and improving lung function.
