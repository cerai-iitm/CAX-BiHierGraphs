Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Right Hip Joint, Acetabular Surface, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical removal of a synthetic substitute, typically a prosthetic implant, from the acetabular surface of the right hip joint through an open approach, which involves a surgical incision to directly access the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to eliminate any dysfunction or complications caused by the synthetic substitute. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to carefully remove the synthetic substitute and restore the natural anatomy of the hip joint. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is often performed in an inpatient setting.
