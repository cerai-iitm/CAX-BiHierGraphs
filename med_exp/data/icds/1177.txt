Extirpation of Matter from Right Femoral Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the right femoral artery to remove unwanted matter, such as blood clots, plaque, or other debris, that may be obstructing blood flow or causing harm. The open approach allows direct visualization of the artery and precise removal of the unwanted material. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow, alleviate symptoms, and prevent complications such as limb ischemia or organ damage. The extirpation of matter from the right femoral artery is typically performed under general anesthesia in a hospital setting by a trained vascular surgeon.
