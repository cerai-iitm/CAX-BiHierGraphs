Change Endotracheal Airway in Trachea, External Approach
During an endotracheal intubation procedure, a new endotracheal tube is carefully inserted through the mouth or nose and guided into the trachea, replacing the existing tube. This procedure is typically performed in intubated patients who require a change in their airway management. The tube is manipulated externally through the mouth or nose to reach the trachea, allowing for secure and effective ventilation. The doctor or anesthesiologist performing the procedure takes great care to minimize complications and ensure a smooth transition to the new tube.
