Release Cervical Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves dissecting the affected cervical nerve to release it from surrounding tissue, Scar, or bone that may be compressing or entrapping it, restoring normal nerve function and alleviating associated symptoms such as numbness, tingling, or pain. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the nerve and surrounding anatomy, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and address any areas of compression or damage. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of specialized instrumentation and magnification to aid in nerve dissection and release. Following the procedure, patients may experience improved range of motion, reduced pain, and enhanced overall neurological function.
