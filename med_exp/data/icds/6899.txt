Drainage of Abdominal Wall with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating an incision in the abdominal wall to drain accumulated fluids, such as abscesses or hematomas, and inserting a drainage device to facilitate the removal of the fluid. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately locate and drain the fluid collection. The drainage device is carefully placed to ensure effective drainage and promote healing. Post-operative care typically involves monitoring for signs of infection and adjusting the drainage device as needed to promote optimal recovery.
