Excision of Left Radial Artery, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is the detailed description:

Percutaneous endoscopic excision of the left radial artery is a minimally invasive procedure that involves making a small incision in the skin to access the artery. Through the use of an endoscope, a thin lighted tube with a camera, the surgeon visualizes the artery and removes the targeted section. This approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as vascular malformations or aneurysms.
