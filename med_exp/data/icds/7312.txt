Detachment at Right 5th Toe, Complete, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves complete detachment of the right 5th toe from the surrounding tissue and bone, performed through an open approach that utilizes a surgical incision to access the affected area. The surgery is typically carried out under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient remains comfortable and pain-free during the procedure. The detachment process involves careful separation of the toe from the surrounding soft tissue and bone, taking care to minimize damage to nearby structures. Once the detachment is complete, the surgical site is closed, and the patient is taken to the recovery room for post-operative care.
