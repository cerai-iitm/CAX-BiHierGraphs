Resection of Left Vitreous, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a small incision in the eye to access the vitreous humor, a clear gel-like substance that fills the center of the eye. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully remove the vitreous humor from the left eye through a percutaneous approach, which is a minimally invasive method that reduces tissue damage and promotes faster healing. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate vision problems caused by vitreous opacities or hemorrhages that obstruct light from entering the retina. By removing the affected vitreous humor, the procedure can restore clear vision and improve overall eye health.
