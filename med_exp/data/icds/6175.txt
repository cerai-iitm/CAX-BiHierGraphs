Reposition Left Hip Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves adjusting the position of the left hip joint using a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, which reduces trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster recovery. A small incision is made in the skin, and specialized instruments are used to reposition the joint, followed by the insertion of an internal fixation device to stabilize the joint and facilitate healing. The internal fixation device, such as a plate or screw, is designed to provide temporary support until the joint has fully recovered. This procedure is often performed to treat hip joint instability or deformity resulting from traumatic injury, congenital conditions, or degenerative diseases.
