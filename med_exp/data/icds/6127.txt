Replacement of Right Knee Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically replacing the right knee joint with an uncemented synthetic substitute through an open approach, requiring a large incision to access the joint. The damaged or diseased joint is removed and the artificial joint is implanted, secured without cement, and aligned to restore normal knee function. The uncemented synthetic substitute is designed to integrate with the surrounding bone, promoting a strong and stable joint. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, ensuring accurate implant placement and minimizing potential complications.
