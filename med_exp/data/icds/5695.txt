Fusion of Thoracic Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing a joint in the thoracic region of the spine using autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The posterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the spine from the back, and the posterior column is the specific area targeted. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and muscles to directly visualize the spine, allowing the surgeon to manually place the autologous tissue substitute and stabilize the joint. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and restore stability in the thoracic spine.
