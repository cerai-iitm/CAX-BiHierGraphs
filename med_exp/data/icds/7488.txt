Compression of Chest Wall using Intermittent Pressure Device
This procedure involves the use of an external device that applies intermittent pressure to the chest wall to facilitate respiratory therapy or other medical treatment. The device is designed to compress the chest wall in a repetitive, cyclical manner to improve lung expansion and gas exchange. The compression helps to loosen and clear mucus from the airways, making it easier to breathe and promoting overall respiratory health. By applying controlled pressure to the chest, this therapy can be particularly beneficial for patients with respiratory conditions such as chronic bronchitis, cystic fibrosis, or pneumonia.
