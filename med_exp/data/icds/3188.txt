Resection of Left Large Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the left large intestine, also known as the left colon, to remove a portion of it. The affected section of the intestine is carefully identified and isolated from surrounding tissues and blood vessels before being surgically resected. The remaining healthy portions of the intestine are then reconnected to restore normal digestive function. The entire procedure is performed under general anesthesia and may take several hours to complete, requiring a hospital stay for post-operative recovery and monitoring.
