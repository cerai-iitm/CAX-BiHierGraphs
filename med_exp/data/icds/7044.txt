Inspection of Respiratory Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic Approach
This medical procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visually examine the respiratory tract. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth or nose, or through a tracheostomy, to inspect the airways and lungs. The procedure allows healthcare providers to directly observe the respiratory tract and identify any abnormalities, such as inflammation, bleeding, or tumors, that may be causing symptoms. This visual inspection can help guide further diagnosis and treatment, and may be performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort.
