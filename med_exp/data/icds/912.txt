Restriction of Intracranial Artery with Extraluminal Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open placement of an extraluminal device to constrict or occlude an intracranial artery, with the primary goal of preventing blood flow to a specific region of the brain. The procedure is performed through an open craniotomy, allowing the surgeon to visually access the targeted artery and accurately position the device. The device is typically implanted around the exterior of the artery, reducing blood flow to the affected area while minimizing damage to surrounding brain tissue. By restricting blood flow, the procedure aims to alleviate conditions such as aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or vascular malformations.
