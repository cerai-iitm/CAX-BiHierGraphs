Fluoroscopy of Gallbladder and Bile Ducts using Other Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the gallbladder and bile ducts, facilitating the diagnosis of gallstones, bile duct obstruction, or other abnormalities. A contrast agent other than iodine or air is administered to enhance X-ray images of the biliary system, allowing for a more detailed examination. During the procedure, the patient is positioned on an X-ray table, and the fluoroscope is placed over the upper right abdomen to capture images of the gallbladder and bile ducts. The resulting images provide valuable information for guiding further treatment or intervention.
