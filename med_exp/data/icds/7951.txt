Fluoroscopy of Left Internal Mammary Bypass Graft using Other Contrast
This imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left internal mammary bypass graft, which is a blood vessel used to reroute blood flow around a blockage in the coronary arteries. During the procedure, a contrast agent is injected into the graft to enhance visualization, allowing the healthcare provider to assess the graft's patency and detect any potential blockages or stenosis. The fluoroscopy machine produces continuous X-ray images that are displayed on a monitor, enabling the healthcare provider to guide the procedure in real-time. The goal of the procedure is to evaluate the functionality of the bypass graft and identify any potential issues that may require further intervention.
