Excision of Left Hand Tendon, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left hand to access the affected tendon. The damaged or diseased tendon is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate pain, improve range of motion, and restore normal hand function. The surgical site is thoroughly cleaned and closed, and the patient may require post-operative rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility in the hand. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as tendonitis, tendinosis, or traumatic injuries that have caused tendon damage.
