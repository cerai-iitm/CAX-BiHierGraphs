Excision of Sigmoid Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the sigmoid colon through a minimally invasive approach, utilizing an endoscope inserted through a small incision in the skin. The sigmoid colon, which is the lower part of the large intestine, is carefully dissected and excised using specialized instruments guided by the endoscope. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as diverticulitis, colon cancer, or bowel obstruction. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for smaller incisions, less tissue damage, and faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery.
