Replacement of Mitral Valve with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the diseased or damaged mitral valve with a zooplastic tissue valve, which is a bioprosthesis derived from animal sources. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart, and the mitral valve is carefully excised and replaced with the zooplastic valve. The new valve is then securely attached to the heart tissue to ensure proper function and blood flow. The entire process is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a skilled cardiothoracic surgical team to ensure a successful outcome.
