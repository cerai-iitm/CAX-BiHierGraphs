Performance of Cardiac Pacing, Continuous
This procedure involves the continuous operation of a cardiac pacing system, which uses electrical impulses to regulate the heart's rhythm and ensure a consistent heartbeat. The pacing system is typically used to treat conditions such as bradycardia, heart block, and atrial fibrillation, where the heart's natural pacing is disrupted. The continuous pacing function allows the device to monitor the heart's activity and respond accordingly, providing a personalized treatment plan to each patient. The goal of this procedure is to restore a normal heart rhythm, alleviating symptoms and improving the patient's overall quality of life.
