Bypass Sigmoid Colon to Cutaneous, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass of the sigmoid colon through a minimally invasive, percutaneous endoscopic approach, where a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the skin to visualize the colon. The bypass allows for the diversion of feces from the diseased or obstructed sigmoid colon to the skin, where it is collected in a pouch or bag. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as colon cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, or traumatic injury, and may be a temporary or permanent solution depending on the individual case. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces recovery time and minimizes surgical scarring compared to traditional open surgery.
