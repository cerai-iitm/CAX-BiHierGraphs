Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Abdominal Wall, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves revising a previously placed synthetic substitute in the abdominal wall through an open approach, which typically involves making a larger incision to access the affected area. During the procedure, the healthcare provider will remove the existing synthetic material and replace it with a new one, ensuring a secure and stable repair of the abdominal wall. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the healthcare provider to precisely correct any defects or irregularities. The goal of this procedure is to restore the integrity and function of the abdominal wall, alleviating symptoms and improving overall health.
