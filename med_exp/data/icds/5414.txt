Reposition Right Upper Femur with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon accesses the right upper femur through an open incision to reposition the bone into its normal anatomical alignment. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are then implanted to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to make precise adjustments and ensure proper alignment. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and mobility to the affected limb, alleviating symptoms such as pain, limited range of motion, and limb deformity.
