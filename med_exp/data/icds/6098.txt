Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Metal on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the diseased or damaged right hip joint with a metal on polyethylene synthetic substitute through an open approach. The metal component is typically made of titanium or stainless steel, while the polyethylene component is a medical-grade plastic. The open approach allows the surgeon to access the hip joint through a single, larger incision, providing a clear view of the joint and surrounding tissues. The new synthetic joint is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of a healthy hip joint, alleviating pain and restoring mobility to the patient.
