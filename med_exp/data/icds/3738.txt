Extraction of Perineum Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an external incision to access the perineum, a sensitive area between the anus and genitals. The goal is to remove skin from this region, which may be necessary to treat conditions such as skin cancer, chronic infections, or irreparable skin damage. The external approach allows surgeons to directly visualize the area and carefully extract the affected skin, minimizing damage to surrounding tissues. The extracted skin may be sent for further examination or testing, and the wound is typically closed with sutures or staples to promote healing.
