Drainage of Right Kidney Pelvis with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the detailed description:

The procedure involves a minimally invasive percutaneous approach to drain the right kidney pelvis using a drainage device, typically performed under image guidance. The procedure aims to relieve obstruction or infection in the kidney by creating a pathway for urine to flow out of the body. A small incision is made in the skin, and a thin drainage tube is inserted through the skin and into the kidney pelvis to remove excess fluid or debris. The drainage device is designed to remain in place for a period of time, allowing for continued drainage and promoting healing.
