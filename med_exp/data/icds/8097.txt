Fluoroscopy of Left Renal Artery using Other Contrast
This interventional radiology procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left renal artery, which is responsible for supplying blood to the left kidney. During the procedure, a contrast agent other than iodine or barium is injected into the artery to enhance image quality and facilitate diagnosis. Real-time imaging guidance is provided by fluoroscopy, allowing the operator to accurately guide the catheter and inject the contrast agent. The procedure is typically performed to diagnose and treat conditions affecting the left renal artery, such as stenosis or aneurysms.
