Excision of Right Clavicle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the right clavicle, also known as the collarbone, to remove part or all of the bone. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, ensuring precise excision of the damaged or diseased portion of the clavicle. The procedure may be necessary to treat conditions such as bone cancer, severe fractures, or bone infections that cannot be managed with other treatments. The goal of the surgery is to alleviate symptoms, restore function, and improve overall quality of life.
