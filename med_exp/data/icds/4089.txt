Extirpation of Matter from Right Neck Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a cut in the skin of the right neck to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles, Fat, and other organs. The goal is to remove any unwanted material, such as infected or damaged tissue, foreign objects, or abnormal growths. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the area and carefully extract the targeted material. Once the extirpation is complete, the incision is closed, typically with sutures or staples, and the patient is left to recover.
