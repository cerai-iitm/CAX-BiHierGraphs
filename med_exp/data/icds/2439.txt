Supplement Left Diaphragm with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a synthetic material is used to supplement the left diaphragm through a percutaneous endoscopic approach. This means making a small incision in the skin to access the diaphragm, and using an endoscope to visualize the area. The synthetic substitute is then placed to provide additional support to the diaphragm, helping to improve breathing and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath or difficulty inhaling. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for a faster recovery time and less post-operative pain compared to traditional open surgery.
