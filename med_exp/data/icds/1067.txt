Dilation of Right Femoral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the right femoral artery, which is a major blood vessel located in the groin area. A device is then inserted through the incision and guided into the artery to dilate, or widen, the vessel to improve blood flow. The device is designed to be placed within the artery, where it can help to overcome blockages or narrowing that may be restricting blood flow to the legs. The goal of the procedure is to restore adequate blood flow to the affected leg and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness.
