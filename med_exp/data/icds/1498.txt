Repair Left Hand Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the affected vein in the left hand. The vein is then repaired through direct visualization and palpation, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and make necessary adjustments. The repair may involve closing a tear or hole in the vein, reattaching a detached segment, or removing a blood clot or other obstruction. Once the repair is complete, the incision is closed and the hand is restored to its normal function.
