Excision of Neck Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves a surgical excision of skin tissue from the neck area, utilizing an external approach to access the affected site. The goal of this diagnostic excision is to remove a lesion or suspicious growth for further examination and testing, such as biopsy or histopathology. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the skin tissue and remove the targeted area with minimal invasion. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for examination to determine the presence of any abnormalities or diseases.
