Excision of Right Radius, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the right radius bone in the forearm. The affected portion of the bone is then removed through an open approach, which allows the surgeon to visually examine the area and diagnose any underlying conditions or diseases. The excision of the right radius may be necessary to treat various conditions, such as bone cancer, osteomyelitis, or severe fractures that cannot be repaired. The open approach provides the surgeon with direct visualization of the surgical site, allowing for precise removal of the affected bone and minimizing damage to surrounding tissues.
