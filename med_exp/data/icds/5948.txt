Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the lumbosacral joint, which connects the lower back and pelvis, using an interbody fusion device via an anterior approach. The anterior column of the spine is accessed through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to implant the device that promotes bone growth and stabilizes the joint. This fusion aims to alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, and weakness by stabilizing the spine and reducing motion between the vertebrae. The anterior approach allows for a more direct access to the lumbosacral joint, enabling the surgeon to perform the procedure with greater precision and accuracy.
