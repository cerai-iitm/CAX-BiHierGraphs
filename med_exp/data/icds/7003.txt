Insertion of Infusion Device into Peritoneal Cavity, Open Approach
The peritoneal cavity is accessed through an open incision, allowing direct visualization of the abdominal cavity. A small infusion device is then carefully placed within the peritoneal cavity, enabling the administration of medications or fluids directly into the abdominal space. The device is secured in place to ensure proper function and minimize the risk of complications. The open approach allows for precise placement and visualization of the device, facilitating effective treatment and care.
