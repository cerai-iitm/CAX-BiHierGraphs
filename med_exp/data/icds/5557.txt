Supplement Left Fibula with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a opening is made in the skin and soft tissue to access the left fibula, a bone in the lower leg. The goal of the operation is to provide additional support and structure to the fibula by supplementing it with a biological substitute, which is derived from the patient's own tissues. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully implanted into the fibula to enhance its stability and promote healing. This open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and ensure proper placement of the substitute, promoting optimal bone growth and repair.
