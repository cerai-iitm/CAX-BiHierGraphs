Beam Radiation of Brain using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This non-invasive procedure utilizes high-energy photons with energies between 1-10 MeV to deliver beam radiation directly to the brain, targeting specific areas or tumors. The treatment is typically administered in a series of fractions, spaced over several days or weeks, to minimize damage to surrounding healthy tissue. The precise delivery of photon radiation allows for effective treatment of brain cancers, arteriovenous malformations, and other conditions, while minimizing side effects and promoting improved patient outcomes. By carefully calibrating the energy and intensity of the photon beam, medical professionals can tailor the treatment to meet the unique needs of each patient.
