Immobilization of Left Hand using Splint
The medical professional applies a rigid or semi-rigid device to the left hand to restrict movement and promote healing, typically after an injury or surgery. The splint is carefully fitted and adjusted to ensure proper alignment and immobilization of the affected area. This procedure helps to reduce pain, swelling, and inflammation, while also protecting the hand from further injury. By immobilizing the left hand, the splint allows the affected tissues to heal and recover in a stable and optimal environment.
