Resection of Gallbladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of the gallbladder through a minimally invasive endoscopic approach, where a small incision is made in the abdomen allowing a thin tube with a camera and light on the end to visualize the gallbladder. The surgeon then inserts specialized instruments through additional small incisions to carefully dissect and remove the gallbladder, which is then removed through one of the small incisions. The laparoscope provides a clear view of the internal structures, enabling the surgeon to perform the procedure with precision and accuracy. The percutaneous endoscopic approach minimizes trauma to surrounding tissues and organs, reducing post-operative pain and promoting a faster recovery.
