Drainage of Left Ureter with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
During this procedure, a drainage device is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to relieve blockage or obstruction in the left ureter, allowing for the free flow of urine from the kidney to the bladder. The drainage device is carefully placed to ensure optimal drainage and alleviate symptoms such as severe pain, nausea, and vomiting. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to accurately visualize the ureter and surrounding structures. By restoring normal urinary flow, this procedure can help prevent complications like kidney damage or infection.
