Dilation of Left Peroneal Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using a balloon-tipped catheter to widen the left peroneal artery, allowing for improved blood flow to the lower leg. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a catheter is inserted through a small puncture in the skin and advanced to the narrowed section of the artery. Once in place, the balloon is inflated to compress the plaque and dilate the artery, resulting in improved vessel patency. Following the procedure, the catheter and balloon are removed, and the puncture site is closed.
