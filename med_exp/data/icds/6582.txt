Extirpation of Matter from Vagina, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the removal of abnormal or unwanted material from the vagina through a natural or artificial opening. The goal of the extirpation is to eliminate any harmful or infectious substances that may be present in the vagina, promoting a healthy environment and preventing potential complications. The procedure may be performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health. The extirpation process typically involves a careful and gentle approach to avoid causing any damage to the surrounding tissues or structures.
