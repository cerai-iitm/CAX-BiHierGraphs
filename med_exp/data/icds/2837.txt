Excision of Jejunum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the jejunum, a segment of the small intestine. The diseased or damaged portion of the jejunum is then carefully removed, and the remaining healthy portions are reattached to restore normal intestinal function. The surgical site is meticulously closed, and the patient is closely monitored for post-operative recovery. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the intestine and perform the excision with precision, ensuring optimal results.
