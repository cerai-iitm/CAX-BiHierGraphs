Reposition Left Tarsal, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning the tarsal bones in the left foot to correct deformities or misalignments. The external approach is used, which means the surgeon makes an incision on the surface of the skin to access the affected bones. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the foot and ankle, often to alleviate pain, improve mobility, and prevent further complications. The repositioning process may involve realigning, stabilizing, or fusing bones to achieve optimal results.
