Resection of Right Testis, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scrotum to access the right testicle, which is then carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding tissue and blood vessels. The testicular artery and veins are ligated to prevent bleeding, and the surrounding tissue is closed in a watertight manner to prevent infection. The entire right testicle is then removed, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding structures such as the epididymis and vas deferens. Finally, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the area is cleaned and dressed.
