Hypothermia, Single
Induced hypothermia is a medical procedure where the body's temperature is intentionally lowered to a range of 32-34°C (90-93°F) to achieve a state of reduced metabolism and oxygen demand. This therapeutic hypothermia is used to protect the brain and other organs from injury during certain medical procedures or after a cardiac arrest. The controlled cooling process helps to reduce inflammation, oxidative stress, and cell death, promoting tissue preservation and recovery. The goal of this technique is to improve neurological outcomes and reduce mortality rates in patients who require this type of intervention.
