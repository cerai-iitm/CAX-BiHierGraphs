Ultrasonography of Superior Vena Cava, Guidance
This medical imaging procedure uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the superior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the upper body to the heart. During the procedure, a handheld transducer is gently moved across the skin, emitting ultrasonic waves that bounce off the superior vena cava and return to the transducer as echoes. These echoes are then converted into real-time images, which are displayed on a monitor for the healthcare provider to interpret. The guidance obtained from these images helps physicians diagnose and treat conditions affecting the superior vena cava, such as blood clots, narrowing, or occlusion.
