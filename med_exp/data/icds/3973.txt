Drainage of Pelvic Region Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a drainage device into the pelvic region to remove accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The minimally invasive approach allows for reduced trauma to the surrounding tissue and minimizes the risk of complications. The drainage device is carefully placed through the skin and into the affected area, enabling the removal of excess fluid and promoting healing. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device and to monitor the effectiveness of the treatment.
