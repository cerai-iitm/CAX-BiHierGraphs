Perfusion of Hepatobiliary System and Pancreas Donor Organ, Single
This procedure involves the perfusion of a single donor organ, specifically the hepatobiliary system and pancreas, to preserve its functionality and viability for transplantation. During the process, a solution is pumped through the organ to maintain a stable temperature, flow rate, and pressure, mimicking the natural blood flow and oxygenation. This helps to reduce cellular damage and organ deterioration, ensuring that the organ remains healthy and functional for implantation into the recipient. The goal of this procedure is to increase the success rate of transplantation and improve patient outcomes.
