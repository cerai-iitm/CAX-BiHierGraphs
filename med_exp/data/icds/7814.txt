Monitoring of Cardiac Electrical Activity, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of electrodes or sensors through the skin to record and analyze the electrical activity of the heart. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal invasiveness, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. The monitoring process provides essential information about the heart's rhythm, rate, and conduction, helping healthcare professionals diagnose and treat various cardiac conditions, such as arrhythmias, tachycardias, and heart blocks. Accurate data from this procedure enables clinicians to develop personalized treatment plans and make informed decisions about further interventions or therapies.
