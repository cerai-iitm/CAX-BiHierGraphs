Performance of Cardiac Output, Continuous
This invasive hemodynamic monitoring procedure involves the insertion of a specialized catheter into a peripheral artery or vein, which is then advanced to the heart, enabling continuous measurement of cardiac output. This real-time monitoring allows healthcare professionals to track changes in cardiac function and respond promptly to any deviations from normal values. The data obtained helps guide fluid management, vasopressor therapy, and other critical care interventions, ultimately improving patient outcomes in high-acuity settings. By providing accurate and continuous cardiac output measurements, this procedure plays a vital role in optimizing cardiovascular care.
