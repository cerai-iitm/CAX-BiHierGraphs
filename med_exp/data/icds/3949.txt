Drainage of Back Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves draining accumulated fluid or pus from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the back region. An open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. A drainage device is inserted to facilitate the removal of the unwanted fluid or pus, promoting healing and reducing the risk of infection. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health.
