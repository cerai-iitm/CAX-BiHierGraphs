Removal of Stimulator Generator from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the trunk area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where the stimulator generator is located. The generator is carefully disconnected from surrounding tissues and removed through the open approach, allowing for direct visualization of the surgical site. The procedure requires careful dissection to avoid damaging nearby nerves or blood vessels. Once the generator is removed, the incision is closed, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
