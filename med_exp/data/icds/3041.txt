Release Small Intestine, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through the mouth or nose to release a portion of the small intestine that has become obstructed or adhered to surrounding tissue. The endoscope is guided through the digestive tract to the affected area, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the intestine and perform the release without the need for open surgery. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal trauma to the surrounding tissue and promotes faster healing. The release of the small intestine restores normal digestive function and alleviates symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
