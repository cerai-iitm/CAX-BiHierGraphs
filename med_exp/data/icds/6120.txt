Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Ceramic on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left hip joint with a ceramic on polyethylene synthetic substitute, utilizing an uncemented fixation method. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues during the operation. The ceramic material used in the artificial joint provides a durable and wear-resistant surface, reducing the risk of implant failure. The polyethylene component helps to simulate the natural movement and function of the hip joint, promoting a smoother and more comfortable recovery.
