Excision of Left Thyroid Gland Lobe, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a thyroid gland lobe through a small incision in the skin, guided by diagnostic imaging techniques to ensure precise localization. A percutaneous approach is used, where a needle or scope is inserted through the skin to access the thyroid gland, allowing for a minimally invasive and less traumatic excision of the affected lobe. The diagnostic nature of this procedure implies that it is performed to obtain a tissue sample for examination and diagnosis, rather than for therapeutic purposes. The left thyroid gland lobe is specifically targeted in this procedure, which may be necessary to diagnose and manage conditions such as thyroid cancer, nodules, or goiters.
