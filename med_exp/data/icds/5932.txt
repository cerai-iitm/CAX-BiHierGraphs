Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a posterior approach is utilized to access the lumbar vertebral joint, which is then fused using a non-autologous tissue substitute. This involves an open approach, where the surgeon makes an incision to visualize the affected area. The non-autologous tissue substitute, often a bone graft or artificial material, is used to promote bone growth and stabilize the joint. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and restore mobility by creating a solid fusion of the lumbar vertebral joint.
