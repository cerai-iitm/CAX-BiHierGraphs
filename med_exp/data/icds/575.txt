Replacement of Thoracic Aorta, Ascending/Arch with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This complex surgical procedure involves replacing a diseased or damaged section of the thoracic aorta, specifically the ascending and arch portions, with a zooplastic tissue graft using an open approach. The procedure requires a median sternotomy, which involves dividing the breastbone to access the thoracic cavity, and careful dissection of surrounding tissues to expose the affected aortic segment. The diseased aorta is then removed and replaced with a zooplastic tissue graft, which is carefully sutured in place to ensure a secure and durable connection. After the graft is in place, the sternotomy is closed and the chest is closed, typically with wires and sutures.
