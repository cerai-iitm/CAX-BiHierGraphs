Inspection of Vagina and Cul-de-sac, External Approach
This procedure involves a visual examination of the vagina and cul-de-sac, which is the space between the uterus and rectum, through an external approach. During the procedure, the healthcare provider will use a speculum to gently open the vagina, allowing for a clear view of the vaginal walls and cervix. The examination may help identify any abnormalities, such as growths, inflammation, or signs of infection. The procedure is typically performed as part of a routine gynecological exam or to investigate symptoms such as abnormal bleeding, pelvic pain, or discharge.
