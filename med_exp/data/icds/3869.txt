Resection of Finger Nail, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically removing a portion or the entire fingernail through an external approach, where the surgeon makes an incision on the skin around the nail to access the nail bed. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as ingrown toenails, nail trauma, or nail infections that do not respond to conservative treatments. The surgery is usually performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort, and the incision site is closed with stitches or surgical glue after the procedure. Following the procedure, patients may experience some pain, swelling, and bruising, but these symptoms typically resolve on their own within a few days.
