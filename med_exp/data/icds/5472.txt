Reposition Left Tibia with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left tibia bone to its correct anatomical alignment through an open approach, where the skin and underlying tissues are incised to access the bone. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then inserted into the medullary canal of the tibia to stabilize the bone and facilitate healing. The device is secured in place to provide structural support and hold the bone in its corrected position, promoting optimal recovery and reducing the risk of further injury or malunion. Through this procedure, the treating physician aims to restore normal function and mobility to the affected lower extremity.
