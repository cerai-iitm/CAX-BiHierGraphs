Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of a lumbar vertebral joint using a non-autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft material derived from a donor or synthetic source. The posterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the affected area through an incision in the back, and performs the fusion in the posterior column, which refers to the rear section of the spine. The open approach involves a larger incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the spine and perform the complex procedure with greater precision. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the spine, alleviate pain and restore mobility to the affected area.
