Excision of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the right upper lung lobe through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach. The operation is typically performed under general anesthesia, using an endoscope to visualize the lung tissue and guide the surgical instruments. The percutaneous approach allows for smaller incisions, reducing post-operative pain and recovery time. The diagnostic excision is usually performed to examine the lung tissue for signs of disease or injury, such as cancer, infection, or trauma.
