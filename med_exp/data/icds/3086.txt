Removal of Extraluminal Device from Stomach, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the stomach and remove an extraluminal device, such as a gastronomy tube or a foreign object, through a small incision in the skin. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The endoscope is guided through the mouth and esophagus into the stomach, and specialized instruments are used to grasp and retrieve the device. The entire procedure is usually completed within 30-60 minutes, and patients can often resume their normal activities within a few hours.
