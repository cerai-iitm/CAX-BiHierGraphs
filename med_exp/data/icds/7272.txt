Detachment at Right Foot, Partial 2nd Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of a portion of the second toe from the right foot through an open approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area. The goal of this operation is to correct deformities or injuries affecting the second ray of the foot, which includes the second toe and its associated bones and soft tissues. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully separates the affected portion of the second toe from the surrounding tissues and bones, taking care to preserve as much healthy tissue as possible. The detached portion is then removed, and the incision is closed, allowing the patient to heal and recover from the surgery.
