Drainage of Stomach with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a drainage device into the stomach through the skin to remove fluids or air that have accumulated due to various conditions such as gastrointestinal obstruction, perforation, or inflammation. A small incision is made in the abdomen, and a catheter or drainage tube is guided through the skin and into the stomach using imaging guidance. Once in place, the device allows for the drainage of stomach contents, alleviating symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, and abdominal distension. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can be done on an inpatient or outpatient basis, depending on the nature of the underlying condition.
