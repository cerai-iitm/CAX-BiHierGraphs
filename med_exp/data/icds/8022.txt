Fluoroscopy of Bilateral External Carotid Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This diagnostic procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, to visualize the bilateral external carotid arteries, which supply blood to the neck and face. Low osmolar contrast medium is injected into the arteries to enhance visualization, allowing for better imaging of the blood vessels and detection of any abnormalities or blockages. The procedure is typically performed in a fluoroscopy suite or radiology department, and may be used to guide interventional procedures or to diagnose conditions such as carotid artery stenosis or dissection. The images obtained provide real-time information, allowing the physician to assess blood flow and vessel anatomy.
