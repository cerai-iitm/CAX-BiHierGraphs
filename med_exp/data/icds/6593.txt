Insertion of Other Device into Uterus and Cervix, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
During a minimally invasive procedure, a medical professional inserts a specialized device into the uterus and cervix through the natural vaginal opening or a surgically created incision. This device is carefully placed to achieve a specific therapeutic goal, such as treating uterine abnormalities or facilitating gynecological procedures. The insertion process typically involves gentle manipulation and guidance under imaging technology or direct visualization to ensure accurate placement. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal uterine function, alleviate symptoms, or prepare the uterus for further interventions.
