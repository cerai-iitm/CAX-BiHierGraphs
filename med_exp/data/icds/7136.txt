Control Bleeding in Left Upper Arm, Open Approach
During an open approach procedure, a surgical incision is made in the left upper arm to access the bleeding site. The surgical team then identifies and isolates the source of bleeding, which may be a severed blood vessel, artery, or vein. Once the source is identified, the bleeding is controlled through various techniques, such as ligation, cauterization, or application of hemostatic agents. The wound is then closed, and the arm is stabilized to promote healing and prevent further bleeding.
