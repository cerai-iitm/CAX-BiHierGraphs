Fusion of 2 or more Cervical Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two or more cervical vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device, which is inserted through a posterior approach, allowing the surgeon to access the anterior column of the spine through an open incision. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize and align the cervical spine, relieving pressure on the surrounding nerves and muscles. During the operation, the surgeon will prepare the site by removing any damaged or herniated discs, and then insert the interbody fusion device to promote bone growth and fusion between the affected vertebrae. As the bone fuses, the patient's spine will become more stable, reducing pain and inflammation in the neck and surrounding areas.
