Restriction of Left Common Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device into the left common iliac artery to restrict blood flow or mitigate stenosis. The minimally invasive approach enables the surgeon to access the targeted artery through a small incision in the skin, reducing tissue trauma and promoting faster recovery. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the device is precisely positioned within the artery to achieve the desired occlusion or narrowing, minimizing the risk of complications. The restricted blood flow helps alleviate symptoms associated with peripheral artery disease, ultimately improving overall cardiovascular health.
