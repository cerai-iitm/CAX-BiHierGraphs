Excision of Pancreas, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of tissue or a sample from the pancreas through a minimally invasive method, using an endoscope inserted through the skin to guide the excision. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan, to ensure accurate targeting of the pancreas. A small incision is made in the skin, and the endoscope is inserted through this incision to visualize the pancreas and guide the excision. The excised tissue or sample is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
