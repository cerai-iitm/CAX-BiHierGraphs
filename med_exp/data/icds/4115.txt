Extraction of Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the chest area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue and connective tissue underneath the skin. The surgeon then manually removes the affected tissue and fascia, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissue and structures. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as infection, inflammation, or traumatic injury that affect the chest area. After the procedure, the incision site is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
