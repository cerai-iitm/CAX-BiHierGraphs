Bypass Coronary Artery, One Artery from Left Internal Mammary with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This procedure involves using the left internal mammary artery as a graft to bypass a blockage in one coronary artery, restoring blood flow to the heart. The surgery is performed through an open approach, where the chest is opened to access the heart and coronary arteries. A section of the saphenous vein, typically from the leg, is harvested and used as a conduit to bypass the blockage. The internal mammary artery is then connected to the coronary artery beyond the blockage, allowing oxygen-rich blood to flow freely to the heart muscle.
