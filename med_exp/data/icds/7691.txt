Introduction of Nutritional Substance into Upper GI, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of a nutritional substance into the upper gastrointestinal tract, also known as the upper GI, through a natural or artificial opening. The upper GI includes the esophagus, stomach, and small intestine, and this procedure is used to provide essential nutrients to patients who have difficulty consuming food orally. The nutritional substance may be in the form of a liquid formula or medication, and is delivered through a tube or other device inserted into the upper GI. The goal of this procedure is to support the patient's nutritional needs and promote overall health and well-being.
