Extirpation of Matter from Intracranial Artery using Stent Retriever, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a stent retriever through an incision in the groin area to access the intracranial artery. The stent retriever is guided through the artery using fluoroscopic imaging to the site of occlusion, where it traps and removes the obstructing material, such as a blood clot. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes around 1-2 hours to complete. Once the procedure is complete, the stent retriever is removed, and the incision site is closed.
