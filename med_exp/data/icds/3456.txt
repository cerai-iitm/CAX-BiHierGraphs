Extraction of Left Hepatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the extraction of blockages or stones from the left hepatic duct, a vital passageway for bile drainage from the liver. The procedure is performed via a natural or artificial opening, allowing the physician to access the duct using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The goal of the procedure is diagnostic, aiming to identify and address any underlying conditions, such as gallstones or strictures, that may be impeding bile flow. The minimally invasive nature of the procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery.
