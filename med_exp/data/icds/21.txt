Drainage of Intracranial Epidural Space, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access the epidural space, which is the outermost membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord. The goal is to drain any abnormal accumulation of blood, fluid, or pus that may be exerting pressure on the brain or causing infection. The open approach requires a craniotomy, where a portion of the skull is temporarily removed to allow access to the epidural space. The affected area is then carefully cleaned and drained, and the skull is closed once the procedure is complete.
