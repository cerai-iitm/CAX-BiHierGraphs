Repair Diaphragm, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a thoracic incision to access the diaphragm, a dome-shaped muscle that separates the chest cavity from the abdominal cavity. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the diaphragm and repair any defects or injuries, such as hernias or tears, using sutures or prosthetic materials. The goal of the procedure is to restore the diaphragm's normal function, which is essential for breathing and maintaining proper organ placement. Following the repair, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored to ensure a smooth recovery.
