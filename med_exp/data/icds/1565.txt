Excision of Portal Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to carefully excise a portion of the portal vein, a vital blood vessel responsible for carrying blood from the digestive organs to the liver. The procedure typically requires a laparotomy, which involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the portal vein. The surgeon then meticulously dissects the vein to identify the affected segment, which is then carefully excised and removed. The remaining healthy portions of the vein are then repaired and sutured to restore normal blood flow.
