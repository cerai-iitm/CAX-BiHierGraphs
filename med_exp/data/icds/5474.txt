Reposition Left Tibia with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the manipulation and realignment of the left tibia bone into its proper position, followed by the insertion of internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods through small incisions in the skin. The purpose is to stabilize and hold the bone in place, promoting healing and facilitating recovery. This minimally invasive percutaneous approach reduces tissue damage, scarring, and risk of infection compared to open surgery. The goal is to restore normal function and anatomy to the affected leg, enabling the patient to regain mobility and reduce pain.
