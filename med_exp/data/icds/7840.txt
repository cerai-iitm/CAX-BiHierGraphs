Measurement of Cardiac Pacemaker, External Approach
This procedure involves the use of an external device to measure the functioning of a cardiac pacemaker, a small medical device implanted in the chest to regulate abnormal heart rhythms. The measurement process typically involves attaching electrodes to the skin, which transmit electrical signals from the pacemaker to an external monitoring device. This allows healthcare professionals to assess the pacemaker's battery life, lead function, and overall performance, ensuring it is working effectively to maintain a healthy heart rhythm. The measurement provides valuable insights for pacemaker maintenance and potential adjustments to optimize its function.
