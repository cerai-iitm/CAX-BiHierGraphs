Excision of Multiple Parathyroid Glands, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of multiple parathyroid glands through an open approach, where an incision is made in the neck to access the affected glands. The goal of the operation is to correct hyperparathyroidism, a condition characterized by excessive production of parathyroid hormone, which can lead to weakened bones, kidney stones, and other health issues. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully identify and excise the diseased glands, taking care to preserve the surrounding tissue and nerves. The removal of multiple glands is often necessary to alleviate symptoms and prevent recurrent disease.
