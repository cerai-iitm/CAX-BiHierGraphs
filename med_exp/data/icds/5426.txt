Reposition Left Upper Femur, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the upper end of the left femur, also known as the femoral head, to its normal anatomic alignment. The operation is performed through an external approach, where the surgeon makes an incision on the skin and accesses the affected area. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully manipulates the bone to restore its natural position, ensuring proper alignment and mobility of the hip joint. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pain, improve mobility, and prevent further damage to the surrounding soft tissues and joints.
