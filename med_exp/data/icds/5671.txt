Fusion of 2 or more Cervical Vertebral Joints with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more cervical vertebral joints using an autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft derived from the patient's own tissue. The anterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the spine through an incision in the front of the neck. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the spine and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to meticulously place the tissue substitute and stabilize the affected joints. The goal of this procedure is to restore spinal stability, alleviate pain, and improve mobility in patients with conditions such as degenerative disc disease or spinal instability.
