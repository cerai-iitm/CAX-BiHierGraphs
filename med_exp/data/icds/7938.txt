Fluoroscopy of Multiple Coronary Artery Bypass Grafts using Low Osmolar Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize multiple coronary artery bypass grafts, which are surgically created paths that redirect blood flow around blocked or narrowed coronary arteries. Low osmolar contrast, a type of iodinated contrast medium, is injected into the grafts to enhance visibility under X-ray. The fluoroscope, a continuous X-ray beam, produces real-time images of the grafts, allowing the practitioner to assess their patency and detect any potential blockages or stenoses. The goal of this procedure is to evaluate the function and integrity of the bypass grafts, ensuring that they are properly redirecting blood flow to the heart muscle.
