Fluoroscopy of Left Common Carotid Artery using Other Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a type of medical imaging that uses X-rays to guide the injection of a contrasting agent into the left common carotid artery. The contrasting agent helps to highlight the artery and its surrounding structures, allowing for clear visualization during the procedure. Fluoroscopy provides real-time imaging, allowing the healthcare provider to closely monitor the injection process and ensure accurate placement of the contrasting agent. The resulting images help diagnose and treat conditions affecting the left common carotid artery, such as narrowing or blockage of the artery.
