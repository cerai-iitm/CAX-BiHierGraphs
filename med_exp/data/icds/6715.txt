Destruction of Prostate, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through the urethra or a small incision to access the prostate gland. The endoscope allows the surgeon to visualize the prostate and target the specific areas that require treatment. Using specialized instruments, the surgeon then destroys the affected prostate tissue, reducing the size of the gland and alleviating symptoms such as urinary retention and frequency. The entire procedure is typically performed on an outpatient basis, allowing patients to return home the same day.
