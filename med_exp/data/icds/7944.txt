Fluoroscopy of Left Heart using Other Contrast
Here is the description:

This diagnostic procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left heart structures in real-time, allowing for detailed assessment of cardiac function and anatomy. A specialized contrast agent, distinct from traditional iodinated or gadolinium-based agents, is administered to enhance image quality and facilitate visualization of cardiac structures. The fluoroscopy machine uses continuous X-ray beams to produce high-definition images of the left heart, enabling precise evaluation of cardiac chamber sizes, valvular function, and blood flow patterns. The procedure provides valuable insights for diagnosing and managing various cardiovascular conditions, such as congenital heart defects, ventricular dysfunction, and cardiac valve disease.
