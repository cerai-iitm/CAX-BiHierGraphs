Resection of Bladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the bladder through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach, allowing for smaller incisions and reduced recovery time. During the procedure, a scope with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through a small incision in the skin and guided to the bladder, where the targeted tissue is then resected. This technique provides direct visualization of the bladder, enabling precise removal of the affected area while minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues. The procedure is often performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as bladder cancer, tumors, or other abnormalities.
