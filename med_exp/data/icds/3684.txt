Excision of Left Lower Arm Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the left lower arm to access the affected area, followed by the removal of damaged or diseased skin tissue. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the skin and underlying structures, enabling precise excision of the targeted skin segment. The procedure is often performed to treat skin cancers, benign growths, or traumatic injuries, and may involve closure of the wound with sutures or staples. The goal of the procedure is to restore the integrity of the skin and underlying tissues, promoting healing and improving overall arm function.
