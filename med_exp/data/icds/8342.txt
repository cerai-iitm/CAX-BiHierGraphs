Electroconvulsive Therapy, Unilateral-Single Seizure
This therapeutic treatment involves the administration of a controlled electrical current to the brain, intentionally triggering a seizure, to help alleviate severe mental health conditions such as depression, schizophrenia, and bipolar disorder. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia, and muscle relaxants are used to prevent physical convulsions. In a unilateral approach, the electric current is applied to one side of the brain, typically the non-dominant side, to minimize cognitive side effects. The goal of this treatment is to stimulate changes in brain chemistry, leading to improved mood regulation and reduced symptoms of mental illness.
