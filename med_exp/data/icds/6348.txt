Excision of Bladder, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open removal of the bladder, which is typically performed to treat bladder cancer, traumatic injury, or benign conditions such as interstitial cystitis. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the bladder, carefully dissecting and isolating the organ from surrounding structures before excising it. The surgery often involves the reconstruction of the urinary tract, which may involve the creation of a urinary diversion, such as an ileal conduit or a neobladder. The goal of the procedure is to remove the diseased or damaged bladder and restore urinary function while minimizing complications and promoting optimal health outcomes.
