Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two or more lumbar vertebral joints using a nonautologous tissue substitute, which is a material that is not derived from the patient's own body. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the back to access the spine. The open approach indicates that the procedure is performed through a traditional open incision, rather than through a minimally invasive or laparoscopic approach. The goal of the procedure is to stabilize the spine and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, and weakness in the lower back and legs.
