Excision of Right Lobe Liver, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves a surgical incision to access the right lobe of the liver, where a portion of the liver tissue is removed for diagnostic purposes. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the liver and surrounding tissue to identify any abnormalities or lesions. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to determine the cause of any symptoms or conditions. The goal of this procedure is to diagnose and potentially treat conditions such as liver cancer, cirrhosis, or other liver diseases.
