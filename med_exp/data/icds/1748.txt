Excision of Mesenteric Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through the skin to visualize the mesenteric lymphatic system. Under imaging guidance, the healthcare provider makes a small incision in the skin to access the mesenteric lymphatic tissue, and then carefully excises the targeted tissue using specialized instruments. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for minimal invasion and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery. The excised tissue is then sent for further examination and diagnosis, providing valuable insights into the underlying condition.
