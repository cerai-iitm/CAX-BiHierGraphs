Replacement of Right Breast with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the replacement of the right breast with a synthetic substitute, utilizing an open approach to access the breast tissue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a surgical incision to facilitate the removal of the existing breast tissue and implantation of the synthetic substitute. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural shape and feel of a breast, restoring the appearance and functionality of the breast. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely place the synthetic substitute and achieve optimal aesthetic results.
