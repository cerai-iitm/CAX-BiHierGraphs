Reposition Right Mandible with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right mandible, or lower jawbone, to its correct alignment using an open approach. The operation requires an incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to reposition the bone and secure it with an internal fixation device. The internal fixation device, typically consisting of plates and screws, is used to hold the bone in place while it heals, providing stability and promoting proper alignment. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal jaw function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, difficulty chewing, or restricted jaw movement.
