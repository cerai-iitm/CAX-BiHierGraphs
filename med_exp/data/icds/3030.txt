Occlusion of Stomach, Pylorus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the intentional closure or blockage of the stomach, specifically the pylorus, through an open incision. The pylorus is the lower portion of the stomach that connects to the small intestine, and occlusion of this area can be performed to treat conditions such as gastric cancer, bleeding, or perforation. During the open approach, the surgeon makes a large incision in the abdomen to access the stomach and pylorus, allowing for direct visualization and manipulation of the tissues. The goal of the procedure is to prevent the flow of food and fluids through the pylorus, which can help alleviate symptoms and improve overall health.
