Drainage of Intracranial Subdural Space with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the scalp to access the intracranial subdural space, which is the area between the surface of the brain and the thin layer of tissue that covers it. The goal of the procedure is to drain fluid, blood, or other substances that have accumulated in this space, which can be life-threatening if left untreated. To achieve this, a drainage device is inserted through the opening to remove the accumulated substances and relieve pressure on the brain. Post-procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored to prevent complications and ensure proper healing.
