Reposition Lumbar Vertebra, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the patient is placed under general anesthesia and an open approach is used to access the lumbar spine. The surgeon makes an incision in the lower back to expose the affected vertebra, and then carefully repositions it to its normal alignment. Any Herniated or bulging discs, bone spurs, or other spinal deformities may also be addressed during the procedure. The goal of the surgery is to relieve pressure on the surrounding nerves and spinal cord, alleviating symptoms such as back pain, numbness, and weakness.
