Excision of Female Perineum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves a surgical excision of the female perineum, a diamond-shaped region located between the vagina and anus, through an open approach. The open approach involves making a larger incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the perineum, allowing for direct visualization of the area. The excision is performed for diagnostic purposes, typically to obtain a tissue sample for further examination or to rule out any potential abnormalities. The goal of the procedure is to provide a definitive diagnosis and guide further treatment, if necessary.
