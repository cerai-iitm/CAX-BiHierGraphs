Resection of Right Ovary, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the pelvic cavity, where the right ovary is carefully located and removed. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure a smooth procedure. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the blood vessels and other surrounding tissues to free the ovary, which is then removed through the incision. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is taken to recovery for post-operative care.
