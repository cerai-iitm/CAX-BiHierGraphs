Resection of Left Neck Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of abnormal or cancerous lymph nodes and surrounding tissue in the left side of the neck through an open incision. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be indicated for the treatment of various conditions, including metastatic cancer, lymphoma, or benign tumors. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the neck to access the affected lymph nodes and carefully dissect and removes the diseased tissue. The resulting defect is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is closely monitored for any complications during the recovery period.
