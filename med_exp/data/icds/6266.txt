Dilation of Right Ureter with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the introduction of an intraluminal device into the right ureter through a natural or artificial opening to dilate and widen the narrow ureteral passage. The device is carefully guided through the ureter to the desired location, allowing for precise dilation and minimizing risk to surrounding tissues. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can help to alleviate symptoms such as kidney stones, pain, or urinary tract infections. By widening the ureter, this procedure can improve urinary flow and reduce the risk of further complications.
