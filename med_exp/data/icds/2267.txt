Extirpation of Matter from Lingula Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the lingula bronchus, a region in the lung that can be prone to foreign object aspiration or mucus buildup. Using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, the practitioner navigates through the natural or artificial opening to reach the affected area. Forceps, suction, or other specialized instruments are then utilized to carefully extract the obstructing matter, restoring normal respiratory function and alleviating symptoms such as coughing or shortness of breath. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes a faster recovery.
