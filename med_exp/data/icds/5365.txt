Removal of External Fixation Device from Right Tibia, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an external fixation device from the right tibia, a bone in the lower leg, through an external approach. The external fixation device, typically used to stabilize and heal fractures or osteotomies, is carefully disassembled and removed from the skin and underlying tissue. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia or conscious sedation, and may require some manipulation of the soft tissue and bone to ensure safe and complete removal of the device. After removal, the surgical site is thoroughly cleaned and closed, and the patient is provided with post-operative instructions to ensure proper healing and prevent complications.
