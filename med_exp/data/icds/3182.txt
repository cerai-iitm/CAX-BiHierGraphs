Resection of Ileum, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the ileum, a portion of the small intestine. The affected segment of the ileum is then carefully identified and removed, and the remaining healthy portions of the intestine are reconnected to restore normal digestive function. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor for any potential complications. The goal of the surgery is to remove diseased or damaged tissue, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall quality of life.
