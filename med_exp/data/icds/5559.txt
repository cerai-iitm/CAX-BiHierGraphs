Supplement Left Tarsal with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the supplementation of the left tarsal bone with an autologous tissue substitute through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the area. The autologous tissue substitute, which is taken from the patient's own body, is used to enhance or repair the tarsal bone, often to address defects or damage. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the supplementation with precision. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and structure to the left foot and ankle.
