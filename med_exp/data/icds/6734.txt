Excision of Prostate, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of prostate tissue through the skin, typically using a needle or biopsy device, to obtain a sample for diagnostic evaluation. The goal is to diagnose conditions such as prostate cancer, prostatitis, or benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH). During the procedure, imaging guidance such as ultrasound or MRI is often used to ensure accurate targeting of the prostate tissue. The obtained tissue sample is then sent to a laboratory for examination and diagnosis.
