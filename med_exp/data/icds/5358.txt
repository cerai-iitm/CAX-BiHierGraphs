Removal of External Fixation Device from Right Patella, External Approach
During an outpatient procedure, a surgeon removes an external fixation device from the right kneecap, also known as the patella, through an external approach. This involves making a small incision in the skin and tissues surrounding the patella to access the device. The device, which was previously used to stabilize and support the patella during healing, is carefully withdrawn from the body. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes around 30 minutes to an hour to complete, allowing the patient to return to normal activities shortly after.
