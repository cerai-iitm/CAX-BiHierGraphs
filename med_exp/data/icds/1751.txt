Excision of Pelvis Lymphatic, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of lymph nodes in the pelvic area through a small incision in the skin. Using imaging guidance, a needle or scope is inserted through the incision to precisely locate and excise the affected lymph nodes. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and is used to diagnose conditions such as cancer, infection, or inflammation in the pelvic lymph nodes. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to determine the cause of the condition.
