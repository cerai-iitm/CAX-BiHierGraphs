Dilation of Right Common Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, the right common iliac artery is accessed through an open incision to treat conditions such as atherosclerosis or Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD). The surgeon then uses an intraluminal device, such as a balloon angioplasty catheter, to dilate the narrowed or blocked section of the artery. This helps to restore blood flow to the lower extremities, relieving symptoms such as leg pain, numbness, or weakness. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for post-operative care and monitoring.
