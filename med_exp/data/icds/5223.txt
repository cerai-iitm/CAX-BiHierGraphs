Excision of Right Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the right tibia, also known as the shinbone, which is located in the lower leg. The excision process targets a specific section of the tibia, which may be damaged due to injury or disease, and carefully removes the affected area. The open approach method allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissues, ensuring a precise and effective excision. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored to ensure proper healing and recovery.
