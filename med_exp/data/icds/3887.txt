Transfer Neck Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves relocating skin from the neck area to another part of the body, typically to repair or reconstruct damaged or defective tissue. The external approach indicates that the skin is accessed and transferred through an open incision, rather than through a minimally invasive or laparoscopic method. The neck skin is carefully harvested, taking care to preserve blood vessels and other important structures, and then transplanted to the desired location. The goal of this procedure is to restore functional and cosmetic integrity to the affected area.
