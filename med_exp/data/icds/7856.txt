Performance of Cardiac Output, Single, Manual
This procedure involves the manual measurement of cardiac output, which is the volume of blood pumped by the heart per minute, to assess cardiovascular function and diagnose potential heart conditions. A trained healthcare professional performs the measurement using specialized equipment, such as a thermodilution catheter or an arterial catheter, to accurately determine cardiac output. The manual approach allows for a more precise calculation of cardiac output, which is essential for guiding treatment decisions and monitoring patient response to interventions. The resulting data provides valuable insights into cardiac function, enabling healthcare providers to develop effective care plans and improve patient outcomes.
