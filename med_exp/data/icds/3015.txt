Inspection of Lower Intestinal Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This medical procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visually examine the lower intestinal tract, which includes the colon, rectum, and anus. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to inspect the inner lining of the intestines for any abnormalities, such as polyps, ulcers, or bleeding. During the procedure, the healthcare provider may also collect tissue samples or remove any growths or abnormalities found. The goal of this procedure is to diagnose and treat conditions affecting the lower intestinal tract, such as colon cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, or gastrointestinal bleeding.
