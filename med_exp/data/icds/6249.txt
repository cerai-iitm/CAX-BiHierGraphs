Bypass Bilateral Ureters to Ileocutaneous, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This complex urological procedure involves creating a detour for urine flow by connecting both ureters to a segment of the ileum, a part of the small intestine, through a skin opening. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows surgeons to access the ureters and ileum through small incisions in the skin, utilizing specialized instruments and cameras for visualization. The bypass procedure is typically performed to divert the flow of urine away from a diseased or damaged bladder or urethra, restoring normal urinary function and alleviating symptoms. Post-operatively, the urine flows from the ileum through the skin opening, which requires a urostomy bag for collection.
