Excision of Right Saphenous Vein, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the skin to access the right saphenous vein. Through an endoscope, a thin tube with a camera and light, the surgeon visualizes the vein and removes the damaged or diseased section using specialized instruments. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete. The percutaneous endoscopic approach offers less tissue trauma, reduced scarring, and a faster recovery time compared to traditional open surgery.
