Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Pulmonary Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device through the skin and into the left pulmonary artery, allowing for targeted delivery of medications or fluids directly to the lungs. Performed under imaging guidance, a small incision is made in the skin, and a thin catheter is advanced through the pulmonary artery to the desired location. The infusion device is then inserted through the catheter, enabling precise control over medication administration. This approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes optimal treatment outcomes for patients requiring prolonged infusion therapy.
