Excision of Right Lower Femur, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the right lower femur, also known as the distal femur, which is the lower end of the thigh bone. The surgeon then removes a portion or all of the affected femur, which may be necessary to treat conditions such as bone tumors, infected bone, or severe fractures that cannot be repaired. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the bone and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to safely excise the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to restore functional mobility and alleviate pain and discomfort in the affected leg.
