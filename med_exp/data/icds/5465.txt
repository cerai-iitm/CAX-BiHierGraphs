Reposition Right Tibia with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the repositioning of the right tibia, which is the shinbone, to its normal anatomical position. To achieve this, a minimally invasive, percutaneous approach is used, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the bone. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then inserted into the medullary canal of the tibia through this incision, which helps to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The device is designed to provide internal support and fixation to the bone, allowing it to heal in the correct position.
