Excision of Epiglottis, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of the epiglottis, a flap-like structure located at the entrance of the larynx, through a natural or artificial opening. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. Using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a light and camera, the surgeon visualizes the epiglottis and dissects it from surrounding tissues, then carefully excises it. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as epiglottitis, which can cause airway obstruction and breathing difficulties.
