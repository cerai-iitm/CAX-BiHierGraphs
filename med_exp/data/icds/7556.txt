Packing of Nasal Region using Packing Material
This procedure involves carefully placing a packing material within the nasal region to control bleeding, promote healing, or maintain tissue structure. The packing material is gently inserted into the nasal cavity, taking care to avoid any sensitive areas or fragile structures. The packing helps to apply gentle pressure, reducing swelling and promoting clotting of any bleeding blood vessels. By maintaining the packing in place, the nasal region can heal properly, and any further complications can be minimized.
