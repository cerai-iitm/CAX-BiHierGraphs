Excision of Left Cephalic Vein, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left cephalic vein, a superficial vein located in the upper arm. The vein is then carefully dissected and excised, or removed, from the surrounding tissue. The cephalic vein is a common site for intravenous access, and excision may be necessary due to damage, infection, or other complications. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the vein and surrounding tissue, enabling precise dissection and removal of the affected vessel.
