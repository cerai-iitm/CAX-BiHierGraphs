Reposition Left Upper Femur with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the realignment of the left upper femur bone through a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the bone. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then inserted through the incision to stabilize the bone, allowing for proper healing and alignment. The device is designed to provide internal support and fixation, enabling the bone to regain its natural position and function. This minimally invasive procedure reduces tissue damage and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open approaches.
