Excision of Left Lower Arm Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of skin tissue from the left lower arm through an external approach, typically used to diagnose underlying conditions or lesions. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia, and the skin is carefully dissected to expose the affected area. Once the skin is removed, the underlying tissue is examined for signs of disease or infection, and any necessary biopsies or further testing are conducted. The surgical site is then closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
