Plain Radiography of Splenic Arteries using Other Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of other contrast agents to visualize the splenic arteries through plain radiography. The contrast medium is injected into the arterial system, allowing the X-ray beams to produce detailed images of the splenic artery and its branches. The resulting radiographs provide valuable information about the anatomy and potential abnormalities of the splenic arteries, such as stenosis, aneurysms, or occlusions. The procedure is essential in diagnosing and monitoring vascular conditions affecting the spleen and adjacent organs.
