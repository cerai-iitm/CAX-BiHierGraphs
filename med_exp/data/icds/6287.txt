Dilation of Bladder Neck, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the bladder neck and dilate it through a natural or artificial opening to treat conditions such as bladder neck contracture or stenosis. The endoscope is inserted through the urethra, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the bladder neck and perform the dilation under direct vision. The dilation is achieved using specialized instruments or balloons, which are guided through the endoscope to gently stretch and widen the bladder neck. This procedure can help to relieve symptoms such as urinary retention, pain, or difficulty urinating by improving the flow of urine through the bladder neck.
