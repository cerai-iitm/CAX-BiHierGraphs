Detachment at Right Upper Leg, Low, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the lower portion of the right upper leg to access the affected area. The detachment process involves separating or releasing the affected tissue or structure from the surrounding tissue or bone. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the issue. The procedure requires careful dissection and handling of the surrounding tissue to ensure minimal damage and promote optimal healing.
