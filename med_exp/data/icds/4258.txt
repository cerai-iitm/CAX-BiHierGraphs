Repair Left Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This procedure involves making small incisions in the skin to repair damaged subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the left lower leg. The percutaneous approach allows the surgeon to access the affected area through tiny puncture sites, minimizing tissue disruption and promoting faster healing. The damaged tissue is then sutured or repaired using specialized instruments and techniques to restore normal anatomy and function. The goal of this procedure is to restore strength, stability, and mobility to the left lower leg.
