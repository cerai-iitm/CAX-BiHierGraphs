Excision of Thoracic Spinal Cord, Open Approach
This invasive surgical procedure involves making an incision in the thoracic region to access the spinal cord, followed by the removal of a targeted section of the thoracic spinal cord to treat conditions such as spinal cord tumors, abscesses, or hematomas. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the affected area and remove the damaged or diseased tissue with precision. The procedure requires great care to avoid damaging surrounding nerve roots and spinal cord structures to minimize potential neurological deficits. Postoperative care typically involves close monitoring and rehabilitation to aid in the patient's recovery and management of potential complications.
