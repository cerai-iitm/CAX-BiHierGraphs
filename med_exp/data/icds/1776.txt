Excision of Thymus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest cavity to access the thymus gland, a small organ located in the thoracic cavity, behind the breastbone. The thymus gland is carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding tissue and structures. The goal of this open approach is to completely excise the thymus gland, which may be necessary to treat certain medical conditions such as myasthenia gravis or thymoma. The procedure requires careful handling of nearby vital structures, including nerves, blood vessels, and organs, to minimize potential complications and ensure a successful outcome.
