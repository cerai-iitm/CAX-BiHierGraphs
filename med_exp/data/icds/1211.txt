Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Femoral Artery, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

The procedure involves inserting an infusion device through the skin and into the right femoral artery using a percutaneous approach, which means that the insertion is performed through a small incision in the skin. This type of procedure is typically done under local anesthesia and with the aid of imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. The infusion device is advanced through the artery to the desired location, allowing for the administration of medications or fluids directly into the bloodstream. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and reduces the risk of complications.
