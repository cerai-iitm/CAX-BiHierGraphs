Supplement Left Tibia with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the left tibia with a non-autologous tissue substitute to repair or replace damaged or diseased bone tissue. The open approach is used, which involves making an incision in the skin and exposing the affected area to access the tibia. The non-autologous tissue substitute, such as a bone graft or synthetic material, is then inserted to stabilize and support the weakened bone. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain and limited mobility.
