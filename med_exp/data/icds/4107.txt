Extirpation of Matter from Left Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the left lower leg through an open incision. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected area, carefully dissecting the tissue to locate and extract the foreign material. The affected tissue is thoroughly cleaned and debrided to prevent infection and promote healing. The wound is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, to facilitate recovery and restore function to the affected leg.
