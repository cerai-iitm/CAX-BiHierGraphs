Extraction of Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the esophagus through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for the extraction of tissues or foreign objects from the esophageal tract. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the removal of targeted tissues or objects under direct visualization. The procedure is usually performed under sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and cooperation. The extracted tissues are then sent for further examination and diagnosis to guide treatment decisions.
