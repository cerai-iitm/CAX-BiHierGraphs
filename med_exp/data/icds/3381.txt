Drainage of Pancreas, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a light and camera is inserted through the mouth or a small incision to access the pancreas. The endoscope is guided through the natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth, stomach, or a surgically created opening, to reach the pancreas. The goal of the procedure is to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances from the pancreas, which can help alleviate symptoms of pancreatitis or other pancreatic disorders. This minimally invasive approach allows for effective drainage and can reduce the risk of complications compared to open surgical methods.
