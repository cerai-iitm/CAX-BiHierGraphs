Drainage of Amniotic Fluid, Therapeutic from Products of Conception, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the therapeutic drainage of amniotic fluid from the products of conception through a natural or artificial opening. The goal of this intervention is to remove excess fluid that may be causing discomfort, pain, or complications during pregnancy. The procedure is typically performed under the guidance of a healthcare provider and may involve the use of specialized instruments or equipment to carefully drain the amniotic fluid. By relieving the pressure and discomfort associated with excess amniotic fluid, this procedure can help promote a healthier pregnancy and reduce the risk of complications.
