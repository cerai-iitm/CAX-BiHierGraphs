Drainage of Right Knee Joint, Percutaneous Approach
The procedure involves inserting a needle or a small tube through the skin and into the right knee joint to drain excess fluid or other substances that may be causing pain, swelling, or inflammation. This percutaneous approach is a minimally invasive method that allows for the removal of unwanted material without the need for open surgery. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure and reduce discomfort in the knee joint, promoting healing and improving range of motion. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and guided by imaging technologies such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device.
