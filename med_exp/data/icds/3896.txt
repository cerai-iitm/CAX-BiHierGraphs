Transfer Right Lower Arm Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves the transfer of skin from one area to another on the right lower arm. The external approach is utilized, which means the skin is harvested from the donor site and transferred to the recipient site on the same arm. This technique is often used to repair skin defects or replace damaged skin with healthy skin. The goal of the procedure is to restore the appearance and function of the affected area, promoting healing and tissue repair.
