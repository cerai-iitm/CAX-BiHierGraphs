Excision of Right Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The damaged or diseased tissue is then carefully excised, and any necessary repairs or closures are made to restore the integrity of the affected area. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the target tissue, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the affected tissue and minimize trauma to surrounding structures. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms, restore function, and promote healing in the right lower leg.
