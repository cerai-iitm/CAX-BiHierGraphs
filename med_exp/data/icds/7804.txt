Monitoring of Central Nervous Electrical Activity, Intraoperative, Open Approach
Intraoperative monitoring of central nervous system electrical activity is a crucial aspect of surgical procedures, ensuring the health and integrity of the brain and spinal cord. This invasive approach involves opening the skull or spine to directly access the Central Nervous System (CNS), allowing for real-time monitoring of electrical impulses during surgery. This precise monitoring enables surgeons to identify potential complications, adjust their technique, and make data-driven decisions to minimize the risk of neurological damage. By continuously tracking CNS activity, surgeons can provide optimal care and improve patient outcomes.
