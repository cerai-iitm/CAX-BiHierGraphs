Restriction of Right Common Iliac Artery with Branched or Fenestrated Intraluminal Device, One or Two Arteries, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of a branched or fenestrated intraluminal device in the right common iliac artery to restrict blood flow and treat conditions such as aneurysms or stenosis. The device is introduced through a small incision in the groin or arm, using a catheter guided by imaging technology. Once in place, the device expands to fit the artery, diverting blood flow and reducing pressure on the affected area. This percutaneous approach offers a less traumatic alternative to open surgery, promoting faster recovery and reduced risk of complications.
