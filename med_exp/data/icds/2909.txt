Excision of Greater Omentum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the greater omentum, a double layer of fatty tissue that attaches the stomach to other organs in the abdominal cavity. The open approach involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the greater omentum. The diagnostic purpose of the procedure is to determine the cause of symptoms or to identify the presence of a disease or condition affecting the greater omentum. The removed tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
