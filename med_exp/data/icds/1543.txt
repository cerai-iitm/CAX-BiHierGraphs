Dilation of Portal Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of an intraluminal device into the portal vein through a small incision in the skin, allowing for dilation of the vessel to improve blood flow. The portal vein is accessed using a percutaneous approach, which eliminates the need for open surgery. The intraluminal device is carefully guided into position using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement. The dilation procedure helps to treat conditions such as portal hypertension, which can lead to complications including bleeding and liver damage.
