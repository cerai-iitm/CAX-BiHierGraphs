Introduction of Nutritional Substance into Peripheral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a thin needle or catheter into a peripheral vein, typically in the arm or hand, to administer nutritional substances such as amino acids, vitamins, and minerals directly into the bloodstream. The nutritional substance is prepared according to the patient's specific needs and is slowly infused into the vein over a period of time. This method allows for efficient and targeted delivery of essential nutrients, which can be especially beneficial for patients who have difficulty absorbing nutrients through oral consumption or have increased nutritional requirements due to medical conditions. The percutaneous approach ensures a minimally invasive and relatively painless experience for the patient.
