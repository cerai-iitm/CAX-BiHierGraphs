Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Cervical Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

Surgical insertion of internal fixation devices, such as plates or rods, into the cervical vertebral joint is performed to stabilize and align the spine. This open approach involves making an incision in the neck to access the affected joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and accurately place the fixation device. The device is carefully secured to the vertebrae to provide stability and support, promoting fusion and healing. By immobilizing the joint, the device helps to alleviate pain, restore function, and improve mobility in the neck and arm regions.
