Repair Right Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
During this open approach procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the right lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue that surround muscles and other structures. The damaged or defective tissue and fascia are then repaired, restored, or strengthened using sutures, grafts, or other materials to promote healing and improve function. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as lacerations, tears, or defects in the tissue and fascia, or to relieve symptoms like pain, swelling, or limited mobility. Following the repair, the incision is closed, and the leg is immobilized to allow for proper healing and recovery.
