Reposition Left Ulna with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left ulna bone to its correct anatomical position, typically required to treat fractures or deformities. Through an open surgical approach, the surgeon accesses the affected area to realign the bone and stabilize it with an intramedullary internal fixation device. This device is inserted into the medullary cavity of the ulna, providing internal support and promoting healing. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay, followed by a rehabilitation period to restore full function and mobility.
