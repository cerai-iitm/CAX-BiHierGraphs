Replacement of Right Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the femoral surface of the right hip joint with a synthetic substitute, utilizing a cemented fixation method to secure the implant. The process is performed through an open approach, providing direct visualization of the joint and allowing for precise placement of the prosthetic component. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and restore mobility in patients with severe hip joint damage or degeneration. The cemented synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement of the hip joint, promoting a return to normal function and daily activities.
