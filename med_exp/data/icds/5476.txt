Reposition Left Tibia with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the surgical repositioning of the left tibia to its normal anatomical position, typically performed to treat a fracture or deformity. The intramedullary internal fixation device is inserted through a small incision in the skin, and guided through the marrow canal of the tibia to stabilize the bone. The device is designed to provide internal support and facilitate healing, allowing the bone to grow back together in its proper alignment. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and promotes a faster recovery compared to open surgical techniques.
