Bypass Left Kidney Pelvis to Cutaneous with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves creating a bypass between the left kidney pelvis and the skin to allow urine to drain when the normal flow is blocked or obstructed. A synthetic substitute, such as a graft or stent, is used to connect the kidney pelvis to the skin. The procedure is performed percutaneously, meaning through the skin, under image guidance, to minimize incisions and promote faster healing. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate kidney damage and associated symptoms, such as pain, infection, and kidney stone formation, by restoring urine flow and reducing pressure in the kidney.
