Detachment at Right 1st Toe, High, Open Approach
Surgical detachment of the right 1st toe is performed to address severe deformities, injuries, or infections that cannot be treated with other methods. This open approach involves a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to sever the toe from the foot. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve rehabilitation and physical therapy to ensure a smooth recovery. The goal of the detachment is to restore functionality and alleviate pain, allowing the individual to regain normal mobility and quality of life.
