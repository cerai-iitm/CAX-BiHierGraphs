Fluoroscopy of Left Kidney, Ureter and Bladder
This diagnostic imaging procedure uses continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time images of the left kidney, ureter, and bladder, allowing clinicians to visualize the internal structures in motion. During the procedure, a fluorescent dye may be injected into the urinary system to enhance visibility. The fluoroscopy machine rotates around the patient, capturing images from multiple angles, which are then displayed on a monitor for the healthcare provider to interpret. This non-invasive procedure is commonly used to diagnose and monitor conditions affecting the urinary tract, such as kidney stones, tumors, or obstructive disorders.
