Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Left Knee Joint, Tibial Surface, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left knee to access the tibial surface, which is the upper part of the shinbone. The synthetic substitute, such as a failed implant or artificial joint, is then carefully removed from the knee joint through the open approach. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal knee function and alleviate pain or other complications caused by the faulty implant. Following removal, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically advised to follow a rehabilitation program to promote healing and regain strength in the knee.
