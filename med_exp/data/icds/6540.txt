Excision of Right Fallopian Tube, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower abdomen to access the pelvic cavity, where the right fallopian tube is located. The tube is then carefully dissected and removed, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and organs. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve a hospital stay to recover. The goal of the surgery is to remove the diseased or damaged fallopian tube to alleviate symptoms and improve overall health.
