Assistance with Cardiac Output using Balloon Pump, Continuous
This invasive cardiac procedure involves the insertion of a balloon pump to assist with cardiac output, providing temporary mechanical support to the heart. The balloon pump is designed to increase myocardial oxygen perfusion and decrease workload on the heart, thereby improving cardiac function. The continuous assistance provided by the balloon pump helps to stabilize the patient's hemodynamic status, reduce symptoms, and potentially prevent further cardiac damage. The procedure is typically performed in critically ill patients, such as those experiencing cardiogenic shock, acute myocardial infarction, or cardiac arrest.
