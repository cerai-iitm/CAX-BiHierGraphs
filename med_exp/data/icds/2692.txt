Dilation of Duodenum with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves inserting a specialized device through a natural or artificial opening to reach the duodenum, a section of the small intestine. Once in place, the device is used to gently dilate or stretch the duodenum, helping to widen the narrow passage and relieve any blockages or obstructions. This minimally invasive approach allows for a more precise and targeted treatment, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. By dilating the duodenum, this procedure can help restore normal digestive function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
