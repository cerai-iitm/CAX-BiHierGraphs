Excision of Left Upper Leg Muscle, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the left upper leg to access the muscle tissue. The affected muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, from the surrounding tissue and surrounding structures. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the area and enables the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the damaged or diseased muscle tissue. The incision is then closed, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
