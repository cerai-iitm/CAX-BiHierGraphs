Fluoroscopy of Thoracic Aorta using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the thoracic aorta, which is the portion of the aorta that passes through the chest cavity. During the procedure, a contrast agent is injected into the bloodstream to enhance the visibility of the aorta and its surrounding structures. The fluoroscope, an X-ray machine that captures continuous images, is then used to guide the procedure and produce real-time images of the aorta. This allows the healthcare provider to assess the aorta's structure and function, diagnose any abnormalities, and guide interventions or further treatment.
