Excision of Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of a portion of the stomach tissue through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for diagnostic examination and potential treatment of stomach-related conditions. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. A flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through the mouth or a small incision, enabling the surgeon to visualize and access the stomach lining. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
