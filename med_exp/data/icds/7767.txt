Irrigation of Female Reproductive using Irrigating Substance, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the flush of the female reproductive system to aid in the diagnosis of potential issues, utilizing a sterile irrigating substance introduced through a natural or artificial opening. The goal of this diagnostic irrigation is to collect a sample of the irrigated fluid or debris, which can then be sent to a laboratory for further examination. This helps healthcare providers identify any abnormalities, such as infections, inflammation, or cellular changes, that may be present in the reproductive tract. By flushing out the system, this procedure enables clinicians to gather valuable information to inform a diagnosis and guide subsequent treatment.
