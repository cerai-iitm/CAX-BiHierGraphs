Release Right Ureter, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the right ureter, a muscular tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder. The goal of the procedure is to release the ureter from any obstruction, adhesion, or compression that may be impeding its normal function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the ureter and surrounding tissues, enabling precise dissection and repair. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve temporary placement of a stent in the ureter to ensure proper healing and drainage.
