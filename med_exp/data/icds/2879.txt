Excision of Transverse Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to excise a portion of the transverse colon through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is inserted through the opening, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the colon and identify the area to be excised. The excision is typically performed using specialized instruments, such as forceps or snares, that are inserted through the endoscope. The excised tissue is then removed through the opening, and the area is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing.
