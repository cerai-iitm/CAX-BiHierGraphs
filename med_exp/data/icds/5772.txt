Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Elbow Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an orthopedic surgeon accesses the right elbow joint through an open incision to remove an internal fixation device, such as plates, screws, or rods, that was previously implanted to stabilize and heal a fracture or other bone defect. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the operation. The surgeon carefully dissects the soft tissue and removes the internal fixation device, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding nerves, blood vessels, and muscles. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is advised to follow a rehabilitation program to restore strength and mobility in the affected elbow joint.
