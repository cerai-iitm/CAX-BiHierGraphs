Drainage of Perineum Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating an incision in the perineum, the area between the scrotum and anus, to drain an abscess or infected tissue in the subcutaneous layer and fascia. The open approach allows for direct visualization and exploration of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to remove any infected or damaged tissue and promote healing. The procedure is often performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and may require a temporary drain to be left in place to facilitate the removal of fluid or pus. Post-operatively, patients may experience pain, swelling, and bruising in the perineum, which can be managed with pain medication and wound care instructions.
