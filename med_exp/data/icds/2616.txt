Bypass Jejunum to Ileum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass between the jejunum and ileum, two sections of the small intestine, to allow for the passage of digestive contents. The open approach requires a large incision in the abdomen, allowing the surgeon to visualize the intestines and perform the bypass. The jejunum is disconnected from the proximal intestine and reattached to the ileum, creating a detour around a diseased or damaged section of the intestine. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as intestinal obstruction, cancer, or traumatic injury.
