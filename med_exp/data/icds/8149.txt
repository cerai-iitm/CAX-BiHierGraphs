Fluoroscopy of Inferior Vena Cava using Other Contrast
This radiological procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the inferior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the lower body to the heart. During the procedure, a specialized contrast agent is administered to enhance the visibility of the vena cava and its surrounding structures. Real-time X-ray images are then taken using a fluoroscope to guide the procedure and ensure accurate placement of the contrast agent. The resulting images enable physicians to assess the patency and integrity of the vena cava, which is essential for diagnosing and treating various vascular conditions.
