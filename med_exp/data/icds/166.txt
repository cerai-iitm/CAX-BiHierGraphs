Removal of Drainage Device from Cerebral Ventricle, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a drainage device that has been previously implanted in the cerebral ventricle to drain excess cerebrospinal fluid. The removal is performed through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, which involves making a small incision in the skin to access the ventricle. The drainage device is carefully disconnected and withdrawn from the ventricle through the incision, and the site is then closed. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal cerebrospinal fluid flow and alleviate symptoms such as headache, vomiting, and seizures.
