Division of Left Upper Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left upper arm to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue just beneath the skin. The purpose of the procedure is to divide or separate these layers, which may be necessary to relieve compression or tension on surrounding muscles and nerves. During the open approach, the surgeon makes a direct incision to visualize the affected area and performs the division of the subcutaneous tissue and fascia under direct vision. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or tingling in the arm.
