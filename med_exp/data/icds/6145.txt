Replacement of Right Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Metal Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the femoral surface of the right hip joint with a metal synthetic substitute through an open approach. The damaged or diseased joint is accessed through an incision, allowing the surgeon to remove the affected femoral head and replace it with a metal implant designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the original joint. The metal synthetic substitute is secured to the surrounding bone, recreating a stable and functional joint. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and restore mobility and flexibility to the hip joint, enabling patients to resume normal daily activities.
