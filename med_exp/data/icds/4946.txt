Excision of Sternum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove a portion of the sternum, also known as the breastbone, for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the sternum. The affected section of the sternum is then carefully excised, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples. The excised tissue is sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in diagnosing the underlying condition.
