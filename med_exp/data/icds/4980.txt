Excision of Left Radius, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to remove the left radius bone in its entirety. The operation is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a thorough preoperative evaluation to assess the patient's overall health and identify any potential risks. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the forearm to access the radius bone, carefully dissecting the surrounding tissues to avoid damaging the surrounding nerves and blood vessels. The entire radius bone is then removed, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to facilitate healing.
