Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the lumbosacral joint, which connects the lowest part of the spine to the sacrum, using a patient's own tissue substitute, such as bone or other biological material, to promote healing and stability. The operation is performed through a posterior approach, meaning the surgeon accesses the joint from the back, and focuses on the posterior column, the rear portion of the spine. The open approach indicates that the surgery involves a larger incision to allow for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding anatomical structures. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate chronic back pain, instability, or other symptoms caused by degenerative disc disease, spinal deformity, or other conditions affecting the lumbosacral joint.
