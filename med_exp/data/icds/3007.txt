Inspection of Stomach, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to visually examine the stomach and surrounding tissue. During the open approach, the surgeon makes a larger incision to access the stomach, allowing for a more direct and thorough inspection. The stomach is carefully inspected for any abnormalities, injuries, or diseases that may be present. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to diagnose and treat conditions such as stomach ulcers, bleeding, or cancer.
