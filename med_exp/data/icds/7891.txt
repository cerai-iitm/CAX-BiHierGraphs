Perfusion of Urinary System Donor Organ, Single
This medical procedure involves the passage of a fluid through the urinary system of a single donor organ to preserve its function and viability for transplantation. The goal of this perfusion is to maintain the organ's integrity and prevent damage during transportation and storage. The procedure is a crucial step in the organ donation process, ensuring that the urinary system functions properly after transplantation. By perfusing the donor organ, healthcare professionals can improve the chances of a successful transplant and reduce the risk of complications in the recipient.
