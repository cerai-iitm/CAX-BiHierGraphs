Performance of Urinary Filtration, Single
This procedure involves the use of a device to filter urine in the urinary tract, aiming to remove blood clots, stones, or other debris that may be obstructing the flow of urine. The filtration process is performed on a single occasion, and may be done in conjunction with other medical interventions to address underlying conditions. The goal of urinary filtration is to restore normal urinary function, alleviate symptoms such as pain or frequent urination, and prevent complications like kidney damage or infection. By removing obstacles in the urinary tract, this procedure helps to improve overall urinary health and promote a faster recovery.
