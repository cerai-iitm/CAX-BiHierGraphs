Fluoroscopy of Superior Vena Cava using Other Contrast, Guidance
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, to visualize the superior vena cava, a large vein that carries deoxygenated blood to the heart. During the procedure, a contrast agent, other than the standard iodinated or gadolinium-based agents, is injected into the vein to enhance image quality. The use of fluoroscopic guidance allows the healthcare provider to precisely monitor the flow of the contrast agent and obtain real-time images of the superior vena cava. The resulting images help diagnose and treat conditions affecting the superior vena cava, such as venous thrombosis or compression.
