Bypass Trachea to Cutaneous with Tracheostomy Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves creating a bypass connection between the trachea and the skin using a tracheostomy device, allowing for air to pass directly from the trachea to the outside of the body. A small incision is made in the neck and a tracheostomy tube is inserted through the skin and into the trachea, establishing a temporary airway. The tube is secured in place, enabling the patient to breathe more easily and facilitating respiratory function. This life-saving intervention is often performed in emergency situations, such as when a patient's airway is obstructed or compromised.
