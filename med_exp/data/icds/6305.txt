Drainage of Left Kidney Pelvis with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to relieve blockage or obstruction of the left kidney pelvis, allowing for the free flow of urine. Using imaging guidance, the interventional radiologist or urologist creates a small incision in the skin to access the kidney pelvis. A drainage catheter or tube is then advanced through the skin and into the kidney pelvis to establish a pathway for urine drainage. This percutaneous approach typically offers less risk and scarring compared to open surgical methods, promoting a faster recovery and reduced hospital stay.
