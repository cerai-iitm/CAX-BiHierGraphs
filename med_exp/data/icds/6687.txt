Resection of Bilateral Fallopian Tubes, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both fallopian tubes through a natural or artificial opening in the body, typically performed laparoscopically with the aid of a camera and specialized instruments. The surgeon makes small incisions in the abdomen, allowing insertion of the laparoscope and instruments to visualize and carefully excise the fallopian tubes. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as ectopic pregnancy, chronic pelvic pain, or to prevent future pregnancy. The removal of the fallopian tubes eliminates the possibility of pregnancy, making it a permanent form of birth control.
