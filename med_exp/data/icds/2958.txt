Extirpation of Matter from Peritoneum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the peritoneum, a membrane lining the abdominal cavity. The surgeon then manually removes or aspirates unwanted matter, such as infected material, blood, or foreign objects, from the peritoneum. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the peritoneal cavity, enabling the surgeon to thoroughly remove the unwanted substance. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as peritonitis, an inflammatory response to infection or irritation, and to promote healing and recovery.
