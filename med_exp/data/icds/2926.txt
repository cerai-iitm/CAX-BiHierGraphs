Excision of Mesentery, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a portion of the mesentery, a fold of membrane that attaches the small intestine to the back of the abdominal wall, through a small incision in the skin. Using image-guided technology, a physician inserts a specialized instrument through the incision to access the mesentery and excise the targeted tissue. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be used to diagnose and treat conditions such as mesenteric tumors or inflammatory bowel disease. The percutaneous approach allows for minimized scarring and reduced recovery time compared to open surgical techniques.
