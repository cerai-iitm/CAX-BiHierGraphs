Fusion of Cervical Vertebral Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of a cervical vertebral joint using a synthetic substitute, accessed through an anterior approach. The anterior column is the focus of the procedure, which is performed through an open approach, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area. The synthetic substitute is carefully placed to stabilize and support the cervical spine, promoting healing and fusion of the vertebral joint. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, and limited mobility, and to improve functional ability in the neck and surrounding areas.
