Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually guide the drainage of the peritoneal cavity, a region in the abdominal space, through a small incision in the skin. Using a percutaneous approach, a thin tube is inserted through the skin to drain excess fluid or infected material from the cavity. The endoscope allows the physician to directly visualize the cavity and surrounding tissues, ensuring accurate placement of the drainage tube and minimizing the risk of damage to nearby organs. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or local anesthesia, and patients are usually monitored for a short period post-procedure to ensure there are no complications.
