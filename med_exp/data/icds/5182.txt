Excision of Lumbar Vertebra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lower back to access the lumbar vertebra, allowing the surgeon to remove a portion or entire vertebra to alleviate pressure on surrounding nerves or to treat conditions such as spinal stenosis, herniated discs, or spinal tumors. The operation typically requires general anesthesia and involves dissecting through muscles and ligaments to expose the affected vertebra. Once exposed, the surgeon carefully removes the damaged or diseased vertebra, which may be replaced with a bone graft or artificial implant to maintain spinal stability. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is closely monitored during the recovery period.
