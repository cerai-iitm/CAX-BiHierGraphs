Inspection of Nasal Mucosa and Soft Tissue, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the visual examination of the nasal mucosa and soft tissue through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for a detailed evaluation of the nasal passage and surrounding tissue. The endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, is gently inserted through the nostrils or a previously created opening, providing a clear view of the internal structures. The procedure enables clinicians to identify any abnormalities, such as inflammation, bleeding, or growths, and can help in the diagnosis and treatment of various nasal and sinus conditions. The minimally invasive nature of the procedure ensures minimal discomfort and rapid recovery for the patient.
