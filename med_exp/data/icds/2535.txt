Extraction of Lower Tooth, Multiple, External Approach
This oral surgical procedure involves the removal of multiple teeth from the lower jaw, performed through an external approach, requiring an incision in the gums to access the affected teeth. The surgeon will typically administer a local anesthetic to numb the area before making the incision, and then carefully extract the targeted teeth, taking care to preserve as much surrounding tissue and bone as possible. The extraction site may require sutures or stitches to close the incision and facilitate healing. Following the procedure, patients may experience some discomfort, swelling, and bleeding, which can be managed with pain medication and post-operative care instructions.
