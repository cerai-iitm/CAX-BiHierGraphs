Replacement of Left Occipital Bone with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the left occipital bone is replaced with a synthetic substitute to restore normal skull structure and function. The operation involves an open approach, requiring a surgical incision to access the affected area. The synthetic material used may be a custom-made implant or an off-the-shelf product, carefully selected to match the patient's natural bone anatomy. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness caused by a damaged or defective occipital bone, and to promote optimal healing and recovery.
