Detachment at Left 5th Toe, High, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically detaching the left 5th toe at a high level, typically through an open incision. The surgical approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures, enabling the surgeon to precisely detach the toe. The procedure may be performed to address a range of conditions, including severe toe deformities, trauma, or gangrene. Following detachment, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically required to undergo a period of post-operative care to ensure proper healing.
