Resection of Right Thyroid Gland Lobe, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right lobe of the thyroid gland through an open approach, which means a larger incision is made in the neck to access the thyroid gland. The surgeon will carefully dissect the tissues and ligate the blood vessels to isolate the right lobe, and then remove it from the surrounding tissues. The remaining thyroid tissue is then closed and the incision is sutured or stapled closed. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as thyroid cancer, nodules, or goiter that affect only one side of the thyroid gland.
