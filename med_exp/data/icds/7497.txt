Compression of Left Upper Leg using Pressure Dressing
This procedure involves applying a pressure dressing to the left upper leg to provide compression, which helps to promote blood flow and reduce swelling. The pressure dressing is carefully wrapped around the affected area to provide even pressure, which aids in the healing process. Compression therapy is often used to treat a range of conditions, including deep vein thrombosis, edema, and wounds. By applying sustained pressure, the dressing helps to push excess fluid back into the bloodstream, reducing discomfort and promoting recovery.
