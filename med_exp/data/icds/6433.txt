Repair Right Ureter, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the abdominal wall to access the right ureter, allowing the surgeon to visualize the affected area. The damaged or injured portion of the ureter is then identified and repaired using sutures or other specialized instruments. The goal of the repair is to restore normal urinary flow and function, alleviating symptoms such as pain, infection, or obstruction. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the ureter, enabling the surgeon to accurately repair the damage and ensure a proper recovery.
