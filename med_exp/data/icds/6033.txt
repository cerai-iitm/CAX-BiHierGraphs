Release Left Ankle Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue of the left ankle joint to release tension or restrictions caused by scar tissue, adhesions, or contractures. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling precise release of the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal range of motion, alleviate pain and stiffness, and improve overall ankle function. Post-operatively, the patient may require immobilization, physical therapy, and rehabilitation to ensure optimal healing and prevent recurrence.
