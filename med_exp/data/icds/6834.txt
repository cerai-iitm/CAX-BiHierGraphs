Control Bleeding in Gastrointestinal Tract, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of endoscopic instruments inserted through the skin to control bleeding in the gastrointestinal tract. The healthcare provider uses a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, to locate the source of bleeding. Once located, specialized instruments are used to stop the bleeding through various methods such as applying heat, injecting clotting medications, or using mechanical devices to compress the bleeding site. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for a minimally invasive and targeted treatment of gastrointestinal bleeding, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery.
