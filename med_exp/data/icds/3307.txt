Dilation of Common Bile Duct with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using a thin tube called a catheter to reach the common bile duct through the skin, allowing for the insertion of an intraluminal device to gently dilate the duct and restore proper flow. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the device. The dilation of the common bile duct can help to relieve blockages and allow for the normal flow of bile, alleviating symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and fever. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery time and minimal scarring compared to open surgical procedures.
