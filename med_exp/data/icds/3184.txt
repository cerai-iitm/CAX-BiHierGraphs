Resection of Large Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the large intestine, also known as the colon, to remove a portion of the intestine that is damaged or diseased. The surgeon will then carefully dissect and remove the affected section of the intestine, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and organs. Once the diseased portion is removed, the healthy ends of the intestine are reconnected, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal bowel function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, bleeding, and bowel obstruction.
