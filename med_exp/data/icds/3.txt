Bypass Cerebral Ventricle to Peritoneal Cavity with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a shunt system to drain excess cerebrospinal fluid from the cerebral ventricles to the peritoneal cavity, relieving pressure on the brain. The synthetic substitute tube is guided through a small incision in the skin using an endoscope, allowing for real-time visualization and precision placement. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. By bypassing the ventricles and redirecting fluid flow, this procedure helps to alleviate symptoms associated with hydrocephalus, such as headaches, seizures, and cognitive impairment.
