Resection of Right Ureter, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This percutaneous endoscopic procedure involves the removal of a portion of the right ureter through a minimally invasive approach. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia and utilizes an endoscope inserted through a small incision in the skin to visualize the ureter. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully resect the affected segment of the ureter, which is then removed through the incision site. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal urinary function and relieve symptoms associated with ureteral obstruction or injury.
