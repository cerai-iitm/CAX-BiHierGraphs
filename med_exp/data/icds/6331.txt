Excision of Left Kidney, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the left kidney through a small incision in the skin using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to guide the surgical instruments. The procedure is performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the instruments. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to examine the kidney tissue for any abnormalities or diseases, such as cancer or benign tumors. The percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery and less post-operative pain compared to open surgery.
