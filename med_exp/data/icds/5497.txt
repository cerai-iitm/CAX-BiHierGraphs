Reposition Left Fibula, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the repositioning of the left fibula bone through a small incision in the skin. The percutaneous approach allows for a less extensive opening, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. During the procedure, the surgeon uses specialized instruments and imaging guidance to carefully manipulate the fibula back into its normal anatomical position. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the affected limb, alleviating pain and promoting return to normal activity.
