Performance of Urinary Filtration, Prolonged Intermittent, 6-18 hours Per Day
This procedure involves the use of a device to filter waste products from the blood, mimicking the natural function of the kidneys, for a prolonged intermittent period of 6-18 hours per day. The filtration process helps to remove excess fluids, electrolytes, and waste products from the blood, improving overall health and quality of life. The procedure is typically performed in a hospital or clinical setting under the close supervision of medical professionals. It is often used to support individuals with kidney failure or those who require ongoing dialysis treatment.
