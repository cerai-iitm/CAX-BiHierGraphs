Drainage of Left Ovary, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the left ovary, allowing the surgeon to drain any accumulated fluid, blood, or pus that may be causing discomfort, infection, or other complications. The open approach enables direct visualization of the ovary and surrounding tissues, facilitating the removal of any abnormal contents and ensuring a thorough clearance of the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms, restore normal ovarian function, and prevent further complications. The surgeon will close the incision site once the procedure is complete, promoting healing and minimizing the risk of infection.
