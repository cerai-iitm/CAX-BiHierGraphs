Fluoroscopy of Left Common Carotid Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This procedure uses low osmolar contrast material and fluoroscopy to visualize the left common carotid artery, allowing clinicians to examine the blood vessels and identify any blockages or abnormalities. The contrast agent is injected through a catheter, and X-rays are taken as it flows through the artery, creating real-time images on a monitor. These images help diagnose conditions such as atherosclerosis, stenosis, or aneurysms, and guide therapeutic interventions like angioplasty or stenting. The procedure is typically performed in an interventional radiology or cardiac catheterization laboratory under local anesthesia and conscious sedation.
