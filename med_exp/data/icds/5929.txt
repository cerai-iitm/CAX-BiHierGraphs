Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the lumbar vertebral joint using an interbody fusion device, which is inserted through a posterior approach to stabilize the anterior column of the spine. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and accurately place the device. The goal of the procedure is to restore spinal stability and alleviate symptoms such as chronic back pain, numbness, or tingling in the legs. By achieving a solid fusion, the device helps to redistribute stress and pressure across the spine, promoting long-term healing and reducing the risk of further degeneration.
