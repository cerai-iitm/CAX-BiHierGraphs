Resection of Left Upper Lobe Bronchus, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves an open approach to resect, or remove, the left upper lobe bronchus, which is a vital airway passage that connects the trachea to the lungs. The procedure typically requires a thoracotomy, a type of incision made in the chest wall to access the lungs and bronchial structures. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects and removes the diseased or damaged portion of the left upper lobe bronchus, taking care to preserve as much healthy lung tissue as possible. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal breathing function and alleviate symptoms associated with bronchial diseases or lesions.
