Bypass Coronary Artery, Three Arteries from Aorta with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves bypassing blockages in three coronary arteries by creating a detour using autologous venous tissue, which is harvested from the patient's own body. The open approach requires a thoracotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart, and the aorta is used as the point of attachment for the bypass grafts. The autologous venous tissue is sewn onto the aorta and the coronary arteries, diverting blood flow around the blocked areas and restoring normal blood flow to the heart muscle. The goal of this complex procedure is to relieve symptoms of coronary artery disease, such as chest pain and shortness of breath, and reduce the risk of heart attack.
