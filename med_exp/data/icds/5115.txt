Reposition Right Thumb Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This medical procedure involves repositioning a misplaced or dislocated phalanx bone in the right thumb using an open approach, where the skin is incised to access the affected area. The procedure requires the use of an internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, to stabilize the bone and hold it in place while it heals. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the thumb, alleviating pain and discomfort, and improving range of motion and dexterity. Recovery typically involves a period of immobilization followed by physical therapy to regain strength and mobility in the affected thumb.
