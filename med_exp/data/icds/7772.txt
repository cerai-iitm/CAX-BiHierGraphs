Irrigation of Joints using Irrigating Substance, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small instrument through the skin to inject an irrigating substance directly into the affected joint, helping to remove debris, reduce inflammation, and promote healing. The percutaneous approach allows for precise targeting of the joint area, minimizing damage to surrounding tissue. The irrigating substance used is specifically designed to help flush out bacteria, fragments, and other substances that may be contributing to the patient's symptoms. By effectively cleaning the joint, this procedure can help reduce pain, improve range of motion, and promote a faster recovery.
