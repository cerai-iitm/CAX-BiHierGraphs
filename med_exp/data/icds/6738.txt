Excision of Prostate, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the prostate gland through a natural or artificial opening, using an endoscope to guide the operation. The endoscope is inserted through the urethra or a small incision in the abdomen, providing a clear view of the prostate and surrounding tissues. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to carefully dissect and remove the prostate gland, minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues and structures. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be recommended for patients with benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) or prostate cancer.
