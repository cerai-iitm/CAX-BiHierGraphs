Excision of Thoracic Vertebra, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a thoracic vertebra through an open approach, which means a larger incision is made to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visually examine the vertebra and surrounding tissue. The goal of this diagnostic excision is to identify the underlying cause of symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or weakness, and to gather information about the vertebra's condition. The procedure typically takes place under general anesthesia, and the surgical team takes great care to minimize damage to surrounding muscles, nerves, and blood vessels. Following the excision, the removed vertebra is sent to a laboratory for further examination and testing to aid in diagnosis.
