Excision of Right Foot Muscle, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the affected muscle tissue in the right foot is carefully exposed to allow for precise excision. The surgeon makes a deliberate incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the targeted muscle, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding nerves and blood vessels. Once the muscle is isolated, it is carefully removed, and the site is closed using sutures or staples to promote healing. Following the procedure, the patient may require a period of immobilization and rehabilitation to restore normal function to the affected foot.
