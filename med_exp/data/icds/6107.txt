Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the damaged or diseased right hip joint with a synthetic substitute, utilizing an uncemented method to secure the implant in place. The open approach is used, which involves making a larger incision to access the joint, allowing for a more direct visualization of the affected area. The uncemented synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the hip joint, promoting a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, restore mobility, and improve the overall quality of life for the patient.
