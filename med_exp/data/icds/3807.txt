Replacement of Neck Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Partial Thickness, External Approach
During this surgical procedure, a portion of the neck skin is replaced with a substitute made from the patient's own tissue, obtained from a different area of the body. The external approach involves making an incision in the skin to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to remove the damaged skin and replace it with the autologous tissue substitute. This partial thickness replacement aims to restore the skin's natural function and appearance, promoting optimal wound healing and minimizing scarring. The use of autologous tissue eliminates the risk of rejection, as the substitute is derived from the patient's own body.
