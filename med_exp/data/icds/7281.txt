Detachment at Left Foot, Partial 4th Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of a portion of the 4th ray on the left foot through an open approach. The 4th ray consists of the 4th metatarsal bone, 4th phalanx, and surrounding soft tissue. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually access the affected area, enabling a more precise detachment of the targeted bone and tissue. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper function and alleviate pain or discomfort in the affected foot.
