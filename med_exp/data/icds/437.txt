Extirpation of Matter from Pericardium, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the chest to access the pericardium, a sac that surrounds the heart, in order to remove unwanted material or debris. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as pericarditis, an inflammation of the pericardium, or to remove blood or fluid that has accumulated in the pericardial space. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully opens the pericardium and uses specialized instruments to suction or manually remove the unwanted material. After the material has been removed, the pericardium is closed, and the incision is sutured or stapled shut.
