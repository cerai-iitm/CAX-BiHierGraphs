Excision of Left Large Intestine, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a portion of the left large intestine through a small incision in the skin using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the area. The endoscope is guided through the intestine to the affected area, and specialized instruments are used to excise the diseased or damaged section of the intestine. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as cancer, diverticulitis, or bowel obstruction. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for less tissue damage and trauma, resulting in less post-operative pain and a faster recovery time.
