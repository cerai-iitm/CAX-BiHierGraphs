Excision of Omentum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the omentum, a fold of peritoneum that attaches the stomach to other abdominal organs, through small incisions using an endoscope. Under visualization, the omentum is carefully dissected and excised, allowing for precise control and minimized tissue trauma. The percutaneous endoscopic approach enables a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to traditional open surgery. The excision of the omentum may be performed to treat various conditions, including omental torsion, omental infarction, or omental metastasis.
