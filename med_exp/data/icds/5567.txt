Revision of External Fixation Device in Left Lower Femur, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the revision of an external fixation device in the left lower femur, a surgical intervention aimed at adjusting or replacing the existing device to promote proper healing or correction of a deformity. The percutaneous approach is used, which involves making small incisions in the skin to access the fixation device, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster recovery. The revision process may involve adjusting the alignment of the bone, replacing worn-out or damaged components, or re-tightening loose parts to ensure the device is securely in place. The goal of this procedure is to restore the stability and function of the left lower femur, alleviating pain and discomfort while promoting optimal bone healing.
