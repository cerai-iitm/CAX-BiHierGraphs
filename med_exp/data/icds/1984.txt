Extirpation of Matter from Left Maxillary Sinus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the left maxillary sinus to remove abnormal or infected material, such as pus, polyps, or debris, that is causing symptoms like facial pain, pressure, or congestion. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the sinus cavity, enabling the surgeon to carefully extract the unwanted material and promote healing. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may involve making an incision in the cheek or facial bone to access the sinus. Following the procedure, the sinus is irrigated to remove any remaining debris, and the incision site is closed.
