Occlusion of Colic Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using a percutaneous approach to insert an intraluminal device into the colic vein to intentionally occlude or block it. The device is typically inserted through a small incision in the skin, and guided to the target area using imaging technology such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. Once in place, the device is deployed to occlude the colic vein, disrupting blood flow and alleviating symptoms related to venous insufficiency or varices. The procedure is often performed under local anesthesia and is usually well-tolerated, with minimal risk of complications.
