Reposition Left Maxilla with External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left maxilla, which is the upper jawbone, to correct deformities or injuries. The correction is achieved through a percutaneous approach, where small incisions are made in the skin to access the bone. An external fixation device is then attached to the bone, which provides stability and holds the jawbone in its new position. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the upper jaw, improving overall facial aesthetics and oral health.
