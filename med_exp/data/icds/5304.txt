Insertion of External Fixation Device into Right Tibia, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves the insertion of an external fixation device into the right tibia through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The device is placed through small incisions in the skin, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. The external fixator is attached to the bone using pins or screws, providing stability and supporting the bone's natural healing process. Once in place, the device allows for adjustments to be made as the bone heals, ensuring proper alignment and minimizing the risk of complications.
