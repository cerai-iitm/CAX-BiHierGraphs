Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Superior Vena Cava, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small, flexible device into the superior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the upper body to the heart. The device is introduced through the skin using a percutaneous approach, typically through a small incision in the chest or neck. Once in place, the device helps to maintain blood flow and prevent obstruction or blockage of the superior vena cava. This procedure is often performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the device.
