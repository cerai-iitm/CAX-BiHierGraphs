Insertion of Other Device into Right Foot, Open Approach
During an open procedure, a healthcare provider makes an incision in the skin of the right foot to access the underlying tissue. The purpose of the procedure is to insert a device, which could be a mechanical or electronic device, into the right foot to treat a specific medical condition or injury. The device is carefully placed in the foot to provide support, stability, or stimulate healing, and the incision is then closed. This procedure requires a surgical environment to minimize the risk of infection and ensure a successful outcome.
