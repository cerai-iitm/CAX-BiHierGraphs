Excision of Spinal Meninges, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the spinal meninges, which are the protective membranes surrounding the spinal cord. The surgeon then carefully removes the desired portion of the meninges, taking care to avoid damaging the delicate spinal cord or nerve roots. The approach is typically used to treat conditions such as meningiomas, meningeal cysts, or spinal cord injuries. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and remove the affected tissue.
