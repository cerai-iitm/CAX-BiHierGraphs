Restriction of Left Pulmonary Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an intraluminal device into the left pulmonary artery to restrict blood flow and alleviate associated symptoms. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure precise device placement. The intraluminal device, such as a stent or coil, is designed to narrow the vessel lumen, reducing blood flow and pressure in the affected area. By restricting blood flow, the procedure can help treat conditions such as pulmonary hypertension, anomalous vessels, or abnormal blood vessel connections.
