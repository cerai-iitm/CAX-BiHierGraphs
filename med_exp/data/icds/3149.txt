Reposition Stomach, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visually guide the repositioning of the stomach through a small incision in the skin. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth and guided through the esophagus to the stomach, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the stomach and surrounding tissues. Once in position, specialized instruments are used to gently reposition the stomach to its normal anatomical location. This procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety.
