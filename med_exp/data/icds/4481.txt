Release Right Lower Arm and Wrist Muscle, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the right lower arm and wrist area to release tension in the muscles, tendons, and soft tissues. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the underlying causes of muscle constriction. The release of the muscles and soft tissues helps to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited range of motion in the wrist and arm. By relieving compression and tension, this procedure can improve functional mobility and overall quality of life for patients.
