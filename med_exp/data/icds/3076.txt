Release Peritoneum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the peritoneum, a layer of tissue that lines the abdominal cavity. Through a small incision, the endoscope is guided into the peritoneal cavity to identify and release any adhesions or scar tissue that may be causing obstruction or other complications. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal tissue disruption and promotes faster healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal peritoneal function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, bloating, and digestive issues.
