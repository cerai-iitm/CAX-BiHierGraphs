Dilation of Left Popliteal Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small balloon catheter through a tiny incision in the leg to reach the left popliteal artery. The balloon is then inflated to widen the artery, improving blood flow and relieving blockages. A guidewire guides the catheter through the artery, allowing the surgeon to navigate the vessel and precisely target the area of blockage. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and carries fewer risks and less recovery time compared to open surgery.
