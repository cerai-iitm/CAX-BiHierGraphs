Assistance with Cardiac Output using Impeller Pump, Continuous
A life-sustaining device is used to provide continuous mechanical circulatory support, augmenting cardiac function by increasing blood flow and pressure. This extracorporeal pump utilizes an impeller mechanism to propel blood through the cardiovascular system, alleviating the workload of the heart. The device is designed to operate continuously, providing ongoing assistance to maintain adequate perfusion of vital organs. By taking over a significant portion of the heart's pumping function, this technology enables the heart to rest and recover, improving overall cardiovascular health.
