Reposition Right Tibia with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive surgical procedure is performed to reposition the right tibia, utilizing a percutaneous approach to reduce trauma to surrounding soft tissues. Under fluoroscopic or CT guidance, a small incision is made in the skin, and specialized instruments are inserted through the incision to manipulate and reposition the bone. An internal fixation device, such as a plate or rod, is then implanted to stabilize the tibia and promote healing. The entire procedure is typically performed under regional or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort.
