Insertion of Other Device into Products of Conception, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the placement of a device into the products of conception, which can include the fetus, placenta, or amniotic sac, through the natural birth canal or a surgically created opening. The device is carefully inserted to facilitate further medical intervention, monitoring, or treatment. The objective of this procedure is to address specific complications, abnormalities, or concerns related to the pregnancy. The insertion of the device is typically performed under ultrasound guidance or other imaging techniques to ensure accurate placement and minimize potential risks.
