Change Feeding Device in Upper Intestinal Tract, External Approach
This procedure involves the replacement of a feeding device, such as a gastrostomy tube, in the upper intestinal tract through an external approach. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may involve creating a new stoma or replacing the existing one. The healthcare provider will make an incision in the abdominal wall and insert a new feeding tube into the stomach or small intestine, securing it in place with sutures or staples. After the procedure, the patient will be monitored for any signs of complications, such as infection or bleeding.
