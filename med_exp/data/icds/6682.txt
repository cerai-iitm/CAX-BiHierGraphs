Resection of Left Fallopian Tube, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of the left fallopian tube through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach, which involves making a small incision in the skin and inserting a thin tube with a camera and light on the end to guide the surgeon. The endoscope allows the surgeon to visualize the fallopian tube and surrounding tissues, enabling precise dissection and removal of the tube. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and takes approximately 30-60 minutes to complete. The percutaneous endoscopic approach minimizes tissue trauma and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical techniques.
