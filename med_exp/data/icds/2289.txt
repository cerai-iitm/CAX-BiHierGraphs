Extraction of Right Lower Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the mouth or nose to access the lungs, allowing the physician to visually examine the right lower lobe bronchus and extract tissue or mucus for diagnostic purposes. The endoscope is equipped with a specialized instrument that enables the removal of the targeted tissue or mucus, which is then sent to a laboratory for further analysis. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort and promote relaxation. The extracted tissue or mucus is analyzed to diagnose conditions such as lung cancer, infections, or inflammatory diseases affecting the lower lobe bronchus.
