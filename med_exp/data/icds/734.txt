Excision of Left Radial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
During a percutaneous procedure, a small incision is made in the skin to access the left radial artery. The physician then uses specialized instruments to excise, or remove, the targeted segment of the artery. This minimally invasive approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster healing. The goal of the procedure is to treat vascular diseases or defects affecting the left radial artery, improving blood flow and overall circulatory health.
