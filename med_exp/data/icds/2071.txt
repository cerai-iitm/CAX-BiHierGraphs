Dilation of Right Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the right main bronchus, a major airway in the lung. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or nose, allowing the doctor to directly view the bronchus and identify any areas of narrowing or blockage. Once the area is visualized, a tool is used to gently dilate, or widen, the bronchus to improve airflow and relieve symptoms such as wheezing or shortness of breath. The dilation process is typically performed under local anesthesia or sedation to minimize discomfort during the procedure.
