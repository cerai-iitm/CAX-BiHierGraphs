Introduction of Cytarabine and Daunorubicin Liposome Antineoplastic into Central Vein, Percutaneous Approach, New Technology Group 3
Cytarabine and daunorubicin liposome, a combination of chemotherapy drugs, is introduced into a central vein through a percutaneous approach. This procedure involves inserting a small catheter or needle through the skin and into the vein, allowing the medication to be delivered directly to the bloodstream. The liposome technology allows for targeted delivery of the antineoplastic agents, increasing their effectiveness and reducing side effects. This innovative approach enables healthcare providers to treat certain types of cancer, such as acute myeloid leukemia, with a more precise and potent treatment.
