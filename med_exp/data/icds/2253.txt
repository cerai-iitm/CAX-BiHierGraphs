Excision of Left Pleura, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope inserted through the skin to visualize the left pleura, a thin layer of tissue surrounding the lungs. The aim is to remove a portion of the diseased or damaged pleura to alleviate symptoms such as pain, infected fluid accumulation, or difficulty breathing. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be accompanied by additional interventions to drain fluid or remove scar tissue.
