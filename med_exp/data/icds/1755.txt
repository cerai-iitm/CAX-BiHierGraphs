Excision of Aortic Lymphatic, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the open excision of a lymphatic vessel or structure located in the aorta, the largest artery in the body, for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon makes an incision in the chest or abdomen to access the aorta and the affected lymphatic vessel. The lymphatic vessel is then carefully dissected and removed, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples. The excised tissue is sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in the diagnosis of a underlying condition.
