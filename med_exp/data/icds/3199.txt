Resection of Descending Colon, Open Approach
Surgical removal of the descending colon is performed through an open approach, involving a large incision in the abdomen to access the affected area. The procedure typically involves mobilization of the colon, ligation of blood vessels and lymphatic structures, and resection of the diseased or damaged portion of the descending colon. The remaining healthy ends of the colon are then joined together to restore bowel continuity, allowing for normal digestive function to resume. The surgery is usually performed under general anesthesia and may require a temporary colostomy or ileostomy to divert fecal flow during the healing process.
