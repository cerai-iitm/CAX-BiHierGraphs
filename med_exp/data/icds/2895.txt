Excision of Rectum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the rectum and remove a portion of it through a small incision in the skin. The endoscope is equipped with a camera and specialized instruments that allow the healthcare provider to carefully excise the affected area. The diagnostic excision is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate localization and removal of the targeted tissue. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical techniques.
