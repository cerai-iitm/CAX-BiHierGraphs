Extirpation of Matter from Left Pleural Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left side of the chest to access the pleural cavity, a space between the lungs and chest wall. The goal is to remove unwanted material, such as blood, fluid, or pus, that has accumulated in the pleural cavity and may be causing symptoms such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, or infection. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and manually remove the unwanted material, promoting healing and restoring normal lung function. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications.
