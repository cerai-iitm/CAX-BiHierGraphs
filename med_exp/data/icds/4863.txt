Replacement of Left Frontal Bone with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left frontal bone with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision to access the affected area. The damaged or diseased bone is removed and replaced with a synthetic implant designed to mimic the natural bone's structure and function. The synthetic substitute is carefully shaped and fitted to ensure a secure and stable implantation. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural contours and functionality of the left frontal bone, improving overall cranial structure and potentially alleviating symptoms such as pain, swelling, or cosmetic concerns.
