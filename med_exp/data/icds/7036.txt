Inspection of Pelvic Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through a small incision in the skin to visualize the pelvic cavity. The scope allows the healthcare provider to examine the pelvic organs and tissues in real-time, enabling the detection of any abnormalities or diseases. The percutaneous endoscopic approach eliminates the need for a large incision, reducing recovery time and scarring. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort during the examination.
