Extirpation of Matter from Inferior Vena Cava, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of unwanted material, such as blood clots or debris, from the inferior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the lower body to the heart. Through a small incision in the skin, a catheter is inserted and guided to the inferior vena cava using imaging technology, allowing the surgeon to extract the problematic material. This percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical methods. The removal of the unwanted material helps restore normal blood flow and reduces the risk of serious health consequences.
