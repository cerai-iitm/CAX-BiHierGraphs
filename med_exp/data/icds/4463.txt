Extraction of Right Lower Arm and Wrist Muscle, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an open incision in the right lower arm and wrist to access and remove muscle tissue. The surgical approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and extract the targeted muscle. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as muscle tumors, infections, or traumatic injuries that require surgical intervention. The open approach allows for a more comprehensive treatment of the affected area, promoting optimal healing and functional recovery.
