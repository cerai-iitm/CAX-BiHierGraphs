Resection of Right Pelvic Bone, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of the right pelvic bone through an open approach. The operation typically requires a large incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize the pelvis and surrounding tissues. The resected bone segment is carefully removed, and the surgical site is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to treat various conditions, such as tumors, infections, or bone deformities, affecting the right pelvis.
