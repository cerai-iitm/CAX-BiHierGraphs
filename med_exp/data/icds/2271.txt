Extirpation of Matter from Right Middle Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During a minimally invasive procedure, a flexible endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to access the right middle lung lobe. The endoscope, equipped with specialized instruments, is used to visualize and remove unwanted matter, such as blood, pus, or foreign objects, from the affected lung area. This preciseness enables the healthcare provider to target the specific site of the problem, reducing the risk of complications and promoting optimal lung function. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort and cooperation.
