Performance of Urinary Filtration, Intermittent, Less than 6 Hours Per Day
This procedure involves the intermittent use of a urinary filtration device, which is a medical device designed to remove waste and excess fluids from the body. The device is used for a period of less than 6 hours per day, providing temporary relief from urinary retention or incontinence. The filtration process helps to regulate electrolyte balance, reduce the risk of urinary tract infections, and improve overall kidney function. By maintaining a healthy flow of urine, this treatment can significantly enhance the quality of life for patients with chronic kidney disease or other urological disorders.
