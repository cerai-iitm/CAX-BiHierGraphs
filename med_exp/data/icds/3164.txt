Reposition Rectum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the rectum, which has become displaced from its normal position. The goal of the operation is to manually reposition the rectum to its correct anatomical location, restoring its normal function and alleviating symptoms such as pain, constipation, or difficulty controlling bowel movements. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the rectum and surrounding tissues, enabling precise correction of the malposition. Following repositioning, the incision is closed, and the patient typically requires postoperative care to facilitate healing and recovery.
