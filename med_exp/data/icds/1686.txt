Supplement Inferior Vena Cava with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the inferior vena cava, the largest vein in the lower body, with a non-autologous tissue substitute through an open approach. The objective is to repair or reinforce the affected area of the vena cava, which may be damaged due to injury, disease, or conditions such as vena cava syndrome. The non-autologous tissue substitute, sourced from outside the patient's body, is carefully implanted into the vena cava to restore its function and promote proper blood flow. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the vena cava, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the supplementation procedure.
