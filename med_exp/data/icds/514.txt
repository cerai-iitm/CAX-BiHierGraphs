Removal of Cardiac Lead from Heart, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the chest cavity to manually extract a dysfunctional or infected cardiac lead from the heart, a delicate operation that requires careful navigation of the cardiovascular system to avoid damaging surrounding tissue or disrupting heart function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the lead and surrounding anatomy, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the lead while minimizing bleeding and trauma to the heart. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve cardiopulmonary bypass to ensureBlood flow and oxygenation during the operation. Once the lead is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications or signs of cardiac distress.
