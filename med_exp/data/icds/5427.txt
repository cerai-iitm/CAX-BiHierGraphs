Reposition Right Femoral Shaft with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right femoral shaft, which is the upper part of the thigh bone, to its normal anatomical position using an open approach. The procedure is performed to correct deformities, misalignments, or fractures of the femur. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the femoral shaft, and then uses internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods to stabilize the bone in its correct position. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the affected limb, relieve pain and discomfort, and promote healing.
