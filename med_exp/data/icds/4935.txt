Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Skull, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves revising or replacing a synthetic substitute, such as a prosthetic or implant, that has been placed in the skull to repair or replace damaged or missing bone. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize the substitute and surrounding tissue. The goal of the revision is to restore function, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall quality of life. The procedure is often performed to address complications or issues with the original implant, such as infection, implant failure, or bone resorption.
