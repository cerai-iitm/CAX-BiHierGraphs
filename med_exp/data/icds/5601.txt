Drainage of Left Wrist Joint, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small drainage tube through the skin and into the left wrist joint to remove excess fluid or blood that has accumulated due to inflammation, injury, or infection. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery and less scarring compared to open surgery. The drainage tube is guided into place using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure precise placement and minimize risk of complications. The goal of the procedure is to reduce pain, swelling, and stiffness in the wrist joint, restoring range of motion and function.
