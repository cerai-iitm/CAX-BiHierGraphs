Reposition Thoracic Vertebra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of a thoracic vertebra to correct an anatomical abnormality or misalignment. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected vertebra, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the bone to its normal alignment. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or tingling caused by the misaligned vertebra. The surgeon may use various instruments and implants to stabilize the vertebra in its new position, promoting proper spinal alignment and function.
