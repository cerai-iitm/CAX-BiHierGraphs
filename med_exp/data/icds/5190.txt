Excision of Right Pelvic Bone, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the right pelvic bone through a minimally invasive approach, where the surgeon makes a small incision in the skin and uses specialized instruments to access the bone. The percutaneous approach allows for reduced bleeding, less tissue damage, and shorter recovery times compared to traditional open surgery. The excision of the pelvic bone may be necessary to treat conditions such as bone tumors, fractures, or infections that have not responded to other treatments. During the procedure, the surgeon will use imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scans, to accurately locate and remove the affected bone tissue.
