Drainage of Jejunum with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device into the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, through a percutaneous endoscopic approach. A flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, known as an endoscope, is guided through the mouth and into the small intestine to visualize the jejunum and assist in the placement of the drainage device. The device is designed to drain fluids or other substances that may be accumulating in the jejunum, such as in cases of intestinal blockage or obstruction. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for a faster recovery time and reduced risk of complications compared to traditional open surgical methods.
