Inspection of Uterus and Cervix, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the abdominal wall to visually examine the uterus and cervix. The endoscope is equipped with a camera and light, allowing for a clear and magnified view of the internal structures. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for an open surgical incision, reducing post-operative pain and promoting faster recovery. The inspection allows for the diagnosis and evaluation of various uterine and cervical conditions, such as polyps, fibroids, or abnormal bleeding.
