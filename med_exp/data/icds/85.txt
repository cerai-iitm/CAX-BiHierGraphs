Excision of Cerebellum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access the cerebellum, a structure located at the base of the brain that coordinates muscle movements and maintains posture and balance. The surgeon then carefully removes a portion of the cerebellum, which is then sent to a laboratory for diagnostic testing to identify any abnormalities or diseases. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the cerebellum and surrounding brain tissue, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and excise the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to obtain a tissue sample for diagnostic purposes, which can help guide further treatment or management of the underlying condition.
