Repair Tricuspid Valve, Open Approach
This open-heart surgery procedure involves making a large incision in the chest to access the tricuspid valve, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect and manually repair or replace the valve to restore its proper function. The tricuspid valve is located between the right atrium and ventricle and plays a crucial role in preventing blood from flowing backwards into the atrium. During the procedure, the surgeon will use specialized instruments and techniques to repair or replace the valve, which may involve closing holes or tears, repairing or replacing leaflets, or reshaping the valve annulus. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as fatigue, swelling, and shortness of breath.
