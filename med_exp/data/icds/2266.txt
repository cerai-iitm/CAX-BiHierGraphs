Extirpation of Matter from Left Upper Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to remove foreign matter or debris from the left upper lobe bronchus, a passageway that connects the trachea to the lungs. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth or nose, and advanced to the affected area. Once the endoscope is in place, specialized instruments are used to extract the unwanted material, helping to restore normal breathing function and alleviate symptoms such as coughing or shortness of breath. The goal of this minimally invasive procedure is to safely and effectively clear the airway and promote healthy lung function.
