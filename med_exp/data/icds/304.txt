Bypass Coronary Artery, Two Arteries from Left Internal Mammary, Open Approach
This coronary artery bypass grafting procedure involves creating a detour around a blocked or narrowed section of the coronary artery, restoring blood flow to the heart. The surgeon uses the left internal mammary artery as the graft source, and connects it to two coronary arteries, bypassing the blocked or narrowed areas. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart, and the procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia. The goal of this complex surgical intervention is to relieve symptoms of coronary artery disease, such as chest pain and shortness of breath, and to reduce the risk of heart attack or other cardiac complications.
