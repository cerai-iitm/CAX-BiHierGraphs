Dilation of Ampulla of Vater, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves inserting a flexible tube with a camera and light through the mouth or a previously created opening to visualize the ampulla of Vater, a muscular structure located at the junction of the bile and pancreatic ducts. The scope is guided to the ampulla, where a balloon or dilator is advanced through the scope to widen the opening, allowing for improved drainage of bile and pancreatic juices. This dilation helps to relieve blockages, reduce pressure, and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and may be used to treat conditions such as choledocholithiasis, benign strictures, or Sphincter of Oddi dysfunction.
