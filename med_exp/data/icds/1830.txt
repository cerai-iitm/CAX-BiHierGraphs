Resection of Spleen, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the spleen through small incisions in the abdomen, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the area. A small camera and specialized instruments are inserted through the incisions, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the spleen. The percutaneous approach reduces recovery time and scarring compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be recommended for individuals with conditions such as splenomegaly, splenic rupture, or hematological disorders.
