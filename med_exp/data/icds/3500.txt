Removal of Intraluminal Device from Hepatobiliary Duct, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the hepatobiliary duct to manually remove an intraluminal device, typically a stent or catheter, that has been previously inserted to facilitate drainage or treatment. The open approach allows for direct visualization and access to the duct, enabling the surgeon to carefully extract the device. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may require a small incision in the abdomen to access the duct. Following removal of the device, the incision is closed and the patient is monitored for any complications.
