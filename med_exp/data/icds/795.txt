Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Axillary Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the skin to access the left axillary artery, a major blood vessel located in the armpit region. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a thin catheter is advanced through the incision site and navigated into the artery, allowing for the careful placement of an infusion device. The device is designed to deliver medications or fluids directly into the arterial system, often for prolonged periods or high-dose therapy. Once the device is securely in place, the incision site is closed, and the infusion device is connected to a pump or other delivery system.
