Monitoring of Arterial Pulse, Peripheral, Percutaneous Approach
This non-invasive procedure involves placing sensors on the skin to measure the arterial pulse in the peripheral arteries, typically in the arms or legs. The percutaneous approach allows for real-time monitoring of blood flow and oxygenation, providing valuable insights into cardiovascular health. The sensors are usually attached to a portable device that displays the pulse waveform and calculates related parameters, such as heart rate and blood pressure. By continuously monitoring the arterial pulse, healthcare professionals can quickly identify any changes or abnormalities, enabling timely interventions to prevent complications.
