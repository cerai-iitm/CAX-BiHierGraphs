Excision of Bladder, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the bladder and remove a portion of it through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope, equipped with specialized instruments, is inserted through the opening, allowing the surgeon to identify and excise the affected area of the bladder. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and treat bladder-related conditions, such as cancer, while minimizing tissue trauma and promoting faster recovery.
