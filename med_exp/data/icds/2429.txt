Supplement Right Middle Lobe Bronchus with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, a synthetic substitute is used to supplement the right middle lobe bronchus, which is a vital part of the lung's airway system. This complex operation involves making a large incision in the chest to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to carefully place the synthetic material in the correct position. The goal of the procedure is to improve breathing and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, wheezing, or coughing caused by damage or disease affecting the right middle lobe bronchus. The synthetic substitute helps to restore normal lung function, promoting better respiratory health and overall well-being.
