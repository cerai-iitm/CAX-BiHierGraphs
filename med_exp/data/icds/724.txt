Excision of Left Internal Mammary Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to access the left internal mammary artery, a vital blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the heart. The goal of the operation is to excise, or remove, a portion of the artery, which may be damaged or diseased, causing coronary artery disease or other cardiovascular conditions. During the open approach, the surgeon carefully dissects the artery from surrounding tissues and ligates any branches or tributaries before excising the affected segment. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to ensure proper recovery and monitoring.
