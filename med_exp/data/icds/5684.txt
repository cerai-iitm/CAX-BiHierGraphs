Fusion of 2 or more Cervical Vertebral Joints, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more cervical vertebral joints using an open approach from the posterior aspect of the spine, focusing on the anterior column. The goal of this complex operation is to stabilize the spine, alleviate pain, and restore function to the affected joints. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the back of the neck to access the cervical spine, and then uses bone grafts or other materials to fuse the targeted joints. The resulting solid bond between the vertebrae helps to reduce motion, alleviate pressure on nearby nerves, and promote long-term spinal health.
