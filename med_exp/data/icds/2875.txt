Excision of Transverse Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the transverse colon, a segment of the large intestine that connects the ascending colon to the descending colon. The surgeon carefully dissects and frees the transverse colon from surrounding tissues and blood vessels, taking care to minimize damage to nearby organs. The affected portion of the transverse colon is then removed, and the remaining colon is surgically closed. The incision is subsequently closed, usually with sutures or staples, to complete the procedure.
