Replacement of Bilateral Breast using Deep Inferior Epigastric Artery Perforator Flap, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the reconstruction of both breasts using a deep inferior epigastric artery perforator (DIEP) flap, which is a type of autologous tissue reconstruction. During the open approach, the surgeon extracts tissue from the abdominal area and transplants it to the breast region, carefully reattaching blood vessels to ensure proper circulation. The DIEP flap is considered a popular choice for breast reconstruction due to its ability to provide a natural, soft-tissue appearance and sensation. By using this microsurgical technique, the surgeon can create a more symmetrical and natural-looking breast shape, enhancing the overall aesthetic outcome.
