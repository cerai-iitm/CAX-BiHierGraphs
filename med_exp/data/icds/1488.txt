Removal of Infusion Device from Upper Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of an infusion device from a vein in the upper extremity. The infusion device, which is typically used for administration of medications or fluids, is carefully withdrawn from the vein through a small skin incision. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is often performed under local anesthesia and guided by ultrasound or fluoroscopy to ensure accurate device removal and minimal trauma to surrounding tissues.
