Reposition Left Ulna, External Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning the left ulna bone through an external approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the affected bone. The goal is to restore the bone to its normal anatomical position, often to treat conditions such as fractures, dislocations, or malalignments that can cause pain, limited mobility, and other complications. During the procedure, the surgeon may use internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods to stabilize the bone and promote healing. Post-operatively, the patient may require immobilization and rehabilitation to ensure proper recovery and optimal outcomes.
