Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Hip Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate, rod, or screw, that was previously implanted in the left hip joint to treat a fracture or other orthopedic condition. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize the fixation device and carefully extract it from the bone. The removal process typically involves disassembling the device and gently prying it away from the surrounding bone and soft tissue. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal mobility and alleviate any discomfort or pain caused by the presence of the internal fixation device.
