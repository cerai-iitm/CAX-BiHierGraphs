Transfer Left Abdomen Muscle, Transverse Rectus Abdominis Myocutaneous Flap, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves transferring the muscle and skin of the left abdomen to reconstruct a defect or deformity, using a transverse rectus abdominis myocutaneous (TRAM) flap, which is a type of tissue flap that preserves the muscle and skin of the abdomen. The open approach is used to access the abdomen, allowing the surgeon to carefully harvest the TRAM flap and transfer it to the desired location. The muscle and skin are then reshaped and secured in place to restore the natural contour and integrity of the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to improve the aesthetic and functional outcomes for patients with complex abdominal defects or deformities.
