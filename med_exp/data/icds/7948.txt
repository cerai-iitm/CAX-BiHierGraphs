Fluoroscopy of Right Internal Mammary Bypass Graft using Other Contrast
This fluoroscopic procedure involves the use of other contrast agents to visualize the internal mammary bypass graft on the right side, allowing healthcare professionals to assess graft patency, detect any potential complications, and guide interventions. During the procedure, a fluoroscope is used to produce continuous X-ray images, enabling real-time visualization of the graft and its surrounding anatomy. The use of contrast agents helps to highlight the graft and enhance image quality, facilitating accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. The procedure is typically performed in a hospital or outpatient setting by a trained interventional radiologist or cardiothoracic surgeon.
