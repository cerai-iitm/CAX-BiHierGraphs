Removal of Liner from Left Hip Joint, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the left hip joint to remove a liner, a component of a hip replacement implant, that has worn out or failed. The open approach requires a larger incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and carefully extract the liner. The goal of this procedure is to replace the worn-out liner with a new one, restoring the smooth movement and function of the hip joint. The surgery typically requires general anesthesia and may involve a hospital stay, followed by a period of rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility.
