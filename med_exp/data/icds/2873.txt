Excision of Ascending Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of the ascending colon through an endoscopic approach, where a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or anus to visualize the colon. The ascending colon is the first part of the colon and is located in the lower right abdomen. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as colon cancer, adenoma, or inflammatory bowel disease. The endoscopic approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure with less risk of complications and a faster recovery time compared to open surgery.
