Release Bilateral Ovaries, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the ovaries, followed by the gentle release of adhesions or scar tissue that may be binding or constricting the ovaries bilaterally. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the ovaries and surrounding tissues, enabling a more precise and effective release of any attachments. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal ovarian function and mobility, which can help alleviate symptoms such as pelvic pain, discomfort, or infertility. By releasing the ovaries, this procedure can also improve overall reproductive health and fertility outcomes.
