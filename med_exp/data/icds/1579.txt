Excision of Left Lesser Saphenous Vein, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the left lesser saphenous vein through a small incision in the skin. Using an endoscope, a tiny camera and light, the surgeon visualizes the vein and removes it through the percutaneous approach. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and is often used to treat varicose veins or venous insufficiency. The excision of the vein helps to alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and skin discoloration, improving overall circulation and quality of life.
