Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Lumbosacral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the lumbosacral joint, a complex anatomical region where the lower spine meets the pelvis. The goal is to stabilize and realign the joint by inserting an internal fixation device, which can be a plate, screw, or rod, to provide structural support and promote healing. The procedure requires great precision and care due to the proximity of delicate spinal nerves and blood vessels. By internalizing the fixation device, patients can experience improved spinal stability, reduced pain, and enhanced mobility.
