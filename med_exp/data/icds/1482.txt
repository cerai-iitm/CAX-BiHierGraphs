Occlusion of Upper Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small intraluminal device through the skin to occlude an upper vein. Under imaging guidance, a thin catheter is navigated through the vein to the targeted site, where the device is deployed to block blood flow. The device is designed to expand and anchor itself within the vein, providing a permanent solution to address venous insufficiency or other vascular conditions. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal tissue damage and rapid recovery, reducing the risk of complications compared to open surgical procedures.
