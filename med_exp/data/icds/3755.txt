Insertion of Tissue Expander into Bilateral Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open insertion of a tissue expander into both breasts to create space for future breast reconstruction. The operation is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a hospital stay. The tissue expander is a temporary device that is implanted beneath the breast tissue and slowly filled with saline over time to stretch the skin and muscle, preparing it for a permanent breast implant. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural appearance and shape of the breasts, often following a mastectomy or breast tissue removal.
