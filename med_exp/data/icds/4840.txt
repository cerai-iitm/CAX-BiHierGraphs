Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Skull, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device from the skull through an open approach, requiring a surgical incision to access the device. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and cooperation. The device, which is usually made of metal, is carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding bone and soft tissue, taking care to avoid damage to nearby structures. The wound is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
