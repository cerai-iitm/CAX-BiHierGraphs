Extirpation of Matter from Left Popliteal Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of thrombi, emboli, or other unwanted material from the left popliteal artery through a small incision in the skin. Under imaging guidance, a specialized catheter is inserted through the incision and advanced to the affected area, where it captures and extracts the unwanted material, restoring blood flow to the leg. The percutaneous approach allows for smaller incisions, reduced bleeding, and decreased risk of complications compared to open surgery. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, and weakness in the leg, and to prevent potential long-term damage to the affected limb.
