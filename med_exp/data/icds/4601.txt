Transfer Right Lower Leg Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of a muscle from one location to another in the right lower leg, using an open approach that requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to restore muscle function, alleviate pain, or improve mobility in the lower leg. The surgeon carefully identifies the donor muscle and recipient site, then transplants the muscle to the new location, securing it with sutures or other fixation devices. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically required to undergo a period of rehabilitation to restore strength and function to the affected leg.
