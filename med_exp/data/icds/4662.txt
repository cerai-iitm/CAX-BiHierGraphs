Reattachment of Left Upper Leg Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves reattaching a tendon in the left upper leg through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to expose the affected area. The tendon is carefully reattached to the surrounding tissues and bone using sutures or other fixation devices to restore its natural function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately repair any damage. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient undergoes a period of rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility in the affected leg.
