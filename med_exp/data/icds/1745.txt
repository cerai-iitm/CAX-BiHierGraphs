Excision of Mesenteric Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the mesentery, a fold of tissue that attaches the small intestine to the back of the abdominal wall. The procedure is typically performed to remove abnormal or diseased lymphatic tissue, such as cancerous lymph nodes, from the mesentery. During the open approach, the surgeon manually explores the mesentery to identify and remove the affected lymphatic tissue, which may involve dissecting and ligating blood vessels and other structures. The goal of the procedure is to remove the diseased tissue and prevent further complications or spread of disease.
