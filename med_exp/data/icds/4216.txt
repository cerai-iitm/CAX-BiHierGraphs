Removal of Tissue Expander from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to remove a tissue expander from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the trunk. The open approach involves making a incision in the skin to access the expander, which is then carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding tissue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and may take around 30-60 minutes to complete. After removal, the incision is closed with sutures or staples, and the patient is taken to the recovery room for post-operative care.
