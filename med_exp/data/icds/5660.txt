Fusion of Cervical Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of cervical vertebral joints using an autologous tissue substitute, which is naturally derived from the patient's own body. The operation is performed through a posterior approach, accessing the spine from the back, and focuses on the posterior column of the vertebrae. The open approach requires a surgical incision to visualize the affected area, allowing the surgeon to precisely place the autologous tissue substitute to promote bone growth and fusion. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the cervical spine, relieve pain, and restore mobility by merging two or more vertebrae into a single, solid unit.
