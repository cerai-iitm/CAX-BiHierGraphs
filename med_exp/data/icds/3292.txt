Dilation of Right Hepatic Duct, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous dilation of the right hepatic duct is a minimally invasive procedure that involves making a small incision in the skin to access the duct. Under imaging guidance, a wire is advanced through the skin and into the duct, followed by a catheter and a balloon dilator. The balloon is then inflated to widen the duct, improving drainage and relieving blockages. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as bile duct stones, strictures, or tumors that obstruct the normal flow of bile.
