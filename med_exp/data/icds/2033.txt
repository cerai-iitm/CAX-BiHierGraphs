Change Tracheostomy Device in Trachea, External Approach
This procedure involves replacing a tracheostomy tube with a new one in the trachea through an external approach, typically performed at the bedside or in a clinic setting. The old tube is carefully removed, and the tracheal stoma is inspected for any bleeding, granulation tissue, or other complications. A new tracheostomy tube is then inserted through the existing stoma, and the cuff is inflated to ensure a proper seal and prevent air leaks. The patient is then monitored for any signs of respiratory distress or other complications following the tube change.
