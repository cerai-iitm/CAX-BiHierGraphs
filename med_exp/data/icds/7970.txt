Ultrasonography of Heart with Aorta, Transesophageal
This non-invasive procedure uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the heart and its surrounding blood vessels, including the aorta. A flexible tube with an ultrasound transducer at its end is inserted through the mouth and guided into the esophagus, allowing for closer proximity to the heart. The transducer emits sound waves that bounce off the heart and are converted into images, providing valuable information about cardiac structure and function. This test is particularly useful for evaluating heart valve function, detecting blood clots, and assessing cardiac chamber size.
