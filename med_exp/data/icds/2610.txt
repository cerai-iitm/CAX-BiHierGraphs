Bypass Stomach to Jejunum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves creating a connection between the stomach and the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, using an endoscope inserted through the mouth and guided through the stomach to the jejunum. The goal is to reroute food from the stomach to the jejunum, bypassing the duodenum, the first part of the small intestine. This percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for the creation of a gastroduodenal anastomosis, a direct connection between the stomach and the jejunum, to facilitate digestion and absorption. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as gastric outlet obstruction or unresectable gastric cancer.
