Extraction of Left Humeral Shaft, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left arm to access the humeral shaft, which is the long bone of the upper arm. The goal of the procedure is to extract or remove a portion or entirety of the humeral shaft, which may be necessary due to fracture, infection, or tumor. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the surrounding soft tissue and bone to access the humeral shaft, taking care to preserve surrounding nerves and vessels. The extracted bone is then typically replaced with a prosthetic or bone graft to restore function and stability to the arm.
