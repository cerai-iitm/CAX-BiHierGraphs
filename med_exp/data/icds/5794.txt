Replacement of Left Shoulder Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left shoulder joint with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, providing improved mobility and reduced pain for patients experiencing severe arthritis, fractures, or joint degeneration. The procedure typically begins with an incision in the shoulder area, allowing the surgeon to access the joint and remove the damaged or diseased tissue. The synthetic substitute, designed to mimic the natural joint's function, is then implanted and secured in place, ensuring a stable and smooth range of motion. Post-operative rehabilitation is crucial to achieve optimal outcomes and regain full shoulder functionality.
