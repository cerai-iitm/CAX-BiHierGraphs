Dilation of Left Femoral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left femoral artery, allowing for the insertion of an intraluminal device to dilate the artery. The open approach enables direct visualization of the artery, facilitating precise placement of the device to widen the narrowed or blocked vessel. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with peripheral artery disease, such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the leg. By dilating the artery, the risk of complications, including heart attack and stroke, is reduced.
