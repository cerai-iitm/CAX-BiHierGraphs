Bypass Coronary Artery, One Artery from Left Internal Mammary with Autologous Arterial Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass in a single coronary artery using the left internal mammary artery as the donor vessel. The procedure is performed using an open approach, wherein the chest is surgically opened to access the heart. The autologous arterial tissue from the left internal mammary artery is harvested and grafted to the blocked coronary artery, allowing blood to flow around the blockage and restore normal blood flow to the heart muscle. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms of coronary artery disease, such as chest pain and shortness of breath, and reduce the risk of heart attack.
