Fluoroscopy of Right External Carotid Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
Here is the description:

Fluoroscopy is a real-time x-ray imaging procedure used to guide the injection of a low osmolar contrast material into the right external carotid artery. During the procedure, a small amount of contrast material is injected through a catheter inserted into the artery, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the blood vessels and their pathways on a live x-ray screen. This imaging guidance helps to facilitate diagnostic or therapeutic procedures, such as angioplasty or stenting, to restore blood flow to the brain or face. The low osmolar contrast material is used to minimize the risk of adverse reactions and improve image quality.
