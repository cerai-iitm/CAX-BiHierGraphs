Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Pancreatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During endoscopy, a thin, flexible tube is inserted through the mouth or via an artificial opening to access the pancreatic duct. A specialized device is then guided through the tube and placed inside the duct to facilitate drainage or obstruction relief. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure precise placement of the device. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal pancreatic function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
