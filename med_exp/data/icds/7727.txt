Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Spinal Canal, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a therapeutic substance into the spinal canal to treat a range of conditions, including chronic pain, inflammation, and infection. The substance is carefully guided through the skin and into the epidural space, where it can target the affected area and provide relief from symptoms. The percutaneous approach minimizes tissue disruption and scarring, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the therapeutic substance.
