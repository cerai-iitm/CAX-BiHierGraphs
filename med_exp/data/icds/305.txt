Bypass Coronary Artery, Three Arteries from Coronary Artery with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This cardiac surgical procedure involves creating a detour or bypass around blocked or narrowed sections of three coronary arteries, restoring blood flow to the heart. The surgeon uses a section of autologous venous tissue, harvested from the patient's own body, to create a graft that connects the aorta to the coronary artery, bypassing the damaged area. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart and coronary arteries. The bypass graft redirects oxygenated blood flow, reducing the risk of heart attack and relieving symptoms such as angina.
