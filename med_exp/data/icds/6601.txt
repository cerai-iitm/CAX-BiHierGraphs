Inspection of Uterus and Cervix, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the uterus and cervix through either a natural or artificial opening. During the inspection, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the vagina or an incision, allowing the healthcare provider to directly view the inside of the uterus and cervix. This enables the identification of any abnormalities, such as polyps, fibroids, or inflammation, that may be causing symptoms or affecting fertility. The procedure can also help guide further treatments, such as biopsies or surgical interventions, or provide reassurance of a healthy reproductive system.
