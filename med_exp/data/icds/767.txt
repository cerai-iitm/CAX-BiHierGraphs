Extirpation of Matter from Intracranial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter, such as blood clots or plaque, from an artery located within the skull through a small incision in the skin. Under imaging guidance, a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision and navigated to the target artery to extract the obstructive material. The percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow and prevent further neurovascular damage.
