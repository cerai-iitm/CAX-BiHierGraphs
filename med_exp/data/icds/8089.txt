Fluoroscopy of Superior Mesenteric Artery using Other Contrast
This radiological procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the superior mesenteric artery, which supplies blood to the small intestine. A type of contrast agent, other than the standard ones, is inserted into the bloodstream to enhance the visualization of the artery and its surrounding structures. The fluoroscope uses continuous X-rays to produce real-time images of the artery and its blood flow, allowing the physician to diagnose and monitor conditions such as stenosis or blockages. The procedure is guided by a radiologist or interventional radiologist to ensure accurate interpretation of the images and effective treatment planning.
