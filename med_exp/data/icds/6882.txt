Drainage of Left Pleural Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain fluid or air from the left pleural cavity, the space between the lungs and chest wall. Under endoscopic guidance, a drainage device is inserted through the skin to access the pleural cavity, allowing for the removal of excess fluid or air that may be causing respiratory distress. This percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing compared to open surgical methods. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort.
