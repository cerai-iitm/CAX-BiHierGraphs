Fluoroscopy of Superior Vena Cava using Low Osmolar Contrast, Guidance
This fluoroscopically guided procedure involves injecting a low osmolar contrast medium into the superior vena cava to visualize its structure and blood flow. Real-time imaging is used to provide precise guidance for the procedure, allowing for accurate placement of the contrast agent. The low osmolar contrast medium reduces the risk of allergic reactions and kidney damage compared to high osmolar contrast agents. The resulting images aid in diagnosing and treating various conditions affecting the superior vena cava, such as stenosis or thrombosis.
