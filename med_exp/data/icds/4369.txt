Drainage of Right Trunk Muscle, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Percutaneous drainage of the right trunk muscle is a minimally invasive procedure that involves the insertion of a small catheter or needle through the skin to remove excess fluid or debris from the affected muscle. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort, and the patient is monitored for any signs of complications during and after the procedure. The goal of the procedure is to obtain a sample of the fluid or tissue for further examination and to provide relief from pain and swelling associated with muscle inflammation or injury.
