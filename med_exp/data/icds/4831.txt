Extraction of Left Mandible, Open Approach
Surgical extraction of the left mandible, also known as the lower jawbone, involves an open approach to access the affected area. This procedure typically requires a surgical incision in the skin and soft tissue to expose the mandible, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove the bone. The open approach enables direct visualization of the surgical site, facilitating precise dissection and extraction of the mandible. The goal of this procedure is to remove the affected bone and alleviate associated symptoms or conditions, such as infection, tumor, or trauma.
