Dilation of Right Femoral Artery with Two Drug-eluting Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the description:

A minimally invasive procedure is performed to widen the narrowed or blocked right femoral artery using a percutaneous approach. Through a small incision, two drug-eluting intraluminal devices are inserted and expanded to facilitate blood flow. These devices, coated with medication, prevent the growth of new blockages and promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore adequate blood flow to the affected leg, relieving symptoms such as pain, numbness, and weakness.
