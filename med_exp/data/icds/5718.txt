Fusion of Thoracolumbar Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of joints in the thoracolumbar region of the spine, which connects the thoracic and lumbar vertebrae, using a patient's own tissue substitute. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, which means the surgeon accesses the spine from the back, and involves an open incision to allow for direct visualization of the spinal structures. The anterior column, which is the front part of the spine, is the focus of the fusion procedure. The goal of this surgery is to stabilize and strengthen the thoracolumbar spine, relieving pain and promoting healing in patients with conditions such as degenerative disc disease or spinal fractures.
