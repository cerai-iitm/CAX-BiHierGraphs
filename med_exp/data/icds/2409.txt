Resection of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a thoracotomy, a surgical incision in the chest wall, to access the right lower lung lobe. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia and requires a hospital stay. The surgeon carefully dissects and removes the affected lobe, taking care to avoid damage to surrounding tissues and structures. The remaining lung tissue is then repositioned, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to facilitate healing.
