Extirpation of Matter from Abdomen Skin, External Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves the removal of unwanted material or substances from the skin of the abdomen through an external approach. The surgeon makes an incision in the abdominal skin to access the affected area, and then carefully extracts the unwanted matter. The goal of this procedure is to relieve symptoms, prevent infection, and promote healing in the affected skin. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and remove the material in a controlled manner.
