Supplement Lumbar Vertebra with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a surgeon makes an open incision to access the lumbar spine, where a natural tissue substitute is used to supplement and support the vertebra. The autologous tissue, typically harvested from the patient's own body, is carefully implanted to promote spinal stability and relieve pressure on surrounding tissues. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, and tingling in the legs, and to improve overall spinal function. The surgeon meticulously places the tissue substitute to ensure proper alignment and support, allowing the patient to recover and regain optimal mobility.
