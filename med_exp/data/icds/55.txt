Drainage of Lumbar Spinal Cord with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to remove excess cerebrospinal fluid or blood that has accumulated in the lumbar spinal cord, relieving pressure and decreasing the risk of further damage. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing trauma to surrounding tissues and promoting faster recovery. The drainage device is carefully guided into place using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT, to ensure accurate placement and minimize complications. Once in place, the device allows for the controlled drainage of the accumulated fluid, helping to restore normal spinal cord function and alleviate associated symptoms.
