Dilation of Bladder Neck with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the bladder neck, allowing the healthcare provider to carefully widen the narrow passage. An intraluminal device is then inserted through the natural or artificial opening to dilate the bladder neck, improving urinary flow and reducing symptoms of obstruction. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to ensure patient comfort. By dilating the bladder neck, the procedure helps to relieve urinary retention, frequency, and incontinence, and can significantly improve quality of life for patients with bladder neck obstruction.
