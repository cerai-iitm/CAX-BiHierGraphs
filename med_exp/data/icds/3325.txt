Drainage of Liver, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small tube through the skin and into the liver to drain excess fluid or abscesses that have accumulated in the liver. Under imaging guidance, a needle is inserted through the skin and into the liver to access the affected area. The fluid is then removed through the tube, and the area is cleaned and drained to promote healing and prevent further infection. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort and promote relaxation.
