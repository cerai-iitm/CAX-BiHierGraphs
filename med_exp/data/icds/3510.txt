Removal of Intraluminal Device from Pancreatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of an intraluminal device from the pancreatic duct through a natural or artificial opening. The device, which is inserted into the pancreatic duct to facilitate drainage or relieve blockages, is carefully extracted using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope is guided through the natural opening of the mouth or an artificial opening created in the abdominal wall to access the pancreatic duct. The procedure requires precision and skill to avoid damaging the delicate tissues and structures surrounding the duct.
