Assistance with Respiratory Ventilation, Less than 24 Consecutive Hours, Continuous Positive Airway Pressure
This procedure involves providing respiratory support to patients who require continuous breathing assistance for less than 24 hours, utilizing Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) to maintain a constant air pressure in the airways during inhalation and exhalation. The goal of this treatment is to improve oxygenation and lung function in individuals with respiratory distress or failure, such as those with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), pneumonia, or heart failure. The CPAP device delivers pressurized air through a mask or nasal interface, helping to keep the airways open and allowing for more efficient gas exchange. By providing this respiratory support, healthcare professionals can help patients breathe more easily and reduce the risk of complications.
