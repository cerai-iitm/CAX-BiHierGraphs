Extraction of Left Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left foot to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue beneath the skin. The damaged or diseased tissue and fascia are then carefully extracted from the foot through the open incision, allowing for removal of any abnormal or infected tissue. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as abscesses, infections, or tumors in the foot. Following extraction, the incision is closed, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
