Bypass Right Femoral Artery to Popliteal Artery with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
A surgical bypass procedure is performed to redirect blood flow around a blocked or damaged section of the right femoral artery, which supplies oxygenated blood to the legs. The popliteal artery, located behind the knee, is used as the distal anastomosis site to restore blood flow to the lower extremity. An autologous venous tissue graft, harvested from the patient's own body, is used to create the bypass conduit. The open approach involves a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to meticulously suture the graft in place and reestablish blood flow.
