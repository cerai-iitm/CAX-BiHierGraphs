Monitoring of Central Nervous Electrical Activity, External Approach
This procedure involves the use of external devices to track and record the electrical activity of the central nervous system, typically through electroencephalography (EEG) or other non-invasive methods. The goal is to assess brain function, diagnose neurological disorders, and monitor the effectiveness of treatments. Sensors or electrodes are placed on the scalp or skin to detect the electrical impulses generated by the brain, which are then amplified and recorded for analysis. This continuous monitoring enables healthcare professionals to identify patterns, abnormalities, or changes in brain activity, facilitating timely interventions and personalized care.
