Bypass Common Bile Duct to Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a connection between the common bile duct and the small intestine to facilitate bile drainage, thereby relieving obstructed bile flow. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the common bile duct and small intestine. The bile duct is then sewn to the small intestine, allowing bile to bypass the obstructed area and flow directly into the intestine. This procedure can help to alleviate symptoms such as jaundice, itching, and abdominal pain caused by blocked bile ducts.
