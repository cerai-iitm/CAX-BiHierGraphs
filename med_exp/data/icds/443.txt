Extirpation of Matter from Left Pulmonary Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter or other specialized instrument through the skin to access the left pulmonary artery, allowing the removal of unwanted material or substances, such as blood clots or other emboli, that may be obstructing blood flow or causing harm. The percutaneous approach enables the procedure to be performed through a small incision, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. Under imaging guidance, the interventional radiologist or cardiologist navigates the instrument to the target site, where the unwanted material is carefully extracted to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms. This procedure is often performed to treat life-threatening conditions, such as pulmonary embolism, and can significantly improve patient outcomes.
