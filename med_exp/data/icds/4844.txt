Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Facial Bone, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the facial bone to access and remove a synthetic substitute, such as a prosthetic or implant, that has been previously inserted. The open approach requires a surgeon to make a larger incision to visualize the area and carefully extract the substitute. The goal of the procedure is to eliminate any complications or infections associated with the synthetic material and restore the natural anatomy of the facial bone. The surgery typically requires general anesthesia and may involve a multidisciplinary team of surgeons and specialists.
