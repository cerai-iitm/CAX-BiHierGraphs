Introduction of Anti-inflammatory into Spinal Canal, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to inject anti-inflammatory medication directly into the spinal canal to reduce swelling and relieve pain. A needle is inserted through the skin and guided to the targeted area in the spine using fluoroscopic or CT scan guidance. The medication is then injected into the epidural space, which is the area outside the sac that surrounds the spinal cord, to deliver the therapeutic agent directly to the affected area. This approach is useful in treating conditions such as herniated discs, spinal stenosis, and degenerative disc disease.
