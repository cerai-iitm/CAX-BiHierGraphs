Resection of Large Intestine, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the large intestine from the inside. Through a small incision, specialized instruments are inserted to remove a portion of the large intestine, allowing for targeted treatment of conditions such as cancers, blockages, or perforations. The percutaneous endoscopic approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. This precise and less traumatic approach enables a faster return to normal activities and daily routines.
