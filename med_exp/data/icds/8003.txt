Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Common Carotid Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a type of X-ray imaging, to visualize the bilateral common carotid arteries, which are the main arteries supplying blood to the head and neck. A low osmolar contrast medium is injected into the arteries to enhance visibility during the procedure. The contrast medium is designed to have fewer adverse effects and is used to highlight the arteries and their blood flow. The resulting images provide valuable information for diagnosing and treating conditions affecting the carotid arteries, such as stenosis or occlusion, which can lead to stroke or other cardiovascular events.
