Dilation of Left Ureter with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
The percutaneous endoscopic approach is utilized to access the left ureter, allowing the insertion of an intraluminal device to dilate the ureter and restore proper urinary flow. This minimally invasive procedure is performed under fluoroscopic guidance, enabling the healthcare provider to visualize the ureter and accurately place the device. The intraluminal device is designed to gently stretch and widen the ureter, alleviating any obstruction or narrowing that may be causing urinary tract symptoms. By dilating the ureter, this procedure aims to relieve symptoms such as pain, frequency, or difficulty urinating, and promote improved kidney function.
