Insertion of External Fixation Device into Left Tibia, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an external fixation device into the left tibia, a long bone in the lower leg, through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The device is inserted through small incisions in the skin, typically under imaging guidance, to stabilize and hold the bone in place while it heals. The external fixation device consists of a series of pins or wires that are inserted into the bone and connected to an external frame, which provides stability and support to the affected leg. This procedure is often used to treat complex fractures, deformities, or infections of the left tibia that require stabilization and realignment.
