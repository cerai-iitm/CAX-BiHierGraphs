Repair Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision to access the right knee joint, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and repair damaged or diseased tissues, such as cartilage, ligaments, or bone. The open approach enables the surgeon to thoroughly clean and debride the joint, removing any debris or infected tissue, and perform complex repairs or reconstructions as needed. The procedure may be indicated for a range of conditions, including severe arthritis, fractures, or ligamentous injuries, and is typically performed under general anesthesia. Throughout the procedure, the surgeon will take care to preserve as much healthy tissue as possible, restoring normal joint function and alleviating symptoms such as pain and stiffness.
