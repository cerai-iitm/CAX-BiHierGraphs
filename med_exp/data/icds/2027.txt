Bypass Trachea to Cutaneous with Tracheostomy Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass between the trachea and the skin to facilitate breathing, using a tracheostomy device. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the trachea, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the site. The tracheostomy tube is then inserted through the skin and connected to the trachea, bypassing the upper airway. This Life-sustaining procedure is often performed in emergency situations or for patients with severe respiratory distress.
