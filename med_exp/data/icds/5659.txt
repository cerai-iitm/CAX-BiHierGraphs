Fusion of Cervical Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more vertebrae in the cervical spine using a graft made from the patient's own tissue, which is obtained from a donor site, typically the hip or ribcage. The anterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the neck from the front, allowing for a more direct route to the affected vertebral joint. The open approach involves making a larger incision to expose the joint, allowing for removal of damaged or diseased tissue and implantation of the autologous tissue graft. The goal of the procedure is to stabilize the spine, relieve pressure on surrounding nerves and blood vessels, and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, and weakness.
