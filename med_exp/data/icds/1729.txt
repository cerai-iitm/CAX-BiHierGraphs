Excision of Right Axillary Lymphatic, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right axillary region to access the lymphatic tissue. The goal of the operation is to remove lymph nodes or a portion of the lymphatic system to examine for signs of disease or infection. A diagnostic excision is performed to collect tissue samples for further examination, such as biopsies, to aid in diagnosis and treatment planning. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the lymphatic tissue, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the targeted tissue.
