Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Peritoneal Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open removal of synthetic material, such as mesh or prosthetic material, that has been implanted in the peritoneal cavity. The peritoneal cavity is the region of the abdomen that contains the intestines and other organs, and the synthetic material may have been used to repair a hernia or other defect. The open approach requires a larger incision in the abdomen, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the area and carefully remove the synthetic material. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor for any potential complications.
