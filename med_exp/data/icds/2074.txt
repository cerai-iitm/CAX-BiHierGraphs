Dilation of Right Middle Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is a detailed description tailored to the ICD-10 procedure code 0B758ZZ:

During this endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or nose to reach the right middle lobe bronchus. Once in position, the bronchoscope is used to visualize the bronchus and identify any areas of narrowing or obstruction. A specialized instrument is then guided through the bronchoscope to gently dilate the narrowed segment of the right middle lobe bronchus, allowing for improved airflow and relief of respiratory symptoms. The entire procedure is performed under visualization, allowing the physician to accurately assess and treat the affected area.
