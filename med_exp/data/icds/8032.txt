Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Vertebral Arteries using Other Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the bilateral vertebral arteries, which are vital blood vessels that supply the brain. During the procedure, a specialized contrast agent is administered to enhance the visibility of the arteries, allowing for a clearer image of the blood vessels. The fluoroscope emits continuous X-rays to produce real-time images of the arteries, which are then projected onto a monitor for the healthcare provider to interpret. The resulting images provide valuable information to help diagnose and treat conditions affecting the vertebral arteries, such as blockages or narrowing.
