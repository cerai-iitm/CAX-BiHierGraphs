Resection of Cervix, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the cervix, the lower part of the uterus that opens into the vagina, through an open approach, where a large incision is made in the abdomen to access the cervix. The procedure is usually performed to treat conditions such as cervical cancer, dysplasia, or severe cervical abnormalities. During the procedure, the surgeon will make an incision in the lower abdomen, dissect the cervix from surrounding tissues, and remove the entire cervix or a portion of it, depending on the extent of the condition. The goal of the procedure is to excise the affected tissue and prevent further complications or cancer spread.
