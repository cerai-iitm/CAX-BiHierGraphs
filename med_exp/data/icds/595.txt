Supplement Mitral Valve with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the mitral valve is accessed through an open approach, involving a thoracotomy or median sternotomy incision, to expose the heart. The defective or diseased mitral valve is then reinforced with a zooplastic tissue, a bioprosthetic material derived from animal tissue, to enhance its function and restore normal blood flow. The zooplastic tissue is carefully sewn or attached to the natural valve to ensure a secure and stable supplement, allowing for improved valve closure and reduced regurgitation. This procedure is often performed to treat mitral regurgitation, mitral stenosis, or other valvular diseases, aiming to alleviate symptoms and improve the patient's overall heart function.
