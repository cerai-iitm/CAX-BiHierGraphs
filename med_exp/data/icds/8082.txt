Fluoroscopy of Hepatic Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This imaging procedure utilizes fluoroscopy to visualize the hepatic artery, which supplies blood to the liver, and involves the administration of a low osmolar contrast medium to enhance the visibility of the targeted blood vessel. The contrast agent is injected through a catheter inserted into an artery, allowing the healthcare provider to guide the catheter to the hepatic artery under continuous X-ray monitoring. The real-time fluoroscopic images enable the accurate assessment of blood flow and possible blockages or narrowing in the hepatic artery. This diagnostic procedure is essential in evaluating liver function and detecting conditions such as hepatic artery stenosis or thrombosis.
