Excision of Stomach, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the stomach and identify the area targeted for removal. Under imaging guidance, a small incision is made in the skin, and specialized instruments are inserted through the incision to excise the specified portion of the stomach. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the excised tissue is then removed through the small incision. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery.
