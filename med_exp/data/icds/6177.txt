Reposition Right Knee Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right knee joint to its normal anatomic alignment, followed by the implantation of an internal fixation device to stabilize the joint. The operation is performed through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. The internal fixation device, such as plates, screws, or rods, is secured to the bone to hold the knee joint in place, promoting healing and preventing further injury. The goal of this procedure is to restore joint stability, relieve pain, and improve mobility and function of the right knee.
