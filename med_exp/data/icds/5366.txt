Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Left Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, that was previously implanted in the left tibia (shinbone) to treat a fracture or other orthopedic condition. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the device, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and carefully remove the implant. The procedure requires careful dissection and manipulation to avoid damaging surrounding soft tissues and bones. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
