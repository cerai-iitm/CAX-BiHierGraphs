Excision of Pelvis Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the pelvic lymphatic system through a small incision. Under image guidance, the healthcare provider then uses specialized instruments to remove the targeted lymphatic tissue or nodes through the same incision. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced tissue trauma, less post-operative pain, and a faster recovery time compared to traditional open surgical methods. The excised tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
