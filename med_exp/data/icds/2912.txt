Excision of Greater Omentum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the greater omentum, a layer of fatty tissue that covers the abdominal organs, and remove it through small incisions. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and minimizes tissue damage. The diagnostic excision is performed to examine the tissue for any abnormalities or diseases, such as cancer, and to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of various conditions. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be performed in conjunction with other diagnostic or therapeutic procedures.
