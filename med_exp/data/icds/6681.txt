Resection of Left Fallopian Tube, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower abdomen to access the left fallopian tube, which is then carefully dissected and removed. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as ectopic pregnancy, fallopian tube cancer, or severe tubal damage. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly and perform the resection with precision. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
