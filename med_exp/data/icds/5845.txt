Drainage of Right Hip Joint, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain the right hip joint, utilizing a percutaneous approach to access the joint through a small incision in the skin. Under imaging guidance, a thin tube or drainage catheter is inserted through the incision to evacuate any accumulated fluid, pus, or debris from the joint space. This procedure is often performed to relieve pain, reduce inflammation, and prevent further joint damage. The percutaneous approach allows for a less traumatic and less invasive alternative to open surgical drainage, promoting a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications.
