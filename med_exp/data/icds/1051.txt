Dilation of Right External Iliac Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through a small incision in the groin area to reach the right external iliac artery, which is then dilated using a balloon to widen the narrowed or blocked vessel. A drug-eluting intraluminal device, such as a stent, is then placed within the artery to maintain its patency and prevent future blockages. The stent slowly releases medication to help prevent the buildup of plaque and reduce the risk of restenosis. This percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery and less scarring compared to traditional open surgery.
