Reattachment of Right Upper Leg Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repairing a detached tendon in the right upper leg through an open approach. The operation requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize the tendon and surrounding tissues. The detached tendon is then reattached to the bone using sutures or other fixation devices, restoring its normal function and anatomy. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, weakness, and limited mobility in the affected leg.
