Excision of Right Ovary, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves a open abdominal incision to access the pelvic cavity, where the right ovary is carefully located and removed. The surgical team takes great care to minimize tissue damage and bleeding during the excision, which is typically performed under general anesthesia. The ovary is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis. The patient typically requires several days of recovery in the hospital before being discharged to continue their post-operative care at home.
