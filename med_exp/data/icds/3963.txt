Drainage of Buttock Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous drainage of the subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the buttock area using a drainage device. The doctor makes a small incision in the skin to access the affected area, and then inserts the drainage device to remove any accumulated fluid, abscess, or infected material. The device is left in place to continue draining the area until the infection has cleared or the fluid has been fully removed. The goal of this procedure is to promote healing, reduce the risk of further infection, and alleviate symptoms such as pain and swelling.
