Resection of Sigmoid Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening With Percutaneous Endoscopic Assistance
During this minimally invasive procedure, a surgeon uses a combination of laparoscopic and endoscopic techniques to remove a portion of the sigmoid colon through a natural or artificial opening. A flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, is inserted through the anus or a small incision to guide the surgeon's instruments. The affected section of the sigmoid colon is then resected, and the remaining healthy portions are reattached using sutures or staples. This approach allows for a faster recovery, less scarring, and reduced risk of complications compared to traditional open surgery.
