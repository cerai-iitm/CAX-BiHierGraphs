Excision of Stomach, Pylorus, Open Approach
This invasive surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the stomach and pylorus, a region where the stomach meets the small intestine. The goal of the operation is to remove a portion or the entire stomach, along with the pylorus, to treat conditions such as stomach cancer, bleeding, or obstruction. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully isolates the affected area, cuts out the diseased tissue, and closes the incision site. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the organs and surrounding tissues, ensuring a precise and thorough removal of the affected area.
