Replacement of Right Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves replacing damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the right lower leg with a autologous tissue substitute, using an open approach. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected area, and then removes the damaged tissue and fascia. The autologous tissue substitute, which is derived from the patient's own tissue, is then implanted to repair and reconstruct the affected area. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the tissue substitute and restore normal function to the right lower leg.
