Inspection of Mediastinum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope to visually examine the mediastinum, a region in the chest cavity that contains vital organs and structures. Through a small incision in the skin, the endoscope is inserted and guided to the mediastinum, allowing the physician to directly visualize the area and identify any abnormalities. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, and may be used to diagnose conditions such as mediastinitis, lymphoma, or sarcoidosis. The percutaneous endoscopic approach offers a less invasive alternative to traditional open surgical methods, resulting in less trauma to the surrounding tissues and reduced post-procedure recovery time.
