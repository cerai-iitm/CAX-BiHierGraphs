Supplement Left Common Carotid Artery with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the left common carotid artery and supplementing it with a synthetic substitute to restore normal blood flow. The synthetic material, carefully selected to match the natural artery, is sutured into place to create a reinforced conduit for blood to flow from the heart to the brain. The open approach allows for direct visualization and precise control of the surgical site, ensuring accurate placement of the substitute and minimizing potential complications. Once completed, the procedure promotes improved blood circulation and helps alleviate symptoms associated with carotid artery disease.
