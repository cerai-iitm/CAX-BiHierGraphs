Drainage of Left Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
The procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue beneath the skin. The goal of the procedure is to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or other substances that may be causing infection, pain, or swelling in the affected area. The surgeon will carefully dissect through the tissue to locate the source of the problem and remove any unwanted material, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues. Once the drainage is complete, the incision is closed, and the area is dressed to promote healing.
