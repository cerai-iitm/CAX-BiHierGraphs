Inspection of Mouth and Throat, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This medical procedure involves visually examining the mouth and throat using an endoscope, a flexible or rigid tube with a camera and light on the end, inserted through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope allows the healthcare provider to directly visualize the inside of the mouth and throat, enabling them to identify any abnormalities, such as signs of infection, inflammation, or cancer. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes around 15-30 minutes to complete. During the procedure, the healthcare provider may also collect tissue samples or remove any abnormal growths or lesions for further examination.
