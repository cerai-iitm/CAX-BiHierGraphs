Detachment at Right Lower Leg, Low, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves separating a portion of tissue or a body part at the lower end of the right lower leg, near the ankle, through an open incision. The detachment is typically performed to treat conditions such as severe injuries, infections, or tumors that require removal of damaged or diseased tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely detach the targeted tissue or body part. The goal of the procedure is to restore function, alleviate symptoms, and promote healing in the affected limb.
