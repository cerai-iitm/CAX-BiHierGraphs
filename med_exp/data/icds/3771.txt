Repair Face Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves restoring skin integrity on the face through an external approach, often to address defects or damage caused by trauma, injury, or disease. The surgical process typically begins with a thorough cleaning and disinfection of the affected area, followed by the careful excision of damaged skin tissue. The resulting defect is then closed using sutures or other closure techniques, taking care to minimize scarring and promote optimal healing. The goal of this procedure is torestore a normal appearance and function to the facial skin, improving overall aesthetic and psychological well-being.
