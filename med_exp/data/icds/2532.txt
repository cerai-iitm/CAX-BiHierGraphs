Extraction of Upper Tooth, Multiple, External Approach
This procedure refers to a dental surgery where multiple upper teeth are extracted using an external approach. This method is employed when the teeth are severely damaged or impacted, requiring direct access for removal. The procedure involves making an incision in the gum tissue to expose the roots of the teeth, followed by their careful extraction. After the extraction, the wound is closed, and the patient is advised on postoperative care to ensure a smooth recovery.
