Resection of Bilateral Ovaries, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both ovaries through an open incision in the abdomen, typically performed under general anesthesia. The surgeon makes a horizontal or vertical incision in the lower abdomen to access the pelvic cavity, then carefully dissectates and frees the ovaries from surrounding tissues and blood vessels. The ovaries are then removed and any necessary repairs to surrounding tissues are made before the incision is closed. The goal of this procedure is often to treat cancer, endometriosis, or other conditions affecting the ovaries, and may also be performed as a preventive measure for high-risk individuals.
