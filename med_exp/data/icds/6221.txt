Supplement Right Knee Joint, Tibial Surface with Liner, Open Approach
Here is the description:

During this surgical procedure, an open approach is utilized to access the right knee joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the tibial surface. A liner is then implanted onto the tibial surface to provide a smooth, articulating surface for the knee joint. This supplement enhances the natural movement and function of the knee, alleviating pain and discomfort caused by damaged or worn-out joint surfaces. By doing so, the procedure aims to restore normal knee function, improve mobility, and enhance the overall quality of life for the patient.
