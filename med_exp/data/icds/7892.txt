Collection of Cerebrospinal Fluid from Indwelling Device in Nervous System
This procedure involves the collection of cerebrospinal fluid from an indwelling device, such as a shunt or catheter, that is implanted in the nervous system, typically in the brain or spine. The fluid is extracted through the device, allowing for the diagnosis and monitoring of conditions such as meningitis, encephalitis, or hydrocephalus. The collection of cerebrospinal fluid is usually performed by a trained healthcare professional, typically in a hospital or clinical setting, using sterile techniques to minimize the risk of infection. The fluid sample is then sent to a laboratory for analysis, providing valuable information for healthcare providers to develop an appropriate treatment plan.
