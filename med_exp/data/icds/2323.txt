Inspection of Trachea, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves a non-invasive examination of the trachea through the skin, allowing for visualization of the tracheal tube and surrounding tissues. A flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through a small incision in the skin, providing a clear view of the trachea. This approach enables healthcare professionals to assess the trachea for any abnormalities, bleeding, or other issues that may be affecting breathing. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes a faster recovery.
