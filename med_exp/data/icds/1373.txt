Restriction of Right External Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an intraluminal device to restrict blood flow in the right external iliac artery. The device is navigated through a small incision in the skin and guided to the target site using fluoroscopic imaging. Once in place, the device is expanded to reduce the artery's diameter, thereby reducing blood flow to the targeted area. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and is often used to treat conditions such as aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or bleeding.
