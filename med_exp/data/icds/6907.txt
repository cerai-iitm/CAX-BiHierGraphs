Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves surgically opening the peritoneal cavity to drain accumulated fluid or infected material, allowing for a visual examination of the abdominal organs and identification of any underlying conditions. During the open approach, the surgeon makes a incision in the abdominal wall to access the peritoneal cavity, taking care to avoid damaging nearby organs and structures. The surgical team will then utilize specialized instruments to remove the peritoneal fluid or infected material, and may collect samples for further laboratory analysis. Following drainage, the incision is closed, and the patient is often monitored for any post-operative complications.
