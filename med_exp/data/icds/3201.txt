Resection of Sigmoid Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the sigmoid colon, which is the lower part of the large intestine. The affected portion of the sigmoid colon is then removed, and the remaining healthy sections are reattached to restore normal bowel function. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly and perform the resection manually. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor for any post-operative complications.
