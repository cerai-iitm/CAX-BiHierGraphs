Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through the skin and into the peritoneal cavity to remove excess fluid or infected material that has accumulated in the space. The percutaneous approach utilizes a minimally invasive technique, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The drainage tube is carefully guided into position using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement. Once in place, the tube allows for the drainage of unwanted fluid, helping to alleviate symptoms and treat underlying conditions such as peritonitis or ascites.
