Replacement of Right Lower Arm Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Partial Thickness, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing damaged or wounded skin on the right lower arm with an autologous tissue substitute, which is a bio-compatible material derived from the patient's own body. The partial thickness replacement is done using an external approach, where the skin is accessed through an incision made on the outer surface of the arm. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural function and appearance of the skin, promote wound healing, and improve mobility and flexibility in the affected arm. By using autologous tissue, the risk of rejection and allergic reactions is minimized, and the patient's natural skin tissue is preserved.
