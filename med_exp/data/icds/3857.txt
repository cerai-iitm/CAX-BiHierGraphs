Replacement of Bilateral Breast using Latissimus Dorsi Myocutaneous Flap, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both breasts and replacement with tissue from the back, using a latissimus dorsi myocutaneous flap. The flap, which includes skin, fat, and muscle, is dissected from the back and transposed to the chest to recreate the breasts. The open approach involves a surgical incision to access the tissues and perform the reconstruction. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural appearance and contour of the breasts, often following a mastectomy or trauma.
