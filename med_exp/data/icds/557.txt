Replacement of Aortic Valve with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
During a complex open-heart surgery, a diseased or damaged aortic valve is removed and replaced with a zooplastic tissue valve, a biological valve harvested from animal sources. The surgeon makes a large incision in the chest to access the heart and aorta, allowing for direct visualization and precise control during the procedure. The zooplastic valve is then implanted, carefully secured, and tested to ensure proper function and blood flow. This replacement surgery aims to restore normal blood flow, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall cardiovascular health.
