Release Lesser Omentum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the lesser omentum, a double-layered fold of peritoneum that attaches the stomach to the liver. The goal of the procedure is to release or free the lesser omentum from any adhesions or attachments that may be causing complications. The surgeon carefully dissects the omentum to restore its normal anatomy and function, which can help to alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomy and enables the surgeon to precisely release the omentum and address any related issues.
