Drainage of Peritoneum with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain excess fluid that has accumulated in the peritoneum, a membrane lining the abdominal cavity. A small incision is made in the abdominal wall, and a drainage device is inserted through the skin and guided into the peritoneum using imaging guidance. The device allows for the removal of fluid, which can help alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain and swelling. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes a faster recovery compared to open surgical procedures.
