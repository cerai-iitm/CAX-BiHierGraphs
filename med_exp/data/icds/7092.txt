Repair Oral Cavity and Throat, External Approach
This invasive procedure involves an external approach to repair defects or injuries in the oral cavity and throat, requiring a surgical incision to access the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to restore functionality and aesthetics to the oral cavity and throat, which may have been compromised due to trauma, disease, or congenital anomalies. The surgical approach may involve reconstruction of soft tissue, bone, or skin to achieve optimal results. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the patient may require hospitalization and post-operative care to ensure a smooth recovery.
