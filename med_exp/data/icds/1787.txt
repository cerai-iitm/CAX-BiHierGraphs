Extraction of Thorax Lymphatic, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of lymphatic tissue or fluid from the thorax, or chest cavity, through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope. The endoscope is inserted through the opening, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the lymphatic tissue or fluid and extract a sample for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort, and it can be used to diagnose conditions such as lymphoma, tuberculosis, or sarcoidosis. The extracted sample is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
