Inspection of Liver, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a needle or catheter through the skin to access the liver, allowing for direct visualization and examination of the liver tissue and any lesions or abnormalities that may be present. The percutaneous approach enables a thorough inspection of the liver without the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and minimizing scarring. Under image guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan, the healthcare provider can precisely target the area of interest, ensuring a safe and effective procedure. The inspecting scope or instrument is carefully manipulated to obtain a clear view of the liver's internal structures, enabling the healthcare provider to diagnose and potentially treat liver-related conditions.
