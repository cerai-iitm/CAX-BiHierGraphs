Excision of Oral Cavity and Throat, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical incision to access the oral cavity and throat, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove affected tissue or lesions. The open approach enables direct visualization of the area, facilitating precise excision of the targeted tissue. The surgery may be performed to treat various conditions, such as tumors, cysts, or other abnormalities in the oral cavity and throat. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal oral function and alleviate symptoms related to the condition being treated.
