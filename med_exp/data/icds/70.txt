Excision of Dura Mater, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the dura mater, a protective membrane covering the brain and spinal cord. The dura mater is then carefully excised, or removed, to allow for further intervention or to alleviate pressure on the surrounding tissues. The open approach requires a craniotomy, which involves temporarily removing a section of the skull to access the dura mater. Following the excision, the incision is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the patient is closely monitored for any post-operative complications.
