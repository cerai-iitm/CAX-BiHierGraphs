Bypass Lower Esophagus to Jejunum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the creation of a bypass between the lower esophagus and the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, using a percutaneous endoscopic approach. Under endoscopic guidance, a narrow tube is inserted through the mouth and navigated to the lower esophagus, where a small incision is made to access the jejunum. A artificial connection is then created between the two, allowing food to bypass the obstructed or damaged portion of the esophagus and flow directly into the small intestine. This minimally invasive procedure can provide palliative relief for patients with advanced esophageal cancer or other conditions that obstruct the esophagus.
