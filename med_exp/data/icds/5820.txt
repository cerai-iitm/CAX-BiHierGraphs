Reposition Left Metacarpophalangeal Joint, External Approach
This procedure involves realigning the left metacarpophalangeal joint, which connects the metacarpal bone in the hand to the phalanges bone in the finger, into its proper position. The metacarpophalangeal joint is accessed through an external approach, meaning the surgeon makes an incision on the surface of the skin to gain direct access to the joint. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal alignment and function to the joint, which may have become displaced due to trauma, injury, or other conditions. By repositioning the joint, the procedure aims to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility in the hand and finger.
