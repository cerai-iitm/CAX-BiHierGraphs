Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Femoral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into the right femoral vein, which is a large vein located in the groin area. The procedure is done percutaneously, meaning through the skin, using a needle and guidewire to access the vein. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the infusion device is then advanced through the vein and positioned securely. Once in place, the device allows for the administration of medications, fluids, or nutrients directly into the bloodstream.
