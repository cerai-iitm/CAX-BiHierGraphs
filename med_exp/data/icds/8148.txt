Fluoroscopy of Inferior Vena Cava using Other Contrast, Guidance
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the inferior vena cava, a major blood vessel that carries deoxygenated blood from the lower body to the heart. During the procedure, a contrast agent other than X-ray dye is administered to enhance the visibility of the vessel. Fluoroscopic guidance allows the healthcare provider to navigate the imaging equipment and capture precise images of the inferior vena cava. The resulting images aid in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the vessel, such as compression, obstruction, or clotting.
