Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Toe Phalanx, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device from the right toe phalanx through an open approach, where an incision is made to expose the fixator. The device, typically consisting of plates, screws, or rods, is carefully extracted to alleviate discomfort, restore normal toe function, and prevent any potential complications. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the internal structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately locate and remove the device. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the toe is immobilized to facilitate healing and speedy recovery.
