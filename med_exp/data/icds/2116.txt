Drainage of Right Middle Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
During a diagnostic procedure, a surgical drain is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to remove fluid or air from the middle lobe of the right lung. This is typically performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as pneumonia, pleural effusion, or lung abscess. The goal of the procedure is to drain the affected area to relieve symptoms and prevent further complications. The procedure is usually performed under sterile conditions and may involve imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scans, to ensure accurate placement of the drain.
