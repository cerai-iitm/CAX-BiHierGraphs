Drainage of Pericardial Cavity with Drainage Device, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this open surgery, a physician creates a small incision in the chest to access the pericardial cavity, a fluid-filled sac surrounding the heart. A drainage device is then inserted into the cavity to remove excess fluid or blood that may have accumulated, relieving pressure on the heart. The device is carefully placed to ensure effective drainage and minimize the risk of complications. Once the procedure is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored to ensure the heart is functioning properly.
