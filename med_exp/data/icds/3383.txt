Excision of Liver, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the liver, allowing the surgeon to visualize and excise the affected portion of the liver tissue. The open approach provides direct visualization of the liver and its surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the diseased or damaged tissue. The procedure may be performed to treat various liver conditions, including tumors, cysts, or injuries. The surgical team will work to control bleeding, repair any damage to surrounding tissues, and close the incision to promote healing.
