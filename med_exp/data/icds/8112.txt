Fluoroscopy of Right Lower Extremity Arteries using Low Osmolar Contrast
This non-invasive diagnostic imaging test uses X-rays and a low osmolar contrast agent to produce real-time images of the arteries in the right lower extremity. During the procedure, a contrast agent is injected into an artery in the leg, allowing the doctor to visualize the blood vessels and identify any blockages or narrowing. The fluoroscopy machine captures continuous X-ray images, which are displayed on a monitor, enabling the doctor to guide the procedure and take precise images of the affected area. The resulting images help diagnose conditions such as peripheral artery disease, and guide further treatment.
