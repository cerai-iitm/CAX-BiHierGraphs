Division of Vagus Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the vagus nerve, a complex nerve that plays a crucial role in various bodily functions, including heart rate regulation, digestion, and respiration. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully identifies and isolates the vagus nerve, and then surgically divides it to address specific health conditions or injuries. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms, such as alleviating seizures, treating epilepsy, or managing gastroparesis.
