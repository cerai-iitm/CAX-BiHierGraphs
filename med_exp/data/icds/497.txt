Map Conduction Mechanism, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a catheter to map the conduction mechanism of the heart, allowing doctors to identify abnormal electrical pathways that may be causing irregular heart rhythms. Through a small incision in the skin, the catheter is guided to the heart, where it records the heart's electrical activity and creates a detailed map of the conduction system. This information is then used to guide further treatment, such as ablation therapy, to correct abnormal heart rhythms. By pinpointing the exact location of electrical misfires, doctors can develop a personalized treatment plan to restore a normal heart rhythm.
