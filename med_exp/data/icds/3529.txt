Resection of Left Lobe Liver, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left lobe of the liver through an open approach, which requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the liver. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and involves careful dissection of the liver tissue and ligaments to separate the left lobe from the rest of the liver. The left hepatic artery, left hepatic vein, and left bile duct are carefully isolated and ligated to prevent bleeding and bile leakage. The entire left lobe is then removed, and the remaining liver tissue is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing.
