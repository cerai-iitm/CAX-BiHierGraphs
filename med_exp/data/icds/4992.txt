Excision of Left Finger Phalanx, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a phalanx bone from the left finger through an open approach, which means the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected area. The phalanx is a type of bone found in the fingers and toes, and excision of the left finger phalanx is typically performed to treat conditions such as infection, trauma, or deformity. During the procedure, the surgeon dissects the soft tissues to expose the phalanx bone, and then carefully removes the bone to alleviate the underlying condition. The surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, to facilitate the healing process.
