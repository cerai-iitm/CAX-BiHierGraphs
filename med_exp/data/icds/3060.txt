Release Ascending Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the use of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, known as an endoscope, which is inserted through the mouth and guided through the digestive system to the ascending colon. The endoscope is equipped with instruments that allow the healthcare provider to visualize and gently release any adhesions or blockages in the ascending colon. The percutaneous approach indicates that the procedure is minimally invasive, with no large incisions required, and is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bowel function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, bloating, and constipation.
