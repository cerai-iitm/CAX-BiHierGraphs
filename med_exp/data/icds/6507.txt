Drainage of Uterus with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device into the uterus through a natural or artificial opening to remove excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may have accumulated due to various conditions such as infection, inflammation, or post-operative complications. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms, restore uterine health, and prevent potential complications. The drainage device is carefully placed to ensure effective drainage and may be left in place for a short period of time to allow for proper healing. After the procedure, patients are closely monitored to ensure any potential complications are addressed promptly and effectively.
