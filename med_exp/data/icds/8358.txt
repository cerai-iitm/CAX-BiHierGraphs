Introduction of Inactivated Coagulation Factor Xa into Peripheral Vein, Percutaneous Approach, New Technology Group 2
This medical procedure involves the insertion of inactivated coagulation factor Xa into a peripheral vein through the skin, utilizing a percutaneous approach. The factor Xa is a protein that plays a crucial role in the blood clotting process, and its inactivated form is used to prevent excessive clotting. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. By introducing the inactivated factor Xa into the peripheral vein, the procedure aims to regulate blood coagulation and prevent thrombotic events.
