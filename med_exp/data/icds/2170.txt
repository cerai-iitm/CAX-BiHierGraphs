Excision of Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the trachea, also known as the windpipe, through an endoscopic approach, which allows for visualization of the internal structures without the need for a large incision. The procedure can be performed through a natural opening, such as the mouth or nose, or through an artificial opening created by the surgeon. The endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, is inserted through the chosen opening and guided to the trachea, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the excision. The goal of the procedure is to remove diseased or damaged tissue, tumors, or other lesions from the trachea, restoring normal breathing function and improving overall health.
