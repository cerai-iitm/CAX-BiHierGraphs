Resection of Gallbladder, Open Approach
The surgical removal of the gallbladder is performed through an open approach, involving a large incision in the abdomen to access the organ. This procedure is typically performed to treat gallstones, chronic inflammation, or cancer of the gallbladder. During the surgery, the gallbladder is carefully dissected from surrounding tissues and blood vessels, and then removed from the body. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to carefully control bleeding and ensure complete removal of the gallbladder.
