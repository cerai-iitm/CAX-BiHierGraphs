Insertion of Monitoring Device into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, allowing the insertion of a monitoring device to track vital signs or other health metrics. The device is carefully placed beneath the skin and fascia to ensure accurate readings and minimize potential complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and precisely position the device for optimal performance. Once the device is in place, the incision is closed, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
