Drainage of Right Upper Arm Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the external approach to drain the right upper arm skin, which is a minimally invasive technique used to remove fluid or other substances that have accumulated beneath the skin. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and involves making a small incision in the skin to access the affected area. The healthcare provider then uses specialized equipment to drain the fluid, which can help to relieve symptoms such as pain, swelling, and redness. After the procedure, the incision is closed and the arm is monitored for any signs of infection or other complications.
