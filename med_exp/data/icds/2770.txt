Drainage of Rectum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the anus or an existing colostomy stoma to remove fluid, pus, or other substances from the rectum. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms such as pain, pressure, or infection in the rectal area. The drainage device is carefully placed to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and organs, and the procedure may be performed under local or general anesthesia depending on the individual case. The drainage is typically continued until the underlying condition resolves or improves, and may be performed in conjunction with other treatments such as antibiotics or surgical repair.
