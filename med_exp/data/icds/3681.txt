Excision of Right Lower Arm Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision on the outer aspect of the right lower arm to access the skin tissue. The excision of skin tissue is then performed to diagnose and potentially treat various skin-related conditions, such as skin cancer, lesions, or ulcers. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and remove the problematic skin tissue. The goal of this procedure is to provide an accurate diagnosis and potentially improve the patient's skin health and appearance.
