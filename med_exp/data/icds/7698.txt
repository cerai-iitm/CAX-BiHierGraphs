Introduction of Nutritional Substance into Lower GI, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of a nutritional substance, such as a formula or nutrient-rich solution, into the lower gastrointestinal (GI) tract through a natural or artificial opening. The goal of this intervention is to provide essential nutrients to the body when oral intake is not possible or sufficient, often in cases of malnutrition, digestive disorders, or surgical interventions. The nutritional substance is carefully introduced into the lower GI tract, which includes the small intestine, colon, and rectum, to facilitate absorption and utilization by the body. This procedure may be performed in a controlled medical setting, such as a hospital or clinic, under the guidance of a healthcare professional.
