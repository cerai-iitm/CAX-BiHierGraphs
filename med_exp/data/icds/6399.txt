Inspection of Bladder, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the urethra or a surgically created opening to visually examine the inner lining of the bladder. The endoscope is equipped with a camera and light that allows the healthcare provider to inspect the bladder's mucosal surface, identify any abnormalities, and diagnose conditions such as bladder cancer, inflammation, or stones. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia or sedation to minimize discomfort, and it can be used to guide further treatments or biopsies. The entire process typically takes around 15-30 minutes to complete, depending on the complexity of the case.
