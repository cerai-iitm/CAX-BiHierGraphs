Resection of Right Lobe Liver, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove the right lobe of the liver, typically performed to treat liver cancer, benign tumors, or liver trauma. The surgery requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the liver, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and remove the damaged or diseased portion of the liver. The remaining liver tissue is then sutured to ensure proper blood flow and function. Following the procedure, the patient will require close monitoring to prevent complications and promote a smooth recovery.
