Excision of Right Inferior Parathyroid Gland, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the right inferior parathyroid gland, which is then carefully removed by a trained surgeon. The parathyroid glands, located near the thyroid gland, play a crucial role in regulating calcium levels in the blood, and excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as hyperparathyroidism. During the open approach, the surgeon makes a small incision in the neck, carefully dissecting the tissue to locate and isolate the affected gland, and then removes it to restore normal parathyroid function. The procedure requires great precision and attention to detail to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and nerves.
