Excision of Brain, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This percutaneous brain excision procedure involves the use of a needle or other device inserted through the skin to remove a sample of brain tissue for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as CT or MRI, to ensure accurate targeting of the affected area. The excised tissue sample is then sent to a laboratory for examination to aid in the diagnosis of a brain disorder or disease. This minimally invasive procedure allows for a rapid and precise diagnosis, enabling timely and effective treatment.
