Excision of Mitral Valve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the chest to access the heart, then carefully removing the diseased or damaged mitral valve. The surgeon will typically use a heart-lung machine to circulate blood during the operation, allowing the heart to be stopped temporarily. The excised valve is then sent to a laboratory for examination, and the area is closed to promote healing. The goal of this open approach is to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and swelling.
