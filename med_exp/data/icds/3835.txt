Replacement of Left Lower Leg Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Partial Thickness, External Approach
This procedure involves replacing damaged or diseased skin on the left lower leg with a partial thickness autologous tissue substitute, which is a laboratory-grown skin substitute derived from the patient's own cells. The external approach is used, where the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected area. The autologous tissue substitute is then carefully implanted to promote wound healing and tissue regeneration. This procedure is often performed to treat severe skin lesions, wounds, or burns on the left lower leg that have not responded to other treatments.
