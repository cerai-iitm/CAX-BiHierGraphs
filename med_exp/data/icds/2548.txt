Repair Upper Lip, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision on the exterior of the upper lip to gain access to the affected area, allowing the surgeon to repair damaged tissue, close wounds, or correct deformities. The external approach provides direct visualization of the lip's external surface, enabling precise repair and reconstruction. The surgeon may use sutures, grafts, or other techniques to restore the lip's natural shape and function. The goal of this procedure is to improve the appearance and function of the upper lip, enhancing overall facial aesthetics and oral function.
