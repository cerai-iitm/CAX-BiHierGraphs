Inspection of Pleura, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the visualization of the pleural cavity through a minimally invasive approach, using an endoscope inserted through the skin and soft tissues of the chest wall. The endoscope allows the healthcare provider to directly inspect the pleura, a thin layer of tissue that lines the chest cavity and surrounds the lungs. The procedure is performed to diagnose and manage various pleural conditions, such as pleural effusions, pneumothorax, or suspected pleural malignancy. During the procedure, the healthcare provider may also collect samples of tissue or fluid for further examination.
