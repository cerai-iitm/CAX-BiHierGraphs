Repair Right Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the skin to repair damaged subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the right hand. The subcutaneous tissue, a layer of fat and connective tissue, and fascia, a layer of connective tissue surrounding muscles, are carefully repaired to restore normal anatomy and function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, ensuring a thorough and precise repair. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and limited mobility in the right hand.
