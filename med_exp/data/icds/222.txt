Excision of Femoral Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to excise, or remove, the femoral nerve, a major nerve located in the thigh that supplies the lower extremities. The open approach requires a skin incision to access the nerve, and the procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The surgical excision of the femoral nerve is usually performed to alleviate chronic pain, numbness, or other symptoms resulting from nerve damage or compression. The goal of the procedure is to restore functional mobility and alleviate chronic discomfort in the affected leg.
