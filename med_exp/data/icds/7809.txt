Monitoring of Central Nervous Electrical Activity, Intraoperative, External Approach
During surgical procedures, continuous monitoring of central nervous system electrical activity is crucial to prevent potential brain damage. This invasive technique involves placing external electrodes or sensors on the scalp or skin to detect and record the brain's electrical impulses in real-time, providing surgeons with vital information to adjust their surgical approach. By analyzing the electrical activity, healthcare providers can identify potential complications, such as cerebral ischemia or seizures, and take prompt action to mitigate them. This intraoperative monitoring enables surgeons to optimize surgical techniques, reduce risks, and improve patient outcomes.
