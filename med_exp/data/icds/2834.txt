Excision of Duodenum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the duodenum, the first part of the small intestine. The duodenum is then excised, or surgically removed, using specialized instruments inserted through the endoscope. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The use of an endoscopic approach allows for reduced tissue trauma and faster recovery compared to traditional open surgical methods.
