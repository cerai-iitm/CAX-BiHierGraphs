Excision of Buttock Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of diseased or damaged subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the buttock region through an open approach, where the surgeon makes an incision in the buttock area to access the affected tissue. The excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as chronic wounds, ulcers, or infections that have not responded to conservative treatments. The surgical excision of the subcutaneous tissue and fascia allows for the removal of infected or damaged tissue, promoting a clean wound bed for subsequent closure or reconstruction. The procedure requires careful planning and execution to minimize tissue trauma and preserve vital structures in the buttock area.
