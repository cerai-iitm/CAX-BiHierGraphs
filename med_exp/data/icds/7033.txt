Inspection of Retroperitoneum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a scope through the skin to visually examine the retroperitoneum, a region of the abdominal cavity behind the peritoneum. The scope is equipped with a camera and light, allowing the healthcare provider to directly visualize the retroperitoneal space and any abnormalities that may be present. The percutaneous endoscopic approach is a less invasive alternative to traditional open surgery, resulting in reduced risk of complications and shorter recovery time. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to ensure patient comfort.
