Drainage of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage tube through a natural or artificial opening to remove excess fluid, air, or pus from the right upper lung lobe. The goal is to diagnose and treat conditions such as pleural effusion, pneumothorax, or lung abscess, which can cause respiratory distress and discomfort. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure precise placement of the drainage tube. After the procedure, the patient is monitored for any complications and the drainage tube is removed once the fluid has been successfully drained.
