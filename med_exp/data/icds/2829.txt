Excision of Small Intestine, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of a portion of the small intestine through a natural or artificial opening, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the area. The procedure is typically performed for diagnostic purposes, such as to examine a suspected lesion or tumor, or to collect tissue samples for further examination. The endoscope is guided through the natural opening, such as the mouth or anus, or through an artificial opening created surgically, allowing the physician to access the affected area of the small intestine. The excision of the small intestine tissue is then carried out using specialized instruments, providing valuable information for diagnosis and treatment planning.
