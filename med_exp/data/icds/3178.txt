Resection of Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the small intestine, which is then carefully isolated and resected, or removed, to treat conditions such as blockages, inflammation, or cancer. The surgeon will typically explore the abdominal cavity to identify the affected section of intestine and carefully dissect it from surrounding tissues. The resected portion of intestine is then removed and, if necessary, the remaining ends are reattached to restore normal digestive function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the intestine and surrounding structures, enabling a more precise and effective procedure.
