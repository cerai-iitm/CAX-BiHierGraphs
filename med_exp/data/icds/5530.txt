Supplement Lumbar Vertebra with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this open surgery, the lumbar vertebra is accessed through a direct incision to repair or replace damaged or diseased bone tissue with a synthetic substitute, such as a metal or plastic implant. The goal of the procedure is to restore the natural structure and function of the spine, alleviating symptoms like back pain, numbness, or tingling in the legs. The substitute material is carefully selected to match the natural curvature and alignment of the spine, ensuring optimal support and stability. Once in place, the synthetic substitute promotes bone growth and fusion, leading to long-term spinal stability and improved quality of life.
