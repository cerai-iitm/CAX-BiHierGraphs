Destruction of Left Common Iliac Artery, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous destruction of the left common iliac artery is a minimally invasive procedure that involves accessing the artery through the skin to intentionally occlude or ablate the blood vessel. This procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the artery and surrounding structures in real-time. The artery is accessed using a catheter or other specialized instrument, through which an ablative energy source, such as radiofrequency or cryoablation, is applied to destroy the targeted section of the artery. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or bleeding that cannot be managed through other means.
