Repair Chest Wall, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves a direct approach to the chest wall to repair defects or injuries affecting the thoracic cage, including ribs, cartilages, and sternum. The approach enables surgeons to visualize the affected area directly, allowing for precise repair and reconstruction of the chest wall. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the damaged area, and then uses sutures, plates, or other materials to stabilize and repair the chest wall structures. The goal of the procedure is to restore the integrity and function of the chest wall, improving breathing and overall respiratory function.
