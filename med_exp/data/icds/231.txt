Release Median Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the wrist to access the median nerve, which is then carefully released from any surrounding tissue or structures that may be compressing or entrapping it. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure on the nerve and alleviate symptoms such as numbness, tingling, or pain in the hand or fingers. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the nerve and surrounding tissues, enabling precise dissection and release. Following the procedure, the incision is typically closed and the wrist is immobilized to facilitate healing and recovery.
