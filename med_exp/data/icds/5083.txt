Reposition Left Humeral Shaft with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves manually realigning the left humeral shaft to its anatomically correct position, utilizing an open approach that allows for direct visualization of the affected area. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are then implanted to stabilize the bone and facilitate the healing process. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the humeral shaft, allowing the surgeon to directly manipulate the bone and surrounding soft tissues. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment, relieve pain, and improve functional mobility in the affected arm.
