Fluoroscopy of Right Vertebral Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of X-rays and a low osmolar contrast medium to visualize the right vertebral artery. During the procedure, a catheter is inserted through an artery in the groin or arm and guided to the right vertebral artery, where the contrast medium is injected. Fluoroscopy, a type of continuous X-ray imaging, is then used to capture real-time images of the artery and its blood flow. The resulting images help diagnose and evaluate conditions such as vascular stenosis, aneurysms, or other abnormalities affecting blood flow to the brain.
