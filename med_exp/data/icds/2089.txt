Drainage of Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the mouth or nose to visualize the trachea, allowing the physician to identify and drain accumulated secretions, blood, or other debris that may be obstructing the airway. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the precise removal of obstructive material, promoting clearer breathing and reducing the risk of respiratory complications. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and cooperation. The goal of this intervention is to restore patency of the trachea, improving oxygenation and overall respiratory function.
