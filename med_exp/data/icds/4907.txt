Reposition Right Mandible with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves repositioning the right mandible, or lower jawbone, to its correct anatomical position through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The surgery is performed using small incisions, allowing for less tissue damage and scarring. Internal fixation devices, such as plates and screws, are inserted through these small incisions to stabilize the bone and facilitate healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper jaw function and alignment, alleviating symptoms such as pain, difficulty chewing, and limited jaw mobility.
