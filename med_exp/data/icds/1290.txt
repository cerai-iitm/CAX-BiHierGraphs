Repair Hepatic Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to expose the hepatic artery, which is then repaired using sutures or grafts to restore normal blood flow to the liver. The surgical approach allows for direct visualization of the artery and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to carefully repair any damage or defects. The repair may involve reattaching a detached artery, closing a laceration, or replacing a diseased segment with a healthy graft. The goal of the procedure is to restore optimal blood flow to the liver and surrounding tissues, promoting healthy function and preventing complications.
