Bypass Right Hepatic Duct to Small Intestine, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves creating a direct connection between the right hepatic duct and the small intestine, allowing bile to flow directly from the liver into the intestine. The open approach requires a surgical incision in the abdomen, through which the right hepatic duct is accessed and connected to the small intestine. The bypass allows for normal bile drainage and digestion, improving overall liver function and alleviating symptoms associated with bile duct obstruction. By diverting bile flow from the blocked duct, this procedure helps restore normal digestive processes and improves the patient's overall health.
