Division of Stomach, Pylorus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually guide the division of the stomach or pylorus through a small incision in the skin. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth and guided to the pylorus, where specialized instruments are used to divide the targeted area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and can be used to treat conditions such as gastric outlet obstruction or pyloric stenosis. The percutaneous approach allows for reduced recovery time and less post-operative pain compared to traditional open surgical methods.
