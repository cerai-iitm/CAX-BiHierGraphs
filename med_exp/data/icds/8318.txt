Beam Radiation of Other Bone using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This therapeutic procedure involves the use of high-energy photons with energies ranging from 1 to 10 million electron volts (MeV) to destroy cancer cells in bones other than the skull, ribs, sternum, and thoracic vertebrae. The treatment is typically administered using external beam radiation therapy, where a machine outside the body directs the photon beam to the targeted bone area. The photon beam is shaped and focused to minimize damage to surrounding healthy tissue and organs. The procedure requires precise planning and imaging to ensure accurate delivery of the radiation dose to the affected bone.
