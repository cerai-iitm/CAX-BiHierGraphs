Detachment at Left Foot, Partial 1st Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves separating the first ray, consisting of the big toe and the first metatarsal bone, from the rest of the left foot through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision. The detachment is partial, meaning that only a portion of the ray is separated, rather than the entire structure. This procedure may be necessary to treat conditions such as diabetic foot ulcers, severe infections, or traumatic injuries. The goal of the procedure is to remove damaged or infected tissue, promote healing, and improve overall foot function.
