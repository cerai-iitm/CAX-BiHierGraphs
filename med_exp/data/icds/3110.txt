Repair Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the small intestine, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect and manually repair any damaged or diseased portions of the intestine. The open approach provides direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to carefully suture or remove any damaged sections of the small intestine. The repair may involve resecting and re-anastomosing the intestine, or repairing any perforations or holes in the intestinal wall. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal intestinal function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
