Excision of Small Intestine, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the small intestine, which is then visually examined and explored to identify any abnormalities or diseases. The surgeon will remove a portion of the small intestine, which may be sent for further examination or testing to aid in diagnosis. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the small intestine, enabling the surgeon to perform a thorough examination and excision of the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to diagnose and potentially treat conditions affecting the small intestine, such as tumors, ulcers, or inflammatory diseases.
