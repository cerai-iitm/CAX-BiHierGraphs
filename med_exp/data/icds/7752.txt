Irrigation of Skin and Mucous Membranes using Irrigating Substance
This procedure involves flushing the skin and mucous membranes with a sterile solution to remove dirt, debris, or bacteria, promoting healthy tissue and aiding in wound healing. The irrigating substance helps to cleanse the area, reducing the risk of infection and promoting a clean environment for recovery. The gentle flow of the solution helps to loosen and remove any foreign particles, allowing the affected area to breathe and heal more efficiently. This procedure is often used to treat skin conditions, wounds, or burns, and may be performed in a hospital or clinical setting by a healthcare professional.
