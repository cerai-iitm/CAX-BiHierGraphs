Resection of Aortic Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of the aortic lymphatic system, which is a network of vessels and nodes that help to filter out toxins and disease-causing agents from the body. Through an open approach, a surgical incision is made to access the aortic lymphatic system, allowing the surgeon to visualize and carefully remove the affected portion. The goal of the procedure is to treat conditions such as lymphatic disorders, cancer, or infections that have spread to the aortic lymphatic system. The procedure requires meticulous skill and precision to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and ensure proper healing.
