Dilation of Sigmoid Colon with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an intraluminal device to dilate the sigmoid colon through a flexible tube inserted via a natural or artificial opening. The device is guided through the colon under endoscopic visualization, allowing for real-time monitoring of the procedure. The dilation of the sigmoid colon can be used to treat conditions such as strictures or narrowing, improving the passage of stool and reducing symptoms such as constipation or abdominal pain. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort.
