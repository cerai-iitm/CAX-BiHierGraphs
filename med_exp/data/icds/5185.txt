Excision of Sacrum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the sacrum, a triangular bone located at the base of the spine. The surgeon then carefully removes the sacrum, either partially or entirely, depending on the underlying condition or injury. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the affected area. The goal of this procedure is often to treat conditions such as cancer, trauma, or degenerative diseases that affect the sacrum and surrounding tissues.
