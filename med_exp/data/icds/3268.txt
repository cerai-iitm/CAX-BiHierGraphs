Bypass Left Hepatic Duct to Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical intervention involves the creation of a bypass between the left hepatic duct and the small intestine, performed through an open approach. The procedure is typically indicated for patients with a blocked or damaged left hepatic duct, which can cause bile to accumulate in the liver and lead to life-threatening complications. During the surgery, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the affected area and constructs a new pathway for bile to flow from the left hepatic duct into the small intestine, restoring the normal flow of bile and improving the patient's digestive function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomy, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any anatomical complexities or abnormalities.
