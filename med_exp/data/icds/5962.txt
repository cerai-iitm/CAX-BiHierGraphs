Fusion of Left Sacroiliac Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the permanent joining of the left sacroiliac joint to reduce pain and instability, using a tissue substitute harvested from the patient's own body. The open approach requires an incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to carefully prepare the bone surfaces and insert the autologous tissue substitute. The substitute helps to promote bone growth and fusion, stabilizing the joint and alleviating symptoms. Once the fusion is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient begins the recovery process, which may involve physical therapy and rehabilitation to restore normal function and mobility.
