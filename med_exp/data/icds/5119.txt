Reposition Left Thumb Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves repositioning a dislocated or malformed phalanx bone in the left thumb through a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the affected area. The surgeon uses specialized instruments and imaging guidance to manipulate the bone into its correct position. An internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, is then inserted through the incision to hold the phalanx in place while it heals. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the thumb, alleviating pain and improving mobility.
