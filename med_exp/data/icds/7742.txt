Introduction of Anesthetic Agent into Joints, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves injecting an anesthetic agent directly into a joint through the skin, utilizing a percutaneous approach, to numb the area and provide temporary pain relief. The anesthetic is carefully administered using a fine needle and syringe, guided by imaging techniques or anatomical landmarks to ensure accurate placement. This minimally invasive procedure is often performed to diagnose or treat conditions such as joint inflammation, damage, or overuse, as well as to provide pain management for chronic conditions like osteoarthritis. The benefits of this approach include reduced discomfort, improved mobility, and a faster recovery time compared to more invasive surgical procedures.
