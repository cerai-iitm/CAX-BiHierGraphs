Resection of Transverse Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of the transverse colon using an endoscope inserted through the skin. The endoscope is equipped with a camera and specialized instruments that allow the surgeon to visualize and resect the affected area of the colon. The procedure is performed percutaneously, meaning through the skin, eliminating the need for open surgery. The resected portion of the colon is then removed through the same incision, reducing trauma to the surrounding tissue and promoting faster recovery.
