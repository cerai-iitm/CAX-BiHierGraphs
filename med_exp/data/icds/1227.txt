Occlusion of Gastric Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small device through the skin to block the flow of blood in the gastric artery, a vessel that supplies the stomach, to treat conditions such as gastrointestinal bleeding or tumors. The device is guided through a tiny incision in the skin and advanced through the artery to the target site, where it is deployed to occlude the vessel. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the device. The occlusion of the gastric artery can help reduce bleeding or shrink tumors, improving patient outcomes and reducing the risk of complications.
