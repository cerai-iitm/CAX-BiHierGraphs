Dilation of Left Cephalic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small, flexible tube called a catheter into the left cephalic vein through a small incision in the skin. The catheter is guided to the target area using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy. Once in place, the catheter is used to introduce a dilator, which gently widens the vein, restoring blood flow and relieving any restrictions. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, allowing the patient to remain awake but feeling minimal discomfort during the procedure.
