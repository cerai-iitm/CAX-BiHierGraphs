Repair Right Knee Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right knee to access and repair a damaged or torn tendon. The surgeon visually inspects the tendon and surrounding tissue to identify the extent of the damage and removes any debris or damaged tissue. The tendon is then repaired using sutures or other fixation devices to restore its normal function and strength. The incision is closed, and the knee is immobilized to allow the tendon to heal properly.
