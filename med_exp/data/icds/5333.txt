Release Lumbar Vertebra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lumbar region to access the affected vertebra, allowing the surgeon to manually release tension or compression on the spinal cord or surrounding nerves. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the lower back or legs. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the vertebra and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the underlying cause of the compression. By releasing the lumbar vertebra, the procedure aims to restore normal spinal function and promote long-term recovery.
