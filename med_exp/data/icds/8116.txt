Fluoroscopy of Left Lower Extremity Arteries using Other Contrast
This diagnostic procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray imaging technique, to visualize the arteries in the left lower extremity. A contrasting agent is administered to enhance the visibility of the targeted blood vessels. The examination is performed to diagnose and evaluate arterial diseases, such as atherosclerosis, stenosis, or occlusion, in the left leg. The real-time imaging capabilities of fluoroscopy enable the radiologist to guide the procedure and capture precise images of the arterial structures.
