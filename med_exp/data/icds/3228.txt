Supplement Small Intestine with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the supplementation of the small intestine with a biocompatible autologous tissue substitute to enhance its functional capacity. The open approach is used, which involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the small intestine, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and perform the supplementation. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's own body, reducing the risk of rejection and increasing the chances of successful integration with the existing tissue. The goal of this procedure is to improve the absorption of nutrients and alleviate symptoms associated with small intestine disorders.
