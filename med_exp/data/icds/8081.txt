Fluoroscopy of Abdominal Aorta
The abdominal aorta is visualized using continuous X-ray beams and real-time imaging to guide the procedure, allowing for clear observation of blood vessels and internal structures. This non-invasive diagnostic test helps identify blockages, aneurysms, and other abnormalities in the abdominal aorta, a crucial artery that supplies oxygenated blood to the abdominal organs. The imaging process involves the patient lying on an examination table, while a fluoroscope is positioned above the abdomen to capture high-quality images of the aorta from multiple angles. A contrast agent may be injected into the bloodstream to enhance visibility of the aorta and its branches.
