Extirpation of Matter from Left Brachial Artery, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left arm to access the brachial artery, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and remove unwanted matter or debris from the artery. This open approach enables the surgeon to manually extract or dissolve the obstructing material, restoring blood flow and alleviating symptoms. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of specialized instruments and imaging technology to guide the surgeon. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored to ensure a smooth recovery.
