Introduction of Nutritional Substance into Upper GI, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, through the mouth or a artificial opening in the upper gastrointestinal (GI) tract to deliver nutritional substances directly into the stomach or small intestine. The endoscope guides the healthcare provider to the desired location, allowing for targeted administration of nutrition. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The goal of this intervention is to provide essential nutrients to patients who have difficulty consuming sufficient calories orally or have impaired digestion.
