Supplement Abdominal Wall with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the use of a patient's own tissue to supplement and reinforce the abdominal wall, utilizing an open approach to access the area. The autologous tissue substitute is harvested from the patient's body and then implanted to provide additional support and stability to the abdominal wall. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the procedure with greater precision. The goal of this procedure is to restore the integrity of the abdominal wall, alleviating symptoms and improving overall abdominal function.
