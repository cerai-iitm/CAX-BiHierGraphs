Drainage of Left Pleural Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating an incision in the chest wall to access the left pleural cavity, a space between the lung and chest wall, to drain fluid or air that has accumulated. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and remove the excess fluid or air, which can help alleviate symptoms such as difficulty breathing, chest pain, or coughing. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve inserting a drainage tube to facilitate the removal of the fluid or air. Post-procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications or further treatment.
