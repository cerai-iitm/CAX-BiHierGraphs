Extraction of Ampulla of Vater, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
Here is the description:

This procedure involves the removal of the ampulla of Vater, a small muscle that controls the flow of bile and pancreatic juice into the small intestine, through an endoscopic approach. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for minimally invasive visualization and treatment. The ampulla is accessed using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, which is guided through the mouth or a small incision. The goal of the procedure is to obtain diagnostic information about the ampulla or surrounding tissue, such as biopsies or imaging, to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of underlying conditions.
