Bypass Coronary Artery, One Artery from Aorta with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves redirecting blood flow around a blocked or narrowed coronary artery by creating a detour, or bypass, using a segment of healthy blood vessel, typically a vein, taken from the patient's own body. The open approach requires a surgical incision in the chest, which allows the surgeon to access the affected coronary artery and attach the harvested vein graft to the aorta, rerouting blood flow around the blockage. The goal of this procedure is to restore adequate blood flow to the heart muscle, alleviating symptoms such as angina and reducing the risk of heart attack. The autologous venous tissue used in this bypass grafting procedure helps minimize the risk of graft rejection and promotes healthier blood flow.
