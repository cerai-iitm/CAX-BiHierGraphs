Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Metal on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the damaged or arthritic left hip joint is replaced with an artificial joint made of a metal head and a polyethylene synthetic socket. The orthopedic surgeon performs the operation using an open approach, making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the affected joint. The metal and polyethylene components are carefully implanted to restore the natural anatomy of the hip joint, enabling the patient to regain range of motion, reduce pain, and improve overall mobility. The artificial joint is designed to mimic the natural movement of the hip, allowing the patient to engage in daily activities with minimal discomfort or restriction.
