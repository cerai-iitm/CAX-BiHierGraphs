Drainage of Intracranial Subdural Space with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to remove accumulated fluid or blood from the subdural space, a region located between the surface of the brain and the dura mater, the outermost protective membrane. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive insertion of the drainage device, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and guided by imaging techniques such as CT or MRI scans to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure on the brain, alleviate symptoms, and prevent further complications associated with intracranial subdural space accumulation.
