Resection of Thyroid Gland, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of all or part of the thyroid gland through an open approach, where an incision is made in the neck to access the gland. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as thyroid cancer, nodules, or hyperthyroidism. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the thyroid gland from surrounding tissues and blood vessels to remove the affected portion. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for any complications.
