Transplantation of Left Kidney, Allogeneic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves transplanting a donor kidney into the recipient's body through an open approach, where a large incision is made in the abdomen to access the surgical site. The allogeneic kidney, sourced from a genetically non-identical donor, is carefully implanted in the left side of the recipient's body, replacing the diseased or damaged kidney. The transplantation process involves connecting the transplanted kidney to the recipient's blood vessels and urinary tract, allowing for proper blood flow and waste elimination. The open approach allows the surgical team to visually inspect the surgical site and ensure a secure and precise implantation of the new kidney.
