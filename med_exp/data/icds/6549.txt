Excision of Uterus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the uterus, which is then carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding tissues and ligaments. The surgeon takes care to minimize bleeding and preserve surrounding structures, such as the bladder and ureters. The uterus is then removed through the abdominal incision, and the site is closed in layers to promote healing and minimize scarring. This open approach allows for direct visualization of the uterus and surrounding anatomy, enabling the surgeon to perform the procedure with precision and accuracy.
