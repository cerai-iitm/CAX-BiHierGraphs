Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Pulmonary Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A percutaneous insertion procedure is performed to place an infusion device into the right pulmonary vein, allowing for targeted and localized delivery of medication or other therapeutic agents directly into the pulmonary vasculature. The device is inserted through the skin and guided through the veins using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement. Once in position, the infusion device can be used to deliver treatment directly to the affected area, minimizing systemic effects and promoting optimal therapeutic outcomes. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes rapid recovery for the patient.
