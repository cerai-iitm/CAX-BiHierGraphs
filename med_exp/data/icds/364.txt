Dilation of Coronary Artery, Two Arteries with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the dilation of two blocked coronary arteries using a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the artery. A thin, flexible tube called a catheter is inserted through the incision and guided to the blockages, where a small inflatable balloon is used to widen the arteries. Additionally, an intraluminal device, such as a stent, is placed within the arteries to keep them open and improve blood flow. The goal of this procedure is to restore blood flow to the heart, reducing the risk of heart attack and other complications.
