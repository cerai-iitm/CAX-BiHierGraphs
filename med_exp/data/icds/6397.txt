Inspection of Ureter, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the use of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end to visually examine the ureter, which is the tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder. The scope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the doctor to directly visualize the ureter and detect any abnormalities, such as stones, strictures, or tumors. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. During the procedure, the doctor may also remove any blockages, take tissue samples, or perform other therapeutic interventions as needed.
