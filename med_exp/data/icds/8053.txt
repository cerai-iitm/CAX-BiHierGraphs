Fluoroscopy of Left Pulmonary Artery using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left pulmonary artery, which is responsible for transporting oxygenated blood from the heart to the lungs. Contrast material, other than the typical iodine-based or barium-based agents, is injected into the artery to enhance image quality and allow for better visualization of the artery's structure and any potential abnormalities. Fluoroscopy uses continuous X-ray beams to guide the procedure, providing real-time images of the artery and its surrounding tissues. The procedure is often performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as pulmonary embolism, arterial stenosis, and aneurysms.
