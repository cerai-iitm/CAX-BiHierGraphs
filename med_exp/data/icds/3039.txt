Release Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the small intestine, which is then released from any adhesions, scar tissue, or other attachments that may be obstructing its normal functioning. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the intestine and surrounding tissues to identify and address any underlying issues. The release of the small intestine can help to alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, bloating, and digestive problems. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal intestinal function and promote healthy digestion.
