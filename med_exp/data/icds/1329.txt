Supplement Right Femoral Artery with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this surgical procedure, the right femoral artery is accessed through an open incision to repair or replace a damaged or diseased segment. A nonautologous tissue substitute, such as a synthetic graft or biologic material, is used to supplement the affected artery. The substitute material is carefully sutured in place to restore blood flow and functionality to the artery. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the supplementation with precision and accuracy.
