Detachment at Right Middle Finger, Mid, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the middle phalanx bone in the right middle finger, specifically at the mid-shaft level. The open approach indicates that the surgeon makes a direct incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected area. The detachment process is typically performed to treat conditions such as severe fractures, bone deformities, or tissue damage that cannot be repaired. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal finger function and alleviate associated pain and discomfort.
