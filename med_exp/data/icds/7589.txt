Irrigation of Indwelling Device using Irrigating Substance, External Approach
This procedure involves flushing an indwelling device, such as a catheter or port, with a sterile irrigating substance to remove any blockages or debris. The external approach indicates that the device is accessed through the skin rather than through an open surgical site. The goal of irrigation is to restore patency and ensure proper function of the device, which is essential for effective treatment or therapy. The use of an irrigating substance helps to clear any obstructions and prevent complications associated with a malfunctioning device.
