Reposition Right Acetabulum with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
Here is the detailed description:

The surgical procedure involves repositioning the right acetabulum, which is thesocket of the hip joint, to its normal anatomic position. This is achieved through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The repositioning is accomplished using an internal fixation device, which helps to stabilize the acetabulum and promote healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the hip joint, alleviating pain and improving mobility.
