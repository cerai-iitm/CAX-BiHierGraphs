Reposition Left Upper Femur with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left upper femur, which has been fractured or displaced, to its anatomical position. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the damaged bone. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then inserted into the femur's medullary canal to provide stability and support during the healing process. This device helps to hold the bone in place, allowing it to heal in its correct position.
