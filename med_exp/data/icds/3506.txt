Removal of Intraluminal Device from Hepatobiliary Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of an intraluminal device from the hepatobiliary duct using an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening. The device is carefully extracted from the duct with the aid of specialized instruments and visualization provided by the endoscope. The hepatobiliary duct is a vital conduit for bile drainage from the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine, and the removal of the device restores normal flow and function. The endoscopic approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure with reduced risk of complications and faster recovery time compared to open surgical methods.
