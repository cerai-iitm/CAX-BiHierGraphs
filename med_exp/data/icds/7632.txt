Introduction of Nutritional Substance into Central Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a needle or catheter through the skin to access a central vein, usually in the chest or neck, to deliver nutritional substances, such as total parenteral nutrition, directly into the bloodstream. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The nutritional substance is administered through the catheter, providing essential nutrients, electrolytes, and fluids to patients who have difficulty consuming them orally or through other means. This method is often used for patients requiring long-term nutrition, such as those with gastrointestinal disorders or cancer.
