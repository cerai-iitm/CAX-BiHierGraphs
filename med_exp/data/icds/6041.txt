Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Hip Joint, Open Approach
The removal of an internal fixation device from the right hip joint is a surgical procedure performed through an open approach, involving a skin incision to access the hip joint. This procedure is typically done to remove hardware such as plates, screws, or rods that were previously implanted to stabilize the hip joint following a fracture or injury. The surgeon will carefully dissect the soft tissue to expose the internal fixation device, and then remove the device in its entirety. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and may involve a hospital stay to monitor for potential complications.
