Dilation of Coronary Artery, One Artery with Three Drug-eluting Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves widening a narrowed or blocked coronary artery to restore blood flow to the heart. A percutaneous approach is used, where a small incision is made in the skin and a catheter is inserted through an artery in the leg or arm. Three drug-eluting stents are then placed in the affected artery to keep it open and prevent re-blockage, while also releasing medication to prevent further narrowing. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance, allowing the cardiologist to visualize the artery and ensure accurate placement of the stents.
