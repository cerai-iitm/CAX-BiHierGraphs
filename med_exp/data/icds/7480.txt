Change Packing Material on Right Upper Leg
The procedure involves replacing the packing material used to apply pressure or protection to a wound or incision on the right upper leg to promote healing, manage drainage, or prevent infection. The old packing material is carefully removed to assess the wound or incision and clean the area. New packing material, such as gauze or foam, is then applied to the affected area, ensuring a snug and secure fit to facilitate the healing process. The new packing material is typically secured in place with adhesive tape or bandages to prevent shifting or dislodgement.
