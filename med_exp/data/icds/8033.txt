Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Vertebral Arteries
This fluoroscopically guided procedure involves real-time imaging to visualize the vertebral arteries on both sides of the neck, allowing for precise assessment of their structure and function. The imaging process provides valuable diagnostic information for conditions such as vertebral artery stenosis, occlusion, or dissection, which can cause stroke or other neurological symptoms. During the procedure, a patient is positioned on an examination table and a fluoroscope is used to produce continuous X-ray images of the arteries, guiding the healthcare provider to navigate the placement of catheters or other medical devices. The resulting images help diagnose and inform treatment for vascular disorders affecting the vertebral arteries.
