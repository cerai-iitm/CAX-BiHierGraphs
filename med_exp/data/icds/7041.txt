Inspection of Gastrointestinal Tract, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visualize the gastrointestinal tract, allowing for direct examination of the stomach, small intestine, and other digestive organs. The percutaneous endoscopic approach enables real-time visualization of the tract's internal structures, facilitating the identification of abnormalities, such as ulcers, bleeding, or tumors. During the procedure, the endoscope is guided through the skin and into the gastrointestinal tract, providing high-definition images that aid in diagnosis and treatment. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and minimize discomfort.
