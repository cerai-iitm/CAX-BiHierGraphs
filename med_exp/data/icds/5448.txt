Reposition Left Lower Femur with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves realigning the left lower femur bone to its correct position, using a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. A small incision is made in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected area, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. An internal fixation device, such as a rod or plate, is then inserted to stabilize the bone and promote proper alignment. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the device and optimal bone alignment.
