Dilation of Right Popliteal Artery, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the detailed description:

During this minimally invasive procedure, a catheter is inserted through the skin and guided to the right popliteal artery to widen the narrowed or blocked artery. A tiny balloon is then inflated to compress the plaque or blockage against the artery wall, increasing blood flow to the lower leg. Once the artery is dilated, the balloon is deflated and removed, allowing the blood to flow more freely. This percutaneous procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopy guidance to minimize risks and ensure accurate placement of the catheter.
