Drainage of Left Pleural Cavity with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a small incision in the chest wall to access the left pleural cavity, a space between the lungs and chest wall. The goal is to drain excess fluid or air that has accumulated in the cavity, which can cause respiratory distress and discomfort. A drainage device, such as a chest tube, is inserted through the incision to facilitate the removal of the unwanted fluid or air. The device is carefully placed to ensure proper drainage and minimize complications.
