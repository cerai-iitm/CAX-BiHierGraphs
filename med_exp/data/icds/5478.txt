Reposition Left Tibia with Ring External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
During this procedure, the surgeon repositions the left tibia, also known as the shinbone, to its normal anatomical alignment. To achieve this, a ring external fixation device is applied percutaneously, meaning through small incisions in the skin, to stabilize the bone and facilitate the repositioning process. The ring device consists of a circular frame that is attached to the bone with pins or wires, allowing for precise control over the bone's movement and alignment. By repositioning the tibia with the external fixation device, the surgeon can restore normal function and mobility to the affected leg.
