Drainage of Right Inguinal Region, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right inguinal region to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to drain excess fluid, blood, or pus that has accumulated due to an underlying condition. This open approach enables direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and treat the source of the problem. During the procedure, the surgeon may remove any infected tissue or debris and flush the area with saline solution to promote healing. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote tissue repair and minimize the risk of further complications.
