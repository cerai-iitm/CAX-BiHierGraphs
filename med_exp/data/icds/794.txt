Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Axillary Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the right axillary artery through a small incision in the skin, followed by the insertion of an infusion device under fluoroscopic guidance. The device is carefully placed to ensure proper placement and function, allowing for the delivery of medications, fluids, or contrast agents as needed. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgery and allows for faster recovery. The infusion device is designed to provide a safe and efficient way to administer therapeutic agents over an extended period.
