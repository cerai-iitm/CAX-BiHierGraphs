Replacement of Left Hand Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left hand artery with a tissue substitute harvested from the patient's own body, utilizing an open approach to access the affected area. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully selected and refined to ensure a precise match with the natural artery, promoting optimal blood flow and minimizing the risk of rejection. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a direct incision in the skin to expose the left hand artery, allowing for accurate dissection and removal of the damaged or diseased artery. The autologous tissue substitute is then carefully implanted and secured in place, restoring normal blood flow and promoting healthy functioning of the hand.
