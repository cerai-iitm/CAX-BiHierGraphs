Extirpation of Matter from Intracranial Subdural Space, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of abnormal material, such as blood or pus, from the subdural space, a region between the surface of the brain and the dura mater, the outermost protective membrane of the brain. The procedure is performed through a percutaneous approach, where a small incision is made in the skin to access the affected area. Using specialized instruments, the surgeon carefully extracts the unwanted material, helping to alleviate pressure on the brain and promote healing. The percutaneous approach allows for quicker recovery and less trauma to surrounding tissues compared to open surgical techniques.
