Extirpation of Matter from Left Subclavian Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the removal of unwanted material or substances from the left subclavian vein, which is a major blood vessel located under the collarbone. The procedure is typically performed using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the instruments. The healthcare provider makes a small incision in the skin and inserts a needle or catheter into the left subclavian vein to extract the desired material. The extracted material may include blood clots, foreign objects, or other substances that are obstructing blood flow or causing harm to the patient.
