Reposition Left Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left tibia, also known as the shinbone, to its correct anatomical position. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the bone. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the leg, alleviating symptoms such as pain, limited mobility, and deformity. Following repositioning, the bone is stabilized with internal fixation devices, such as plates or screws, to promote healing and prevent further displacement.
