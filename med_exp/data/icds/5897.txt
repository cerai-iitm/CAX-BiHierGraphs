Excision of Left Hip Joint, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the left hip joint, with the primary intention of exploring and examining the joint to gather diagnostic information. The goal is to identify any underlying conditions or abnormalities that may be causing pain, discomfort, or mobility issues. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the soft tissue to expose the joint, allowing for a visually inspection and possible collection of tissue samples. The gathered information is then used to inform further treatment options or guide additional diagnostic testing.
