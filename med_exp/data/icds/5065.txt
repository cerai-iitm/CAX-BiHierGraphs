Reposition Thoracic Vertebra, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a percutaneous approach to reposition a thoracic vertebra, which is a vertebra located in the mid-back region. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement and alignment of the vertebra. A small incision is made in the skin, and specialized instruments are inserted through the incision to manipulate and reposition the affected vertebra. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal spinal alignment, alleviate pressure on surrounding nerves and blood vessels, and relieve associated back pain and discomfort.
