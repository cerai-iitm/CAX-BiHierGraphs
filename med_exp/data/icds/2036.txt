Destruction of Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the destruction of a portion of the trachea through a natural or artificial opening. The destruction is typically performed using specialized instruments passed through an endoscope, allowing for visualization of the trachea during the procedure. The procedure may be performed to remove abnormal tissue growths, such as tumors or granulomas, or to alleviate symptoms associated with tracheal stenosis or other conditions. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal airflow and breathing function by removing the affected portion of the trachea.
