Extirpation of Matter from Left Ulnar Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left ulnar artery, a vital blood vessel in the forearm. The goal is to remove unwanted matter, such as blood clots or other obstructions, from the artery to restore blood flow and prevent further complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the artery and remove the unwanted material under direct visualization. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for recovery.
