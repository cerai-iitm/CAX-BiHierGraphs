Release Ascending Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the ascending colon, which is the upper part of the large intestine. The goal of the operation is to release the ascending colon from adhesions or other abnormal attachments that may be causing bowel obstruction or other complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the area and perform the release manually. Once the colon is freed, the incision is closed, and the patient can recover from the procedure.
