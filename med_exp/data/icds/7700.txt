Introduction of Anti-inflammatory into Lower GI, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light is inserted through the anus or an artificial opening to visualize the lower gastrointestinal (GI) tract, which includes the colon, rectum, and anus. An anti-inflammatory agent is then introduced into the lower GI tract through the endoscope to reduce inflammation and swelling in the affected area. The medication is typically delivered directly to the site of inflammation to provide targeted relief and promote healing. This minimally invasive procedure is performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote a smooth recovery.
