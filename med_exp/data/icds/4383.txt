Drainage of Left Hip Muscle, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
During this minimally invasive procedure, a healthcare professional uses imaging guidance to insert a small drainage tube through the skin and into the affected left hip muscle. The purpose of the procedure is to evacuate any unwanted fluid, such as blood or pus, that may have accumulated in the muscle tissue. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may help alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and limited mobility in the hip area. Once the drainage is complete, the tube is removed and the incision site is cleaned and dressed.
