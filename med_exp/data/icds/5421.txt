Reposition Left Upper Femur with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning the left upper femur, also known as the femoral neck or shaft, to its correct anatomical position following a fracture or deformity. An open approach is used, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. Once the bone is repositioned, an internal fixation device, such as a plate, screw, or rod, is implanted to stabilize the bone and promote healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal functioning of the hip joint and alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, or limited mobility.
