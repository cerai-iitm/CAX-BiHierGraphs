Repair Left Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left hand to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue and connective tissue that surround muscles, nerves, and blood vessels. The damaged or torn tissue and fascia are then repaired using sutures or other materials to restore normal anatomy and function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the damaged tissue. The goal of this procedure is to restore strength, mobility, and sensation to the left hand by properly repairing the damaged subcutaneous tissue and fascia.
