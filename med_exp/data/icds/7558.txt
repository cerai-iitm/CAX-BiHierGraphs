Removal of Nasal Packing Material
This procedure involves the careful extraction of nasal packing material that was previously inserted to control bleeding, promote healing, or allow for surgical access during a nasal procedure. The removal process typically takes place in a clinical setting, where a healthcare professional gently grasps the packing material with forceps or a similar instrument and withdraws it from the nasal cavity. The removal of nasal packing material is usually a swift and painless process, although some discomfort or sensation of pressure may be experienced by the patient. Following the procedure, the patient may be advised to refrain from blowing their nose, strenuous activities, and other actions that may dislodge the newly exposed nasal tissues.
