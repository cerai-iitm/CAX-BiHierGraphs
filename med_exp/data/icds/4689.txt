Repair Left Lower Arm and Wrist Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the damaged tendon in the left lower arm and wrist. The surgeon then repairs the tendon using sutures or other materials to restore its normal function and relieve symptoms such as pain and limited mobility. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the damaged tendon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately repair the tendon and promote healing. Post-operatively, the individual may require physical therapy to restore strength and range of motion in the affected arm and wrist.
