Inspection of Hepatobiliary Duct, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visualize the hepatobilitary ducts, allowing for direct examination of the ductal system. The endoscope is equipped with a light and camera, which enables the physician to visually inspect the ducts for any abnormalities or blockages. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for a minimally invasive and relatively low-risk procedure, reducing the need for open surgery. During the procedure, the physician can also perform diagnostic or therapeutic interventions, such as biopsy or stone removal, if necessary.
