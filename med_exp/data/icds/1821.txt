Resection of Aortic Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the aortic lymphatic system through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach, allowing for a smaller incision and reduced tissue trauma. A scope is inserted through the skin and guided to the affected area, where specialized instruments are used to resect the targeted section of the lymphatic system. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as lymphedema or lymph node cancer. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery.
