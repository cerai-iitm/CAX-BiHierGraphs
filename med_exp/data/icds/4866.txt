Replacement of Right Temporal Bone with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right temporal bone with an autologous tissue substitute, utilizing an open approach. The.open approach involves making a large incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the temporal bone. The autologous tissue substitute, typically harvested from the patient's own body, is then carefully shaped and secured in place to restore the natural anatomy of the temporal bone. This complex and delicate procedure requires a high degree of surgical skill and precision to achieve optimal results and minimize potential complications.
