Alteration of Face, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making intentional changes to the facial structure through an open approach, where the skin is incised to access the underlying tissues. The goal of the alteration is to improve the overall appearance of the face, which may be desired for aesthetic or reconstructive purposes. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the facial tissues, enabling the surgeon to make precise adjustments to achieve the desired outcome. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully close the incision site to minimize scarring and promote optimal healing.
