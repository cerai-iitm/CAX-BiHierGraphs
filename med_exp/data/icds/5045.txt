Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Ulna, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screw, from the right ulna bone in the forearm. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the internal fixation device, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and carefully remove the device. The removal of the internal fixation device is typically performed when the fracture has fully healed and the device is no longer needed. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and may take approximately 30-60 minutes to complete, depending on the complexity of the case.
