Excision of Thoracic Vertebra, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
During this minimally invasive procedure, a small incision is made in the skin to access the thoracic vertebra. Using imaging guidance, a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to remove a sample of tissue or bone from the affected vertebra. The entire procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, reducing recovery time and minimizing discomfort. The diagnostic excision helps physicians accurately diagnose and stage spinal conditions, guiding further treatment and management plans.
