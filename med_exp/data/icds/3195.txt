Resection of Ascending Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdomen to access the ascending colon, which is the first part of the large intestine that connects to the small intestine. The damaged or diseased portion of the ascending colon is carefully removed, and the remaining healthy sections are reconnected to restore normal bowel function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and precisely dissect the tissues. The resection aims to eliminate diseased tissue, relieve symptoms, and prevent potential complications associated with colon disease.
