Irrigation of Peritoneal Cavity using Irrigating Substance, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the insertion of a thin catheter or needle through the abdominal wall to access the peritoneal cavity, a space between the lining of the abdominal wall and the organs. A sterile irrigating substance is then introduced through the catheter to flush out the cavity, allowing for the removal of any debris, fluid, or other substances. The peritoneal cavity is gently lavaged to collect specimens or to perform diagnostic assessments, such as evaluating for infection or cancer. This minimally invasive percutaneous approach allows for a more comfortable and rapid recovery compared to open surgical procedures.
