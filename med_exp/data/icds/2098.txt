Drainage of Right Lower Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During a bronchoscopy procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or nose to visualize the right lower lobe bronchus. The bronchus is then drained of any accumulated fluid, mucus, or blood using suction or irrigation techniques. The procedure is performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and manage conditions such as bronchiectasis, lung cancer, or pneumonia, and to improve lung function and alleviate symptoms.
