Transfer Right Abdomen Muscle, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically transferring a muscle from one location to another in the right abdomen, utilizing an open approach that allows the surgeon to visually examine the area. The goal of this procedure is to restore functionality and strength to the affected area, which may have been damaged due to injury or disease. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdominal wall to access the muscle, carefully dissecting and mobilizing the tissue to its new location. The transferred muscle is then secured in place with sutures or other fixation devices to ensure proper healing and integration with surrounding tissues.
