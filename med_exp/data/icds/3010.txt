Inspection of Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This medical procedure involves the visualization of the stomach through the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, inserted through the mouth or a surgical incision. The endoscope allows the physician to examine the lining of the stomach, identifying any abnormalities such as ulcers, inflammation, or tumors. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. During the procedure, the physician may collect tissue samples for further examination or remove any foreign objects or lesions found in the stomach.
