Excision of Right Toe Phalanx, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a phalanx bone from the right toe through an open approach, where the skin is incised to access the affected area. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to expose the phalanx bone, then carefully excises the bone using specialized instruments. The removal of the phalanx bone may be necessary to treat conditions such as osteomyelitis, trauma, or severe arthritis. Following the excision, the incision is closed, and the wound is dressed to promote healing.
