Excision of Right Acetabulum, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the right acetabulum, the cup-like structure in the pelvis that forms the socket of the hip joint. The purpose of the procedure is diagnostic, aiming to identify any abnormalities or diseases affecting the acetabulum. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully excises, or removes, a portion of the acetabulum to collect tissue samples or to visualize the underlying structures. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the hip joint, enabling the surgeon to accurately diagnose conditions such as osteoarthritis, bone tumors, or hip dysplasia.
