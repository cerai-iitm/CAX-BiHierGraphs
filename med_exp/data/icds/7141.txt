Detachment at Right Lower Arm, Mid, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves detaching a portion of the right lower arm at the mid-humeral level, where the humerus bone is surgically separated through an open approach. The objective of this procedure is to treat severe injuries, infections, or tumors that cannot be managed with other treatments. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the mid-humeral region, allowing for the detachment of the affected segment of the arm. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a multidisciplinary team of medical professionals for post-operative care and rehabilitation.
