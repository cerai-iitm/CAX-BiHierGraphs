Transfer Left Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Skin, Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure based on the provided ICD-10 code and long title:

During this surgical procedure, the skin, subcutaneous tissue, and fascia of the left hand are transferred to a new location, allowing for the reconstruction of damaged or deficient tissue. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin to directly visualize the area and perform the transfer. The subcutaneous tissue and fascia are carefully dissected and mobilized to ensure proper blood supply and nerve function. The transferred tissue is then secured in place to promote healing and optimal functional outcome.
