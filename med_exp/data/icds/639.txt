Revision of Short-term External Heart Assist System in Heart, External Approach
This procedure involves the revision of a short-term external heart assist system in the heart, which is performed through an external approach. The external heart assist system is a mechanical device that provides temporary support to the heart, increasing cardiac output and improving blood flow to vital organs. During the revision procedure, the surgical team accesses the system through a small incision in the chest wall, allowing for adjustment or replacement of the device as needed. The goal of the revision is to optimize the device's function, ensuring that it continues to provide adequate circulatory support to the patient.
