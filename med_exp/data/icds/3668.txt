Excision of Back Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the back to access the affected area, followed by the careful removal of damaged or diseased skin tissue. The excision may be performed to treat a variety of conditions, including skin cancer, wounds, or lesions. Once the affected skin is removed, the wound is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and dressed to promote healing. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, ensuring precise removal of the damaged tissue.
