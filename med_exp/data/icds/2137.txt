Drainage of Right Lung, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During a diagnostic endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided through the airways to the right lung. The tube allows for visualization of the lung tissue and identification of any abnormalities or blockages. The procedure enables the drainage of accumulated fluid or pus from the right lung, which can help alleviate symptoms such as difficulty breathing, chest pain, and coughing. The use of an endoscope provides a minimally invasive approach, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery.
