Excision of Left Hip Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to remove the left hip joint, a complex ball-and-socket joint that connects the femur to the pelvis. The goal of the excision is to treat various hip joint disorders or injuries, such as severe arthritis, bone tumors, or traumatic fractures, that cause debilitating pain, limited mobility, and impaired quality of life. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left hip area to access the affected joint, carefully dissects the surrounding soft tissues, and removes the damaged or diseased joint components. The resulting joint space is then prepared for subsequent reconstruction, replacement, or prosthetic implantation to restore hip function and alleviate symptoms.
