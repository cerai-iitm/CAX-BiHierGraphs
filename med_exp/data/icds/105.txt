Excision of Lumbar Spinal Cord, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This diagnostic procedure involves a surgical incision to access the lumbar region of the spinal cord, allowing for the removal of a section of the spinal cord tissue for examination and diagnosis. The open approach enables direct visualization of the spinal cord and surrounding structures, facilitating a more precise excision of the targeted area. The excised tissue is then sent for further examination, such as histopathological analysis, to help identify any underlying conditions or abnormalities. The goal of this procedure is to provide a definitive diagnosis and guide further treatment options for the patient.
