Excision of Left Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left breast through an open incision, often performed as a treatment for breast cancer or to prevent the development of breast cancer in high-risk individuals. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the breast tissue, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the affected area. The procedure typically begins with an incision in the breast, followed by dissection of the breast tissue and glands, and ultimately, the removal of the entire left breast. Following the excision, the surgical site is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for any complications or adverse reactions.
