Excision of Right Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skin to access the affected area of the right foot, where subcutaneous tissue and fascia are carefully excised to remove damaged, infected, or necrotic tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the treatment site, facilitating precise dissection and removal of the targeted tissue. The goal of this procedure is to promote healing, relieve symptoms, and restore normal function to the right foot. Post-operatively, the wound is typically closed, and the patient may require pain management and wound care instructions to ensure a smooth recovery.
