Replacement of Thoracic Aorta, Descending with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a portion of the thoracic aorta, specifically the descending segment, with zooplastic tissue, a type of biological tissue harvested from animal sources, such as cows or pigs. The open approach requires a thoracotomy, a surgical incision in the chest wall, to access the affected area. The zooplastic tissue isimplanted to restore blood flow and alleviate symptoms associated with thoracic aortic disease, such as aneurysms, dissections, or stenosis. The goal of the procedure is to improve cardiovascular function and reduce the risk of complications or rupture.
