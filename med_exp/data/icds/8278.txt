Beam Radiation of Spleen using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This non-invasive procedure uses high-energy photons with an energy range of 1-10 MeV to deliver targeted radiation therapy to the spleen, a vital organ located in the upper left abdomen that filters the blood and stores red blood cells. The beam radiation is carefully planned and controlled to ensure precise targeting of the spleen, minimizing exposure to surrounding healthy tissues. During the treatment, the patient is positioned on a treatment table and the photon beam is directed at the spleen from multiple angles, allowing for effective treatment of the targeted area. The goal of this procedure is to destroy cancer cells, reduce pain, and alleviate symptoms related to splenic disorders or cancer that has spread to the spleen.
