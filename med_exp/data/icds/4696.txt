Repair Left Upper Leg Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left upper leg to access and repair a damaged tendon. The tendon is then carefully repaired using sutures or other materials to restore its normal function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, ensuring a precise and thorough repair. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the leg is immobilized to allow the tendon to heal properly.
