Fluoroscopy of Left Vertebral Artery
This fluoroscopically guided procedure involves the insertion of a contrast agent into the left vertebral artery to visualize and assess blood flow and vessel structures. Real-time X-ray imaging is used to guide the procedure, allowing for precise placement of the contrast agent and visualization of the artery. The procedure is typically performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as vertebral artery stenosis, vasospasm, or arteriovenous malformations. It is often performed in conjunction with other diagnostic tests, such as angiography or CT scans, to provide a comprehensive understanding of vascular anatomy and circulation.
