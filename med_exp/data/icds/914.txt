Restriction of Intracranial Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a tiny device through a small incision in the groin area to reach the intracranial artery. The device is then placed inside the artery to restrict blood flow and relieve pressure. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance and local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. The goal of this treatment is to prevent further bleeding or rupture of the affected artery.
