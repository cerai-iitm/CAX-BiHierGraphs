Repair Left Hand Tendon, Open Approach
During this open procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left hand to access the damaged tendon. The tendon is then repaired using sutures or other materials to reattach or reconnect the torn or damaged portions. The surgeon may also remove any scar tissue or debris that may be contributing to the tendon damage. Once the repair is complete, the incision is closed, and the hand is immobilized to allow the tendon to heal properly.
