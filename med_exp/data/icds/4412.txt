Excision of Left Upper Arm Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected muscle in the left upper arm. The diseased or damaged muscle is then carefully dissected and removed, taking care to preserve surrounding nerves and blood vessels. The incision is subsequently closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing and minimize the risk of infection. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, weakness, or limited mobility in the left upper arm, and to improve overall arm function and quality of life.
