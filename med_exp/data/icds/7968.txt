Ultrasonography of Right and Left Heart
This non-invasive diagnostic imaging test uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the heart's structure and function, providing valuable information on cardiac valves, blood flow, and chamber sizes. The procedure involves a handheld probe called a transducer that is placed on the chest, which sends and receives sound waves, generating real-time images of the right and left sides of the heart. This test is commonly used to diagnose cardiovascular conditions, such as heart failure, valve problems, and coronary artery disease, and to monitor the heart's response to treatment. The resulting images enable healthcare providers to assess cardiac function and make informed decisions about patient care.
