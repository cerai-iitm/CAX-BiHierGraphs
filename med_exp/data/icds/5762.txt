Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Cervical Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screw, from a cervical vertebral joint through an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the joint. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, stiffness, and limited mobility caused by the fixation device, which was initially implanted to stabilize the spine following injury or surgery. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully exposes the cervical vertebral joint through the incision and removes the internal fixation device, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding soft tissues and structures. After the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is usually advised to undergo physical therapy to regain strength and range of motion in the neck.
