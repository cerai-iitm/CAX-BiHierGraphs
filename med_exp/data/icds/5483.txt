Reposition Right Fibula with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right fibula bone to its normal anatomical alignment, followed by the insertion of an intramedullary internal fixation device through an open incision. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the fibula and promote healing by holding the bone in place with the internal fixation device. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area and directly access the fibula, enabling precise repositioning and fixation. The intramedullary internal fixation device is designed to provide long-term stability to the fibula, facilitating optimal recovery and minimizing the risk of future complications.
