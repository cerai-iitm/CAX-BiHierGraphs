Dilation of Abdominal Aorta with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a percutaneous approach to dilate the abdominal aorta, which is narrowed or blocked, using an intraluminal device. Through a small incision in the groin, a catheter is inserted and guided to the affected area, where a balloon or stent is deployed to widen the aorta. This helps to improve blood flow and relieve symptoms such as abdominal pain, cramping, and fatigue. The procedure is often performed under local anesthesia and can be completed in about an hour, with patients typically able to return to their normal activities within a few days.
