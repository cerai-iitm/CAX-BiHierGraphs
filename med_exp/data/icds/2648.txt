Destruction of Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope to reach the stomach through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the physician to visually examine the stomach lining and identify areas of diseased or damaged tissue. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the destruction of the affected tissue, using various methods such as electrocautery, cryotherapy, or laser ablation. The destruction of stomach tissue is typically done to treat conditions such as bleeding ulcers, cancer, or precancerous lesions. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgical methods.
