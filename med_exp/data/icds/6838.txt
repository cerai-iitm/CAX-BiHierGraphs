Control Bleeding in Respiratory Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves stopping bleeding in the respiratory tract, which includes the lungs, trachea, bronchi, and other airways. The procedure can be performed through a natural opening, such as the mouth or nose, or through an artificial opening, created surgically or due to a traumatic injury. The goal of the procedure is to identify and control the source of bleeding, which can be caused by trauma, tumors, or other conditions, and restore normal breathing function. The procedure may involve suturing, cauterization, or other techniques to achieve hemostasis and maintain airway patency.
