Removal of Other Device on Abdominal Wall
This procedure involves the surgical removal of a device, other than a graft or implant, that has been previously placed on the abdominal wall. The device may be causing complications, such as infection, pain, or discomfort, or may be no longer necessary. The removal process typically involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the device, followed by careful dissection to free the device from surrounding tissue. The device is then carefully extracted, and the incision site is closed to promote healing.
