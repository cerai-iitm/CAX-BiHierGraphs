Destruction of Left Atrium, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the heart, specifically the left atrium. The goal of the procedure is to destroy or remove a portion of the left atrium, which is the upper chamber of the heart that receives oxygenated blood from the lungs. This is typically done to treat conditions such as atrial fibrillation, which is a type of irregular heartbeat, or to remove a tumor or lesion that has developed in the left atrium. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the heart and surgical site, ensuring precise destruction or removal of the targeted area.
