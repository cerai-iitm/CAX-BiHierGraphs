Occlusion of Gastric Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of an intraluminal device to occlude the gastric vein, a blood vessel that carries blood from the stomach to the liver. The device is positioned within the vein to block blood flow, which can be used to treat conditions such as gastric varices or bleeding. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate device placement. Once the device is in place, it remains permanently to provide long-term occlusion of the gastric vein.
