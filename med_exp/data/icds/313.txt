Bypass Coronary Artery, Three Arteries from Right Internal Mammary, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves redirecting blood flow around three blocked or narrowed coronary arteries to improve heart function and alleviate symptoms of coronary artery disease. The operation utilizes the right internal mammary artery as the graft source, which is harvested from the patient's own body. The grafts are then attached to the blocked arteries, creating a detour for blood to flow around the blockages, restoring oxygen-rich blood to the heart. The open approach requires a sternotomy, a surgical incision in the chest, to access the heart and coronary arteries, allowing the surgeon to perform the complex bypass procedure.
