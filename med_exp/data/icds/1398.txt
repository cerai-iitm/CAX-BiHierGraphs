Dilation of Left Innominate Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a catheter through the skin to access the left innominate vein, a major blood vessel that drains blood from the left side of the head and neck. The catheter is guided to the narrowed or blocked segment of the vein using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. Once in place, a balloon or other dilation device is inflated to widen the vein, restoring blood flow and relieving symptoms such as swelling, pain, or difficulty breathing. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and typically takes about 30-60 minutes to complete.
