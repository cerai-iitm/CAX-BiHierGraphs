Removal of Neurostimulator Lead from Spinal Cord, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a neurostimulator lead from the spinal cord through an open approach, which requires a skin incision to access the lead. The lead is carefully dissected and freed from surrounding tissue and bone, allowing for its safe removal from the spinal cord. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize patient discomfort. The goal of this procedure is to relieve patients from the complications or side effects associated with the implanted lead.
