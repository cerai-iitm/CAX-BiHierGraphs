Reposition Left Radius, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of the left radius bone through an open approach, which requires a skin incision to access the affected area. The orthopedic surgeon carefully manipulates the bone to restore its normal anatomical position, ensuring proper alignment and function of the forearm. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the bone and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately reposition the radius and address any associated soft tissue damage. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
