Dilation of Left Hepatic Duct with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a thin, flexible tube through the skin and into the liver to reach the left hepatic duct. A specialized device is then used to gently dilate or widen the duct, allowing for the relief of any blockages or narrowing that may be impeding bile flow. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the device. By opening up the duct, this procedure can help restore normal bile drainage and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and jaundice.
