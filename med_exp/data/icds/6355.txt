Excision of Bladder Neck, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this minimally invasive procedure, a flexible endoscope is inserted through the urethra to access the bladder neck. Utilizing specialized instruments and guided by visual observation, the surgeon carefully excises the affected tissue or lesion at the bladder neck. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize discomfort. The excision of the bladder neck tissue is achieved through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for a more precise and controlled removal of the targeted area.
