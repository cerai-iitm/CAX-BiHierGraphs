Extraction of Back Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the back to remove a lesion, tumor, or other abnormal growth. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area, enabling precise removal of the targeted tissue. The extraction is typically performed under local anesthesia, and the wound is closed with sutures or staples to promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to remove the unwanted skin growth, alleviate symptoms, and improve the patient's cosmetic appearance.
