Repair Lower Back, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves a direct incision into the lower back region to access and repair damaged or diseased spinal structures, such as herniated discs, facet joints, or vertebrae. The external approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect and manipulate the tissues, enabling a more comprehensive repair. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or weakness in the legs, and to restore normal spinal function and stability. The surgical site is carefully closed and dressed to promote healing and minimize the risk of complications.
