Restriction of Middle Colic Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small intraluminal device through the skin to restrict blood flow in the middle colic artery, a branch of the superior mesenteric artery that supplies blood to the large intestine. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the device. The intraluminal device, such as a stent or coil, is designed to reduce blood flow to the affected area, helping to control bleeding or manage other vascular conditions. This percutaneous approach minimizes tissue damage and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical techniques.
