Dilation of Duodenum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end through the mouth or an artificial opening to reach the duodenum, the first part of the small intestine. Once in place, a specialized instrument is used to gently dilate or stretch the duodenum to relieve any blockages or narrowings that may be obstructing the passage of food and fluids. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and may require fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the dilation device. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal digestive function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
