Removal of Drainage Device from Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove a drainage device from the peritoneal cavity, a fluid-filled space in the abdominal area. The percutaneous approach involves making a small incision in the skin to access the peritoneal cavity, allowing for the removal of the drainage device. This procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure precise placement and removal of the device. The removal of the drainage device helps to alleviate symptoms and promote healing in the peritoneal cavity.
