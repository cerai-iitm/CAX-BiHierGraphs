Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves exposing the lumbar vertebral joint through an open incision to remove an internal fixation device, such as a rod, plate, or screw, that was previously implanted to stabilize the spine. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, restore mobility, and improve spinal alignment by removing the device that is no longer needed or has become problematic. The removal process involves carefully dissecting the tissue around the device, disconnecting it from the bone, and extracting it from the body.
