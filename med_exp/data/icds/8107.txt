Fluoroscopy of Pelvic Arteries using Other Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the pelvic arteries, which are blood vessels that supply oxygenated blood to the pelvic region. A special contrast agent, other than routine iodine-based contrast, is injected into the arteries to enhance the visibility of the blood vessels. Under real-time X-ray guidance, the movement and flow of the contrast agent are observed to detect any blockages, narrowing, or abnormalities in the pelvic arteries. The procedure is essential for diagnosing and treating various conditions, including peripheral artery disease, aneurysms, and stenosis.
