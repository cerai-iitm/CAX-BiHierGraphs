Insertion of Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device into Right Tibia, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an intramedullary internal fixation device is inserted into the right tibia through an open approach, allowing for direct visualization of the bone and surrounding tissue. The device is designed to provide stability and support to the tibia, promoting healing and alignment of the bone. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the tibia, and the procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and mobility to the affected leg, reducing the risk of further injury or complication.
