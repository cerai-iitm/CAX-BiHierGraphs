Excision of Greater Omentum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the greater omentum, a fold of peritoneum that attaches to the stomach, through small incisions using an endoscope. The procedure is performed under image guidance to ensure precise excision of the targeted tissue. The endoscope is inserted through the abdominal wall, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and dissect the greater omentum from surrounding tissues. The excised tissue is then removed through the same small incision, resulting in less tissue trauma and promoting faster recovery.
