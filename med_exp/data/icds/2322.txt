Inspection of Tracheobronchial Tree, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually examine the tracheobronchial tree, which includes the trachea and bronchi, through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, is inserted through the mouth or nose, or through a tracheostomy tube, allowing the healthcare provider to directly observe the airways. This inspection enables the identification of any abnormalities, such as tumors, bleeding, or blockages, and helps in diagnosing respiratory conditions, including chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and lung cancer. The procedure is often performed under local anesthesia and sedation to minimize discomfort and promote relaxation.
