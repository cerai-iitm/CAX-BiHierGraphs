Dilation of Left Common Iliac Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a drug-eluting stent through a small incision in the skin to dilate the left common iliac artery, which is narrowed or blocked. The stent is designed to release medication slowly over time to help prevent the artery from narrowing again. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the stent is advanced through a catheter to the blocked area, where it is expanded to widen the artery, restoring blood flow to the legs. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can help relieve symptoms of peripheral artery disease, such as leg pain, weakness, or cramping.
