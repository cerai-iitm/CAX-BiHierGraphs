Removal of Infusion Device from Peritoneal Cavity, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the abdominal wall to access the peritoneal cavity, allowing the surgeon to locate and carefully remove the infusion device, typically a catheter or port, that has been implanted for administering medication or fluids. The device is gently grasped and withdrawn from the cavity, and the surrounding tissue is inspected for any signs of infection or damage. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing. This procedure is typically performed under general or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure a safe and effective outcome.
