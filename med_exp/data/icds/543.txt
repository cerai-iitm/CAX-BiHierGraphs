Repair Aortic Valve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the chest to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the aortic valve, which regulates blood flow from the heart to the rest of the body. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest bone is divided to expose the heart, and the patient is connected to a heart-lung machine to maintain circulation during the operation. Once the valve is exposed, the surgeon can repair or replace the valve with a bioprosthetic or mechanical valve, depending on the individual case. After the repair, the incision is closed, and the patient is taken off the heart-lung machine, requiring postoperative care to ensure a smooth recovery.
