Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Abdominal Wall, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a synthetic substitute, such as a mesh implant, from the abdominal wall through an open approach. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the synthetic material. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety. The removal of the synthetic substitute is often necessary to treat complications such as infection, adhesion, or hernia recurrence resulting from the original implant.
