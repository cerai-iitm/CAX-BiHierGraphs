Drainage of Right Pleural Cavity, Open Approach
The procedure involves making a surgical incision in the right side of the chest to access the pleural cavity, a space between the lungs and chest wall. The goal is to drain accumulated fluid or air from the pleural cavity to relieve pressure and promote healthy lung expansion. During the open approach, the surgeon visually examines the cavity to identify the source of the problem and remove the causative factor, if possible. The drainage process is typically accomplished using suction or gravity-assisted devices to remove the excess fluid or air, followed by closure of the incision site.
