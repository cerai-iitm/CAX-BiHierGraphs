Drainage of Abdominal Wall with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous drainage of the abdominal wall involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to remove infected or excess fluid that has accumulated in the abdominal wall. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or CT scan, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device. The procedure is usually done to treat conditions such as abscesses, hematomas, or seromas that have developed in the abdominal wall. The goal of the procedure is to provide effective drainage and promote healing of the affected area.
