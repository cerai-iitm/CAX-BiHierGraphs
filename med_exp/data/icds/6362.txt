Extirpation of Matter from Right Kidney, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using imaging guidance to access the right kidney through the skin, allowing for the removal of unwanted material or debris from the kidney. A small incision is made in the back or flank, and a specialized instrument is inserted through the incision to reach the kidney. Under fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance, the instrument is used to extract the unwanted material, which can include stones, blood clots, or other obstructions. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, and is often used to treat conditions such as kidney stones or hematuria.
