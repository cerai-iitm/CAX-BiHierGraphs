Fluoroscopy of Single Coronary Artery Bypass Graft using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize a single coronary artery bypass graft. During the procedure, a special type of contrast medium, other than the standard iodinated or gadolinium-based agents, is injected into the graft to enhance its visibility under X-ray. The fluoroscope generates continuous X-ray images, which are displayed on a monitor, allowing the healthcare provider to assess the graft's patency, stenosis, or other abnormalities. The precise visualization of the graft's anatomy and blood flow enables accurate diagnosis and treatment planning for cardiac conditions.
