Destruction of Left Lower Lobe Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the destruction of the left lower lobe bronchus through a natural or artificial opening, utilizing specialized instruments and visualization technology to access the targeted area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort and cooperation. Once access is gained, the bronchus is destroyed using energy sources such as electrocautery, laser, or cryotherapy to remove diseased or damaged tissue. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as lung cancer, bronchiectasis, or abscesses that affect the left lower lobe bronchus.
