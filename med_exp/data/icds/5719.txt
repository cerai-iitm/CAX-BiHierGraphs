Fusion of Thoracolumbar Vertebral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing the thoracolumbar vertebral joint using an interbody fusion device through an anterior approach, which requires an open incision to access the anterior column of the spine. The goal of the procedure is to stabilize the spine and relieve symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or tingling in the legs. During the procedure, the surgeon will remove any affected intervertebral discs or bone fragments and replace them with the interbody fusion device, which helps to maintain the natural spacing of the vertebrae. The fusion process allows the vertebrae to grow together, creating a solid bond that provides long-term stability and support.
