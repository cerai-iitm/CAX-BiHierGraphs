Fluoroscopy of Dialysis Shunt/Fistula using Other Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure uses fluoroscopy to visualize the dialysis shunt or fistula, which is a surgically created connection between an artery and a vein to facilitate hemodialysis. Contrast material, other than iodine-based or gadolinium-based agents, is injected into the shunt or fistula to enhance visibility. Real-time X-ray imaging is then used to evaluate the flow of contrast through the shunt or fistula, allowing clinicians to assess its patency and detect any potential problems. The procedure helps identify issues such as stenosis, thrombosis, or aneurysm formation, enabling timely interventions to maintain the functionality of the dialysis access.
