Excision of Left Ureter, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the left ureter through a small incision in the skin using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The procedure is performed percutaneously, meaning through the skin, allowing for reduced recovery time and less scarring. The endoscope guides the surgeon to the affected area, enabling precise excision of the diseased or damaged portion of the ureter. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize discomfort and ensure optimal outcomes.
