Dilation of Cervix with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device into the cervical canal through the natural or artificial opening to gently stretch and widen the cervix, allowing for safe passage of medical instruments or fetal passage during childbirth. The device is carefully placed within the cervical canal to achieve adequate dilation, which may be necessary for various gynecological procedures or obstetric deliveries. The objective is to minimize trauma to the cervix and surrounding tissues while ensuring sufficient dilation for a successful medical intervention or birth. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and requires close monitoring to prevent any complications or adverse effects.
