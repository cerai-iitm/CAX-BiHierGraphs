Insertion of Infusion Device into Upper Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A percutaneous procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into a vein located in the upper extremity, typically the arm or hand. This involves making a small incision in the skin and guiding the device through the vein under fluoroscopic or ultrasound guidance. The infusion device is designed to deliver medications, nutrients, or other fluids directly into the bloodstream, and its placement in the upper vein allows for easy access and management. Once in place, the device is secured and the incision site is closed, enabling patients to receive necessary treatments while minimizing discomfort and reducing the risk of complications.
