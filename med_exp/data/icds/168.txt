Removal of Drainage Device from Cerebral Ventricle, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a drainage device from the cerebral ventricle, which is a fluid-filled space within the brain. The external approach is used to access the ventricle, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove the device without causing further damage to the surrounding brain tissue. The drainage device is typically inserted to relieve pressure on the brain caused by hydrocephalus or other conditions, and its removal is often performed when the condition has stabilized or the device is no longer necessary. The procedure requires great precision and care to avoid complications and ensure a successful outcome.
