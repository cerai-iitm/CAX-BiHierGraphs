Supplement Heart with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Percutaneous endoscopic approach is utilized to access the heart, which involves inserting an endoscope through the skin and tissue to reach the heart. Autologous tissue substitute is then introduced through the endoscope to supplement the heart, providing additional support and structure. This minimally invasive procedure reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open-heart surgery. The autologous tissue substitute is derived from the patient's own body, eliminating the risk of rejection or reaction to foreign materials.
