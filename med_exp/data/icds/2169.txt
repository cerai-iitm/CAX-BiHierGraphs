Excision of Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a part of the trachea through an endoscopic approach, which utilizes a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end to visualize the internal structures of the trachea. The excision is performed via a natural or artificial opening, allowing for minimally invasive access to the tracheal tissue. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to collect tissue samples for further examination and to diagnose conditions affecting the trachea, such as tumors, inflammation, or abnormalities. The endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical methods.
