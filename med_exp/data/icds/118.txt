Extirpation of Matter from Cerebellum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the cerebellum, a region at the base of the brain, to remove unwanted matter such as a tumor, abscess, or foreign body. The goal of the extirpation is to alleviate symptoms and restore normal brain function by removing the problematic material. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the cerebellum and surrounding structures, enabling precise dissection and removal of the targeted material. The procedure typically requires a craniotomy, which involves temporarily removing a portion of the skull to access the cerebellum, and may involve the use of neurosurgical instruments and advanced imaging technologies.
