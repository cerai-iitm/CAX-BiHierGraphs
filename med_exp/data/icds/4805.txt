Excision of Skull, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the skull, followed by the removal of a portion of the skull bone. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to accurately excise the targeted section of the skull. The excised bone may be sent for further examination or diagnosis, and the surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures or staples. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary to treat conditions such as skull fractures, tumors, or infections.
