Repair Female Perineum, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the restoration of the female perineum, a sensitive area between the vagina and anus, through an external approach. The goal is to repair any damage, lacerations, or defects in the perineal muscles, skin, or other tissues. The external method allows surgeons to access the affected area directly, enabling them to meticulously repair and reconstruct the perineum, promoting healing and restoration of normal function. The procedure often follows childbirth or other traumatic injuries to the perineal region.
