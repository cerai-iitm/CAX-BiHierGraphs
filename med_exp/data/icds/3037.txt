Release Stomach, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin and into the stomach to release scar tissue or adhesions that may be causing blockages or obstruction. The endoscope, equipped with specialized instruments, allows the physician to visualize the stomach and surrounding tissue, and then carefully dissect and release the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal stomach function and alleviate symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain.
