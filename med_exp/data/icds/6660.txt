Reposition Vagina, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visually guide the repositioning of the vagina through small incisions in the skin. The endoscope provides a clear view of the internal anatomy, allowing the surgeon to accurately manipulate the vaginal tissues into their normal position. This approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort during the procedure.
