Excision of Mesentery, Open Approach
Surgical removal of the mesentery, a fold of membranous tissue attaching the small intestine to the back of the abdominal wall, is performed through an open approach, involving a large incision in the abdomen to access the affected area. This procedure is typically done to treat conditions such as traumatic injury, cancer, or inflammatory diseases affecting the mesentery. The surgeon carefully dissects and removes the diseased or damaged portion of the mesentery, taking care to preserve surrounding vital structures and blood vessels. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal anatomy and function, alleviate symptoms, and improve overall health outcomes.
