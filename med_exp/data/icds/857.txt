Repair Intracranial Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skull to access the affected intracranial artery, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the damaged vessel directly. The open approach enables the surgeon to correct stenosis, aneurysms, or other defects in the artery, restoring blood flow and reducing the risk of stroke or rupture. The procedure requires a craniotomy, where a section of the skull is temporarily removed to provide access to the artery, and may involve the use of stents, grafts, or other materials to reinforce the artery. Once the repair is complete, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any complications.
