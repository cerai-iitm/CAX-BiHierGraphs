Introduction of Regional Anesthetic into Epidural Space, Percutaneous Approach
During this procedure, a regional anesthetic is administered into the epidural space, a region outside the sac that surrounds the spinal cord, through a percutaneous approach, which means the anesthetic is injected through the skin. The epidural space is targeted to achieve regional anesthesia, which numbs a larger area of the body, such as the abdomen, chest, or legs. The percutaneous approach involves inserting a needle through the skin and into the epidural space, allowing the anesthetic to be introduced directly into the target area. This procedure is typically performed to provide pain management during surgical procedures, labor, or for chronic pain relief.
