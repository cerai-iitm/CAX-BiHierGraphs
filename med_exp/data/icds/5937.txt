Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more lumbar vertebral joints using an autologous tissue substitute, which is taken from the patient's own body. The posterior approach is employed, where the surgeon accesses the spine through an incision in the back, and the posterior column is stabilized through an open approach. The autologous tissue substitute is used to facilitate bone growth and fusion between the affected vertebrae, providing long-term stability and relief from back pain. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the spine, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the tissue substitute and achieve a successful fusion.
