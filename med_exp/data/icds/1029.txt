Dilation of Left Common Iliac Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the groin area to access the left common iliac artery, which is narrowed or blocked, and using a specialized device to widen the artery. A drug-eluting intraluminal device, also known as a stent, is then placed inside the artery to keep it open and prevent future blockages. The stent is coated with medication that helps prevent the growth of scar tissue, reducing the likelihood of restenosis. Once the device is in place, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications.
