Excision of Left Abdomen Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left abdomen to access the underlying muscle tissue. The damaged or diseased muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate symptoms such as pain, weakness or limited mobility. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the affected area and precisely remove the targeted muscle tissue. Following the excision, the incision site is closed, and the patient is monitored for a smooth recovery.
