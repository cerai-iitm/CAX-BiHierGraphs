Resection of Uterus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening With Percutaneous Endoscopic Assistance
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the uterus through a natural or artificial opening, assisted by percutaneous endoscopic techniques, which allow for visualization of the internal organs and tissues through small incisions. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and involves the use of specialized instruments and equipment to carefully dissect and remove the uterus. The endoscopic assistance enables the surgeon to precisely visualize the anatomy and minimize tissue trauma, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The resultant scar tissue is usually minimal, and the procedure is often preferred for its reduced morbidity and shorter hospital stay compared to traditional open surgical methods.
