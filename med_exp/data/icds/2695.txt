Dilation of Jejunum, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small, flexible tube through the skin to reach the jejunum, a section of the small intestine. Once in place, a specialized balloon or dilator is guided through the tube to gently stretch and widen the narrowed or blocked area of the jejunum. This helps to relieve any blockages or strictures that may be obstructing food passage and causing symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and promoting faster healing.
