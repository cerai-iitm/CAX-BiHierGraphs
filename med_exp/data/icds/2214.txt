Excision of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the right lower lung lobe through a percutaneous approach, which means a minimally invasive technique is used, making a small incision in the skin to access the lung tissue. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to obtain tissue samples or to definitively diagnose lung disease or cancer, allowing for precise identification and staging of the condition. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgery. The excision of the lobe is typically performed under general anesthesia, and post-procedure care involves close monitoring for any potential complications or bleeding.
