Excision of Right Atrium, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the right atrium, a chamber of the heart that receives oxygen-depleted blood from the body. The procedure requires a sternotomy, a vertical incision in the sternum, to expose the heart and its structures. The surgeon then identifies the right atrium and carefully excises the targeted tissue or lesion, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding heart structures. Once the excision is complete, the incision is closed, and the chest is closed, usually with wires and sutures.
