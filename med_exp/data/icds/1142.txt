Excision of Right Femoral Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the right femoral artery, which is a major blood vessel located in the groin area. The artery is then carefully dissected and removed, either partially or entirely, depending on the indication for the procedure. The excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as atherosclerosis, aneurysms, or traumatic injuries that affect blood flow to the leg. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the artery and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to meticulously repair or replace the damaged section.
