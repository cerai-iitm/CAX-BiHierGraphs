Monitoring of Intracranial Pressure, Percutaneous Approach
This invasive procedure involves the insertion of a device through the skin to monitor the pressure inside the skull, providing real-time information to healthcare professionals to assess and manage conditions such as traumatic brain injury, cerebral edema, or stroke. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive insertion of the monitoring device, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The monitoring device is typically placed in the intensive care unit (ICU) setting, where continuous observation and prompt intervention are crucial. By continuously tracking intracranial pressure, healthcare providers can make informed decisions to optimize patient care and improve outcomes.
