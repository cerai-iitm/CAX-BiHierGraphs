Repair Right Diaphragm, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the repair of the right diaphragm, a dome-shaped muscle essential for breathing, through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and involves making small incisions in the abdomen to access the diaphragm. Using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, the surgeon visualizes the diaphragm and uses specialized instruments to repair any defects or tears. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal diaphragmatic function, alleviating symptoms such as shortness of breath, chest pain, and abdominal discomfort.
