Insertion of Short-term External Heart Assist System into Heart, Intraoperative, Percutaneous Approach
During cardiac surgery, a short-term external heart assist system is inserted into the heart through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach, providing temporary circulatory support to the patient. This system is implanted in the operating room, typically during complex cardiac procedures, to augment cardiac function and maintain blood flow. The device is inserted through a small incision in the skin, eliminating the need for open-chest surgery. The goal of this intervention is to stabilize the patient's hemodynamic status and facilitate a smooth recovery.
