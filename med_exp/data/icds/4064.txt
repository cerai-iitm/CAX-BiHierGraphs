Excision of Left Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower arm to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The affected tissue and fascia are then carefully excised, or cut out, to remove any damaged or diseased tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely target the area in need of repair. Following excision, the wound is closed, and the arm is typically immobilized to promote healing and minimize the risk of complication.
