Fusion of 2 or more Lumbar Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves fusing two or more lumbar vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device, which is inserted through an anterior (front) approach to the spine. The anterior column of the spine is accessed through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the spine and perform the fusion. During the procedure, the interbody fusion device is placed between the affected vertebrae to promote bone growth and stabilize the spine. Over time, the device facilitates the fusion of the vertebrae, alleviating pain and restoring spinal stability.
