Fusion of Right Sacroiliac Joint with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves opening the skin and soft tissues to access the right sacroiliac joint, with the goal of fusing the joint to stabilize and alleviate chronic pain and discomfort. The fusion process is facilitated by the use of a nonautologous tissue substitute, such as a bone graft or synthetic material, which is implanted to promote bone growth and fusion. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely place the tissue substitute and achieve optimal results. By fusing the joint, the procedure aims to reduce inflammation, improve joint stability, and enhance the patient's overall quality of life.
