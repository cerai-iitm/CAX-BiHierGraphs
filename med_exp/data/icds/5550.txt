Supplement Right Tibia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the supplementation of the right tibia, also known as the shinbone, with a tissue substitute derived from the patient's own body. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to implant the autologous tissue substitute to strengthen and support the weakened bone. The goal of this procedure is to restore structural integrity and alleviate pain or instability caused by bone defects or trauma. By using the patient's own tissue, the risk of rejection or adverse reaction is minimized, promoting a more effective and natural healing process.
