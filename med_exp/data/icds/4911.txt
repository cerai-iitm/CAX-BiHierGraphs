Reposition Left Mandible with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the repositioning of the left mandible to its natural anatomical position using an internal fixation device. The internal fixation device is inserted through a small skin incision, allowing for minimal tissue disruption and promoting faster healing. Once in place, the device provides stability and support to the mandible, helping to restore proper alignment and function. The percutaneous approach allows for a less invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a smoother recovery.
