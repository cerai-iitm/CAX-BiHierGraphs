Excision of Common Bile Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the common bile duct through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the physician to remove obstructive lesions or stones that may be blocking the flow of bile. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the physician to excise the affected portion of the bile duct, restoring normal bile flow and alleviating symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and nausea. The diagnostic nature of this procedure allows the physician to concurrently examine the bile duct and identify any abnormalities or pathologies. The use of endoscopic technology minimizes trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster recovery times compared to traditional open surgical methods.
