Destruction of Right Atrium, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the deliberate elimination of damaged or diseased tissue in the right atrium, which is the upper chamber of the heart that receives oxygen-depleted blood from the body. The open approach utilized in this procedure allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and accurately target the affected region. During the procedure, an incision is made in the chest, allowing the surgeon to access the heart and perform the necessary destruction of the affected tissue in the right atrium. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal cardiac function and alleviate symptoms associated with atrial disease or damage.
