Inspection of Mediastinum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to access the mediastinum, a region in the thoracic cavity that contains vital structures such as the heart, trachea, and esophagus. The purpose of the inspection is to visually examine the mediastinum for any abnormal growths, lesions, or other pathologies. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully inspect the mediastinal structures and tissues, taking note of any abnormalities or potential areas of concern. The inspection may be performed to diagnose conditions such as mediastinal tumors, lymph node enlargement, or inflammation, and to guide further treatment or interventions.
