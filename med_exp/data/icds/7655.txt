Introduction of Other Thrombolytic into Coronary Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a specialized catheter through the skin and into a coronary artery to deliver thrombolytic medication directly to the site of a blood clot. The medication dissolves the clot, helping to restore blood flow to the heart and alleviate symptoms of a heart attack or acute coronary syndrome. Fluoroscopic guidance is typically used to ensure accurate placement of the catheter and monitoring of the procedure. The procedure is usually performed in a cardiac catheterization laboratory by a trained interventional cardiologist.
