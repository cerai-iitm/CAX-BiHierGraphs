Removal of Short-term External Heart Assist System from Heart, Open Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a short-term external heart assist system that provides mechanical circulatory support to the heart. The system is typically implanted via an open-chest approach and is used to support the heart's pumping function, often in emergency situations or during cardiac surgery. The removal procedure is typically performed in an operating room under general anesthesia, and involves careful disconnection and extraction of the device from the heart. Following removal, the incision site is closed and the heart is monitored for any signs of instability or complications.
