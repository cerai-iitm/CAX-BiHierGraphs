Fusion of Right Tarsal Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves fusing the right tarsal joint, a complex joint in the midfoot, using an autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to prepare the joint surfaces and insert the tissue substitute to facilitate fusion. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain and instability in the right foot and ankle caused by conditions such as arthritis, fracture, or deformity. By fusing the joint, the surgeon can promote long-term stability and prevent further degeneration of the joint.
