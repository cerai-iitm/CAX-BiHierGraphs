Drainage of Right Upper Leg Muscle, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the right upper leg to access the affected muscle. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the muscle and identify the area of concern. A drainage device or system is then inserted to remove excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may be accumulating in the muscle, promoting healing and reducing the risk of infection. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure a successful outcome.
