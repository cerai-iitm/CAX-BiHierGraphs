Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Internal Jugular Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves inserting a small medical device, such as a central venous catheter or port, into the right internal jugular vein through a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The internal jugular vein is located in the neck, and the device is inserted through a small incision in the skin. The infusion device is placed under the skin, with the catheter tip positioned in the internal jugular vein, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or nutrients directly into the bloodstream. This procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the device.
