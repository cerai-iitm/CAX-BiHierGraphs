Repair Cecum, Open Approach
A surgical operation is performed to repair the cecum, a pouch-like structure at the beginning of the large intestine, through an open approach, which involves making a larger incision in the abdomen to access the damaged area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. During the operation, the surgeon meticulously repairs the cecum, which may be damaged due to various reasons such as injury, inflammation, or obstruction. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bowel function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting.
