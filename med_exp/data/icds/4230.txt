Removal of Tunneled Vascular Access Device from Upper Extremity Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous removal of a tunneled vascular access device from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the upper extremity. The physician makes a small incision in the skin and carefully dissects the device from the surrounding tissue, taking care to avoid damaging nearby blood vessels or nerves. The device is then gently pulled out through the incision, and the site is closed with sutures or staples. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be done on an outpatient basis.
