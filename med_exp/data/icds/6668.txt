Resection of Left Ovary, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the left ovary, which is then carefully removed from the surrounding tissue and blood vessels. The ovary is then dissected and separated from its attachments, and any additional necessary steps are taken to control bleeding and ensure complete removal. The incision is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, and the patient is taken to recovery for post-operative care. The removal of the left ovary may be performed to treat conditions such as ovarian cancer, ovarian cysts, or endometriosis.
