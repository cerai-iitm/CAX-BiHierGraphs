Pheresis of Plasma, Single
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This invasive, therapeutic procedure involves the separation of plasma from other blood components using a specialized device. During the process, whole blood is drawn into a pheresis machine, which separates the plasma from other blood components, such as red and white blood cells. The plasma is then retained, while the remaining blood components are returned to the patient's bloodstream. The collected plasma can be used for various therapeutic or diagnostic purposes.
