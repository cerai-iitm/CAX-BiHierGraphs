Ultrasonography of Pericardium, Transesophageal
This non-invasive imaging procedure uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the pericardium, the sac that surrounds the heart, from inside the esophagus. The transesophageal approach allows for more detailed images of the pericardium than traditional ultrasound methods, particularly in patients with lung disease or obesity. During the procedure, a specialized probe called a transesophageal transducer is inserted through the mouth and guided down the esophagus to the area behind the heart. The resulting images help healthcare professionals diagnose and monitor pericardial conditions such as pericarditis, pericardial effusion, and constrictive pericarditis.
