Destruction of Conduction Mechanism, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the intentional destruction of the conduction mechanism, typically referring to the nerves or pathways responsible for transmitting pain or aberrant electrical signals. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the targeted area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and directly address the affected conduction mechanism. The goal of this procedure is to disrupt or eliminate the faulty conduction pathways, thereby alleviating symptoms such as chronic pain, tremors, or abnormal heartbeat rhythms. By destroying the problematic conduction mechanism, the procedure aims to restore normal functioning and alleviate symptoms.
