Repair Right Radial Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the right radial artery, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair any damage or defects in the blood vessel. The open approach enables direct visualization of the artery, allowing for precise repair of any tears, aneurysms, or other abnormalities. The surgeon carefully sutures or uses grafts to restore the artery's integrity, ensuring proper blood flow to the hand and fingers. Once the repair is complete, the incision is closed, and the site is inspected for any bleeding or other complications.
