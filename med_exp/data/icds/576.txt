Replacement of Thoracic Aorta, Ascending/Arch with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing a diseased or damaged portion of the ascending and/or arch sections of the thoracic aorta with a synthetic graft using an open approach. The procedure requires a sternotomy, a surgical incision in the chest, to access the affected area. The damaged section of the aorta is removed and a synthetic substitute is implanted, re-establishing blood flow and reducing the risk of rupture or further damage. The procedure is typically performed under cardiopulmonary bypass, allowing the surgeon to work on a still heart and ensuring a safer and more precise operation.
