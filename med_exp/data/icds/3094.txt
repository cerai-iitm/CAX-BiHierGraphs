Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Mesentery, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an open incision to access the mesentery, a fold of membranous tissue that attaches the small intestine to the back of the abdominal wall. Through this incision, the surgeon carefully removes a synthetic substitute, such as a mesh or a prosthesis, that was previously implanted in the mesentery for various reasons including hernia repair or tissue reconstruction. The removal of the synthetic substitute may be necessary due to complications, infections, or rejection of the material by the body. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the area and remove the substitute with precision, reducing the risk of damage to surrounding tissues.
