Extirpation of Matter from Left Femoral Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left femoral artery to remove unwanted material, such as a blood clot or plaque, that may be obstructing blood flow. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected artery, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and remove the material. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal blood flow to the lower extremities, alleviate symptoms such as pain or numbness, and reduce the risk of further complications. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is usually performed in a hospital setting by a vascular surgeon or a qualified healthcare professional.
