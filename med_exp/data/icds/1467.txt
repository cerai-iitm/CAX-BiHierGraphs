Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Internal Jugular Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small infusion device into the left internal jugular vein, which is located in the neck, using a percutaneous approach, where a small puncture is made in the skin. The infusion device is carefully guided through the vein under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement. Once in place, the device allows for the administration of medications, fluids, or other therapies directly into the bloodstream. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can be completed in about 30-60 minutes.
