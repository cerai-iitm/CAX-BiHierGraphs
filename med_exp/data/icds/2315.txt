Insertion of Endotracheal Airway into Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this minimally invasive procedure, an endoscope is utilized to visually guide the insertion of an endotracheal tube through the mouth or nose and into the trachea, ensuring proper placement and ventilation. The endoscope provides clear visualization of the airway, allowing the healthcare professional to navigate complex anatomy and avoid potential complications. The tube is carefully advanced through the vocal cords and into the trachea, secured in place to establish a secure airway. This approach enables precise control and reduces the risk of trauma to surrounding tissues during intubation.
