Excision of Right Upper Arm Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right upper arm to remove a specific area of skin for diagnostic purposes. The external approach is used to access the affected skin area, allowing the surgeon to excise the targeted skin section. The excised skin tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and analysis to determine the underlying cause of any abnormalities or lesions. The goal of this procedure is to diagnose skin conditions or diseases, such as skin cancers, and guide subsequent treatment decisions.
