Repair Abdominal Wall, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves an external approach to repair defects or weaknesses in the abdominal wall, which can be caused by hernias, trauma, or previous surgical incisions. The goal of the procedure is to restore the integrity of the abdominal wall, providing support and stability to the abdominal organs and preventing potential complications. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the abdomen to access the affected area and uses sutures, mesh, or other materials to reinforce the abdominal wall. The procedure requires careful planning and execution to minimize risks and ensure optimal outcomes.
