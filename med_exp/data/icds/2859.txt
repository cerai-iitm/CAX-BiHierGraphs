Excision of Left Large Intestine, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the left large intestine through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the inside of the intestine and guide the excision. The endoscope allows for a minimally invasive approach, reducing the need for open surgery and promoting faster recovery. The diagnostic nature of this procedure enables healthcare providers to examine the removed tissue for signs of disease or abnormalities. This procedure is typically performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may involve a colonoscope or sigmoidoscope depending on the location of the excision.
