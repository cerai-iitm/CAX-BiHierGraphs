Change Drainage Device in Peritoneal Cavity, External Approach
Here is the description:

This procedure involves the replacement of a peritoneal drainage device, a medical instrument used to drain excess fluid or toxins from the peritoneal cavity, which is the space between the lining of the abdominal wall and the organs in the abdomen. The external approach is used to access the peritoneal cavity, allowing the healthcare provider to remove the existing drainage device and insert a new one. This procedure is typically performed under sterile conditions to minimize the risk of infection. The new drainage device is carefully positioned to ensure proper drainage and to prevent complications such as leakage or blockage.
