Restriction of Stomach with Extraluminal Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of an extraluminal device through the skin and endoscope to restrict the stomach, reducing its capacity and promoting weight loss. The device is introduced through a small incision in the abdominal wall and guided through the stomach with the aid of an endoscope, allowing for precise placement. Once in position, the device constricts the stomach, limiting food intake and helping patients to achieve significant weight loss. This percutaneous endoscopic approach offers a less invasive alternative to traditional open surgery, resulting in reduced recovery time and fewer complications.
