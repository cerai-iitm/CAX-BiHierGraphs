Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of lumbar vertebral joints using an autologous tissue substitute, which is harvested from the patient's own body. The posterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the affected area through an incision in the back. The anterior column of the spine is stabilized and fused using the substitute tissue, promoting bone growth and alleviating pain and discomfort. Through an open approach, the surgeon directly visualizes the spine to ensure precise and effective fusion.
