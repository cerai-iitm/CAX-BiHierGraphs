Resection of Bilateral Ovaries, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the removal of both ovaries through a natural or artificial opening in the body, often performed to treat conditions such as ovarian cancer, cysts, or endometriosis. During the procedure, the surgeon will access the ovaries through a small incision, either laparoscopically or through an open surgical approach, and carefully dissect and remove the ovaries from the surrounding tissue. The goal of the procedure is to remove the diseased or damaged ovarian tissue, which can help to alleviate symptoms and improve overall health. Depending on the individual case, this procedure may be performed as an open surgery or a minimally invasive laparoscopic surgery.
