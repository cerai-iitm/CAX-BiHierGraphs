Excision of Left Hand Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the left hand to access the underlying muscle tissue. The affected muscle is then carefully identified and removed through the open incision, allowing for direct visualization of the area. The excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as tumors, infections, or traumatic injuries that affect the muscle function. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal muscle function and promote optimal healing in the left hand.
