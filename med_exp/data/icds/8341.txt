Range of Motion and Joint Integrity Assessment of Musculoskeletal System - Lower Back / Lower Extremity
This assessment involves a comprehensive evaluation of the musculoskeletal system, focusing on the lower back and lower extremity, to determine range of motion and joint integrity. The procedure aims to identify any limitations or abnormalities in movement, flexibility, and joint stability, which can help diagnose musculoskeletal disorders or injuries. A thorough examination of the joints, muscles, and tendons is performed to assess strength, flexibility, and mobility, as well as detect any signs of pain or inflammation. By evaluating the overall function and mobility of the lower back and lower extremity, healthcare professionals can develop effective treatment plans to improve range of motion, reduce pain, and enhance overall musculoskeletal function.
