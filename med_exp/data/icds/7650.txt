Introduction of Other Thrombolytic into Central Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a thrombolytic agent into a central artery to dissolve blood clots and restore blood flow. The percutaneous approach allows the healthcare provider to access the artery through a small puncture in the skin, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The thrombolytic medication is delivered directly to the affected area, helping to break down the clot and restore normal blood flow to oxygen-starved tissues. The goal of this procedure is to treat life-threatening conditions such as stroke, pulmonary embolism, or acute myocardial infarction.
