Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Right Pelvic Bone, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to gain access to the right pelvic bone, where a internal fixation device is then carefully inserted to stabilize and support the bone. The device is typically made of metal and is designed to hold the bone in place while it heals, promoting proper alignment and preventing further injury. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissue, ensuring precise placement of the device. Following the procedure, the incision is closed and the patient is closely monitored to ensure a smooth recovery.
