Excision of Cerebral Hemisphere, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves a surgical excision of a portion of the cerebral hemisphere, a region of the brain responsible for controlling various bodily functions, including movement, sensation, and cognition. The open approach requires a craniotomy, where a section of the skull is temporarily removed to access the brain. The diagnostic purpose of the procedure is to excise a portion of the brain to determine the underlying cause of symptoms, such as tumors, lesions, or abnormal tissue growth. The goal is to obtain a tissue sample for further examination and diagnosis, which may lead to a definitive treatment plan.
