Detachment at Right 1st Toe, Low, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the right 1st toe at a low level, which means the separation occurs closer to the base of the toe rather than at the joint. The open approach indicates that the surgery is performed through a skin incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area. The goal of the detachment is to correct deformities, relieve pressure, or treat conditions such as hammertoe or toe contracture. The detachment may also be necessary due to injury, infection, or tumor.
