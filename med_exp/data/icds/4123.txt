Extraction of Perineum Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the perineal region to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are then carefully extracted to treat conditions such as infection, abscess, or tumor. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to remove the affected tissue and fascia with precision. The procedure is often performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may require a short hospital stay for post-operative care. Afterwards, the wound is closed, and the patient is monitored for potential complications, such as infection or scarring.
