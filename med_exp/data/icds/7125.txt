Revision of Drainage Device in Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the revision of a drainage device located in the peritoneal cavity, a fluid-filled space in the abdomen, using a minimally invasive percutaneous approach. The percutaneous approach allows the surgeon to access the device through a small incision in the skin, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The revision process may involve removing, replacing, or adjusting the existing drainage device to ensure proper function and optimal drainage. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal fluid balance and alleviate symptoms associated with poor drainage, such as abdominal pain, nausea, and infection.
