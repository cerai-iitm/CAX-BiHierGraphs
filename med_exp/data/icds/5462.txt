Reposition Right Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the tibia bone in the lower leg to its normal anatomical position, typically performed to treat deformities, fractures, or dislocations. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect and manipulate the bone. The surgeon will then reposition the tibia, securing it in place using internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods, to promote healing and stability. Post-operative care involves immobilization, pain management, and rehabilitation to restore normal mobility and function to the affected leg.
