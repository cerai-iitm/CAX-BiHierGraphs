Excision of Cervicothoracic Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the cervicothoracic vertebral joint, which connects the neck and upper back, through an open approach. The procedure typically requires a surgical incision to access the affected joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize and excise the damaged or diseased tissue. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure on surrounding nerves and spinal cord, alleviate chronic pain and stiffness, and improve mobility in the neck and upper back. Following the excision, the surrounding tissues and structures are carefully closed and repaired to promote optimal healing and minimize the risk of complications.
