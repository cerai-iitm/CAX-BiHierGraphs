Drainage of Right Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain the right pleural cavity, a space between the lungs and chest wall, by inserting a small tube or catheter through the skin. This percutaneous approach allows for the removal of excess fluid, air, or blood that may have accumulated in the cavity, often as a result of injury, infection, or cancer. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage tube. After the procedure, the tube is connected to a drainage system or bag to collect the drained fluid, promoting lung re-expansion and alleviating symptoms such as chest pain and shortness of breath.
