Excision of Right Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right lower arm to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The affected tissue and fascia are then carefully excised to diagnose underlying conditions or diseases. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the tissue, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure proper removal of the tissue for diagnostic purposes.
