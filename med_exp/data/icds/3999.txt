Drainage of Left Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Here is the description:

A percutaneous procedure is performed to drain the left hand's subcutaneous tissue and fascia for diagnostic purposes. The healthcare provider makes a small incision in the skin to access the affected area. Using specialized equipment, the accumulated fluid or debris is drained from the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. This minimally invasive procedure helps diagnose and treat conditions such as infections, inflammation, or traumatic injuries affecting the left hand.
