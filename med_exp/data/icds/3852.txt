Replacement of Left Breast using Deep Inferior Epigastric Artery Perforator Flap, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the reconstruction of the left breast using a deep inferior epigastric artery perforator (DIEP) flap, which is a type of autologous tissue flap harvested from the patient's abdomen. The flap is carefully dissected and transferred to the chest area, where it is reshaped to form a natural-looking breast. The DIEP flap is preferred over other types of flaps due to its robust blood supply and ability to maintain sensation. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the operative site, enabling the surgeon to precisely control bleeding and optimize tissue dissection.
