Occlusion of Left Common Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the minimally invasive occlusion of the left common carotid artery using an intraluminal device, which is inserted through the skin to block blood flow in the artery. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate device placement. The intraluminal device is designed to reduce blood flow to the targeted area, which can help to treat conditions such as aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or tumors. Following the procedure, the access site is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications or adverse reactions.
