Replacement of Left Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the open replacement of damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia in the left lower arm with an autologous tissue substitute. The autologous tissue substitute is derived from the patient's own body, making it a natural and biocompatible material for the repair. Through an open approach, the surgeon gains direct access to the affected area, allowing for precise dissection and removal of the damaged tissue and subsequent implantation of the substitute. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and anatomy to the left lower arm.
