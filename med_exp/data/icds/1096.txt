Dilation of Right Popliteal Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a percutaneous approach to access the right popliteal artery, which is a vital blood vessel located behind the knee. A specialized device is then inserted through the skin and guided to the targeted area, allowing for the gentle dilation of the artery to improve blood flow. The intraluminal device is carefully manipulated to expand the artery, relieving any blockages or narrowing that may be restricting blood flow to the legs. By widening the artery, this procedure can help to alleviate symptoms such as leg pain, numbness, or weakness, promoting improved circulation and overall health.
