Reposition Right Tibia with External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the realignment of the right tibia bone using an external fixation device, which is attached to the bone through small incisions in the skin. The device is applied percutaneously, meaning through the skin, to stabilize and immobilize the bone, allowing it to heal in its corrected position. The external fixator is typically secured to the surrounding tissue and bone, providing a stable framework for the bone to repair. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the right tibia, often following a fracture or other injury.
