Extraction of Vertebral Bone Marrow, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
Here is the description:

This procedure involves using a needle to collect a sample of bone marrow from a vertebra through the skin, usually from the pelvis or sternum. The bone marrow is then sent to a laboratory for examination to help diagnose various blood disorders, infections, or cancers. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive method of obtaining the bone marrow sample, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The diagnostic information obtained from the extracted bone marrow sample helps healthcare providers create an effective treatment plan for the patient.
