Repair Neck Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the neck to access the affected area, followed by the removal of damaged or scarred tissue and closure of the wound. The goal of the procedure is to restore the normal appearance and function of the skin in the neck area. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the tissue and make precise repairs. The procedure may be performed to repair skin defects, scars, or injuries resulting from trauma or prior surgery.
