Excision of Perineum Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the perineum, the area between the genitals and anus, to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The goal is to remove or excise the targeted tissue and fascia, which may be necessary to treat conditions such as abnormal growths, infections, or trauma. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and precisely remove the tissue and fascia. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and may require post-operative care to manage pain, wound healing, and prevent complications.
