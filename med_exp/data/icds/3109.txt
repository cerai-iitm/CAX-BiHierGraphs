Repair Stomach, Pylorus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the stomach and pylorus through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is guided through the mouth or a small incision, allowing the surgeon to access the affected area without making a large incision. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to repair any damage or abnormalities, such as ulcers, bleeding, or narrowing of the pylorus. The endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications, promotes faster healing, and often allows patients to return to their normal activities sooner.
