Fluoroscopy of Left Subclavian Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left subclavian artery, which is a major blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the left arm. During the procedure, a low osmolar contrast medium is injected into the artery to enhance visibility and allow for clear imaging. Fluoroscopy uses continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time images, enabling healthcare professionals to guide the procedure and obtain precise assessments of the artery's structure and function. By providing detailed images of the left subclavian artery, this procedure helps diagnose and treat a range of vascular conditions, including stenosis, aneurysms, and blood clots.
