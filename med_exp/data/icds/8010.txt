Fluoroscopy of Left Internal Carotid Artery using Other Contrast
During this diagnostic procedure, a special dye, also known as contrast, is injected into the left internal carotid artery to visualize its internal structures. Real-time X-ray images, known as fluoroscopy, are then taken to capture the flow of contrast through the artery, allowing clinicians to assess blood flow and identify any potential blockages or abnormalities. This non-invasive procedure provides valuable information to diagnose and treat conditions such as cerebral vasospasm, carotid artery stenosis, and cerebral aneurysms. The fluoroscopic images are used to guide the clinician in making accurate diagnoses and developing effective treatment plans.
