Drainage of Stomach with Drainage Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the stomach, and then inserting a drainage device to remove fluids, gases, or other substances that may be causing discomfort, pain, or obstructing digestion. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the stomach and surrounding tissue, enabling precise placement of the drainage device. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms, prevent complications, and promote healing. The patient may require post-operative care to manage any potential complications and ensure a smooth recovery.
