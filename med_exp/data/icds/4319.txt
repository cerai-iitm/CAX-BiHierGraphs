Transfer Perineum Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the perineum, which is the area between the anus and genitals. The subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue beneath the skin, are then transferred to a new location to repair or reconstruct the perineum. This transfer is typically done to repair defects or injuries, such as those caused by trauma or surgery. The open approach means that the procedure is performed through a direct incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and manipulate the tissue directly.
