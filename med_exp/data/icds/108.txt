Extirpation of Matter from Brain, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of foreign matter or debris from the brain through a small incision in the skin, typically using imaging guidance to precisely locate the target area. A thin instrument is inserted through the incision to reach the brain, allowing the surgeon to carefully extract the unwanted material without causing additional damage to surrounding tissue. The percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to the skin and underlying structures, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. By promptly removing the offending material, this procedure can help alleviate symptoms and improve patient outcomes.
