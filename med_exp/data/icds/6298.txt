Drainage of Left Kidney with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous drainage of the left kidney involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin and into the kidney to remove excess fluid or urine that has accumulated due to a blockage or obstruction. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to accurately locate the kidney and guide the drainage device. The drainage device is inserted through a small incision in the skin and advanced through the tissue to the kidney, where it is positioned to drain the accumulated fluid. The procedure can help to relieve symptoms such as pain, nausea, and vomiting, and can also help to prevent complications such as kidney damage or sepsis.
