Detachment at Left 3rd Toe, Complete, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the left 3rd toe from the rest of the foot, performed through an open approach. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and detach the toe through a skin incision. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be necessary due to severe injury, infection, or disease affecting the toe. The detached toe is then removed, and the wound is closed to promote healing and prevent further complications.
