Beam Radiation of Pelvic Region using Heavy Particles (Protons,Ions)
This procedure involves the use of heavy particles, such as protons or ions, to deliver beam radiation to the pelvic region. The treatment is typically administered using a specialized machine that accelerates the particles to high energies, allowing for precise targeting of cancer cells while minimizing damage to surrounding tissues. The pelvic region may include organs such as the prostate, bladder, cervix, or uterus, and this type of radiation therapy is often used to treat various types of cancer, including prostate cancer, cervical cancer, and uterine cancer. By using heavy particles, this treatment can provide more effective and targeted radiation therapy with fewer side effects compared to traditional photon-based radiation therapy.
