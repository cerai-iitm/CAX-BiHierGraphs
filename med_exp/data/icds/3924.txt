Division of Lower Extremity Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower extremity to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles. The purpose of the division is to release tension or repair damage to the tissue and fascia, which can be caused by injury, infection, or surgery. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly and perform the division with precision. The procedure is typically performed under general or regional anesthesia and may require post-operative recovery to manage pain and swelling.
