Dilation of Coronary Artery, One Artery with Drug-eluting Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a percutaneous approach to dilate a narrowed or blocked coronary artery, restoring blood flow to the heart. A drug-eluting intraluminal device, also known as a drug-eluting stent, is placed within the artery to help prevent future blockages. The stent slowly releases medication over time to prevent the growth of scar tissue and reduce the risk of restenosis. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance, allowing the cardiologist to visualize the artery and precisely place the stent.
