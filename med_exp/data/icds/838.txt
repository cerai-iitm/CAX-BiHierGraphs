Occlusion of Right Thyroid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves the insertion of a small, flexible catheter through the skin and into the right thyroid artery to occlude blood flow. The catheter is equipped with an intraluminal device, such as a coil or plug, which is deployed to block the artery. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the device. The goal of the procedure is to eliminate blood flow to the thyroid gland, often to treat conditions such as thyroid cancer or thyrotoxicosis.
