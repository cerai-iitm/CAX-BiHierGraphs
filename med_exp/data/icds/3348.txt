Drainage of Gallbladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to guide a drainage tube through the skin and into the gallbladder to remove excess bile or infected material. Under imaging guidance, a small incision is made in the abdomen and a drainage catheter is inserted through the skin and into the gallbladder. The catheter is then secured in place, allowing for the continuous drainage of bile or infected material from the gallbladder. This procedure is often performed as an emergency treatment for acute cholecystitis or gallbladder perforation, and can help to reduce symptoms and prevent complications until surgical removal of the gallbladder can be performed.
