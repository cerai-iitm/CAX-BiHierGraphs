Division of Right Lower Leg Tendon, Percutaneous Approach
This percutaneous procedure involves making a small incision in the skin to access the tendon in the right lower leg, allowing the surgeon to divide the tendon using specialized instruments. The minimally invasive approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissues and promotes faster healing. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate visualization of the tendon. The goal of the procedure is to relieve tension or restriction in the tendon, restoring normal function and alleviating symptoms in the affected leg.
