Drainage of Left Lung, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This medical procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to drain accumulated fluid or air from the left lung. The endoscope, equipped with a camera and light, allows the healthcare provider to visualize the lung tissue and identify the area requiring drainage. A drainage tube or suction device is then inserted through the endoscope to remove the excess fluid or air, helping to restore normal lung function and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath and chest pain. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation to ensure patient comfort and reduce risk of complications.
