Dilation of Right Cephalic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small catheter through the skin to access the right cephalic vein, a superficial vein located in the upper arm. Under imaging guidance, a specialist gently inserts a balloon-tipped catheter into the vein to dilate, or widen, the narrowed or blocked portion, restoring blood flow. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. By dilating the right cephalic vein, doctors can treat a range of vascular conditions, including stenosis and thrombosis, to alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and limited mobility.
