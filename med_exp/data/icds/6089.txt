Repair Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left knee to access the joint, enabling the surgeon to directly visualize and repair damaged or diseased tissue. The open approach allows for a more extensive repair, often necessary for complex injuries or deformities. During the procedure, the surgeon may repair or replace damaged cartilage, tendons, or ligaments, and may also remove any debris or loose fragments. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal knee function and alleviate pain, stiffness, and instability in the left knee joint.
