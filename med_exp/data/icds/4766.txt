Extraction of Left Hand Bursa and Ligament, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left hand to access and remove an inflamed bursa, a fluid-filled sac that reduces friction between tendons and bones, as well as a damaged or inflamed ligament, a fibrous connective tissue that connects bones to other bones. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and remove the problematic tissues. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, reduce inflammation, and improve range of motion in the hand. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia, and the incision is closed with sutures or staples after the procedure.
