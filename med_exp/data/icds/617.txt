Supplement Thoracic Aorta, Ascending/Arch with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This open surgical procedure involves supplementing a section of the thoracic aorta, specifically the ascending or arch portion, with zooplastic tissue, which is a bioprosthetic material derived from animal sources. The goal of the procedure is to restore the aorta's structural integrity and ensure proper blood flow. During the operation, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest to access the affected area and then implants the zooplastic tissue to reinforce the aorta. The use of zooplastic tissue provides a durable and biocompatible solution for repairing the thoracic aorta, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a successful outcome.
