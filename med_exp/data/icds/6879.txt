Drainage of Left Pleural Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain excess fluid or air from the left pleural cavity, a space between the lungs and chest wall, using a percutaneous approach. A drainage device is inserted through the skin and into the pleural cavity to remove the unwanted fluid or air, helping to relieve respiratory distress and improve lung function. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal lung expansion and function, and to alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, chest pain, and coughing.
