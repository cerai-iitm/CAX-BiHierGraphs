Revision of Intraluminal Device in Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the revision of an intraluminal device in the esophagus, which is a muscular tube that carries food from the throat to the stomach. The revision is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to access the device without making a large incision. During the procedure, an endoscope is inserted through the mouth or another natural opening, and specialized instruments are used to revise the device and ensure it is functioning properly. The goal of the procedure is to restore the device's function and alleviate any symptoms caused by its malfunction.
