Reposition Left Radius, External Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the left forearm to access the radius bone. The surgeon then manually repositions the bone to its normal anatomical position, which may have been displaced due to a fracture or other injury. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the forearm and hand. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure optimal results.
