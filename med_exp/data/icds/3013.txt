Inspection of Lower Intestinal Tract, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin to visually examine the lower intestinal tract, allowing for direct visualization of the inside of the colon and rectum. A flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is guided through the abdominal wall, enabling the healthcare provider to inspect the lining of the lower intestine for any abnormalities or signs of disease. This approach is typically used for diagnostic purposes, such as investigating symptoms of abdominal pain, bleeding, or changes in bowel habits. The percutaneous endoscopic approach provides a minimally invasive and relatively low-risk method for evaluating the lower gastrointestinal tract.
