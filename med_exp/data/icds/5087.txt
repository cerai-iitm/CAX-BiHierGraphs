Reposition Left Humeral Shaft, External Approach
This procedure involves the repositioning of the left humeral shaft, which is the long bone of the upper arm, to its normal anatomical position. The procedure is performed through an external approach, meaning it requires an incision to access the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to restore alignment and function to the humerus, which may have been disrupted due to injury or trauma. The repositioning process is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require the use of internal fixation devices, such as plates or screws, to stabilize the bone.
