Dilation of Coronary Artery, Three Arteries with Four or More Drug-eluting Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the dilation of three coronary arteries using a percutaneous approach, where a catheter is inserted through the skin and guided to the blocked arteries. The procedure is performed to open up the narrowed or blocked coronary arteries, which are treated with four or more drug-eluting intraluminal devices, also known as drug-eluting stents. These stents are designed to slowly release medication to prevent the growth of scar tissue and reduce the risk of future blockages. The goal of this procedure is to improve blood flow to the heart, relieve symptoms of coronary artery disease, and reduce the risk of heart attack.
