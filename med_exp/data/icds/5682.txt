Fusion of 2 or more Cervical Vertebral Joints, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of two or more cervical vertebral joints, which are the joints that connect the bones of the neck, using an anterior approach. The anterior approach means the surgery is performed from the front of the neck, allowing the surgeon to access the affected area directly. During the open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the neck to expose the cervical vertebral joints and performs the fusion procedure. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the spine, alleviate pain, and improve mobility in patients with cervical spine instability or degenerative conditions.
