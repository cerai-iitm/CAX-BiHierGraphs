Supplement Left Orbit with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this procedure, an incision is made to access the left orbit, which is the bony cavity that contains the eyeball. The surgical team inserts a synthetic substitute, such as a porous polyethylene implant, to supplement the defective or missing orbital bone structure. The synthetic material is carefully shaped and positioned to restore the natural contour of the left orbit, ensuring proper support and protection of the surrounding tissues and eye. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the anatomical structures, enabling precise placement and fixation of the synthetic substitute.
