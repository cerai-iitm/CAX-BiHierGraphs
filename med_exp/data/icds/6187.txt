Reposition Left Ankle Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left ankle joint to its normal alignment, which is necessary when the joint is dislocated or malaligned due to injury or trauma. During the procedure, an open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected joint. The ankle joint is then manually repositioned to its correct position, and an internal fixation device, such as a plate or screws, is implanted to stabilize the joint and promote healing. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal ankle function, alleviate pain, and prevent future complications.
