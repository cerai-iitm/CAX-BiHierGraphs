Transfer Right Trunk Muscle, Deep Inferior Epigastric Artery Perforator Flap, Open Approach
During a complex reconstructive surgery, the deep inferior epigastric artery perforator flap is harvested from the abdominal region to transfer the right trunk muscle. This intricate procedure involves dissecting the deep inferior epigastric artery perforator flap, carefully preserving its blood supply and surrounding tissue. The flap is then transposed to the desired site, where it is secured and reattached to restore the natural function and appearance of the affected area. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling precise dissection and minimization of tissue trauma.
