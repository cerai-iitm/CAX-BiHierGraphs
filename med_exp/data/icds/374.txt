Dilation of Coronary Artery, Three Arteries, Bifurcation, with Four or More Drug-eluting Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves widening of three blocked coronary arteries at a bifurcation point using a percutaneous approach. A guidewire is inserted through an artery in the leg or arm and guided to the blocked coronary arteries, where a balloon is inflated to compress the blockage. Four or more drug-eluting stents are then placed in the arteries to restore blood flow and reduce the risk of future blockages. The drug-eluting stents slowly release medication to prevent the growth of new blockages and promote healing.
