Removal of Intraluminal Device from Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of an intraluminal device from the esophagus through a natural or artificial opening. The device, which may be a stent, balloon, or other type of instrument, is carefully extracted using an endoscope and specialized tools. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and may require fluoroscopic guidance to ensure safe and precise removal. Following the procedure, the patient may experience temporary discomfort and is usually monitored for any signs of complications.
