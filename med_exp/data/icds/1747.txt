Excision of Mesenteric Lymphatic, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the abdominal cavity, allowing for the precise excision of a mesenteric lymph node through a small incision in the skin. Under image guidance, the healthcare provider inserts the endoscope and specialized instruments through the incision to remove the affected lymph node. The diagnostic excision is typically performed to obtain a tissue sample for further examination and to aid in the diagnosis of conditions such as lymphoma, inflammatory bowel disease, or other cancers. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces post-operative pain and scarring, promoting a faster recovery compared to traditional open surgical methods.
