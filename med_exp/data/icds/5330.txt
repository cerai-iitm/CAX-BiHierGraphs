Insertion of External Fixation Device into Left Metatarsal, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an external fixation device is inserted into the left metatarsal bone through an open approach, which involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the bone. The device is used to stabilize and immobilize the metatarsal bone, allowing it to heal properly in cases of fractures, deformities, or other conditions. The external fixation device consists of pins or wires that are connected to a external frame, which provides structural support and maintains alignment of the bone. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and may require a hospital stay, depending on the complexity of the case.
