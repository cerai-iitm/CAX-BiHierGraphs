Excision of Trachea, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical operation to remove a portion of the trachea, also known as the windpipe, through an open approach. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the neck to access the trachea and removes the affected section. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a temporary tracheostomy to facilitate breathing during the operation. Following the excision, the trachea may be reconstructed or repaired using surgical techniques and materials.
