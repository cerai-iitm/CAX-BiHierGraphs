Excision of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the left lower lung lobe through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or a small incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the lung tissue and perform the excision. The diagnostic aspect of this procedure allows for the removal of tissue samples for examination and diagnosis of lung diseases or conditions. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces trauma to the surrounding tissue and promotes faster recovery.
