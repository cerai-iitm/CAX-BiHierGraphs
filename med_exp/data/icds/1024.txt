Dilation of Right Common Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through a small incision in the groin or arm to access the right common iliac artery. A balloon or other intraluminal device is then guided through the catheter to the blocked or narrowed segment of the artery, where it is inflated to widen the vessel. The device is left in place for a short time to allow the artery to expand, improving blood flow to the legs. After the procedure, the device is removed, and the incision is closed.
