Monitoring of Cardiac Vascular Perfusion using Indocyanine Green Dye, External Approach
This medical procedure involves the use of indocyanine green dye to assess cardiac vascular perfusion, which is the delivery of oxygen and nutrients to the heart tissue. The external approach is used, where the dye is injected into the bloodstream and a specialized camera or imaging device is used to track its circulation and detect any abnormalities in blood flow. This non-invasive technique allows for real-time visualization of cardiac perfusion, enabling healthcare providers to identify areas of poor blood flow and diagnose conditions such as coronary artery disease. The results of this procedure can inform treatment decisions and guide interventions to improve cardiac function.
