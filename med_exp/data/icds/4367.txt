Drainage of Left Hand Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to drain excess fluid or infected material from a muscle in the left hand. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected muscle, and then uses suction or drainage tubes to remove the unwanted material. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure, reduce swelling, and promote healing in the hand. In some cases, the procedure may also involve debridement, or the removal of dead tissue, to prevent further infection.
