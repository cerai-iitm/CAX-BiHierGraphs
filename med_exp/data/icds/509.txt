Release Right Atrium, Open Approach
This invasive cardiac procedure involves making a large incision in the chest to access the right atrium, a chamber of the heart. The surgeon then manually releases any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstructions that may be present in the right atrium, restoring normal blood flow and cardiac function. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomy and precise dissection of any obstructive tissue. The goal of the procedure is to improve cardiac output and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and swelling.
