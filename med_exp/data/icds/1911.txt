Resection of Right Vitreous, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the removal of the vitreous gel from the right eye through a minimally invasive, percutaneous approach, which involves making small incisions in the eye to access the vitreous cavity. The vitreous gel is then carefully removed using specialized instruments, allowing for the treatment of conditions such as vitreomacular adhesion or retinal detachment. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal tissue disruption and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgical approaches. The goal of the procedure is to restore vision and alleviate symptoms associated with vitreous-related disorders.
