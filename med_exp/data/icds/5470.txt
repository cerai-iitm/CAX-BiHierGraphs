Reposition Left Tibia with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the realignment of the left tibia, also known as the shinbone, to its normal anatomical position. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and reposition the bone. An internal fixation device, such as a plate or intramedullary nail, is then used to hold the bone in place and facilitate healing. This procedure is often performed to treat complex fractures or bone deformities that cannot be corrected through non-surgical means.
