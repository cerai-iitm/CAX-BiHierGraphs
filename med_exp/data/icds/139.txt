Insertion of Neurostimulator Lead into Spinal Cord, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the implantation of a neurostimulator lead directly into the spinal cord, which is accessed through an open incision in the skin and underlying tissues. The lead is carefully guided into the spinal cord by the surgeon, who uses fluoroscopic or electro Physiologic monitoring to ensure accurate placement. Once in place, the lead is secured and connected to a pulse generator, which delivers electrical impulses to the spinal cord to interrupt pain signals to the brain. The goal of this procedure is to provide long-term relief from chronic pain in patients who have not responded to other forms of treatment.
