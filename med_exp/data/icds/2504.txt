Excision of Tongue, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion or the entire tongue through an external approach, often performed to treat cancer, tumors, or other abnormalities affecting the tongue. The operation typically requires a incision in the mouth or neck to access the tongue, and the affected area is carefully excised to minimize damage to surrounding tissues. The procedure may involve reconstruction or repair of surrounding structures, such as the floor of the mouth or the tongue itself, to restore normal oral function. The goal of the surgery is to remove the affected tissue, restore normal speech and swallowing function, and improve the patient's overall quality of life.
