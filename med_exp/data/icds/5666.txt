Fusion of Cervical Vertebral Joint with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Anterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the cervical vertebral joint using a non-autologous tissue substitute, which is a material sourced from outside the patient's body. The anterior approach is used, where the surgeon accesses the affected area from the front of the neck, and the anterior column is the specific region of the spine where the fusion takes place. The open approach indicates that a larger incision is made to allow direct visualization of the surgical site. The goal of this procedure is to stabilize the spine and alleviate symptoms such as pain, numbness, or weakness in the neck and arms.
