Replacement of Left Mandible with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left mandible (lower jawbone) with a synthetic substitute, typically made of metal or ceramic materials, to restore function and aesthetics. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision to access the damaged or diseased mandible, removes the affected bone, and implants the synthetic substitute, securing it with screws, plates, or other fixation devices. The synthetic material is carefully selected to match the patient's natural bone structure and provide optimal functional and cosmetic results. Post-operative care and rehabilitation are crucial to ensure proper healing and adaptation to the new prosthetic jawbone.
