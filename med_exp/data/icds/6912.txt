Drainage of Peritoneal Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure uses an endoscope to guide the insertion of a drainage device into the peritoneal cavity to remove excess fluid or infected material. The percutaneous approach allows for a small incision to be made in the abdominal wall, reducing tissue trauma and promoting faster healing. The endoscope provides visualization of the peritoneal cavity, enabling the physician to accurately place the drainage device and ensure effective drainage. By removing excess fluid, this procedure can help alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, distension, and discomfort.
