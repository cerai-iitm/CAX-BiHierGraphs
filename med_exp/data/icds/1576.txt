Excision of Left Saphenous Vein, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the left saphenous vein through a small incision, using an endoscope to visually guide the surgical instruments. A percutaneous approach is used, where a small puncture is made in the skin to access the vein. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can be completed in under an hour. The excision of the left saphenous vein is often performed to treat venous insufficiency, varicose veins, or other vascular conditions.
