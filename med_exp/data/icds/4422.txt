Excision of Left Trunk Muscle, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description tailored to the code 0KBG0ZZ:

This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the left trunk muscle. The muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, from the surrounding tissue. The approach is open, meaning a larger incision is made to allow for direct visualization of the affected area. The procedure may be performed to treat muscle damage or disease, or to remove a tumor or cyst that is affecting the function of the left trunk muscle.
