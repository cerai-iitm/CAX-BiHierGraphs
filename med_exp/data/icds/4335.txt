Transfer Right Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Skin, Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves the transfer of subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the right upper leg, along with the overlying skin, to a new location, utilizing an open surgical approach. The goal of this procedure is to restore or reconstruct damaged or defective tissue, often required in cases of traumatic injuries, congenital defects, or tumor resections. The procedure typically involves a meticulous dissection of the donor tissue, followed by precise reattachment at the recipient site, ensuring proper blood supply and tissue alignment. Post-operative care involves careful monitoring to ensure tissue viability and promoting optimal healing.
