Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Ceramic Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the diseased or damaged right hip joint with a ceramic synthetic substitute through an open approach, which allows the surgeon to visually examine the joint and surrounding tissue. The ceramic material is chosen for its strength, durability, and biocompatibility, reducing the risk of implant rejection or adverse reactions. During the procedure, the surgeon will remove the damaged or arthritic joint and replace it with the ceramic prosthetic, carefully aligning and securing it to surrounding bone and tissue. The open approach allows for precise control and visualization, enabling the surgeon to achieve optimal results and minimize potential complications.
