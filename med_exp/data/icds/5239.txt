Excision of Right Metatarsal, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right foot to access the metatarsal bone, which is one of the five long bones in the midfoot region. The goal of the excision is to remove the metatarsal bone, which may be necessary due to various reasons such as bone tumor, infection, or severe deformity. During the procedure, the surgeon uses an open approach to expose the metatarsal bone, carefully dissecting the surrounding soft tissues to avoid damaging them. The excision is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort and pain.
