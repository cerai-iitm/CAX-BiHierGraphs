Resection of Rectum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the rectum, which is the lower part of the large intestine, through an open incision in the abdomen. The surgery typically requires a large abdominal incision, allowing the surgeon to access the rectum and surrounding tissue. During the procedure, the rectum is carefully dissected from the surrounding tissue and blood vessels, and then removed from the body. The surgery may be performed to treat conditions such as cancer, rectal prolapse, or severe inflammatory bowel disease.
