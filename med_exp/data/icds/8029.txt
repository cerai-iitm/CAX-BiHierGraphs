Fluoroscopy of Left Vertebral Artery using Other Contrast
This diagnostic procedure involves the use of fluoroscopic guidance to visualize the left vertebral artery, which supplies blood to the brain and spinal cord. A contrast agent, other than the standard iodine-based agents, is injected into the artery to enhance image quality and provide detailed information on blood flow and potential blockages. The fluoroscope produces real-time X-ray images, allowing the physician to monitor the contrast agent as it flows through the artery, facilitating the detection of any abnormalities or occlusions. The resulting images aid in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions such as vertebral artery stenosis or occlusion, which can increase the risk of stroke or transient ischemic attacks.
