Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Left Fibula, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the left fibula, a bone in the lower leg. An internal fixation device, such as a plate or rod, is then inserted into the fibula to stabilize and align the bone. The device helps to promote healing and restore function to the affected leg. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissues, enabling precise placement of the fixation device.
