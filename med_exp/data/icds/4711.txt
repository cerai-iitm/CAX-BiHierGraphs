Reposition Left Lower Arm and Wrist Tendon, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower arm and wrist to manually reposition a displaced tendon, allowing it to return to its normal anatomical position and restore proper function. The open approach requires a surgical exposure to access the affected tendon, which is then mobilized and repositioned to its normal location. The procedure aims to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility, and may be necessary to treat conditions such as tendon subluxation or dislocation. Following repositioning, the incision is closed, and the patient may require post-operative rehabilitation to ensure proper healing and restore full range of motion.
