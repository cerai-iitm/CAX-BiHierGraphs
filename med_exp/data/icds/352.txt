Dilation of Coronary Artery, One Artery with Two Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves using a percutaneous approach to access the coronary artery, where a catheter is inserted through the skin and guided to the blockage. Once in place, two intraluminal devices are deployed to dilate the artery, restoring blood flow and improving cardiac function. The devices work to compress the plaque against the artery wall, allowing for a wider channel for blood to flow through. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and can take around 30-60 minutes to complete, depending on the complexity of the case.
