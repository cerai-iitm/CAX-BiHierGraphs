Occlusion of Right Popliteal Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the right popliteal artery, a major blood vessel located behind the knee. The aim is to occlude, or block, the artery to stop bleeding or prevent blood flow to a specific area. During the open approach, the surgeon carefully exposes the artery to ensure accurate dissection and occlusion. The occlusion is typically achieved using sutures, clips, or other materials to close off the artery, promoting healing and resolving symptoms.
