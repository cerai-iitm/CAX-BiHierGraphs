Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Common Iliac Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting an infusion device into the left common iliac vein, a major blood vessel located in the pelvis, through a small incision in the skin. Under imaging guidance, a thin catheter is advanced through the vein to the desired location, and the infusion device is then inserted through the catheter. The device is secured in place, allowing for the administration of medications, nutrients, or other substances directly into the bloodstream. Following the procedure, the incision site is closed, and the patient is monitored for any signs of complications.
