Supplement Radial Nerve with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
During this open approach procedure, a synthetic substitute is used to supplement the radial nerve, which runs from the spinal cord down to the hand and is responsible for controlling sensation and motor function in the arm and hand. The goal of the procedure is to restore function and alleviate symptoms such as numbness, tingling, and weakness in the arm and hand. The synthetic substitute is carefully implanted to support and replace damaged or diseased portions of the radial nerve, promoting optimal nerve function and recovery. This procedure may be performed to treat a range of conditions, including nerve damage, compression, or injury.
