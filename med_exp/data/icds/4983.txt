Excision of Left Ulna, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the left ulna bone in the forearm, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the affected portion of the bone. The open approach enables direct visualization of the anatomical structures, facilitating precise excision of the targeted section of the ulna. The surgery requires careful dissection and retraction of surrounding soft tissues to prevent damage and promote optimal healing. Following excision, the surgical site is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for signs of complications or infection.
