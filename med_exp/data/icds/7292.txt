Detachment at Right 2nd Toe, Mid, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves detaching the middle segment of the right second toe through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The detachment is typically performed to treat conditions such as trauma, infection, or deformity that affect the toe's mobility or functionality. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the toe to expose the affected segment, and then carefully detaches it to restore proper alignment and function. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain, improve mobility, and enhance the overall quality of life for the patient.
