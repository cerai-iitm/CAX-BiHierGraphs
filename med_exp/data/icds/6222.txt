Supplement Left Knee Joint, Tibial Surface with Liner, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves implanting a liner on the tibial surface of the left knee joint to provide a smooth gliding surface, facilitating normal joint movement and reducing friction between the bones. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the area and perform the supplementation. The liner is carefully positioned and secured to ensure proper alignment and functionality of the knee joint. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain and discomfort, restore mobility, and improve overall quality of life for the patient.
