Reposition Right Femoral Shaft with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
During a surgical procedure, the femoral shaft in the right leg is repositioned to its natural alignment to treat fractures or deformities. This is achieved through an open approach, which involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the affected bone. An intramedullary internal fixation device is then inserted into the marrow canal of the femur to provide stability and support to the bone as it heals. This device helps to hold the bone in place, promoting optimal alignment and union, and facilitating a faster recovery.
