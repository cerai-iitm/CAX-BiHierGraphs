Removal of Tunneled Vascular Access Device from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove a tunneled vascular access device, which is a type of catheter, from the trunk area. The device is accessed through a small incision in the skin, and the catheter is gently pulled out from under the skin and fascia, a layer of tissue that covers muscles. This percutaneous approach allows for a quick and relatively painless removal of the device, reducing the risk of complications. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and takes around 30 minutes to an hour to complete.
