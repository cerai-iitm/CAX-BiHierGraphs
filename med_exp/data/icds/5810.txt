Reposition Left Elbow Joint, External Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the skin and soft tissue of the left elbow to access the joint. The joint is then manipulated to restore its normal anatomic alignment, which may have been disrupted due to injury or disease. The surgeon uses visualization techniques and manual manipulation to reposition the joint, ensuring proper alignment and mobility. The incision is closed, and the joint is stabilized to allow for proper healing and rehabilitation.
