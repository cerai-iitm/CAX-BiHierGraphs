Fusion of Lumbar Vertebral Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Posterior Approach, Posterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the lumbar vertebral joint using a synthetic substitute, which is designed to promote spinal stability and alleviate pain. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, meaning the surgeon accesses the affected area from the back of the patient's body. During the operation, the synthetic substitute is implanted into the posterior column of the spine, which provides additional support to the vertebral joint. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the affected area and accurately place the substitute, ensuring a precise and effective fusion.
