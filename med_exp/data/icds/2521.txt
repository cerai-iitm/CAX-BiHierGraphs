Excision of Larynx, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the larynx, also known as the voice box, through an endoscopic approach, which uses a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end to visualize the area. The procedure is performed through either a natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth or a tracheostomy, allowing the surgeon to access the larynx without making a large incision. The diagnostic excision of the larynx is typically performed to treat conditions such as laryngeal cancer, vocal cord lesions, or recurring respiratory papillomatosis. During the procedure, the surgeon uses specialized instruments to carefully dissect and remove the larynx while minimizing damage to surrounding tissues.
