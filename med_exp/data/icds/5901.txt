Excision of Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
The surgical removal of the right knee joint is a complex and invasive procedure that requires an open approach, involving a large incision in the skin and soft tissues to access the affected joint. This procedure is typically performed to treat severe knee joint damage or disease, such as advanced osteoarthritis or cancer, that cannot be managed with non-surgical interventions. The surgery involves the complete excision of the damaged or diseased knee joint, which is then typically replaced with a prosthetic joint or device to restore mobility and alleviate pain. The procedure requires careful dissection and retraction of surrounding soft tissues to access the joint, and may involve the use of specialized instruments and implants.
