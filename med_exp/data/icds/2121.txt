Drainage of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During this procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, known as an endoscope, is inserted through the mouth or nose and guided through the airways to the right lower lung lobe. The scope allows the healthcare provider to visualize the lung tissue and identify any areas of infection or fluid accumulation. A drainage tube or suction device is then inserted through the scope to remove any accumulated fluid or pus, helping to alleviate symptoms and promote healing. The minimally invasive nature of this procedure reduces the risk of complications and allows for faster recovery compared to open surgical methods.
