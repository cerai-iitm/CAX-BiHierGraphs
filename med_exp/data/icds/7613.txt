Introduction of Electrolytic and Water Balance Substance into Peripheral Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous peripheral vein access is established to facilitate the administration of electrolytic and water balance substances into the vein. A needle is inserted through the skin and into the vein, allowing for the infusion of essential fluids and electrolytes to restore balance and prevent dehydration. This minimally invasive procedure is typically performed in a clinical setting, under the supervision of a healthcare professional. The infusion of electrolytic and water balance substances helps to regulate various bodily functions, promoting overall health and wellness.
