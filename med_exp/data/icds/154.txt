Release Spinal Meninges, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the spinal meninges, which are the protective membranes surrounding the spinal cord and nerve roots. The goal of the procedure is to release tension or compression on the spinal meninges, which can be caused by various conditions such as inflammation, infection, or trauma. Through an open approach, the surgeon carefully exposes the affected area of the spinal meninges to relieve pressure and restore normal function. This procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as meningitis, abscesses, or spinal cord compression.
