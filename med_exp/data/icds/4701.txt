Repair Right Ankle Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right ankle to access and repair a damaged or torn tendon. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected tendon, enabling precise repair or reconstruction. The tendon is then sutured or reattached to surrounding tissue to restore normal ankle function and alleviate symptoms such as pain and instability. Post-operative rehabilitation is typically necessary to ensure proper healing and prevent future complications.
