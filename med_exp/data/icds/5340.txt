Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Upper Femur, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate, rod, or screw, from the upper end of the right femur bone, typically performed through an open incision. The device is carefully extracted to avoid damaging surrounding tissue or bone, and the wound is then closed. The goal of the procedure is to relieve symptoms such as pain, stiffness, or limited mobility caused by the device's presence, and to promote proper healing and bone growth. The procedure requires precision and skill to avoid complications and ensure a successful outcome.
