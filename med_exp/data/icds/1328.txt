Supplement Right Femoral Artery with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the supplementation of the right femoral artery with a synthetic substitute using an open approach. The aim is to restore blood flow and improve circulation to the lower extremities. The procedure typically involves a incision in the groin area, followed by the exposure of the right femoral artery, and the attachment of the synthetic graft to the affected artery. The synthetic substitute is typically made of durable materials, such as polyester or ePTFE, and is designed to withstand blood pressure and flow over an extended period.
