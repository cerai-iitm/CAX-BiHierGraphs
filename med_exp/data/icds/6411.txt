Release Left Ureter, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Percutaneous endoscopic release of the left ureter is a minimally invasive procedure that involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the ureter and release any blockages or obstructions. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The surgeon makes a small incision in the skin and inserts the endoscope through the kidney to reach the blockage, then uses specialized instruments to gently release the ureter and restore normal urine flow. The procedure is usually performed to treat conditions such as ureteral stricture, ureteropelvic junction obstruction, or ureteral stones that are causing hydronephrosis or recurring infections.
