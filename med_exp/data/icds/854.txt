Repair Left Radial Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the left radial artery, which is then repaired by a healthcare professional. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the artery, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any damage or defects. The radial artery is a significant blood vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the hand, making prompt and effective repair crucial to prevent complications such as tissue necrosis or gangrene. The procedure requires a high degree of precision and skill to ensure that the artery is properly restored to its normal function.
