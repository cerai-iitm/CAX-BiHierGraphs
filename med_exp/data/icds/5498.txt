Reposition Left Fibula, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left fibula, a bone in the lower leg, to its normal anatomical position using an external approach. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the fibula. The fibula is then repositioned to restore proper alignment and function, and the incision is closed in layers to promote healing. Postoperatively, the patient may require immobilization and rehabilitation to ensure proper recovery and prevent complications.
