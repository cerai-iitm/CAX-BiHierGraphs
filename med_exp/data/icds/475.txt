Insertion of Cardiac Lead into Left Ventricle, Open Approach
This invasive procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the left ventricle. A specialized lead, typically attached to a pacemaker or implantable cardioverter-defibrillator, is then carefully inserted into the left ventricle through the open incision. The lead is guided to the desired location using fluoroscopy or echocardiography to ensure proper placement. Once in place, the lead is secured and the incision is closed, enabling the device to regulate the heart's rhythm and ensure proper cardiac function.
