Ultrasonography of Pancreas
This non-invasive diagnostic test uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the pancreas, allowing healthcare professionals to visualize the organ's structure and identify any abnormalities. The procedure involves placing a transducer on the abdomen, which sends and receives sound waves, and uses a computer to convert the signals into real-time images. It is commonly used to diagnose conditions such as pancreatitis, pancreatic cancer, and pseudocysts, as well as to guide biopsies or other interventional procedures. The test is usually performed in a radiology department or outpatient imaging center and typically takes around 30-60 minutes to complete.
