Replacement of Right Brachial Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
The procedure involves an open surgical approach to replace the right brachial artery with autologous tissue substitute, a biological material harvested from the patient's own body. This complex operation requires a skilled vascular surgeon to carefully excise the damaged or diseased portion of the artery and replace it with the autologous tissue substitute, which is shaped and sized to match the natural artery. The graft is then sutured in place, restoring blood flow to the arm and hand. This procedure is often necessary to treat conditions such as atherosclerosis, aneurysms, or arterial injuries that compromise blood flow to the upper extremities.
