Excision of Right Ulna, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the right ulna, a long bone in the forearm. The affected portion of the bone is then carefully excised, or removed, to treat conditions such as bone tumors, infections, or severe fractures. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissues to ensure precise removal of the affected area. Following the excision, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the wound is dressed to promote healing and prevent complications.
