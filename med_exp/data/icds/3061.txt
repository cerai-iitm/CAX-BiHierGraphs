Release Transverse Colon, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the abdominal cavity is accessed through an incision to reach the transverse colon, which is then carefully freed from surrounding tissues and adhesions. The goal of this intervention is to release any restrictive or constricting forces that may be impeding the normal functioning of the colon. This release is achieved through meticulous dissection and lysis of any fibrotic or inflammatory attachments. The restored anatomy allows for the normalization of intestinal motility and overall digestive function.
