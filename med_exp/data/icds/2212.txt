Excision of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the right lower lung lobe through an open approach, which means a larger incision is made in the chest to access the lung. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to examine the lung tissue and identify any abnormalities or diseases, such as lung cancer or infections. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully dissect the lung tissue to locate the affected lobe and remove it, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues. The removed tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
