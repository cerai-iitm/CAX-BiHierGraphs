Fluoroscopy of Multiple Coronary Arteries using High Osmolar Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of high osmolar contrast material to visualize multiple coronary arteries using fluoroscopy. During the procedure, a contrast agent is injected into the bloodstream, allowing the coronary arteries to be visualized in real-time using continuous X-ray beams. The fluoroscopy machine captures images of the coronary arteries, enabling the identification of any blockages or narrowing. The procedure is used to diagnose and evaluate coronary artery disease, helping physicians develop effective treatment plans.
