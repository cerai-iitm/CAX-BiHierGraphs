Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Left Breast, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an artificial implant or synthetic material from the left breast through an open incision. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the implant and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to carefully extract the synthetic substitute from the breast cavity. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to mitigate discomfort and anxiety. Following removal, the incision is closed, and the breast tissue is rearranged to optimize aesthetic appearance and promote proper healing.
