Drainage of Left Maxillary Sinus with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope to visualize the left maxillary sinus and insert a drainage device through a natural or artificial opening to relieve sinus pressure and promote drainage. The endoscope is guided through the nostril or a small incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the sinus cavity and remove any blockages or debris. The drainage device is then carefully placed to facilitate the outflow of mucus and other fluids, helping to restore normal sinus function. This procedure is often performed under local anesthesia and can be completed in about 30-60 minutes.
