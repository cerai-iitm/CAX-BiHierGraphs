Release Greater Omentum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the greater omentum, a fold of peritoneum that attaches to the stomach, in real-time. Through a small incision, the endoscope and specialized instruments are inserted to release the adherent or scarred greater omentum, restoring its normal mobility. The percutaneous approach avoids the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort.
