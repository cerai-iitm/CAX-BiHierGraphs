Removal of Intraluminal Device from Ureter, Open Approach
During an open surgery, a doctor accesses the ureter to remove an intraluminal device, which is a medical instrument placed inside the ureter to treat or diagnose a condition. This procedure typically requires a skin incision and surgical dissection to expose the ureter, allowing the doctor to carefully extract the device. The doctor may use specialized instruments and visualization tools to locate and remove the device, taking care to avoid damaging the surrounding tissue. After the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any postoperative complications.
