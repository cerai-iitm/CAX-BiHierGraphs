Supplement Right Temporal Bone with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the right temporal bone, which is a complex structure located on the side of the skull, above the ear. The goal of the procedure is to supplement the bone with a synthetic substitute, which is a man-made material designed to mimic the natural bone. The synthetic material is carefully shaped and placed to restore the natural contours of the bone and promote healing. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bone and surrounding tissue, enabling precise placement of the substitute and minimizing risk of complications.
