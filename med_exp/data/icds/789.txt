Extirpation of Matter from Upper Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the upper artery, allowing direct visualization and removal of unwanted material, such as a thrombus or embolus, that is obstructing blood flow. The open approach enables the surgeon to directly identify and extract the matter using specialized instruments. The goal is to restore blood flow and prevent further complications, such as tissue death or organ damage. By removing the blockage, the artery can return to its normal function, promoting healthy circulation and overall well-being.
