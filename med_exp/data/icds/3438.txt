Extirpation of Matter from Gallbladder, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to access the gallbladder through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the physician to visualize the gallbladder and remove unwanted matter or debris. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that enable the physician to extract the unwanted material, which may include gallstones, sludge, or other obstructions. This procedure is typically performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may involve minor surgical incisions or manipulations to facilitate access to the gallbladder. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal gallbladder function and alleviate symptoms related to gallstone disease or other gallbladder disorders.
