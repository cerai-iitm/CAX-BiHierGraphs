Reposition Left Toe Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically repositioning a misaligned phalanx in the left toe through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The phalanx is realigned to its normal anatomical position, and internal fixation devices such as plates, screws, or rods are used to stabilize the bone and facilitate healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment and function to the toe, alleviating pain and improving mobility. Postoperatively, the patient will require a period of recovery and rehabilitation to ensure a successful outcome.
