Resection of Appendix, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the appendix, a small, finger-like pouch attached to the large intestine. The appendix is then carefully dissected and removed from the surrounding tissue and blood vessels. The incision is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the appendix and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to carefully remove the inflamed or infected organ.
