Excision of Left Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the left tibia, followed by the removal of the entire bone. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the bone and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to carefully excise the tibia. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of surgical instruments, such as osteotomes and rasps, to facilitate bone removal. After the tibia is removed, the surgical site is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing and minimize the risk of complications.
