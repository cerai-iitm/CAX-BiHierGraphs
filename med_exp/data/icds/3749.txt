Extraction of Finger Nail, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision on the finger to access the nail plate, followed by careful removal of the entire nail or a portion of it, taking care to avoid damaging the surrounding tissue. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as ingrown toenails, fungal infections, or nail trauma. The external approach allows for direct visualization of the nail bed, enabling the surgeon to precisely extract the affected nail. Following the procedure, the wound is closed and dressed to promote healing and prevent infection.
