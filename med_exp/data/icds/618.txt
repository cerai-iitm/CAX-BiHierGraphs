Supplement Thoracic Aorta, Ascending/Arch with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical supplementation of the thoracic aorta, focusing on the ascending and arch sections, with a synthetic substitute material, utilizing an open approach. The goal is to repair or replace damaged or diseased portions of the aorta, restoring normal blood flow and alleviating symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, or risk of rupture. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize and access the affected area, ensuring precise placement of the synthetic graft. The synthetic material is designed to mimic the natural structure and function of the native aorta, promoting a safe and durable repair.
