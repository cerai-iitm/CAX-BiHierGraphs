Introduction of Regional Anesthetic into Peripheral Nerves and Plexi, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous insertion of a regional anesthetic into peripheral nerves and plexi to numb a specific area of the body, typically for pain management or surgical purposes. The process begins with the identification of the targeted nerve or plexus, followed by the insertion of a thin needle through the skin and into the surrounding tissue. Once in place, a local anesthetic is injected, blocking pain signals to the brain and providing temporary numbness or relief. The effectiveness of the anesthetic is then monitored to ensure optimal pain management.
