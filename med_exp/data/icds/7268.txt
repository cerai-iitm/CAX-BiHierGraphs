Detachment at Right Foot, Complete 1st Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the first ray, which consists of the big toe and the first metatarsal bone, from the right foot. The open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize the anatomical structures and perform the detachment. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as severe deformity, infection, or trauma to the first ray, which cannot be managed with conservative treatments. The goal of the procedure is to restore function and alleviate pain in the affected foot.
