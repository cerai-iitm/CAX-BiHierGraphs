Extirpation of Matter from Aortic Valve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of abnormal or foreign material from the aortic valve using an open approach, where the chest is surgically opened to access the valve. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal blood flow and alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath, fatigue, and dizziness. The removed material may include vegetations, blood clots, or debris accumulated on the valve, which can obstruct blood flow and lead to serious complications if left untreated. The procedure requires a skilled cardiothoracic surgeon and a multidisciplinary team to ensure optimal outcomes and minimize risks.
