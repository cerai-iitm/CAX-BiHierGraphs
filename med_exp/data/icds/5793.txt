Replacement of Left Shoulder Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Glenoid Surface, Open Approach
During an open surgery, a synthetic substitute is implanted to replace the left shoulder joint, specifically the glenoid surface, which is the shallow socket that forms the shoulder joint. This replacement is typically performed to alleviate severe pain and limited mobility caused by degenerative joint disease, trauma, or irreparable damage to the joint. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural joint, allowing for smooth movement and reducing discomfort. The open approach involves a larger incision, which provides the surgeon with a clear view of the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling a more precise and thorough replacement procedure.
