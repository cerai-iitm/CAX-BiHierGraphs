Reposition Right Sacroiliac Joint, External Approach
This procedure involves manually adjusting the right sacroiliac joint to its normal anatomic position using an external approach, typically performed to relieve pain and inflammation caused by misalignment or dysfunction. The sacroiliac joint is located where the sacrum and ilium bones meet, and repositioning it can help restore proper joint mobility and alleviate symptoms. During the procedure, the practitioner may use various techniques, such as joint mobilization or manipulation, to gently move the joint back into place. The goal of this procedure is to improve joint function, reduce pain and stiffness, and promote overall musculoskeletal health.
