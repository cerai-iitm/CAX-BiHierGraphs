Extirpation of Matter from Right Internal Carotid Artery, Bifurcation, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the right internal carotid artery, specifically at the bifurcation point where it divides into two branches. The goal is to remove unwanted matter, such as plaque, blood clots, or other debris, that may be obstructing blood flow to the brain. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the artery and remove the material using specialized instruments. By extirpating the matter, the procedure aims to restore normal blood flow and reduce the risk of stroke or other complications.
