Release Uterus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the uterus, followed by the release of adhesions or scar tissue that may be binding the uterus to surrounding organs or structures. The goal of the procedure is to free the uterus from any restricting attachments, allowing it to function normally and alleviating any related symptoms such as pelvic pain or infertility. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the uterus and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any adhesions or abnormalities. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor for any post-operative complications.
