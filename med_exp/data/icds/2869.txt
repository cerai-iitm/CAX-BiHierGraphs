Excision of Ascending Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the ascending colon, which is then carefully dissected and removed. The excision is performed through an open approach, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the area and ensure complete removal of the targeted section of the colon. The procedure may be performed to treat cancer, diverticulitis, or other conditions affecting the ascending colon, and may be accompanied by additional procedures such as resection of the bowel. The goal of the procedure is to remove the diseased or damaged portion of the colon and restore normal bowel function.
