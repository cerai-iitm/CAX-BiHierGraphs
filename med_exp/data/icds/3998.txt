Drainage of Left Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
Here is the description:

During this surgical procedure, an open approach is used to drain the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the left hand. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected area, allowing for the drainage of any accumulated fluid, pus, or infected material. The open approach enables the surgeon to directly visualize the tissue and fascia, ensuring a thorough removal of any unwanted substances. The procedure aims to promote healing, reduce inflammation, and alleviate symptoms such as pain and swelling in the left hand.
