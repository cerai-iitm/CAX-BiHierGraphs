Drainage of Right Kidney, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a needle through the skin and into the right kidney to drain excess fluids or urine, aiding in the relief of obstruction or infection. Under imaging guidance, a small catheter is advanced through the needle to facilitate drainage, allowing for the removal of unwanted substances and promoting healthy kidney function. The percutaneous approach enables a faster recovery and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgical methods. Post-procedure, the patient may experience some discomfort, which is typically manageable with medication, and is often monitored for a short period to ensure the effectiveness of the drainage.
