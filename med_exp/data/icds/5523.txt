Resection of Left Upper Femur, Open Approach
Surgical removal of a portion of the left upper femur is performed through an open approach, involving a surgical incision to access the affected area. This procedure is typically indicated for conditions such as tumors, fractures, or bone deformities that require removal of a section of the femur to restore function and alleviate symptoms. The surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to expose the left upper femur, and then carefully removes the affected portion of the bone. The surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for post-operative complications.
