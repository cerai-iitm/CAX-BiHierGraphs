Excision of Left Humeral Head, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical operation to remove the head of the left humerus bone, which is the upper arm bone that connects the shoulder to the elbow. The open approach requires a skin incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to visually examine the joint and surrounding tissues. The excision of the humeral head is often performed to treat severe cases of osteoarthritis, fractures, or tumors that have damaged the joint. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pain, restore mobility, and improve overall function of the affected arm.
