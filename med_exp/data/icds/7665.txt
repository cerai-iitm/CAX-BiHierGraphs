Introduction of Other Anti-infective into Eye, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves injecting an anti-infective medication directly into the eye through the skin, typically to treat bacterial, viral, or fungal infections affecting the eye. The percutaneous approach means that the medication is administered through a small incision or puncture in the skin, allowing it to reach the affected area quickly and efficiently. The medication is carefully selected based on the type of infection being treated and the patient's individual needs. The goal of this procedure is to combat infection, reduce inflammation, and promote healing in the affected eye.
