Excision of Intracranial Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a portion of a vein located within the cranium through an open incision in the skull, utilizing direct visualization to carefully dissect and isolate the affected vessel. The procedure requires a craniotomy, which involves temporarily removing a section of the skull to access the intracranial vein. Once the vein is exposed, the surgeon carefully excises the affected segment, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding brain tissue or structures. The goal of this procedure is to relieve pressure, remove obstructions, or treat conditions such as arteriovenous malformations or venous sinus thrombosis.
