Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an orthopedic surgeon removes the damaged or diseased left hip joint and replaces it with a synthetic substitute, which is a man-made artificial joint. The implant is secured in place using bone cement, a special type of adhesive, to ensure a strong and stable fit. The open approach involves making a single incision in the left hip area, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and perform the replacement. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal hip function, alleviate pain, and improve mobility and flexibility in the affected hip joint.
