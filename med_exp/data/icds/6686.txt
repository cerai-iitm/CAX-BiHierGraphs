Resection of Bilateral Fallopian Tubes, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of both fallopian tubes through a small incision using an endoscope, a thin, lighted tube with a camera, to visualize the reproductive organs. The surgeon inserts the endoscope and specialized instruments through the incision to carefully dissect and resect the fallopian tubes, typically performed under general anesthesia. The percutaneous approach allows for less tissue damage, reduced scarring, and a faster recovery time compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is usually performed to treat conditions such as ectopic pregnancy, hydrosalpinx, or as a preventive measure for ovarian cancer.
