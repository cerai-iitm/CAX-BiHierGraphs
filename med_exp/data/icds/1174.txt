Extirpation of Matter from Left External Iliac Artery, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left lower abdomen to access the external iliac artery, a major blood vessel responsible for supplying oxygenated blood to the lower limbs. The affected artery is then carefully exposed and the unwanted matter, such as a blood clot or plaque, is precisely removed to restore normal blood flow. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require temporary clamping of the artery to prevent bleeding. Post-operative care involves monitoring for potential complications, such as infection or bleeding, and promoting wound healing.
