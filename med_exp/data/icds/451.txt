Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Atrium, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to implant an infusion device into the right atrium, a chamber of the heart, through a percutaneous approach. This involves making a small incision in the chest and guiding the device through a vein under fluoroscopic guidance. The infusion device is typically a catheter or port connected to a reservoir that allows for repeated access for administration of medications, nutrients, or other substances. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and takes approximately 30-60 minutes to complete.
