Extirpation of Matter from Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the stomach through an endoscopic approach, utilizing a flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments to access the stomach via the mouth or a surgically created opening. The endoscope allows for visualization of the stomach lining and identification of the offending material, which is then carefully extracted using specialized instruments, such as forceps or suction devices. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may be used to treat conditions such as gastrointestinal obstructive symptoms, bleeding, or foreign body ingestion. The endoscopic approach offers a minimally invasive alternative to traditional open surgery, reducing recovery time and promoting a faster return to normal activities.
