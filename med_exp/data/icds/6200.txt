Resection of Lumbar Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lower back to access the lumbar spine, where a portion of the vertebral disc that is causing pain or discomfort is removed. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure on the surrounding nerves and spinal cord, alleviating symptoms such as back pain, leg pain, numbness, or tingling. The resection is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon may use a microscope or loupe magnification to visualize the disc and surrounding structures. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as herniated discs, degenerative disc disease, or spinal stenosis.
