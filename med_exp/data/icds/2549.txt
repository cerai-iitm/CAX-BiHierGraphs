Repair Lower Lip, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the affected area of the lower lip. The surgeon then repairs any defects or damage to the lip tissue, muscle, or skin, using sutures, grafts, or other necessary materials to restore normal anatomy and function. The goal of the procedure is to improve the appearance and function of the lower lip, and may be performed to correct congenital deformities, repair traumatic injuries, or restore tissue damaged by disease or cancer. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to achieve precise repair and minimize potential complications.
