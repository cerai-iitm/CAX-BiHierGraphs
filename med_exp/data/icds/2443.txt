Revision of Intraluminal Device in Tracheobronchial Tree, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the revision of an intraluminal device located within the tracheobronchial tree, a network of airways that includes the trachea and bronchi. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the physician to access the device without the need for open surgery. Using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, the physician visualizes the device and performs the necessary revisions to ensure proper function. The goal of the procedure is to restore optimal airflow and breathing function by revising the device to its original purpose.
