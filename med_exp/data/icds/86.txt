Excision of Cerebellum, Open Approach
Surgical removal of the cerebellum, a vital structure located at the base of the brain, is a complex and rare procedure performed to treat conditions such as cancer, traumatic injury, or congenital anomalies. This open approach requires a craniotomy to access the cerebellum, followed by careful dissection and excision of the affected tissue. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a team of skilled neurosurgeons and supporting medical staff to minimize potential complications. Post-operative care involves close monitoring to manage potential side effects and ensure optimal recovery.
