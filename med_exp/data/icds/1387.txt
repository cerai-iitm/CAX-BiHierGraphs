Revision of Synthetic Substitute in Lower Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the revision of a synthetic substitute, such as a vascular graft, in the lower artery using an open approach. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow to the affected area by revising or replacing the existing synthetic substitute, which may have become damaged or malfunctioning over time. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the affected artery, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize and repair the synthetic substitute. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor the patient's recovery.
