Ultrasonography of Abdominal Aorta, Intravascular
This non-invasive medical imaging procedure uses high-frequency sound waves to produce detailed images of the abdominal aorta from within the blood vessel. A specialized ultrasound probe is inserted through the skin and into a blood vessel in the arm or leg, then guided to the abdominal aorta to capture images from inside the vessel. The images produced help diagnose and monitor conditions such as abdominal aortic aneurysms, blockages, and blood clots. This intravascular ultrasound provides more detailed and accurate information than traditional ultrasound methods, allowing for more effective treatment and management of vascular diseases.
