Immobilization of Left Lower Arm using Cast
The immobilization procedure involves encasing the left lower arm in a cast to stabilize and support the affected area, allowing for proper healing and recovery. This non-invasive treatment is commonly used to manage fractures, sprains, or strains in the forearm, wrist, or hand, as well as to provide post-operative care following surgical interventions. The cast is carefully molded to the patient's arm to ensure a comfortable and secure fit, allowing for limited mobility while maintaining necessary protection. Throughout the immobilization period, patients are advised to keep the cast clean and dry, and to follow a strict regimen of follow-up appointments to monitor progress and make any necessary adjustments.
