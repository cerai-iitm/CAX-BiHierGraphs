Restriction of Superior Mesenteric Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the percutaneous placement of an intraluminal device to restrict blood flow in the superior mesenteric vein. The goal of the procedure is to reduce blood flow to the small intestine, which can help relieve symptoms associated with certain gastrointestinal conditions. Under fluoroscopic guidance, a catheter is inserted through the skin and advanced to the superior mesenteric vein, where the device is deployed to partially occlude the vessel. The restricted blood flow can help alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, diarrhea, and weight loss.
