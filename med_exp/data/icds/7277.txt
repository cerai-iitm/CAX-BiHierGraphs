Detachment at Left Foot, Complete 2nd Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the 2nd ray, which includes the second toe and its associated metatarsal bone, from the left foot. The operation is performed through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to remove the entire 2nd ray, which may be necessary due to severe trauma, infection, or deformity. The detachment is typically done at the level of the metatarsophalangeal joint, and the surrounding soft tissues are carefully managed to promote optimal healing.
