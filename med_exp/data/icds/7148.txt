Detachment at Right Index Finger, High, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of the right index finger at a high level, typically above the proximal phalanx, using an open approach where the surgeon makes a direct incision in the skin to access the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to remove the damaged or severely injured finger, restoring functionality and alleviating pain or discomfort. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to carefully detach the finger while preserving surrounding tissue and minimizing damage. Following the procedure, the wound is typically closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
