Extirpation of Matter from Ampulla of Vater, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of unwanted material from the ampulla of Vater, a small gland-like structure located at the junction of the pancreatic and bile ducts. Through a natural or artificial opening, a flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted to visualize and extract the obstructing matter. The ampulla of Vater is cleared of debris, allowing for the normal flow of bile and pancreatic juices into the small intestine, relieving symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and jaundice. The minimally invasive approach enables a faster recovery and reduces the risk of complications compared to traditional open surgery.
