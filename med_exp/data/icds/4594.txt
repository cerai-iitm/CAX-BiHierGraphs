Transfer Right Upper Leg Muscle with Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of muscle, skin, and subcutaneous tissue from one location to another on the right upper leg, requiring an open approach to access the affected area. The goal of this complex procedure is to restore function, improve aesthetics, and promote healing in the upper leg. The surgeon will carefully dissect and mobilize the muscle, skin, and subcutaneous tissue, and then reattach it to the new location using sutures or other fixation devices. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the operative site, enabling the surgeon to precisely transfer the tissue and minimize potential complications.
