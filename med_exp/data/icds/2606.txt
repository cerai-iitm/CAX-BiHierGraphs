Bypass Stomach to Jejunum, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical connection of the stomach to the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, through an open incision in the abdomen, allowing food to bypass the duodenum and proximal jejunum. The goal of this surgery is to redirect the flow of food and digestive enzymes, often to treat conditions such as gastric cancer, duodenal ulcer, or intestinal obstruction. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the abdominal cavity and perform the bypass procedure under direct vision. This complex operation requires general anesthesia and a hospital stay, and may be performed in conjunction with other procedures to ensure adequate nutrient absorption and digestion.
