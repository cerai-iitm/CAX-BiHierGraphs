Transfer Right Trunk Muscle with Skin and Subcutaneous Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of the right trunk muscle, along with its associated skin and subcutaneous tissue, to a new location on the body. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the muscle and surrounding tissue, which are then carefully dissected and mobilized for transfer. The goal of this procedure is to restore function, augment strength, or improve mobility in the affected area, often as a result of trauma, injury, or congenital defect. The transferred muscle and tissue are then secured in place, allowing for a stable and functional outcome.
