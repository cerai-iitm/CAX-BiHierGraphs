Extraction of Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the stomach. The endoscope allows the healthcare provider to directly observe the stomach lining and remove a tissue sample or foreign object for further examination. The procedure is typically performed under sedation to ensure patient comfort, and the endoscope is navigated through the throat or an existing incision to access the stomach. The diagnostic extraction of stomach tissue or content helps identify conditions such as ulcers, inflammation, or cancer, enabling accurate diagnosis and treatment.
