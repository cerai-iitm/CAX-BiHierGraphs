Fluoroscopy of Left Kidney, Ureter and Bladder using Low Osmolar Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left kidney, ureter, and bladder, utilizing low osmolar contrast material to enhance imaging. The contrast agent is injected into a vein to highlight the urinary tract, allowing for real-time X-ray images to be taken during the examination. The fluoroscopic guidance enables the healthcare provider to observe the flow of the contrast material through the urinary system, facilitating the diagnosis of any abnormalities or blockages. The low osmolar contrast used in this procedure is a safer and more tolerable option for patients, reducing the risk of adverse reactions and allergic responses.
