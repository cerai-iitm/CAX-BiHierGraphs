Extirpation of Matter from Right Femoral Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This is a minimally invasive procedure where a catheter is inserted through the skin and into the right femoral artery to remove undesirable material, such as blood clots, plaque, or other substances, that may be obstructing blood flow. The percutaneous approach allows for a smaller incision and reduces the risk of complications compared to open surgery. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the catheter is navigated to the affected area, and the unwanted material is extracted, restoring blood flow to the affected limb. The procedure is typically performed in a catheterization laboratory or interventional radiology suite under local anesthesia and moderate sedation.
