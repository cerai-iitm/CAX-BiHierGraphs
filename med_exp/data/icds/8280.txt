Beam Radiation of Axillary Lymphatics using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This procedure involves the use of high-energy photon beams to target and treat axillary lymph nodes, which are located in the armpit region. The photon beams, which have energies ranging from 1 to 10 MeV, are carefully directed to the lymph nodes to destroy cancer cells. The precise delivery of the photon beams is achieved through advanced radiation therapy techniques, ensuring minimal damage to surrounding healthy tissue. This non-invasive procedure is often used in conjunction with other cancer treatments to improve patient outcomes and reduce the risk of recurrence.
