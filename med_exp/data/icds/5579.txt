Drainage of Right Shoulder Joint, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small needle or tube through the skin to drain excess fluid or infected material from the right shoulder joint. Guided by imaging technology, the surgeon targets the affected area to collect the unwanted substance, alleviating pressure and promoting healing. The percutaneous approach avoids open surgery, reducing the risk of complications and scarring. Following the procedure, the patient may receive antibiotics or physical therapy to aid in recovery.
