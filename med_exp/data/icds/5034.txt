Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Thoracic Vertebra, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an open incision in the thoracic spine to access the internal fixation device, which is typically composed of rods, screws, and plates, that was previously implanted to stabilize a fracture or correct a spinal deformity. The device is carefully removed, taking caution not to damage surrounding tissues or disrupt the integrity of the spine. The surgeon may use specialized instruments, such as screwdrivers and pliers, to disassemble and extract the device. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for any potential complications.
