Reposition Right Upper Femur with Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves repositioning the right upper femur to its normal anatomic position using a percutaneous approach, which reduces tissue damage and promotes faster healing. The procedure is typically performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scans, to ensure accurate placement of the internal fixation device. The internal fixation device, such as a screw or plate, is inserted through small incisions to stabilize the bone and facilitate healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms in patients with femur fractures or deformities.
