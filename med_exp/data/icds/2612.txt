Bypass Duodenum to Jejunum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass from the duodenum to the jejunum using an open approach, where an incision is made in the abdomen to access the small intestine. The bypass allows food to flow directly from the duodenum to the jejunum, bypassing a diseased or obstructed portion of the small intestine. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the organs and tissues involved, facilitating the creation of the bypass. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as duodenal cancer, injury, or obstruction, and can help restore normal digestion and nutrient absorption.
