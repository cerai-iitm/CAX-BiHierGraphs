Inspection of Upper Intestinal Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves a visual examination of the upper intestinal tract, which includes the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum, to diagnose and evaluate conditions such as ulcers, bleeding, or gastrointestinal tumors. The inspection is performed through a natural or artificial opening, using a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, allowing the healthcare provider to visually examine the internal lining of the upper intestinal tract. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and cooperation. The diagnostic information obtained from this procedure helps guide further treatment and management of gastrointestinal disorders.
