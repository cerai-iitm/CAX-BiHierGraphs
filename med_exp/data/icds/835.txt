Occlusion of Left Vertebral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the use of an intraluminal device to occlude the left vertebral artery, effectively blocking blood flow through the vessel. The device is inserted through a small incision in the skin, using a percutaneous approach, minimizing tissue damage and promoting quicker recovery. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as vertebral artery aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or fistulas, which can cause bleeding, seizures, or stroke. By occluding the affected artery, the risk of further complications can be significantly reduced, improving patient outcomes.
