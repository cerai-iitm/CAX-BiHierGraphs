Insertion of Infusion Device into Right Radial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small infusion device through the skin and into the right radial artery, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or other substances directly into the arterial circulation. The percutaneous approach involves making a small incision in the skin and using imaging guidance to accurately place the device. The radial artery is accessed in the forearm, just above the wrist, and the device is carefully inserted through the artery wall to avoid damaging surrounding tissues. The infusion device is then secured in place, allowing for continuous or intermittent delivery of therapeutic agents as needed.
