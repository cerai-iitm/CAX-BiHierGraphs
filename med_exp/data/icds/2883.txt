Excision of Descending Colon, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the descending colon through a small incision using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope is inserted through the skin and guided to the site where the descending colon needs to be excised. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as cancer, diverticulitis, or inflammatory bowel disease. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for less tissue trauma, reduced risk of complications, and faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery.
