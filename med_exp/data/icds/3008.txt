Inspection of Stomach, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves inserting an endoscope through the skin to visually examine the stomach, allowing for direct observation of the gastric mucosa and any potential abnormalities. The percutaneous approach enables the insertion of the endoscope through a small incision in the abdomen, reducing the need for invasive surgical procedures. During the procedure, the endoscope is advanced through the abdominal wall and into the stomach, providing high-definition images of the gastric lining and allowing for biopsy or removal of any lesions or abnormal tissue. The entire procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation, ensuring patient comfort and minimizing recovery time.
