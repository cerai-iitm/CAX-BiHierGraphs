Drainage of Left Ankle Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small instrument or needle through the skin to drain fluid or other substances from the left ankle joint. The percutaneous approach means that the procedure does not require a large incision, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The diagnostic nature of the procedure implies that it is performed to gather information about the joint, such as identifying the cause of inflammation or infection. The goal of the procedure is to relieve pressure and reduce discomfort in the ankle joint, as well as to aid in the diagnosis and treatment of underlying conditions.
