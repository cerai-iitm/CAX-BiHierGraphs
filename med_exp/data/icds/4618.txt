Drainage of Right Hand Tendon, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to drain a tendon in the right hand, which is typically performed to relieve tension or pressure caused by inflammation, infection, or trauma. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected tendon and surrounding tissues, ensuring accurate identification and treatment of the affected area. The procedure may involve making an incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the tendon, and then draining the accumulated fluid or debris. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is advised to follow a rehabilitation program to regain strength and mobility in the affected hand.
