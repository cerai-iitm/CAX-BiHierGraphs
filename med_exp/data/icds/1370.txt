Restriction of Right Internal Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through the skin to restrict blood flow in the right internal iliac artery. The device is typically a stent or an occlusive coil that is guided to the target site using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. Once in place, the device reduces blood flow to the affected area, which can help manage conditions such as bleeding, aneurysms, or arteriovenous malformations. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to open surgical procedures.
