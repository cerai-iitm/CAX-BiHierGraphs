Insertion of Internal Fixation Device into Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the lumbar vertebral joint, where a internal fixation device is carefully inserted to stabilize and support the joint. The internal fixation device, such as a plate, rod, or screw, is designed to help restore proper alignment and alleviate pain and discomfort. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, ensuring precise placement of the device. The goal of this procedure is to provide long-term stability and promote healing in the affected joint, improving overall spinal function and mobility.
