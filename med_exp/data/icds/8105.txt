Fluoroscopy of Other Intra-Abdominal Arteries using Other Contrast
Here is the detailed description:

This imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to examine other intra-abdominal arteries. During the procedure, a contrast agent is administered through a catheter inserted into an artery, highlighting the blood vessels and aiding visualization. The fluoroscope produces continuous X-ray images, allowing the healthcare provider to guide the procedure and capture images of the arteries. The resulting images help diagnose and treat conditions affecting the abdominal arteries, such as blockages or aneurysms.
