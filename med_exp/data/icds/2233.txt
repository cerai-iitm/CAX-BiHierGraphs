Excision of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of the left lower lung lobe through a small incision in the skin, utilizing image-guided technology for accuracy. The percutaneous approach allows for reduced recovery time and less scarring compared to open surgery. During the procedure, a small camera and specialized instruments are inserted through the incision to visualize and remove the affected lung lobe. The diagnostic nature of this procedure enables physicians to examine the excised tissue for further evaluation and diagnosis of underlying conditions.
