Excision of Descending Colon, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the descending colon through an endoscopic approach, using a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end to visualize the colon. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the surgeon to access the descending colon without the need for a large incision. The endoscope is guided through the colon, and specialized instruments are used to excise the affected portion of the descending colon. The excised tissue is then removed through the endoscope or a small incision, allowing for a minimally invasive and diagnostic procedure.
