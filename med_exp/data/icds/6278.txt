Dilation of Bilateral Ureters with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the ureters and insert a device to dilate them through a small incision in the skin. The device is carefully guided through the ureters to widen the narrow passages and restore normal urine flow. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery and less scarring compared to open surgery. The goal of this procedure is to effectively manage ureteral stenosis or obstruction, relieving symptoms such as flank pain, nausea, and vomiting.
