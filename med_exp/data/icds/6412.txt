Release Bladder, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the bladder, and then manually releasing any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstacles that may be obstructing the bladder's normal function. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bladder and surrounding anatomy, enabling a more precise release of the bladder. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bladder function, alleviate symptoms such as urinary retention or incontinence, and improve overall urinary health. By releasing the bladder, the surgeon can help restore normal urinary flow and alleviate any discomfort or pain associated with bladder obstruction.
