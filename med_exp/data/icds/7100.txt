Repair Abdominal Wall, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgery utilizes an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through small incisions to visually examine and repair abdominal wall defects or weaknesses. The percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery time and less scarring compared to traditional open surgery. During the procedure, the surgeon uses specialized instruments to reinforce the abdominal wall with mesh or sutures, restoring its natural strength and function. The entire process is performed under continuous visualization, enabling precise and effective repair with minimal tissue damage.
