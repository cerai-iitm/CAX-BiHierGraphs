Extirpation of Matter from Nasopharynx, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the removal of unwanted material or substances from the nasopharynx, which is the upper part of the throat that lies behind the nose. The extirpation process is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing for the extraction of foreign bodies, debris, or other obstructions that may be causing respiratory issues or discomfort. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal breathing and nasal function by clearing the nasal passages and sinuses of any blockages or obstructions. The surgical procedure may be performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health.
