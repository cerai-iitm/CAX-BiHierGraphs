Reposition Upper Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the upper vein, which has become displaced or misaligned. The surgeon then manually repositions the vein to its normal anatomical location to restore proper blood flow. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately reposition the vein. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate symptoms such as pain, swelling, and bruising associated with vein misalignment.
