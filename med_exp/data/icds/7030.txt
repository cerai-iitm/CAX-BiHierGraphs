Inspection of Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a thin, lighted tube with a camera, called an endoscope, through a small incision in the abdominal wall to visually examine the peritoneal cavity. The peritoneal cavity is a space between the abdominal wall and organs that contains fluid and can be affected by various conditions such as infection, inflammation, or cancer. During the procedure, the endoscope is guided through the peritoneal cavity to identify any abnormalities, such as adhesions, tumors, or fluid accumulation, and to collect tissue or fluid samples for further examination. The entire process is usually performed under local anesthesia, and it can help in diagnosing and managing various peritoneal disorders.
