Excision of Vagina, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal or perineal area to access the vagina, followed by the careful dissection and removal of the entire vagina or a portion of it. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a temporary diversion of the urinary system to prevent injury. The goal of the surgery is to treat conditions such as vaginal cancer, severe vaginal injuries, or birth defects that affect the structure and function of the vagina.
