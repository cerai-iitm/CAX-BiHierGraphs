Dilation of Left Internal Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves using a small incision in the groin or wrist area to access the left internal carotid artery. A thin, flexible tube called a catheter is guided through the artery to the blockage site, where a small intraluminal device is deployed to widen the narrowed artery. The device is then inflated to dilate the artery, restoring blood flow to the brain. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may take around 30 minutes to an hour to complete.
