Reposition Left Zygomatic Bone with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves manually repositioning the left zygomatic bone to its normal anatomical position, often due to facial trauma or deformity. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and manipulate the bone. An internal fixation device, such as a plate or screw, is then used to stabilize the bone in its new position, promoting proper healing and alignment. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural contours of the face and improve overall facial functionality.
