Drainage of Right Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right upper leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of connective tissue that surround muscles and other structures. The goal of the procedure is to drain excess fluid, blood, or other substances that may have accumulated in these layers, causing infection, swelling, or other complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area and remove any offending materials, promoting healing and restoring normal function to the leg. By draining the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, the risk of further complications can be reduced, and the patient can recover more quickly.
