Release Right Toe Phalangeal Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves cutting open the skin and soft tissue to access the right toe phalangeal joint, with the goal of releasing the joint from surrounding tissue or adhesions that may be causing stiffness or pain. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding anatomy, ensuring a more precise release of the joint. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as toe deformities, arthritis, or traumatic injuries that have resulted in joint stiffness or limited mobility. By releasing the joint, the surgeon can help to restore normal movement and function to the toe.
