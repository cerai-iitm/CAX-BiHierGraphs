Extirpation of Matter from Transverse Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the abdomen to access the transverse colon, a portion of the large intestine. The colon is then carefully opened to allow for the removal of unwanted matter, such as infected tissue, foreign objects, or other obstructions. The affected area is thoroughly cleaned and flushed to remove any remaining debris, and the colon is closed in a way that promotes proper healing. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the colon and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely target the affected area and minimize damage to surrounding structures.
