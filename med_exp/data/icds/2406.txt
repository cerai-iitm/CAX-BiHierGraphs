Resection of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves using an endoscope inserted through a small incision in the skin to visualize and remove the right upper lung lobe. The percutaneous approach allows for a less traumatic experience and faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and may involve the use of video-assisted thoracoscopic surgery (VATS) to aid in visualization. The resected lung tissue is then removed through the incision site, allowing for a thorough removal of the affected lobe.
