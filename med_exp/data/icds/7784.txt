Measurement of Cardiac Sampling and Pressure, Left Heart, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a catheter through the skin and into a blood vessel to measure pressures and collect samples from the left heart chambers. The catheter is guided through the vasculature to the left heart, allowing for the measurement of cardiac pressures and the collection of blood samples from the left heart chambers. This procedure is typically performed in a cardiac catheterization laboratory or electrophysiology laboratory under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance. The collected data and samples provide valuable information for diagnosing and treating various cardiac conditions, including heart failure, cardiomyopathy, and valvular heart disease.
