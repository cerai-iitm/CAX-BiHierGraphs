Bypass Trachea to Cutaneous with Tracheostomy Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A percutaneous endoscopic tracheostomy is a minimally invasive surgical procedure in which a tracheostomy tube is inserted through the skin and into the trachea using an endoscope for visual guidance. This bypasses the upper airway, allowing for the passage of air directly from the trachea to the outside of the body through the tracheostomy tube. The procedure is typically performed in critically ill patients who require prolonged mechanical ventilation, helping to improve respiratory function and reduce the risk of respiratory failure. After the procedure, the tracheostomy tube is secured in place and attached to a ventilator or oxygen source to assist with breathing.
