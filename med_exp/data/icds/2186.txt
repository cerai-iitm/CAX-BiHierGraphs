Excision of Left Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of a portion of the left main bronchus, which is the primary airway that branches off the trachea and leads to the left lung. The procedure is performed endoscopically, where a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, such as the mouth or a tracheostomy site. The bronchoscope allows the surgeon to visualize the area and remove the affected portion of the bronchus using specialized instruments. This diagnostic procedure is typically performed to examine and biopsy abnormal tissue in the left main bronchus.
