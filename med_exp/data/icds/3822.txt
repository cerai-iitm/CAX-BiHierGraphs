Replacement of Right Hand Skin with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Full Thickness, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the skin on the right hand with a full-thickness autologous tissue substitute, which is a graft composed of the patient's own tissue, taken from another area of their body. The external approach is used to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to remove the damaged skin and replace it with the substitute tissue. The goal of this procedure is to restore the natural appearance and function of the right hand, improving mobility and reducing discomfort. The autologous tissue substitute promotes natural healing and reduces the risk of rejection or complications associated with synthetic materials.
