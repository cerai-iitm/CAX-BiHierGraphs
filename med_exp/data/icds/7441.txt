Abortion of Products of Conception, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This medical procedure involves the removal of pregnancy tissue or products of conception from the uterus through a natural or artificial opening, a process commonly referred to as a miscarriage management or abortion. The method can be performed through the vagina or abdomen, depending on the stage of pregnancy and individual circumstances. The goal of the procedure is to empty the uterus and prevent potential complications, such as heavy bleeding or infection. It may be performed under local anesthesia, conscious sedation, or general anesthesia, and is usually carried out in a clinical setting by a healthcare provider or obstetrician.
