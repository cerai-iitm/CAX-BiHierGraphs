Bypass Sigmoid Colon to Cutaneous, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a detour in the sigmoid colon, redirecting it to an artificial opening on the skin, allowing waste to be eliminated from the body through the newly created stoma. The open approach requires a large incision in the abdomen, giving the surgeon direct visualization and access to the affected area. During the procedure, the sigmoid colon is carefully disconnected from its usual path and reattached to the skin, forming a colostomy. The resulting stoma allows patients to pass stool through the abdominal wall, bypassing the normal anal route, often in cases where the colon has been severely damaged or is obstructed.
