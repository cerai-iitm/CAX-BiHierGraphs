Computerized Tomography (CT Scan) of Intracranial Arteries
This non-invasive diagnostic imaging test uses X-rays and computer technology to produce detailed cross-sectional images of the intracranial arteries, which supply oxygenated blood to the brain. During the procedure, the patient lies still on a table that slides into a large, doughnut-shaped machine, which rotates around their head to capture images from multiple angles. The resulting high-resolution images help healthcare providers diagnose and monitor conditions such as stroke, aneurysms, and arteriovenous malformations. The test is typically performed in a hospital or outpatient setting and takes around 30 minutes to an hour to complete.
