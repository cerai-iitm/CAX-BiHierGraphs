Repair Left Diaphragm, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During this procedure, a minimally invasive approach is used to repair the left diaphragm through a small incision in the abdomen. An endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, is inserted through the incision to visualize the diaphragm and guide the repair. The surgeon then uses specialized instruments to repair the damaged area of the diaphragm, which may involve suturing or patching the defect. The entire procedure is performed under general anesthesia, and patients typically require a few days of hospitalization for recovery.
