Fluoroscopy of Right Internal Carotid Artery using Other Contrast
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

During this imaging procedure, a contrast agent is administered to enhance visibility of the right internal carotid artery. A continuous X-ray beam, known as fluoroscopy, is then used to guide the imaging process, allowing for real-time visualization of the artery. The contrast agent helps to highlight the anatomy of the artery, enabling the detection of any abnormalities, stenosis, or blockages. The resulting images provide valuable diagnostic information for the healthcare provider to assess blood flow and diagnose conditions affecting the right internal carotid artery.
