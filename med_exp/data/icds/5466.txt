Reposition Right Tibia with Monoplanar External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves the realignment of the right tibia, also known as the shinbone, to its normal anatomical position using a monoplanar external fixation device. The device is applied through a percutaneous approach, where small incisions are made in the skin to allow for the insertion of the fixation pins. The monoplanar external fixator is designed to provide stabilization and support to the bone while it heals, allowing for early mobilization and promoting optimal bone growth. The goal of this procedure is to restore proper alignment, relieve pain, and improve overall mobility and function in the affected leg.
