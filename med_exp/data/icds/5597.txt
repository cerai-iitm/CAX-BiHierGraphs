Drainage of Right Wrist Joint, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
During this diagnostic procedure, a needle is inserted through the skin and into the right wrist joint to drain excess fluid or debris. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive method to access the joint, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster healing. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The extracted fluid or tissue samples are then sent to a laboratory for further examination to aid in diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the wrist joint.
