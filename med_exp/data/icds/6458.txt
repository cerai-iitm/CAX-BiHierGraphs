Resection of Left Ureter, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left ureter, a tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder, through an open approach. The surgery is typically performed to treat conditions such as ureteral cancer, injury, or severe obstruction that cannot be resolved with other treatments. The procedure requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the ureter, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect the area and remove the affected portion. The goal of the resection is to restore normal urinary function and alleviate associated symptoms such as pain, infection, or bleeding.
