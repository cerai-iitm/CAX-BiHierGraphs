Resection of Spleen, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the spleen, a vital organ located in the upper left region of the abdomen, just below the diaphragm. The spleen is then carefully dissected and separated from the surrounding tissues and blood vessels, after which it is removed from the body. The spleen plays a crucial role in filtering the blood and storing red blood cells, but it can become damaged or diseased, requiring its removal to alleviate symptoms and prevent further complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the area and directly access the spleen, ensuring a safe and effective removal.
