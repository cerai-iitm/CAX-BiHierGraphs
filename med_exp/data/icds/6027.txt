Release Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves an open approach to release the right knee joint, which is performed to treat conditions such as joint contracture, stiffness, or scar tissue formation that limit mobility and cause pain. The procedure typically involves a surgical incision to access the joint, followed by the release of the contracted tissue or removal of scar tissue to restore normal joint function. The goal of the procedure is to improve range of motion, reduce pain, and enhance overall knee function. Post-operatively, physical therapy is often necessary to restore strength and mobility to the affected joint.
