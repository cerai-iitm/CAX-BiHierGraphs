Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Ceramic on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the right hip joint with a ceramic on polyethylene synthetic substitute, utilizing an uncemented open approach. The operation is typically performed to alleviate severe hip pain, restore mobility, and improve quality of life for patients with damaged or arthritic hip joints. Through an open incision, the surgeon removes the damaged or diseased joint and implants the synthetic ceramic on polyethylene prosthetic, which is designed to mimic the natural hip joint. The uncemented approach allows the prosthetic to bond with the surrounding bone, promoting a secure and stable fit.
