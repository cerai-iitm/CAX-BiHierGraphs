Drainage of Bilateral Lungs, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end through a natural or artificial opening in the chest wall to drain fluid or air from both lungs. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure the patient's comfort. During the procedure, the healthcare provider uses the endoscope to visualize the lungs and identify the area of fluid or air accumulation, and then inserts a drainage tube or device to remove the fluid or air. The goal of the procedure is to relieve respiratory distress and improve oxygenation by re-expanding the lungs.
