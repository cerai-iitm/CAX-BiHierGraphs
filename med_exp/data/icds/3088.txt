Removal of Feeding Device from Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During this minimally invasive procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light at the end, called an endoscope, is inserted through the mouth or a previously created opening to access the stomach. The endoscope allows the healthcare provider to visualize the feeding device, a tube used to provide nutrition, inside the stomach. The provider then carefully removes the feeding device from the stomach using specialized instruments passed through the endoscope, avoiding the need for open surgery. The entire procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote a quick recovery.
