Extirpation of Matter from Left Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the mouth or nose to reach the left main bronchus, allowing for visualization of the airway and removal of foreign matter or debris. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. Using specialized instruments, the physician carefully extracts the obstructing material, restoring normal airflow and alleviating symptoms such as coughing, wheezing, or shortness of breath. The endoscopic approach enables a precise and targeted removal of the matter, minimizing trauma to surrounding tissues and promoting a faster recovery.
