Destruction of Bladder, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the bladder through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the healthcare provider to precisely identify and target abnormal tissue or lesions. Once identified, a specialized instrument or energy source is used to destroy the unwanted tissue, ensuring a thorough and targeted approach to treatment. The endoscopic approach minimizes trauma to surrounding tissues and reduces the risk of complications compared to traditional open surgical methods. The goal of this procedure is to effectively treat conditions such as bladder cancer, tumors, or other abnormalities, promoting optimal bladder function and patient outcomes.
