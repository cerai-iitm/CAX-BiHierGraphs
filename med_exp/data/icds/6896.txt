Drainage of Pericardial Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to drain the pericardial cavity, a sac surrounding the heart, using a percutaneous endoscopic approach. This involves making a small incision in the chest wall, through which an endoscope and a drainage device are inserted to access the pericardial cavity. The drainage device is then used to remove excess fluid or blood that may be accumulating in the cavity, which can help to relieve pressure on the heart and alleviate symptoms such as chest pain and shortness of breath. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the drainage device.
