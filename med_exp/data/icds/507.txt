Occlusion of Right Pulmonary Vein with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small intraluminal device through a small incision in the skin, guided by imaging technology, to occlude the right pulmonary vein, thereby blocking the flow of blood through it. The device is designed to reduce blood flow to the affected area, alleviating symptoms such as shortness of breath and fatigue. The percutaneous approach allows for a faster recovery time and minimal scarring compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, and the patient is usually able to return home the same day.
