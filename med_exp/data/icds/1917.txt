Destruction of Nasal Mucosa and Soft Tissue, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to visualize the nasal passages and identify the affected area of mucosa and soft tissue. Through a small incision, a specialized instrument is inserted to destroy the targeted tissue, which is typically performed to treat conditions such as nasal polyps or tumors. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal trauma to surrounding tissue and reduces the risk of complications. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia, and patients typically experience minimal discomfort and rapid recovery.
