Resection of Right Middle Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This percutaneous endoscopic procedure involves the surgical removal of the middle lobe of the right lung through a minimally invasive approach. During the procedure, an endoscope is inserted through a small incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the lung tissue and remove the affected lobe. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open surgery. The resection of the right middle lung lobe is typically performed to treat conditions such as lung cancer, emphysema, or severe lung damage.
