Excision of Cervical Spinal Cord, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to remove a sample of tissue from the cervical spinal cord for diagnostic purposes. The procedure involves making a small incision in the skin and inserting a specialized instrument through the incision to access the spinal cord. The instrument is guided by imaging technology, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure precise placement. A small sample of tissue is then removed and sent to a laboratory for further examination to help diagnose conditions affecting the spinal cord.
