Extirpation of Matter from Pelvic Region Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the pelvic region to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where abnormal or pathological matter is removed. The goal is to eliminate the unwanted material, which may be causing infection, inflammation, or other complications. The surgeon carefully dissects the affected tissue to locate and extract the problematic matter, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding healthy tissue. By extirpating the unwanted material, the procedure aims to promote healing, reduce symptoms, and improve overall health outcomes in the patient.
