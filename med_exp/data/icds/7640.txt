Introduction of Vasopressor into Central Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to introduce a vasopressor medication into a central vein, typically located in the chest or neck area, to help regulate blood pressure. The percutaneous approach involves making a small puncture in the skin to access the central vein, rather than making a larger incision. A specialized catheter is inserted through the puncture site and guided to the central vein, allowing the vasopressor medication to be delivered directly into the bloodstream. This procedure is often performed in an intensive care unit or other critical care setting to closely monitor the patient's condition and adjust the medication as needed.
