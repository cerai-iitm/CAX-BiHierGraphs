Control Bleeding in Genitourinary Tract, Open Approach
Surgical intervention is performed to stop bleeding in the genitourinary tract, which includes organs such as the kidneys, bladder, ureters, and urethra. An open approach is used, where a incision is made to directly visualize the affected area and control the source of bleeding. This may involve ligating or cauterizing blood vessels, repairing injured tissues, or removing bleeding tumors or lesions. The goal of the procedure is to restore hemostasis and prevent further blood loss, while also addressing any underlying conditions that may be contributing to the bleeding.
