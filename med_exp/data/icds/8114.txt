Fluoroscopy of Right Lower Extremity Arteries
This radiographic procedure involves the use of continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time images of the right lower extremity arteries, allowing for visualization of blood flow and vessel morphology. The fluoroscopy machine is positioned to capture images of the targeted area, which may include the iliac, femoral, popliteal, and tibial arteries. During the procedure, a contrast agent may be injected into the bloodstream to enhance image quality and facilitate diagnosis. The resulting images enable healthcare professionals to identify potential blockages, aneurysms, or other abnormalities that may be affecting blood flow to the lower right leg.
