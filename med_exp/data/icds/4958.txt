Excision of Thoracic Vertebra, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the thoracic region to access the affected vertebra, which is then carefully excised to treat conditions such as spinal tumors, infections, or deformities. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the vertebra and surrounding tissues, enabling precise removal of the affected segment. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed by an orthopedic or neurosurgical team in a hospital setting. After the excision, the incision is closed, and the patient may require postoperative rehabilitation to facilitate recovery and restore spinal stability.
