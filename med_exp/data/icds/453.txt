Insertion of Defibrillator Lead into Right Atrium, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert a defibrillator lead into the right atrium through a small incision in the chest. This percutaneous approach allows the lead to be guided through a vein and into the heart, minimizing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. Once in place, the lead is connected to a defibrillator device that monitors the heart's rhythm and delivers electrical shocks when necessary to restore a normal heartbeat. The entire procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate lead placement and minimize potential complications.
