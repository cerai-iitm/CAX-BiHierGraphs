Resection of Right Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the removal of the right lower lung lobe through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach, utilizing an endoscope inserted through a small incision in the chest wall to visualize the lung tissue. The surgery is typically performed under general anesthesia and requires a thorough evaluation of the patient's overall health before the operation. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional open-chest surgery. This procedure is often used to treat conditions such as lung cancer, pulmonary embolism, or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) that affect the right lower lung lobe.
