Insertion of Cardiac Resynchronization Defibrillator Pulse Generator into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the chest area to create a pocket in the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, a layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles. A cardiac resynchronization defibrillator pulse generator is then inserted into this pocket, which will regulate the heartbeat and prevent abnormal rhythms. The device is connected to lead wires that are positioned in the heart, allowing it to monitor and respond to the heart's rhythms. The incision is then closed, and the device is programmed to provide optimal therapy for the patient.
