Fluoroscopy of Single Coronary Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This medical imaging procedure uses low osmolar contrast to visualize a single coronary artery, providing high-quality images of the artery's structure and blood flow. During the procedure, a small amount of contrast agent is injected into the coronary artery, and fluoroscopy, a type of real-time X-ray imaging, is used to capture dynamic images of the artery. The low osmolar contrast agent helps to reduce the risk of adverse reactions and improve image quality. The resulting images enable healthcare professionals to diagnose and treat conditions such as coronary artery disease, including blockages or narrowing of the artery.
