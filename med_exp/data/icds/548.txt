Repair Left Ventricle, Open Approach
This procedure involves an open approach to access the left ventricle, which is the lower chamber of the heart responsible for pumping oxygenated blood to the rest of the body. The surgical repair is performed to correct defects or damage to the left ventricle, such as ventricular aneurysms, septal defects, or tears. The surgeon makes an incision in the chest to expose the heart and performs the necessary repair, which may involve removing or replacing damaged tissue, patching holes or tears, or reshaping the ventricle to improve its function. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal heart function, improve cardiac output, and alleviate symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, and fatigue.
