Fluoroscopy of Right Kidney using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the right kidney in real-time, allowing for continuous monitoring during the examination. A contrast agent, other than the standard iodine or barium-based dyes, is administered to enhance visibility of the kidney's internal structures. The fluoroscope produces continuous X-ray beams that create a live image of the kidney, enabling the physician to guide diagnostic or therapeutic procedures. The resulting images provide valuable information on kidney function, anatomy, and any potential abnormalities or diseases affecting the organ.
