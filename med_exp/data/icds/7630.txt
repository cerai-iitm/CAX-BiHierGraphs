Introduction of Anti-inflammatory into Central Vein, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a thin needle is inserted through the skin and into a central vein, typically in the chest or neck, under imaging guidance. A steroid medication is then injected through the needle into the vein to reduce inflammation and swelling. This procedure is often used to treat conditions such as allergic reactions, asthma, and rheumatoid arthritis. The percutaneous approach allows for a quick and relatively painless treatment, and the medication is delivered directly to the affected area for fast relief.
