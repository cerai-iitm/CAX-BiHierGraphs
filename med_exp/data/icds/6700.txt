Resection of Cervix, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the cervix, the lower part of the uterus that opens into the vagina, through either a natural opening, such as the vagina, or an artificial opening created during surgery. The cervical resection is typically performed to treat cervical cancer, precancerous lesions, or other benign conditions affecting the cervix. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the cervix and carefully removes the affected tissue, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissues and structures. The goal of the procedure is to remove all cancerous or abnormal tissue, while preserving as much healthy tissue as possible.
