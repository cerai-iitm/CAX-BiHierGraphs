Inspection of Sinus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visualize the sinuses through a small incision in the skin. The procedure allows for a thorough examination of the sinus cavity, including the nasal passages and surrounding bones, to diagnose and treat conditions such as chronic sinusitis, nasal polyps, or tumors. During the procedure, the endoscope is carefully guided through the skin and soft tissue to access the sinus cavity, providing a clear view of the area for the healthcare provider. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize discomfort and promote a smooth recovery.
