Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Right Knee Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of synthetic substitute material from the right knee joint through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. The synthetic substitute, such as a prosthetic device or implant, is carefully extracted to restore normal knee function and alleviate any associated pain or complications. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to precisely remove the substitute material and address any related issues. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient typically undergoes a period of rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility in the affected knee.
