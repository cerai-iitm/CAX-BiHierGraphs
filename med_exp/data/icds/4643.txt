Excision of Right Foot Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skin of the right foot to access the affected tendon. The damaged or diseased portion of the tendon is then carefully excised, or removed, to alleviate symptoms such as pain, stiffness, and limited mobility. The surgical site is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the foot may be immobilized in a cast or splint to allow the tendon to heal properly. Following the procedure, patients may require physical therapy to restore strength and range of motion in the affected foot.
