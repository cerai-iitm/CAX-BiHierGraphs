Revision of Intraluminal Device in Hepatobiliary Duct, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves revising an existing intraluminal device, such as a stent or catheter, within the hepatobiliary duct system through a percutaneous approach, which means accessing the ducts through the skin using imaging guidance. The goal of the procedure is to restore adequate bile flow and alleviate symptoms such as jaundice, itching, and abdominal pain. The revision may involve removing or replacing the existing device, or adjusting its position to ensure proper function. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery.
