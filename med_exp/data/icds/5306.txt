Insertion of Monoplanar External Fixation Device into Right Tibia, Percutaneous Approach
During this procedure, a monoplanar external fixation device is inserted into the right tibia through a percutaneous approach, which involves making small incisions in the skin to minimize tissue damage. The device is carefully positioned to stabilize and align the bone, promoting healing and correcting deformities. The procedure is often performed under image guidance, such as fluoroscopy or Computed Tomography (CT), to ensure accurate placement of the device. The external fixation device remains in place until the bone has fully healed, at which point it is removed, allowing for full recovery and mobilization of the affected limb.
