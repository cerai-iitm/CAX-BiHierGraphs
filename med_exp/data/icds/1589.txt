Extirpation of Matter from Splenic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of unwanted material or substances from the splenic vein, a vital vessel that carries blood from the spleen to the liver, through a small puncture in the skin. A catheter or specialized instrument is guided through the access point to reach the splenic vein, where it is used to extract the unwanted material. The procedure is typically performed under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or CT scan, to ensure accuracy and safety. By extracting the unwanted material, this procedure can help alleviate symptoms and prevent potential complications associated with blockages or occlusions in the splenic vein.
