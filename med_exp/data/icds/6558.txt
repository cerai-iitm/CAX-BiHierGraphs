Excision of Cul-de-sac, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision to access the cul-de-sac, a region in the peritoneal cavity located between the uterus and rectum. The operation is typically performed to remove abnormal growths, such as tumors or cysts, or to treat conditions like endometriosis that affect the cul-de-sac. The surgeon carefully excises the affected tissue or lesion, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding structures and organs. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms, restore normal anatomy, and improve quality of life for the patient.
