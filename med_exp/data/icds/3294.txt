Dilation of Right Hepatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure based on the code and long title:

This endoscopic procedure involves the dilation of the right hepatic duct, which is a tube that carries bile from the liver to the gallbladder, to relieve blockages or constriction. The dilation is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing a flexible tube with a camera and specialized instruments to access the duct. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bile flow and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, jaundice, and fever. By widening the duct, the healthcare provider can remove blockages or gallstones and improve the overall function of the liver and gallbladder.
