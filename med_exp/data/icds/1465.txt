Insertion of Infusion Device into Left Cephalic Vein, Percutaneous Approach
Here is the description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an infusion device through the skin and into the left cephalic vein, which is a superficial vein located in the upper arm. The device is inserted using a percutaneous approach, meaning it is guided through the skin using a needle and guidewire. The infusion device is typically a small, flexible tube that allows for the delivery of fluids, medications, or nutrients directly into the vein. The left cephalic vein is a common site for infusion device insertion due to its accessibility and proximity to the surface of the skin.
