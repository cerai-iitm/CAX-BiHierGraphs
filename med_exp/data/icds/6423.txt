Removal of Intraluminal Device from Ureter, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves using an endoscope to remove an intraluminal device, such as a stent or catheter, from the ureter through a natural or artificial opening. The ureter is a narrow tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder, and the intraluminal device is typically inserted to drain urine or relieve blockages. During the procedure, a specialized endoscope is guided through the urethra or a small incision to access the ureter, allowing the device to be carefully retrieved. The removal of the device can help alleviate symptoms such as flank pain, urinary retention, and hematuria, and can also reduce the risk of complications associated with the device.
