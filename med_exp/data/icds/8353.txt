Extirpation of Matter from Coronary Artery, One Artery using Orbital Atherectomy Technology, Percutaneous Approach, New Technology Group 1
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of orbital atherectomy technology to remove plaque and other obstructing matter from a single coronary artery. During the procedure, a small catheter is guided through an artery in the leg or arm to the blocked coronary artery, where a spinning crown Covered with diamond-coated surfaces is used to sand away the blockage. The resulting debris is then removed from the artery, restoring blood flow to the heart. The percutaneous approach allows for a shorter recovery time and minimizes scarring, making it a safer and more effective treatment option for patients with coronary artery disease.
