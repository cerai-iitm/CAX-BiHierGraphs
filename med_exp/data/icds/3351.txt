Drainage of Left Hepatic Duct with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous drainage of the left hepatic duct is a minimally invasive procedure that involves inserting a drainage device through the skin to drain bile from the duct. This is typically done under imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound, to ensure accurate placement of the device. The procedure is commonly performed to treat conditions such as bile duct obstruction, abscesses, or leaks, and can help alleviate symptoms such as jaundice, pain, and fever. The drainage device is designed to allow bile to flow out of the body, promoting healing and reducing the risk of complications.
