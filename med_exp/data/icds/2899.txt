Excision of Rectum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the rectum through an endoscopic approach, where a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the rectum and guide the excision. The endoscope allows the healthcare provider to directly visualize the rectal tissue and identify any abnormalities, making it possible to precisely remove the affected area. The excision is typically performed using specialized instruments inserted through the endoscope, allowing for a minimally invasive and precise procedure. This diagnostic excision is usually performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as rectal cancer, rectal polyps, or other abnormalities of the rectum.
