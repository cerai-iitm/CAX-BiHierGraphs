Reposition Left Upper Arm Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left upper arm to access the tendon and reposition it to its normal anatomical location. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the tendon and surrounding tissue, enabling a more precise correction of any misalignment or deformation. The repositioning process may involve releasing or repairing surrounding soft tissues, and stabilizing the tendon with sutures or other fixation devices. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal muscle function, alleviate pain and stiffness, and improve range of motion in the left upper arm.
