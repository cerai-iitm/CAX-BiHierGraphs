Excision of Left Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an open incision in the left lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are then surgically excised to remove damaged or infected tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the targeted tissue. The excision of subcutaneous tissue and fascia may be necessary to treat conditions such as necrotizing fasciitis, compartment syndrome, or soft tissue infections. The goal of this procedure is to promote healing, restore function, and prevent further complications in the affected leg.
