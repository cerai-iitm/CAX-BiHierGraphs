Excision of Right Foot Skin, External Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of skin tissue from the right foot through an external approach for diagnostic purposes. The goal of this excision is to examine the underlying skin tissue for any abnormalities or diseases, such as skin cancer or infection. During the procedure, the surgeon will make an incision in the skin of the right foot and carefully remove a sample of tissue for further examination. The excised tissue will then be sent to a laboratory for histopathological analysis to determine the presence of any diseases or conditions.
