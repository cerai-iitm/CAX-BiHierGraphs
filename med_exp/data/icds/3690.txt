Excision of Right Upper Leg Skin, External Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right upper leg to remove a portion of skin tissue, typically to treat skin lesions, cysts, or tumors. The external approach allows the surgeon to directly access the affected area, providing a clear view of the tissue to be excised. The excision is usually performed under local or general anesthesia, depending on the size and location of the skin area to be removed. The resulting wound is then closed, often with sutures or staples, to promote healing and minimize scarring.
