Bypass Bilateral Ureters to Ileocutaneous with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a bypass between the ureters and the ileocutaneous area using an autologous tissue substitute, which is a tissue graft harvested from the patient's own body. The procedure is performed percutaneously, meaning through small incisions in the skin, and utilizes endoscopic guidance for visualization and precision. During the procedure, the ureters are diverted to the ileocutaneous area, allowing for the normal flow of urine to be maintained. This complex operation is typically performed to treat severe ureteral obstruction or damage, and can significantly improve the patient's quality of life.
