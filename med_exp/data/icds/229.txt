Release Brachial Plexus, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the brachial plexus, a complex network of nerves that originates in the spine and extends down to the arm. The goal of the operation is to release any compression, entrapment or constriction of the nerves, which can cause pain, numbness, tingling, or weakness in the arm and hand. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully identifies and frees the affected nerves from any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstacles, allowing them to function normally and alleviating symptoms. By releasing the compressed nerves, this operation can help restore motor function, sensation, and mobility to the affected arm.
