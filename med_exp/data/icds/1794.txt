Extraction of Iliac Bone Marrow, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This invasive diagnostic procedure involves the insertion of a specialized needle through the skin and into the iliac bone, typically located in the pelvis, to collect a bone marrow sample. The sample is then sent to a laboratory for analysis, which helps healthcare professionals diagnose various blood-related disorders, infections, or cancers. The percutaneous approach, also known as a bone marrow biopsy, is performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and is usually an outpatient procedure. The extracted bone marrow sample provides valuable information about the production and development of blood cells, allowing for accurate diagnoses and effective treatment plans.
