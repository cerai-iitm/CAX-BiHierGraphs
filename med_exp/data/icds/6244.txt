Bypass Bilateral Ureters to Ileocutaneous, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the creation of a new pathway for urine to exit the body by connecting the bilateral ureters to the ileum, a section of the small intestine. The open approach requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the ureters and ileum, allowing the surgeon to carefully dissect and connect the urinary tubes to the intestine. The resulting ileocutaneous urinary diversion enables urine to flow from the kidneys, through the ureters, and into the intestine, where it is then eliminated from the body through a stoma, or artificial opening, in the abdominal wall. This complex procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as bladder cancer, trauma, or congenital defects that prevent normal urinary function.
