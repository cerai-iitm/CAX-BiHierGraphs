Insertion of Infusion Device into Left External Jugular Vein, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert an infusion device into the left external jugular vein, a large vein located on the side of the neck. The percutaneous approach involves making a small incision in the skin to access the vein, allowing for the device to be inserted with guided imaging. The infusion device is carefully placed into the vein to facilitate the administration of medications, fluids, or nutrients. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement and minimize complications.
