Dilation of Bilateral Ureters with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through the skin to dilate both ureters, which are tubes that carry urine from the kidneys to the bladder. The percutaneous approach allows the doctor to access the ureters through a small incision in the skin, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster healing. During the procedure, the device is guided through the ureters to widen the narrow passages, allowing for improved urine flow and relief from blockages or strictures. The goal of this minimally invasive procedure is to restore normal urinary function and alleviate associated symptoms such as pain, infection, or kidney damage.
