Release Bladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a thin scope is inserted through the skin and into the bladder to visualize the affected area. The scope is equipped with specialized instruments that allow the physician to carefully release any adhesions or scar tissue that may be obstructing the bladder. The percutaneous endoscopic approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and minimizing the risk of complications. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal bladder function and alleviate symptoms such as painful urination or incontinence.
