Drainage of Common Hepatic Duct with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a drainage device through the skin to access the common hepatic duct, which is a vital structure that carries bile from the liver and gallbladder to the small intestine. The purpose of the procedure is to alleviate blockages or obstructions in the duct, restoring the normal flow of bile and alleviating symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and nausea. During the procedure, imaging guidance is used to precisely locate the duct and navigate the drainage device into place. The goal of the procedure is to provide immediate relief from symptoms and prevent further complications associated with bile duct obstruction.
