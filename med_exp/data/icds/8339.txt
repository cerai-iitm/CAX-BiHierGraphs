Stereotactic Other Photon Radiosurgery of Chest
This minimally invasive procedure utilizes advanced stereotactic technology to deliver a precise and intense dose of photon radiation to a specific area within the chest cavity. The treatment involves fusion of imaging modalities, such as CT and MRI scans, to create a detailed 3D map of the targeted region, allowing for accurate beam placement and reduced risk of radiation exposure to surrounding tissues. Through the use of multiple convergent beams, the radiation dose is concentrated on the affected area, minimizing damage to nearby organs and promoting optimal outcomes. Treatment is typically performed on an outpatient basis, with patients often experiencing minimal side effects and a quick recovery.
