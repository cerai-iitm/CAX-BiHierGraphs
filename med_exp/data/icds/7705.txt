Introduction of Other Antineoplastic into Biliary and Pancreatic Tract, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a needle through the skin to deliver antineoplastic agents directly into the biliary and pancreatic tract to target cancer cells. A radiologist or interventional radiologist guides the procedure using imaging modalities such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy to ensure accurate placement. The antineoplastic agents work to destroy cancer cells or prevent their growth, providing a localized treatment option for patients with pancreatic or bile duct cancer. This approach can help alleviate symptoms, improve quality of life, and potentially increase survival rates for these patients.
