Insertion of Pacemaker Lead into Right Atrium, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest to access the right atrium, a upper chamber of the heart, to implant a pacemaker lead. The lead is carefully guided through the incision and inserted into the right atrium, allowing the pacemaker to regulate the heartbeat. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the heart and surrounding tissues, ensuring accurate placement of the lead. Once in place, the lead is secured and connected to the pacemaker device, which is typically implanted under the skin near the collarbone.
