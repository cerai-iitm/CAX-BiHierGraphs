Restriction of Left Internal Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a catheter to introduce an intraluminal device into the left internal carotid artery through a small incision in the groin area. The device is then deployed to restrict blood flow and prevent further damage to the artery, which may be narrowed or blocked due to atherosclerosis or other conditions. The percutaneous approach allows for faster recovery and less trauma to the patient compared to open surgery. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate device placement.
