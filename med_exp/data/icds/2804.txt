Excision of Esophagogastric Junction, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visually examine the esophagogastric junction. Through this approach, the affected tissue or lesion is identified and removed using specialized instruments, allowing for precise excision with minimal disruption to surrounding tissue. The percutaneous endoscopic approach enables direct visualization and real-time feedback, ensuring accurate removal of the targeted area. This procedure provides an alternative to open surgery, offering reduced recovery time, less scarring, and decreased risk of complications.
