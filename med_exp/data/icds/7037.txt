Inspection of Lower Back, Open Approach
During an open approach inspection of the lower back, a surgical incision is made to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visually examine the muscles, bones, and other tissues of the lumbar region. This procedure may be performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as herniated discs, spinal fractures, or tumors that are causing back pain, numbness, or weakness in the legs. The surgeon may use specialized instruments and imaging technology to guide the inspection and collect tissue samples for further examination. The goal of the procedure is to identify the underlying cause of the patient's symptoms and develop an effective treatment plan to alleviate pain and improve mobility.
