Drainage of Bilateral Ureters with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through a natural or artificial opening to relieve blockages in both ureters. Using an endoscope, the healthcare provider accesses the ureters through the bladder or kidneys to place the drainage device, allowing urine to flow freely and alleviating symptoms of urinary retention. The device is carefully guided into position to ensure proper placement and minimize discomfort. Once in place, the device enables normal urinary function to resume, reducing the risk of complications associated with urinary tract obstruction.
