Excision of Left Upper Leg Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a section of skin from the outer aspect of the left upper leg through an external incision. The affected area is accessed through a skin incision, allowing the surgeon to excise the targeted skin tissue. The excised skin may be sent for further examination, such as biopsy, or may be discarded depending on the underlying condition being treated. The wound is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, to promote healing and minimize scarring.
