Supplement Atrial Septum with Zooplastic Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves supplementing the atrial septum with zooplastic tissue, a type of biological tissue derived from animal sources, to repair or replace damaged or defective tissue. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the atrial septum and perform the necessary repair. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully attach the zooplastic tissue to the existing septum, ensuring a secure and proper fit. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal heart function and prevent complications arising from septal defects or holes.
