Inspection of Right Ankle Region, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the examination of the right ankle region through a small incision in the skin, allowing the physician to visually inspect the area for any signs of injury, disease, or other abnormalities. The percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster healing. During the procedure, the physician may use specialized instruments to gently probe and visualize the ankle joint and surrounding soft tissues. The goal of the procedure is to diagnose and manage conditions affecting the right ankle region, such as sprains, strains, or fractures.
