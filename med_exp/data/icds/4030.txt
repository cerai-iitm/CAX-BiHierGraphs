Excision of Scalp Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the scalp to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are then carefully excised to remove any damaged or diseased tissue. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the area and ensure a thorough removal of the targeted tissue. The excision is typically performed to treat conditions such as scalp lesions, skin cancer, or trauma, and may be done in conjunction with other procedures, such as reconstruction or repair of surrounding tissue. The goal of the procedure is to promote healing, restore function, and improve the appearance of the scalp.
