Insertion of Intraluminal Device into Esophagus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During an endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or a surgically created opening to access the esophagus. The esophagus is visually examined, and an intraluminal device, such as a stent, is inserted to maintain patency and restore swallowing function. The device is carefully placed within the esophagus, using real-time visualization to ensure accurate positioning. Once in place, the device helps to keep the esophageal passage open, enabling food and liquids to pass through more easily.
