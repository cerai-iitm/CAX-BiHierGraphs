Pheresis of Plasma, Multiple
This procedure involves the simultaneous collection of plasma from multiple draws of blood, which are then separated from the remaining blood components using a centrifugation process. The collected plasma is then used for various therapeutic purposes, such as transfusions or the production of life-saving medications. The multiple draws of blood are typically taken from the same venous access site, and the entire process is usually performed in a specialized apheresis facility or blood bank. The plasma is then further processed and tested to ensure its safety and purity before distribution for transfusion or manufacture.
