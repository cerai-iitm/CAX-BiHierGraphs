Reposition Left Humeral Shaft, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left upper arm to manually reposition a displaced or fractured humeral shaft, allowing for realignment and proper healing of the bone. The open approach provides direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to accurately reposition the shaft and stabilize it with hardware or other fixation devices as needed. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal anatomy and function to the left arm, alleviating pain and promoting optimal recovery. Through careful manipulation and stabilization, the surgeon can help restore mobility and strength to the affected limb.
