Control Bleeding in Mediastinum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the mediastinum, a region in the thoracic cavity, to access and control bleeding caused by injury or trauma. The open approach requires a thoracotomy, where the chest cavity is opened to directly visualize and address the source of bleeding. The surgeon will then use various techniques, such as suturing, ligation, or cauterization, to stop the bleeding and repair any damaged tissues or blood vessels. The goal of this procedure is to restore hemostasis, prevent further bleeding, and ensure the patient's overall stability.
