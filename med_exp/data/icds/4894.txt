Reposition Right Zygomatic Bone with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right zygomatic bone to its anatomic position to restore facial symmetry and alleviate associated symptoms. Through an open approach, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the affected bone, allowing for direct visualization and manipulation. Internal fixation devices, such as plates and screws, are then used to stabilize the repositioned bone and promote healing. The goal of this procedure is to improve facial aesthetics and function, as well as relieve pain and discomfort associated with zygomatic bone misalignment.
