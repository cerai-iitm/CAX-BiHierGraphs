Dilation of Right Common Carotid Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the use of an intraluminal device to dilate the right common carotid artery, which is accessed through a percutaneous approach, meaning through the skin. The device is inserted through a small incision in the groin area and guided to the blocked artery using fluoroscopic or X-ray imaging. Once in place, the device expands, widening the artery and improving blood flow to the brain. The goal of this minimally invasive procedure is to restore blood flow and reduce the risk of stroke or other complications resulting from blockages in the carotid artery.
