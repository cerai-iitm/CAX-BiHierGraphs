Excision of Left Hip Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left hip area to access the affected muscle tissue. The damaged or diseased muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, from the surrounding soft tissue and bone. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure optimal outcome. Once the muscle is removed, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to promote healing and reduce the risk of complications.
