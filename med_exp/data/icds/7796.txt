Measurement of Gastrointestinal Pressure, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to measure the pressure within the gastrointestinal tract. The endoscope, equipped with specialized sensors, is guided through the esophagus, stomach, or intestines to capture accurate readings of the intra-luminal pressure. This measurement helps diagnose and monitor gastrointestinal motility disorders, such as dysphagia, gastroparesis, and intestinal pseudo-obstruction. The collected data is then used to develop an effective treatment plan to alleviate symptoms and improve digestive health.
