Replacement of Left Knee Joint, Tibial Surface with Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the tibial surface of the left knee joint with a synthetic substitute, using a cemented fixation method. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to prepare the bone and insert the artificial surface. The cement is used to secure the implant in place, ensuring a stable and durable bond. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal knee function, alleviate pain, and improve mobility in patients with severe knee joint damage or arthritis.
