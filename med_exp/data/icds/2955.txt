Extirpation of Matter from Rectum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During an endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the anus or a surgically created opening to visually examine the rectum. The healthcare provider uses specialized instruments to remove abnormal or foreign material, such as feces, blood, or other substances, from the rectum. This is typically done to treat conditions like rectal bleeding, obstruction, or impaction, or to relieve symptoms of constipation or abdominal pain. The procedure is usually performed under conscious sedation, allowing the patient to recover quickly and return to normal activities soon after.
