Inspection of Peritoneal Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdominal wall to visualize the peritoneal cavity, allowing for direct examination of the abdominal organs and structures. The peritoneal cavity is then carefully inspected for any signs of injury, disease, or abnormality. During the procedure, any adhesions or scar tissue that may be present are gently dissected to allow for a thorough examination of the abdominal contents. The open approach provides optimal visualization and access to the peritoneal cavity, enabling surgeons to diagnose and treat various conditions affecting the abdominal organs and structures.
