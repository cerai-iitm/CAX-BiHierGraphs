Excision of Scalp Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision through the skin and underlying tissue to access the scalp, allowing the surgeon to remove a specific area of skin, typically to excise a lesion, tumor, or damaged tissue. The external approach is used, meaning the incision is made directly through the skin, rather than using an endoscopic or minimally invasive technique. The affected skin is carefully dissected and removed, taking care to preserve surrounding healthy tissue and minimize cosmetic damage. Once the excision is complete, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the area is dressed to promote healing.
