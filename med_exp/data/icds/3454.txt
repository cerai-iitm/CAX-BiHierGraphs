Extraction of Right Hepatic Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
During this endoscopic procedure, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end is inserted through the mouth or a surgical opening to visualize the right hepatic duct. The healthcare provider uses specialized instruments to extract tissue or stones from the duct, which can help diagnose and treat conditions such as gallstones, bile duct obstruction, or cancer. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or anesthesia to ensure patient comfort. The extracted tissue or stones are then sent to a laboratory for further examination to identify any underlying conditions.
