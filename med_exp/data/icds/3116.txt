Repair Ileum, Open Approach
During an open surgery, the ileum, a portion of the small intestine, is repaired through a surgical incision made in the abdomen. The procedure involves manual examination and correction of any defects, lesions, or injuries affecting the ileum, which may be causing obstruction, perforation, or bleeding. The surgical repair may involve suturing, stapling, or resection of the affected segment, and the goal is to restore normal intestinal function and anatomy. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for postoperative recovery and monitoring.
