Replacement of Right Mandible with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This invasive surgical procedure involves replacing the right mandible, also known as the lower jawbone, with an autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The open approach involves making an incision in the skin and soft tissue to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and repair the damaged bone. During the procedure, the surgeon will remove the damaged or diseased section of the mandible and replace it with the autologous tissue substitute, which is carefully shaped and fitted to match the natural contours of the jaw. The goal of this complex procedure is to restore the patient's facial structure and function, as well as alleviate associated symptoms such as pain, difficulty eating, or breathing difficulties.
