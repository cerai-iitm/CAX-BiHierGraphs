Excision of Left Neck Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the left neck area to access the underlying muscle tissue. The affected muscle is then carefully excised, or removed, to address any underlying condition or disease that may be causing symptoms. The open approach requires a larger incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the muscle and surrounding structures to ensure precise removal. Once the muscle has been excised, the incision is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
