Replacement of Right Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Synthetic Substitute, Uncemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the femoral surface of the right hip joint with an uncemented synthetic substitute through an open approach. The goal is to restore function and alleviate pain in the damaged joint, typically caused by osteoarthritis, fractures, or other conditions. The uncemented synthetic substitute is designed to bond with the surrounding bone, providing a stable and durable structure. The open approach allows the surgeon to access the joint directly, ensuring accurate placement and minimized tissue damage.
