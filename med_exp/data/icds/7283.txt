Detachment at Right 1st Toe, Complete, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the complete detachment of the right 1st toe from the rest of the foot through an open approach, where the skin is incised to allow direct visualization of the operative site. The surgery is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. The detachment is accomplished by dissecting the toe away from the surrounding soft tissue and bone, taking care to ligate any blood vessels or nerves to prevent bleeding or damage. The resulting defect is then closed in a layered fashion to promote healing and minimize the risk of complications.
