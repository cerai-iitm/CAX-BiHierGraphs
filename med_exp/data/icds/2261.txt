Extirpation of Matter from Right Main Bronchus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of unwanted matter or debris from the right main bronchus, which is the primary airway that brings air into the right lung. The removal is performed through a natural or artificial opening in the bronchus, allowing the healthcare provider to access the affected area using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal airflow and prevent complications such as infection or respiratory failure.
