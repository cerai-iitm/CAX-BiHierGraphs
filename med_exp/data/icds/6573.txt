Excision of Vulva, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the vulva to access the affected area, followed by the removal of the targeted tissue or lesion. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures, enabling the surgeon to precisely excise the abnormal tissue while minimizing damage to surrounding healthy tissue. The excision is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the procedure. Post-operative care involves close monitoring for potential complications, such as infection or bleeding, and wound care to promote healing.
