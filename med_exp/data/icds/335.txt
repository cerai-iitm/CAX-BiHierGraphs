Destruction of Left Atrium, Percutaneous Approach
Percutaneous destruction of the left atrium is a minimally invasive procedure that involves accessing the heart through a small incision in the groin area. Under X-ray or ultrasound guidance, a specialized catheter is inserted through the femoral vein and advanced to the left atrium, where energy is applied to destroy the targeted tissue. This procedure is often used to treat atrial fibrillation or other arrhythmias by creating scar tissue that helps to block abnormal electrical signals. The treatment is typically performed under local anesthesia and sedation, and patients usually recover quickly with minimal discomfort.
