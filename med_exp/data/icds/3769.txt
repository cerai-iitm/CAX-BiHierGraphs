Removal of Tissue Expander from Left Breast, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, the tissue expander is carefully removed from the left breast through an open approach, which involves making a small incision in the breast skin. The surgeon then gently disconnects the expander from the surrounding tissue and carefully removes it from the breast pocket. Once the expander is removed, the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the breast is reshaped to restore its natural contour. Following the removal, the breast tissue is prepared for the next stage of reconstruction, which may involve the insertion of a permanent breast implant.
