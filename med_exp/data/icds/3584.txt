Resection of Left Adrenal Gland, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
A minimally invasive surgical procedure is performed to remove the left adrenal gland through a small incision, utilizing an endoscope to visualize the area. This percutaneous approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and organs, promoting faster healing and less pain. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia, and the surgeon makes a small incision in the abdomen to access the adrenal gland. The endoscope is inserted through the incision, providing a clear view of the gland, which is then carefully dissected and removed.
