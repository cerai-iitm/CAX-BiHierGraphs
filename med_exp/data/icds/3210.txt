Resection of Greater Omentum, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the greater omentum, a fold of peritoneum that hangs down from the stomach. The procedure is typically performed to remove a portion of the greater omentum that has become diseased or cancerous. Using an open approach, the surgeon carefully separates the omentum from surrounding tissues and removes the affected area. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, to complete the procedure.
