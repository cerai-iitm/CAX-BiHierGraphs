Dilation of Left Axillary Vein, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a small catheter through the skin and into the left axillary vein, a major blood vessel located under the collarbone. The catheter is guided through the vein using imaging guidance, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to the site of blockage or narrowing. Once in place, a balloon is inflated to widen the vein, restoring blood flow and relieving symptoms such as swelling, pain, or numbness in the arm. The procedure is typically performed on an outpatient basis and may be done with local anesthesia to minimize discomfort.
