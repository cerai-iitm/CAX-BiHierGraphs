Insertion of Neurostimulator Lead into Cranial Nerve, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert a neurostimulator lead into a cranial nerve, utilizing a percutaneous approach. This involves making a small incision in the skin and using a thin needle to guide the lead into the targeted nerve. The neurostimulator lead is designed to deliver gentle electrical impulses to the nerve, which can help to alleviate chronic pain, facial spasms, or other neurological symptoms. The percutaneous approach allows for reduced tissue disruption and minimal scarring, promoting a faster recovery and less post-operative discomfort.
