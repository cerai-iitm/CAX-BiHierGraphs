Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Lumbosacral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, typically composed of screws, rods, or plates, from the lumbosacral joint through an open approach. The lumbosacral joint, located at the base of the spine, is accessed through a surgical incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize and extract the internal fixation device. The procedure is often performed to alleviate discomfort, pain, or inflammation caused by the presence of the device, which was initially inserted to stabilize the joint following a spinal injury or surgery. The removal of the internal fixation device can help restore normal spinal function and alleviate symptoms associated with its presence.
