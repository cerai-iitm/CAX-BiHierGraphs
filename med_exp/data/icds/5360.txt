Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Right Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, such as a plate or rod, from the right tibia, also known as the shinbone, through an open approach. The open approach allows the surgeon to make a incision in the skin and directly access the device to be removed. Once the device is removed, the incision is closed, and the patient is treated for any post-operative care. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort during the surgery.
