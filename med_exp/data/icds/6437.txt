Repair Bladder, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the skin and into the bladder to repair damage or defects, utilizing visualization and specialized instruments to restore normal bladder function and anatomy. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for reduced trauma to surrounding tissues and minimizes the risk of complications. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. Repairing the bladder through this method can help alleviate symptoms such as incontinence, recurring infections, and pain, improving overall quality of life.
