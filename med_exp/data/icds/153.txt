Release Facial Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves accessing the facial nerve through an open approach, where a skin incision is made to expose the affected area. The goal of the procedure is to release the compressed or entrapped facial nerve, which is often caused by tumors, cysts, or scar tissue. The surgeon carefully dissects the surrounding tissue to identify the nerve and gently releases it from its constraint, allowing it to function normally again. The procedure is typically performed to alleviate symptoms such as facial weakness, numbness, or paralysis, and can significantly improve facial function and overall quality of life.
