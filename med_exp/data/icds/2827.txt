Excision of Small Intestine, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a small intestine segment using a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, called an endoscope, inserted through a small incision in the skin. The diagnostic excision is performed to examine the intestine for abnormalities, such as cancer, inflammation, or blockages. The endoscope is guided through the intestine to the affected area, and specialized instruments are used to remove the targeted segment. The removed tissue is then sent to a laboratory for further examination to determine the underlying cause of the condition.
