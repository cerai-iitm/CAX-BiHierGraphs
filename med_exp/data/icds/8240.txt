Fluoroscopy of Left Kidney using Other Contrast
This diagnostic imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left kidney in real-time, utilizing a specialized contrast agent other than the standard options. During the procedure, the contrast medium is administered to enhance the visibility of the kidney's internal structures, allowing the healthcare provider to assess its function and morphology. The fluoroscope emits continuous X-rays, which are converted into video images, providing a detailed view of the left kidney's anatomy and any potential abnormalities. The obtained images enable accurate diagnosis and treatment of various kidney-related disorders, such as stones, tumors, or obstruction.
