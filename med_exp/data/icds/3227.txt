Supplement Stomach, Pylorus with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the supplementation of the stomach, specifically the pylorus, with a biocompatible material derived from the patient's own tissue. Through an open approach, the surgeon gains access to the stomach and carefully inserts the autologous tissue substitute to reinforce the pyloric region. This procedure aims to restore the natural function of the stomach and improve digestion by facilitating the passage of food from the stomach into the small intestine. The use of autologous tissue minimizes the risk of rejection and promotes a more natural healing process.
