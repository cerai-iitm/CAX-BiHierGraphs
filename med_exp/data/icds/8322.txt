Contact Radiation of Humerus
This procedure involves the use of a specialized radiation therapy device to deliver a high dose of radiation directly to the humerus bone. The contact radiation therapy is performed in a controlled medical setting, often as an outpatient procedure, and typically takes about 30 minutes to an hour to complete. The treatment is designed to target cancerous cells in the humerus, and may be used in conjunction with other forms of cancer therapy. The precise application of radiation helps to minimize damage to surrounding healthy tissue and nearby organs.
