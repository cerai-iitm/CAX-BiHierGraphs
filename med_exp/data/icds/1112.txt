Dilation of Right Anterior Tibial Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small catheter through the skin and into the right anterior tibial artery to widen the artery and improve blood flow. The catheter is guided through the artery using imaging techniques such as X-ray or ultrasound to ensure accurate placement. Once in place, a balloon or other dilation device is inflated to expand the artery, which helps to relieve blockages and restore normal blood flow to the affected area. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as peripheral artery disease or critical limb ischemia.
