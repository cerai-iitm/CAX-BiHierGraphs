Excision of Scrotum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the scrotum, a sac of skin and muscle that contains the testicles. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as scrotal cancer, severe scrotal injuries, or chronic scrotal infections that are resistant to antibiotic treatment. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully removes the affected scrotal tissue, taking care to preserve surrounding healthy tissue and minimize bleeding. The incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, and the area is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
