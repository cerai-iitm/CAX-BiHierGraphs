Drainage of Intracranial Subdural Space, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, a craniotomy is performed to access the intracranial subdural space, where accumulated blood or fluid is removed to alleviate pressure on the brain. The open approach allows for direct visualization and exploration of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to evacuate the hemorrhage or fluid collection and restore normal cranial pressure. The procedure is often performed in cases of traumatic brain injury, subdural hematoma, or other conditions causing increased intracranial pressure. Post-operative care typically involves close monitoring for potential complications, such as infection or cerebral edema.
