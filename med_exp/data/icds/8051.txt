Fluoroscopy of Right Pulmonary Artery using Other Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the right pulmonary artery, which carries deoxygenated blood from the heart to the lungs. During the procedure, a contrast agent other than the standard iodine-based or barium-based agents is injected into the artery to enhance visibility. The fluoroscope, a continuous X-ray beam, is used to guide the injection and capture real-time images of the artery and its surrounding structures. The resulting images aid in the diagnosis and treatment of conditions such as pulmonary embolism, pulmonary artery stenosis, or other abnormalities affecting the right pulmonary artery.
