Electroconvulsive Therapy, Bilateral-Single Seizure
This therapeutic procedure involves the administration of a controlled electric current to the brain, intentionally triggering a single seizure in the patient, with electrode placement on both sides of the head to stimulate a broader area of the brain. The goal of this treatment is to relieve severe symptoms of mental health conditions, such as depression, catatonia, or schizophrenia, that have not responded to other interventions. The treatment is performed under general anesthesia and muscle relaxation to minimize discomfort and ensure the patient's safety. The procedure is typically repeated multiple times, with the frequency and number of sessions determined by the patient's response to the treatment.
