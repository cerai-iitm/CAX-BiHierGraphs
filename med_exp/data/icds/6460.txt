Resection of Bladder, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to directly access the bladder, allowing the surgeon to visualize the organ and surrounding tissue. The bladder is then carefully dissected and separated from surrounding structures, and the affected portion is removed. The remaining bladder tissue is repaired and closed, and the incision is sutured or stapled shut. The goal of this open approach is to remove cancerous or damaged tissue, relieve symptoms, or improve bladder function.
