Excision of Thoracic Vertebral Disc, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the thoracic region to access the vertebral disc, which is then carefully removed to alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or tingling. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the spine and surrounding tissue, enabling the surgeon to precisely locate and excise the damaged or herniated disc. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as herniated thoracic discs, spinal stenosis, or degenerative disc disease. Following the removal of the disc, the surgical site is closed, and the patient is typically required to undergo a period of recovery and rehabilitation to ensure proper healing.
