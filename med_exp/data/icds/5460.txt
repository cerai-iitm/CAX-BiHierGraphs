Reposition Right Tibia with External Fixation Device, Open Approach
This orthopedic procedure involves repositioning the right tibia, also known as the shinbone, to its normal anatomical position using an external fixation device. The procedure is done through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected bone. The external fixation device is attached to the bone using pins or wires and provides stabilization and support to the tibia as it heals. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal alignment and function to the affected leg, allowing for improved mobility and reduced discomfort.
