Drainage of Retroperitoneum with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This medical procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through the skin to remove excess fluid or infected material from the retroperitoneum, a space in the abdominal cavity that surrounds the kidneys and other vital organs. The percutaneous approach is a minimally invasive method that uses imaging guidance to precisely locate the affected area and insert the drainage device through a small incision. The device is designed to drain the accumulated fluid or infected material, promoting healing and reducing the risk of complications. Once the procedure is complete, the drainage device is removed, and the incision site is closed, allowing the patient to recover from the procedure.
