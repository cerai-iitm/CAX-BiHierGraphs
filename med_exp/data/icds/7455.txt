Delivery of Products of Conception, External Approach
This procedure involves the use of external techniques to facilitate the delivery of a fetus or fetuses from the mother's body. It may be performed vaginally or abdominally, depending on the circumstances of the pregnancy, and may involve instrumental assistance or manual maneuvers to aid in the delivery process. In some cases, this procedure may be used in conjunction with other interventions, such as fetal monitoring or labor augmentation, to ensure a safe and healthy delivery. Overall, the goal of this procedure is to facilitate a safe and successful delivery of the products of conception.
