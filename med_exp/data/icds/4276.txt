Replacement of Left Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this open approach procedure, the damaged or diseased subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the left foot are surgically removed and replaced with an autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The goal of this procedure is to restore functionality and improve the overall health of the left foot. The autologous tissue substitute is carefully prepared and inserted into the affected area, where it is securely positioned to promote optimal healing and tissue regeneration. This procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay, depending on the complexity of the case and the patient's overall health.
