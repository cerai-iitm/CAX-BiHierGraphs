Excision of Stomach, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the stomach, which is then partially or totally removed for diagnostic purposes. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the stomach and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to identify any abnormalities or lesions that may not be visible through less invasive methods. The excision of the stomach can help diagnose and stage gastric cancer, as well as identify other conditions such as ulcers, bleeding, or obstruction. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the removal of surrounding lymph nodes or other tissues for further examination.
