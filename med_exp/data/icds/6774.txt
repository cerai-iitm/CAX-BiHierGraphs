Resection of Prostate, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of all or part of the prostate gland through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a thin, lighted tube with a camera. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and is used to treat conditions such as benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) or prostate cancer. The endoscope is inserted through the urethra or a small incision in the abdomen, allowing the surgeon to visualize the prostate gland and surrounding tissue. The prostate tissue is then carefully resected and removed, with the goal of alleviating symptoms such as urinary frequency, urgency, and pain.
