Drainage of Spinal Canal, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of imaging guidance to precisely insert a small drainage device through the skin and into the spinal canal to remove a sample of cerebrospinal fluid for diagnostic purposes. The procedure is typically performed in an operating room or a specialized procedure suite under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic or CT guidance. The goal of this procedure is to collect a sample of cerebrospinal fluid for laboratory analysis to aid in the diagnosis of conditions such as meningitis, encephalitis, or subarachnoid hemorrhage. The entire procedure usually takes around 30-60 minutes to complete, depending on the complexity of the case and the individual patient's needs.
