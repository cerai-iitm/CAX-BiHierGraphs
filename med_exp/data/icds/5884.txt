Excision of Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower back to access the lumbar vertebral joint, which is a complex structure that connects the vertebrae in the spine. The goal of the procedure is to excise, or remove, the joint in order to relieve pain, improve mobility, and stabilize the spine. During the open approach, the surgeon uses a larger incision to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, allowing for more precise dissection and removal of the affected joint. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is often performed in patients with severe joint damage or degenerative conditions, such as osteoarthritis or spinal stenosis.
