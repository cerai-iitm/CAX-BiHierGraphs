Assistance with Respiratory Ventilation, Greater than 96 Consecutive Hours, Continuous Positive Airway Pressure
This procedure involves the continuous administration of positive airway pressure to assist with respiratory ventilation for an extended period of time, exceeding 96 consecutive hours. The patient receives mechanical support to help them breathe, with a constant flow of air pressure into the airways to keep them open and ensure proper oxygenation. This intervention is typically used in critical care settings, such as intensive care units, to support patients who require prolonged ventilatory assistance. The goal of this therapy is to improve oxygenation, reduce respiratory distress, and promote recovery in patients with compromised lung function or respiratory failure.
