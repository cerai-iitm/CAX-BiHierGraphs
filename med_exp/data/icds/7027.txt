Inspection of Abdominal Wall, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is the description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visually examine the abdominal wall. Through a small incision in the skin, the endoscope is inserted to allow the physician to directly inspect the abdominal wall, facilitating the diagnosis of conditions such as hernias or other defects. The percutaneous endoscopic approach minimizes trauma to the surrounding tissues and reduces post-operative pain and scarring. By providing a clear and detailed visual examination, this procedure enables accurate diagnosis and guides further treatment.
