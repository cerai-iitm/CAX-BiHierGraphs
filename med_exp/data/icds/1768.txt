Excision of Right Inguinal Lymphatic, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right groin area to access the inguinal lymph nodes, which are part of the lymphatic system that helps to fight infection. The purpose of the procedure is diagnostic, meaning it is performed to determine the presence or extent of disease or infection in the lymph nodes. During the procedure, the surgeon will excise, or remove, the right inguinal lymphatic tissue to examine it for signs of disease or abnormality. The open approach refers to the surgical technique used, where a larger incision is made to access the lymph nodes directly.
