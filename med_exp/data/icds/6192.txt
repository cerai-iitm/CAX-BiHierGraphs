Reposition Left Tarsometatarsal Joint with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves realigning the tarsometatarsal joint in the left foot, which connects the long bones in the midfoot to the toes, into its normal anatomic position. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize and manipulate the bones and surrounding soft tissues. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are then used to stabilize the joint and hold the bones in place while they heal. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal alignment and function to the tarsometatarsal joint, alleviating pain and improving mobility in the foot.
