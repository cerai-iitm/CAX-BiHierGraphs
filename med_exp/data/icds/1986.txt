Extirpation of Matter from Left Sphenoid Sinus, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the removal of abnormal or infected material from the left sphenoid sinus, which is one of the air-filled cavities in the skull. During the procedure, an endoscope is inserted through the nostrils or a previously created opening to visualize the sinus cavity and remove the unwanted material. The extirpation is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to ensure patient comfort. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal sinus function, alleviate symptoms such as headaches and congestion, and prevent further complications.
