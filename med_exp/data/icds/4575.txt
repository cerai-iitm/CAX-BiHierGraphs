Transfer Right Thorax Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the transfer of muscle tissue from one location to another within the right thorax, or chest cavity, in order to restore or improve muscle function. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and manipulate the muscle tissue directly. The transferred muscle is typically secured in its new location using sutures or other fixation techniques to ensure proper healing and functioning. The goal of this procedure is to enhance muscle strength and mobility in the right thorax, which can be beneficial for patients with muscle weakness or damage resulting from injury or disease.
