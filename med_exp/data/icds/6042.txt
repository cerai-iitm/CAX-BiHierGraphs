Removal of Spacer from Right Hip Joint, Open Approach
During an open surgical procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the right hip joint to access the artificial spacer, a temporary device previously inserted to maintain joint space and alignment. The spacer is carefully removed to prepare the joint for a more permanent implant, such as a total hip replacement. The surgical team takes great care to preserve surrounding soft tissue and bone to ensure a successful future implantation. The removal of the spacer is a crucial step in restoring normal hip function and alleviating pain and discomfort for the patient.
