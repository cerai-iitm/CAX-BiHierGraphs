Insertion of Feeding Device into Stomach, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This medical procedure involves placing a feeding device, such as a percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) tube, directly into the stomach through a natural or artificial opening. The insertion is typically performed under endoscopic guidance, allowing the healthcare provider to visually inspect the stomach and surrounding tissues. The feeding device is carefully guided through the opening and positioned in the stomach, enabling the patient to receive essential nutrients and fluids. The procedure is often used for individuals who have difficulty eating or swallowing, such as those with neurological disorders, cancer, or other conditions that affect oral intake.
