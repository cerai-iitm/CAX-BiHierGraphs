Resection of Cecum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the cecum, a pouch-like structure located at the beginning of the large intestine. The cecum is then carefully removed, along with any affected surrounding tissue or vessels. The surgical site is thoroughly inspected and any bleeding is controlled before the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples. The goal of this procedure is to treat conditions such as cancer, diverticulitis, or other diseases that affect the cecum.
