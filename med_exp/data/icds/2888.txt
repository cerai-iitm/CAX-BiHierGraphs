Excision of Sigmoid Colon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the abdomen to access the sigmoid colon, which is the lower part of the large intestine. The sigmoid colon is then carefully dissected and removed, along with any associated blood vessels and lymph nodes. The remaining sections of the colon are then reattached, allowing for normal bowel function to be restored. The abdominal incision is then closed, usually with sutures or staples, to complete the procedure.
