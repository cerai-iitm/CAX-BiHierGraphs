Extirpation of Matter from Mediastinum, Open Approach
A surgical procedure is performed to remove abnormal or infectious material from the mediastinum, the region in the chest cavity that contains vital organs such as the heart, trachea, and esophagus. The open approach involves a incision in the chest wall to access the mediastinum, allowing the surgeon to visualize and remove the affected tissue or foreign matter. This procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as mediastinitis, a serious infection of the mediastinum, or to remove a tumor or cyst that is compressing vital structures. The goal of the procedure is to eliminate the source of infection or compression, restore normal functioning of the surrounding organs, and promote healing.
