Repair Left Lower Arm and Wrist Muscle, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the left lower arm and wrist to access and repair damaged or injured muscles. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and treat the damaged muscle tissue. The repair process may involve suturing, grafting, or other techniques to restore normal muscle function and alleviate associated symptoms such as pain, weakness, or limited mobility. Following the procedure, the incision is closed, and the arm is often immobilized to allow the muscles to heal properly.
