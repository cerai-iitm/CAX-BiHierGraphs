Reposition Right Upper Femur with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right upper femur, also known as the proximal femur, to its normal anatomical position. An open approach is used, where a skin incision is made to access the affected area, and an intramedullary internal fixation device is implanted to stabilize the bone. The device is inserted into the medullary cavity of the femur, providing internal support and facilitating proper healing. This treatment is typically performed to correct femoral fractures or deformities, restoring normal function and mobility to the affected leg.
