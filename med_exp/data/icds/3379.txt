Drainage of Pancreas with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Endoscopic drainage of the pancreas is a minimally invasive procedure that utilizes an endoscope to access the pancreas through the mouth, stomach, or small intestine. A drainage device is then inserted through the endoscope to drain fluid or debris from the pancreas, relieving pressure and alleviating symptoms associated with pancreatitis or other pancreatic disorders. This procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or general anesthesia and may be performed in conjunction with other interventions, such as ERCP or stenting. The goal of endoscopic drainage is to restore pancreatic function, reduce pain, and promote healing.
