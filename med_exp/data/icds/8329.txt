Beam Radiation of Head and Neck using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This medical procedure involves the use of high-energy photon beams to target and treat cancerous cells or tumors in the head and neck region. The radiation therapy is designed to destroy or shrink the abnormal growths, alleviating symptoms and improving quality of life. The treatment utilizes photon beams with energies ranging from 1 to 10 million electron volts (MeV), carefully calibrated to minimize damage to surrounding healthy tissue. Through precise calculation and positioning, the radiation is delivered directly to the affected area, optimizing treatment outcomes.
