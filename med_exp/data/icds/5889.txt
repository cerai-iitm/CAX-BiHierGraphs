Excision of Lumbosacral Disc, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the lumbar region to access the lumbosacral disc, a spinal disc located between the lowest vertebra of the lumbar spine and the top vertebra of the sacrum. The damaged or herniated portion of the disc is then carefully excised, or removed, to relieve pressure on surrounding nerves and alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or tingling in the legs. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may involve the use of fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accuracy. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate symptoms and improve mobility and quality of life for patients suffering from lumbosacral disc disorders.
