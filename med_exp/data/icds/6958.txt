Excision of Mediastinum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the mediastinum, a region in the chest cavity that contains vital structures such as the heart, trachea, and esophagus. Through a small incision, the endoscope is inserted into the mediastinum, allowing the healthcare provider to examine the area and identify any abnormalities or lesions. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to collect tissue samples or cells from the mediastinum for further examination and diagnosis. Unlike open surgery, this percutaneous approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery times.
