Excision of Retroperitoneum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the retroperitoneum, a space in the back of the abdominal cavity that contains organs such as the kidneys, pancreas, and adrenal glands. The procedure allows the surgeon to directly visualize the retroperitoneum and excise, or remove, abnormal or damaged tissue, tumors, or other lesions in this region. The open approach enables the surgeon to have a clear view of the area and accurately identify and remove the affected tissue. This procedure is often performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as retroperitoneal fibrosis, tumors, or abscesses that may be causing symptoms such as pain, bleeding, or infection.
