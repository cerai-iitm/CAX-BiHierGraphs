Removal of External Fixation Device from Left Ankle Joint, Percutaneous Approach
The removal of an external fixation device from the left ankle joint is a percutaneous procedure that involves surgically taking out the external fixator, a device that provides stability and support to the ankle joint from outside the body. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize discomfort and pain. During the procedure, the physician makes a small incision in the skin to access the external fixator, and then carefully removes the device and any associated pins or screws. The removal of the external fixation device allows the ankle joint to return to its natural function and movement.
