Excision of Pelvis Lymphatic, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of lymphatic tissue in the pelvic region through an open approach, which means a large incision is made to access the affected area. The diagnostic excision is typically performed to obtain a biopsy sample for further examination, helping to determine the presence or absence of disease or cancer. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the lymphatic tissue to remove the affected nodes or masses, taking care to minimize damage to surrounding tissue and structures. After removal, the tissue is sent to a laboratory for histopathological examination to provide a definitive diagnosis.
