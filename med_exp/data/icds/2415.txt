Resection of Left Lower Lung Lobe, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves removing a portion of the left lower lung lobe through small incisions using an endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and is often used to treat lung cancer, bronchiectasis, or other conditions affecting the lung tissue. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for faster recovery, less pain, and reduced scarring compared to traditional open surgery. The surgeon uses specialized instruments passed through the endoscope to remove the diseased portion of the lung, and the remaining healthy tissue is then closed or staples to complete the procedure.
