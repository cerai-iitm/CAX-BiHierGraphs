Bypass Jejunum to Jejunum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the creation of a bypass between two segments of the jejunum, a part of the small intestine, to reroute the flow of digestive contents. The open approach requires a large incision in the abdomen to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visually inspect and perform the necessary repair. The bypass is constructed by connecting the proximal and distal ends of the jejunum, enabling the passage of food and nutrients while bypassing the damaged or diseased section. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal intestinal function and alleviate symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and malabsorption.
