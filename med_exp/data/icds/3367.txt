Drainage of Ampulla of Vater, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the insertion of a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end into the digestive tract to access the ampulla of Vater, a muscular valve-like structure located at the junction of the pancreatic and bile ducts. The procedure is performed to drain excess bile or pancreatic fluids that have accumulated in the ducts, which can cause symptoms such as abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. During the procedure, the endoscope is guided through the mouth and into the duodenum, where the ampulla of Vater is located, and specialized instruments are used to drain the blocked ducts. The procedure is usually performed by a trained gastroenterologist or endoscopist in a hospital or outpatient setting.
