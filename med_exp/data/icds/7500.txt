Immobilization of Head using Other Device
This procedure involves the use of a specialized device to immobilize the head, providing stabilization and restricting movement to promote healing and recovery. The device is carefully applied to the patient's head to maintain precise alignment and prevent further injury. This immobilization technique is often used to treat conditions such as cervical spine injuries, fractures, or instability. By restricting head movement, the device allows the affected area to heal properly, reducing the risk of further complications or damage.
