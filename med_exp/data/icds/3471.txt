Inspection of Liver, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting an endoscope through the skin and into the liver to visually examine the liver tissue and surrounding structures. The endoscope is equipped with a camera and light on the end, allowing the healthcare provider to view the liver tissue in real-time. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and requires only a small incision, resulting in less tissue damage and scarring compared to open surgical methods. The visualization of the liver tissue enables the diagnosis and monitoring of various liver conditions, such as liver cancer, cirrhosis, and liver cysts.
