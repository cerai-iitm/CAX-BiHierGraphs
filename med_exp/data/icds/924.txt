Restriction of Right Vertebral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through the skin to restrict blood flow in the right vertebral artery, which supplies blood to the brain. The percutaneous approach enables the doctor to access the artery through a small puncture in the skin, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery. The intraluminal device, such as a stent or coil, is carefully guided to the targeted location using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or angiography. Once in place, the device restricts the flow of blood in the right vertebral artery, helping to prevent conditions such as vertebral artery dissection or aneurysm from causing harm.
