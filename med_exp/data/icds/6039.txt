Removal of Interbody Fusion Device from Lumbar Vertebral Joint, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an interbody fusion device from the lumbar vertebral joint through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, numbness, or tingling in the lower back and legs caused by the implanted device. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the tissue and exposes the interbody fusion device, which is then removed from the lumbar vertebral joint. The incision is then closed, and the patient is recovered under post-anesthesia care.
