Fusion of 2 to 7 Thoracic Vertebral Joints with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of 2 to 7 thoracic vertebral joints using an interbody fusion device inserted through a posterior approach, which provides access to the anterior column of the spine. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to accurately place the interbody fusion device and ensure proper alignment of the vertebrae. The procedure aims to restore spinal stability, alleviate pain, and improve mobility by fusing the affected vertebral joints, which can be damaged due to degenerative conditions, trauma, or deformities. The interbody fusion device facilitates bone growth and fusion, promoting long-term spinal stability and reducing the risk of future complications.
