Removal of Tunneled Vascular Access Device from Trunk Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical removal of a tunneled vascular access device, also known as a central line, from the trunk area of the body. The device is typically implanted under the skin and fascia, and its removal requires an open surgical approach to ensure safe and effective extraction. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and may require sutures or staples to close the incision site. The removal of the vascular access device can help prevent potential complications, such as infection, and restore the patient's skin and tissue to a healthy state.
