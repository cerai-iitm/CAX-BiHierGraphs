Change Drainage Device in Hepatobiliary Duct, External Approach
During this procedure, a surgeon accesses the hepatobiliary duct through an external approach, typically through a small incision or opening, to change a drainage device that is no longer functioning properly or has become obstructed. The goal of the procedure is to restore proper bile flow and drainage from the liver and gallbladder. The surgeon carefully removes the existing device and replaces it with a new one, ensuring proper placement and function. This intervention is crucial in relieving symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and infection, and restoring overall liver function.
