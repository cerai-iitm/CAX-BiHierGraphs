Introduction of Anti-inflammatory into Joints, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an anti-inflammatory medication directly into a joint through the skin, using a small needle and syringe. The medication is typically a corticosteroid, which helps to reduce swelling and relieve pain in the affected joint. The percutaneous approach allows for accurate placement of the medication at the site of inflammation, minimizing the risk of systemic side effects. The procedure is often used to treat conditions such as arthritis, tendinitis, and bursitis, and can provide rapid relief from joint pain and stiffness.
