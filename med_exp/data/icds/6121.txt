Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Ceramic on Polyethylene Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically replacing the left hip joint with a ceramic on polyethylene synthetic substitute through an open approach, which allows for direct visualization of the joint. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement and function of the hip joint, providing relief from pain and restoring mobility. The ceramic component is wear-resistant and durable, reducing the risk of fracture and increasing the lifespan of the implant. The open approach allows for precise placement and alignment of the implant, promoting optimal recovery and outcomes.
