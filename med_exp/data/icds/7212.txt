Reattachment of Left Index Finger, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves reattaching the left index finger that has been severed or amputated. The open approach method is used, which involves making a direct incision in the skin and underlying tissues to access the affected area. The surgeon carefully reattaches the finger, reconnecting bones, tendons, and nerves to restore function and range of motion. The goal of the procedure is to achieve optimal functional and aesthetic outcomes, enabling the patient to regain normal use of their hand.
