Removal of External Fixation Device from Right Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an external fixation device previously implanted in the right tibia, also known as the shinbone. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the device, allowing the healthcare provider to carefully dissect the surrounding tissue and remove the fixator. The device is typically removed once the tibia has fully healed and stabilized, and the procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. Following removal, the surgical site is closed, and the patient is advised to follow a strict rehabilitation protocol to ensure proper healing and minimize the risk of complications.
