Insertion of Infusion Device into Superior Vena Cava, Percutaneous Approach
A minimally invasive procedure is performed where a small incision is made in the skin to access the superior vena cava, a major blood vessel located in the upper chest. A guidewire is then inserted through the incision and guided through the vessel to the desired location. An infusion device, such as a central venous catheter, is advanced over the guidewire and positioned within the superior vena cava. The device is secured in place, allowing for the administration of medications, fluids, or nutrients directly into the bloodstream.
