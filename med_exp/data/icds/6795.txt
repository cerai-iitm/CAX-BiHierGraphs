Change Drainage Device in Left Pleural Cavity, External Approach
During an external approach, a medical professional replaces a drainage device in the left pleural cavity to effectively manage fluid buildup and promote lung expansion. This procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to access the affected area and carefully removing the existing drainage device. A new device is then inserted to facilitate the drainage of fluid, such as blood, pus, or air, from the pleural space. Proper placement and securing of the new device are crucial to ensure effective drainage and minimize complications.
