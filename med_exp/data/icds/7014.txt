Inspection of Chest Wall, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to visually examine the underlying tissues and structures. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly observe any abnormalities, injuries, or diseases affecting the chest wall, such as fractures, tumors, or infections. During the procedure, the surgeon may also perform additional interventions, such as repairing damaged tissues or removing abnormal growths. The goal of this procedure is to diagnose and treat chest wall-related conditions, improving respiratory function and overall patient outcomes.
