Removal of Internal Fixation Device from Sacrum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of an internal fixation device, typically consisting of plates, screws, or rods, that were previously implanted in the sacrum to stabilize a fracture or other bone defect. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the sacrum, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the internal fixation device and carefully extract it from the bone. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may involve additional steps, such as repairing or reconstructing surrounding soft tissue or bone. The goal of the procedure is to relieve discomfort, restore normal anatomy, and promote optimal healing and function of the affected area.
