Drainage of Right Lower Arm Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the right lower arm to drain accumulated fluid, pus, or blood that has collected due to infection, injury, or other conditions. The external approach is used to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to effectively drain the accumulated material. The goal of the procedure is to promote healing, reduce the risk of further infection, and alleviate symptoms such as pain and swelling. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may require post-operative care to ensure proper wound healing.
