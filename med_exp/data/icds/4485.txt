Release Right Abdomen Muscle, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right abdomen to access the affected muscle, which is then carefully released from surrounding tissue or muscle fibers to alleviate tension or restriction. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the anatomical structures, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and address the area of constriction. The release of the muscle can help restore normal function, relieve pain, and improve range of motion. By directly visualizing the affected area, the surgeon can ensure a more accurate and effective treatment, leading to better patient outcomes.
