Drainage of Right Pleural Cavity with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device into the right pleural cavity through the skin using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to visualize the area. The goal is to drain excess fluid or air that has accumulated in the space between the lungs and chest wall, known as the pleural cavity, which can cause symptoms such as shortness of breath, chest pain, and coughing. During the procedure, the endoscope guides the insertion of the drainage device, which allows for the removal of the accumulated fluid or air, relieving pressure and promoting healing. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, resulting in less tissue trauma and a faster recovery.
