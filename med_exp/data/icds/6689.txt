Resection of Bilateral Fallopian Tubes, Via Natural or Artificial Opening With Percutaneous Endoscopic Assistance
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of both fallopian tubes through natural or artificial openings, such as the vagina or abdominal cavity, with the aid of a percutaneous endoscope. The endoscope, a thin, flexible tube with a camera and light, allows the surgeon to visualize the internal structures and guide the resection process. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be recommended for individuals with ectopic pregnancies, tubal cancer, or other conditions affecting the fallopian tubes. The percutaneous endoscopic approach generally results in smaller incisions, less post-operative pain, and faster recovery times compared to traditional open surgeries.
