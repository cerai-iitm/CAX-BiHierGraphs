Inspection of Left Lung, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting an endoscope through the skin to visually examine the left lung, allowing for direct visualization of the lung tissue and surrounding structures. The endoscope is equipped with a camera and light, providing high-quality images that help diagnose conditions such as lung cancer, infections, or inflammatory diseases. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and minimizing scarring. During the procedure, the healthcare provider may also collect tissue samples or perform other diagnostic tests to aid in diagnosis and treatment.
