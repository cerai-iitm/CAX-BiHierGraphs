Beam Radiation of Sternum using Photons 1 - 10 MeV
This therapeutic procedure involves the administration of beam radiation to the sternum, a flat bone located in the center of the chest, using photons with energies between 1 and 10 million electron volts (MeV). The goal of this treatment is to destroy cancer cells or relieve symptoms such as pain or discomfort caused by tumors in the sternum. The radiation beam is carefully directed to the targeted area to minimize exposure to surrounding healthy tissues. The photon energy range of 1-10 MeV is suitable for treating superficial or deep-seated tumors, and the radiation dose is tailored to the individual patient's needs.
