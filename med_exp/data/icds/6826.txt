Control Bleeding in Retroperitoneum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the retroperitoneum, a space behind the peritoneum that contains organs such as the kidneys, adrenal glands, and ureters. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the bleeding site and apply clotting agents, sutures, or ligatures to control hemorrhage. The goal of the procedure is to stop the bleeding, restore hemostasis, and prevent further complications such as hemorrhagic shock or organ damage. The surgeon may also repair any damaged tissues or blood vessels during the procedure to promote healing and recovery.
