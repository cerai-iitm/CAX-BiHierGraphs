Extraction of Left Tibia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of the left tibia bone through an open incision, allowing the surgeon to directly visualize the affected area. The open approach enables the surgeon to access the tibia and perform the extraction with precision and control. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to expose the tibia, carefully dissects the surrounding tissue, and then removes the bone. The procedure may be necessary to treat a range of conditions, including severe fractures, bone tumors, or infections that cannot be managed with other treatments.
