Excision of Left Upper Femur, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left thigh to access the upper part of the femur, also known as the proximal femur. The affected portion of the femur is then carefully excised, or surgically removed, to treat a variety of conditions such as tumors, fractures, or bone deformities. The surgeon will typically use specialized instruments and techniques to minimize tissue damage and ensure proper alignment of the remaining bone during the excision process. Following the removal of the affected bone segment, the incision is closed, and the patient is typically monitored for a period of time to ensure a smooth recovery.
