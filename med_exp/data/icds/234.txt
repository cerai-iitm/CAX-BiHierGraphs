Release Lumbar Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the lower back to access the lumbar region, where the affected nerve is carefully located and released from any compression or entrapment. The goal is to alleviate pain, numbness, or tingling in the lower back and legs caused by the compressed nerve. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the nerve and surrounding structures, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address the source of the problem. By releasing the nerve, the procedure aims to restore normal nerve function and improve mobility and quality of life for the patient.
