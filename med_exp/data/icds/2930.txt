Excision of Peritoneum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the peritoneum, a layer of tissue that lines the abdominal cavity and covers the abdominal organs. The diseased or damaged portion of the peritoneum is then carefully removed, taking care to avoid damaging surrounding organs and tissue. The excised tissue is sent to a laboratory for further examination and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the peritoneum and surrounding structures, ensuring a thorough excision and reducing the risk of complications.
