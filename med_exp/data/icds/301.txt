Bypass Coronary Artery, Two Arteries from Aorta with Autologous Arterial Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves bypassing two blocked coronary arteries by creating a detour using autologous arterial tissue, which is harvested from the patient's own body. The open approach requires a sternotomy, where the chest is opened to access the heart, allowing the surgeon to visualize the coronary arteries and perform the bypass. The autologous arterial tissue is grafted from the aorta to the coronary arteries, restoring blood flow to the heart muscle. By using the patient's own tissue, this procedure reduces the risk of rejection and promotes a faster recovery.
