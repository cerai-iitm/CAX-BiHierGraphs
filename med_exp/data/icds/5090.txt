Reposition Left Radius with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves correcting the alignment of the left radius bone through an open approach, which requires a surgical incision to access the affected area. Internal fixation devices, such as plates, screws, or rods, are used to stabilize and hold the bone in place, promoting proper healing and alignment. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. Post-operative care may include immobilization, physical therapy, and regular follow-up appointments to monitor the healing process.
