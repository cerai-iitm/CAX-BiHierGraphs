Fusion of Right Wrist Joint with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves fusing the right wrist joint using an autologous tissue substitute, which is a material harvested from the patient's own body, such as bone or tissue. The open approach requires an incision to access the wrist joint, allowing the surgeon to prepare the bone surfaces and insert the tissue substitute. The substitute is then secured in place using plates, screws, or other fixation devices to promote fusion and stability of the joint. Over time, the patient's body will incorporate the substitute into the natural bone, creating a strong and stable wrist joint.
