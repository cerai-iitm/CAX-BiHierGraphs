Repair Left Sclera, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the external tissue surrounding the left eye to access the sclera, the white, tough outer layer of the eyeball. The goal of the operation is to repair any damage or defects in the sclera, which may be causing vision problems or discomfort. During the procedure, the surgeon will carefully inspect the sclera to identify the area in need of repair and use specialized instruments to restore its natural shape and function. The operation is typically performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort and promote a speedy recovery.
