Repair Perineum Skin, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the restoration of the skin in the perineal region, which is the area between the anus and genitals. The external approach is utilized, where the surgeon makes an incision in the skin to access the damaged tissue. The damaged skin is then repaired using sutures, staples, or other closure methods to restore the integrity of the skin and promote healing. This procedure is often performed to treat skin tears, lacerations, or other forms of trauma to the perineal area.
