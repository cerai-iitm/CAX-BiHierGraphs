Drainage of Cerebral Ventricle with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a drainage device through the skin and into the cerebral ventricle to remove excess cerebrospinal fluid that has accumulated due to injury, infection, or other conditions. The percutaneous approach allows for a quicker recovery time and less tissue damage compared to open surgery. The drainage device is carefully guided into position using imaging technology, such as ultrasound or fluoroscopy, to ensure accurate placement and minimal risk of complications. Once in place, the device helps to alleviate pressure on the brain and relieve symptoms such as headache, nausea, and confusion.
