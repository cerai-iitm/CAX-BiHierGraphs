Inspection of Peritoneal Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the abdominal wall to gain access to the peritoneal cavity, a space between the walls of the abdomen and pelvis. A thin, lighted tube with a camera, called an endoscope, is then inserted through the incision to visually examine the cavity and surrounding organs. The procedure allows for the identification of any abnormalities, such as adhesions, inflammation, or tumors, and may also be used to collect tissue samples for biopsy. The percutaneous approach is typically performed under local anesthesia and has fewer risks and less recovery time compared to open surgical procedures.
