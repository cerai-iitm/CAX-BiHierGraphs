Destruction of Cecum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This endoscopic procedure involves the destruction of the cecum, a part of the large intestine, through a natural or artificial opening. The cecum is located at the beginning of the large intestine and plays a role in the digestion and absorption of nutrients. During the procedure, an endoscope is inserted through the natural opening, such as the mouth or anus, or through an artificial opening created surgically, to reach the cecum. Once the cecum is visualized, specialized instruments are used to destroy the tissue, which may be done to treat conditions such as cancer, inflammation, or obstruction.
