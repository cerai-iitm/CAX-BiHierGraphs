Insertion of Totally Implantable Vascular Access Device into Chest Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the chest area to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, where a totally implantable vascular access device is then inserted. The device is a small, flexible tube that provides a pathway for administering medications, drawing blood, or delivering nutrients directly into the bloodstream. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the surgical site, ensuring precise placement of the device. Once in place, the device is secured and the incision is closed, providing a safe and convenient way to access the vascular system for ongoing medical treatment.
