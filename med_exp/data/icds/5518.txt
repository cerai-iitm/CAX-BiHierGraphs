Reposition Right Toe Phalanx with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves realigning a dislocated or malaligned phalanx bone in the right toe through an open approach, where the skin is incised to access the affected area. The repositioning is accomplished with the aid of an internal fixation device, which provides stability and support to the bone as it heals. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and pain. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal toe function, alleviate pain, and improve overall mobility and alignment of the foot.
