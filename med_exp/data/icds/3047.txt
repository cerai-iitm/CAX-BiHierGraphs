Release Ileum, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, inserted through the mouth or nose to release the ileum, the lower part of the small intestine, from adhesions or blockages. The endoscope is guided through the digestive tract to the site of the obstruction, where specialized instruments are used to carefully release the ileum, restoring normal digestive function. This percutaneous procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be used to treat conditions such as small bowel obstruction or Crohn's disease. The benefits of this approach include minimal scarring, reduced risk of complications, and a shorter recovery time compared to traditional open surgery.
