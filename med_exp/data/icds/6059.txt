Removal of Spacer from Left Knee Joint, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left knee joint to remove a previously implanted spacer, typically used to maintain joint space and facilitate bone growth. The spacer is carefully extracted from the joint, and any surrounding tissue or debris is cleaned and irrigated to prevent infection. Once the spacer is removed, the joint is thoroughly examined to ensure any complications or damage are addressed. The incision is then closed, and the knee is stabilized to facilitate a smooth recovery.
