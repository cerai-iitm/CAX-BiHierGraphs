Reposition Right Femoral Shaft with Monoplanar External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous repositioning of the right femoral shaft, utilizing a monoplanar external fixation device to restore proper alignment and stabilize the bone. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal tissue disruption and reduced risk of complications. During the procedure, the fixation device is inserted through small incisions in the skin, and once in place, it provides stabilization and support to the repositioned femoral shaft. The goal of this procedure is to achieve anatomical alignment and promote healing of the affected bone.
