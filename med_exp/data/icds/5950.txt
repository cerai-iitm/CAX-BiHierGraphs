Fusion of Lumbosacral Joint with Interbody Fusion Device, Posterior Approach, Anterior Column, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the fusion of the lumbosacral joint, which connects the fifth lumbar vertebra to the sacrum, to stabilize and alleviate pain in the lower back. The procedure is performed through a posterior approach, meaning the surgeon accesses the spine from the back, and utilizes an interbody fusion device to facilitate bone growth. An open approach is used, where a larger incision is made to expose the surgical site, allowing for direct visualization of the spine. The goal of the procedure is to restore spinal stability and alleviate symptoms such as back pain, numbness, or weakness in the legs.
