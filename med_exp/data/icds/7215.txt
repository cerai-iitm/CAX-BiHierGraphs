Reattachment of Right Ring Finger, Open Approach
The reattachment of a severed right ring finger involves a complex surgical procedure that requires meticulous attention to detail and precise technique. Through an open approach, the surgeon carefully re-connects the finger, restoring its natural anatomy and function. This may involve repairing damaged tendons, nerves, and blood vessels, as well as re-aligning and stabilizing the bone structure. The goal of the procedure is to restore optimal finger function and sensation, allowing the patient to regain full use of their hand.
