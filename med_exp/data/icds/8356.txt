Replacement of Aortic Valve using Zooplastic Tissue, Rapid Deployment Technique, Percutaneous Approach, New Technology Group 2
This minimally invasive procedure involves replacing a diseased or damaged aortic valve with a zooplastic tissue valve using a rapid deployment technique through a small incision in the skin. The procedure is performed through a percutaneous approach, where a catheter is inserted through the femoral artery in the leg, and guided to the heart using fluoroscopy and echocardiography. Once in place, the valve is expanded and secured, allowing for improved blood flow and cardiac function. This innovative technology enables a less traumatic and faster recovery compared to traditional open-heart surgery.
