Reposition Left Tibia with Monoplanar External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves the repositioning of the left tibia, which is a shinbone in the lower leg, to its correct anatomical alignment. A monoplanar external fixation device is used to immobilize and stabilize the bone, allowing for proper healing. The percutaneous approach is a minimally invasive technique, where small incisions are made in the skin to insert the fixation device. This procedure is typically performed under general or regional anesthesia to manage pain and discomfort during the operation.
