Insertion of Other Device into Hepatobiliary Duct, Percutaneous Approach
During this procedure, a physician uses imaging guidance to insert a device, such as a stent or catheter, into the hepatobiliary duct through the skin. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive insertion, reducing the risk of complications and promoting a faster recovery. Once inserted, the device helps to relieve blockages or strictures in the duct, allowing for the proper drainage of bile from the liver and gallbladder. The device may also be used to deliver medications or other treatments directly to the affected area, providing targeted therapy to the patient.
