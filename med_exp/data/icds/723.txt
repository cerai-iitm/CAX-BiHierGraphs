Excision of Right Internal Mammary Artery, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This procedure involves the excision of the internal mammary artery located on the right side of the body through a minimally invasive percutaneous endoscopic approach. The internal mammary artery is a vital vessel that supplies oxygenated blood to the heart, making this procedure a delicate and complex one. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows for a smaller incision and reduced tissue trauma compared to traditional open surgery. During the procedure, a small camera and specialized instruments are inserted through tiny incisions to visualize and dissect the artery, allowing the surgeon to carefully remove the targeted section.
