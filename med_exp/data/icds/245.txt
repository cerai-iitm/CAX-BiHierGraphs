Repair Radial Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the radial nerve, which is responsible for controlling movement and sensation in the arm and hand. The goal of the repair is to restore normal function and alleviate symptoms such as numbness, weakness, or paralysis in the affected area. During the operation, the surgeon carefully examines and treats any damage to the nerve, which may involve reattaching or reconstructing nerve fibers. Following the repair, the incision is closed and the patient may require post-operative therapy to regain strength and mobility in the affected limb.
