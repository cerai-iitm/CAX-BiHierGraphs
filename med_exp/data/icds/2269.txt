Extirpation of Matter from Right Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope to access the right upper lung lobe through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the removal of unwanted material or debris. The endoscope, equipped with a specialized instrument, is carefully guided through the airways to reach the affected area, enabling the precise extraction of the targeted matter. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation, and may be accompanied by imaging guidance such as fluoroscopy or CT scans to ensure accurate visualization and localization. By removing the obstructing material, this procedure can help restore lung function and alleviate symptoms such as coughing, wheezing, or shortness of breath.
