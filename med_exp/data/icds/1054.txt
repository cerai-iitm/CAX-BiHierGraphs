Dilation of Right External Iliac Artery with Two Intraluminal Devices, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of two intraluminal devices to dilate the narrowed or blocked right external iliac artery, allowing for improved blood flow to the lower extremities. Through a percutaneous approach, a small incision is made in the skin, and a catheter is guided through the femoral artery to reach the affected area. The two devices, such as balloons or stents, are then deployed to expand the artery and restore its normal diameter. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure precise placement of the devices.
