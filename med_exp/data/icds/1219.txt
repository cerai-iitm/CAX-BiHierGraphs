Insertion of Monitoring Device into Lower Artery, Percutaneous Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

A minimally invasive procedure is performed to insert a monitoring device into the lower artery, using a percutaneous approach. This involves making a small incision in the skin and guiding the device through the artery using imaging guidance, such as fluoroscopy or ultrasound. The device is designed to monitor various physiological parameters, including blood pressure, oxygen saturation, and flow rate. The procedure is typically performed in an interventional radiology or vascular surgery setting under local anesthesia.
