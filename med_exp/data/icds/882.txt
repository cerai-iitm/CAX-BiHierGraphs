Supplement Right Ulnar Artery with Autologous Tissue Substitute, Open Approach
During this open surgical procedure, a portion of the right ulnar artery is repaired or reinforced with an autologous tissue substitute, which is tissue taken from the patient's own body. The surgeon makes an incision to access the affected artery and carefully supplements the damaged or weakened section with the tissue substitute, restoring blood flow and promoting healing. This procedure is often performed to treat arterial blockages, aneurysms, or trauma-induced injuries. The use of autologous tissue minimizes the risk of rejection and promotes a more natural and efficient healing process.
