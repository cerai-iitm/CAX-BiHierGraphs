Extirpation of Matter from Right Ureter, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of unwanted material, such as kidney stones or debris, from the right ureter through a percutaneous endoscopic approach. Under imaging guidance, a small incision is made in the skin, allowing a thin, flexible scope to be inserted into the ureter to visualize the area. Using specialized instruments, the obstructing material is then carefully extracted from the ureter, allowing for the restoration of normal urinary function. This procedure is typically performed under anesthesia and may require a hospital stay, depending on individual circumstances.
