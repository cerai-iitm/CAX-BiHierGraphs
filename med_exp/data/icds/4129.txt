Extraction of Right Lower Arm Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the right lower arm to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue beneath the skin. The open approach allows the surgeon to visualize the area directly, facilitating the extraction of the targeted tissue and fascia. The procedure may be performed to treat various conditions, such as infections, tumors, or trauma, that affect the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the right lower arm. The goal of the procedure is to remove the affected tissue and fascia to promote healing, relieve symptoms, and restore function to the affected area.
