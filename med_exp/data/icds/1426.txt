Excision of Left Brachial Vein, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the left brachial vein through small incisions using an endoscope, a thin tube with a camera and light, to visualize the vein. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and takes around 30-60 minutes to complete. The surgeon makes a small incision in the skin near the affected vein and inserts the endoscope to locate the vein, then uses specialized instruments to carefully excise the vein. After the procedure, the incision is closed with sutures or staples, and the patient is usually able to return to normal activities within a few days.
