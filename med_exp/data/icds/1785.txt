Extraction of Left Axillary Lymphatic, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves using a minimally invasive percutaneous approach to extract tissue or cells from the left axillary lymph node for diagnostic purposes. A small incision is made in the skin, and a fine needle or probe is inserted through the incision to access the lymph node. The tissue or cells are then collected through the needle or probe, and sent to a laboratory for further examination and analysis. The goal of this procedure is to help diagnose certain conditions, such as cancer or infection, by examining the lymphatic tissue.
