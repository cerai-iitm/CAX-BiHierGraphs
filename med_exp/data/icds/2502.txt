Excision of Tongue, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision in the mouth to access the tongue, followed by the removal of all or part of the tongue, depending on the extent of the disease or lesion. The surgical approach is typically performed under general anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure optimal visualization of the surgical site. The surgeon carefully dissects the tongue tissue and identifies the affected area, taking care to preserve surrounding nerves and blood vessels. The excised tissue is then sent to a pathology laboratory for further examination and diagnosis.
