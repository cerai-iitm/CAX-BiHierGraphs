Replacement of Right Hip Joint, Femoral Surface with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the femoral surface of the right hip joint with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, where the surgeon makes a incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the joint. The goal is to restore function and alleviate pain caused by damaged or diseased joint surfaces. The synthetic substitute is designed to mimic the natural movement of the hip joint and provide a durable and long-lasting solution. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the joint and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately position the synthetic substitute and ensure a proper fit.
