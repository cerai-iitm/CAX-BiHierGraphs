Resection of Left Large Intestine, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Here is the detailed description:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the removal of a portion of the left large intestine, also known as the descending or sigmoid colon, through an endoscope inserted through the skin. The percutaneous endoscopic approach allows the surgeon to access the intestine through a small incision, reducing recovery time and scarring. The resection is typically performed to treat conditions such as cancer, diverticulitis, or blockages in the intestine. Following the procedure, the remaining intestine is reattached, and the incision is closed, allowing the patient to recover and eventually return to normal bowel function.
