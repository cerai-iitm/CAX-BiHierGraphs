Drainage of Right Lung, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through a natural or artificial opening to drain fluids or air from the right lung. The endoscope is equipped with a camera and light, allowing the physician to visualize the lung and identify the area requiring drainage. A suction device or drainage tube is then inserted through the endoscope to remove the accumulated fluids or gases, helping to restore normal lung function and alleviate respiratory distress. The entire procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance to ensure precise placement of the drainage device.
