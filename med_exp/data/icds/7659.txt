Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Heart, Open Approach
During this procedure, a therapeutic substance is introduced directly into the heart through an open approach, which involves making an incision to access the heart. The substance is administered to treat specific cardiac conditions, such as coronary artery disease or congestive heart failure. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the heart and precise administration of the substance to the affected area. This procedure can be used to deliver medications, stem cells, or other therapeutic agents to the heart tissue to promote healing and improve cardiac function.
