Excision of Right Upper Lung Lobe, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This procedure involves the removal of the right upper lung lobe through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, which is a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth or a small incision, allowing the surgeon to visualize the lung tissue and perform the excision. The diagnostic excision is typically performed to remove a diseased or damaged portion of the lung lobe, and may be used to treat conditions such as lung cancer or bronchiectasis. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may involve a hospital stay to monitor for post-operative complications.
