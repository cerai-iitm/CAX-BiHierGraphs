Reposition Right Ulna with Intramedullary Internal Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This surgical procedure involves the realignment and stabilization of the right ulna bone using an intramedullary internal fixation device, which is inserted through a small incision in the skin. The percutaneous approach allows for minimal tissue damage and reduces the risk of complications. The device is designed to provide structural support and promote healing of the ulna, while also allowing for early mobility and rehabilitation. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal anatomy and function to the affected limb.
