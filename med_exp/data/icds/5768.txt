Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Right Shoulder Joint, Open Approach
This procedure involves surgically opening the right shoulder joint to remove a synthetic substitute, such as an artificial joint or implant, that has been previously inserted. The goal of this procedure is to remove the offending synthetic material and any surrounding tissue that may be damaged or infected. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the joint and surrounding tissues, ensuring a thorough removal of the synthetic substitute. After removal, the joint is closed and the site is cleaned and dressed to promote healing.
