Excision of Abdominal Wall, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of a lesion or tissue from the abdominal wall through a small incision using endoscopic visualization. Under local anesthesia and image guidance, a thin tube with a camera and specialized instruments is inserted through the skin to access the lesion, allowing the surgeon to excise the targeted tissue with precision and control. The percutaneous endoscopic approach reduces trauma to surrounding tissues and minimizes scarring, promoting faster recovery and less discomfort for the patient. This diagnostic excisional biopsy is often performed to diagnose and treat conditions such as abdominal wall tumors, cysts, or hernias.
