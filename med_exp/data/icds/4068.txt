Excision of Left Hand Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin of the left hand to access the underlying subcutaneous tissue and fascia. The damaged or diseased tissue and fascia are then carefully excised, removing any affected or infected areas. The procedure is typically performed under anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure proper removal of the affected tissue. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal function and mobility to the left hand while promoting healthy healing and tissue regeneration.
