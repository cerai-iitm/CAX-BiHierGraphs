Excision of Duodenum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light, to access the duodenum through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope guides the surgeon to the affected area, allowing them to visualize the duodenum and identify the segment that needs to be removed. The duodenum is then carefully dissected and excised using specialized instruments, and the remaining tissue is closed to restore normal anatomy and function. The endoscopic approach enables a less traumatic and more precise excision of the duodenum, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery.
