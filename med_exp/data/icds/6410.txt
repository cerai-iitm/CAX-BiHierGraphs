Release Left Ureter, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the left ureter, which is a narrow tube that carries urine from the kidney to the bladder. The goal of the procedure is to release the left ureter from any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstructions that may be impeding its function. The surgeon will carefully identify and dissect the affected area to free the ureter and restore its normal anatomy and function. The procedure is usually performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay to monitor for any postoperative complications.
