Release Thoracic Nerve, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision to access the thoracic nerve, a complex network of nerves that transmit signals between the spine and chest cavity. The goal of the operation is to release compression or pressure on the thoracic nerve, which can be causing pain, numbness, or tingling in the chest, back, or abdomen. During the procedure, the surgeon carefully dissects the surrounding tissue to expose the affected nerve, then releases any adhesions, scar tissue, or other obstructions that may be constricting the nerve. Once the nerve is fully released, the incision is closed, and the patient can expect significant improvement in their symptoms.
