Dilation of Coronary Artery, Two Arteries, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves expanding the diameter of two coronary arteries to restore blood flow and relieve blockages causing chest pain or heart attack symptoms. Through a small incision in the wrist or groin, a catheter is guided through the blood vessels to the affected coronary arteries, where a balloon or other device is used to dilate the artery, improving blood flow to the heart muscle. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance, and may be combined with stent placement or other interventions to maintain artery patency. Successful dilation can significantly reduce symptoms and improve long-term cardiovascular health.
