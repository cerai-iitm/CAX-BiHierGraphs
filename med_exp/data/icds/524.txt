Removal of Short-term External Heart Assist System from Heart, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous removal of a short-term external heart assist system from the heart, which is a device that provides temporary circulatory support to the heart. The system is typically inserted through a small incision in the groin area and is connected to the heart through a tube. The removal process requires careful manipulation to avoid damaging surrounding tissues and blood vessels. Once the device is removed, the incision site is closed, and the patient is monitored for any complications.
