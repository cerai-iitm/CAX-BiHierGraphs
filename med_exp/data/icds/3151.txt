Reposition Small Intestine, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the manual repositioning of the small intestine into its normal anatomical position through an open incision in the abdominal wall. The procedure is typically performed to alleviate intestinal obstruction, volvulus, or twisting of the intestine, which can cause severe abdominal pain, nausea, and vomiting. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the intestine and surrounding tissues, enabling precise repositioning and correction of any underlying anatomical anomalies. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal intestinal function, alleviate symptoms, and prevent potential complications such as bowel strangulation or perforation.
