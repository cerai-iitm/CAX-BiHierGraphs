Fluoroscopy of Bile Ducts using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure utilizes low osmolar contrast to visualize the bile ducts, allowing for a more precise evaluation of the biliary system. During the procedure, a small amount of contrast agent is injected into the bile ducts, and then fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, is used to capture real-time images of the ducts. This enables healthcare providers to assess the morphology and function of the bile ducts, and diagnose conditions such as blockages, stones, or tumors. The use of low osmolar contrast minimizes the risk of adverse reactions and improves the overall safety of the procedure.
