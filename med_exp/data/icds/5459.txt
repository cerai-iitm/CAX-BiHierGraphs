Reposition Right Tibia with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the right tibia, also known as the shinbone, to its correct anatomical alignment. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manipulate the bone and restore its proper position. An internal fixation device, such as a plate, rod, or screws, is then implanted to hold the bone in place and facilitate healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal function and stability to the affected leg, alleviating pain and discomfort caused by the misaligned tibia.
