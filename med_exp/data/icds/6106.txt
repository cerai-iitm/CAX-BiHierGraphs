Replacement of Right Hip Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Cemented, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the damaged or diseased right hip joint with a synthetic substitute, securely fixed in place using bone cement. The open approach requires a larger incision to access the joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the area and accurately position the implant. The cemented fixation provides immediate stability, enabling rapid recovery and weight-bearing activities. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, restore mobility, and improve overall hip function in patients with severe hip joint damage or degeneration.
