Fluoroscopy of Bilateral Internal Carotid Arteries using Other Contrast
This fluoroscopy procedure involves the use of a contrast agent, other than the standard ones, to visualize the bilateral internal carotid arteries. The internal carotid arteries are responsible for supplying oxygenated blood to the brain, and fluoroscopy allows for real-time imaging of these vessels. The procedure is performed under continuous X-ray guidance, enabling the physician to accurately assess the anatomy and patency of the internal carotid arteries. The use of an alternative contrast agent may be necessary in patients with allergies or contraindications to standard contrast media.
