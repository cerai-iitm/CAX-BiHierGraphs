Drainage of Right Pleural Cavity, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the insertion of a thin tube through the skin and into the right pleural cavity, using an endoscope to guide the process, to drain excess fluid or air that has accumulated in the space between the lung and chest wall. The endoscope provides visualization of the area, allowing the healthcare provider to accurately locate the site of fluid accumulation and safely insert the drainage tube. Once in place, the tube allows for the removal of the excess fluid or air, helping to alleviate symptoms such as shortness of breath and chest pain. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be done in an outpatient setting or hospital.
