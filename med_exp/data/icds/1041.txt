Dilation of Left Common Iliac Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small balloon-tipped catheter through a skin incision in the groin area to access the left common iliac artery. The balloon is then inflated to widen the artery, improving blood flow and relieving any blockages or narrowing. Once the artery is dilated, the balloon is deflated and removed, leaving the artery open and unobstructed. The percutaneous approach eliminates the need for open surgery, reducing recovery time and scarring.
