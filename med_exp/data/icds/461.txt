Insertion of Short-term External Heart Assist System into Heart, Open Approach
This procedure involves the surgical insertion of a short-term external heart assist system, also known as an extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) system, directly into the heart to support cardiac function. The open approach requires a thoracotomy, a surgical incision in the chest, to access the heart and enable the insertion of the device. The ECMO system takes over the pumping function of the heart, allowing the heart to rest and recover from underlying conditions such as cardiogenic shock, cardiac arrest, or heart failure. The device is temporary and can be removed once the heart has recovered sufficiently to resume its normal function.
