Transfer Stomach to Esophagus, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
This procedure involves the percutaneous endoscopic transfer of the stomach to the esophagus, a complex operation requiring great precision. The endoscope is inserted through the mouth and guided to the esophagus, where the stomach is then attached using specialized instruments and techniques. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal esophageal function and alleviate symptoms associated with esophageal disease or injury. Recovery may involve a hospital stay and post-operative care to ensure proper healing and minimize complications.
