Drainage of Small Intestine, Open Approach
The surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the small intestine, which is then drained of any blockages, abscesses, or infection. This open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the intestine and perform any necessary repairs or resections. The drainage process helps to alleviate symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain, and can be performed in cases of intestinal obstruction, Crohn's disease, or other conditions that affect the small intestine. After the procedure, the incision is closed, and the patient is monitored for any post-operative complications.
