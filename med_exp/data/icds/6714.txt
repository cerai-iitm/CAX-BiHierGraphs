Restriction of Cervix, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves using various techniques to narrow or constrict the cervix, which is the lower part of the uterus that opens into the vagina, to prevent or reduce bleeding or to promote healing. The restriction of the cervix can be performed through natural or artificial openings, and may be done using sutures, staples, or other devices. The goal of this procedure is to promote hemostasis, or stop bleeding, and to support the cervix to prevent further complications. This procedure may be performed in cases of cervical lacerations, cervical incompetence, or other obstetric complications.
