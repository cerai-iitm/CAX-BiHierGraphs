Extirpation of Matter from Left Atrium, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest cavity to access the left atrium, a chamber of the heart, with the purpose of removing unwanted matter or substances. The open approach allows for direct visualization and manipulation of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to extract the unwanted material with precision. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia to ensure the patient's comfort and safety. The removal of unwanted matter from the left atrium can help restore normal heart function and prevent potential complications.
