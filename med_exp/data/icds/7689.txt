Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Upper GI, Percutaneous Endoscopic Approach
Percutaneous endoscopic approach is used to introduce therapeutic substances into the upper gastrointestinal (GI) tract, which includes the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum. This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a light and camera, to guide the insertion of the substance through the skin and into the upper GI tract. The therapeutic substance is injected or infused through the endoscope, allowing for targeted treatment of conditions such as gastrointestinal bleeding, ulcers, or cancer. This approach offers an effective and safe alternative to traditional open surgery, reducing the risk of complications and promoting faster recovery.
