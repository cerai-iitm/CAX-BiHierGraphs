Drainage of Back Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating an incision in the back to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue that surround muscles and other structures. The goal of the procedure is to drain excess fluid, blood, or pus that may have accumulated in these tissues, often as a result of infection or trauma. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually examine the affected area and remove any infected or damaged tissue, promoting healing and reducing the risk of further complications. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or conscious sedation to minimize patient discomfort.
