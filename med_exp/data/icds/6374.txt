Extirpation of Matter from Right Ureter, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to remove matter or blockages from the right ureter. The endoscope is inserted through a natural or artificial opening, such as the urethra or a previously created stent, and guided to the affected area of the ureter. Once the blockage is located, specialized instruments are used to remove the matter, and the endoscope is then removed. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia or sedation to minimize discomfort and promote a quick recovery.
