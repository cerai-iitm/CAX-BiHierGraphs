Reattachment of Left Ankle Tendon, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the left ankle to reattach a torn or detached tendon, restoring its natural function and anatomy. The surgeon carefully exposes the affected area, debridement of damaged tissue and debris, and then reattaches the tendon using sutures or other fixation devices. The goal of this procedure is to alleviate pain, improve ankle mobility, and enhance overall foot function. Post-operatively, the patient may require a period of immobilization and rehabilitation to ensure optimal healing and recovery.
