Drainage of Common Bile Duct, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic, Diagnostic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of an endoscope through the mouth or a small incision to access the common bile duct, allowing for the drainage of bile and other fluids from the duct. The endoscope is equipped with specialized instruments that facilitate the creation of a small opening in the duct, enabling the removal of blockages or stone debris. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation and fluoroscopic guidance, ensuring precise navigation and visualization of the duct. By restoring proper bile flow, this procedure can effectively alleviate symptoms such as jaundice, abdominal pain, and vomiting.
