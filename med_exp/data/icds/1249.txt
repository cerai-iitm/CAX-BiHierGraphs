Occlusion of Right Internal Iliac Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a small intraluminal device through a percutaneous approach to occlude the right internal iliac artery. The device is guided through a catheter to the target site, where it is designed to block blood flow and prevent further bleeding or leakage. The procedure is typically performed under fluoroscopic guidance to ensure accurate placement of the device, and may be used to treat conditions such as aneurysms, arteriovenous malformations, or traumatic injuries. By occluding the artery, the device helps to prevent further complications and promotes healing.
