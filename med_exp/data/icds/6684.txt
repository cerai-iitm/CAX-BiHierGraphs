Resection of Left Fallopian Tube, Via Natural or Artificial Opening With Percutaneous Endoscopic Assistance
This minimally invasive surgical procedure involves the removal of the left fallopian tube through a small incision, using a percutaneous endoscopic approach. The surgeon inserts a thin, lighted tube with a camera, known as an endoscope, through a natural or artificial opening to visualize the fallopian tube and guide the resection. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may be done laparoscopically, utilizing small abdominal incisions, or through a vaginal approach. The goal of the procedure is to remove the affected fallopian tube, which can be necessary to treat conditions such as ectopic pregnancy, ovarian cancer, or chronic pelvic pain.
