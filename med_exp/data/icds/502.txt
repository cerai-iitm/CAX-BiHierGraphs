Occlusion of Left Atrial Appendage with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the insertion of a device through a small incision in the leg, which is then guided through the blood vessels to the left atrial appendage, a small pouch in the upper chamber of the heart. The device is designed to obstruct the flow of blood into the appendage, reducing the risk of blood clots forming and stroke. Once in place, the device is expanded and anchored to the surrounding tissue, and the incision is closed. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and fluoroscopic guidance, allowing for real-time visualization of the device placement.
