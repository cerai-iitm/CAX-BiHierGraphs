Restriction of Left Vertebral Artery with Intraluminal Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a small intraluminal device through a tiny incision in the skin to restrict blood flow through the left vertebral artery. The device is guided through the artery using fluoroscopy or other imaging guidance to ensure accurate placement. The procedure is typically performed to reduce blood flow to a specific area, such as an aneurysm or arteriovenous malformation, to prevent rupture or reduce symptoms. The percutaneous approach minimizes trauma to the surrounding tissue and reduces the risk of complications.
