Reposition Left Maxilla with Internal Fixation Device, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves repositioning the left maxilla, the upper jawbone, to its correct anatomical position using an internal fixation device. The open approach requires a surgical incision to access the affected area, allowing the surgeon to manually reposition the maxilla and secure it with a device such as a plate or screw. This procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as facial trauma, congenital deformities, or tumors that affect the maxilla. The goal of the procedure is to restore the natural alignment and function of the facial bones, improving the patient's overall facial structure and appearance.
