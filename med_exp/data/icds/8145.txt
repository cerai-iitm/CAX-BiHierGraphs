Fluoroscopy of Superior Vena Cava, Guidance
This fluoroscopically guided procedure involves the use of continuous X-ray beams to produce real-time images of the superior vena cava, allowing physicians to visualize the internal structures of the vessel. During the procedure, the fluoroscope is positioned to capture images of the superior vena cava, which is the large vein that carries deoxygenated blood from the upper body to the heart. The guidance provided by fluoroscopy enables precise navigation and manipulation of instruments within the vessel, facilitating the diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the superior vena cava. The real-time imaging capabilities of fluoroscopy also minimize the risk of complications and ensure accurate instrument placement.
