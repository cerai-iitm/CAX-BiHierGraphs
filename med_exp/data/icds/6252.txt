Change Drainage Device in Ureter, External Approach
This procedure involves accessing the ureter through an external approach to replace or exchange a drainage device, such as a ureteral stent or catheter, that is used to divert urine flow and relieve obstruction or blockage in the urinary tract. The external approach allows the healthcare provider to visually inspect the ureter and surrounding tissue to ensure correct placement of the new device. The old device is carefully removed, and the new one is inserted through the same incision site, minimizing tissue trauma and promoting optimal healing. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal urinary function and alleviate symptoms such as pain, flank tenderness, and frequent urination.
