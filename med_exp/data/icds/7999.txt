Fluoroscopy of Right Common Carotid Artery using Other Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy, a continuous X-ray beam, to visualize the right common carotid artery in real-time. A contrast agent, other than the standard iodine-based contrast, is administered to enhance the visibility of the artery and its surrounding structures. The fluoroscopic images are then used to guide the physician in diagnosing and treating conditions affecting the right common carotid artery, such as stenosis or occlusion. The procedure is typically performed in a hospital setting by an interventional radiologist or vascular surgeon.
