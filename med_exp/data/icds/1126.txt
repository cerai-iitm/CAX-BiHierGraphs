Dilation of Right Peroneal Artery, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves accessing the right peroneal artery through the skin to widen the narrowed or blocked vessel, restoring blood flow to the lower leg. A small incision is made in the skin to introduce a balloon-tipped catheter, which is guided through the artery to the blockage site. The balloon is then inflated to compress the plaque or blockage, and the catheter is removed once the artery is widened. This percutaneous approach offers a safer and less painful alternative to open surgery, with faster recovery times and reduced risk of complications.
