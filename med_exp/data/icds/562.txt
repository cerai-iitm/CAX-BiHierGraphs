Replacement of Aortic Valve with Synthetic Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a catheter through an artery in the leg or chest, guiding it to the aortic valve, and replacing the diseased valve with a synthetic substitute. The new valve is expanded using a balloon, allowing it to take over the function of the old valve and ensuring proper blood flow from the heart. During the procedure, fluoroscopy and echocardiography are used to guide the doctor and confirm the correct placement of the new valve. The entire procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and takes around 1-2 hours to complete.
