Excision of Face Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the skin to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia of the face. The damaged or diseased tissue and fascia are then carefully excised, or cut out, to remove the affected area. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the targeted tissue and fascia. The resulting wound is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, to promote healing and minimize scarring.
