Fluoroscopy of Left Internal Carotid Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the left internal carotid artery, which supplies blood to the brain. A low-osmolar contrast medium is injected into the artery to enhance image quality and allow for better visualization of any blockages or stenosis. The procedure is typically performed by an interventional radiologist or neurointerventionalist and is used to diagnose and assess conditions such as carotid artery disease, stroke, and transient ischemic attack (TIA). The resulting images provide valuable information for treatment planning and management of cerebrovascular disease.
