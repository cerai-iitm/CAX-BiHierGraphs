Performance of Urinary Filtration, Multiple
This medical procedure involves the simultaneous filtration of multiple urine samples to remove waste and excess substances, typically performed to assess kidney function or diagnose underlying urinary tract disorders. The filtration process helps to isolate and identify specific components of the urine, such as bacteria, proteins, or blood cells, which can indicate infections, diseases, or other health issues. During the procedure, a sterile filter is used to separate the urine into its various components, allowing healthcare professionals to analyze and interpret the results. The filtered samples are then examined under a microscope or using specialized equipment to provide accurate and detailed information for further diagnosis and treatment.
