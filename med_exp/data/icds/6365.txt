Extirpation of Matter from Left Kidney, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin to access the left kidney, allowing the surgeon to manually remove abnormal or foreign material from the kidney. The open approach provides direct visualization of the kidney, enabling the removal of the unwanted matter and any other debris that may be present. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal kidney function by eliminating any obstructions or harmful substances that may be affecting kidney health. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia and may require a hospital stay for post-operative care and recovery.
