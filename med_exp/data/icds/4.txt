Destruction of Dura Mater, Open Approach
This procedure involves making an incision to access the dura mater, a protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord, to intentionally damage or destroy a portion of it. The open approach requires a craniotomy or surgical opening of the skull to expose the affected area, allowing the surgeon to visualize and precisely target the damaged dura mater. Destruction of the dura mater may be necessary to relieve pressure, remove a tumor or lesion, or repair a tear or defect. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal cerebrospinal fluid flow, alleviate symptoms, and promote healing.
