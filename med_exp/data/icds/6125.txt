Replacement of Left Hip Joint with Synthetic Substitute, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves replacing the left hip joint with a synthetic substitute through an open approach, utilizing a surgical incision to access the affected area. The procedure typically begins with the administration of general anesthesia, followed by the dislocation of the femur from the hip socket to facilitate access to the damaged joint. The damaged or diseased portions of the hip joint are then removed, and the synthetic substitute is implanted, secured in place with bone cement or a porous surface to facilitate integration with the surrounding bone. The surgical site is then closed, and the patient is typically required to undergo a period of rehabilitation to restore normal hip function.
