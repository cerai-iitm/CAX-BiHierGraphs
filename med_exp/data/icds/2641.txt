Change Drainage Device in Upper Intestinal Tract, External Approach
This procedure involves replacing a drainage device in the upper part of the gastrointestinal tract, such as the esophagus, stomach, or small intestine, through an external approach. The drainage device is typically used to relieve blockages or obstructions in the upper intestinal tract, allowing fluids and semi-solid contents to drain out of the body. During the procedure, the healthcare provider makes an incision in the skin to access the affected area and exchanges the existing drainage device with a new one. The new device is carefully placed to ensure proper drainage and to prevent complications such as infection or leakage.
