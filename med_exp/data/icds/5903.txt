Excision of Left Knee Joint, Open Approach, Diagnostic
During an open surgical procedure, the left knee joint is thoroughly examined and explored to identify any abnormalities or disease processes. This diagnostic excision involves surgical incision into the knee joint to collect tissue samples or remove abnormal tissue for further examination. The procedure allows for a detailed evaluation of the joint's internal structures, enabling accurate diagnosis and effective treatment planning. The goal of this procedure is to obtain a precise diagnosis, which can inform subsequent treatment decisions and improve patient outcomes.
