Excision of Right Upper Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach, Diagnostic
This surgical procedure involves the removal of subcutaneous tissue and fascia from the right upper leg through an open approach, which means a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The goal of this diagnostic procedure is to examine the tissue and fascia for any abnormalities or diseases, such as cancer or infection. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a careful incision in the right upper leg to expose the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which is then carefully excised and sent to a laboratory for further examination. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort.
