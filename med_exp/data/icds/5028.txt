Insertion of External Fixation Device into Left Finger Phalanx, Percutaneous Approach
During this minimally invasive procedure, a surgeon inserts a small external fixation device through the skin and into the phalanx bone of the left finger. The device is typically composed of pins or wires connected to an external frame, which provides stability and alignment to the bone as it heals. The percutaneous approach involves making small incisions in the skin, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster recovery. This procedure is often used to treat finger fractures, deformities, or infections that require external stabilization to achieve proper healing.
