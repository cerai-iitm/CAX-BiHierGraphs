Detachment at Left Foot, Partial 5th Ray, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves the detachment of a portion of the 5th ray, or the smallest toe, on the left foot. The detachment is done using an open approach, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The partial removal of the 5th ray is typically done to treat deformities, injuries, or infections that affect the toe, and can help to alleviate pain and improve mobility. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the surgical site, enabling the surgeon to precisely detach the affected portion of the toe and repair surrounding tissues as needed.
