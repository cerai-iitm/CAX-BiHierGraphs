Excision of Toe Nail, External Approach
This surgical procedure involves the removal of a nail from the toe through an external approach, typically performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. Theurgery is usually indicated for ingrown toenails, fungal infections, or Damage to the nail bed. The procedure involves gently elevating the nail plate and removing the offending nail or portion of the nail, taking care to avoid damaging the surrounding tissue. After the procedure, the wound is cleaned and dressed to promote healing and prevent infection.
