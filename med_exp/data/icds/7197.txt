Excision of Right Wrist Region, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right wrist region to excise, or remove, a specific area of tissue or a lesion. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the affected area, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and remove the targeted tissue or growth. The excision site is typically closed with sutures or staples, and the procedure is often performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize patient discomfort. Post-operative care typically involves immobilization of the wrist and hand, as well as pain management to facilitate a smooth recovery.
