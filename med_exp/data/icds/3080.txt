Removal of Intraluminal Device from Upper Intestinal Tract, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
The removal of an intraluminal device from the upper intestinal tract is a minimally invasive procedure that utilizes an endoscope to access the device through the mouth or a surgical opening. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation, and the endoscope is navigated through the upper gastrointestinal tract to the location of the device. Once the device is located, specialized instruments are used to grasp and remove it from the intestinal tract. The procedure is usually performed on an outpatient basis and has a low risk of complications.
