Dilation of Left Lower Lobe Bronchus with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
During a minimally invasive procedure, an intraluminal device is utilized to dilate the left lower lobe bronchus through a natural or artificial opening using endoscopic guidance. This intervention is often performed to alleviate bronchial stenosis or obstruction, improving airflow and respiration. The procedure typically involves inserting a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the affected area and deploy the device precisely. By widening the airway, this treatment can provide significant relief from breathing difficulties and related symptoms.
