Insertion of Monitoring Device into Small Intestine, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves inserting a monitoring device into the small intestine through a natural or artificial opening using an endoscope, a flexible tube with a light and camera on the end. The device allows for real-time monitoring of physiological data, such as pH levels, temperature, or pressure, to aid in the diagnosis and management of various gastrointestinal conditions. The procedure is typically performed under conscious sedation or local anesthesia, and the device is usually left in place for a short period of time, ranging from several hours to several days. Once the data collection is complete, the device is Removed, and the results are analyzed to inform treatment decisions.
