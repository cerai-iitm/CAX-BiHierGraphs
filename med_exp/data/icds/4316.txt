Transfer Buttock Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves transferring tissue and fascia from the buttock area to another location in the body, typically to repair or reconstruct damaged tissue. This open approach procedure requires a surgical incision to access the underlying tissue and fascia, which is then carefully dissected and transplanted to the recipient site. The goal of this procedure is to restore form and function to the affected area, improving overall aesthetic and functional outcomes. The transferred tissue and fascia are secured in place using sutures or other fixation methods to ensure proper healing and integration.
