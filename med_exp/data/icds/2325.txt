Inspection of Trachea, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the use of an endoscope, a flexible tube with a camera and light on the end, to visually examine the trachea, or windpipe, through a natural or artificial opening. The endoscope is carefully inserted through the mouth or nose, or through a tracheostomy tube, allowing the healthcare provider to inspect the trachea for any abnormalities, injuries, or diseases. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia and may be done to diagnose conditions such as tracheal stenosis, tumors, or bleeding. The healthcare provider may also use specialized instruments passed through the endoscope to collect tissue samples or remove foreign objects from the trachea.
