Excision of Retroperitoneum, Percutaneous Approach, Diagnostic
This procedure involves using a percutaneous approach to excise tissue or a lesion from the retroperitoneum, a space in the abdominal cavity that surrounds the kidneys and other organs. The diagnostic excision is typically done under image guidance, such as CT or ultrasound, to precisely locate and remove the targeted tissue or lesion. The procedure is usually performed through a small incision in the skin, allowing for a minimally invasive approach that reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery. The excised tissue is then sent for further examination to help diagnose and treat any underlying conditions.
