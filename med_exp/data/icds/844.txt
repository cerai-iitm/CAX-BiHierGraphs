Removal of Infusion Device from Upper Artery, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical operation to extract an infusion device from an upper artery through an open approach, requiring a skin incision to access the affected artery. The surgeon carefully dissects the tissue to expose the infusion device, takes necessary precautions to control bleeding, and gently removes the device from the arterial wall. The artery is then repaired, and the incision is closed, usually with sutures or staples, to restore normal blood flow and promote healing. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure optimal outcomes.
