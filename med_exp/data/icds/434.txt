Extirpation of Matter from Tricuspid Valve, Open Approach
This procedure involves a surgical operation where the heart is accessed through an open incision to remove unwanted material, such as blood clots or vegetations, from the tricuspid valve. The tricuspid valve is one of the four heart valves that ensure blood flows in one direction, and any obstacles in this valve can disrupt the normal flow of blood. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the chest and opens the heart to visualize the tricuspid valve, then carefully removes the unwanted material to restore normal heart function. The goal of this procedure is to improve blood flow, alleviate symptoms, and reduce the risk of complications.
