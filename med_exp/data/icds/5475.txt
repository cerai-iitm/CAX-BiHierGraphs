Reposition Left Tibia with External Fixation Device, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the correction of a deformity or misalignment of the left tibia, a bone in the lower leg, using an external fixation device. The device is attached to the bone through small incisions in the skin, allowing for precise repositioning and stabilization. The percutaneous approach means that the procedure is minimally invasive, reducing tissue damage and promoting faster healing. Under anesthesia, the surgeon makes a series of small punctures in the skin to access the affected area, then adjusts the bone into its proper position using the external fixation device.
