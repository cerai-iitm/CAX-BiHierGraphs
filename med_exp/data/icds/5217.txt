Excision of Left Lower Femur, Open Approach
In this surgical procedure, the left lower femur is excised through an open approach, involving a skin incision to access the affected area. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the femur, enabling the surgeon to remove the targeted portion of the bone. The excision may be performed to treat conditions such as tumors, infections, or severe fractures that cannot be repaired through other means. The surgical site is then closed, typically with sutures or staples, to promote healing and facilitate recovery.
