Fluoroscopy of Superior Vena Cava using Other Contrast
This procedure involves the use of fluoroscopy to visualize the superior vena cava, a major vein that carries deoxygenated blood from the upper body to the heart. A specialized contrast agent is introduced into the bloodstream to enhance the visibility of the superior vena cava under X-ray fluoroscopy. The contrast agent is different from the standard iodine-based contrast media, and is used to highlight the vessel and its surrounding structures. The resulting images help healthcare professionals diagnose and treat conditions affecting the superior vena cava, such as venous stenosis or thrombosis.
