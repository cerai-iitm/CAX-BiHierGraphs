Release Abdomen Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdominal wall to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which is the layer of connective tissue that surrounds muscles. The goal of the procedure is to release tension or adhesions in the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which can be causing pain, limited mobility, or other symptoms. The open approach allows the surgeon to visually inspect the area and manually release any restrictive tissue or adhesions. The procedure can be used to treat a range of conditions, including compartment syndrome, nerve entrapment, or scar tissue buildup.
