Restriction of Abdominal Aorta with Branched or Fenestrated Intraluminal Device, One or Two Arteries, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of a branched or fenestrated intraluminal device in the abdominal aorta to restrict blood flow, typically to treat complex aortic aneurysms or dissections. The device is inserted through a small incision in the groin or arm, using X-ray guidance and local anesthesia. The branched or fenestrated design allows for blood flow to one or two arteries, maintaining perfusion to vital organs while isolating the damaged aortic segment. The procedure is often performed in high-risk patients who are not candidates for open surgical repair.
