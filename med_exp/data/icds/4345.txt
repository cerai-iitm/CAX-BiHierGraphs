Transfer Left Foot Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves transferring subcutaneous tissue and fascia from one area of the left foot to another, using an open approach. The goal of this procedure is to repair or reconstruct damaged or deficient tissue, promoting healing and improving overall foot function. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an incision in the left foot to access the affected area, carefully transferring the subcutaneous tissue and fascia as needed. The procedure may be performed to address various conditions, such as injuries, infections, or congenital deformities.
