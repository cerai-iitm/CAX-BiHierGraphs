Resection of Jejunum, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the abdomen to access the jejunum, a section of the small intestine, and removing a portion of it. The goal of the resection is to treat conditions such as obstruction, injury, or cancer that affect the jejunum, and to restore normal intestinal function. During the open approach, the surgeon carefully dissects and isolates the affected segment of the jejunum, and then removes it, repairing or reconnecting the remaining healthy tissue as needed. The resected segment is then sent for histopathological examination to confirm the diagnosis and guide further treatment.
