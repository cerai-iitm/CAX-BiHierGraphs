Supplement Left Femoral Artery with Nonautologous Tissue Substitute, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the placement of a nonautologous tissue substitute, such as a synthetic graft, into the left femoral artery to supplement its structure and function. The percutaneous approach allows the surgeon to access the artery through a small incision in the skin, reducing tissue trauma and promoting faster healing. Under fluoroscopic guidance, the substitute is deployed through a catheter, expanding to fit the artery's diameter and providing support to the arterial wall. This intervention aims to improve blood flow, alleviate symptoms, and prevent further vascular complications.
