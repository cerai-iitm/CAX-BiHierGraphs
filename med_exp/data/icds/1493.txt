Repair Right Brachial Vein, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the skin and underlying tissue to access the right brachial vein, which is a major blood vessel located in the upper arm. The goal of the operation is to repair any damage, defects, or blockages in the vein, such as tears, lacerations, or narrowing, which can disrupt blood flow to the arm and hand. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly visualize the vein and make any necessary repairs using sutures, patches, or other materials. The procedure is typically performed under general anesthesia or regional anesthesia to minimize discomfort and ensure proper healing.
