Extirpation of Matter from Left Internal Carotid Artery, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the neck to access the left internal carotid artery, allowing the surgeon to physically remove or extirpate unwanted matter, such as a blood clot or plaque, from the artery. The open approach requires careful dissection and retraction of surrounding tissues to visualize the artery and perform the extirpation. The goal of the procedure is to restore blood flow and prevent stroke or other complications. The procedure typically requires general anesthesia and is performed in a hospital setting by a trained vascular surgeon.
