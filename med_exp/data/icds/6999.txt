Insertion of Infusion Device into Cranial Cavity, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves making a small incision in the scalp to access the cranial cavity. A hollow needle or introducer is then inserted through the incision to create a pathway for the infusion device, which is typically a catheter or port. The device is carefully guided into the cranial cavity through the introducer, allowing for the targeted delivery of medications or other therapies directly into the brain. Once in place, the device is secured and the incision is closed, providing long-term access for future treatments.
