Resection of Thorax Lymphatic, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making a large incision in the chest wall to access the lymphatic system, allowing the surgeon to remove affected or diseased lymphatic tissue or nodes. The open approach provides direct visualization of the thoracic cavity, enabling the surgeon to precisely identify and resect the targeted lymphatic structures. The procedure is often performed to treat conditions such as lymphoma, lymph node cancer, or metastatic tumors that have spread to the thorax. By removing the diseased lymphatic tissue, the procedure aims to alleviate symptoms, improve respiratory function, and prevent further disease progression.
