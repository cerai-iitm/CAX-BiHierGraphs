Removal of Zooplastic Tissue from Heart, Open Approach
During an open-heart surgery, the surgeon carefully accesses the affected area of the heart to remove artificial or zooplastic tissue that has been implanted or grown in the heart. This tissue removal procedure is often performed to treat complications arising from previous cardiac interventions or device implantations. The goal of the procedure is to restore normal heart function by eliminating the obstruction or damage caused by the zooplastic tissue. The removal process involves careful dissection and excision of the artificial tissue to prevent further complications and ensure a successful outcome.
