Removal of Synthetic Substitute from Right Hip Joint, Femoral Surface, Open Approach
Here is the description:

This surgical procedure involves the removal of a synthetic substitute, such as a hip implant or prosthesis, from the right hip joint, specifically from the femoral surface. The open approach is used, where a surgical incision is made to access the affected area. The procedure aims to remove the synthetic material that has been implanted to replace or support the natural hip joint, which may have failed or become damaged. The removal of the synthetic substitute is a complex process that requires great skill and care to ensure a successful outcome and minimize potential complications.
