Fluoroscopy of Superior Mesenteric Artery using Low Osmolar Contrast
This medical imaging procedure involves the use of fluoroscopic guidance to visualize the superior mesenteric artery, which supplies blood to the small intestine. During the procedure, a low osmolar contrast medium is injected into the artery to enhance visibility. Real-time X-ray images are then taken to capture the flow of the contrast agent through the vessel, allowing for the assessment of blood flow and detection of any blockages or abnormalities. The resulting images provide valuable diagnostic information for healthcare providers to diagnose and treat conditions affecting the superior mesenteric artery.
