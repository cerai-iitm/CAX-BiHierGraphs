Resection of Upper Tooth, Single, Open Approach
This oral surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the gums to access the affected upper tooth, followed by careful dissection and removal of the tooth from its socket. The surgical site is then closed, usually with sutures, to promote healing and minimize bleeding. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to numb the area, and the patient may be advised to take pain medication and antibiotics as needed after the surgery. The removal of the tooth may be necessary due to severe decay, trauma, or other oral health issues that cannot be treated with restorative procedures.
