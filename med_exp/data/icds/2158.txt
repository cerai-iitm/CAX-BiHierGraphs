Drainage of Left Pleura, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest wall to access the left pleural space, which is the area between the lungs and chest cavity. The goal of the procedure is to drain accumulated fluid, blood, or air from the pleural space, which can be caused by various conditions such as pneumonia, cancer, or chest trauma. During the procedure, the surgeon inserts a drainage tube or catheter through the incision to remove the excess fluid or air, and may also perform additional procedures to close any air leaks or repair damaged tissue. The open approach allows for direct visualization of the pleural space and surrounding tissues, enabling the surgeon to accurately identify and address any underlying issues.
