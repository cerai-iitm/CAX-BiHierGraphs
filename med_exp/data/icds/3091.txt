Removal of Feeding Device from Lower Intestinal Tract, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the removal of a feeding device, such as a gastrostomy tube, from the lower intestinal tract through a percutaneous approach, which means the removal is done through the skin. The feeding device is typically inserted through the abdominal wall to provide nutrition to individuals who have difficulty ingesting food orally. The removal process typically requires a healthcare professional to access the device through the skin and gently pull it out of the body. The procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia and may require some pressure to be applied to the area to reduce bleeding.
