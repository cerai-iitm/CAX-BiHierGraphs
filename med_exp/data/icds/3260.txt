Revision of Feeding Device in Lower Intestinal Tract, Percutaneous Approach
This procedure involves the revision of a feeding device located in the lower intestinal tract through a percutaneous approach, which means the surgeon accesses the device through the skin. The goal of the revision is to adjust or replace the existing device to ensure proper nutrition and hydration. During the procedure, the surgeon makes a small incision in the skin to access the feeding device, which is then revised or replaced as needed. The percutaneous approach allows for a minimally invasive procedure, reducing recovery time and scarring.
