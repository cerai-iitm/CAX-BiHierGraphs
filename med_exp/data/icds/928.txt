Revision of Infusion Device in Upper Artery, Open Approach
Here is a detailed description of the procedure:

This surgical procedure involves the revision of an infusion device that has been previously implanted in an upper artery. The goal of the revision is to correct any issues or complications related to the original device, such as infection, device malfunction, or device migration. During the procedure, the surgeon makes an open incision in the skin to access the affected artery, carefully removes the existing infusion device, and replaces it with a new one. The new device is then secured in place, and the incision is closed to promote healing.
