Control Bleeding in Pelvic Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the pelvic cavity to directly visualize and control bleeding caused by trauma, injury, or surgical complications. The open approach allows the surgeon to directly access the site of bleeding and stop the hemorrhage using various techniques such as suturing, ligating, or cauterizing the affected blood vessels. The goal of the procedure is to restore hemostasis and prevent further blood loss, which is essential to prevent shock, organ failure, and even death. In some cases, the procedure may also involve repairing damaged tissues or organs in the pelvic cavity to prevent future complications.
