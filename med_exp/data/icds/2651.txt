Destruction of Duodenum, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This minimally invasive procedure involves the destruction of the duodenum, which is the first part of the small intestine, through an endoscopic approach. The procedure is performed through a natural or artificial opening, allowing the physician to visualize the duodenum and perform the destruction without the need for open surgery. The destruction of the duodenum is typically done to treat conditions such as bleeding, tumors, or other lesions that are causing harm to the patient. The endoscopic approach reduces the risk of complications and promotes faster recovery compared to traditional surgical methods.
