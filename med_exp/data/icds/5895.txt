Excision of Right Hip Joint, Open Approach
During this surgical procedure, an incision is made in the skin and soft tissue to access the right hip joint, allowing the surgeon to visualize the joint and remove damaged or diseased tissue, cartilage, or bone. The open approach enables a more direct and detailed examination of the joint, facilitating the removal of damaged structures and promoting optimal healing. The procedure may be performed to treat conditions such as osteoarthritis, inflammatory arthritis, or hip joint damage resulting from injury or trauma. The goal of the procedure is to alleviate pain, restore function, and improve mobility in the affected joint.
