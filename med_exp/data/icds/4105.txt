Extirpation of Matter from Right Lower Leg Subcutaneous Tissue and Fascia, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an open incision in the right lower leg to access the subcutaneous tissue and fascia, which are layers of tissue beneath the skin. The purpose of the procedure is to remove unwanted matter, such as infectious material, dead tissue, or foreign objects, from the affected area. The surgeon uses an open approach to directly visualize the site and carefully extract the unwanted material, taking care to preserve as much healthy tissue as possible. The goal of the procedure is to promote healing, reduce the risk of infection, and improve overall function in the affected leg.
