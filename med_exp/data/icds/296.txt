Bypass Coronary Artery, Two Arteries from Left Internal Mammary with Autologous Venous Tissue, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves creating a detour, or bypass, around blocked or narrowed coronary arteries, restoring blood flow to the heart. The operation uses both the left internal mammary artery and autologous venous tissue to bypass two coronary arteries. During the open approach, the surgeon opens the chest cavity to access the heart and grafts the vessels together. The goal of this complex procedure is to improve blood flow, relieve symptoms, and reduce the risk of heart attack or other cardiac complications.
