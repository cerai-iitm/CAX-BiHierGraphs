Control Bleeding in Pericardial Cavity, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves making an incision in the chest cavity to access the pericardial space, which is the sac that surrounds the heart. The goal of the procedure is to identify and control any sources of bleeding within the pericardial cavity, such as from a laceration or rupture of the heart or its surrounding vessels. The surgeon will visually examine the area to locate the source of the bleeding and use various techniques, such as suturing or cauterization, to stop the bleeding and restore hemostasis. Once the bleeding is controlled, the incision is closed, and the patient is closely monitored for any further complications.
