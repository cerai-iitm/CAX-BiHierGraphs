Drainage of Liver with Drainage Device, Percutaneous Approach
This minimally invasive procedure involves the use of a drainage device to remove fluid or infected material from the liver through a small incision in the skin. Under imaging guidance, a thin tube or catheter is inserted percutaneously, allowing the targeted area of the liver to be drained. The procedure is typically performed to treat conditions such as liver abscesses, cysts, or bile leaks, and can provide rapid relief from symptoms such as pain, fever, and jaundice. The drainage device is usually left in place for a period of time to ensure complete drainage and resolution of the underlying condition.
