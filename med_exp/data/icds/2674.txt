Dilation of Lower Esophagus with Intraluminal Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening Endoscopic
This procedure involves the insertion of an intraluminal device through a natural or artificial opening in the body to dilate, or widen, the lower esophagus. The device is guided through the esophagus via an endoscope, allowing the healthcare provider to visualize the area and perform the procedure with precision. The dilation process helps to relieve esophageal strictures or narrowing, allowing for improved passage of food and reduction of symptoms such as dysphagia and odynophagia. The procedure is typically performed under sedation or general anesthesia to ensure patient comfort and safety.
