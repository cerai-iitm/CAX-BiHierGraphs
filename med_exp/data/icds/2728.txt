Drainage of Stomach with Drainage Device, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the insertion of a drainage device through a natural or artificial opening to remove fluids or substances from the stomach. The device is carefully placed to allow for effective drainage, which can help to relieve symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, or abdominal distension. The goal of this procedure is to restore normal digestive function and alleviate discomfort. The insertion site is typically cleaned and prepared to minimize the risk of complications, and the drainage device is secured in place to ensure proper function.
