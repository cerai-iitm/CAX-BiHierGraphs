Transfer Left Trunk Muscle with Skin, Open Approach
This surgical procedure involves transferring muscle tissue from the left trunk area, along with its accompanying skin, to a new location on the body. The open approach is used, which means a large incision is made to access the muscle and skin to be transferred. The goal of this procedure is to restore function, improve mobility, or enhance aesthetics in the affected area. The transferred muscle and skin are carefully reattached to the new site, and the incision is closed in layers to promote optimal healing and minimize scarring.
