Introduction of Other Therapeutic Substance into Mouth and Pharynx, Via Natural or Artificial Opening
This procedure involves the administration of a therapeutic substance through the mouth or pharynx, either through natural openings such as the mouth or through artificial openings created for this purpose. The substance is introduced directly into the oral or pharyngeal cavity to achieve a specific therapeutic effect, such as reducing inflammation, fighting infection, or promoting healing. The procedure is typically performed in a clinical setting by a trained healthcare professional, who takes precautions to ensure the substance is delivered safely and effectively. The goal of this intervention is to provide targeted treatment to the affected area, minimizing the risk of systemic side effects and promoting optimal outcomes.
