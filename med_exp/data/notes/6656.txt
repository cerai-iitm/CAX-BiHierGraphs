Chief Complaint : Mr. ___ is a ___ year old male who presents to the ED due to 
complain of a sore throat and FOE that demonstrated laryngeal 
tumor. In the ED patient reports he has had a globus sensation 
in his throat for the past 3 months. Initially 3 weeks ago he 
started noticing hoarseness and having difficulty with solid PO 
intake. Since this time he has only been eating baby food and 
yogurt. 

He denies shortness of breath, difficulty swallowing, drooling,
difficulty sleeping or lying flat, or any other symptoms. 

On arrival ENT was consulted and patient underwent fiberoptic 
exam, which showed in the larynx a large fungating mass along 
the glottic and supraglottic region involving the epiglottis. He 
also underwent a CT Neck which showed a 3.0 x 2.7 x 5.0 cm 
irregularly enhancing soft tissue lesion within the laryngeal 
vestibule involving the glottis, subglottis and superior trachea 
as well as the lower hypopharynx which demonstrates likely 
invasion into the right thyroid cartilage, involvement of the 
cricoid cartilage and circumferential involvement at the level 
of the thyroid cartilage resulting in severe airway
narrowing.

Due to these findings patient was taken into the operating room 
tonight. He underwent a direct laryngoscopy, biopsy and awake 
tracheostomy. He is now admitted to the SICU for postoperative 
airway watch.
Past Medical History : atrial fibrillation on Eliquis 
HFrEF, recovered EF
HTN
COPD
Emphysema
